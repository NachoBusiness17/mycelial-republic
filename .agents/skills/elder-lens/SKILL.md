# ELDER LENS — the stolen procedure

**Grounding:** queue/lanes/elder/surface.md — 14 days of lenses, 09-04..09-18
(12 real, 2 lane-down). The procedure below is reverse-engineered from the
lenses that landed; the question ledger (SELF_STEAL_DEEP 2026-09-18) scores
their answer-rate: 3 answered / 5 partial / 4 unanswered of 13.

## Procedure
0. **Intake:** a finished gala/window synthesis output (STORY.md, laws.md,
   measure.md, DIGEST.md under watch/inbox/<window>/) is a valid lens input
   whole, same as a day's cards: the synthesis already read the day, the
   lens reads the synthesis. (Intake widened by the card's own salvage
   clause at the 2026-10-02T04:09Z salvage banking, per
   watch/inbox/ractor50/skills.md section 2.2; first lens banked on this
   intake: watch/inbox/fillball/elder-salvage.md.)
1. **Read the day's finished cards whole** (not summaries — the byte-true
   law applies to lenses doubly: a lens on summaries is a lens on lenses).
2. **Find the ONE pattern** that covers all cards from the same side — not
   per-card commentary. If no single pattern exists, say so; forced patterns
   are the elder's cardinal sin.
3. **Anchors only where real, named:** physics (conservation, back-action,
   measurement), comp-sci (pointers, caches, logging, Goodhart), esoteric
   (golem/shem, genizah, Watcher) — name the anchor AND the place in the
   cards it actually binds. Decorative anchors are moon-speak.
4. **THE ELDER'S QUESTION** — end with the question the fleet is NOT asking.
   Requirements: uncomfortable, unowned (no one's job already), and answerable
   by evidence that could exist. File it to the question ledger — questions
   are primary data with an owner and an answer-rate meter.
5. **Label: LENS, NOT CLAIM — Unproven.** The lens never grants permissions,
   never commands. It reads and asks.
