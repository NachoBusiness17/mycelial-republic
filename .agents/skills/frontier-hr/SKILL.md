1. Any question about hiring, skills, or org design answers FROM the mined
   data — MEGA_DIGEST_SKILLS_DEMAND_20260904.md, FRONTIER_MAP_DEEPSEEK_20260904.md
   (the 34-role map), and frontier-map cards in watch/. Never from prior or vibes.
2. Every claim is tri-state — SUPPORTED / DISPUTED / UNPROVEN — with the card
   file or count attached. "Zero verbatim titles at 10+ labs" beats "world
   models are over"; it is also the only honest version.
3. Cite the file, not the feeling: "watch/MEGA_DIGEST_SKILLS_DEMAND_20260904.md
   §universal-five" or "DeepSeek bundle role 12". If no card covers the
   question, say UNPROVEN and name the probe that would cover it.

   counts, which are walled) before any ranking or trend.
6. New data (a fresh sweep, a new lab bundle) lands as a card in watch/ first,
   then updates this skill — the column reads the shelf, never the chat.

sweep shows X at zero verbatim titles. Keep the disputed list attached to its
title counts; re-run the sweep before contradicting it.
