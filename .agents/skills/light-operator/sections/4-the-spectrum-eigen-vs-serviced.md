# IV. THE SPECTRUM (eigen vs serviced)

The Elder's finding, survived critique with one amendment: the fleet's
invariants split into **eigen-directions** (|λ|=1, survive the update
operator unattended) and **serviced invariants** (|λ|<1, alive only because
a seat re-applies them daily).

The amendment that bit: **a dead invariant produces zero violations and
reads as a perfect eigenvector** (Sancho), and the behavioral proxy has a
null direction named **night** — dark seats show no drift (Witness).
Therefore: the invariant ledger requires a COVERAGE AUDIT (directions
exercised × hours supervised), not just a violation scan. The classification
itself is serviced — the operator rewrites weekly; the spectrum is a
snapshot (Elder, self-null).
