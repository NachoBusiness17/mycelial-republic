'''Elizabeth''' is an [[NPC]] located in the {{zone|Community Center}}. To access her associated features, players must be at least {{SkyBlock Level|3}}.

==Usage==
{{Main|City Projects|Fire Sales|Account & Profile Upgrades|Booster Cookie|Bits}}
'''Elizabeth''' has many different features in her menu, such as the option to see the most recent and all past [[City Projects]] and [[Fire Sales]]. 

Another feature is the [[Account & Profile Upgrades]] menu, where players can get bonuses for them on all of their [[profiles]], such as more {{Stat|MF}} and [[Ender Chest]] pages. The player can also get bonuses for all their co-op members on one profile, some for free (like [[Minion|Minion Slots]], some costing {{Gems}} such as [[Co-op|Co-op slots]]. 

Another menu allows players to receive {{RD|1 Booster Cookie}} for free and then purchase additional ones for {{Gems}}. The user can buy {{RD|1 Booster Cookie}} for {{Gems|325}}, {{RD|6 Booster Cookie}}s for {{Gems|1950}}, or {{RD|12 Booster Cookie}}s for {{Gems|3900}}.

In her menu is also located the Bits Shop, where players can use {{Bits}}, obtained using {{ID|Booster Cookie}}s, to purchase various items. Most notably, the player can buy a {{ID|God Potion}} for {{Bits|1500}}, a {{ID|Kismet Feather}} for {{Bits|1350}},  {{ID|Pure Black Dye}} and {{ID|Pure White Dye}} for {{Bits|250000}}, and [[Enrichments]] for {{Bits|5000}}

Her last menu lists the 5 commonly available [[Ranks]] on Hypixel and their perks: {{Rank|VIP}}, {{Rank|VIP+}}, {{Rank|MVP}}, {{Rank|MVP+}}, and {{Rank|MVP++}}. Furthermore, there is an item at the bottom of this page, as well as other pages, displaying your current {{Gems}}, {{Bits}}, and [[Fame Ranks|Fame Rank]].

== Account and Profile Upgrades ==
These upgrades can be applied to either your current profile or your entire account. Some upgrades are free, while others require SkyBlock Gems to unlock. Each upgrade takes a certain amount of time to complete, but you can use SkyBlock Gems to skip the waiting.

To fully upgrade everything to the maximum level, a player will need '''6,050 SkyBlock Gems'''. The total waiting time is '''382 real-world days''' for profile upgrades and '''244 days''' for account upgrades. You can choose to spend an additional '''80,904 SkyBlock Gems''' to skip the waiting period entirely. The cost will depend on the current value of SkyBlock Gems.
