Players can earn '''Fame Ranks''' by contributing to [[City Projects|city projects]] and spending [[SkyBlock Bits|Bits]] and [[SkyBlock Gems|Gems]] with [[Elizabeth]].

== Earning Fame ==
Players earn 1 Fame per [[Bits|Bit]] spent at Elizabeth, and 200 for every [[SkyBlock Gems|Gem]] spent in the community shop. Players could also earn 200 Fame for every contribution made to a [[City Project|city project]].
