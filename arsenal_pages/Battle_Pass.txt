Removed Content
This content of the game is no longer available within the public game.
It will likely not be brought back into the game.
The 
Battle Pass
 is a feature in 
Arsenal
 introduced in the 
Summer Update II
. It has two versions: Free and Premium. The Premium battle pass rewards could only be obtained if the user had 
ROBLOX
 Premium, paid 399 Robux, or 14,999 B$. It included exclusive rewards, such as 
melees
, 
skins
 and 
kill effects
. However, the free battle pass rewards could be obtained by anyone. To progress in the battle pass, players had to gain kills. Players got a new reward from the battle pass every 150 kills.
Battle Pass
The first row of the battle pass indicates the free rewards, while the second row indicates rewards for players with a Premium subscription, 
399 Robux
, or 14,999 B$.
