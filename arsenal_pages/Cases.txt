A 
case
 is a lootbox that users can buy from the 
Shop
, and it gives out a random item depending on what case is opened. If players got a duplicate item in a case, they will get 50% of the 
B$
 that was spent to purchase the case back. Players will also receive a Character Case upon their first win.
Due to local laws preventing lootboxes in games, cases are disabled in Belgium, the Netherlands, Australia and for users in the United Kingdom who are under 18.

The 
Flair Case
 costs 600B$ and gives out 
kill effects
, 
announcers
 and 
taunts
. It won't give out event-exclusive or code-exclusive announcers, taunts or kill effects. Duplicates from flair cases give players 300B$ back. Flair case used to contain melees, but they were moved to a separated case in the 
Minor Monday Update
.
The 
Character Case
 costs 1200B$ and contains 
skins
. Duplicate skins are also collectable and it will not give out any event-exclusive or code-exclusive skins. In the 
Summer Update III
, the case was split in 11 different cases each containing their own set of skins.
On May 2nd, 2022, a 12th crate was released containing skins exclusive to the crate.

The 
Unusual Case
 was a limited-time crate that costed 24000B$, introduced in the 
Halloween Update
. It gave out 
skins
 aswell as 
Knives
, But no gloves!. 
This case would be removed from the shop shortly after the first Halloween event ended. However, the case would return with different Halloween-exclusive unusuals in it every Halloween event. The 
Halloween Update IV
, the 
Frights in the Night Update
 and the 
Day of the Dead Update
 are currently the only Halloween events to exclude the Unusual Case.
Originally, in the first couple minutes of the Halloween Update, the case costed 30000B$.
Introduced in the 
Winter Update: Part II
, the 
Gift Case
 costs 15000B$. Similar to the original system for the Character Case, it contains all of the 
skins
 featured on the twelve separated cases. Unboxing one would give each skin for everyone in the server, including the purchaser.
This case also has a 5% chance of an unusual effect. It was removed on January 1st, 2022, but it came back in the 
Christmas Update V
. The 
Winterfest Update
 is currently the only Christmas event to exclude the Gift Case.

Updates
Ever since the 
2.6 Update
, the chance of getting a duplicate has been lowered, which means cases are more likely to give players items they haven't unlocked yet.
In the 
August Update
, there's a 1% (now 2%) chance of getting an unusual skin in the Character Cases. After opening the duplicates, the duplicate skin will be shown via (2X).
Since the 
September Update
, the 
Starter Pack
 now gives the player a Flair and a Character Case.
Since the 
Minor Monday Update
, melees have been moved to a separated Melee Case and are no longer available in the Flair Case.
The 
Summer Update II
 introduced the Weapon Skin Case.
Since the 
Pre-Halloween Update II
, users can swipe through cases with a scroller.
In the 
Prime Gaming Update
, the Cases section in the 
Shop
 was moved to be the first option.
This was changed back on an unknown date.
In the 
Summer Update III
, the Character Case was split into 11 different cases.
The 
Winter Update: Part II
 introduced the Calling Card Case.
On August 23rd, 2022, cases were disabled in Belgium and the Netherlands.
Trivia
The Character Case model appears to be based on the cases from 
Counter Strike: Global Offensive
.
This model was also used for the Unusual Case and The Crate of All Time.
Most of the cases are based on real life ammo cases.
The mesh on top of the flair case is the 
Bloxxer
 T-Shirt.
The model of the Summer 2021 Crate was taken from Flanker's 
Summer Striker backpack
 from 
Typical Colors 2
.
The model of the Flair/Melee/Weapon Skin cases were used for the 
Fitten Co. Cosmetic Crate Arsenal
 in 
Typical Colors 2
.
