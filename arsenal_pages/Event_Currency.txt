Event Currencies
 are pickups introduced in the 
Summer Update
 to unlock several new Locker items if you collected a certain amount. When you kill a player, that player drops a ticket along with the health pack and ammo pack they will drop. To collect it, just walk over it. It will also make a little bell chime.
Event Currency can take different forms, such as tickets, fireworks or candies. The functionality remains the same, however, the only thing that differs is its appearance.
In the 
Throwback Thumbtack
 event, the method of obtaining Event Currency was changed to be based on secured kills, rather than collectible pickups from dead players.
In the 
Halloween 2020
 event, players would receive currency by playing rounds, completing contracts, and beating the 
Hackula Boss
. Once the player had obtained the required amount of currency, they could buy an item from the contracts shop. Unlike any other event, currency would be deducted based on the price of said item.
In the 
Summer Care Package
, a spinning wheel was introduced. Players should able to obtain tickets by getting kills in order to use the spinning wheel to get a single skin, however the chances of getting one is very low until it was increased on the 
Halloween 2021
 event.
In addition, the event currencies usually match the icon placed on the event button tab. The only exceptions to this are Throwback Thumbtack, which has a crudely drawn Delinquent face called Del2.0 that was made by 
SkyCityXD
 6 months prior as its icon; Holiday Havoc, which uses stockings as its icon; and the Valentines Event, which uses a heart as its icon.
The daily event for Christmas introduced rewards stretched over a certain time period, where players can log in each day to receive a new event-themed reward. If they missed the rewards, they still can obtain them by catching up a day they missed the reward which costs 25 Robux per use until the event ended.

Trivia
All of the Advent Calendar skins from 2019, 2020 and 2022 respectively had no signature weapons originally.
In addition to this, originally all items except skins and cash from the 2020 event had placeholder icons.
Despite the 2020 event being titled "12 Days of Christmas", there were actually 13 days in total in the event.
There was originally going to have an extremely rare ticket drop change for an "Unusual Ticket" option on the spinning wheel currency that could give players a random unusual effect with the obtained skin. However, this has been scrapped on development.
The New Years event was originally going to have a feature to "Catch Up" if a user missed the unusual, but it was scrapped.
The 7th day for Advent Calendar 2022 originally going to be a 
taunt
, however it wasn't finished by the time Advent Calendar was released. So, it instead showed up as Delinquent with a Placeholder 
unusual effect
.
It is also the purpose of creating an entirely new 
calling card
 to give players an actual item instead of simply nothing.
Developer Krystal Flynn mentioned events that prominently included Dominus hood skins were scheduled to return as the same annually, so developers specifically don't need to make new content in order for them to focus on the actual updates that was being developed on.
The 2023 Travelling Hearts event was originally scheduled to be conclude on March 1st.
The menu icon for Spring Spirit's Egg Hunt was taken from an image in 
ROBLOX Creator Marketplace
 called 
eggs
.
