ARSENAL RARITY HUB
Rarity
, also known as 
Quality
, is the state of being a rare item. It is defined by color by a square located at the top right of an inventory item.

Accessible Rarities
Default (#474747)
Default items that are obtained when the player first start the game. Default 
announcers
, 
skins
 and 
kill effects
 all fall under this category. However, Suspicious Stranger skin's rarity is labelled as Default despite not being a stock skin.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 1
Common (#008100)
Common items are reserved for cheap flairs obtained in the 
shop
, event items that don't require many 
revenues
. The flair items are worth around 600-1200B$.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 1
Rare (#2079ff)
Rare items are more expensive and worth around 750-1250B$. The color of Rare rarity was changed to be more lighter in 
Summer Update II
, as the HEX code was 
#00aaff
.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 5
Epic (#8000ff)
Epic items are the most frequent in terms of any rarities and worth around 600-1600B$. The color of Epic rarity was changed to be more lighter in Summer Update II, as the HEX code was 
#aa00ff
.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 10
Legendary (#ff4000)
Legendary items are habitually the most expensive in both the 
shop
 and 
events
. They are worth around 1200-2400B$. The color of Legendary rarity was changed to be more lighter in Summer Update II, as the HEX code was 
#b55b0c
.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 50
Admin (#ff0004)
Admin items are usually items that have a lot of rarity in them. The only obtainable Admin item is Delinquent That's Cool 
skin
, albeit available every April 20th in the shop. The color of Admin rarity was changed to be more lighter in Summer Update II, as the HEX code was 
#aa0000
.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 150
Promo (#ffff00)
Promo items are mostly promotional material from collaborations of content creators, subscription services, brands or other 
ROBLOX
 games. 
Community event prizes
, purchasing correlated 
ROBLOX
 accessories, items that references non-
ROBLOX
 games and 
VIP skins
 also incorporated in the rarity.
Rarity Addition:
 June 12, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 100
Bundle (#ffaaff)
Bundle items are reserved for in-game bundles, the only bundles to have this rarity completely are the 
Opera GX Bundle
 and Day of the Dead Bundle. Froggy 
melee
 is also categorized as Bundle.
Rarity Addition:
 January 4, 2020
Crafting
 XP:
 0
Quest Item (#00007f)
Quest Item is a rarity usually associated to items that are earned from 
quests
, such as 
Citrus Quest
, 
Prologue Quest
, 
Sword Hunt
.
Rarity Addition:
December 20, 2021
Crafting
 XP:
 1
Retired (#aaff7f)
Retired items are items that have been released in past 
events
 and have been revamped for newer releases. The only item that currently has this rarity is Seeker of Hearts 
skin
 from 
Valentine's Day 2020
.
Rarity Addition:
February 14, 2022
Crafting
 XP:
 0
Inaccessible Rarities
Tooltip Label (Various)
Tooltip Label is a rarity that appears as placeholder for items that have no rarity assigned. The only items to have this rarity are certain 
taunts
 used for testing.
Rarity Addition:
January 11, 2019
Crafting
 XP:
 N/A
Legendary-Old (#b55b0c)
Legendary-Old is a leftover rarity, as it uses the old HEX code of Legendary rarity. A developer-exclusive skin, 
BuffBrickbattle
 is assigned to this rarity for unknown reasons.

June 20, 2020

Trivia
It is likely the Bundle rarity was created for 
Gamepass Bundles
, as it was made around the same time the bundles were introduced. However, it was left unused officially until 
Opera GX Update
 for an unknown reason.
Tooltip Label rarity could appear when nothing is set as a favorite item in Career Page or in the 
shop
 while loading assets.
