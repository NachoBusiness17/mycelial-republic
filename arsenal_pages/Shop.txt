Arsenal Shop Hub
The Shop is where players can buy cosmetics such as 
skins
, 
melees
, 
kill effects
, 
taunts
 and more by spending 
BattleBucks
. The items can be purchased through the Daily Shop or can be obtained via 
cases
. Exclusive items can be obtained through 
bundles
. Players can also purchase BattleBucks using Robux.
Daily Shop
The Daily Shop is where new items appear every day.
There are 8 items, which can be 
skins
, which always appear in the Daily Shop, 
melees
, 
kill effects
, and 
taunts
. Their prices range from 600 
B$
 to 2400 B$. Previously, 
announcers
 also appeared in the Daily Shop, but were removed on an unknown date.
The Daily Shop resets every day at 1 AM PST.
Cases
Also known as crates, Cases (which are located in the "Cases" tab) can be bought to get 
kill effects
, 
announcers
, 
taunts
, 
melees
, 
skins
, 
weapon skins
 and 
calling cards
. A case is of five kinds: flair, character, melee, weapon skin and calling card.
Flair Cases can be bought for 600 
B$
; the player can get kill effects, taunts and announcers from it.
Melee Cases can be bought for 900 B$; the player can get melees from it.
Character Cases can be bought for 1200 B$; the player can get skins from it.
Weapon Skin Cases can be bought for 900 B$; the player can get weapon skins from it.
Calling Card Cases can be bought for 600 B$; the player can get calling cards from it.
More information on Cases can be viewed 
here
.
Bundles
Bundles, as the name suggests, give a bundle of exclusive cosmetics, that can only be obtained from said bundle. They cost Robux, and are located in the "Bundles" tab. They can contain 
skins
, 
melees
, 
taunts
, 
calling cards
 and more.
More information on Bundles can be viewed 
here
.
