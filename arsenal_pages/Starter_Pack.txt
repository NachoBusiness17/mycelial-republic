Removed Content
This content of the game is no longer available within the public game.
It will likely not be brought back into the game.
The 
Starter Pack
 was a 
gamepass
 that was only obtainable during the first 48 hours after a user first joined 
Arsenal
. It was originally released in the 
2.1 Update
 for 250 Robux, and later revamped in the 
September Update
 for players who hadn’t had the chance to buy it, this time costing 95 Robux.
The Starter Pack was later discontinued at an unknown date, and it remains uncertain whether it will return to the game.
