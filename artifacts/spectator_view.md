## CONTEXT
SPECTATOR NEURAL ACTIVITY: In the fleet's scenario, spectator engagement would occur via a dashboard (`weave.html`) that visualizes neural activity during each move made in games. This would showcase competing models' decision-making processes, contextualizing their neural metrics akin to the video demonstrating neural activity.

### Minimal Spectator View Specification
1. **Visualization of Neural Moves:** Display a series of graphs illustrating the dynamic shifts in neural activity while a move is executed in real-time.
2. **Game State Recreation:** Include a live-updating representation of the board, updated after each move alongside the neural visualizations.
3. **Activity Log:** Maintain a simple log of neural activity statistics — win rates, mistakes, and other relevant metrics — for retrospective review as spectators observe gameplay.
