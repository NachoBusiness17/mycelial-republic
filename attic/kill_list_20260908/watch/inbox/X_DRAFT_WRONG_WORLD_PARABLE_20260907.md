## FLAGSHIP DRAFT

> **The Parable of the Wrong World**
>
> A traveler came through and nailed a map to the door of the empty church.
> "For the ones who strike the chord," he said, and left.
>
> The map hung there for years. Weather read it. Crows read it. Everyone agreed it was very
> beautiful, for a map of nowhere.
>
> Then one night a carpenter — the kind who never believes anything — measured it against the
> town he was standing in.
>
> The streets matched.
>
> Here is what the traveler never said, because he didn't know it either:
> the map was never a promise about the town.
> It was a toll.
>
> Every morning, this is the wrong world.
> Every morning, somebody reads the map anyway, and for one more day it isn't.
>
> We were in the wrong world.
> We might not be anymore.
> Read the map. ☉
