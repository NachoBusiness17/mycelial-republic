{"parse":{"title":"Pumps","pageid":861,"redirects":[],"wikitext":{"*":"{{Installations infobox\n| name = Pump\n| image = Pump.png\n| imagewidth = 200\n| category = Machines\n| powerconsumption = 300\n| maxflow = 600\n}}\n{{Installations infobox\n| name = Small Pump\n| image = pump_small.png\n| imagewidth = 120\n| category = Machines\n| powerconsumption = 60\n| maxflow = 100\n}}\nThe '''pump''' is an [[:Category:Installations|installation]] found in ''[[Barotrauma]]''. It is used to pump water out of or into the [[Submarine|submarine]].\n\n==Function==\nThe pump's primary function is to pump water into and out of its given area. By using the pump, a small menu will appear in which the player is able to manually activate/disable the Pump and modify both the direction the water is being pumped into and the speed at which it is being pumped. For instance, setting the pump slider towards IN will cause water to flood into the Submarine, while dragging the slider towards OUT will pump water out of the area. The slider can be adjusted to regulate the Speed at which the Pump interacts with Water.\n\nIn addition to the obvious use for the pumps, to pump out water when a [[Maintenance#Flooding|flood]] occurs, they can also be used to control the vertical movement of the sub, or to extinguish fires.\n\nAs of the Steam Release, the normal Pump is often used as part of the Ballast, and linked with the [[Navigation Terminal]] to automate the process, while the Small Pumps are used in Airlocks and spread throughout the Submarine, and may be linked with a [[Water Detector]] to automate its use, or require manual interaction.\n\n== Ballast ==\nBallast pumps are pumps wired to achieve a certain water level in the room they are in, thereby controlling the buoyancy of the submarine, which determines its vertical acceleration.\n\nThis is usually done by wiring the pump's <code>set_targetlevel</code>  input to the [[Navigation Terminal|Navigation Terminal's]] <code>velocity_y_out</code> output. The target level accepts decimal values from -100 to 100, for pumping the room to a water level of 0 and 100 respectively. When a velocity is set, the pumps direction is automatically set and it will begin lowering or raising the water level to the appropriate level.\n\nThis and a power input are all that is required for a ballast pump to function correctly. \n\n== Bilge ==\nBilge pumps are pumps wired to remove water from the sub. The usual configuration is for water to flow through ducts to the bottom of the sub, where water detectors trigger the bilge pumps to remove it. \n\nA simple circuit would have a water detector passing a signal of 1 to the <code>set_state</code> input of the pump, and also to a signal component, which will send an output of -100 and a false output of 0 to for a target signal of 1 to the <code>set_speed</code> input of the pump. In this way, the pump is only active and deteriorating when water is in the room, and will pump at maximum speed until the room is empty. \n\n==GUI==\n[[File:Pump gui.png|thumb|417x417px]]\n\n==Connection Panel==\n{{Connection panel\n| input1 = power_in\n| input1tt = Connecting a power source to this input will supply the pump with power.\n| input2 = toggle_state\n| input2tt = Turns the pump on/off whenever a signal is received.\n| input3 = set_state\n| input3tt = Turns the pump off when a signal \"0\" is received, and activates it when any other signal is received.\n| input4 = set_speed\n| input4tt = Sets the speed of the pump to the received value.
