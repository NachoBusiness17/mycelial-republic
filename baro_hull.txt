{"parse":{"title":"Hull","pageid":3737,"wikitext":{"*":"Hulls are rectangular areas in which water will not spawn. They are used to define areas which are inside the submarine, and also to designate rooms within the submarine for the [[Status Monitor]] and command callouts. \n\nIn order for a Hull to be watertight it must be fully enclosed by other structural objects such as [[Walls|internal walls and shells]]. A vent linked to an [[oxygen generator]] must be used to fill a Hull with oxygen. \n\n<br />\n\n=== Submarine Editor ===\nThe Hull object is found in the Structure category of objects, and can be made visible or invisible via the Visibility Options. \n\n=== Gaps ===\nGaps are another object found in the Structure category, and are the means by which air and water can move between Hulls. Doors and Hatches come with a predefined Gap as part of the object.\n[[Category:Submarines]]"}}}
