‎Introduction

The guide below provides an overview for the Security talents, their viability, as well as mathematical and crafting visualizations for your ease of use - updated as of 1.8.7.0 (April 16th, 2025).

You will find the following features in the guide:
• Analysis and viability of Security skill trees.
• Potential talents to avoid.
• Crafting visualizations for Security related armaments




The Security officer is a highly versatile class, offering great defensive, offensive and manufacturing capabilities - alongside its rather unique specializations, this class is a solid pick for any team, ranging from duo campaigns to large submarine crews.

Primary responsibilities
  • Operating Turrets.
  • Repelling intruders, both human and animal
  • Fabrication of Weapons and Ammunition.

Primary responsibilities
  • Weapon logistics (fabricating and delivering submarine ammo to gun/shell racks) ensuring your crew is always restocked and armed with the best quality weapons
  • Manufacturing explosives and restocking railgun shells & depth charges with appropriate explosives
  • Communicating with the crew to ensure steady supply of ammunition crafting materials
  • Maintaining order on the submarine by i) monitoring escort mission personnel for sabotage activity ii) checking multiplayer submarine players for sabotage equipment and illegal weapons iii) confiscating griefing equipment and neutralizing griefers
  • Ensuring timely reloading of submarine guns

Passive talents - Tier 2








Specilization 1 - Frogman

This talent tree is for those who wish to become terror of the seas - you will become one with water, gaining enhanced offensive capabilities during underwater expeditions, while unlocking powerful craftable tools to bring down the fiercest creatures you encounter.

Specilization 2 - Enforcer

This talent tree takes you down the path of the brute, dominating human,alien and monster alike with sheer firepower and unbreakable will; the perks in this tree will keep you standing with multiple survivability and tank based talents.












Specilization 3 - Gunner

You are here because you don't take half measures - you like firepower, and you mean to deliver a lot of it to the enemy in a tree dedicated to crafting automatic weapons, improving explosives and increasing damage.













Crafting tree
This section will visualize the crafting tree that Security officers can use to improve their crafting efforts in the future. Do note that weapons that require other class talents, like the Revolver, will not be included, as they cannot be crafted by Security.
