They can also be used to seal [[doors]] and [[hatches]]. The only way to un-seal a door is by using a [[Plasma Cutter]].\n\nStructures being affected by the welding tool are highlighted by a rectangle. The color of that rectangle shows the condition of the structure: from red indicating poor condition, to green indicating good condition. Sealing a door will have a grey progress bar.\n\n==Usage==\nThe Welding Tool is held with both hands. Holding down the right-click (mouse2) button aims towards the cursor.
