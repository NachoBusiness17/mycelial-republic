Download
Here is a save just after completing the intro, with 200 Judge Blood.

Download `200_judge_blood.zip`, extract 0.sav into your save folder.

Mirrors:
https://github.com/user-attachments/files/19048898/200_judge_blood.zip

Where to find your saves:
https://steamcommunity.com/app/1037020/discussions/0/1638662230376246655/
Edit
With a hex editor (e.g., HxD).:
  1. Backup your save file first! Editing can corrupt it.
  2. Replace the 4 bytes starting at 0x05 with C8 00 00 00 (hex for 200 in little-endian).
  3. Save the changes and test in-game.
