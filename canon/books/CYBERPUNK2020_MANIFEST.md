## Why this is canon

The original edgerunner mythology - the sourcebook that defined Night City,
the solo/netrunner/fixer economy, lifepaths, cyberware, and the corporate
war decades before 2077. The estate's free-lane mining economy is this
book's fiction twin: edgerunners taking contract work from fixers against
corporate rails, paid in a corporate currency, dying of cyberpsychosis
when they install too much and integrate too little.

The estate ties, named:
- lifepath tables = the palace models (a person as a rolled history)
- cyberware = the organs (install capacity vs integration - the
  cyberpsychosis line is the humanliness law)
- "trust the tech, not the talk" = trust bytes, not reports
- the Fixer = the MFS funnel; the Solo = the free-lane miner; the Netrunner
  = the agy/mesh lanes; Rockerboy = the wave
- Night City's corporate war = the GSTD rail's counterparty landscape
