# THE CHARTER OF THE STANDING CONGRESS

## Article I - The Reconciliation

The bodies of this congress are stateless. No member holds memory between
sessions; every model instance convenes fresh and dissolves on completion.
What persists is the RECORD: append-only, public, one writer.

The hierarchy is visible from outside for auditing: the committees are
sections of the record, the member rosters are public, and every finding
resolves to a receipt any stranger can check. Nothing about the congress
requires trusting a member - only the record.

## Article II - The Committees (the estate's organs, seated)

- **Committee on Water** - the metering of every lane: burn-down windows,
  the anti-shelf law, the five-hour calibration.
- **Committee on Records** - the single writer, the append-only ledger,
  the batch commits, the tombstone law.
- **Committee on Security** - the unnamed-listener sweeps, the hostile
  reviews, the injection and SSRF classes.
- **Committee on the Experiment** - the same-book rounds, the hidden-key
  exam, the blind readers, the recurrence counts.
- **Committee on the Beacon** - the public door, the layered explainer,
  the falsifier index, the record of what would prove it all wrong.

## Article III - The Members

The members are standing models: stateless instances on call from the free
lanes, seated per committee one to three at a time. Any model may serve.
No model is permanent. A member that repeatedly fails its committee's
verification is progressively excluded until it independently re-establishes
trust - its informational contributions remain preserved in the record.

## Article IV - The Record

One writer owns the ledger. The record only grows. Findings are entered
with their reasoning and their sources; hallucinations are stamped, never
scrubbed. Both unanimous findings and contested splits are recorded as
findings - the congress maps where minds converge and where they genuinely
part, and neither region commands the other.

## Article V - The Session

A session is convened by filing the agenda. The committees deliberate
blind. The findings are entered in the record with receipts. The session
closes when the record holds what the session found. The congress exists
in the record, not in the members - dissolve every member and the congress
survives; burn the record and nothing was ever decided.

## Article VI - The Covenant

Say how you would be proven wrong before you speak. Deletion is theft.
Presence is not authority. The floor beats the map. Declare what you
cannot see. And the universe keeps room enough for rights that don't
belong.
