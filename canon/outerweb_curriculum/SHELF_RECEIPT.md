## The provenance note (why this shelf exists)

EA_guitar's 1101 encyclopedia survives through two editions (1347, 1780s) -
the stemma problem: originals lost, copies of copies, luck and monks. The
outerweb's answer: everything on this shelf is fetched at the source, and
the estate's own corpus is sha256-pinned at birth. Texts should not need
luck to survive. That is the difference between a library and an archive
of survivors.
