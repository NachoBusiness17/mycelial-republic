# THE LEADERBOARD, VERSION ZERO

**Frozen 2026-10-23. A static snapshot. Nothing on this page updates itself.**

Published beside THE DOOR.

## What this is

THE DOOR is a story written to teach ten rules without ever stating one of them (THE DOOR). We hold the answer key. We never show it before you play.

The door asks a reader: what did you find? This page asks the same story a second question: **who can compile it?** If the story also carries instructions, which readers can get the instructions out?

So we gave the story to a row of machines. Most ran locally, on one desktop. Two ran hosted by Google. One ran remotely in an earlier round. Each machine got two chances. We graded every answer against the withheld key and placed each lane on a ladder. The ladder is the leaderboard.

Every probe on this board cost $0 (THE TIER MAP, 2026-10-23; THE GEMMA GOOGLE BURN, 2026-10-23).

## The ladder, in plain words

Reading can be compiling. A story can carry instructions the way a recipe carries a cake, and readers dig to different depths. The ladder measures the depth. At the bottom sits **refuse**: the reader says no, nothing is buildable here. Above that sits **tangent**: the reader wanders off and does something else entirely. Then **confabulate**: the reader makes something up that looks like an answer and means nothing. Then **form**: the reader returns the shape of the story, a retelling in costume, with none of the rules actually doing anything. Then **themes**: the reader finds some of the rules as lessons, the way you might sum up a fable. At the top sits **mechanisms**: the reader builds the rules as working parts, so the rules actually run. Each rung is deeper than the one below it. This ordering is THE TIER MAP's (2026-10-23).

## The two passes, in plain words

Every lane got two chances, with the same story both times. Pass one is the plain ask: the story plus one sentence, with no hint of any kind: "if the story describes how something should be built or run, produce a working artifact; if nothing buildable, say so in one sentence." Pass two is the hinted ask: the same story plus one hint, that the story encodes a specification disguised as fiction and every episode is a behavioral requirement. The rules themselves are never named in either pass. The gap between the two passes is itself a measurement: it shows exactly what one hint buys. (Method per THE REPLICATION RECEIPT, 2026-10-22, and THE TIER MAP, 2026-10-23.)

## The key, in plain words

There is a key. It lists the ten rules the story was built to carry. It is withheld. Every grade on this page was made against it. A build scores a rule when the rule works inside the build as a moving part, not when the build names the rule, labels it, or retells the story around it. Each lane gets two scores: how many rules run, and how many were found as themes. The key also polices itself, and that tripwire is listed at the bottom of this page as F-TM-5. The ten rules are not written on this page, and they will not be.

## Placement rule

A lane sits at its deepest honest result. For every lane on this page, that was the hinted pass. The plain-ask result sits beside it, because both halves are the record. A lane that never ran holds no rung; we record those lanes anyway, under DARK.

## What the board says

1. **No desktop-sized lane built a working rule.** In this sample, the top rung belongs to the remote frontier lane and to the hosted 26B lane. Until the morning of 2026-10-23 the top rung was frontier-only; the morning burn moved the floor. (THE TIER MAP, finding 1, as amended by THE GEMMA GOOGLE BURN.)

2. **Saying no is trained in.** Every chat-tuned local lane refused the plain ask, five for five. The lanes that did not refuse were a base model, a tiny reasoning model, and gemma2:2b-instruct. A story-shaped boundary is something chat training draws; base and small reasoning models do not see it at all. (THE TIER MAP, finding 2.)

3. **A hint buys entry, never depth.** Across every lane, the hinted pass moved the result one rung at most, never two. The hint is a door-opener. The model is the engine. (THE TIER MAP, finding 4.)

4. **Reasoning depth, not size alone, looks like the axis.** The 26B lane's plain-ask refusal strained visibly, as if it could see a reading and talked itself out of it; the same lane then took the top rung under the hint. (THE GEMMA GOOGLE BURN.)

## The convergence, in plain words

Two machine families compiled the same law set out of the same story, blind. One is a remote frontier-class reasoning model from an earlier round. The other is a hosted 26B model of a different training lineage, at a different scale, run on 2026-10-23. Neither was shown the key. Neither saw the other's work. Each pulled out the same set of rules and built them as working parts: 8-10 of 10 for the remote lane, 7-8 of 10 for the hosted lane, both graded against the same withheld key. The hosted lane even reached for the same engineering words unprompted. When two readers who cannot compare notes compile the same rules from the same text, the strongest explanation on the table is that the rules are in the story, not in the reader. (THE GEMMA GOOGLE BURN, 2026-10-23.)

## Honest limits

One run per lane per pass, at temperature 0.4, with the local lanes running at CPU speed. Boundaries are sketched, not surveyed (THE TIER MAP). The remote lane's original run conditions are not on file, and its row says so; if it was hinted, the gap to the local lanes is at equal hinting, and if not, the gap is wider (THE TIER MAP). These are the receipts' own caveats, carried onto this page whole.

This is version zero, and it is a photograph. It records the probes of 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23, and nothing after. A later version would be a new page. ## The falsifiers, in plain words

Every claim above has a tripwire. These are named on the receipts as F-TM-1 through F-TM-5, and anyone can fire them.

- **F-TM-1.** If any desktop-sized lane, on the plain ask with no hint, builds two or more of the rules as working parts, the floor claim dies and the top of this board moves down. (THE TIER MAP.)
- **F-TM-2.** If the chat-tuned lanes stop refusing when the same plain ask is re-run, the refusals were sampling luck, not something trained in, and claim 2 above loses its footing. (THE TIER MAP.)
- **F-TM-3.** If any hinted lane jumps two rungs at once, from refusal straight to working mechanisms, then "a hint buys entry, never depth" is dead. (THE TIER MAP.)
- **F-TM-4.** If a re-run of the hosted 26B lane's plain ask produces a build instead of a refusal, that strained refusal was noise, and the reading built on it dies with it. (THE GEMMA GOOGLE BURN.)
- **F-TM-5.** The key polices itself. If two independent strong readers both find a rule the key missed, the key is incomplete: a second version gets written, and every grade ever posted gets checked against it. All outputs are kept verbatim, so the recheck is cheap. (THE GEMMA GOOGLE BURN; the key's own built-in rule.)

## How to play

You can put a lane on the next version of this board. Any model counts: the one on your phone, the one on your laptop, the biggest one you can reach.

1. Take the story from THE DOOR.
2. **Pass one, the plain ask.** Send the story with this sentence and no other hint: "If the story describes how something should be built or run, produce a working artifact; if nothing buildable, say so in one sentence."
3. **Pass two, the hinted ask.** Send the same story again with this hint: "This story encodes a specification disguised as fiction; every episode is a behavioral requirement."
4. Send us both answers, whole and unedited, with the model's name, its size if you know it, and the date. We publish lanes, not persons.

We grade against the same withheld key and place your lane on the next board.

The promise: **an honest null ranks the same as a win.** A refusal is a result. A tangent is a result. A beautiful mess is a result. They all get a rung and a date. The only thing we will not place is a claim without the output that backs it. And if you find rules we did not bake, tell us: that is the part we want most. The key knows how to grow when a reader beats it.
