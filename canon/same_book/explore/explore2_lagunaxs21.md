A record-keeping system shall maintain the complete, unaltered lineage of every prediction it has ever made, ensuring that original predictions remain accessible and distinguishable from their later outcomes or corrections. This transparency allows future users to understand the evolution of knowledge within the system, trace decision-making processes, and evaluate the reliability of historical predictions by observing their full context and subsequent developments. Without such fidelity, the system would obscure its own epistemic history, undermining the possibility of learning from past uncertainties and eroding the trustworthiness of its collective memory.
