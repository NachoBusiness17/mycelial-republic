Chaos is often not one monster and not pure chance. Three quiet machines intertwine:

1. **Money machine** — capital and foundations quietly steer option sets (what gets funded, staffed, normalized).
2. **Secrets machine** — asymmetric information and kompromat silence careers and shape who can speak.
3. **Fracture machine** — trauma and inverted experience turn people into headlines that cover the structure.

Single-cause explanations (only conspiracy / only incompetence / only "human nature") are high-entropy terminating knots. Interference between charts is signal, not noise.

**Personal rope:** your own unexamined knots make one story feel "obvious." Elias rule — strike own side first so the machines cannot aim you only at the neighbor.

Entropy grounding: multi-chart sampling lowers false precision. Observer charts without collapsing to one frame.
