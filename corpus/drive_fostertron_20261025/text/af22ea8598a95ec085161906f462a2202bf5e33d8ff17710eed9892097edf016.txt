Overview & Purpose

This kit provides citizen journalists, researchers, and concerned members of the public with a replicable, source-driven methodology to investigate claims surrounding JetBlue Airways' use of dynamic pricing — particularly allegations of "surveillance pricing" or personalized pricing based on individual consumer data and browsing behavior.
The July 1, 2026 X thread (linked above) resurfaced concerns originally ignited by a now-deleted April 18, 2026 JetBlue customer service reply. In that exchange, a traveler reported a $230 fare increase overnight while attempting to book travel for a funeral. The official @JetBlue account reportedly suggested clearing cache/cookies or using incognito mode. The post was deleted; JetBlue stated it was an employee error and that pricing is based solely on real-time availability and demand.
Important Distinction: Traditional dynamic pricing (widely used across airlines) adjusts fares based on aggregate factors like remaining inventory, time-to-departure, forecasted demand, and competitor pricing. "Surveillance" or personalized pricing would use individual-level data (cookies, device fingerprint, browsing history, inferred urgency or willingness-to-pay) to show different prices to different users for the same flight at the same moment. The latter raises significant transparency, privacy, and fairness questions.
This framework is designed to be evidence-gated. All findings should be clearly labeled as SOURCED, INFERRED, or SPECULATIVE. Multiple independent sources are required before drawing conclusions.

Investigation Framework
Follow these phases sequentially. Document every step with timestamps, sources, and evidence labels. Prioritize primary sources (filings, official statements, raw data) over secondary reporting.

1
Map the Public Record
Archive all X threads, screenshots, and replies using archive.today, wayback.archive.org, or webrecorder.io
Collect every media article and official JetBlue statement with publication dates
Search X Advanced for complaints using keywords: "JetBlue" + ("price jumped" OR "fare increased" OR "cookies" OR "incognito") since:2026-04-01
Document JetBlue's privacy policy (current + archived versions)

2
Corporate Disclosures
Review JetBlue 10-K / 10-Q filings (EDGAR) for "revenue management", "dynamic pricing", "PROS", "pricing algorithm", "ancillary revenue"
Transcribe or search earnings call transcripts (Seeking Alpha, investor site) for CEO/CFO comments on pricing strategy
Analyze JetBlue investor presentations and annual reports for revenue management technology disclosures
Compare with competitor disclosures (Delta, United, American) for industry norms

3
Regulatory & Legal Track
Obtain full text of congressional letter (Casar/Gallego) and any JetBlue response
Monitor Phillips v. JetBlue (and consolidated "In re JetBlue Surveillance Pricing Litigation") docket via PACER or Justia
Search DOT Airline Consumer Protection complaint database and BTS data for patterns
Review any proposed legislation (Casar Stop AI Price Gouging Act, Gallego One Fair Price Act)

4
Empirical Data Collection
Develop price-tracking protocol: Same route/dates, multiple browsers (incognito vs logged-in), different devices, time-stamped screenshots
Request or scrape public fare data via BTS DB1B (origin-destination survey) and compare volatility
Aggregate consumer complaints from Reddit, Trustpilot, BBB, and DOT database around fare volatility
Document any patterns during high-emotion periods (holidays, emergencies, bereavement travel)

5
Synthesize & Report

Evidence Log: Maintain running table of all findings with source, date, claim type (SOURCED/INFERRED/SPECULATIVE), and confidence.
Red Flags to Flag: Inconsistent statements, rapid price changes uncorrelated with obvious demand signals, lack of clear consumer disclosure, third-party data sharing without consent language.
Output Formats: Thread series, public report PDF, data visualization (fare curves), or submission to lawmakers / journalists.

Core Questions to Answer

Does JetBlue (or its revenue management provider PROS) use individual consumer behavioral data to set or adjust fares in real time?
What specific data elements are collected during the booking flow, and with whom are they shared?
How does JetBlue define and distinguish "standard dynamic pricing" from "surveillance pricing" in internal policy and public communications?
Are there observable patterns of disproportionate price increases during consumer "tough times" (bereavement, medical, job loss, natural disasters)?
Has JetBlue made material changes to its website tracking, privacy disclosures, or pricing communication since April 2026?
How do JetBlue's practices compare to peers, and what is the current regulatory posture of DOT / FTC on personalized airline pricing?

Red Flags & Indicators of Concern

Inconsistent Statements
Official denials that contradict the plain language of deleted customer service replies or internal vendor relationships.
Lack of Auditability
No consumer-facing explanation of why a specific price was shown or changed. No effective way to verify "real-time availability" claims.
High-Urgency Exploitation
Documented or alleged sharp increases coinciding with documented personal hardship (funerals, medical travel, evacuations) without clear bereavement or flexible policies.
