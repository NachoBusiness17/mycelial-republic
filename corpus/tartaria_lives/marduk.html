The new boss. The usurper. The bright one who took the throne after the sky stopped being still.

This page is the library section.

If you need the full argument for Marduk/Merodach/Jupiter in the Saturn polar-lock weave, it is here.

Who is Marduk?

Marduk is the patron god of Babylon, later identified with the planet Jupiter. His Sumerian writing is Amar-Utu — "calf of the sun/solar calf". Hebrew calls him Merodach. greeks folded him into Zeus. The Hut-Dwelling-romans™ into Jupiter.

Later priests even called other gods "aspects of Marduk", as if the new king had absorbed the whole old cabin et.

He is not a copy of Enlil.
He is not a copy of Enki.
He is a composite victor who borrows both of their powers to legitimize a new hierarchy.

In the Enuma Elish he is the son of Ea/Enki and Damkina. His function is not "be Enki". His function is to replace the old stationary high god [read: Anu/El/Kronos/Saturn] with a moving, storming, word-of-power solar-planetary order.

The old dark-eye is demoted or slain. The bright storm-god takes the throne.

That is the whole job description.

The standard questions:

Is Marduk Jupiter?

Yes, in the astronomical system the Akkadians and later Chaldeans built into the Tower of the Seven Spheres. Jupiter is the bright usurper on the same axial current, further back than the inner wanderers, massive enough to torque the lock.

Is Marduk the Sun?

Not originally. He becomes solarized.

The name Amar-Utu already leans toward the sun-god Utu. After the flip, Jupiter and the new bright Sun get braided together in ritual, the same way Zeus, Horus-of-the-Horizon, and Sol Invictus get braided. The myth is the handover. The astronomy is the excuse.

Is he Enlil or Enki?

No. Enki is his father in the epic. Enlil is the older storm-sovereign Marduk replaces as head of the council. Marduk borrows Enki's cunning and Enlil's command so the new regime looks legal.

Is Tiamat Saturn?

No. Tiamat is the waters above [read: the plasma sheath, the luminous cocoon, the chaotic mother-ocean that wrapped the Saturn–Earth system].

When the lock fails she becomes the toroid-beast. Marduks' winds and net are the storm that splits the sheath. Half of her body is "..put up to roof the sky..." That is the firmament story after the glow collapsed.

Is this Saturn worship / Satan?

No. That is the smear that comes after. Kronos/Saturn was the Golden Age father. Later theology flipped the fallen polar sovereign into a devil so the new solar churches could own the calendar.

Marduk is the other half of that smear, he's the son who kills the father and calls it creation.

Was El just a generic word for “god”?

Not at the start. In the oldest Canaanite and Phoenician usage, El is the proper name of the supreme high god [read: father of the pantheon, head of the council, the one whose word is final]. Only later, when the post-flip solar hierarchy needed managing, did el slacken into a generic, like greek 'theos' or latin 'deus'.

El is one of the oldest surviving labels for the pre-flip high god. [Beth'el, House of Saturn]

The migrant-greeks gave him a new accent and called him Kronos.

From El to EL-lites, indeed.

The physics, in one block

The coupling was a Birkeland current filament [read: a braided plasma trunk from Earth's old north ~ Hudson Bay ~ to Saturn's south pole].

The land-bulge faced that pole.

The pull was not a separate gravity trick. It was one coherent field. The filament was the spinal column, the graft, Jacob's Ladder. The sheath was the glowing skin [read: ionized hydrogen, helium, trace water] purple twilight on the inside, a shield from the outside.

The seven wanderers sat in harmonic nodes of that current.

The beasts on the reliefs [read: lions, griffins, snakes, scorpions, bulls] are the toroid instabilities at the base of the column, later fossilized into the new zodiac.

Jupiter/Marduk is the torque.

His mass and motion on the same axis eventually destabilized the tether.

The crust slipped and wandered. It passed through transitional positions, including the ~104° African pole that Ethical Skeptic maps. Then it locked on Hudson Bay for the last stable Golden Age frame.

Then it flipped to today's Arctic.

That single failure-and-slip explains:

  • the 15–18° east-of-true-north skew on hundreds of pre-1000 CE sites, still converging on Hudson Bay as a ghost pole
  • monument clustering in the 30–60° band after Eurasia tore and slid
  • copper and oil stacked in the Hudson Bay socket.. a broken current, not a coincidence
  • black mat, flash-freeze, desertification, red-flora ghosts
  • why the official 'ice age ™ is asked to do work the cave walls never painted
  • why blue arrives late in language
  • pre-Moon myths
  • the worldwide memory of a sky that once stood still
  • The Firmament, 'Waters-Above' in Genisis

The official timeline calls it 'gradual change over millions of years'. Uniformitarianism.

The stones, the myths, and the biology say otherwise. The sky didn't just move. The world turned.

Why Saturn?

It was the closest brown-dwarf 'sun' captured on a transient galactic filament. Earth had no special role in a galactic design document. It was neighborhood timing. A brief alignment delivered the Golden Age field. Then the plug got yanked, and the glow turned to storm.

The Enuma Elish

The Enuma Elish is not Genesis. It is a press release.

'..When on high the heavens had not been nmed…'

[synopsis] ~Apsu and Tiamat mix their waters. The old gods are noisy. Ea kills Apsu. Tiamat arms a monster-host [read: hydra, dragon, scorpion-man, bull, fish-man] and gives Qingu the Tablet of Destinies.

The old high ones cannot face her. Marduk bargains..".. if I fight, my word fixes fate instead of yours..."

They crown him. He nets her, drives winds down her throat, splits her like a dried fish. Half roofs the sky. He stations the gods, builds Babylon, makes man from rebel blood to do the gods’ labor.

Fifty names are hung on him so no old title is left unused. ~

That is not "how the world began".
That is how the new regime tells the story of the snap.

  • Tiamat's monster-brood = the toroid beasts of the dying column
  • The winds = the storm after the sheath ruptured
  • Splitting her to make firmament and earth = waters-above falling, sky going transparent, ground rearranged
  • Stealing the Tablet of Destinies = seizing the right to name the new sky
  • Fifty names = every old office folded into the victor

~On the fourth of Nisan, while the epic was recited, they covered the faces of Anu and Enlil. The old kings were ritually hidden so the new one could speak.~

That is the tell.

Together they describe the three functional layers of the old polar configuration, the fixed source, the connecting current, and the ordered biosphere that thrived under it.

Later kings and knowledge-bringers claimed legitimacy by 'receiving the me' from this triad, a clear post-flip attempt to re-anchor authority in the lost order.

The family tree, without the fog

Zeus's father was called El in older traditions.

El is the stationary father who rules from the zenith. The migrant-greeks syncretized him with Kronos [Saturn, ruler of the Golden Age] then had Zeus overthrow him. [and later start 'eating his children' [ie: the 7-wanderers]

The Bible uses El for the high god long before the name is merged into a singular Yahweh.

Same handover. Different accent.

Osiris -> Horus of the Horizon [Harakhty] is the Egyptian twin.
Kronos -> Zeus is the greek twin.
Anu ->Marduk is the Babylonian twin.

The gods did not just evolve in stories. Something in the actual sky changed, and the myths were adjusted to match. The original meanings did not disappear. They were overwritten.

The Tower of the Seven Spheres is not 'Babel' first

The Akkadians [read-source: Anderson's "..early Chaldeans", Turanian/Tartarian of the Armenian mountains] were star-worshippers who built planetary temples.

Merodach belongs in that system.

The seven-stage tower was a color-coded instrument:

  1. Saturn — black, bitumen
  2. Jupiter — orange
  3. Mars — blood-red
  4. Sun — gold
  5. Venus — pale yellow
  6. Mercury — blue, vitrified
  7. Moon — silver

Jupiter has a stage because Jupiter was already in the choir.

After the flip, that stage becomes the victor's seat. The later 'Tower of Babel' sermon is what you get when a seven-sphere observatory is rewritten as a pride story.

The hardware was a planetary temple.

The paperwork became a warning.

Akkadian seals carrying Merodach and celestial glyphs went to 'remote regions'.

The statue-and-prophecy cycle around Nebuchadnezzar I reads as political astrology. Jupiter's lord "predicted" after the fact.

That is how you legitimize a king with a stolen sky.

Mainstream rebranding of Chaldeans as a late Semitic southern people obscures the Akkadian root the older books still printed. Whether one accepts Anderson's Tartarian frame or not, the star-system is the same, with seven spheres, and Marduk on Jupiter, El/Saturn as the old high name.

Akkadian/Tartarian winged lions.
Greek Potnia Theron grasping beasts.
Marduk taming Tiamat as griffin/dragon/chaos-host.

These are not zoo decorations.

They are the column's toroids turned to stone after the current died [read: bulls, scorpions, panthers, lions, snakes, dragons, Griffins]. The hero who "tames" them is the knowledge-bringer/beast-tamer of the storm.

So, Gilgamesh, Marduk, Herakles, the falcon king etc.

Same pose, different passport.

What Marduk is not

He is not the Golden Age father.
He is not the plasma column.

He is not a space-daddy wih a boarding pass and a gold-fetish

He is NOT 'Quranos.

He is the mythic name for the body whose torque ended the lock, then the political name for the priesthood that explained the new moving sky as a coronation.

Physics first. Cult second.

The cult is what you do when the 'I Am' goes silent and someone has to say who won.

One sentence to keep

Marduk is Jupiter wearing Enki's bloodline and Enlil's crown, standing on Tiamat's split body, telling the world that the still sky is dead and the storm is king.

Same file, other accents are, Zues v. Kronos, Horus of The Horizon after Osiris. The office changes, the lock does not come back.

The flip was the effect.
