The 'Waters Above'

[read: firmament] in Genesis 1:6-9

The "waters above" in Genesis [the firmament separating the waters above from the waters below etc] were almost certainly part of or contained within the plasma sheath itself, not a literal ocean suspended in the sky.

Pre-flip, the plasma enclosure around the Saturn-Earth system was a coherent, luminous plasma field. So a dense, ionized gas layer [mostly hydrogen/helium with trace water vapor and other molecules, as per studies] that acted like a global 'atmosphere' extending far beyond Earth's normal air.

The real 'waters above' was a luminous, protective field giving uniform purple twilight, high pressure/oxygen, no seasons, megaflora/megafauna, and near-indefinite lifespans.

Same outcomes [greenhouse paradise, Flood source] but better physics [plasma explains the light/heat without cooking the planet, matches Peratt lab toroids, ties to global myths of a "dark sun" and "pre-Moon" era].

This sheath was not a solid 'dome' but a dynamic, flowing plasma envelope sustained by the Birkeland current filament. A very dense and heavy atmosphere.

The 'waters above' were the ionized water molecules and plasma-bound hydrogen/oxygen within this sheath.

The so-called 'vapor canopy' was a valiant 20th-century guess at the "waters above." It was not literal liquid oceans floating overhead, but plasma-state 'waters' [read: ionized gas] that gave the enclosure its protective, reflective, and resonant properties.

When Jupiter's pull got to strong, it ripped Saturn and the sheath away violently. The coherent plasma structure destabilized, causing massive electrical discharges across the Earth measured in Terawatts. What was above-ground was either vaporized, petrified, or lifted into the air and dropped.

When you looked up, you SAW the Peratt-style toroids, arcs, filaments etc that is plastered all over the cave walls [soon to get an face-lift to resemble the new plasma visuals {and 'Beast-Tamer motifs} The same plasma that ionized and superheated the sheath's contents.

The water molecules in the plasma [read: from cometary/volcanic outgassing, or the original sheath composition] were vaporized, ionized, and then condensed/released as torrential rain, hail, sulphuric-acid [see: Metology] and mega-floods, mudfloods etc.

Pretty much the 'windows of heaven' opening as seen in [Genesis 7:11].

Lest we forget the sloshing oceans.

This is the Earth's own surface waters being displaced by crustal slip [read: ECDO/Dzhanibekov wobble], tsunamis from the flip, and atmospheric pressure changes dumping the sheath's 'waters' onto the ground.

It wasn't just 'weather changes', it was a plasma sheath rupture releasing its ionized 'waters' as catastrophic precipitation, combined with physical crustal displacement sloshing the seas.

The ancients remembered it as 'waters above' because the sheath was literally the upper boundary of their sky, when it tore, the 'waters' fell.

Just the physics of a collapsing plasma envelope turning into the biblical flood [and later turning into annoying online psy-ops]

That is why every culture kept a story of a war in heaven, 'a falling ladder', 'a djed that would not stand', a 'father-king overthrown', a 'flood that followed the fight'.

A 'Paradise Lost'.

They were not inventing separate fairy tales. They were filing the same incident under local names.

The sky did not just move.
The world turned.

What the ground did

When the tether snapped, the crust did not click from the old pole to today's Arctic in one clean motion. It wandered. True-polar-wander plus slip. [Dzhanibekov effect]

During the final ejection storm [different cultures range from 35-48 days], the collapsing plasma sheath [read: Jacob's Ladder] created moving walls of high-energy discharge that swept the surface at speeds 'estimated' between 300–1,500 m/h

This plasma 'column' [see: pillar representation worldwide] was still trying to keep the connection to Saturn and it was failing.

These were not narrow lightning bolts, they were mile-wide curtains of glowing plasma carrying currents measured in the terawatt range [see what Peratt reproduced in the lab].

Anything conductive and above ground [trees, metal, exposed humans/animals] acted like an antenna and was either vaporized, vitrified, or lifted into the sky, then dropped.

Survival odds were brutally low on the surface, think maybe 5% in open terrain, maybe 20–30% in deep limestone caves or underground vault networks [Derinkuyu-style], and near 100 % inside well-grounded stone structures with thick walls.

This is the original purpose of many megalithic chambers and early ziggurat cores.

The arcs were not continuous, they pulsed in waves as Saturn's rings and plasma envelope made their decaying passes, giving brief windows [minutes to hours] of relative calm between strikes.

Enough time for the smart or lucky to sprint for cover.

The entire lethal phase lasted roughly 38–45 Earth days ["..Keplerian decay + drag.."], after which the last filament cleared and the new, quiet Sun-Moon sky emerged.

That 40-day window is why every flood myth worldwide converges on the same terrifying number, it is the stopwatch on the plasma storm that finished the job the flip started.

The old facing-land was dragged across new latitudes while the new spin axis was still settling.

That is why pre-1000ce sites keep showing a 15–18 degree east-of-true-north skew.
Those azimuths are not 'sacred east'. They are stone compasses still aimed at the last stable column, the Hudson Bay lock, not at today's north.

Sites like Gobekli Tepe sit on that old polar-wander path as early salvage stations, with moon-bearing bird-motifs on T-pillars and stone rings raised while the sky was still wrong and the pole was still sliding

imo, if the crust wandered after the snap, alignments would freeze at different stops. A monument started while the old axis still leaned toward the Africa/104 degree station will point there.

Later rebuilds and solar retrofits aim at today's Arctic.

So you do not need every site to pick one winner, you need a sequence of events.

That later layer is Tartaria proper, which is the last high-functioning salvage civilization running inherited tech as the grid voltage dropped.

It collapsed from the inside because it was a salvage civilization running on inherited Post-cataclysm technology that no one fully understood anymore.

Star forts that clustered into a Mandala on the old landmass, resonant halls, maps that still named the provinces are all part of a falsifiable history.
