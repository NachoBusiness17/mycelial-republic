Talbott, Thornhill, Cardona, Peratt, Maxwell, McLachlan, the Electric Universe lane [read: polar Saturn, plasma column, enclosure, purple glow, myth as sky-memory]. That map was already on the table when this work popped its head up to calibrate.

They built the sky-room. This room is the handshake and the line. This model puts the crime scene on the ground where you can go measure it.

One event. Many flashlights

This page is the library room for credit and for separation.

Researching the same cosmic cataclysm produces overlap. Off-north alignments, plasma petroglyphs, pre-Moon statements, red-flora ghosts. That is not theft. That is what happens when more than one honest researcher stares at the same broken pole.

This library does not pretend those names were never read.
It also does not rent their church.

Walk The Sky Before the Flip first.

💡
Head down on architecture, regalia, plants, alignments, books. Form the claim. Then look up. Sometimes someone already noticed it. Sometimes the piece is new. Credit the first. Keep the shovel.

What they actually laid down

The Electric Universe/Saturnist line did the hard first work.. it refused to treat world-myth as children's stories and refused to treat rock art as doodles.

Immanuel Velikovsky broke the ice.

Close planets, living memory, catastrophe in human time. Wrong overall clock, useful pieces. Cardona later said it plainly,'Velikovsky had details right and the arrangement wrong'.

David Talbott [The Saturn Myth, 1980]

This is the mythic blueprint.

Saturn as the stationary polar "sun". A Golden Age of a still zenith. Enclosure. Golden Age father (Kronos/Shani). Motifs as eyewitness, not invention. A crescent on the band, a cosmic mountain/stair between worlds.

In his stacked model the lineup is tight and specific.. Saturn hiding Jupiter, Venus in the face of Saturn, Mars in the face of Venus, Earth axially locked so the whole circus sits fixed in the north.

Change that order, he said, and the argument unravels.

Dwardu Cardona

(God Star and the rest) gave the body a biography with a proto-Saturn as a dim stellar host [brown-dwarf class] Earth inside a glowing envelope, the purple dawn. Flare, rearrangement, Venus as a late birth in some tellings.

Myth first, then geology as supporting wreckage.

Wallace Thornhill

He gave the physics vocabulary the myths were missing. Electric Universe as discharge physics. Toroids, Z-pinch, lab-replicable figures. Sky as electrical, not a gravity-only ballet. Birkeland currents, sheaths, stars as electrical objects. Lab plasma.

With Talbott he kept the sky-show honest as electric. He did not write the land-bulge primer. The lane stayed "sky phenom" [a repeating electrical circus] more than one divorce with a forwarding address on the continents.

Anthony Peratt

He put the petroglyphs in a lab.

Intense plasma columns produce the same 'squatter man'. stacked toroids, and stick-figures the rocks remember.

That is not a vibe. That is a replicable instability.

This model uses it for the beasts and the column. It does not need Peratt to have mapped Hudson Bay.

Jordan Maxwell

He was the infamous symbol-hound.

Shared symbols and linguistic roots across later religions. He saw the surface connections. He stopped before the sky-event that forced the rewrite.

He did not name the still dark-sun as the reason the rewrite was needed. Shani as the slow dark one is the Hindu fossil he walked past.

Troy McLachlan

[and the "Saturn death-cult" internet] Symbolic reconstruction plus a "the cult is still running" layer.

Useful as drama. Not required here.

He took Talbott's inversion [fallen father becomes feared lord] and ran it into a living occult continuity. Useful as trauma branding. Not required as a bloodline that still runs the banks.

This weave treats the smear as coping, not as a board of directors.

Ev Cochrane

[and the Thunderbolts circle]

[They] kept the chronicle with archetypes first, Saturn theory as a work in progress.

They set it. This researcher kept walking the path.

💡
Respect the archive. Do not pretend it already included a spectrometer on a ziggurat stair.

They set the foundation.. The stationary polar Saturn, plasma enclosure, catastrophic breakup, symbols as eyewitness, Peratt-phase chaos in stone.

That is enough to owe them a bow.

Where they stopped

Most of that lane treats Earth's crust as a passive stage.
Sky-show. Myth. Petroglyph. Then the file closes.

They do not have to explain:

  • why landmasses cluster the way they do
  • why the oldest salvage belt sits 30–60°N
  • why pre-1000 CE monuments obsessively sit 15–18° east of today’s north
  • why Tartaria is on the maps after the wreck
  • why first floors are buried
  • why the Age-Of-Ice™ is doing four jobs
  • why faculty show up after the flood with the same kit

Without a landmass-facing lock and a flip, Saturn stays "up there".
With the primer on the ground, the myth is a crime scene you can measure.

Thornhill's arc is often a repeating electrical circus [close encounters, nova weather] more thunderstorm than one divorce.

This model is one lock, one yank, one salvage.

Maxwell saw religions as rewritten older systems. The missing piece is the stationary dark sun that had to be reframed after the stack failed [Aten as a short experiment, Shani as the slow-goer who was not slow when he sat still].

McLachlan's occult-elite continuity is not load-bearing here. Trauma can invert a lost king into a devil without a single bloodline holding the keys.

Fossils first. Cabals optional.

Their axial config vs this one

Say it without fog.

They had a sky that stood still.
They did not all have the same moon story.
They did not put the facing-landmass on the table as the primer.
That is the split.

Axial config and the moon (the split questions)

Their sky: polar Saturn, column, enclosure. Crust as audience.

This sky: same column, but the land-bulge faces Saturn. Hudson Bay is the socket. Continents under the lock. After Jupiter's torque the crust slips and wanders [Africa lean as a stop], then Arctic north.

The 15–18° skew is the fingerprint of the last stable lock, not decoration.

The moon

This model treats "time before the moon" as a class of statement, not poetry.

Proselenes, Dogon, Hopi/Zulu strands, the crescent as "dark-eye" first and later relabeled. Capture after the snap does two jobs [restabilize the wobble, write tides and months onto a world that had a fixed polar light].

EU/Saturn-myth work notices pre-Moon lore. It does not have to make the moon a newcomer-stabilizer on a timeline that also produces Tartaria, buried grade, and an Ice Age caption.

That file [moon] will get its own room. Until then it lives on The Ice Age.

Why the overlap is honest

Off-north temples. Plasma petroglyphs. Pre-moon talk. A dark polar sun. A father overthrown by a storm-heir. Red plants that look like they miss a different lamp.

If two people stare at that pile, they will rhyme.

Cardona already wrote at length about spectrum and biology under a different sun so if a sentence here meets a sentence there, that is the same wound, not a stolen paragraph.

The rule on this site stays the one already posted.. head down in the stones, then pop up to calibrate. Credit on first contact. No requirement to tattoo the whole EU roster on every new fossil.

Short and Sweet

Classic stack (Talbott, Cardona, much of the EU line).

What hangs in the north is a tight shish-kebab of planets.. Saturn as the great disk, Venus as the bright pupil in its face, Mars as the inner dark, Jupiter often hidden behind.

The "column" is the cosmic mountain or stair, and in Thornhill's telling Mars is the body that slides up and down that axis between Saturn and Earth. Earth's job is simply to sit still on that shared axis so the circus does not move.

Breakup is electrical crisis and rearrangement when proto-Saturn meets the Sun's domain. The Moon is optional or late depending on the author [not the load-bearing clock].

Proof lives in myth, glyph, and the plasma lab.

This Model~

What hangs in the north is Saturn as the polar brown-dwarf cathode, with the seven wanderers locked in the harmonic nodes of one Birkeland trunk [not Venus-in-Saturn as the only legal picture].

The column is a single braided filament, Jacob's Ladder, socketed at the old north, Hudson Bay as Saturn's south-pole touchdown. Earth's job is a facing-land bulge, a Pangea-like mass locked toward that dark sun; the continents are part of the machine, not a stage under a sky-show.

Breakup is Jupiter's torque snapping the tether, the sheath falling, the crust slipping and wandering [Africa lean, Hudson Bay ghost, then today's Arctic). The Moon is a latecomer, captured after the yank, the pin that stops the next flip and starts the tides.

Proof is still myth, glyph, and lab [plus azimuths, the mineral socket, red-flora ghosts, and the indoor fossils {pillars, chandeliers, crowns lingams etc].

Same still sky. Different machine on the ground.

Standard questions

Did they have a moon as newcomer?

Some threads in the Saturnist/EU world treat the present moon as late or secondary. It is not their universal load-bearing axiom the way it is here. Here the moon is the pin that stops the next flip and starts tides.

Did they use Birkeland currents?

Thornhill and Peratt did, yes, as the physics of columns and glyphs. Talbott was mythic column first.

This weave is one trunk, one socket, one snap.

Is this just Thunderbolts with Tartar-Sauce™?

No. Thunderbolts kept the sky electric. This page keeps that and adds the torn map, the salvage epoch, and the fossils you can walk into.

Is McLachlan the same theory?

Same father. Different last chapter. He wants a cult that never died. This site wants a scar you can survey.

The test

If a Saturn Config claim cannot touch a map, a skew, a pigment, a building, a tablet, or a date-problem, it stays in their folder. If the stones aim somewhere other than the claimed lock, this lock fails too.

If "the cult still runs the world" is doing the work that Hudson Bay and the caves should do, the square peg is in.

The theory does not get to eat Peratt's pictures or Talbott's motifs. It also does not get to hide behind them.

What this is not

This is not a hit piece on the dead and the living who did the first mapping.
This is not 'I invented Saturn overhead', Talbott wrote that book.
This is not a claim Cardona never mentioned plants or light. He did. Cross-over is allowed.
This is not Velikovsky's Venus-comet timetable wearing a new Tartarian hat.
This is not "EU is a cult, therefore ignore Peratt’s lab".
This is not permission to skip citing them and then cry originality.
This is not a demand that their stacked Venus–Mars pupil is false in every frame, only that it is not this model's primary machine.
This is not a death-cult unmasking of every lodge on Earth.

This is not Thunderbolts-as-church with a mudflood sticker.
This is not permission to skip their books. They are on the shelf.
This is not a claim that every overlapping conclusion is theft. Same scars. Same event.

This library makes the 'New-Moon' a timeline hinge and will file it as its own room.

This is not the last word. It is the ground floor they did not pour.
