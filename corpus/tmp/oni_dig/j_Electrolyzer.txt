The **Electrolyzer** is a machine that uses electricity to split [Water](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Water "Water") into its constituent [Oxygen](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Oxygen "Oxygen") and [Hydrogen](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Hydrogen "Hydrogen").

Both outputs have a minimum temperature of 70°C 158°F and will be hotter if the inputs were hotter.

## Usage

Electrolyzers require more planning than other oxygen sources as not only do they require a water supply via [pipes](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Liquid_Pipe "Liquid Pipe") and [pumps](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Liquid_Pump "Liquid Pump"), but the hydrogen must also be handled. Another aspect is the temperature of the oxygen being created. The gas outputs are always 70°C 158°F or hotter, so it is advised to cool the oxygen before using it in the core of the colony, or to cool the colony itself.

Electrolyzers may be used to meet a colony's Oxygen needs for as long as there is a source of water. The produced hydrogen will rise up and can be collected at ceilings using a [Gas Pump](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas_Pump "Gas Pump"). Alternately the electrolysis can be performed in a small air-tight room and the gases pumped out before the pressure rises above the electrolyzer's operational limit. The hydrogen can then be separated from the oxygen using a [Gas Filter](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas_Filter "Gas Filter") and directed to a [Hydrogen Generator](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Hydrogen_Generator "Hydrogen Generator"), producing a small amount of power. The Oxygen can be distributed within the colony via [Gas Vents](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas_Vent "Gas Vent") or sent to other uses such as an [Atmo Suit Dock](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Atmo_Suit_Dock "Atmo Suit Dock"), [Oxylite Refinery](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Oxylite_Refinery "Oxylite Refinery"), or a [Telescope](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Telescope "Telescope"). Multiple vents can be used, instead of a single vent, to ensure that there is always a place for the Oxygen to go as an individual vent will stop releasing oxygen as soon the vent's environment has reached its operational the pressure limit. A [Geyser](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Geyser "Geyser") may be used to provide water for an Electrolyzer-based oxygen supply.

To supply oxygen for 1 duplicant requires 112.5 g/s, or 67.5 kg/cycle of water used (assuming the electrolyzer works at 100% capacity).

### [Heat](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Guide/Temperature_Management "Guide/Temperature Management") economy

Consider cooling water as little as possible or even pre-heat it before delivering into electrolyzers. The input water has a higher specific heat capacity than the gases produced, which makes gases easier to cool down afterwards.

It gets better – assuming that the base is at 0°C 32°F, 1 kg of mixed oxygen and hydrogen produced at 70°C 158°F carries as much heat as 1 kg of water at 19.45°C 67.01°F, which means that, when using water above 19.45°C 67.01°F, the electrolysis becomes net heat negative, removing $4 . 1 7 9 \textrm{ } \text{kDTU}/°\text{C}/\text{s}$ (1 kg water per second $\times$ the specific heat of [water](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Water "Water")) per degree centigrade above that), up to 70°C 158°F when the outputs also begin to rise in temperature and the efficiency drops to around 3 kDTU/°C. With near-boiling 96°C 204.8°F water, the heat removed is ~288 kDTU/s, which is comparable to 3.6 [AETNs](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Anti_Entropy_Thermo-Nullifier "Anti Entropy Thermo-Nullifier").

Note, that electrolyzer will not process water but will instead release [steam](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Steam "Steam") into the environment if the input is too hot.

As a corollary, feeding water colder than 19.45°C 67.01°F will _create_ heat.

That said, the 70°C 158°F output is sure to cook any farm relying on a steady temperature of 30°C 86°F or lower without any additional heat transfer/deletion. For each kilogram of water input, the electrolyzer outputs mass with a heat capacity of ~1.161$\frac{\text{DTU}}{\text{g} ^{\circ} \text{C}}$0.65$\frac{\text{DTU}}{\text{g} ^{\circ} \text{F}}$. The hot hydrogen typically (1) remains in the system, (2) is deleted along with its heat by hydrogen generator(s), or (3) is later cooled by other means. Given that a duplicant consumes 100 g/s oxygen, cooling the total output of an electrolyzer requires $1 1 6 . 1 \textrm{ } \text{DTU}/°\text{C}/\text{s}$ per duplicant, or, for the +40°C+72°F drop from 70°C 158°F to 30°C 86°F, 4.64 kDTU/s. This is slightly less heat than is deleted by a single fertilized [Wheezewort](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Wheezewort "Wheezewort") in oxygen (~5 kDTU/s).

If we consider cooling only the oxygen, the heat change is $1 0 0 . 5 \textrm{ } \text{DTU}/°\text{C}/\text{s}$ per duplicant, or for the same +40°C+72°F temperature change, ~4 kDTU/s. In contrast, cooling the corresponding input water (112.6 g) for the same temperature difference would require ~18.8 kDTU/s, ~4.7$\times$ more. In other words, cooling only the oxygen reduces the cooling needed by ~78.6%.

## Mechanics

The Electrolyzer has a maximum pressure limit of 1.8 kg. It checks the pressure of any [Gases](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas "Gas") on the four tiles it occupies; [Liquids](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Liquid "Liquid") are not counted toward this limit.

## Tips

*   The Electrolyzer is an [Algae](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Algae "Algae")-free way of producing oxygen and should be researched before you run out of algae.
*   Filling the Electrolyzer with anything other than [Water](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Water "Water") will cause damage to it. This limitation includes [Polluted Water](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Polluted_Water "Polluted Water").
*   The Electrolyzer has a 10 kg internal reserve, which lasts for 10 seconds under ideal conditions.
*   The Electrolyzer will not remove [Food Poisoning](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Food_Poisoning "Food Poisoning") or [Slimelung](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Slimelung "Slimelung") germs within the water, and will output the germs with the Oxygen. Both germs will gradually die out in Oxygen.
*   Using [High Pressure Gas Vents](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/High_Pressure_Gas_Vent "High Pressure Gas Vent") will more efficiently spread oxygen in the base. To avoid [Popped Eardrums](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Popped_Eardrums "Popped Eardrums"), use automation to shut off the vent [below 4kg](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Atmospheric_Pressure#:~:text=Additionally,_if_the_Atmospheric_Pressure,lasts_for_half_a_cycle. "Atmospheric Pressure").
*   The Electrolyzer can be combined with the [Anti Entropy Thermo-Nullifier](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Anti_Entropy_Thermo-Nullifier "Anti Entropy Thermo-Nullifier") to cool down its output for very little cost in [Hydrogen](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Hydrogen "Hydrogen"). In addition, the AETN it can prevent the electrolyzer from overheating if the two are close enough.

## Bugs

Placing the Electrolyzer with two [Gas Pumps](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas_Pump "Gas Pump") on the left and right sides of a $6 \times 2$ sealed room will result in significant amount of hydrogen disappearing, effectively producing only 75 g/s of hydrogen (about 66% of what it should be). The workaround is to make the room 3 tiles high (with pumps and electrolyzer placed on the ground).

## Trivia

The tooltip is a reference to the movie "Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs", in which a machine is invented that can turn water into food.
