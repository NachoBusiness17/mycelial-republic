The **Gas Pump** is one of the [buildings](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Building "Building") required for atmosphere management. It draws in [Gas](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas "Gas") from the tiles that it occupies with a rate of up to 500g/s and runs it through the connected [Gas Pipes](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas_Pipe "Gas Pipe"). Gas Pipes can transfer up to 1000 g of gas, so to fully utilize the Gas Pipes, two Gas Pumps are necessary.

## Tips

*   Gas flow always goes from green to white.
*   The Gas Pump will continue to consume the same amount of power while pumping less, unless they are temporarily disabled, so putting a [Atmo Sensor](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Atmo_Sensor "Atmo Sensor") set to "above 500" nearby will increase their power efficiency.
*   When submerged in a mixed gas, the Gas Pump can create gas packets over 500g, though the total rate will be still limited to 500g/s.
*   The Gas Pump's maximum drawing rate is half that of the maximum generation rate of an [Electrolyzer](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Electrolyzer "Electrolyzer"), so to handle all the generated gas of one Electrolyzer you need two Gas Pumps.
*   The Gas Pump has a base overheat temperature of 75°C 167°F and will overheat in hot gases. Choose [Gold Amalgam](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gold_Amalgam "Gold Amalgam") or [Steel](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Steel "Steel") as the building material according to the environment temperature.
*   Because of the relatively low throughput of the Gas Pump compared to the [Liquid Pump](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Liquid_Pump "Liquid Pump") (20x higher throughput) and the [Conveyor Loader](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Conveyor_Loader "Conveyor Loader") (40x higher throughput), it might be preferable to condense the gas into Liquid or Solid before transporting a large amount of gas.
