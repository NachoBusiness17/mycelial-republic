1 kg

**High Pressure Gas Vent** is similar to a [Gas Vent](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Gas_Vent "Gas Vent"), difference being that it can expel gas into more highly pressurized environments.

The maximum pressure with this Vent is 20 kg per tile.

Entering an over-pressurized atmosphere will [stress](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress "Stress")[Duplicants](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Duplicant "Duplicant") with [Popped Eardrums](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Popped_Eardrums "Popped Eardrums"), unless they are wearing an [Atmo Suit](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Atmo_Suit "Atmo Suit").
