**Morale** is needed to keep [Duplicants](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Duplicant "Duplicant") happy. The exact morale needed to do so is based on the duplicant's [job](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills "Skills"). Poor morale will rapidly increase their [Stress](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress "Stress") until they break, so it is recommended that upper level jobs are saved until several morale improvements are made. Generally, there are very few Morale Penalties.

Required Morale depends on the amount of training a Duplicant has had. The more skills they have been trained in, the greater the Morale they require to stave off stress. Advanced skills increase the Morale requirement more than basic skills do.

If duplicants have a morale higher than expected, they can become [Overjoyed](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Morale#Overjoyed_Responses), giving them short lasting but strong buffs, such as higher skills, improvements to buildings' decor, etc...so if one wishes to have overjoyed duplicants, it is recommended to make a lot of furniture buildings as well as Recreation rooms to keep duplicants overjoyed more often.

Without traits, the Morale from Food ranges from -1 to +16 (this range is affected by the presence of Gourmet/Shrivelled Tastebuds/Kitchen Menace). Duplicants will start eating in their downtime when below 3,300 kcal.

Duplicants have a perception of [Decor](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Decor "Decor") and [Food](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Food_(Building) "Food (Building)"). The higher the average Decor during the last cycle and the higher the [Food Quality](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Food_Quality "Food Quality"), the higher the Morale Boost will be. Morale penalties will be incurred if below the individual's perception. Normal Decor Perception is at 0, exceeding it can yield up to +12 Morale when reaching 120 difference and failing to will decrease Morale by -1. The Morale Boost is not linear.

Using [rooms](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Room_Overlay "Room Overlay"), having downtime and interacting with certain buildings will confer a Morale Boost. If there is a [Recreation Room](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Recreation_Room "Recreation Room") available with Oxygen on its floor, duplicants will use certain furniture only if they do not yet have the Morale Boost.

Depending on duplicant's [Interests](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Duplicant#Interests "Duplicant"), the highest Morale achievable is 94-105. The lowest is -1.

### Morale and Relation to [Stress](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress "Stress")

If your Duplicant has:

*   2 points or more OVER their expected level --> stress reduction of 20% per cycle
*   1 point over their expected level --> stress reduction of 10% per cycle
*   exactly meets their requirement --> stress reduction of 5% per cycle
*   for each point UNDER the requirement --> 2.5% stress per cycle per point, up to a maximum of -25% stress per cycle obtained at 10 points below morale expectations.

Keep in mind that the morale requirement is only _one_ factor relating to stress - stress debuffs such as [Popped Eardrums](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Popped_Eardrums "Popped Eardrums") or [Sopping Wet](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Sopping_Wet "Sopping Wet") will cancel out good morale, leading to rising stress even if your morale needs are completely met.

### Overjoyed Responses

Overjoyed responses are positive effects that can occur when the morale of a duplicant is higher than necessary for their morale requirements. If morale exceeds expectation by over 8 points, there is a 0.08333% chance the Duplicant will have an overjoyed response. This probability rises linearly with morale up to a maximum of 0.20833% chance with 20 excess morale. Overjoyed responses are checked and triggered on each new schedule block (25 seconds). With the minimum 8+ over expectations, this averages to once every 30,000 seconds, or one overjoyed response per duplicant every 50 cycles, on average. With the maximum 20+ over expectations, the average is once per 12,000 seconds / 20 cycles.

    *   Reduced Great Hall boost from +6 to +4.

*   **[U59-736649](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Versions/U59-736649 "Versions/U59-736649")**: Significantly reduced [Beach Chair](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Beach_Chair "Beach Chair") work time.
