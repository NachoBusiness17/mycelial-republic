**Research** is an [Errand](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Errand "Errand") done at [Science Buildings](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Science_Buildings "Category:Science Buildings") (such as [Research Station](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Research_Station "Research Station"), [Super Computer](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Super_Computer "Super Computer"), [Telescope](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Telescope_(Base_Game) "Telescope (Base Game)"), [Virtual Planetarium](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Virtual_Planetarium "Virtual Planetarium")) as well as [Geysers](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Geyser "Geyser"). Research speed can be improved by higher duplicant [Science](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Science "Science") and a lit workspace.

Viewing the Research Tree requires the colony have either a Research Station, Super Computer or Virtual Planetarium.

Providing materials to a science building is instead a [Supply](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Supply "Supply") Task

## Base Game

The Research Station is available at the start of the game. It provides [Novice Research](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Novice_Research "Novice Research") points which are required to research every tech in the game. In order to perform Novice Research, you must have [power](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Power_(Building) "Power (Building)") and [Dirt](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Dirt "Dirt") delivered to your [Research Station](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Research_Station "Research Station"). As you will not have access to the higher tier power generators when you start, you will need a [Manual Generator](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Manual_Generator "Manual Generator"). Each Novice Research point costs 50 kg of dirt. A total of 154.3 tons of dirt will be needed to complete all research.

The Super Computer is available after researching the [Advanced Research](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Advanced_Research_(Research) "Advanced Research (Research)") tech. Requiring a [Duplicant](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Duplicant "Duplicant") with the [Advanced Research skill](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills "Skills"), it provides [Advanced Research](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Advanced_Research "Advanced Research") points which are needed for every tech in the third tier of the research tree and beyond. It requires [power](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Power_(Building) "Power (Building)") and 50 kg of [Water](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Water "Water") per research point. A total of 150 tons of water will be needed to complete all research.

The [Telescope](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Telescope_(Base_Game) "Telescope (Base Game)") is available after researching the [Celestial Detection](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Celestial_Detection "Celestial Detection") tech. It requires a duplicant with the [Field Research skill](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills "Skills"), and is required to view the [Starmap](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Starmap "Starmap") and allows for the research of interstellar bodies.

The [Virtual Planetarium](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Virtual_Planetarium "Virtual Planetarium") is available after researching the [Computing](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Computing "Computing") tech. It needs to be operated by a duplicant with the [Astronomy skill](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills "Skills") and provides [Interstellar Research](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Interstellar_Research "Interstellar Research") points which are needed for tier 8 research and higher. Every Interstellar Research point costs power and 1 [Data Bank](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Data_Bank "Data Bank"). A total of 3,200 data banks will be needed to complete all research. Data Banks can only be acquired via [space missions](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Starmap "Starmap") with [Research Modules](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Research_Module "Research Module") installed.

## Tips

*   Before the introduction of [Geotuner](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Geotuner "Geotuner"), errands leveling up a Duplicant's [Science attribute](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Duplicant#Attributes "Duplicant") were finite, and guides from this era suggest limiting research to a single duplicant conducting all research. This is no longer required - aside from the Geotuner, the [Emulsifier](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Emulsifier "Emulsifier")'s errand also count towards science experience, and is even easier to scale up.

*   Completing the entire Research Tree in the base game requires 3,085 Novice Research points, 3,000 Advanced Research points and 3,200 Interstellar Research points. 
    *   In Spaced Out!, the entire research tree requires 3,560 Novice Research points, 3,460 Advanced Research points, 3,105 Applied Sciences Research points, and 1,980 Data Analysis Research points.

*   Analyzing [Geysers](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Geyser "Geyser") takes a long time, and many geysers are very dangerous. Either wait until the geyser is dormant, or seal the geyser chamber beforehand and make sure that researcher wears an [Atmo Suit](https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/wiki/Atmo_Suit "Atmo Suit").
