LAYER 1 — PRIMITIVES (7)

Seven operators, not twelve labels. Each glyph is a verb with an electrical ancestor.

EMIT
publish light others navigate by — frontier pass, room broadcast.
ancestor: LED / antenna
STORE
bank charge now; spend it free later — KV/prefix cache, 99.5% hit @ ~50K tok/credit.
ancestor: capacitor
DISSIPATE
pay the heat — every emit burns output at 3.3x input. max_tokens is a budget line.
ancestor: resistor
GATE
pass only what survives the check; one way, no vibes through.
ancestor: diode / falsifier
INTEGRATE
accumulate the running area — totals, not instants; savings compound.
ancestor: op-amp integrator
DIFFERENTIATE
read the slope — drift, rate, EDG compare; req/s and stale-hash live here.
ancestor: op-amp differentiator
GROUND
terminate where everything returns — 127.0.0.1. Mandatory, or the circuit does not render.
ancestor: ground / binding

LAYER 2 — NAMED CIRCUITS (the Philbrick cookbook)

Fleet behaviors as compositions of primitives. HARD RULE, enforced by the draw code: any circuit whose path does not terminate in GROUND is refused — the ground wire is the launch law; a build without a falsifier doesn't render.

LAYER 3 — THE HYPERSPECTRAL TRACK

One session, rendered as a continuous spectrum: each phenomenon is a wavelength, intensity is magnitude, savings is the area under the curve.

RGB is 3 bands; multispectral is N; hyperspectral is continuous. Fleet phenomena are a spectrum. Each wavelength is a primitive at work — gold light, teal cache, red burn, red-spike correction — and the ink line is what the operator keeps: the integrated area. Receipted: 99.5% prefix hit @ ~50K tok/credit; output 3.3x input; harness baseline 0.71; wide lane 50/50 @ p50 1.7s.
