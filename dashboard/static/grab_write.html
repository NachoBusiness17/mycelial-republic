Grab & Write

hallucination is the write with no reach
The deterministic shape to the stochastic only holds if it is made by touching the thing, not by predicting it. The bones are drawn from the horse that is actually running.
The rule: never let the write get ahead of the reach. The reach is the grounding act; the write is the reach's immediate output. Write what you haven't touched = hallucination. Write as the touch lands = grab-write.

Two states, one gap

✗ Hallucination
The write with no reach. The model writes from its prior expectation of what should exist, with no contact on the thing itself. The fiction lives in the gap between what I assume exists and what I actually touched.
✓ Grab & Write
Reach and write collapsed into one motion. When the reach lands you're holding the thing, so what you write is the thing. Zero separation — and that separation is exactly where hallucination hides.

Replicate it — the loop

1 · Reach
Touch real bytes first — reach_loop.self_steal. Is it already there?
2 · Reach again
What did we intend to be here — reach_loop.corpus_intent.
3 · Write as it lands
Materialize it only if not already there — reach_loop.auto_code.
4 · Verify
The observer catches writes that still hallucinated and folds them to the failure-KB.
