Playing = exploring the fleet's own knowledge. The maze is the INTERFACE TO THE CORPUS:
rooms are invariants, corridors are citations, the fold ring is verification, the dark
patches are un-researched space. Solving a room = learning a law = verifying it = mining
the next room's seed. The game, the research program, and the verification sweep are ONE
loop wearing three skins.
