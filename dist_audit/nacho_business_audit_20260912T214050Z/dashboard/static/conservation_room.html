THE SOWING ROOM

a fable of the conservation clause · salon unified-fold C3 · 2026-09-06
THE LAW: a sow never lands in its own store.

Six pits. Seven seeds. Beyond the last pit lies THE FIELD — the ground that keeps what it is given. Behind the field, always, YOUR STORE — the mouth that feeds only you. Lift a pit's whole hand and drop the seeds one by one to the right. If the hand would cross into your own store, the room reads REFUSED — measure-or-no-move.

Two doors at every pit: THE SOWING (record door) — perform the real move, and if it reproduces the recorded repair, the chain advances. THE TELLING (narrative door) — spend a seed on a lovely line; the record does not move. The fable closes only when every seed sits in the field along the recorded repair.
