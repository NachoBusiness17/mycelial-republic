## One sentence

We build **trustworthy judgment inside an operator-owned boundary**, with **effortless human seal** on irreversible acts — not “AI runs my life,” and not a butler that needs root.

## Design goals (must hold for any agent surface)

1. **Boundary-owned data** — personal vault / residual / pack lives with the operator; AI works *inside* or against scoped exports, not as vendor root over life.
2. **Scoped, revocable access** — short-lived, least-privilege tokens (read X for N days; no write unless explicit). Password dumps and permanent root are out of order.
3. **Notice → draft → nod** — agent finds waste, mismatch, price hike, rope; drafts action; human thumbs-up. Full silent autonomy is **not** the goal.
4. **Irreversible = L3** — secrets, money movement, cancel, dispute send, identity acts wait for human. Default: `harness_yolo: false` spirit everywhere.
5. **Audit as first-class** — every material proposal and act files a note (vault note, residual edge, handoff, or chord log). No shrug-off path.
6. **Clone / continuity with eyes open** — continuity of judgment is required; a single mega-vault is a single attack surface. Prefer scoped vaults, tiers (T0–T3), and revocable packs over one immortal root store.
7. **No throne** — tool for the vigilant; no core-mirror privilege; life-ops never rewrites constitution or seat law.

## Explicit non-goals (now)

- Disposable legal “agent persona” APIs and industry liability insurance frameworks (industry / regulation; 3–5y in the public post — we do not pretend they shipped).
- Full financial/life clone on a vendor server.
- Celebrating “butler demo” while residual, seats, or consent path is fake.
- Treating weight training as the only path to agency (practice + harness first).

## Field alignment (how to read external “AI butler” posts)

When public posts describe ideal life-admin agents (scoped OAuth, personal vault, draft-then-approve, agent-as-lawyer, liability insurance):

- **Adopt:** architecture of seals (scope, boundary, nod, audit).
- **Defer:** industry identity APIs and insurance as *our* ship criteria.
- **Refuse:** root access sold as help; full autonomy as success metric; single vault without attack-surface honesty.
