A photographic reproduction of the sole original Latin manuscript plus an English translation and explanatory chapters. Frontinus was Rome's water commissioner in AD 97: he audited the aqueducts, measured flow, reported illegal diversions, and wrote it up so a successor could be held to it. Infrastructure governance by measurement -- the closest surviving analogue to the water-computer problem.
