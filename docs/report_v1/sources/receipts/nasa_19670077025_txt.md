abstract: The design of the reentry steering for the Apollo spacecraft is discussed in detail. Error performance is the critical design factor. A great deal of effort has been spent to find a system that would steer properly in spite of navigation errors. The most significant error is in the initial indicated rate-of-climb. To achieve this error performance a computed reference trajectory is used during the critical supercircular phase, with control gains that have been chosen on a statistical basis to minimize the miss distance.
