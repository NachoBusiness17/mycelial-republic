abstract: The Apollo Entry Terminal Point Guidance algorithm has flown onboard Mars lander missions but requires significant parameter tuning. For future human to Mars missions, an operationally simpler yet robust guidance is desired, motivating evaluation of the Fully Numeric Predictor Corrector Entry Guidance (FNPEG) algorithm. For a representative Mars low L/D entry, descent and landing mission, Apollo and FNPEG were compared within an established 6 degree-of-freedom simulation.
