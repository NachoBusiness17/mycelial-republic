== Overview ==
The '''Apple''' is an obtainable, multi-harvest, uncommon [[Crops|crop]] that was added to the release of the game.

== Appearance ==
The Apple produce appears as a sphere as its base, with a brown stem and a leaf pointing upwards connected to it. The base can be painted in red, dark red, and green. The crop features a large brown trunk with branches that hold round, spherical leaves. The seed resembles a solid, round cube with a green leaf sprouting on top of it.

== Obtainment ==
The Apple can be obtained from the [[Seed Shop]] for {{Sheckle|400}} or {{Robux|29}} with a '''52.631578%''' (10/19) of stocking.
