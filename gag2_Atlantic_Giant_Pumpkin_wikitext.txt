== Overview ==
The '''Atlantic Giant Pumpkin''' is a multi-harvest, legendary [[Crops|crop]]. It was added in the Fall Harvest update.

=== <u>Ability</u> ===
The Atlantic Giant Pumpkin can grow infinitely high and is immune to watering. Both the plant and its produce are immune to any mutation. As it continues to grow, its rate of growth slows down.

To be added.

== Obtainment ==
The Atlantic Giant Pumpkin can be obtained through the Fall Harvest [[Seed Shop]] for {{Leaf|600,000}} or {{Robux|379}}. It has a 2% chance to restock.

== Trivia ==
{{Trivia section}}
* The Atlantic Giant Pumpkin is the only plant that cannot get mutations or be watered.
** It's also the first plant that grows infinitely high.
** However, the Atlantic Giant Pumpkin can get the [[Mutations|Gold]] and [[Mutations|Rainbow]] mutations through the [[Weather|Gold Moon]] and the [[Weather|Rainbow Moon]] weathers.
* The Atlantic Giant Pumpkin is the highest valuing plant in leaves. It is also one of the most profitable plants based on fruit value and seed value.
