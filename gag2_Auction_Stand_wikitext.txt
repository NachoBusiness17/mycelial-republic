== Overview ==
Every 30 minutes, the NPC offers a selection of items including Seeds, Crops, Pets, Gears, Crates, Seed Packs, and Eggs.  

Prices start high and drop over time, so buying early costs more but waiting risks losing the lot to another player. Lots have limited global stock and cannot be restocked once sold out or expired. 

For full details, see [[Mechanics|Auction]].

== Appearance ==
The Auctioneer is an NPC located at the Auction Stand. He wears a cowboy hat with a brown jacket and trousers.
