== Overview ==
'''Badges''' are items that serve the function of an achievement, and are generally obtained by visitors to a Roblox experience after fulfilling a certain developer-chosen requirement<ref>[[w:c:roblox:User_badge|User badge from Fandom Roblox Wiki]]</ref>; for example, via in-experience progression.
