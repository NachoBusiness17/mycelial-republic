== Overview ==
The '''Carrot''' is an obtainable, single-harvest, common [[Crops|crop]] that was added to the release of the game.

== Appearance ==
The Carrot produce appears to be a rectangular orange-yellowish body with four green leaves protruding from the top; its crop uses the same appearance, albeit half of it in the ground. The seed is a small orange block with two thin, saturated, dark green leaves sticking out from the top.

== Obtainment ==
The Carrot can be obtained from the [[Seed Shop]] for {{Sheckle|1}} or {{Robux|3}} with a '''100%''' (1/1) chance of stocking.

== Trivia ==
{{Trivia section}}
* The Carrot produce uses the same model as the one in Grow a Garden 1.
* As of now, the Carrot seed is the cheapest seed a player can purchase, along with [[Maple Carrot]].
