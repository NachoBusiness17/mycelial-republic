== Overview ==
The '''Common Sprinkler''' is an obtainable common [[Gears|gear]] that was added to the release of the game.

== Appearance ==
The Common Sprinkler resembles a typical blue sprinkler with a cylindrical body.

== Obtainment ==
The Common Sprinkler can be obtained from the [[Gear Shop]] for {{Sheckle|3,000}} or {{Robux|7}} with a '''50%''' chance of stocking.

== Usage ==
The Common Sprinkler makes all nearby plants grow 1.5x faster and increases the chance of larger plant sizes by 7. It lasts for 2 minutes.
