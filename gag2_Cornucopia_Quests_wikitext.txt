== Overview ==
'''Cornucopia Quests''' are a mechanic in ''Grow a Garden 2'' where the player can complete certain tasks through Pilgrim at the Pilgrim Stand. This feature is exclusive to the [[Fall Harvest]] world.

Players are given 5 quests at a time, resetting daily. Completing all 5 quests in a set rewards the player with 2x Cornucopia. 

At least 1 of the 5 quests is guaranteed to be a passive-type quest (Tame Pets, Steal from People, or Grow Tall Plant).

== Rewards ==
Once the player has completed 5 Quests, they will be granted 2x Cornucopia. The Cornucopia can be opened for various rewards.

When the quest set resets, progress restarts and a new set of 5 quests is generated.

=== <u>Cornucopia Rewards</u> ===
Each Cornucopia grants 2 rolls. Each roll picks a category first, then an item within that category.
