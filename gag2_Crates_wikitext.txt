== Overview ==
'''Crates''' are one of the core features in [[Grow a Garden 2]], providing cosmetics to decorate gardens. Cosmetics are mainly used for miscellaneous and creative purposes, and do not provide gameplay enhancements.

== Obtainment ==
Can be bought at the [[Props Shop]] for [[Sheckles]], {{Robux}} or {{Leaf}}.
