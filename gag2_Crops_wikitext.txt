== Overview ==
'''Crops''' are one of the core features of the game, serving as the main source of income.

They are planted as seeds in a garden, which grow over time into plants, and they produce fruit that can be harvested once they are fully grown. Single-harvest crops can only be harvested one time because the entire plant is harvested as produce (ex. [[Carrot]]). However, multi-harvest crops produce one or more fruit that can be harvested, but the plants can’t be harvested themselves (ex. [[Apple]]). The harvested produce can be exchanged for {{Sheckle}} by selling it to the [[Sell Stand]].

'''Crops''' will decay over time due to inactivity. This can be identified by the crop desaturating (losing color) in appearance. This can be prevented by watering the plant with a [[Gears|watering can]] or waiting for a [[Weather|rain]] weather event. This does not apply to any [[Mutations|mutated]] crops.
