'''Currencies''' are one of the core features in [[Grow a Garden 2]], used to purchase items, gears, props, and other upgrades throughout the game.

== Overview ==
Currently, [[Sheckles|Sheckles]] are the only Currency available in the game. Sheckles are primarily earned by selling Crops at the [[Sell Stand]], and can be spent in shops such as the [[Gear Shop]], [[Seed Shop]], and [[Props Shop|Prop Shop]]. They can be used to buy Seeds, Gears, and Props. The appearance of the Sheckle is shown as 2 gold coins. The coins have a darker gold outline, and have rims inside. One coin is on top of the other.
