== Overview ==
'''Eggs''' are items in [[Grow a Garden 2]] used to obtain [[Pets]].

Eggs are hatched to obtain [[Pets]], which have passive abilities and improves the player's garden. Eggs vary in rarity, with rarer eggs having a higher chance of hatching rarer pets.
