== Overview ==
The '''Fall Harvest''' is a not a separate experience from Grow a Garden 2, but instead is in a unique place with it's own data, separate from the main place. It can be entered through the Explorer Stand, more specifically Ethan. The Fall Harvest stays for 60 days before being removed. Fall Harvest is almost the same as the Garden Valley but with fall-based themes.

== Mechanics ==
=== <u>Currency</u> ===
A new currency was intruduced, {{Leaf}}. It can be obtained through selling [[Crops|crops]], [[Pets|pets]] or through [[Mechanics|stealing]].

=== <u>Quests</u> ===
On the map, an NPC will go around which the player can interact with. With this NPC the player can complete various quests for the Cornucopia. 

More info: [[Cornucopia Quests]]

=== <u>Maple Plants</u> ===
Various new plants were introduced, mainly reskinned plants with brown colors. The only change is the appearance and name.
