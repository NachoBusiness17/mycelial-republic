== Overview ==
The '''Freeze Ray''' is a [[Gears|gear]] that was introduced on the game's release.

== Appearance ==
The '''Freeze Ray''' appears as a black blaster with cyan decorating. The sides feature glowing cyan panels, while the barrel is wrapped with bright cyan bands. At the front is an icy blue muzzle, and beneath it hangs an icicle-shaped crystal.

== Obtainment ==
The Freeze Ray could've been obtained from the [[Store]] for {{Robux|749}}, but was replaced with the [[Grappling Hook]]. However, it can still be obtained from the game's website store.

== Usage ==
The '''Freeze Ray''' can be used to shoot a blue beam of ice at any player to freeze them for a short duration of time, stopping that player from stealing.
