== Overview ==
'''Gears''' are one of the core features in [[Grow a Garden 2]]. Unlike [[Props]], which are purely cosmetic, gears are functional: most improve gardening — [[Sprinklers]], watering cans, and tools like the [[Trowel]] — while the rest are consumable [[Mushroom]] buffs that temporarily change the player's speed, jump, or size, or gadgets such as the [[Flashbang]] and [[Teleporter]] used to defend a plot or disrupt other players.

== Obtainment ==
Gears are bought from the [[Gear Shop]] at the center of the map, run by George, using {{Sheckle}} or {{Robux}}. Its stock rotates on a timer, so not every gear is available at once — the only exceptions are the [[Sign]], [[Lantern]], and [[Wheelbarrow]], which are always available as one-time purchases. See the [[Gear Shop]] page for full restock mechanics.

== Usage ==
Each gear serves a distinct purpose:
* '''Gardening gears''' such as the [[Common Watering Can]] and the [[Super Sprinkler]] enhance crop growth, with higher tiers providing stronger boosts — the [[Super Watering Can]], for example, increases growth speed by 300x temporarily.
* '''[[Mushroom]] gears''' like the [[Speed Mushroom]], [[Jump Mushroom]], and [[Invisibility Mushroom]] grant the player temporary effects such as increased speed, higher jumps, or changes in size.
* '''Defensive and utility gears''' such as the [[Gnome]] (which protects crops), the [[Flashbang]], and the [[Teleporter]] are used to guard a garden, interact with other players, or move around the map.
* '''Equippable gears''' like the [[Sign]], [[Lantern]], and [[Wheelbarrow]] are held or placed and remain permanently usable once purchased.

== Restocking ==
The [[Gear Shop]]'s stock is not fixed. On each restock, every gear has an independent restock chance that determines whether it is offered, along with a stock quantity range that sets how many can be bought before it sells out. A few gears — the [[Sign]], [[Lantern]], and [[Wheelbarrow]] — have no restock chance and are always available as one-time purchases, and premium gears sold for {{Robux}} are permanently in stock. For the full restock timer and mechanics, see the [[Gear Shop]] page.

The full list of gears, with their prices, rarities, stock counts, and restock chances, can be found below.
