== Overview ==
'''Gears''' are one of the core features in [[Grow a Garden 2]], providing enhancements to the player's gardening. Gears can also be used to defend your garden and [[Stealing|steal]] plants easier and for fun.

== Obtainment ==
Gears can be obtained from the [[Gear Shop]], ran by George.

The player can also get gears from the [[Store]].
