== Overview ==
The '''Ghoul Garden''' is a not a separate experience from Grow a Garden 2, but instead is in a unique place with it's own data, separate from the main place. 

It can be entered through the Explorer Stand, more specifically Ethan.<br>The Ghoul Garden stays for ? days before being removed. Ghoul Garden is almost the same as the Garden Valley but with halloween-based themes.

== Mechanics ==
=== <u>Witch's Cauldron</u> ===
On the map, there is a cauldron where players can brew various items via recipes. The player can only brew at night. If a brew fails, the ingredients will still be consumed.

Once the player failed a brew, there is a 10-second timer where the player can use their [[Cauldron Charm]] to rescue the brew.

=== <u>Currency</u> ===
A new currency was introduced, {{Candy Corn}}. It can be obtained through selling [[Crops|crops]], [[Pets|pets]], [[Mechanics|stealing]] or through the Witch NPC.

== Monsters ==
Every 1-4 minutes, a monster spawns around the map from which the player can get materials for the cauldron.

More Info: [[Monsters]].

== Pumpkin Smash ==
Every 25 seconds, 2 pumpkins spawn around the map for the player to destroy. Once destroyed, it will grant rewards.
