'''Grow A Garden 2''' is a game that was a successor of Grow A Garden. Both were made by a group of developers, owned by @DevNamedDavid. 

Players start with a small plot of land to plant seeds and take care of them while they grow into different flowers, fruits, and vegetables. Players can harvest and sell produce to earn an in-game currency called [[Sheckles]] that can then be utilized to purchase seeds, gears, eggs, decorative items, and items that can protect your base.

For [[Sheckles]], they will be able to expand their plot of land, buy Gears, and buy Seeds. This game notoriously features [[stealing]], an action that can only occur at night. 

Different types of [[Weather|weather/events]] can also occur, for example, Midas's Luck or the Rainbow Event, which has a chance to give players' crops rare mutations, or boost growth speed.

There is a Mailbox next to the player's garden, which when clicked, allows the player to send and get Seeds, Seed Packs, Gears, etc.
