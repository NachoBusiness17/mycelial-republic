'''Guilds''' are a core feature in [[Grow a Garden 2]], allowing players to team up, progress together, climb the leaderboards, and earn exclusive rewards.

== Overview ==
Guilds are groups managed through the NPC '''Gilbert'''. Guild owners can invite other players and expand their guild's member capacity by purchasing additional slots. 

To invite a player to a guild, open the Guild page and navigate to the Invite tab. To accept or decline an invite, players must interact with the [[Mechanics|Mailbox]]. The maximum capacity for any guild is 50 members.

Guild member slots must be purchased one tier at a time. Pricing depends on the guild's current size:

== Creation ==
Guilds can be created by interacting with the Guild Stand managed by [[NPCs|Gilbert]], located alongside the [[Sell Stand]] and the [[Props Shop]] in the center of the map. Creating a guild costs {{Robux|99}} but can be joined for free if Invited.

The creation form requires the following fields:
{| class="fandom-table mw-collapsible mw-collapsed"
! Field !! Rules
|-
| '''Name''' || 3-24 characters. Allowed: Letters, numbers, spaces, underscores, dashes, and apostrophes.
|-
| '''Tag''' || 2-5 characters. Letters and numbers only.
|-
| '''Description''' || 1-200 characters.
|}
Upon creation, the guild owner can also customize the guild's icon and select three distinct guild colors.

== Roles & Management ==
Guilds feature three progressive roles, each with specific permissions:
{| class="fandom-table mw-collapsible mw-collapsed"
! Role !! Limit !! Permissions
|-
| '''Owner''' || 1 || Full management: Edit guild settings, promote/demote Elders, kick members, and disband the guild.
|-
| '''Elder''' || 5 || Moderation: Invite new players to the guild.
|-
| '''Member''' || Slot Cap || Standard: Baseline participant permissions.
|}

=== <u>Guild Editing</u> ===
Only the Owner can edit a guild's name, tag, description, colors, and icon. Changing the guild name triggers a 7-day cooldown before it can be changed again.

=== <u>Leaving & Disbanding</u> ===
Any Member or Elder can voluntarily leave a guild at any time. The Owner cannot leave the guild directly; instead, they must choose to disband it, which permanently deletes the guild and removes all members.

=== <u>Chat Tag</u> ===
When being in a guild, the player will have a chat tag next to their name, which displays in the regular chat.

=== <u>Guild Feed</u> ===
Guild Members can create gift requests for [[Crops|Seeds]], [[Crops|Fruits]], [[Props Shop|Prop Crates]], [[Seed Packs]] and [[Eggs]]. Guild Requests last for 6 hours. Every member can only have 1 request open, and each member can donate up to 10 items per request.

The feed also shows guild activity posts, such as legendary/mythic pet finds, egg hatches, big sales and mutations. Members can also react with emojis to posts.

== Guild Competition ==
Guilds face off in a weekly competition. Points are calculated by the weight of harvested plants, awarded at a rate of 1 point per gram.

At the end of each weekly cycle, rewards are calculated by leaderboard placement and delivered directly to participants via the [[Mechanics|Mailbox]].
