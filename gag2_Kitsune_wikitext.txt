== Overview ==
The '''Kitsune''' is a secret [[Pets|pet]], added on August 7.

== Appearance ==
To be added.

== Obtainment ==
The Kitsune can be obtained through the [[Exclusive Fall Chest]] or through the [[Exclusive Harvest Egg]].

== Ability ==
Sneaks out at night to steal fruit from empty gardens and turns it Chakra.

Big, Mega or Rainbow does not buff the ability. 

=== <u>Ability Info</u> ===
Every 5-10 seconds, the Kitsune checks for an empty garden and has an 85% chance per check to attempt a steal.

A successful steal takes 3 seconds before the fruit is delivered back to the player, at which point it has a 20% chance to become mutated with Chakra. 

After a successful steal it goes on a 10 second cooldown; a failed check only cools down for 3 seconds before trying again.
