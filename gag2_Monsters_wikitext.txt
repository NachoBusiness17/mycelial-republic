== Overview ==
'''Monsters''' are roaming enemies that can spawn during the Ghoul Garden event. Monsters spawn every 1-4 minutes, and up to 4 monsters can be active at once.

They chase nearby players, deal damage, and can be defeated using the [[Shovel]]. They have nameplates and HP displays while alive.<br>
Defeated monsters have a chance to drop a unique item.
