== Overview ==
The '''Muffin Bake Event''' was the first event that premiered on August 22, 2026. It introduced new [[Pets|pets]], [[Crops|seeds]], items, mechanics and more.

== Mechanics ==
=== <u>Baking</u> ===
Players can interact with the Baker's ovens to bake [[Gears|Muffins]] using baking ingredients. There are 3 Baker ovens available, with each oven able to bake 1 muffin at a time. Recipes refresh every hour.

To begin baking, the player must have all ingredients required by the selected recipe. Favorited fruits are not counted toward recipe requirements and will not be used for baking.

Once a bake has started, the oven enters the ''Baking'' state and displays the remaining time.<br>
Once the timer reaches zero, the oven becomes ''Ready'' and the player can interact with it to claim the finished muffin.

=== <u>Personal Ovens</u> ===
Players can place up to 5 Personal Ovens in their garden. Personal Ovens take 75% of the normal recipe duration, reducing the amount of time required to bake by 25%.

=== <u>Insta-Bake</u> ===
While an oven is baking, players can instantly finish the bake by using an [[Insta-Bake]].

Instantly completing a bake only removes the remaining bake time and does not change the resulting muffin.

=== <u>Muffin Exchange</u> ===
Finished muffins can be exchanged for Bake Sale rewards. Each trade requires a specific number and type of muffin.
