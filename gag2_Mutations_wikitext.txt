== Overview ==
'''Mutations''' are unique and visual transformations that significantly increase the selling price of [[Crops|crops]] when harvested. 

Each [[Crops|crop]] can carry exactly one mutation at any given time. Mutations are calculated when a [[Crops|crop]] updates or upon harvest. The final crop sell value is determined by its base price multiplied by both its physical size scale and its mutation multiplier.

=== <u>Mechanics & Obtainment</u> ===
Mutations can be obtained in several distinct ways:
* '''Random Growth/Harvest Chance:''' Standard [[Crops|crops]] have a base 1% chance to mutate upon harvest. Only specific, highly adaptable mutations can be triggered naturally during growth stages; the rest are strictly rolled during harvesting. (AKA. When you pick up your plant during a weather event, it has 1 final chance to adopt the mutation)
* '''Special Seeds:''' Planting [[Gold Seed|Gold Seeds]] or [[Rainbow Seed|Rainbow Seeds]] guarantees a mutation on the grown [[Crops|crop]].
* '''Weather & Celestial Events:''' Certain high-tier mutations can only occur during specialized global weather cycles on the [[Weather|Time & Weather Cycle]] page, such as Snowfall, Lightning storms, or Blood Moons.
* '''Pet Modifiers:''' [[Pets]] like the [[Pets|Unicorn]] or [[Pets|Golden Dragonfly]] passively multiply the base mutation chances of crops in your garden.
