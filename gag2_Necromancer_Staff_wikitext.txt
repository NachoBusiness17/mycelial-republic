== Overview ==
The '''Necromancer Staff''' is an unreleased mythic [[Gears|gear]].

== Appearance ==
To be added.

== Obtainment ==
The Necromancer Staff can be obtained from the Ghoul Garden [[Gear Shop]] for {{Candy Corn|10,000,000}} or {{Robux|TBA}} with a '''TBA%''' chance of stocking.

== Usage ==
The Necromancer Staff lets the player claim every wild pet on the map at once, for free. The pets still can be bought by other players while walking to the owners plot.

The gear is consumed on use.
