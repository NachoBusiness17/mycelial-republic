== Overview ==
'''Pets''' are companions in [[Grow a Garden 2]] that provide passive abilities and bonuses to the player's garden.

Pets can be obtained through various methods, including hatching [[Eggs]], purchasing with {{Robux}}, or receiving them as gifts from other players.

== Map Spawns ==
Pets can be found spawning randomly around the map. Each spawned pet is marked with a visible countdown timer, indicating how long it will remain before disappearing. Players can purchase these pets using {{Sheckle}} before the timer reaches zero; if the timer expires, the pet vanishes and can no longer be obtained from that spawn point.

== Variants ==
Pets can roll one of 2 variants when hatched, which alter their size, appearance, and ability strength. A pet's size class (Big / Mega) and its Rainbow status are rolled independently, so a pet can be both at once (e.g. a Rainbow Mega pet).

=== <u>Size Variants</u> ===
* Big: A slightly rarer version. Doubles the size of the pet and boosts the passive by 2x.
* Mega: A very rare version. Quadruples the size of the pet and boosts the passive by 3x.

=== <u>Rainbow Variant</u> ===
Rainbow is a visual tier rolled separately from size that boosts a pet's ability. The Rainbow ability boost is a flat x1.25 multiplier on the pet's base ability.

== Pet Mutations ==
'''Pet Mutations''' is the core mechanic that allows wild-spawned pets to appear with unique traits and enhanced stats. There are currently two primary mutations in the game: Big and Mega.

If the spawn rates of these mutations were the same as the egg chances, Big would have a one in 50 chance and Mega would have a one in 1000 chance.

== Big Pets ==
Big Pets are a variant of normal pets that are noticeably larger and much harder to obtain, having only a 1/50 chance of replacing the normal variant. Big Pets give their ability a 2x boost.
This specific mutation stands as the second rarest mutation in the game, beaten only by Mega Pets.

== Mega Pets ==
Mega Pets are a variant of normal pets that are significantly larger and far rarer than Big Pets, only replacing the normal variant with a 1/1000 chance. Mega Pets give their ability a 3x boost.
