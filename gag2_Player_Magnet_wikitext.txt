== Overview ==
The '''Player Magnet''' is a {{TierText|Mythic}} [[Gears|gear]] that was added to the first update.

== Appearance ==
It has blue on the right-hand side with a studded texture and a silver tip, and red on the left-hand side with the same counterparts of the other half.

== Obtainment ==
The Player Magnet can be obtained from the [[Gear Shop]] for {{Sheckle|7,000,000}} or {{Robux|1,149}} and is always in stock.

== Usage ==
Sucks nearby players towards the player for 10 seconds.
