== Overview ==
'''Seed Packs''' are bundles containing a variety of seeds, ranging from common crops to exclusive event varieties.
Each pack contains randomized rewards with varying rarity tiers.

When opening, there is a Skip button which when pressed, immediately stops the rolling.

According to an in-game tip using sprinklers can boost your luck when opening a seed pack.
