[[File:Storeseed.png|thumb|The Seed Shop.]]
The '''Seed Shop,''' also known as '''Sam's Shop,''' is a major location in [[Grow a Garden 2]] handled by Sam; where players can purchase [[Crops|Seeds]] to plant in their gardens. This is where the majority of the in-game seeds are located. 

== Overview ==
The Seed Shop is one of the core locations in Grow a Garden 2. Players visit it to buy seeds using in-game currency or {{Robux}}. The shop's stock rotates on a 5-minute timer, meaning not all seeds are available at all times. Players can instantly restock the Seed Shop for {{Robux|39}} so you don't have to wait for the 5-minute timer. Seeds can also be physically gifted to other players with {{Robux}}.

It operates on a stock system, meaning that not all crop seeds are consistently available at all times. However, there are notable exceptions: Carrot, Strawberry, Blueberry and Tomato seeds are guaranteed to be available. If an item is sold out, like in other stores, the purchase button will be red and say "NO STOCK." But even if there is no stock on a seed, you can still purchase it with {{Robux}}.

The main stock refreshes every '''5 minutes''', giving a chance for seeds that were previously unavailable to become available in the shop. ''The rarer the seed is, the less likely it will be available in the shop.''

Stockings on the '''Seed Shop''' are shared '''globally''' across all servers, meaning all players are seeing the same stock. However, the purchases of one player do not affect the stock for others, as the act of purchasing is entirely client-sided.

== Appearance ==
The '''Seed Shop''' is a small stall with its roof covered with a white and green striped cloth. Behind the stall is an interactable shop NPC named Sam.
