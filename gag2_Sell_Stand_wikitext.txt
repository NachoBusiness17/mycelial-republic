The '''Sell Stand''' is a core shop where players can sell their harvested [[crops]], fruits, and other gathered produce to the NPC Steven in exchange for [[Sheckles]]. 

The stand provides interfaces for players to either look through the individual worth of their fruits and sell specific single items, or immediately cash out their entire inventory at once.

== Overview ==
The Sell Stand serves as the economic hub of the map, converting a player's labor (produce) directly into [[Sheckles]].

== Double or Nothing ==
'''Double or Nothing''' is a luck-based mechanic available at the Sell Stand that allows players to increase the payout of their items by double or lose their entire inventory. 

You can '''double or nothing''' many times, there is no fee for doing '''double or nothing'''

== Daily Deals ==
'''Daily Deals''' is a shop mechanic allowing players to sell their [[crops]] for 2.2x the price (some sources citing a 2.5x increase) not stacking with the sell prices multiplier.

'''daily deals''' are available every 12 hours for a one time use.

== View sell prices ==
The '''view sell prices''' option allows players to see the sell multiplier of each [[Crops|crop]] they harvest. the change in multipliers range from 2x-4x and serve as a multiplier to the base prices (visible by hovering over the [[Crops|crop]] you wish to sell) allowing the player to maximize the [[sheckles]] they may receive from selling multiple of the same [[Crops|crop]]. these do not interfere with daily deals and only work within the sell inventory.
