== Overview ==
The '''Shadow Dragon''' is a super [[Pets|pet]], added in the Fall Harvest update.

== Appearance ==
To be added.

== Obtainment ==
The Shadow Dragon has a 0.00464% chance to spawn anywhere on the Fall Harvest map, purchasable for {{Leaf|30,000,000}}.

== Ability ==
Flies around your gardens and has a chance to apply the Veil mutation when you plant a seed.

=== <u>Ability Info</u> ===
Has a 10% chance to apply Veil when planting a seed. This increases to 20% if the dragon is Big, or 30% if Mega. For a Rainbow variant, these chances become 12.5%, 25%, and 37.5%, respectively.
