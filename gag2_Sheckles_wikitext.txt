'''Sheckles''' are the main form of currency in [[Grow a Garden 2]] and the original ''Grow a Garden''. 

Grow A Garden 2 uses the "'''¢'''" currency symbol on the right side of numbers in-game to denote Sheckles, as well as a specific green '''Sheckles''' icon on the left.

== Obtainment ==
A player can earn Sheckles by:
* Selling plants, fruits, or pets at the [[Sell Stand|Sell Stuff stand]].
* Purchasing separate packages of them in the Robux Shop.
* Playing with friends in the same server. Every friend in the same server as a player provides a '''+10% Sheckle boost''', which can reach a maximum of '''70%'''. Players can add friends to the server by clicking the "+" icon to the right of your total sheckles.

== Uses ==
Sheckles are used to buy items from various areas around the map:
* The [[Seed Shop]]
* The [[Gear Shop]]
* The [[Props Shop]]
* Purchasing Pets around the map

== Trivia ==
* A player can see how many Sheckles they have in the bottom left corner of the screen, or by looking at the leaderboard in the top right.
* The origin for this currency is the Mesopotamian Shekel, a coin that was used around 3000 BC, and currently the Shekel (New Israeli Shekel) is the currency used by the country of Israel.
* If you have a large amount of Sheckles and want to see the exact amount you have, you can just hover over your total Sheckles icon and you can see the exact amount you have.
