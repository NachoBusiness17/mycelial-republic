== Overview ==
The '''Shovel''' is a [[Gears|gear]] that allows you to hit other players & remove crops/produce.

== Appearance ==
The Shovel appears to be an ordinary real life shovel with a brown handle and a gray blade.

== Obtainment ==
The player will receive the shovel instantly when joining the game, no purchases necessary.

== Usage ==
The shovel can remove crops, fruits, and placed gears from a player's garden by holding the object.

If a player attempts to shovel any kind of crop, produce or any placed gear, a pop-up appears, asking the player if they want to shovel it, and that doing so cannot be undone.

Then the player can choose either to cancel the action or confirm it and shovel the crop, the produce, or the gear.

You can also use the Shovel to hit someone with a shovel to retrieve your plants that someone may have stole, as well as hitting them to knock them out of your Garden if they are found in it.
