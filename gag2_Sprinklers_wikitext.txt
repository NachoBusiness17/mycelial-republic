'''Sprinklers''' are a type of [[Gears|gear]] that can be placed on a player's plot to automatically water nearby [[crops]], helping them grow over time.

== Overview ==
Sprinklers are placed on a player's plot and provide a passive watering effect to crops within their range. Higher-rarity sprinklers generally cover a larger area and may provide additional growth benefits. Sprinklers can be purchased from the [[Gear Shop]], run by [[George]].

== Trivia ==

* (Unconfirmed) The 2-minute sprinkler timer doesn't seem to tick down while offline — one player left the game for a full hour and returned to find the sprinkler still active with effects intact.
* Placing a second sprinkler of the same type near a crop that already has one active does not stack or add to the luck multiplier and growth speed bonus.
* Stacking/overlapping sprinklers of different types does add to the luck multiplier and growth speed.
