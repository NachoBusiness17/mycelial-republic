'''Stealing''' is a main mechanic in Grow a Garden 2. It allows players to take fruits from other players' gardens for completely free, making it one of the most significant new features compared to the original [https://www.roblox.com/games/126884695634066/Grow-a-Garden Grow a Garden], in which stealing costed {{Robux}} or Tokens.

== Overview ==
Once night arrives, the player will have two options: Steal, or Protect. To steal, go to another player's garden, and as long as the garden owner is not in their garden, the player can steal any kind of crop(s) from them, however, [[Bamboo]] and [[Mushroom|Mushrooms]] take 3 seconds to steal.

If the player wants to protect their crops, the player should stay in their garden; this will make it locked, preventing anyone from stealing from the player. There are also [[Gear Shop|gears]], [[Seed Shop|plants]], and [[Props|builds]] that can help protect the garden.

This mechanic can commonly happen as the main objective at night, but can sometimes be replaced with another type of event that gives out seeds with special mutations or increases crop chances, etc. These replacements can sometimes distract players, letting the player easily steal from them without noticing.

If someone is in the process of stealing a player's crop, the player can hit them with the [[Shovel]], to retrieve the stolen crop.

== Common Stealing Techniques ==
These are the commonly used techniques for stealing:

1. '''The "Wait" Trick'''

A player waits for the owner of a garden to leave their garden to do other activities (stealing other's fruits, visiting the [[Seed Shop]] or [[Gear Shop]] or [[Sell Stand]], or trading outside the garden), then the player can start the process of stealing.

2. '''The AFK Method'''

A player will find an owner of a garden who is AFK outside their garden, then sneak in and start stealing.

3. '''The "Immediate Join Snipe" Technique'''

When a player joins, they usually spawn outside their garden. Players can use this small advantage to attempt to steal crops from that player who just joined the server.

4. '''The Wheelbarrow Method'''

When a friend joins a server (or an alt account friended), a player can use the [[Wheelbarrow]] to attempt to place the owner outside of their garden (if inside their garden). If the owner of the garden is AFK, the alt/friend can be used to steal from the garden of the AFK owner who is inside the wheelbarrow. The only thing is to avoid unequipping the wheelbarrow as that may lead to the owner teleporting back inside their garden. This is similar to Steal a Brainrot's "Web Slinger" method.

5. '''The Special Night Technique'''

As said earlier, when a [[Weather#GoldMoon|Gold Moon]] or [[Weather#RainbowMoon|Rainbow Moon]] rises, both of those spawn their respective seeds. The player can use this advantage due to the fact that the owner of a garden will look for those seeds instead of protecting crops. The player can then initate stealing when the garden is unlocked.

== Trivia ==
* Stealing is completely free, no Robux required.
* In order to steal crops in the original Grow a Garden, players needed to pay {{Robux|37}} or 37 Tokens.
* The player must bring the fruit back to their garden to receive it, and the teleport buttons will not be active when they begin the process of stealing a crop until the player returns to their garden. This prevents players from instantly teleporting to their Garden to keep the crop.
* You can tell if a player's garden is available for stealing once an unlocked icon appears on top of their garden.
* The game might have a movement anti-cheat, in order to stop exploiters from easily stealing crops, unlike the original [https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Grow_a_Garden_Wiki Grow a Garden].
* This mechanic is very similar to the stealing mechanic inside the popular Roblox game "Steal a Brainrot".
*Once the owner of the targeted garden leaves while someone is stealing, the player stealing will get to keep the fruits stolen.
