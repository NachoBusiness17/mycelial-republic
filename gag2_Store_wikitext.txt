== Overview ==
The '''Store''' is a shop in Grow a Garden 2 where a player can buy or gift another player gears, items and {{Sheckle}} or {{Leaf}} with {{Robux}}. 

This shop can be found by pressing the Shop button on the top of the screen.

Due to gambling laws, certain offers may be hidden if the player is in Australia, Belgium, the U.K or the Netherlands.
