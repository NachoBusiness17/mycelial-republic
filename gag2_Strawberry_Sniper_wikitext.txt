== Overview ==
The '''Strawberry Sniper''' is a '''mythical''' gear that was added on June 21, it has a [Unknown] chance of restocking at a '''price of [[File:TextSheckles.png|frameless]] 13,000,000.'''

== Usage ==
The item can be used to '''stop players from stealing''' your fruits from your garden during the [[Weather|Night Event]], where players have the ability to [[Stealing|steal]]. 

'''This item can be used infinitely with a 3 second cooldown.'''

== Appearance ==
A green and red sniper with the textures of strawberry seeds.

== Obtainment ==
It can be purchased in the [[Gear Shop|Gears Shop]].
