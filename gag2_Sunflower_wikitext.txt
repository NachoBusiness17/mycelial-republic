== Overview ==
The '''Sunflower''' is an obtainable, multi-harvest, legendary [[Crops|crop]] that was added to the release of the game.

== Appearance ==
The '''Sunflower''' produce features a golden blocky flower with long rectangular petals and a darker center. The '''Sunflower''' plant is a usual light green stem with the '''Sunflower''' growing at the top of the stem.

== Obtainment ==
The Sunflower can be obtained from the [[Seed Shop]] for {{Sheckle|5,000,000}} or {{Robux|599}} with a '''1.7857142857%''' (1/56) chance of stocking.
