== Overview ==
'''Weathers''' are one of the main features of the game and can help the crop's growth speed go faster and increase their values by having their crops mutated. 

Every active server experiences the same weather at the same time. A crop can only receive one weather mutation at a time.

== Notes ==
* Gold and Rainbow Moon do spawn their respective seeds in private servers. 
** The fewer people in the server, the lower the chance a gold/rainbow seed will spawn. 
** The more people in the server, the higher the chance a gold/rainbow seed will spawn.
** A gold/rainbow seed '''cannot''' spawn if there are less than 2 players in the server.
