== Overview ==
The '''Wheelbarrow''' is a {{TierText|Legendary}} [[Gears|gear]] that was added to the game's release. A friend is required in the same server as the player to allow usage.

== Appearance ==
The '''Wheelbarrow''' is a one-wheeled gear with a red body to carry your friends, and a steel frame to hold it together. The body has a stud texture, while the rest of the wheelbarrow has no texture.

== Obtainment ==
The Wheelbarrow can be obtained from the [[Gear Shop]] for {{Sheckle|500,000}} or {{Robux|129}} and is always in stock.
