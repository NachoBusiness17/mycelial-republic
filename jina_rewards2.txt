## Rewards

[Random events](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Events "Random Events") can yield rewards in many forms. ## Auto-rewards[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

According to the game files stored in `ftl.dat`, there are several types and tiers of rewards. Most of these reward types have tiers (a tier will be denoted here onward by a letter T): **Low** (low scrap multiplier or low amount of resources), **Medium**, **High** (high scrap multiplier or high amount of resources), and **Random** (one of the other 3 tiers is randomly chosen). These tiers and the scrap sector determine how much of the reward you will receive. The reward types are listed below: (Note: the grey-colored text in parentheses on this page shows examples of the reward notation on the event pages, however, on the actual event pages these notations are blue-colored links except for the tier word preceding the link.)

### Standard[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T scrap + low resources (2 random resources among fuel, missiles, and drone parts) + roughly a 3% chance to include a bonus weapon, augmentation, or drone schematic. (low/medium/high scrap with resources)
    *   Guaranteed weapon or drone schematic rewards outside the auto-reward will prevent the bonus from being rolled.
    *   Guaranteed augment rewards outside the auto-reward will be overwritten by this, if the bonus is an augmentation.

### Stuff[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T resources (2 random resources among fuel, missiles, and drone parts) + low scrap + roughly a 6% chance to include a bonus weapon, augmentation, or drone schematic. This type of reward is most often used in non-scripted (i.e. not guaranteed to occur) ship surrenders. (Medium/High tier - medium/high resources with some scrap; Low tier - low resources and scrap)
    *   If the 6% roll for a bonus weapon, augmentation, or drone schematic is successful, the scrap reward tier will also match the overall reward tier rather than being low tier. [[3]](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#cite_note-Calculated_FTL._Bonuses.-3)(e.g. the surrender reward in the [Pirate bribing you for unknown ship](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate_bribing_you_for_unknown_ship "Pirate bribing you for unknown ship") event is High tier resources: if the 6% bonus item reward chance is rolled successfully, then the scrap part of the reward will also be High tier)
    *   Ships surrender offers in [fights with default rewards](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Fights_with_Default_Rewards "Category:Fights with Default Rewards") and [fights with default rewards (Lanius)](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Fights_with_Default_Rewards_(Lanius) "Category:Fights with Default Rewards (Lanius)") are random tier. A successful 6% bonus item roll modifies the scrap part of the reward to match the resources tier. (e.g. if a Medium tier is rolled, the reward will be: item + medium resources + medium scrap)
    *   Guaranteed weapon or drone schematic rewards outside the auto-reward will prevent the bonus from being rolled.
    *   Guaranteed augment rewards outside the auto-reward will be overwritten by this, if the bonus is an augmentation.

### Fuel[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T fuel & T scrap. (low/medium/high fuel and scrap)

### Missiles[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T missiles & T scrap. (low/medium/high missiles and scrap)

### Drone parts[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T drone parts & T scrap. (low/medium/high drone parts and scrap)

### Scrap only[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T scrap. (low/medium/high scrap)

### Fuel only[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   T fuel. (low/medium/high fuel)

### Weapon[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   A random weapon & T scrap. (weapon with low/medium/high scrap)
    *   Guaranteed weapon or drone schematic rewards outside the auto-reward will overwrite this.

### Augment[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   A random augmentation & T scrap. (augmentation with low/medium/high scrap)
    *   Guaranteed weapon or drone schematic rewards outside the auto-reward will overwrite this.
    *   Guaranteed augmentation rewards outside the auto-reward will be overwritten by this.

### Drone[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   A random drone schematic & T scrap. (drone schematic with low/medium/high scrap)
    *   Guaranteed weapon or drone schematic rewards outside the auto-reward will overwrite this.

## Default rewards[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Most ship fights in the game yield default rewards, and are marked as [Fights with Default Rewards](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Fights_with_Default_Rewards "Category:Fights with Default Rewards"). Note that Auto-ship fights with default rewards (i.e. technically granting only medium scrap with resources) are excluded from this category and instead placed into a separate [category](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Auto-ship_fights "Category:Auto-ship fights"). Here is the list of default rewards:

### Destroying the ship[]

_The ship explodes, leaving behind a substantial collection of useful scrap material._

*   You receive medium [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

### Killing enemy crew[]

*   [**3x**](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Template:DuplicateEvent/doc "Template:DuplicateEvent/doc"): _There are no more life-signs remaining on the ship. You strip it of useful materials._
    *   You receive medium [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

*   [**2x**](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Template:DuplicateEvent/doc "Template:DuplicateEvent/doc"): _There are no more life-signs remaining on the ship. You strip it of useful materials._
    *   You receive high [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

*   [**2x**](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Template:DuplicateEvent/doc "Template:DuplicateEvent/doc"): _With the crew dead, you are able to take the fuel out of storage. You also take all the scrap you can manage._
    *   You receive high[fuel and scrap](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Fuel).

*   _Now that their ship has been emptied of hostiles, you search it. Eventually you find a prisoner who offers to join your crew._
    *   You receive a crewmember with low scrap.

*   _You find a weapon system on their ship. With no crew to stop you, you can install it on your own._
    *   You receive a weapon with low scrap.

## Default rewards: Lanius[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Most [fights against Lanius](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Fights_with_Default_Rewards_(Lanius) "Category:Fights with Default Rewards (Lanius)") ships in [Abandoned sector events](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Abandoned_Sector_Events "Category:Abandoned Sector Events") yield these rewards. They are similar to the default rewards listed above, with the following differences: (short summary)

*   Destroying enemy ship: 
    *   High scrap reward is possible (1 in 4).

*   Killing enemy crew: 
    *   No weapon reward; instead, a drone schematics reward is possible (1 in 8).
    *   Fuel reward chance is lower (1 in 8 vs 2 in 9).

### Destroying the ship[]

*   [**3x**](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Template:DuplicateEvent/doc "Template:DuplicateEvent/doc"): _The ship explodes, leaving behind a collection of useful scrap material._
    *   You receive medium [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

*   _The ship explodes, revealing their cargo of unprocessed metal. You collect a significant amount of scrap._
    *   You receive high [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

### Killing enemy crew[]

*   [**3x**](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Template:DuplicateEvent/doc "Template:DuplicateEvent/doc"): _There are no more life-signs remaining on the ship. You strip it of useful materials._
    *   You receive medium [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

*   [**2x**](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Template:DuplicateEvent/doc "Template:DuplicateEvent/doc"): _There are no more life-signs remaining on the ship. You strip it of useful materials._
    *   You receive high [scrap with resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Standard). (3% chance for a bonus item)

*   _With the Lanius dispatched you are able to take the fuel out of storage. You also take all the scrap you can manage._
    *   You receive high[fuel and scrap](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Fuel).

*   _Now that the Lanius ship has been emptied of hostiles, you search it. Eventually you find a prisoner who offers to join your crew._
    *   You receive a crewmember with low scrap.

*   _You find a drone schematic on their ship. With no crew to stop you, you can install it on your own._
    *   You receive a drone schematic with low scrap.

## Surrender rewards[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Besides the scripted events, certain enemies can make a surrender offer when their ship's hull is low. When you accept the surrender offer, the fight ends immediately (your weapons' projectiles and asteroids will miss the enemy ship, beams will stop; all enemy projectiles, already launched, and asteroids can damage your ship and crew).

For the actual surrender rewards, see: [surrender rewards](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Stuff).For ship-specific surrender chances and requirements, see: [surrender offers](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Enemy_Ships#Surrenders_and_escape_attempts "Enemy Ships").For events with scripted surrenders, see: [Ship surrender Events](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Ship_surrender_Events "Category:Ship surrender Events").
### Slug surrender rewards[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

Most Slug ships do not shown their actual surrender reward offer, contrary to the usual surrender offers with the exact amount of [T resources](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Stuff) (and, possibly, an item), which a player can see and also reject. In case with Slug ships the actual surrender reward is shown only after the acceptance of their surrender, which cannot be rejected when the reward is revealed. The reward can be any of these:

*   _"Here! We have lotsss of fuel! Take it all!"_
    *   You receive high[fuel](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Fuel_only).

*   _They transfer a meager amount of material. "There... now keep your word..."_
    *   You receive low[resources and scrap](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Stuff). (6% chance for a bonus item)

*   _"Take it and leave ussss be."_
    *   You receive medium[resources with some scrap](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Rewards#Stuff). (6% chance for a bonus item)

## Notes[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fftl.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRewards%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D20&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   The following rewards are in the game's files, but are not mentioned above: 
    *   Unused Auto-rewards: 
        *   Missiles only: T missiles.
        *   Drone parts only: T drone parts.
        *   Item: A random weapon/augmentation/drone schematic & T scrap. It is technically a random selection of the "Weapon", "Augment" and "Drone" auto-rewards.

    *   Item Modification: Used for specifying exact values of scrap/fuel/missiles/drone parts to be added/removed. They often appear as costs for certain choices. 
        *   They appear in the code as <item_modify><item type="..." min="..." max="..."/></item_modify> (multiple item tags can be used for multiple resource changes)
        *   Auto-rewards may overwrite item modification if they happen to award the same resource, which causes some bugs.
