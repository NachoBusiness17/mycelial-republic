## What Emerged
*None of this was scripted. The characters' compulsions collided.*

- Rinulo's Shadow surfaced during the trial. The party saw something their companion had been hiding. It changes how they look at each other.
- The Wizard's pattern-seeking and the Paladin's truth-tasting created an unexpected synergy — patterns revealed lies, lies revealed patterns neither could see alone.
- The party proved resistant to engineered desire. But the Loophole Weaver expected this. The refusal WAS the desired outcome — it proves the heroes can be predicted, which means they can be controlled.
- Nothing that happened was scripted. The characters' compulsions — their inability to NOT do certain things — created the story. The DM only described the world. The characters described themselves.
