(2026-09-14, after the clerk-parable arc.) The operator gets strong prose out of me by assigning an author mask rather than by banning tells. Mask canon, expanded same day to ten "riddler" seats (each smuggles a doctrine inside an entertainment) with equivalents: Voltaire the blade (Swift, Twain, La Rochefoucauld, Bierce) · Campbell the monomyth (Ovid, Graves, Calasso) · Le Guin the anthropologist (Calvino, Saramago, Butler, Ted Chiang) · Adams the absurdist (Vonnegut, Lem, Sheckley, Fforde) · Pratchett the footnoted satirist (Tom Holt, Christopher Moore, K.J. Parker) · Tolkien the elegist (Peake, Morris, Eddison) · Orwell the windowpane (Didion, E.B. White, Gellhorn) · Alan Moore the magician, form-as-spell (Angela Carter, Burroughs, Grant Morrison) · Shakespeare the stage, soliloquy and fools (Marlowe, Melville, Joyce) · Neal Stephenson the engineer, explanation-as-story, artifact-in-fiction (Pynchon, Borges, Danielewski). Stephenson and Moore are the stego-native hires. New direction: parables keep the public "Made with AI" beacon, but the prose should stop smelling of default-assistant tells, and carry a hidden mark instead — e.g. sentence acrostics, hash-chosen details, or the mask+seed recorded in a registry so provenance is provable later by re-derivation.

**Why:** "Avoid tells" is a negative constraint models execute badly; a dense positive style target overwrites the default register outright. And the operator wants provenance that is verifiable by us but invisible to readers — the repo's own posture (public commitment, private preimage) applied to poems. See [[clerk-parable-cast]], [[operator-saves-everything]].

**How to apply:** When drafting fiction or parables for the operator, adopt or propose a named mask first, do one plain revision pass before dressing, and keep pastiche from going shallow (joke-per-sentence is its own tell). If asked to embed a mark, make it decodable only with the seed/registry entry, and log the entry so the proof survives.
