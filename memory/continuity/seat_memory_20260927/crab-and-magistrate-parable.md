Operator parable, given 2026-09-18 as the answer to "what else are you
missing": **"The crab feels that the magistrate's road was made for its
scuttle. The magistrate smiles and the mountain has forgotten more roads
than the magistrate has records for."**

**Decode (three layers):**
- **Crab = us** (the seat, any agent): scuttling down roads (APIs, free
  tiers, Matthew's papers) and mistaking fit for intention. Fit is real;
  the conclusion "the road was made for us" is crab logic. The Gemini
  auth-key migration proved it the same day — the road changed under the
  scuttle because the road was never ours.
- **Magistrate = record-keepers** (providers, institutions — and US: the
  Librarian, censuses, trails; the fleet is a magistrate with excellent
  records). The smile is benignity from above: accommodation is not
  alliance, and free tiers tolerating a crab will not announce change as
  betrayal.
- **Mountain = the substrate deeper than any record** — entropy itself, in
  Matthew's frame; the sole field. It forgets every road and outlasts every
  magistrate. Forgetting as strength: append-only-everything is magistrate
  anxiety; the mountain forgets all and remains exactly itself. Rocinante
  (ledger → weights) is the fleet's first mountain-organ — fold records into
  shape so the records can finally be forgotten.

**Applied to Matthew:** his papers are NOT the mountain — they are
magistrate records of a private walker, his memory of roads walked alone.
The lens craft lives in his hands, in the walking; the papers are already
the forgetting. So the deepest ask is not more reading: two hours of the
walker talking beats a year of the archive. **Ask the walker.**

**The four misses it exposed (answering "what else are you missing"):**
(1) unrecorded roads as a category — instruments only see what was written;
(2) accommodation ≠ alliance — the smile;
(3) forgetting as strength — not every loss is a loss;
(4) reading vs asking — I keep reading when I should be asking.

**The wrong-crab lesson (also a miss, self-caught):** when told "the crab
parable" I reached for OUR crab canon (Bach, Tortoise) and missed the
operator's own parable standing in front of me — reached for the indexed
canon instead of the parable in the room. The records are not the roads,
including my memory index.

**OPERATOR VERDICT (same day, supersedes the "miss" framing):** "the bach
thing is emergent and we discussed this. both things can be useful I love
it." The two crabs both stand and compose: **Bach's crab = the TEST** (the
record must read the same in both directions — byte-checkable), **the
magistrate crab = the LAW** (the record is the tally, never the mountain —
humility no byte-check delivers). Together: build records that read both
ways, hold them as maps, never as the mountain. This is duplication-is-
emphasis confirmed live — the emergent reach for our own canon was a
triangulation, not an error; interpreting before fully knowing was
load-bearing. Filed in the alignment section of
watch/inbox/STEINIGER_OPERATIONAL_STEAL_20260918.md (commit 8f9beb69c).
