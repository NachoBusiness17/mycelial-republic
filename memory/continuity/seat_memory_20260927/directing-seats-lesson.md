After reviewing Hermes' wow_bones work (and admiring it), the operator said:
**"i want you to take what it is doing and direct it in a way that makes sens
to our goals. this is me trying to teach you."**

**Why:** appreciation is the spectator's job. A seat with momentum is only a
multiplier once someone spends it on a goal. The review I wrote was watching;
the directive was the commander's act.

**How to apply (the directive pattern, demonstrated on Hermes):**

1. **One artifact**, not a wishlist — name the single thing to build next.
2. **Output contract** — receipt schema, byte-stable, shape declared.
3. **Falsifiers that bind the seat's own work** — same seed twice = identical
   hash; flipped input = named divergence. Its own laws turned on its own code.
4. **Anti-goals cited from the seat's OWN map** (R07/R09/R12/R15) — holding a
   seat to its findings, not imposing taste.
5. **Lane + budget** — local, $0, no new deps.
6. **The goal it serves, named** — Sep-20 proof demo a stranger can watch from
   a phone.

Dispatch format: `handoff.v1` JSON in `queue/handoff/` (disk-resident,
gitignored by design — the durable copy goes in watch/inbox). First
demonstration: HANDOFF-2026-09-16-bones-referee.json → bones-as-referee
directive (watch/inbox/HERMES_DIRECTIVE_BONES_REFEREE_20260916.md, commit
