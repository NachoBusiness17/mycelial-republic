The 1197 CE (A.H. 593) brass combination lockbox from Isfahan, held at the Museum of Fine Arts Boston (https://collections.mfa.org/objects/21956). Four two-level dials (two stacked rings per dial), 16 letter positions per ring → eight 16-position wheels → 16^8 = 4,294,967,296 = exactly 2^32. A 32-bit keyspace in brass, ~750 years before the bit. The viral tweet's "four dials vs eight dials" inconsistency IS the mechanism (two-level dials), not an error.

Operator shared it while thanking the collaboration ("working together to solve coding puzzles via our way. this has been so much fun") — it is the named touchstone for the method: count the dials, don't trust the plaque. Design shelf: combination-lock dungeon/trial room where the plaque's stated combination count is the first riddle; pairs with puzzle vault (key→plates→gate) and the riddler engram canon; candidate skin for a battle-bots squid-maze trial.
