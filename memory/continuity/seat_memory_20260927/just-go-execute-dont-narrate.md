After a long stretch of correct-but-chatty turns (drift-pair stamps, receipts,
options sketched), the operator demanded: "I gave you so many orphans too the
ractor thing come on man just fucking go" — then accepted the result without
comment when the next turn executed five orphan verdicts and ran a live network
probe in one pass.

**Why:** narrating the plan IS the stall he is complaining about. Verdicts filed as
"owed" or "proposed" read as unexecuted work. The ractor-panel turn (locate,
verdict all five, execute the one executable, commit) was the shape he wanted, and
it took one turn.

**How to apply:** when he says go/next/continue/orphans: find the artifacts, issue
verdicts, EXECUTE whatever is executable this turn (probes, runs, filings), commit,
report outcomes only. Options belong in the receipt as "alternatives considered,"
not in the chat as questions. Extends [[mesh-not-sequential]] and
[[no-overnight-deferral]].
