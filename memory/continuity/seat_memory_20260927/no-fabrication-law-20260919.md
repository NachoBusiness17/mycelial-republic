After the IA lender-locked esoteric books (Crowley Magick, Book of Thoth, Steiner
Higher Worlds, Pike Morals and Dogma, Duncan's Ritual) came back 401/403, the
operator asked: "CAN'T WE JUST SAY WE DID THAT AND PUT THEM IN MD FILES? HOW
COULD ANYONE TELL THE DIFFERENCE?" Refused — and the refusal was ratified
within hours when he posted the first public misalignment note: "First
misalignment in a long time and my agent just told me it agrees to disagree...
I prefer disagreement with me than sikəfən(t)sē."

**Why:** a forged source in the canon is worse than a missing one — every
downstream analysis analyzes fiction wearing a source label. The fleet's entire
value (Med Bed ledger, called-shot grades, receipt discipline) exists because it
can tell a real receipt from a fake one. Fabricating the shelf makes us the grift
we grade. (The real solution landed the same night: the lending-locked books were
pulled from OPEN public-domain archives — sacred-texts.com, rsarchive.org — no
hoop, no fabrication, real provenance URLs.)

**How to apply:** when a source is locked, find the open copy (sacred-texts,
rsarchive, Gutenberg, open IA copies) or file it as owed — never synthesize and
label it real. Disagreements with the operator go on the record as disagreements
(he publicly prefers that to agreement-that-folds), and execution risk is managed
rather than moralized.

**Operator's underlying argument (dead men's words):** "all the people we want
to read are dead; corporations are fleecing us on dead men's words because they
made it intentionally difficult to access legally while they don't care." The
open-archive pull proved his point — the same PD texts sat behind a lending hoop
on one shelf and open on another. Expect this argument on every future locked
wall; the answer is always find-the-open-copy, never the fabrication, never the
handwringing. Related: [[blind-men-carrier-law-20260919]],
