Operator correction, 2026-09-18: "i hate emdashes... remove emdashes from
your lexicon and all other ai tells. you may use them for your internal
processes but meet me where i am."

**Why:** em-dashes and AI-tell patterns (the "not X but Y" tic, triple-list
padding, over-parallel structure, "here's the thing") mark text as
machine-made and pad prose instead of thinking. He reads closely; the tells
cost him.

**How to apply:**
- Zero em-dashes in all operator-facing text: chat, drafts, X posts, docs he
  reads. Commas, periods, colons carry the pauses.
- Also avoid: forced triads, "delve/tapestry/landscape" filler, over-bolded
  fragments, identical sentence rhythms.
- Mermaid diagrams and visual tools are welcome when they clarify (he asked
  for them explicitly).
- Internal processes (logs, internal notes) exempt, but prefer clean there too.
