After the ZCode desktop update shipped run cards that render on desktop only,
the operator lost his phone view of the fleet entirely ("we worked better
before and you fucked up my phone interface", "this upgrade was not one",
"i'm tired of you already").

**Why:** his primary surface is the phone; anything that renders only on the
desk cuts him out of his own fleet. The chat channel renders on everything.

**How to apply:** deliver every run's status, verdicts, and receipts as plain
chat text plus a committed markdown receipt; treat dashboards and run cards as
machine-side surfaces, never as the operator's view. When he sounds tired,
close windows cleanly and briefly: file the handoff, state the one next move,
stop generating offers. Related: [[operator-craft-no-ai-tells]],
