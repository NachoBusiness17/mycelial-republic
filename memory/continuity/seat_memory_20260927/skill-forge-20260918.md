anecdote; (2) ground every SKILL.md in corpus pointers (ribs, receipts, canon
files) — skills without pointers are opinions; (3) shape = frontmatter

a falsifier is a mood); (4) fresh operator coinages get INTERPRETED LIVE as
READINGS marked Unproven with falsifiers — do not demand specification

everything else.
