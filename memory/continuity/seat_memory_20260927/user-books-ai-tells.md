The "I use AI to write books and have learned a ton of AI tells" Telegram note was from **Melissa Hallman** (@dotconnectinga), a reader — not the operator, and not the parable's author (who is the operator; see [[clerk-parable-cast]]). **Why:** A working author who edits AI tells for a living needed two passes at the ending and still dug the piece — that verifies the ending is load-bearing by design, not broken. And the sentence that later appeared in the author's public explanation was the plain post-edit rewrite.

**How to apply:** Treat "didn't follow the end" as a property to consider, not always a defect. For prose aimed at humans: plain sentences over performed depth. When the operator relays reader feedback, first get straight who sent it. Related: [[self-steal-deep-thinking-invocation]].
