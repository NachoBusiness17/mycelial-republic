 The Way of Initiation GA 10 

 This book is an early version of the first half of the book
 entitled Knowledge of the Higher Worlds and Its Attainment .
 The book Initiation and Its Results corresponds to the second
 half of Knowledge of the Higher Worlds and Its Attainment . 
 Chapter titles are: The Superphysical World and Its Gnosis,
 How to Attain Knowledge of the Higher Worlds, The Path of Discipleship,
 Probation, Enlightenment, Initiation, The Higher Education of
 the Soul, and, The Conditions of Discipleship. 
 Translated from the original German by Max Gysi.

 Week 25 

 Sun: Now to myself I may belong 
 Moon: And bright, broad inner light 
 Mars: In darkness of space and time. 
 Mercury: To sleep, nature beings press 
 Jupiter: To awaken depths of soul 
 Venus: And in waking, carry sunbeams 
 Saturn: Into cold winter torrents. 
 —Tr. Steiner Online Library

 Week 51 
 Sun: Into the inner Being of Man 
 Moon: Pour the riches of the senses; 
 Mars: The World-Spirit is found 
 Mercury: In the mirror image 
 Jupiter: Of human eyes,
