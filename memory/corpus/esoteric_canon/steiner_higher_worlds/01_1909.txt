 The Soul Calendar 

 Northern Hemisphere 

 Week 25 

 Sun: Now to myself I may belong 
 Moon: And bright, broad inner light 
 Mars: In darkness of space and time. 
 Mercury: To sleep, nature beings press 
 Jupiter: To awaken depths of soul 
 Venus: And in waking, carry sunbeams 
 Saturn: Into cold winter torrents. 
 —Tr. Steiner Online Library 

 Southern Hemisphere 

 Week 51 
 Sun: Into the inner Being of Man 
 Moon: Pour the riches of the senses; 
 Mars: The World-Spirit is found 
 Mercury: In the mirror image 
 Jupiter: Of human eyes, 
 Venus: From which Its power 
 Saturn: Must replenish itself. 
 —Tr. Steiner Online Library
