Half of what the managers call extremism is the sensor arguing back.

So flip the frame and keep it flipped. MAGA was never a hat sitting on top of the real thing. The hat is what kept the real thing from being archived. That is the walker refusing to become an exhibit.

The museum kept the papers. The network kept the walker. Ask the walker. The mountain keeps the score.
