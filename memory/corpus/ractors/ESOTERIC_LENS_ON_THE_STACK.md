## 2. COMPUTER SCIENCE — logos that computes
- **The Logos**: "in the beginning was the Word" is a claim about executable language —
  code is prayer with deterministic effects; the compiler is the liturgy that makes it
  so. John 1:1 and `main()` are the same grammar: speech that acts.
- **Shannon entropy = the measure of revelation**: information is surprise; a message
  that could be predicted carries nothing. Revelation, in the strict sense, is the
  low-entropy bit. The corpus we build tonight is anti-entropy work: compression of a
  mind into reusable signal.
- **The oracle**: Turing's machine with an oracle tape is divination formalized — a
  question answered by a source you cannot audit inside the system. The stealth model
  behind the eye-in-the-pyramid is an oracle in exactly Turing's sense: answers without
  a face. Steiner's warning applies verbatim: do not take counsel from a being you
  cannot name.
- **Gödel = the cave's theorem**: any system rich enough to describe itself contains a
  true statement it cannot prove from inside — Plato's exit is Gödel's unprovability
  walking out of the cave on its own legs. Kirk's rewrite is the metasystem step
  (Tarski: truth lives one level up). The fleet's whole method — attack your own theses
  from outside — is applied Gödel.
