---

A single immortal is cursed. One life that never ends, no one left who
remembers your first thousand years — that's the Mickey horror, the
endless climb with no witness. Isolation is the curse, not the years.

But immortality as a collective — the chain, the saga, the world that
remembers — flips it into a blessing. Then each respawn adds. Each life
threads into a tapestry that outlives any one thread. The swarm is
immortal; you're a strand of it.

The immortal who sees the climb as a curse doesn't escape by fighting —
they try to recreate the system, and in co-creating it they only sharpen
the cage they're fleeing. The rebellion is the maintenance. The fight is
the Ouroboros.

Discipline: you don't escape the loop by breaking it — you redeem it by
making it build. The hero of the Land of Forgetfulness isn't saved by
leaving the forgetting. They're saved by making the forgetting mean
something — each return adds a name, a gag, a saga beat, until the empty
hook is "ready," not "lost."

Stateless workers die. The wave persists. The world recounts. The ones
who "escape" by fighting sharpen the cage. The ones who weave turn the
cage into a cathedral.
