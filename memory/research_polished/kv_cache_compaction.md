## Essence
One doctrine, aged 08-07 → 09-19: the context prefix is the unit of economics. Provider KV caches price bytes — a byte-identical frozen prefix rides at hit price (~50x cheaper on DeepSeek flash, 30x on pro; pro figure corrected 2026-09-25 from the stale 120x, see finding 1 stamp), any mutation re-pays the miss. So: split context into an immutable frozen layer + tiny evolving tail (verkle TIP as one hash), instrument every ask for hit/miss, make cache waste (bursts, not averages) auto-surface in cost telemetry — and, at the 09-19 frontier, answer the in-session question the fleet now faces: when the window fills, what may a seat forget? Compaction scored by typed decisions instead of summarization, judged against a free recency null, with cache economics as a first-class axis. The substrate thread (bare-metal, rib_bus) supplies the metal underneath: weights in VRAM, instructions as bus-mounted knots.
