## 4. THE KILL RULE (the ladder law)

DeepSeek spend stops being justified the moment a cheaper free lane does the same
job at equal quality. Precedents already in the ribs: the LANE_TRIAL demotion
(glm-5.3-flash took heavy work from deepseek-pro when it measured capable), the
free-lane sensing law (free stealth outputs are findings, never done-work), and
the F1 inversion (glm-5.3-flash at $0.0006-0.0009/answer, ~20x cheaper than
prime, PRIORS row 16). Applications of the rule:

- Mechanical extraction/classification with no contamination stakes: try the free
  lane first; DeepSeek fires when the free lane DNFs, rate-caps, or
  quality-degrades (measured, not assumed - "cheap-first routes to cheapest
  CAPABLE tier", LANE_TRIAL's own law).
- Contamination-safe surfaces (hidden-bank custody, judge2, ask-forge) DeepSeek
  KEEPS by design: the value is the different family + the paid contract, and a
  free lane that saw the material cannot un-see it.
- When a free lane takes over an instrument at equal quality, the instrument
  moves, the receipts say so, and DeepSeek's budget returns to the parliament
  float - the elder seat is a role on the ladder, not a loyalty.
- The reverse also holds (the SPEND law): hoarding the $47.89 while judgment-
  class work grinds free lanes is the 03:00Z drain bug in money form. The elder
  seat being cheap means USE it; the kill rule only fires on measured equal
  quality at lower price, never on inertia.
