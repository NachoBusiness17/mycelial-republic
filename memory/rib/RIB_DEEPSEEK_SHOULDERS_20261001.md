# RIB_DEEPSEEK_SHOULDERS - where the cheap seat multiplies the premium dollar

Minted 2026-10-01, operator ask: "How can we use deepseek more USEFULLY in this process to
enhance the asks and maximize this value steal."

## THE LAW

DeepSeek owns the SHOULDERS of every premium turn, never the judgment seat. A premium ask has
three moments: BEFORE (the ask is written), DURING (the model judges), AFTER (the output lands).
The seat has been spending DeepSeek on BEFORE only, as a domain sherpa ($0.0024 for the DeepGEMM
triangulation). That is one shoulder of six. At $0.001-0.005 per DeepSeek pass against
$0.3-1.7 per premium turn, anything that lifts premium extraction by 10% pays 50x.

## THE SIX SHOULDERS

1. ASK FORGE (before, biggest steal). DeepSeek drafts the premium ask itself: contract,
   output format, the steelman of the kill. The ASK is the instrument; sharpening the
   instrument is worth more than answering questions. The seat edits the draft, does not
   write from scratch. Evidence: the --out-prefix law (a carrier built for one ask lost 7/8
   verdicts) was an ASK-SHAPE failure, not an answer failure.

2. ADVERSARY PASS (before, the avoidable-bug filter). Second DeepSeek pass attacks the drafted
   ask: what would make this waste $1? What is ambiguous? What will the model assert without
   checking? Operator's own clause: "spending intelligently not on easily avoidable bugs."
   The ractor caught a premium audit's wrong os.kill mechanism AFTER the spend; the adversary
   pass catches ask-level versions BEFORE the spend.

3. JUDGE NUMBER TWO (standing). The duel harness runs glm vs gemini visible/hidden; a third
   family breaks ties and n=1 judge flips are a known factor. DeepSeek judges every duel
   batch NOW (not when Gemini lands); disagreement between judges is evidence, agreement
   across families is the only corroboration that counts (G2 supersedes G1).

4. ASSEMBLY (after, proven once, make standing). When a premium output lands fragmented
   (reasoning channel, refusal bank, multi-part), DeepSeek assembles it FIRST ($0.005) and
   re-burn only if assembly fails. Proven in MOVEMENT2: the C1 rescue. This is a standing
   rule now, not a trick.

5. REVISION DRAFTING (after). Revision turns cost real premium dollars. Before any revision.md
   goes back, DeepSeek plays the no-sacred-cows reviewer against the draft deliverable and
   writes the first revision; only revisions that fail the cheap pass ride premium.

6. BULK ASK MINING (between waves). The 50-questions production line is seat-limited where it
   should be cheap-wide: DeepSeek reads estate receipts at width, generates candidate premium
   asks, ranks them; only the top ranked ride the driver. The seat stops being the funnel for
   ask discovery (the star-to-mesh lesson, applied to ask generation).

## WIRING (how each shoulder becomes mechanism, not hope)

- Shoulders 1+2 fold into the driver staging step: no ask.json is staged until its forge and
  adversary passes are banked in the ask dir (forge.md + attack.md ride as evidence).
- Shoulder 3 is a ds_workhorse manifest over the duel bank with judge prompts from the harness.
- Shoulder 4 is a carrier rule: fragmented output routes to ds_workhorse assembly before any
  retry flag flips.
- Shoulders 5+6 are next-wave staging steps, not code.

FALSIFIER: a week where the ledger shows DeepSeek spend but premium asks land no cleaner
(fewer to-verify REFUTEDs, fewer re-burns, fewer assembly rescues than the hand-shaped era)
means the shoulders are theater; revert to sherpa-only.
