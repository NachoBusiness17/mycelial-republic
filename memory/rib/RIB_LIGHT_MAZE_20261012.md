# RIB_LIGHT_MAZE (2026-10-12, operator law, verbatim-seeded)

**The operator's coinage, context:** after the hot-lattice reader fired and the cache-hit/door insight landed, his words: "This is literally the light maze concept. You can see until the edge of what exists within the known values, so therefore you can get right up to the edge keeping it cheap, yet then scheduling the thing that exists outside of the known value on a free lane or a frontier, whatever works. It's a no sacred cows situation."

## The law

1. **The known values are lit corridors** - hot weights in VRAM, cached environments, banked receipts, existing organs, answered questions. Movement inside the lit region is nearly free and fully visible.
2. **Ride the light to the edge.** Work stays in the lit region as long as the work actually lives there. Cache-hit cost, organ reuse, prior-art first.
3. **Never open doors blindly.** The thing that lives OUTSIDE the known values (a cold model, an unanswered question, an unexplored path) is not paid for in-place - it is scheduled onto whatever cheap lane exists for darkness: free lanes, flash tiers, frontier models, overnight miners. The edge is explored at exploration prices.
4. **Dark work returns as light.** When the frontier lane answers, the result enters the known values - the maze grows one corridor. Every run must grow the lit area; a run that does not is either waste (kill it) or exploration (and exploration rides free lanes, never premium ones).
5. **No sacred cows.** No lane loyalty, no model loyalty, no throne. "Free lane or frontier, whatever works" - the route is empirical, decided per unit of work by where the light is.

## What it unifies (it is the meta-law; these are its faces)

- **Driving ladder / tier ladder** - judgment climbs only = stay lit, escalate only at the edge.
- **The Descent Protocol** - the prior-art sweep is light-maze step 2: never re-enter a lit corridor with a premium ask.
- **JIT fine-tuning law** - deliver light to the edge exactly when the model is thinking there.
- **The hot lattice** - the VRAM lit map, read live (scripts/hot-lattice.ts).
- **Self-steal** - the residue banking grows the maze between runs; the light maze explains WHY self-steal has value.
- **The frontier lanes (frontier-mine/hr/researcher, overnight miners)** - the estate's professional dark-walkers; they come back carrying light.
- **GSTD proof-of-inference** - settlement pays cells for HOLDING light honestly; reputation is the memory of who kept their corridor lit.

## The measurable form

Lit-area growth per run: known values banked per token spent. Instruments that already meter it: the self-steal meter, consensus verdict rates, the reputation ledger, receipt counts. A lane is earning its keep iff the lit area grows per token on it.

## The organ it calls for (next rung, not yet built)

**The light-maze router:** a pre-flight check per unit of work - is this inside the known values (hot lattice read + corpus/receipt search)? If yes: run in-place at cache-hit cost. If no: dispatch to a free/frontier lane and bank the return. The router is the tier ladder made autonomous.

## Falsifier

The law dies if routing by the light maze costs MORE than blind premium routing over a month (metered: tokens + doors opened per completed task), or if any premium lane completes work that a free lane had already lit.
