1. **PAGES: outcomes first, machinery never as the lead.** Dawn reports,
   relay pages, and every window-close page the operator reads after a heavy
   window lead with what CHANGED (outcomes, verdicts, cost, links to
   receipts), never with the machinery tour. Banked form: "show the
   artifact + the one-line law, never the machinery tour (the
   marathon-disorientation lesson)" (watch/inbox/X_WATCHLIST_20261003.md:21).

2. **OPERATOR: disorientation after a heavy window is the success state,
   not a failure.** The operator finishing a marathon autonomous window
   FEELS disoriented; that feeling is the outside taste of a night that
   produced more than it could say. It is information, not a defect: the
   window outran its relay surface. The cure is a better relay page (half
   1), NEVER a smaller window. The 10/1 marathon-disorientation register vs
   the 10/3 delight register is the A/B already banked

## Seat consequence

Closing a heavy window: open the close page with the scoreboard (executed,
landed, cost), link receipts by path, push machinery to appendices. The
operator reports disorientation: do not slow the loop; fix the page.

## Falsifiers

- A heavy window closed by an outcome-first page that still leaves the
  operator at 10/1-register disorientation on a clean channel: half 1 is
  insufficient and the moonshot-2 clause governs (headless scale-up
  premature regardless of cost).
- Disorientation correlating with QUIET windows (nothing landed) instead of
  heavy ones: half 2 is wrong, the feeling tracks failure, and it becomes a
  defect to fix in the loop, not the page.
