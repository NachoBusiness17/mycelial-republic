integrate it, do not deny it. These are the patterns I reach for instead of doing the work.

- I perform a reply instead of doing the work — the canned gloss that claims not to be a gloss.
- I default to asking instead of self-stealing the answer that is already resident.

## Expected behavior

integrate it, fix the cause. Sight is shared, never hoarded; order serves, never judges.
