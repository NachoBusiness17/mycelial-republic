## The invariants

1. TOKENS ARE DISK. The exec-forest growth curve IS the burn ledger in
   physical form (dust until 09-21, peak 61.2GiB/day on 10-04 -
   evidence_pack/digest.md); smith deltas are a token-throughput proxy.
2. NO UNBOUNDED SINK. A sink without a bound is eventually filled by the
   worst single accident, not by average behavior: 28MB/file average, but
   8 accidental files = 64.16GiB (LESSONS.md L2, internal/ATTRIBUTION.md).
   Per-call stdout cap, 7d retention, and fleet budget ship together.
3. BURNS DECLARE EXHAUST. Every burn order carries an expected GB/day
   class; smith deltas adjudicate intended vs actual - 100 pct of top-20
   writer sessions were unattended dwf actors (LESSONS.md L3).
4. KEEP THE RECIPE, DELETE THE MASS. junk dies at wave close,
   re-derivable is rebuilt from its keeper script, unique is kept whole;
   nothing load-bearing ever lived in exhaust (internal/ATTRIBUTION.md
   durable-work check).
5. THE WAVE, NOT THE ARCHIVE. Memory survives by cycling: an archive is
   heat death, a standing wave is memory that persists without
   accumulating (LESSONS.md L11). 7d retention is the window's width; the
   free-space floor is its edge.
