## The law

The minimal recursive self-improvement loop has four organs, and the operator holds
all four at personal scale while the estate holds them at fleet scale - one blueprint:

1. NOTHING LOST (the seed law): every change passes versioning before it exists.
   Personal: precommit hooks. Fleet: verkle knots, residual + registry + leaf, the
   filed workday (docs/DNA.md). The commit chain IS the shared seed every self boots.
2. DETECTION AT THE CHANGE BOUNDARY: a pitfall exists the moment a commit fixes,
   reverts, or fixups it. The commit graph is a pitfall detector running at cadence.
   Fleet: burn verdicts, gap registers, the ratchet.
3. FEEDING IS TIMED DELIVERY, NOT ACCUMULATION (the JIT law): fine-tuning = the
   pitfall-bypass corpus compressed into weights, arriving mid-thinking at zero
   context cost; ribs/receipts-at-boot = the same food served live. Same mechanism,
   different serving time; at small scale serving-live wins (GPS verdict), at scale
   compression pays.
4. THE FOLD: nightly, the accumulated delta improves the base. Personal: diff ->
   base model. Fleet: dawn reports, the night burn, state_snapshot, improve --once.
   A commit is an (intent label, delta) pair - the cleanest SFT trace boundary; this
   is the mechanism MASS measured (fine-tune on own traces) and the operator named.

## Controls (the riders that keep it honest)

- Dead-salmon curation before any fold: reverts and fixups are pitfalls detected but
  NOT bypassed - compost, never training food. No phantom pitfalls in the corpus.

## Falsifiers

- Recurring-pitfall rate must trend down week over week, or the mesh is a diary, not
  a self (instrument: count in the diffs).

  on held-out pitfalls, or the JIT framing overclaims (it is distillation wearing a
  clock).
