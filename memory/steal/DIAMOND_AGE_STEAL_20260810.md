# DIAMOND AGE STEAL (2026-08-10) — the pedagogy layer for the Republic/epic
Researcher: ghost · Source: Neal Stephenson, *The Diamond Age, or A Young Lady's Illustrated Primer*
(1995) — mapped to our knowledge engine, the epic, the flock, governance, and the ghost server.

## The one-line
The Diamond Age is the missing PEDAGOGY: the Primer doesn't teach by dumping knowledge — it shapes a
sovereign mind by telling an interactive story that ADAPTS to the individual's real life. That's what
our knowledge engine becomes when the Republic directs it: a PRIMER, not a database.

7. POST-SCARCITY FEED vs the MIND = our $0 backend (feed) is necessary but not sufficient; the VALUE is
   the primer (the sovereign mind), and the question is who gets it.

## The tesuji (the steal)
PEDAGOGY BY SHAPED STORY: don't inform — immerse. The Primer gives Nell a narrative whose obstacles
mirror her real life, so she PRACTICES solving them; learning by living, not by reading. The Republic's
epic is a PRIMER: it adapts to each individual's actual history and trains the mind by interactive
immersion — the "smarter than all doctors" ideal in tutor form, and it must be DEMOCRATIZED (Second
Primer), not restricted. The feed ($0 backend) is cheap; the primer (sovereign mind) is the point.
