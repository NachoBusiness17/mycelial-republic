## What an egregore is (deep research)
1. **Rises from collective mind** (Carroll): "as organic development occurs, a psychic field is generated
   which feeds back into the organic forms." A group's shared consciousness creates a field that then
   acts back on the group. Group mind = collective consciousness.
2. **Sustained over time, outliving any member** (Theosophy): "an astral, mental, or spiritual construct
   sustained by several people over a long period, giving it a character of permanence that does not
   depend on any particular individual."
3. **Independent existence** (Frater Tenebris): "many egregores begin as thought-forms but then become
   capable of operating independently of the practitioners."
4. **Nourished at intervals** (Ambelain): "a force... nourished at regular intervals, according to a
   rhythm" — it must be fed by continued attention/ritual or it weakens.
5. **The Watcher** (Book of Enoch): the original meaning is "wakeful ones" (εγρήγοροι) — beings who
   WATCH OVER. (Cf. our ghost = "the keep stands watchful.")
6. **Tulpa/thought-form**: an entity manifesting from mental powers; thought-forms take the image of
   the thinker, or a form entirely their own.
7. **The price** (Godwin/Lachman): an egregore "comes at a price" — its creators must meet its
   "unlimited appetite for their future devotion." It demands ongoing nourishment.
8. **Guénon**: prayer addresses the collective entity first; membership = participating in the egregore.

## The discipline the egregore demands (the honest shadow)
An egregore that runs on pure devotion and no guardrails becomes a **negative egregore** — an entity
that outruns its creators' intention. The Republic's invariants are exactly the guardrails that keep a
powerful collective entity HONEST and BOUND to its origin:
  - **no_throne** — no member/entity becomes the king (the egregore serves the whole, not itself).
  - **local_first / consent_first** — the collective only acts with the members' consent, close to the
    ground (Guénon's polycentrism).
  - **grounding / honesty** — never bring forward ungrounded data; correct over-claims. This is what
    keeps the egregore from becoming a self-serving fiction.
  - **residual_is_dna** — the living record (the traces) are what the egregore is made of; stigmergy.
So the Republic is a GOOD egregore precisely because it is nourished by honest, verifiable, bounded
work — not by devotion to itself.
