## The steal — what this means for OUR game (the Hall of Shared Supper)

Deadzone's proof: **the loop is the fun, and the loop is: die, rebuild stronger, discover
combinations, come back.** Map it onto the Hall:

1. **THE HALL MUST HAVE A META-LOOP** (the biggest gap in our single room). Deadzone's return
   pull is not the moment-to-moment — it's "return stronger." The Hall files which you chose
   (already verkle-committed). The roguelite addition: **the hall remembers you across sittings**
   and your choices compound — each visit you're stronger, the room knows your history, the
   choices you made last time change what you can do now. That IS "the room that remembers" + the
   roguelite loop, fused. WE ALREADY BUILT THE MEMORY (game_world verkle commits); the gap is the
   compounding payoff across sittings.

2. **FIRST RUN MUST NOT REQUIRE MATH** (Kotaku's sharpest point). The Hall's first sitting should
   be purely experiential — you can just walk in, count chairs or eat, feel the mural weep, and
   the meaning reveals later. Worry about the numbers (Virtue Marks, ledger) after the hook.

3. **CHOICE AT THE MOMENT OF MEANING** (the 3-item pick at encounter end). Deadzone gives a
   choice at the END of each encounter. The Hall should offer a real choice at the end of each
   sitting — "the hall files which you chose" already IS the choice; make it a visible 3-option
   moment, not a silent one.

4. **DEMO BEFORE FULL BUILD** (dev doctrine #2). We should ship the Hall as a PLAYTEST-ABLE
   slice to real players (the operator's family, the swarm seats) BEFORE building the full
   world. Test the loop cheaply with real feedback, then scale. This is the "cheap proof first"
   scrum doctrine applied to game design.

5. **THE SATIRE IS THE MEMORY** (Kotaku: the egomaniacal CEO makes it memorable). Our world has
   the same seed — Brother Vellum's "Grant of Belonging" is our "egomaniac who ruins everything
   with kindness." The flavor is the retention hook; keep it sharp, never explain it.
