## Swiss (International Typographic Style) mapped onto that

- Grid = the mudlib / RIB. Not decoration. Muller-Brockmann: the grid is the constitution of the page.
- Hierarchy by scale, not boxes. Room title is the poster. Metadata is 11px caps. We had inverted Swiss: tiny narrative, candy frames.
- Objectivity: the room bytes are the photograph. Do not illustrate a keep that is not in the module.
- Reduction: cut until the six frames are type on a grid. Hairline, not card-in-card. Hermes DESIGN already said flat/not boxed -- the wave ignored it.
- One accent: down seats in red. Verified/up is mute. Gold chrome was ornament.
- Flush left, ragged right. Sans (Neue Haas / Helvetica / system grotesk).
- Modular: unit, quest, room, who, log, bar -- same cells as WoW vanilla, Swiss-skinned.
