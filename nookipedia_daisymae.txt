'''Daisy Mae''' is a [[boar]] who debuted in {{NH}}. She visits the [[player]]'s [[Town|island]] every Sunday in order to sell [[turnip]]s. She takes over the role established by [[Joan]], her grandmother, in previous games.

==Characteristics==
===Appearance===
Daisy Mae is an orange boar, who wears a blue dress with a red bow and a white hood, with similar pound-sign patterns to Joan's work pants. She has a drip of blue mucus running from her nose. She has black eyes shaped like ovals, brown hair, large red cheeks, and a basket with turnips on her head. Her hands and feet are brown.

===Personality===
Daisy Mae likes selling turnips, just like her grandmother, Joan. However, she also has a bad habit of eating the turnips she is selling, because they taste too good to her. She also appears to sneeze a lot.

==Appearances==
===In {{NH|short|nolink}}===
[[File:Daisy Mae NH 2.png|100px|left|thumb|Daisy Mae in {{NH|short|nolink}}.]]
In {{NH}}, Daisy Mae visits the player's [[town|island]] every Sunday once [[Nook's Cranny]] is built, arriving at 5 AM and leaving at 12 PM. She sometimes indirectly mentions her grandmother, Joan, whom she refers to as "Gram-Gram." If the following requirements have been met, Daisy Mae [[letter|mail]]s the player three {{I|bamboo shoot|NH}}s the day after any turnip purchase:
*Buy turnips from her on 3 different weeks.
*Buy an aggregate total of at least 100 turnips.

With the {{NHVer|2.0|full}}, Daisy Mae also mails the player one of the following [[DIY|cooking recipes]] after they have purchased some turnips:
*{{I|Bamboo-shoot soup|NH}}
*{{I|Jarred bamboo shoots|NH}}
*{{I|Kabu ankake|NH}}
*{{I|Turnip salad|NH}}
{{Clear|left}}

After [[The Roost]] is built, Daisy Mae will appear inside the cafe from 12 PM to 1 PM every Sunday. She can also appear if her [[Amiibo|amiibo]] is scanned. She can be seen eating a [[Item:Roost sablé cookie (New Horizons)|Roost sablé cookie]] as she is too young to drink coffee yet. This is shared with [[Leila]].

===In {{PC|short|nolink}}===
[[File:Daisy Mae PC icon.png|100px|left|thumb|Daisy Mae in {{PC|short|nolink}}.]]
Daisy Mae is a guest character in {{PC|short}}. She hosted the [[Garden event|Daisy Mae's Pickin' Patch]] from April 30 to May 11, 2020. In that event, the player would obtain items from the [[Farm-Fresh Veggie Patch Set]]. The included flowers in the set were Farmer's Eggplants, Farmer's Peppers, and Farmer's Tomatoes. The included creatures are [[Lilac Seedwing]], [[Orange Seedwing]], [[Silver Seedwing]], and [[Golden Seedwing]].

==Trivia==
*According to the official companion guide for {{NH|short|nolink}} by Future Press, Daisy Mae has a bad habit of eating turnips rather than selling them, much to Joan's dismay.
**Despite this, it is revealed in {{HHP|short|nolink}} that Joan expresses amusement at Daisy Mae enjoying eating turnips, and even if going overboard with turnip meals, Daisy Mae can seemingly never have enough.
*Daisy Mae's [[birthday]] was said to be [[May]] {{Date|5}} in the companion guide, and this was also included in the game's internal parameters. Since Version {{NHVer|1.1.0}}, this was changed to [[March]] {{Date|30}}, which is the date on her [[amiibo]] card. [[Wilbur]]'s birthday was also changed.
**Likewise, she currently shares her birthday with [[Felicity]].
*One of [[Isabelle]]'s quotes when asked about [[Environment rating|island evaluation]], relayed from Daisy Mae, suggests that she took over Joan's role due to her weakening knees.
