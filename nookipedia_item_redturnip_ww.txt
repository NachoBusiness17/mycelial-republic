A '''red turnip''' is an item in {{WW}}. This item can be [[Food|eaten]].

A red turnip can be obtained by planting a {{I|red turnip seed|WW}}. Once planted, it grows each day it is watered, until becoming fully grown six days after being planted. If it is not watered, it wilts. As the first step of the [[trading game]], a red turnip can be given to [[Wendell]] to receive either a {{I|turban|WW}} or a {{I|country guitar|WW}}.
