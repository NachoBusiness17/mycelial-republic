'''Spoiled turnips''' are an item in {{NH}} and are considered one of the various [[trash]] items alongside the {{I|boot|New Horizons}}, {{I|empty can|New Horizons}}, {{I|old tire|New Horizons}}, {{I|used fountain firework|New Horizons}}, and any [[lost item]]s. If left on the ground, spoiled turnips will attract [[ant]]s and [[fly|flies]]. As a trash item, it is penalized by the [[Happy Home Academy]] if inside a [[player house|house]].

Spoiled turnips generate if the player did not sell their {{I|turnips|New Horizons}} before 5 AM on Sunday or the player time-traveled backwards. It cannot have any price fluctuation and will only sell for {{Currency|Bells|100}} at [[Nook's Cranny]].
