'''Spoiled turnips''' are an item in {{WW}} and are considered one of the various [[trash]] items alongside the {{I|boot|Wild World}}, {{I|empty can|Wild World}}, and {{I|old tire|Wild World}}. If left on the ground, spoiled turnips will attract [[ant]]s and [[fly|flies]].

Spoiled turnips generate if the player did not sell their {{I|turnips|WW}} before 6 AM on Sunday or the player time-traveled backwards. It cannot have any price fluctuation and will sell for {{Currency|Bells|0}}.
