'''[[Turnips]]''' are an item in {{NH}}. Up to 10 bundles of 100 can be stacked together, for a total of 1,000 turnips. These items are [[food|edible]] and by eating them, the player will earn energy to be able to relocate [[tree]]s or break [[rock]]s. The player's energy will be maxed out as a result, and the player cannot eat further turnips until an energy is used on a rock or a tree.

Turnips can be obtained from [[Daisy Mae]] on Sundays from 5 AM to 12 PM for between {{Currency|Bells|90}} and {{Currency|Bells|110}} per turnip. In the {{HHP|short}} DLC, the player can also obtain turnips by speaking to [[Joan]] in the [[hospital]] [[facility]] every Monday. On Mondays through Saturdays, they can be sold at [[Nook's Cranny]] for between {{Currency|Bells|9}} and {{Currency|Bells|660}} per turnip; there are two sell prices each day, and it changes at 12 PM. If they are not sold, they turn into {{I|spoiled turnips|NH}} at 5 AM on the next Sunday.
