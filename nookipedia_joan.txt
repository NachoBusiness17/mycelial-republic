'''Joan''', also known as '''Sow Joan''', is a [[boar]] in the {{SER}} who visits the [[player]]'s [[town]] each Sunday morning to sell [[turnip]]s. The name "Sow Joan" is a reference to the real-life stock market index, the "{{Wp|Dow Jones Industrial Average}}". She often mentions how she has been selling turnips for around sixty years.

In {{NH}}, Joan's role of selling turnips is instead fulfilled by her young granddaughter, [[Daisy Mae]]. She also mentions her son and daughter-in-law, Daisy Mae's parents, when invited to the Hotel.

==Characteristics==
===Appearance===
[[File:Joan DnM.png|thumb|100px|Artwork of Joan from {{DnM}}.]]
Joan is a brown boar who wears a blue dress with a white hood, and work pants with pound-sign patterns. She has a yellow nose, black hair, large orange cheeks, a pair of white tusks and a basket with turnips on her back. Her hands and feet are also yellow. Her eyes have beige circle linings, white rims, and black pupils shaped like diamond stars.

===Personality===
Joan likes selling turnips, sharing this trait with her granddaughter, Daisy Mae.

===Voice===
Joan is one of two characters in ''Animal Crossing'' who has a unique voice from all other characters. The other character who shares this trait is [[Farley]].

==Appearances==
===In {{PG|nolink}}===
[[File:Joan PG.png|thumb|left|100px|Artwork of Joan from {{PG|nolink}}]]
In {{DnM}} and {{PG}}, Joan appears in town every Sunday before noon, selling turnips to the player. Unlimited white turnips can be bought each week from Joan, which can be sold to Tom Nook, which will have different prices depending on the "Stalk Market." These turnips are only profitable between Monday and Saturday.

If the player travels on the [[train]], Joan can be seen sleeping in the background, occasionally waking up in surprise, before going back to sleep.
{{Clear|left}}

===In {{WW|short|nolink}} and {{CF|short|nolink}}===
[[File:WW Joan.png|thumb|Joan selling turnips in {{WW|nolink}}]]
In {{WW}}, she continues to sell turnips, though she now also sells [[red turnip]] seeds for 1,000 [[Bell]]s which can be planted in the ground and must be watered each day so they can grow to their full potential and later sold to [[Tom Nook]] for profit. Only one red turnip can be sold to the player each week. Her appearance in {{CF}} is functionally identical to {{WW|nolink}}.

===In {{NL|short|nolink}}===
[[File:NL Joan.jpg|thumb|left|Joan selling turnips in {{NL|nolink}}]]
In {{NL}}, Joan no longer sells red turnips. However, she will let the player buy more than 990 turnips, unlike {{WW|short|nolink}} and {{CF|short|nolink}}. Joan can be asked to explain her business and turnips. She wishes not to explain how it works, but will not complain if prompted.
{{Clear|left}}

===In {{NH|short|nolink}}===
[[File:NH Joan Mention.jpg|thumb|A quote for a three-star island from Daisy Mae in {{NH|nolink}} that mentions Joan]]
Joan is sometimes indirectly mentioned by her granddaughter, Daisy Mae, who refers to her as "Gram-Gram." Additionally, {{I|Joan's poster|NH}} can be obtained from [[Nook Shopping]] after inviting her to [[Harv's Island|Photopia]] via her [[amiibo]]. Since the {{NHVer|2.0}} Update, Joan can visit [[The Roost]]. She will bring Daisy Mae along if invited to The Roost. She can also be scanned into Photopia via her amiibo card.

In {{HHP}}, she can be found in the [[hospital]] every Monday. If talked to, she will give the player turnips for free. Joan will have a 50% chance of giving the player 30 turnips, 30% chance of giving the player 50 turnips, and 20% chance of giving the player 100 turnips.

===In {{aF|short|nolink}}===
[[File:aF Joan Visit Sunday.png|thumb|left|Joan visiting town on a Sunday to sell turnips in {{aF|nolink}}]]
In the [[Board Game]] mode in {{aF}}, Joan visits town each Sunday to sell turnips to the players. She also appears as a standard visitor; if a player lands on a special-event space while she is visiting, she either gives the player 10 to 30 turnips or makes them pay 1,000 Bells to "help make things right" after her turnip field was vandalized.
{{Clear}}

==Trivia==
*Joan is one of four characters in the series with a voice specific to themselves, alongside [[Farley]], [[Luna]], and [[Lloid]]. Joan's voice is much lower and gruffer than that of the rest of the female characters.
*Joan is the second character to appear in {{DnM}}, {{DnM+}}, {{PG}}, and {{DnMe+}}. She appears in the background of the train sleeping after [[K.K. Slider]] introduces the game.
*Joan shares her [[birthday]] with [[Pierce]].
