Your '''Champion''' is a [[Cards|card]] in your [[Starter Deck]] determined by the [[:Category:Clans|Primary Clan]] you choose before a run.

You will always draw your champion on the first turn of a [[Battle]].

You cannot permanently duplicate your champion, and the only way to upgrade them is through the [[Overworld Events|Dark Forge]] event found during Limbo, The Waxen Arches and Scorched Plains [[Rings]].
