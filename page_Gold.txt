'''{{PAGENAME}}''' ({{Value||Gold}}), or '''Coins''', is the [[:Category:Resource|resource]] used to buy [[Upgrades]] for [[Cards]] at [[Merchants]]. At the start of the game, the player starts with {{Value|50|Gold}}. There are several ways to collect more {{Value||Gold}} during the run:

* collect {{Value||Gold}} in [[Overworld Events]] such Forgotten Boons and Divine Boons.
* kill, or trigger extinguish of the [[Collector]] enemy.
* Play cards that generate Gold, such as [[Wickless Tycoon]], [[Petty Theft]].
* Collect {{Value||Gold}} by skipping Rewards.
* Receive {{Value||Gold}} as a [[Battle Effects|Trial Reward]].
* Receive {{Value||Gold}} in [[Concealed Caverns]] Events.

'''{{PAGENAME}}''' can also be used as a way to increase [[Score]].
