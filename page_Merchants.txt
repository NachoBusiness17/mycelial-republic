[[File:Merchant.png|thumb|The Shopkeeper]]
'''Merchants''' are an [[Overworld Events|Overworld Event]] that allows the player to spend gold in exchange for purging [[Cards]] from their deck, upgrading units or spells and obtaining [[Artifacts]].

==Prices==

Each Merchant will sell three items at a time. Their inventory can be re-rolled once per Merchant for a cost of {{Value|50|Gold}}.

Merchant prices increase at certain [[Covenant Ranks]]:
* At Covenant 11, re-roll costs 20% more. The cost becomes {{Value|60|Gold}}.
* At Covenant 16, card removal costs 20% more. The initial cost becomes {{Value|60|Gold}}, increasing each time it is used, to a maximum of {{Value|300|Gold}}.
* At Covenant 18, goods cost 20% more. At Merchants of Magic or Steel, Common upgrades cost {{Value|25|Gold}} and Rare upgrades cost between {{Value|95|Gold}} and {{Value|120|Gold}}. At Merchant of Trinkets, Artifacts cost between {{Value|195|Gold}} and {{Value|245|Gold}}.<sup>[verify]</sup>

''See: [[Upgrades]] for a list of available upgrades''

===Card Removal===

All Merchants allow removing cards from the deck. The initial cost is {{Value|50|Gold}}, increasing each time it is used, to a maximum of {{Value|250|Gold}}. The removal cost is shared by all Merchants through the run.

==Types of Merchants==

===Merchant of Steel===
[[File:Merchant of Steel.png|thumb|128px]]
The Merchant of Steel sells 2 different Common upgrades for {{Value|20|Gold}} and 1 Rare upgrade between {{Value|80|Gold}} and {{Value|95|Gold}}.

Each item is randomly selected from the possible choices. There are three Common and four Rare upgrades which may be offered in any run. There is also a Common upgrade for each clan; only the two from the player's clans can be offered, providing a total of five possible Common upgrades in a given run.

[[File:Merchant of Magic.png|thumb|128px]]

The first upgrade offered is always '''Emberstone'''. The second Common upgrade is either '''Powerstone''' or '''Surgestone'''. The Rare upgrade is random.
