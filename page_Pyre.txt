The '''Pyre''' is your main health pool. It is at the top of the Train above the three floors of the Train.

The Pyre starts with {{Value|80|Pyre Health}} at the start of the game, increasing by {{Value|30|Pyre Health}} every time you defeat a [[Enemies|Major Boss]], up to a maximum of {{Value|140|Pyre Health}}. There are also [[Artifacts]] that can affect the health of the Pyre, such as [[Precious Plating]], which increases the '''Pyre Health''' by {{Value|40|Pyre Health}}. The starting Pyre Health is further modified by the [[Covenant Ranks|Covenant Rank]]. The Pyre can lose health through being attacked or having its health sacrificed in [[Concealed Caverns|Concealed Cavern Events]] such as [[Concealed Caverns#Cave of a Thousand Eyes]].

The Pyre, while unable to be defended directly, is capable of warding off [[Enemies]]. The Pyre has a base attack of {{Value|20|Attack}} and will retaliate against all enemies in the '''Pyre Room'''. Attacks from the Pyre act as if the Pyre had Sweep, attacking all enemies in the room. After you defeat a Boss, the Pyre gains {{Value|10|Attack}}. There are also Artifacts that can affect the attack value of the Pyre, such as [[Vapor Funnel]], which decreases the attack of the Pyre by 5 in exchange for applying Dazed to enemies that enter the '''Pyre Room'''. It is unwise to rely on the Pyre's own battle stats, as damage taken by the Pyre carries over between fights.
