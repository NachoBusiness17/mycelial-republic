The '''Roundrel''' is a [[:Category:Classes|class]] introduced in v0.9.03 of ''[[Peglin (Game)|Peglin]]''. They can be unlocked by inflicting an enemy with 50 [[Spinfection]] as the [[Peglin]] and start with the unique [[Roundreloquence]] relic.<br>
Roundrel is a mix between dealing high [[Critical Peg|Critical]] damage and inflicting [[:Category:Debuffs|negative]] [[:Category:Status Effects|status effects]].
<!--first revealed in [https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1296610/view/3321980253161449390 the 1.0 roadmap].-->


==Strategy==
''NOTE: The following assumes you are starting with the Roundreloquence relic in a normal run and not a custom loadout.''
*Roundrel naturally inflicts a random status effect whenever it attacks. While this sometimes has minimal influence, it can be highly effective when spread across multiple enemies. Be sure to take piercing Orbs and/or such as [[Sphear]] and [[Prime Rod of Frost]] to affect as many enemies as possible.
**Piercing Orbs that apply status effects such as [[Cauldron Bubball]] and [[Go For The Icircle]] are ideal for this play.
*[[Mask of Sorrow]] is a recommended relic should it be offered, as it plays to the effects of Roundrel perfectly and works with just about every strategy it can be used in.
*A full [[Spinfection]] build is easily doable as Roundrel with Orbs such as [[Spinfectious Sting]] and [[Toil and Trouball]], plus the relic [[Grubby Gloves]].
*Roundrel is well suited to making a [[Blind]] build that is highly effective against bosses. Be sure to use Orbs such as [[Darkness Eterball]] and relics such as [[Sand Arrows]] to get the most benefit.
*Even if you don't have an Orb that applies either Blind or Bramble, [[Branch of Ember]] is a good take, as it can sometimes deal both status effects at once.

==Trivia==
*Its name is a portmanteau of "round" and "scoundrel".

==================== Haglin ====================

'''Haglin''' is a character that the player can meet in a [[Haglin Scouting|special event]]. They will remark about working on setting up a [[shop]], and offer the player a "free sample"; allowing them to upgrade or remove an [[:Category:Orbs|orb]], or do neither and continue with their journey.

They will run the [[shop]] in the future, as evidenced by the [[Haglin Scouting#Transcript|event dialogue]] and roadmap.<ref name="Roadmap">https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1296610/view/3321980253161449390</ref>


==Scary Haglin ==
In the [[Scary_Haglin|scary haglin]] [[:Category:Events|event]], the player can meet "haglin" and must upgrade or remove an [[:Category:Orbs|orb]] at the cost of health.

This option cannot be skipped, unlike in the [[Haglin Scouting|regular event]].

==================== Encounters ====================
'''Encounters''' are the main content of ''[[Peglin (Game)|Peglin]]'', and house various [[:Category:Enemies|enemies]], [[events]], and [[Chest|chests]] that the player can find.

[[File:Map.png|none|thumb|''The [[map]] for the [[Forest]]''|link=Map]]

Each location on the [[map]], officially called a 'node' in the game, is considered an encounter, which can be divided into one of five categories:
*[[#Fight|Fight]]
*[[#Miniboss|Miniboss]]
*[[#Boss|Boss]]
*[[#Mystery|Mystery]]

==Fight==
Regular skulls mark an [[:Category:Enemies|enemy]] encounter, and will spawn a random group of said [[:Category:Enemies|enemies]] as well as generate a [[Pegboard|board]] from the enemy pool.

[[File:Enemy_map.png|none|thumb|''The [[map]] icon for the an [[enemy]] encounter''|link=Enemies]]

==Miniboss==
Red Skulls mark a [[:Category:Minibosses|miniboss]] encounter. These house strong, unique enemies that will give the player a challenge. Additionally, completing such an encounter grants the player one of two [[:Category:Relics|relics]] to choose from.

[[File:Elite_map.png|none|thumb|''The [[map]] icon for a [[miniboss]] encounter''|link=Miniboss]]

==Boss==
'''[[:Category:Bosses|Boss Fights]]''' will always appear at the very end of each area, and have their own unique icon to denote which boss will spawn. Defeating a boss lets the player choose one of three [[:Category:Boss Relics|boss relics]].

[[File:Slime_boss_map.png|none|thumb|''The [[map]] icon for the [[Slime Boss]]''|link=Slime Boss]]

==Mystery==
'''Mystery''' spaces are denoted a question mark (?) on the map, and will be a random [[Events|event]], [[:Category:Enemies|enemy]] encounter, or [[chest]]. This is the only encounter icon that can result in [[Events]].

In [[Cruciball]] 2 or higher, [[:Category:Minibosses|miniboss]] can appear as well.

[[File:Mystery_map.png|none|thumb|''The [[map]] icon for a mystery encounter''|link=Events]]

==Chest==
'''[[Chest|Chests]]''' are marked by the chest icon, and will give the player a random [[:Category:Relics|relic]] that can aid their journey. The player can also choose to skip the relic if they want.

[[File:Chest_map.png|none|thumb|''[[Chest|Chests]] as they appear on the [[map]]''|link=Chest]]

The '''Balladin''' is a [[:Category:Classes|class]] introduced in v0.9.03 of ''[[Peglin (Game)|Peglin]]''. They can be unlocked by ending a battle with 100+ [[Ballwark]] as the [[Peglin]] and start with the unique [[Balladroit]] relic and a [[Sphear]].<br>
Balladin centers on high power and high defense, with Orbs and relics that increase [[Muscircle]] or add [[Ballwark]].
<!--first revealed in [https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1296610/view/3321980253161449390 the 1.0 roadmap].-->

==Strategy==
{{Strategy}}

''NOTE: The following assumes you are starting with the Balladroit relic in a normal run and not a custom loadout.''
*[[:Category:Orbs|Orbs]] that add Muscircle and Ballwark while in flight, such as [[Renewed Vigorb]] and [[Protectorb]], are better options than those that add their buff when fired, as they will also add the other buff for every instance of the first being added.
*It is possible to use a much riskier self-damage strategy as the Balladin, assuming you have either sufficient Ballwark generation and/or healing in the form of [[Popping Corn]].
*[[Orbust]] adds both Muscircle and Ballwark when fired, effectively enabling it to add both additional Muscircle and Ballwark for Balladin.
*Balladin does not favour [[Critical Peg|Critical Pegs]]. If offered, taking the [[Unpretentious Pendant]] is a big power spike, especially at [[Cruciball]] 3.
*[[Spiral Slayer]] gives both Muscircle and Ballwark at the start of battles, making it an effective relic to choose if offered at the start of the game.
*If you have good Muscircle or Ballwark generation, [[Training Weight]] and [[Essential Oil]] should be considered, as they will increase the production of their respective buff.
**[[Mask of Joy]] serves the same purpose, but for both Muscircle and Ballwark.
*Orbs that gain power from Ballwark such as [[Irresponsiball]] and [[Best Orbfence]] are good options for Muscircle builds, since they can use the additional Ballwark to be made even stronger.
*A build made around [[Shield Peg|Shield Pegs]] can be amazingly effective as Balladin, especially if combined with the relics [[Ballpeen Hammer]] and/or [[Fiery Furnace]].
*[[Brassicaceae Knuckles]] can form the basis for an effective [[Refresh Peg]] build with Balladin. Adding to it with Orbs and relics like [[Refreshircle]] and [[Leaf The Rest For Later]] can make this a potent strategy.

==Trivia==
*Its name is a portmanteau of "ball" and "paladin".

'''Peglintuition''' is the [[:Category:Starter Relics|starter relic]] for [[Peglin]].

It makes battles and chests have an additional option to choose from (similar to the [[Eye of Turtle]]), and was added to "help reduce the inherent randomness of having access to many different orbs and relics<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1296610/view/3488629295080671192</ref>

==Tips==
*In terms of relic rewards, [[Chest|Chests]] become more valuable than [[:Category:Minibosses|Minibosses]] due to a similar reward without the risk of losing health. However, chest rewards will not contain any [[Gold]] or [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]], and minibosses can potentially provide healing (e.g. if you have a [[Doctorb]] and/or [[:Category:Health Relics|health relics]]).


==Trivia==
*Its name is a portmanteau of "Peglin" and "Intuition"
