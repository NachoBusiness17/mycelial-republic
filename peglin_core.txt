==================== Shop ====================
The '''Shop''' is run by [[Haglin]], and allows players to buy and remove [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]], and purchase [[:Category:Relics|relics]] using [[gold]]. There are always 5 orbs, 4 relics and 1 orb removal to choose from.

Orb costs are based on their rarity, with [[:Category:Common Orbs|common orbs]] costing 15, [[:Category:Uncommon Orbs|uncommon orbs]] costing 25 and [[:Category:Rare Orbs|rare orbs]] costing 35, with relics always costing 50. The orb removal starts at 30, and increases with 15 each time it's used.

The [[Pegboard|board]] layout after the shop will change depending on what you buy, with each [[:Category:Pegs|peg]] cluster representing the item in the shop menu, removed when the item is bought.

The '''Peg''' (or '''Standard Peg''') is the base variant of a [[:Category:Pegs|peg]], and has no special effects on its own. [[:Category:Orbs|Orbs]] can [[bounce]] off of it and accumulate damage equal to their base damage stat (maximum of 999 per peg).

[[Critical Peg|Critical]] and [[Refresh Peg|refresh]] pegs will sometimes override standard pegs on the [[Pegboard|board]].

[[Bomb]] pegs will spawn alongside standard pegs, and occasionally change their position on the [[Pegboard|board]].

==================== Cruciball ====================
{{Spoiler}}

'''Cruciball''' is a game mode akin to the ascension system from other roguelike games. Each of the 20 levels adds more hazards, such as increasing [[:Category:Enemies|enemy]] health, changing [[:Category:Areas|map generation]], etc.

Players must beat the [[Peglin (Game)|game]] to unlock the mode, and then beat the game with the previous level to unlock the next level (e.g. beating the game on Cruciball 1 will unlock Cruciball 2).

==================== Gold ====================
{{Item
|image=[[Gold.png]]
|description=Used to buy things from the [[shop]] and after battles
}}

'''Gold''' (often referred to as "Coins") is a [[:Category:Shop|currency]] that is earned by activating [[Coin Peg|coin pegs]]. Players use gold to purchase [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]], upgrades, and healing at the end of each battle. It can also be used to purchase [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]], [[:Category:Relics|relics]], and orb removals from [[Shop|Haglin's shop]].

Several [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] and [[:Category:Relics|relics]] will interact with Gold, and as such are categorized as [[:Category:Gold Orbs|Gold Orbs]] and [[:Category:Gold Relics|Gold relics]].
