The '''Spinterest Payment''' is an [[:Category:Uncommon Orbs|uncommon orb]] that increases its damage based on how much [[Gold]] the player has.


==Strategy==
{{Strategy}}
*You want to grind a lot of gold with the help of relics and orbs to use this orb. And of course, you'll also have to refrain from using it all!


==Trivia==
*Its name is a pun on "interest payment"

The '''Daggorb''' is one of the [[Stone|starting orbs]] that the player gets when beginning a new run with [[Peglin]] or [[Roundrel]]. It has high [[Critical Peg|critical damage]], but minimal regular damage. 

When leveled up, only the critical damage scales, while the regular damage remains the same.

{{Availability|Peglin=Available|Roundrel=Available|Balladin=Available|Spinventor=Unavailable}}

==Strategy==
{{Strategy}}
*Daggorb works best in a build that revolves around Critical Pegs and damage. Increasing the damage through [[Spinesse]] or with relics such as [[Complex Claw]] or [[Short Stack]] as well as the number of Critical Pegs with the [[Lucky Penny]] relic or [[Critiball]] are recommended.
**Generally, there are other orbs with higher Critical damage than Daggorb that better fit the role, but typically come with drawbacks that can make them risky. At the very least, Daggorb is a consistent and reliable Orb to have with no major drawbacks.
*Daggorb becomes less useful by [[Cruciball]] Level 3, when there is only one Critical Peg on the board. It may be wise to remove it if you aren't finding a use for it, therefore.
*Daggorb shares the same damage stats as [[Eviscorbate]]. However, both orbs have different targeting functions and therefore have different synergy effects. For instance, Daggorb works well if given additional piercing with relics such as [[Au Auger]] and [[Prime Rod of Frost]].

==Trivia==
*The name is a portmanteau of "dagger" and "orb".

==================== Fight ====================
A '''Fight''', '''Battle''' or '''Enemy Encounter''' is a type of [[Encounters|Encounter]] where the player will face of against various  in order to progress through the game. 
Fights are the main gameplay mechanic of the game and will test the player's skills in order to emerge victorious, and gain new additions to the player's arsenal, at the cost of potentially costing the player HP or causing a game over.

There are several types of fights: there are standard battles, [[:Category:Elite Enemies|Elite encounter]] as well as [[:Category:Bosses|Boss encounter]] encounters.

==Flow==
A battle starts with spawning in select enemies and shuffling the orbs in the player's [[Satchel]]. Then, these phases are repeated until all enemies (or the player) die:<!-- Note that these are not official names. -->

# ''Priming Phase'': During this phase, the player can [[Targeting|Target]] an enemy currently on the battlefield and aim or [[Discard]] their primed [[:Category:Orbs|orb]]. 
# ''Flight Phase'': After the orb is fired, flight phase is entered. In this phase, the orb bounces on the [[Pegboard]] until it leaves it and the orb is thrown so that it can [[Hit]] and thus [[Activate]] [[:Category:Pegs|pegs]]. During this phase, targeting is no longer possible, and certain relics start to give effects.
# ''Throwing Phase'': After the orb leaves the pegboard, it is thrown according to the orbs throw type ([[Projectile Attack|Projectile]], [[:Category:Targeting Orbs|Targeting]] or All) and deals damage to every enemy it hits.
# ''Enemy Phase'': After the orb is thrown, the enemy phase starts. Starting from the closest enemy, they will each take their actions, which can be attacking [[Peglin]] and/or moving, depending on the specific enemy. After all of that is over, if the spawning spot is empty, a new enemy from the battle queue spawns.

Once a player has exhausted all their orbs in their current orb queue, Peglin [[Reload|reloads]], where all orbs in the Satchel (except for thrown one-time use orbs) are reshuffled and a new queue is formed. When this happens, the enemies gain an additional enemy phase (2 on [[Cruciball]] 15 or higher) unless the player has [[Decoy Orb]] and it is the first reshuffle.

== Post-Battle ==
Once a battle is finished and the player emerged victorious, the post-battle rewards screen shows up. Here, the player can:

* Buy one of three new [[:Category:Orbs|Orbs]] and add it to the Satchel at the cost of 25 {{Icon|gold}} for a level 1 orb, 35 for a level 2 or 45 for a level 3. Note that, unlike [[Shop|Shops]], [[Rarity|Orb Rarity]] plays no role in the price. If the player has [[Peglintuition]] or [[Eye of Turtle]], an additional option is given for each of these relics.
* [[Upgrade]] one of the [[:Category:Orbs|Orbs]] in the player's [[Satchel]] for 25 {{Icon|gold}}. This can be done repeatedly, but the price will increase by 10 {{Icon|gold}}each upgrade.
* Heal <!-- How much? -->for 25 {{Icon|gold}}.
* If the player has [[Basalt Toadem]], increase their Max HP by 4 for 25 {{Icon|gold}}.

If the fight was an [[:Category:Elite Enemies|Elite encounter]], the player then gets to chose one of two [[:Category:Relics|Relics]] to gain, or three if the player has [[Eye of Turtle]] (here, [[Peglintuition]] does '''not''' increase the number of options), or if the fight was a [[:Category:Bosses|Boss encounter]], the player gets to choose one of 3 [[:Category:Boss Relics|Boss Relics]], or fourif the player has [[Eye of Turtle]].<!-- Is this correct? --> 

After the rewards have been choosen, the player enters [[Navigation]] mode with 5 holes at the bottom, where either the center is burning and causes Misnavigation if there are two paths, or two outermost holes are burning if there's only one path.

==================== Pebball ====================
{{Orb
|image=stone.png

The '''Pebball''' is one of the [[Daggorb|starting orbs]] that the player gets when beginning a new run. The player starts with 3 every run (4 on [[Cruciball]] 1 or higher, where they also gain +0/−1). They have no special effects, and follow standard [[Bounce|bouncing]] physics.

{{Availability|Peglin=Available|Roundrel=Start and Event Only|Balladin=Start and Event Only|Spinventor=Start and Event Only}}

==Strategy==
*Given their general sub-par stats — and how they become weaker once you start playing Cruciball — Pebballs are usually not good orbs to have and should be removed as soon as possible.
**However, taking the [[Suffer the Sling]] relic early on is a good improvement that makes Pebballs slightly better than average — albeit still inferior to the [[Sworb]].
*Having multiple Pebballs is useful if you have an [[Orbelisk]], especically Level 2 or 3 Orbelisk can make Pebballs are powerful.
==Notes==
*Aside from the [[Peglin]], Pebballs aren't offered to the player after battles and can only be obtained through events. Similarly, they also have several events in which they exclusively can be removed.
