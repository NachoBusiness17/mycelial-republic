==================== Refresh Peg ====================
{{Peg
|image=refresh_peg.png
|effect=Activates a [[Refresh]]
}}

The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a special type of [[:Category:Pegs|peg]] that refreshes all the pegs on the board when hit, not including [[bomb]] and [[Critical Peg|critical]] pegs. {{PAGENAME}}s do not contribute to orb damage unless the player has the [[Strange Brew]], and unlike [[Critical Peg|Critical Pegs]], they do not move after each turn (unless the player has the [[Tactical Treat]]).

By default, there are two refresh pegs on the pegboard at all times, in random locations, overriding a random normal [[Peg]] (or one of a few other [[:Category:Pegs|peg types]]) on the [[board]]. They cannot override [[Critical Peg|critical pegs]] or other {{PAGENAME}}s.

Several [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] and [[:Category:Relics|relics]] will interact with [[:Category:Refresh|Refreshes]], and as such are categorized as [[:Category:Refresh Orbs|Refresh Orbs]] and [[:Category:Refresh Relics|Refresh Relics]].

==================== Pegboard ====================
{{Stub}}

[[File:Board.png|thumb|''The board in a regular enemy [[Encounters|encounter]]''|400x400px]]

[[File:Enemy_area.png|thumb|''The enemy area''|400x400px]]

The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' (or '''board''') refers to the play area where [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] are launched and bounce off of [[:Category:Pegs|pegs]].

Above it is the '''enemy area''', where [[:Category:Enemies|enemies]] will move across the screen towards the player.

[[:Category:Bosses|Bosses]] and [[:Category:Events|events]] have unique board layouts, but otherwise the board layout is chosen at random from a set of layouts for [[:Category:Enemies|regular]] and [[:Category:Minibosses|miniboss]] [[encounters]].

{{Peg

==================== Critical Peg ====================
{{Peg
|image=critical_peg.png
|effect=Activates Critical Mode
}}

{{Mechanic
|name=Critical Mode
|image=critical.gif
|effect=[[Orbs]] now use their critical damage value
}}

The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a special type of [[:Category:Pegs|peg]] that makes fired [[Category:Orbs|orbs]] deal their critical damage value when it is [[Activate|activated]]. {{PAGENAME}}s do not contribute to orb damage unless the player has the [[Strange Brew]].

The effect applies retroactively, meaning that a {{PAGENAME}} can be hit anywhere in a chain and have its effect apply to all pegs hit, past or present.

<br />
When activated, [[Peg|standard pegs]] will become red (as seen below), and [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] will use their critical damage value. The effect wears off after the orb leaves the [[Pegboard|board]].

By default, there are two {{PAGENAME}}s in random locations on the [[pegboard]], overriding a random normal [[Peg]] (or one of a few other [[:Category:Pegs|peg types]]) on the [[board]]. They cannot override [[Refresh Peg|refresh pegs]] or other {{PAGENAME}}s. They change location every time an [[Category:Orbs|Orb]] is fired.

Several [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] and [[:Category:Relics|relics]] will interact with criticals, and as such are categorized as [[:Category:Critical Orbs|Critical Orbs]] and [[:Category:Critical Relics|Critical Relics]].

==================== Refresh ====================
{{Mechanic
|name=Refresh
|image=refresh.gif
|effect=All [[pegs]] on the [[board]] are replaced.
}}

'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a keyword that refers to any effect that immediately restores all (or a percentage of) popped [[Peg]]s to the [[Pegboard]], enabling them to be [[Hit]]/[[Activate]]d again. The standard means of triggering a Refresh is to Activate a [[Refresh Peg]], but certain [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] and [[:Category:Relics|Relics]] can refresh the pegboard through their own effects; these (along with Orbs and Relics that have special interactions with Refresh Pegs) are referred to as [[:Category:Refresh Orbs|Refresh Orbs]] and [[:Category:Refresh Relics|Refresh Relics]].

==================== Muscircle ====================
{{Status
| applicable_to = Player, [[:Category:Enemies|Enemies]]
| effect = {{color|strength|{{PAGENAME}} X}} grants all [[Orbs|orbs]] {{color|dmg_bonus|+{{color|strength|X}}/+0}}, or attacks deal {{color|enemyBonusDmg|+{{color|strength|X}} damage}} if applied to enemies.
| duration = Until end of fight or Until Killed
| image = strength_tooltip.png
| icon_image = strength.png
}}

'''{{PAGENAME}}''' (or '''Strength''') is a [[:Category:Buffs|buff]] that increases all [[:Category:Orbs|orb's]] non-crit damage, or [[Enemy|enemy's]] base damage by 1.

Wtih this status, you can rely on non-crit shot to deal higher damage.
It is great for consistently dealing high damage, and because of that, most of source orbs have some risk.

Eventually, You will not want to trigger Crit, since if regular damage is higher than crit damage, triggering crit actively reduce your damage output.

This also lead to anti-synergy with [[Critsomallos Fleece]] since it increase damage based how big between regular damage and crit damage gap is. If regular damage is same or higher than crit damage, it do nothing at all.

While it is bad for Crit synergy, despite [[Allorbnothing]] does not have interaction with {{PAGENAME}} itself, it interact with regular damage modifier and increase the crit damage surprisingly massively.

When an enemy could apply {{PAGENAME}} to themselves, in most cases it will scales large later. 
Which will be some kind of time limit, you should defeat the that enemy as fast as it won't deal lethally high damage.

Despite its effect different between player and enemies, tooltip stays as same as player's.

==================== Ballwark ====================
{{Status
|effect={{color|shield|{{PAGENAME}}}} is armor that protects HP. Damage taken will reduce {{color|shield|{{PAGENAME}}}} first, and will only damage HP if damage is greater than {{color|shield|{{PAGENAME}}}}.
|image=ballwark_tooltip.png
|duration=Until take damage or Until end of fight 
|applicable_to=Player, [[Enemy]]
}}

'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[:Category:Buffs|buff]] that protects the character from losing HP to incoming damage.

When attacked, {{PAGENAME}} stacks equal to the incoming damage will be reduced; if damage received is greater than the amount of {{PAGENAME}}, all stacks are removed with little animation and the character loses HP equal to the difference between damage and {{PAGENAME}}.

Therefore, it can be considered a temporary uncapped healing method for that particular battle, and an easy way to tank. 
However, since it doesn't affect actual health, it synergizes with things that relates remaining HP.
i.e. [[Angry Tomahawk]] and [[Monster Training]].

Note that this effect cannot protect from few unblockable damage, which is unblockable self-damage orbs i.e. [[Orblation]] and [[Nosforbatu]], and, all Max HP-draining attack also ignores {{PAGENAME}}.

Also, losing {{PAGENAME}} is '''different from losing HP'''. Therefore, it is incompatible with some relics that proc when you lose health. i.e. [[Bastion Reaction]] with self-damage. This, still, could be proc when you're using the orb that deals unblockable self-damage. 

When it's applied to an enemy, it functions exactly the same as it does to the player, but since enemies don't have the concept of inheriting health, it can be considered pure healing, except for [[Painbow Slime]]. Somehow, you can also 'steal life' with [[Infernal Ingot]].

==================== Bounce ====================
All [[:Category:Orbs|orbs]] have some level of '''Bounciness''', which dictates how far they will move when hitting a [[:Category:Pegs|peg]], as well as the amount of force [[Bomb|bombs]] apply when detonated by them.

[[Rubborb|Rubborbs]] have the most bounciness, followed by [[Infernorb|infernorbs]]. When at level 3, [[Orbsium|orbisum]] has the lowest bounciness, followed by [[Bouldorb|bouldorbs]].

Some orbs have unique ways of bouncing, like the [[Oreb]]

==================== Exploitaball ====================
{{Status
| effect = Enemies with Exploitaball X take 10X% more damage from critical attacks.
| duration = Until killed or Until end of fight
| applicable_to = [[Enemies]]
| image = [[File:Exploitaball tooltip.png|400px]]
}}

'''Exploitaball''' is a status effect that supports Roundrel's [[Critical Peg|crit]] synergies by making enemies take more damage from [[Critical Peg|critical attacks]].

For every level of Exploitaball an [[Enemies|enemy]] has, it will take 10% more damage from [[Critical Peg|critical attacks]].
