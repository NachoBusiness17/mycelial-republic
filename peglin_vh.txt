'''v0.9.15''' was an update released on June 6 that added a few new orbs & relics while addressing a few issues.

==Content Changes==
*[[Beckoning Crit]] [New Common Relic]: Every 7 [[Gold|Coins]] collected triggers a crit.
*[[Bag of Orange Pegs]] [New Rare Relic]: Provides a damage multiplier that increases the fewer unpopped [[:Category:Pegs|pegs]] you have remaining on the board after your shot.
*[[Spinterest Payment]] [New Orb]: An [[:Category:Orbs|orb]] that gets stronger the more gold you're currently holding(/hoarding)
*[[Highway Orbbery]] [New Orb]: Can be upgraded past level three any number of times
*[[Ballad|Ballad's]] base damage has been reduced.
*[[Shop]] chance in ‘?’ nodes has been reduced.
*[[Orbert’s Story]] now has a 7% increased chance for [[Chest|chests]] to grant a rare relic (description will be updated later due to technical limitations).
*[[Basalt Toadem]] [Relic]: Requirement removed, will always offer max HP increase now.
*Forge event upgrade damage has been reduced.
*All [[:Category:Status Effects|status effect]] icons have been redrawn and freshened up. We also now have a visual language to distinguish between buffs and rebuffs.
*[[Au Auger]] art has been updated.
*[[Bouldorb]]: Artwork changed to have an outlight and a slight recolour to help differentiate from stones.
*[[Balladin]] [Class]: Starting [[Ballwark]] increased to 6
*[[Protectorb]] and [[Bucklorb]]: Gain less frequent, but 3 times as potent
*[[Critical Peg|Crit]] and [[Refresh Peg|Refresh pegs]] that were created by orbs or relics now have slightly different art so that it's more clear which ones will move betewen shots (and other interactions)


==Bugfixes==
*Reduced instances of Refresh pegs occasionally disappearing and having their count reduced for the rest of battle
*Tooltips on upcoming orbs have been fixed to not display through other windows
*[[:Category:Piercing Orbs|Pierce]] should now work properly on [[Dull Peg|Dull Pegs]]
*Removed exploit that made it possible to access locked classes via the character select screen
*The [[Double-Edged Sworb]] fixed to not deal self-damage when hitting first refresh peg (if drawn with no pegs to refresh)
*[[Morbid]] now stacks and should act like [[Orbsium]] multihit if Morbid is >1
*[[Demon Wall]] art updated to accommodate for recent camera changes

==================== Alien's Rock ====================
{{Relic
| image = Aliens_Rock.png
| description = Targeted attacks also hit nearby enemies.
| id = <!--DOUBLE_DAMAGE_HURT_ON_PEG-->
| rarity = Rare
}}

The '''Alien's Rock''' is a rare [[:Category:Relics|relic]] that causes splash damage within 1 "space" adjacent of the targeted enemy when using [[Targeting|targeted orbs]]. Its area of effect is pictured below, with 💀 being the currently targeted enemy:<br>
❌✅❌<br>
✅💀✅<br>
❌✅❌

==Strategy==
{{Strategy}}
* Alien's Rock is a very strong relic for builds with many targeted orbs, especially in [[Fight|Fights]] with multiple enemies. Increasing the amount of hit enemies from 1 to 3 with this relic is quite strong.
* Alien's Rock works on [[Ballanx]], allowing it to hit enemies up to 2 spaces away.
* [[Roundrel]] generally prefers Alien's Rock over other piercing relics such as [[Prime Rod of Frost]]. This is due to them having a large amount of targeted orbs, many of which are strong like [[Evasive Maneuvorb]].

==Trivia==
* The name of this relic is a reference to the YouTuber [https://www.youtube.com/@Aliensrock Aliensrock], who often plays Peglin along with other puzzle-like games.

==================== Spiffy Crit ====================
{{Relic
| image = Spiffy crit.png
| description = Attacks apply 1 Exploitable to enemies, or 2 Exploitable on crit.
| rarity = [[:Category:Rare Relics|Rare]]
}}

The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[:Category:Rare Relics|rare relic]] that applies [[Exploitaball]] to enemies. 

Exploitabll is applied to every enemy hit with the attack, this includes attacks that hit all enemies, e.g. [[cauldron Bubball]], and [[projectile Attack]] which pass through enemies.

==Trival==
* The relic may be a reference to the Spiffing Brit: https://www.twitch.tv/thespiffingbrit / https://www.youtube.com/@thespiffingbrit
