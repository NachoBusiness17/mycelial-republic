'''Clan Battles''' are a competition between [[Clans (Pet Simulator 99)|clans]]. These were introduced with [[Update Log (Pet Simulator 99)|Update 2]] and allow the player to obtain limited [[Huge Pets (Pet Simulator 99)|huge pets]], [[Booths (Pet Simulator 99)|booths]], and [[Hoverboard (Pet Simulator 99)|hoverboards]]. Clan Battles usually take place over a period of 1 week.
