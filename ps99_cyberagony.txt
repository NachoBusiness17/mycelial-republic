The '''Huge Cyber Agony ''' was an {{Exclusive99}} [[List of Pets (Pet Simulator 99)|pet]] in [[Pet Simulator 99]]. Previously it could be obtained from the [[Tech Chest (Pet Simulator 99)|Tech Chest]], but it has been replaced by the [[Huge Circuit Cat (Pet Simulator 99)|Huge Circuit Cat]] as the main prize.
