}}'''Eggs''' are a core feature in [[Pet Simulator 99]]. As of [[Update Log (Pet Simulator 99)|Update 68]], there are '''281 eggs''' that can be purchased using [[Currencies (Pet Simulator 99)|coins]]. Each egg contains [[List of Pets (Pet Simulator 99)|pets]] that follow the player and collect coins.
