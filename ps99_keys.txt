'''Keys''' are a type of [[Items (Pet Simulator 99)|item]] in [[Pet Simulator 99]]. They can be used to open chests and unlock rooms. Majority of keys require the lower and upper halves to be combined before the key can be used. Using some keys levels up the [[Mastery (Pet Simulator 99)|Keys Mastery]].
