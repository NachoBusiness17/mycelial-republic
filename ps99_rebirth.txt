The '''Rebirth''' is a prestige feature in [[Pet Simulator 99]]. The player can unlock special features in-game and receive rewards for rebirthing. Everything progression-wise used to be retained after rebirthing. Currently as of [[Update Log (Pet Simulator 99)|Update 79]] rebirths do not 're-birth' the player. The maximum number of rebirths is 9 as of [[Update Log (Pet Simulator 99)|Update 79]].
