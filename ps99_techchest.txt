The '''Tech Chest''' is a feature in [[Pet Simulator 99]]. It can be found between the [[Rainbow Machine (Pet Simulator 99)|Rainbow Machine]] and the [[Tech Spinny Wheel (Pet Simulator 99)|Tech Spinny Wheel]], which is located on the left side of [[List of Areas (Pet Simulator 99)|Area 100 (Tech Spawn)]]. It can be opened by using a [[Items (Pet Simulator 99)|Tech Key]]. Opening the Tech Chest will reward the player with 6 random items, with the best possible item being the [[Huge Robot (Pet Simulator 99)|Huge Robot]].
