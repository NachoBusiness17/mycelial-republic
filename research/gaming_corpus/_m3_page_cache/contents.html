Choose a chapter or the level within each chapter from the list below. Where possible the text is formatted exactly as it appears on the computer interface terminals. The order of the terminals on each level is as Bungie originally dictated and may not be in the same order as you would find if you play the game. Some terminals have more than one message. Typically, they may contain:
  1. an 'unfinished' message - indicating that you have not yet completed your mission;
  2. a 'failure' message - indicating that you have failed to complete your mission successfully (e.g. not saved enough BOBs);
  3. a 'success' message - indicating that you have successfully completed your mission.
Note: levels on the Pfhor ship have no computer interface terminals.
