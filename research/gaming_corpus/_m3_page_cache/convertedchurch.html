There are three terminals on this level. You can see their in-game order in this in-game map.

A Converted Church in Venice, Italy (Terminal 0: 1st Message)

This is the message you get at the start. It is a recorded message from Blake.

Your mission on this level was given to you at the end of the previous level by High Admiral Tfear. The humans are escaping through the cone of this volcano, they have activated containment fields to to suppress the lava flow to aid their escape. The containment fields are powered by multiple, redundant sources throughout the cone. You must deactivated them all.

When you descend into the cone you will see multiple deactivated switches. So activating them deactivates them? Once you activate one you will hear a platform sound and then it will deactivate.

Even though Blakes message says that Pfhor dropships have entered the cone there are no Pfhor on this level. Only Bobs and VacBobs. But don't be fooled these guys are quick on the draw.

A Converted Church in Venice, Italy (Terminal 2: 1st Message)

The location of this terminal message is odd as it is found after you have deactivated all the power switches and made your way through the long twisting overhead path. Yet Tfear message is giving you your objective on this level again but with a slight twist. There is now a master generator in the basement which you must deactivate. Lava bath time.

While Tfear says there are thirty power switches throughout the cone there are in fact thirty one that need to be deactivated before you can reach this terminal.

Also note his last words... "I will transport you out from any terminal."

Think of it as a chance to save what little remnant of your species still lives.  There are thirty power switches throughout the cone.  You must deactivate them all to access the basement level, where the master generator is located.  I can't justify sacrificing any of my troops to assist you, but if you seal the tunnel, the fleeing humans will almost certainly surrender.

A Converted Church in Venice, Italy (Terminal 2: 2nd Message)

It you refuse Tfear's warm embrace not once but twice and make the long trek back to this terminal this is the 2nd "success" message you will read.

This is the first time this logon graphic has been used outside of the dream levels. The image was used in Marathon 2 for the ancient S'pht AI that Durandal called Thoth.

While you end up on the next level regardless of what terminal you take, by taking this terminal your consciousness has been irrevocably altered by your decision. YOU chose this path.

Think about it.
