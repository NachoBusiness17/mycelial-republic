Naw Man He's Close (Terminal 0: 1st Message)

You cannot read this message when you start the level. This is very unfortunate as it sets the scene for a new timeline and what your primary mission is here (opening airlocks).

The reason for this error is that the level was not given a Mission type (e.g. Repair or Exploration). This would have allowed the 1st (unfinished) message to be initially display until the objective had been achieved allowing the 2nd (success) message to be displayed.

Naw Man He's Close (Terminal 0: 2nd Message)

This is the message you read when you first arrive on this level. However this is the secondary objective for the level. Reading it at the start is likely to cause confusion in terms of the flow of the narrative. The location of the "transport pads" is not given and you might be forgiven if you think it is your current location.

Once you achieve your primary objective (opening airlocks) the level loops back to this terminal.

The original intent would have been for the player to read this 2nd message to get the secondary objective for the last part of the level.
