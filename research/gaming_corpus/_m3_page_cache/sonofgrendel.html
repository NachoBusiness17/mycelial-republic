Depending on which terminal you took to get to this level you are either thinking  "What the fuck is going on?"  or  "Ok I am looking for the S'pht AI" .

There are a total of nine terminals (0-8) on this level. You can see their in-game order in this in-game map. All but one (Terminal 4) are intralevel teleport terminals. The exit is not a terminal but S'pht teleport pad shown in  Terminal 5  and  Terminal 8 .

Find out how the Long Views were achieved on this level in Hastur's Workshop.

Some of the terminal images on this level contain legacy Marathon 2 textures. Find out more about this on the Textures section.
