There are four terminals on this level. See their in-game order in this in-game map.

Thing What Kicks has the distinction of being the only level in the Trilogy that had its own spoiler web site with a step by step guide to solving the Central Tower door opening puzzle. The web site is long gone now but you can see a preserved copy of it here.

Find out how Greg Kirkpatrick used Tag Switches to make the door opening puzzle on this level. This was featured in Hastur's Workshop.

Thing What Kicks. . . (Terminal 0)

If you don't get a déjà vu feeling at the start of this level looking out across at the other terminal you will by the end of the level.

Map in the terminal too small? Then check out this in-game map of this level and the previous one stitched together.

If you end up in the water at the start of this level then it's not your lucky day. There are eight player starting positions two of which are placed over water. What are the odds you will end up in the drink? Greg Kirkpatrick has a sense of humor. Don't worry... the underwater teleport pad will get you back to the start.
