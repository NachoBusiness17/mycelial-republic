## CANON AS BANKED
- **Structure:** Vol I *Quicksilver* (Clarke Award), Vol II *The Confusion* (Locus), Vol III *The System of the World* (Locus); 1660 Restoration to 1714. Core theme per Wikipedia: "Europe's transformation away from feudal rule and control toward the rational, scientific, and more merit-based systems" of governance and finance. "The sciences of cryptology and numismatics feature heavily in the series."
- **The Royal Society as verification engine:** Daniel Waterhouse, "an English natural philosopher and Dissenter," secretarial spine of the Society — experiments demonstrated before witnesses, minutes kept, credit attributed (the Newton–Leibniz calculus priority dispute is the Cycle's great credit-war). Derbyshire: "the deep theme of the Cycle is the birth of modern ways of thinking in science, religion, politics, and business."
- **Alchemy vs natural philosophy:** Enoch Root — "The principal alchemist of the tale is the mysterious Enoch Root" — the ageless bridge. Darby quotes Root defining the natural philosopher: "one who tries to prevent his ruminations from straying, by hewing to what can be observed, and proving things, where possible, by rules of logic … Rather like a judge in a court, who insists on facts, and scorns rumour, hearsay and appeals to sentiment" (Q 14). And on method-failure: "We are empiricists … But in pinning our hopes on the Philosophic Mercury we have decided in advance what it is that we seek to discover, and that is never right" (Q 31–33) — pre-registration and falsifiability, three centuries early.
- **Money as verified metal:** Vol III *Currency* — Newton as Master of the Royal Mint, the Great Recoinage of 1696, Jack Shaftoe's counterfeiting/clipping, the Trial of the Pyx (independent assay of the Mint's own coin — the Mint is audited BY ITS OWN STANDARD). Derbyshire on the Pyx as Ham's test: "The steadfast goldsmith protected the sanctity of England's commerce by his actions." Roger Comstock names the machine: "the Royal Society, the Bank of England, Recoinage, the Whigs, and the Hanoverian Succession are all elements of it" — one protocol stack, not five inventions.
- **Credit as record:** Eliza the financier and spy; the Bank of England; credit exists because an append-only record of obligations exists (Derbyshire: an obligation ledger, "the sum of his obligations"). Alchemy's transmutation claims fail; the assay passes only what can be weighed and re-weighed.
- **Publication style as law:** Newton hoarded (the Final Word that never came); Leibniz published fast to the *Acta Eruditorum* — Darby: it was Leibniz's "mode of communication that became the preferred style of the scientific community, involving learned journals and peer review." Derbyshire on Leibniz's Logic Mill / characteristica universalis: "anyone, in any country, speaking any native language, should be able to grasp what was meant by using the calculus ratiocinator" — the universal notation, ancestor of RSA/Gödel.
- **Waterhouse the survivor-compiler:** Daniel outlives the founders to compile the record; Derbyshire quotes the last page: "A System of the World is not like that … drive shafts the wrong length, cog wheels that do not mesh … Never complete … always a-mending, endlessly argued over" — the system is never done, only maintained.
- **Longitude:** the standing prize — value paid only on a verified working method at sea (bounty-driven research = the fleet's enter-funding shape: DeepMind $10M, OpenAI credits; the prize buys verifications, not promises).
