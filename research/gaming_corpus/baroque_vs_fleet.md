## THE DESIGN LINE — the fleet's Royal Society rule

**No claim enters the record on the claimant's word: publish through the gate, replicate through the salon, seal in the chain with your name on it.** The Society's three instruments, one per failure mode:

1. **cite_gate = publication** (nothing is "known" until a primary source is pinned — kills the uncited rumor).
2. **salon blind witnesses = replication** (nothing is SUPPORTED until a non-claimant organ has looked — kills the self-report; tri-state keeps DISPUTED honest).
3. **verkle chain + hash = the minute-book** (nothing is history until it is hashed with a seat attached — kills both erasure and unattributed credit).

The Cycle's one-line moral for us: the Royal Society did not make England richer by being clever; it made **verification cheap**, and the wealth followed. Same for the fleet — every protocol above exists to drive the price of "is this true?" toward zero, because that is the only commodity our supply of trust can be coined from.

*Mint-mark: the Assay Master of the record is not the person who struck the coin. Never has been. That is the whole trick.*
