## MECHANICS AS BANKED
- **Zones:** three distinct zones + a 4th area containing the Forge; zone-based run format; zone variety praised.
- **Permadeath/run structure:** death ends the run — classic roguelite loop.
- **Neural Lattice (the meta-progression core):** "Death is only the beginning, each run rebuilds you stronger through your Neural Lattice." Combines weapons, augments, elements, perks, items BETWEEN runs; persists across death.
- **The Forge:** final progression sink, Zone 4; currency from Zone 4 spent at forges in safe rooms.
- **Co-op:** supported; community questions re private lobbies.
- **Reception:** tight gunplay + meta-progression depth (NGOHQ); sci-fi horror tone "nodding to the classics" (Rogueliker); "well-structured roguelite FPS" (Stack Up).
