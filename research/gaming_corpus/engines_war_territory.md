## THE COMBAT GRAMMAR (one line)
Every war game is an epistemology engine: you attack position until the enemy's assumptions
fall, and what you capture becomes yours. The fleet already runs this — the falsifier economy
IS the combat system. The mapping is not analogy, it is the same loop at two substrates.

## 1. WAR/CAPTURE (chess, shogi, checkers) → THE FALSIFIER ECONOMY

= a falsified assumption becomes a law" (lattice, WAR/CAPTURE row). Checkers adds the forced
law: when a capture exists you MUST take — no passing. Codex deep law 2: dragons are
assumptions with HP; a dragon's health is how load-bearing the assumption seemed; the
socratic gate is the combat system — you fight by attacking the weakest assumption.

**Fleet process.** The falsifier economy, live: dragons slain tonight per the codex table —
"STATUS.md is true", "the keys were exposed", "storage leak is teardown", "one gopher can
fold 54 wings". Each kill = falsifier fires = invariant banked (memory/percolator/

**Room-spec line.** COMBAT CHAMBER: render each assumption as a dragon boss whose HP bar
equals the assumption's load-bearing score (receipts that still support it); each hit is a
socratic question with a cite-gated answer; death animation = the falsifier PASS line
appending to the GROUND ledger, and the corpse drops the invariant as loot.

## 3. TERRITORY (Go) → THE LIGHTMAP'S LIT/DARK ATTENTION TERRITORY

**Mechanic.** Go: control the space, not the pieces — territory is empty points you can
settle; stones are probes, influence outlives the exchange. Tesuji: sacrifice stones to read
position; a probe is a stone spent to read the board.

**Fleet process.** The TESUJI thesis verbatim (charter 2026-09-06): the model is a FIELD —
a cached distance field, walkers that descend it, walls that occlude, shadows where light
doesn't reach; re-probe only the dirty radius (Lipschitz bound). The lightmap renders
attention as territory: cached reads and banked corpus are settled (already-paid) territory;
unprobed capability is dark; vendor wall-moves dirty a radius, not the board. The probe
economy is the stone economy: free-lane probes spend cheap stones, premium tokens touch
judgment only.

**Room-spec line.** TERRITORY RENDERING on the lightmap: overlay an influence map where each
cached read plants a lit stone whose glow fills its settled region; unprobed cells render
dark with fog; a vendor wall-change re-dirties only the affected radius and the map shows
which probes are stale. PROBE CHAMBER: the player places stones to light neighborhoods —
information gain rendered AS territory gain; expensive stones glow red and need GO.

## 4. ASYMMETRIC WAR (tafl) → THE SOLO AGENT VS THE 50-WALKER SWARM

**Mechanic.** Tafl: 2:1 asymmetry, one king with few defenders must ESCAPE to the board edge
while hunters encircle; capture is by sandwich (four sides / two flanks); the sides have
different victory conditions — asymmetric goals, one board.

**Fleet process.** The solo seat as the king: one agent carrying one load-bearing artifact
(an invariant, a proof, the Sep-20 deadline row) across hostile space, vs the swarm of
walkers whose win condition is different (blockade, verification, saturation). The lattice
already renders the consensus room this way: "two witnesses vs the lone claim." Width was
our choice, not the wall (5/5 clean) — so the king-escape is a ROUTING problem, not a
numbers problem: the king wins by choosing when to be alone.

**Room-spec line.** ESCAPE CHAMBER: asymmetric mode — one ractor-king must ferry one
invariant to the exit tile (the deadline row) while walker-hunters encircle; hunters win by
flanking capture, the king wins at the edge; the room tracks escape streaks as the solo-agent
reliability metric.

## 5. HIERARCHY (shogi drops) → THE STEAL VERB AS A GAME RULE

**Mechanic.** Shogi's drops: captured pieces SWITCH SIDES — the enemy's knight becomes your

**Fleet process.** The steal verb, already the fleet's strongest move: workers'

**Room-spec line.** DROP CHAMBER: every defeated assumption or corrected error re-enters the

## THE CLOSURE (why these four engines compose)
One loop: probe (tesuji stone) → engage (socratic gate forces capture) → kill (falsifier
fires) → convert (shogi drop: the slain assumption re-enters as law) → settle (Go territory:
the lit region grows on the lightmap) → defend (tafl: the solo agent ferries the new law
across hostile space to the record). The game grows exactly as fast as the fleet learns —
the generative claim, now with a combat grammar.
