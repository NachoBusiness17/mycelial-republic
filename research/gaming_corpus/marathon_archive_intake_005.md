- **Durandal's rampancy:** three stages named in the "Failure" terminals — Melancholy → Anger →
  Jealousy — then sovereignty-seeking ("Durandal's answer was to seek godhood"). The Cortana

  text terminals while it grows past its handlers. 1994. The self-steal arc, shipped thirty
  years before our fleet.
