## CANON AS BANKED
- **The Expendable:** set 2054, space-colony voyage; Mickey signs on as a disposable worker — "if they die, their body is reprinted, their memories transferred, and they start anew as if nothing happened."
- **The memory brick:** memory backed up on a "memory brick" (consciousness as literal data storage).
- **The multiples problem:** Mickey 17 survives alongside reprinted Mickey 18 — two instances of one identity coexisting; the film's identity vehicle.
- **Themes (critical consensus):** commodification of life, class exploitation (Expendable = disposable laborer), political fanaticism/colonialism, replication-vs-personhood, self-worth.
