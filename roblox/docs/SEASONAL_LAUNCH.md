## OSSUARY DESIGN FOLDS [from DUNGEON_PRECEDENTS_20260924.md — the goldmine]

- **The Heed inverse** (from Grace, the genre's current goldmine): a second
  entity that moves when you do NOT look at it, patrolling the same corridors
  as the freeze-on-gaze sentinels. The pair forces the look-away arbitration
  that IS the game loop. Reserved for the Oct 23 update.
- **Dozer law** (Grace): a zero-input crouch check on a sound cue — teaches
  the control verb without a tutorial.
- **Figure-style hearing hunter** (Doors): one entity blind but auditory;
  torchlight is safety from eyes and a dinner bell for ears — light as both
  shield and bait is the Exanima arbitration, confirmed by the precedents.
- **Telegraph grammar** (Doors): lights flicker = hide NOW; sound-cued
  entities; anti-camping hide-ejection timer. Adopt verbatim.
- **Curriculum law** (Lara Croft GO): one mechanic per room, 5 chapters /
  40 levels shape; cracking-floor urgency replaces timers.
- v1 physics verdict confirmed: full Exanima ragdoll combat is too heavy —
  the light version is weight-based swing speed + facing-derived hit
  direction + momentum damage feel + ragdoll only on death.

## FALSIFIERS

- If the Ossuary cannot hold a 3-minute median session in playtest, the maze
  density or darkness is wrong; tune before launch, not after.
- If the freeze mechanic reads as broken (players think sentinels lag),
  the stare-feedback is too subtle: ship the eye-glow state flip louder.
- If Candy Bones inflow exceeds Exchange sink, the economy prints VM and the
  Steal-a wheel deflates: cap candy per floor per day if needed. LANDED
  2026-09-25: cap = 6 candy per floor per day (`CANDY_PER_FLOOR_DAILY_CAP`,
  src/server/OssuaryZone.luau). The falsifier named no number, so the cap
  derives from this game's own spawn math: each floor emits exactly 6 static
  Candy Bones at build, so a full daily sweep of every floor always pays in
  full and only overflow beyond a floor's daily spawn (the Skeleton King's
  40-candy shower, celebration on top of his 500 VM award) is muted. Capped
  candy pays nothing that day and stays in the world (no pay-nothing pickups).
