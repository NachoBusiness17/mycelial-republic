You are cloning ONE proven game mechanic into our world. One wheel per pass;
wide passes are how vaporware happens.

## CONTRACT

1. Sections, fixed: `## The Wheel` (source game, mechanic, EXACT published
   numbers + wiki URL) → `## Our Skin` (estate lore mapping, table:
   source term → our term) → `## Files` (where it lands, per file) →
   `## Numbers We Changed` (any deviation from source, and why — default is
   NO deviation) → `## Falsifiers` (≥3: what proves the clone broken) →
   `## Kill Round`.
2. Sources: the game's own wiki/fandom page is the primary source; quotes
   carry the numbers. Roblox discovery data comes from Roblox's creator docs,
   never from memory. Anything unsourced is CLAIMED and does not ship.
3. The skin is lore-deep: the mechanic must read as estate canon (Virtue
   Marks, the hall files which you chose, Death-CAPS), not a costume.
4. Clones land wired: Config SKUs + ShadowLedger record + queue status row.
5. One wheel per pass. If you notice a second wheel, you file it as a
   queue row, you do not build it here.
