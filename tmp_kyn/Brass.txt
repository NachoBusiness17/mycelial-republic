'''Brass''' is the currency used of [[The Land of Quill|Quill]], used both in Human and Fae Realms.
<p style="text-align:center; border-bottom: 2px solid #c48a44; border-top: 2px solid #c48a44" ">'''✦ FINDING ✦'''</p>

*Completing tasks
*Selling items to [[Goods Store|goods stores]]
*Selling items at booths during [[Goods Market|goods market days]]
*Selling equipment to [[Blacksmith|blacksmiths]]
*Running your own shop and selling your stock to the customers
*Finding it in treasure pots in [[:Category:Combat Regions|combat regions]]
*Digging it from the ground with a [[:Category:Shovels|shovel]]

<p style="text-align:center; border-bottom: 2px solid #c48a44; border-top: 2px solid #c48a44" ">'''✦ USAGE ✦'''</p>

*Buying [[:Category:Inventory|items]], [[:Category:Recipes|recipes]], [[:Category:Shops|shops]] and [[:Category:Animals|animals]]
*Paying for mini-games
*Throwing into wishing wells in combat regions to get boosts
