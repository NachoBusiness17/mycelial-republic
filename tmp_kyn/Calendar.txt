'''Journal''' is a vital part of your life as it informs you about current events regarding the game. You can open it by pressing "C".

Inside you will find 2 tabs - your current [[Tasks|task]] list and a calendar with event records such as holidays or villagers' birthdays (by birth season and birth day). You can get to know these days from noticeboards in each village or by hearing them from villagers (secrets and gossips). You can also discover villager's birthday by giving them a gift on this day. 

Events are marked with yellow stars while villagers' birthday - with red ones.
