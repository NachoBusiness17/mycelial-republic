Cooking is a skill taught by one of the [[:Category:Masters|Masters]].

== RECIPE BOOK ==
[[File:Cooking Recipe Book.gif|right|600px]]
* The coloured tabs at the side of the book represent the different kinds of food you can make: Soups, pies, stews, jams, puddings, bread and sandwiches. Recipes are added to the book as you discover them.
* Each recipe shows the ingredients required to make it and will be greyed out if you don't have them all in your inventory.
* Cooked foods have traits that can affect the person eating them. At first, these are shown with '???' but will be revealed once you have made the recipe.
* You can use the Filter by Trait button to show only recipes with a particular trait. This can be useful if you plan on going on a date, into combat, or for many other things.






== SOUPS ==
[[File:Cooking Soup Minigame.gif|right|600px]]
* Select one of each item shown in the shopping list on the left of the screen. The ingredient can be removed by selecting it again.
* Higher quality ingredients will improve the quality of your soup, but they are more difficult to cook with.
* Once you're happy with your chosen ingredients, select the soup bowl button at its base to start cooking.

=== Grinding ===
* Blend each ingredient and add it to the pot before time runs out.
# Select an ingredient from the shelf to put it into the grinder.
# Press the Hob’s head to start the grinder rotating.  Press again to stop once the arrows line up.  If you miss, the grinder will shake and will rotate more slowly.  You have three chances per ingredient.
# Once the ingredient is ground up, it is added to the pot.  The cooking timer flips over and starts running.  Grind the other two ingredients before all the sand runs to the bottom of the timer.


== PIES ==
[[File:Cooking Pie Minigame.gif|right|600px]]
* Select one of each item shown in the shopping list on the left of the screen. The ingredient can be removed by selecting it again.
* Higher quality ingredients will improve the quality of your soup, but they are more difficult to cook with.
* Flour and eggs don't have quality variations.
* Once you're happy with the selection in the tray, select the pie button at its base to start baking.
* The eggs and flour will magically combine to make the pie dough before you start chopping the ingredients.

=== Chopping ===

# Choose an ingredient from the shelf to move it to the Chopping Board.
# The best knife for the job will appear. Use it to chop the ingredients - finding the right rhythm can help you chop more efficiently. 
# Once you've sliced sufficiently, the Chopping Board will tip up and allow you to scrape the ingredients into the Pie Dish. Repeat this until all ingredients are added.

=== Pastry Rolling ===

# Roll out the pastry by changing the angle of the rolling pin and pushing it along to make the dough cover the target circle. Try not to roll too much and waste your pastry, or you may not have enough to cover your pie completely!
# Once you've finished rolling out, the pastry is automatically trimmed and used to top your pie.

=== Baking ===

# Use the paddle to slide your pie into the oven to begin baking it.
# Watch the Baking Meter on the right. As the pie cooks, orange and green bars will appear for each ingredient. Aim to stop the meter on each of the green bars. But don't wait too long - the bars will shrink as the pie cooks, and once they disappear, your pie will be burned! The more accurate you are here, the better the quality of your pie.
# Once you've made your three marks on the Meter, use the paddle to take your pie out of the oven.

== STEWS ==
[[File:Cooking Stew Minigame.gif|right|600px]]
* Select one of each item shown in the shopping list on the left of the screen. The ingredient can be removed by selecting it again.
* Higher quality ingredients will improve the quality of your soup, but they are more difficult to cook with.
* Once you're happy with your chosen ingredients, select the stew pot button at its base to start cooking.

=== Cooking and Chopping ===

* Some ingredients take longer to cook than others, so each has its own timer. Aim to get all three timers to run out simultaneously, with all three ingredients simmering in the pot.
# Choose the ingredient with the most sand in its timer first, and it will move to the chopping board.
# The best knife for the job will appear. Use it to chop the ingredients - finding the right rhythm can help you chop more efficiently. 
# Once you've sliced sufficiently, the Chopping Board will tip up and allow you to scrape the ingredient into the pot, and the ingredient's timer will flip over and start running.
# Choose the ingredient with the next-longest cooking time and chop it. You can choose when to scrape it into the pot in order to match up the time remaining on the sand timers.

== JAMS ==
[[File:Cooking Jam Minigame.gif|right|600px]]
* Select one of each item shown in the shopping list on the left of the screen. The ingredient can be removed by selecting it again.
* Higher quality ingredients will improve the quality of your soup, but they are more difficult to cook with.
* Once you're happy with your chosen ingredients, select the jam pot button at its base to start cooking.

=== Cooking and Chopping ===

* Aim to keep the pot at a simmering point through the cooking time - not too cold and not too hot.
# Choose an ingredient from the shelf to move it to the Chopping Board.
# The best knife for the job will appear. Use it to chop the ingredients - finding the right rhythm can help you chop more efficiently. 
# Once you've sliced sufficiently, the Chopping Board will tip up and allow you to scrape the ingredient into the pot, starting the timer.
# The pot's temperature will drop each time an ingredient is added. Use the lever to adjust the heat and keep it in the simmering zone in the middle of the thermometer - if it stays in the cold blue area or the red boiling area for too long, your jam-making attempt will fail!
# Keep an eye on the temperature while chopping the other ingredients. You can switch between chopping and using the temperature lever at any time. Make sure that you add all three ingredients before the timer runs out.
