There are many, many ways to earn money in Kynseed. This page lists a few of the most efficient methods.

*'''Balloon game:''' play this game in the far right hut of [[Tir Na Nog]]. Keep declining prizes until you get ones worth more than the 10 Brass it cost to play; [[Little Sap]] is one that works.
* '''Binding bands:''' every once in a while, you will find a quest to retrieve a [[Binding Band]] for a townsperson. Instead of returning the item to them, sell it in your shop for 38-50 Brass each.
* '''Chicken trick:''' Feed chicken in the town to get them friendly enough that they lay eggs every day when you walk by. After gathering the eggs, feed chicken a [[Gassy Apple]] and shoot them with a [[Slingshot]] to collect [[Chicken Drumstick]]. If you use a Gassy Apple with more stars, you may get 2-3 Drumsticks per chicken.
* '''Fairground booth:''' Sell a bunch of [[Rotten Apple|Rotten Apples]] or [[Dandelion|Dandelions]] for 4 Brass each. You don't get a barter prompt below 5 Brass, so you can walk away from the screen and just leave it up there to sell them all while you're doing something else.]
* '''Mining:''' Go to [[Homesteads]], the south mine has 4 copper stations and a Little Sap. The Sap is worth 15-20 Brass on its own. Tin Swords also sell for 12 brass.
* '''Selling items''': Each day, go to every shop and sell either Dandelions, Rotten Apples or [[Mess]] items for 1 Brass each. If you sell up to the max each shop allows, you can make 25 Brass per shop, per day.
* '''Spouse gathering: '''Once married, use the book in your home to send your spouse out on gathering trips. Sending them to Mellowfields or Summerdown gives items worth more money.
