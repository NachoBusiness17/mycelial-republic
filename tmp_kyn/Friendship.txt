The player can earn '''Friendship''' with each of the inhabitants of [[The Land of Quill|Quill]] by completing [[tasks]] for or gifting [[:Category:Items|items]] to characters. Friendship is measured with '''Friendship Points (FP)'''. 

== Friendship system ==
The player has an FP score with each inhabitant. There are multiple ways the player can gain and lose FP; the current amount will determine what types of interactions the player can have with those characters or their [[:Category:Households|household]].

=== Gaining/Losing FP ===
The player can gain FP by:

# Gifting ''neutral'' items that the inhabitant does not like/hate (+10 FP normally, +35 FP on birthdays)
# Gifting ''liked'' items (+20 FP normally, +35 FP when gifted for the first time, +50 FP on birthdays)
# Completing tasks with an FP reward

''Note: FP rewards for gifts can be increased by gifting items with a high [[Item Quality|quality]] rating''<!-- Information missing -->

The player can lose FP by:

# Gifting ''disliked'' items (-50 FP normally, -75 FP on birthdays)
# Putting characters in a bad mood (-10 FP per day)
# [[Pranking]] inhabitants

=== Friendship levels ===
There are 7 levels of friendship. To have reached a certain friendship level with a character, the player's current FP with that character must have passed a set threshold. To reach the '''Love''' level, the player must reach the threshold ''and'' complete the character's [[Tasks|NPC Task]].

== Gifts ==
The player can gain FP once per day for giving a character a gift. The amount of FP gained is determined by whether or not the character is ''neutral'' (does not like or dislike the item) or ''likes'' the gift, the gifted [[Item Quality|item quality]], and if the gift is given on the character's birthday. More FP is awarded when a liked item is gifted for the first time. If a gift is disliked by a character, then the player will instead lose FP.

=== Character responses when receiving a gift ===
After receiving a gift from the player, characters can respond with one of several options, based on how much they liked the gift.

"Great" responses:

* Fantastic! Thanks!
* Just what I always wanted!
* You know just what I needed.
* How very, very kind of you.
* Amazing!
* Wonderful!
* Oh! How very kind!
* Love it!
* You are a star.

"Good" responses:

* Lovely stuff.
* That's nice.
* Thanks for that.
* Thank you!
* Ooh very nice.
* Very kind of you.
* How kind.
* This will do nicely.
* Many gratitudes.

"OK" responses:

* Acceptable.
* That's fine thanks.
* Hmmm thanks I suppose.
* This will do I suppose.
* Not bad.
* Cheers.
* Appreciate the gesture.
* That's ok.
* Thank you.

"Bad" responses:

* Are you trying to wind me up?
* I hate these!
* Unacceptable!
* Unacceptaaaaaable!
* Why would you give me this?!
* Are you joking?
* What a terrible thing to give me.
* Terrible!
