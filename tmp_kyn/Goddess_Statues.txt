'''Goddess Statues '''allow you to make an [[Offerings|offering]] and travel between them in exchange for an [[apple]]. You can only travel between the statues you have already discovered.

They count as [[Mapstones]] when you are trying to uncover the map. Meaning you need to click on them to discover them to reveal that part of the map. :)[[File:World_map_statues.png|630px|thumb|left|World Map - Blue swirls indicate Goddess Statues]]
