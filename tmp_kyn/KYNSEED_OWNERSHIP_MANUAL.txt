Greetings. If you are reading this, you have taken ownership of the Kynseed.<br>
There are a few things you should know to help increase your enjoyment of this most magical of artefacts.<br>
Once planted near your home, you can then...<br>

See Fae and converse with them freely.<br>
Examine the roots to see your family tree and unlock rewards for fulfilling the criteria.<br>
Pass on your skills and knowledge to your nominated heir.<br>

Simply interact with the tree to access options.

Your tree grows over many years and represents your lineage... their success or failure, reputation and wealth.<br>
The tree will also represent family, your combat success or failure, and so on.

The tree will set you specific 'Challenges', which you can view by interacting with it. Fill these objectives during your lifetime for rewards. These rewards are Stat Tokens, which will increase your stats; Fairweather Tokens, which reduce the cost of arcane items; and Stamina Tokens, which give you an extra dot of stamina in combat.

When you are ready to pass on to your heir, interact and choose the appropriate option.

Good luck and good life.
