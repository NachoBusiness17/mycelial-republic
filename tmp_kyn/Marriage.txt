'''Marriage''' is a gameplay feature in Kynseed, which gives the player an option to marry a NPC (Non-Playable Character).

== Getting married==
In order to get married, you will need have a marriageable NPC reach the "love" feeling with you. A [[White Rose]] can only be offered at the beginning of each week. You must also be an adult in game in order for you to offer the rose.

Once you are at this stage you can offer the white rose (picked on [[Druida Day]] in [[Loverwood]] to the left of the marriage ceremony area), this will trigger the [[Road to Marriage]] [[Tasks|quest]] that will give you a new task to complete every day for a week.
Tasks range from taking partner on a date of their choice, collecting an [[egg]], collecting a [[Charmweed]], collecting [[flour]] etc. The process is described in the book [[The Ritual of Marriage|"The Ritual of Marriage - By Isla Walker"]] found in [[Loverwood]].

On the final day, you will be tasked to bake a [[Pie of You and I]] with the items you had collected. You will be able to bake the pie on the [[stove]] inside your house.

Once you have the pie, you will go to the [[High Priest|high priest]] in [[Loverwood]] to exchange the pie with the rings you will use to propose. From here, you must return to your future spouse with the rings and gift them.

Upon gifting the rings to your future spouse, you will be required to pick a day for the wedding. Once this day is reached, you must return to the high priest in the Loverwood to begin the marriage ceremony. During the ceremony, one of the spirits will choose you and your spouse a provide a bonus as detailed below.
