'''Masters''' do not age or love as they've travelled to Mirryr and been blessed by the [[:Category:Goddesses|Goddesses]] - therefore you can't marry them. In exchange for their longevity, they need to pass their knowledge to others. This event was described by Marshtyt in his [[Marshtyt - A Biography|biography]].

They will teach the player skills in exchange for certain items.

There is one Master for each of the following skill trees:
* [[Fishing]] - [[Twig]]
* [[Cooking]] - [[Mother Hubbard]]
* [[Melee]] - [[Jogon]]
* [[Ranged]] - [[Moon]]
* [[Exploring|Exploration]] - [[Sibling]]
* [[Gardening]] - [[Marshtyt]]
