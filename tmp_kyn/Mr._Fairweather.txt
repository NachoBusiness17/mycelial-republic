'''Mr. Fairweather''' is a hare-like [[Fel Fae]] that offers you the [[The Kynseed]] in exchange for your body when you die at the age of 50. Mr. Fairweather is able to travel into others' dreams and speak with them, often using this ability to make deals with the humans of Quill. He speaks only in rhyme and is adamant that he is a kind Fae, though the deals he makes always seem to come with a very high price. He lives in the barren Fae realm of [[Briar]].

==History ==
Before you ever arrive in the Vale, Mr. Fairweather tricks [[Twig]] into making a deal with him, speaking to him through his dreams. Twig leaves a ''[[TWIG's TORN OUT DIARY NOTE]]'' before meeting with Mr. Fairweather, who promises him the skills of the best fisherman and the fastest runner. The price for Twig's new skills is that he must bring others to Mr. Fairweather for a deal as well.

Twig will take you to see him during the night. Mr. Fairweather will offer you the Kynseed, saying that it will allow you to carry on consciousness through generation after generation of your children. In exchange for this, you will die at the age of 50, and give your body to Mr. Fairweather. All generations afterwards must do the same. He offers you this in the form of a contract. The minimum contract length is 1000 years, and the payment is 5 years of your life, paid upfront.

==The Contract==
''"By taking ownership of the KYNSEED, you agree to the stipulations in this contract. The Kynseed will allow you to SEE and SPEAK with all things FAE and grow your FAMILY TREE. Your children will carry on your legacy and as your tree grows, so does your power. Once planted, the Contract begins. You may uproot the Kynseed and pass it to any of your children who are of age, and join them in spirit, thus continuing to monitor your bloodline.''

''In return for the Kynseed you agree that on the day of your 50th birthing day, you willingly give your body to Mr. Fairweather, and every heir who inherits the Kynseed must do the same. If you have no heirs to pass the Kynseed to, you will be offered an orphan to take your place. If you die before your time, Mr. Fairweather agrees to revive you at a cost of 1 year of your LIFE.''

''This contract is binding upon signing this paper. The initial cost of the Kynseed is 5 years of your life, payable upfront. Life tax is payable at the end of each human calendar year.''

''Signed Mr Fairweather."''

==The Price of Life==
Mr. Fairweather uses longevity as a currency and you will need to pay him years of your life in exchange for his "help". 
* You can view how many years of life tax you owe by viewing your character in the Character screen.
* If you are killed at any point before the age of 50, Mr. Fairweather will resurrect you for the price of 1 year of your life (which is taken at tax time).
* In combat zones, you have a varying number of 'combat chances' where you can die without Mr. Fairweather visiting. Once you have used up all of your chances, a year of your life will be taken by Mr. Fairweather.
* After the prologue ends, you may visit Mr. Fairweather in Briar using the [[Obscura]] at a [[Henge]]. He has a variety of items for sale, each costing years of life. Only three items will be offered at a time but these items will be reloaded eventually. Some items have passive effects while others need to be equipped.
* After finishing the main storyline, Mr. Fairweather will offer [[Customisation]] objects for the price of REP instead of life years.

* Fairweather is 20 feet (~6,1 meters) tall. It is unknown if the ears are part of the height.
