==Goddesses offerings==

Offerings can be given to [[Goddess Statues]] on [[Goddess Day]] (every Solsday). There are total of six [[Goddesses]] and three bowls for offerings. Each bowl has two Goddesses that share it, but only one per bowl can have an offering at the time. By giving them offerings the player can gain boons (blessings), which last a full week. However, there is always a curse attached to the blessing. The star quality of the item determines what blessings a player can gain, so if they want to have a certain blessing, they need to have the same quality of item than what the blessing of a choice is.

It's also good to remember that each Goddess has also preferred acceptable offering items so even if the player has certain good quality items available in [[inventory]], it's not always possible to offer it to a Goddess if it's something she doesn't prefer. A player can get up to 3 blessings and curses in total.

===Offering system ===
[[File:Offerings example.png|center|Giving Aurore an offering she likes|alt=|800x800px]] 

There seems to be pretty straightforward system to all offerings - there are three offering bowls and each bowl is shared by two goddesses. There are eight blessings and curse choices in total per bowl: four for each goddess that share the bowl. Giving one offering to the other for a blessing will make the counterpart goddess give a curse in return.
<p style="font-size:9pt" span>''Example:'' Giving an offering for <span style="color:#ffb533">'''Freyl'''</span> will make her grant a blessing for the player, but <span style="color:#639d37">'''Druida'''</span> will give a curse in return. If the player decided that instead <span style="color:#639d37">'''Druida'''</span> should get an offering for a blessing, <span style="color:#ffb533">'''Freyl'''</span> will then give a curse in return.</p>

==Brounies offerings==
/*This information needs to be checked if it's still up-to-date*/

Offerings to [[Brounie]]s are gifted daily. You put them to Offering Bowls located next to your and villagers' houses. When the wrong or none offering is given, mischevious Brounies will prank you and make your apples rot. On the other hand, when the right gift is placed in the bowl they are willing to help you out. Brounies seem to be happy with gifts of dandelions. 

'''Tip: '''So far only [[Rotten Apple|rotten apples]] jinx is implemented therefore there is no merit in giving offerings during wintertime as apples don't grow in this season.
