'''Reputation''' is a parameter that tells the player's Reputation and Renown level. Every time the player has gained enough reputation points, they can go to meet the [[Grandmasters]] of [[The Circle|the Circle]] who give the player various items as a reward to help them with their journey. Raising ones reputation also helps certain new [[tasks]] and [[:Category:Events|events]] to become available.

There's a few ways you can gain reputation.

One is completing notice board tasks, which gives a small amount of reputation.

Another is fighting in any of the three areas (such as [[Simplewood]]).

The fastest way to get reputation is to simply buy store(s) and then either work in them personally or by hiring staff to run them for you. As long as you (or your staff) does a good job, you will literally be swimming in reputation and hitting level 10 in a matter of a few in-game weeks.
