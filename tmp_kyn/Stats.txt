'''Stats''' are a gameplay feature that affects the player character's performance in the game. There are various ways to alter them. The NPCs (Non-Playable [[Characters]]) also have their own stats.

There are 10 '''stats''' in Kynseed:

*'''Charm''' - [[Friendship|FP Gains]], offset REP losses
*'''Stamina''' -  [[:Category:Combat|Combat]] Stamina regeneration speed
*'''Strength''' - Combat damage modifier, Amount of [[Blacksmith]] anvil hits
*'''Intelligence''' - Increases Sandbox XP gain, Increases weapon XP gain, Helps with [[apothecary]] crafting
*'''Barter''' - Helps with Bartering
*'''Speed''' - Movement Speed, Increase combat parry window
*'''Accuracy''' - Combat critical hit chance, helps with blacksmith crafting
*'''Constitution''' - Combat resistance to certain enemy attacks, helps with apothecary crafting
*'''Toughness''' - Reduces damage taken from blocked enemy attacks
*'''Virility''' - Dictates your chance to have kids.
