'''Talents''' are abilities that give special skills or buffs. NPCs start with one Talent each. 

You cannot gain Talents through normal gameplay; they must be inherited. When you marry and have a child with a NPC, your baby will inherit the NPC's Talent plus one random new Talent (2 total). If you pass on the Kynseed to that child and marry again, that baby will inherit the NPC's Talent, one of the player's Talents, plus one random new Talent (3 total). Adopted children start with one random Talent.
