The Kynseed is an item gifted the protagonist by [[Mr. Fairweather|Fairweather]].

The Kynseed is a magical seed that grows as the player raises their renown, giving each generation six tasks to complete.

Three of them are always the same, but the remaining three are always randomized.

== Standard Tasks ==
The Kynseed will always give you the following three tasks:

* Earn a certain amount of Brass.
* Earn a certain amount of Reputation.
* Earn a certain amount of Friendship points.

== Random Tasks ==
The Kynseed will always give you three random tasks. Completing those tasks gives you Stat seeds, allowing you to increase one stat of your choice by 1. Note that the list below is currently incomplete.

* Personally sell 500 items in the Item Shop (you need to buy an item shop to complete this).
* Craft 50 Potions (done in an Apothecary - any Potion counts towards this). 
* Cook 50 meals.
* Cut 5000 tall grass (the grass that's purely decorative, not the stuff you plant).
* Catch 500 fish (any fish counts towards this).
* Gift 250 items.
* Plant and harvest 500 items.
* Get 15 positive outcomes on the Wheel of Delight.
* Get 15 negative outcomes on the Wheel of Delight.
* Destroy 15 fire breathing statues in combat.
