Marriage in Quillian society is more than just an excuse to have a party and get drunk.

Marriage is a statement of intent. That intent is to prove your love for not just your partner but also a love of nature and our fair Goddesses.

To love another, one must love all around them, which the proposal ritual proves. Once you have found the partner of your dreams and their love for you is complete, you can start the process.

Firstly, speak to the High Priest in Loverwood to gain the Recipe for the Pie of You and I.

Then, gift the White Rose, which you will also find in Loverwood, to your beloved.

They will ask you to prove your love with the customary baking of the Pie.

Gather ingredients in your own time and bake the Pie.

Take the Pie to the High Priest and give it to him. He will offer it to the Goddesses. If they smile on the offering, you will be given a Binding Band!

Take this Band to your lover and gift it to them, and you can then choose the date if they accept!

Now you are ready to be married and enjoy all the considerable benefits of marriage, such as cooking and foraging!
