==Personality==
He seems to be on good terms with both of the twins, though he can appear to be a bit distant as he doesn't interact much with you other than giving you tasks. He is a hard worker, seen milking the cow every day.

==History==
He adopted the twins from the woman who lived in a shoe. Not much is known about him except the fact that he's of poor health and lives alone at Willowdown Farm after the death of his wife, Rose, with no other family to speak of. The last time he saw his wife was during [[Summertide]] Festival when, as hinted, she has been chosen as the human sacrifice for the [[Druida|Goddess]].  

He worked on Willowdown Farm his whole life. It is mentioned that he adopted the twins in order to get help for maintaining the farm, though he reassures them the main reason was that he and his wife always wanted to have children and is very happy he finally has someone who can inherit the farm. 

== Trivia ==
* This NPC's previous name was Father, but was later changed to Uncle Bill.
*According to him he's a great fisherman.
* During the prologue on the 6th day, he mentions your family can't afford a pig from Pig Market. If you buy a pig nevertheless, he and your [[Sibling]] will make a comment on it, both being surprised and glad for you.
