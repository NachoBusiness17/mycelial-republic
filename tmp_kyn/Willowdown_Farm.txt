'''Willowdown Farm''' is home to the player, their [[Sibling]] and [[Uncle Bill|uncle]]. There is an entrance to [[Crumblechalk Mine]] as well as a cellar that contains [[Rock Monster|a secret]]. The majority of the farm layout can be [[Customisation|customised]] by the player.
