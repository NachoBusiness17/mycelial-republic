'''Children''' can be obtained through two methods:

* '''Option 1: Adoption'''
** A child can be adopted from '''[[The Shoe]]''' for 500 brass. The player is given the option to select from three available children.
* '''Option 2: Biological'''[[File:Rabbit corndoll.png|thumb|Rabbit Corndoll]]
** 1st generation children gain Talent from spouse + 1 random talent.
** By gifting a [[Rabbit Corndoll]] to your spouse, you can trigger the Wheel of Delight. This can be done multiple times per day, at any time. Spinning the Wheel of Delight has the following potential effects:
*** Stork: A baby will arrive in one week, plus gain significant (16-34) FP with spouse.
*** Volcano: Gain significant FP (10-34) with spouse.
***Windmill: Minor change in FP (-1 to 5) with spouse.
*** Smoking Chimney: Minor change in FP (-1 to 5) with spouse.
***Drooping Flower: Lose minor FP (4-5) with spouse.
*** Sleeping Dragon: Lose significant FP (10-34) with spouse.
*** Burning Flower: Lose significant FP (10-34) with spouse.

== Aging ==
Children age normally just as all the adults and the player. It's good to note that if the player owns some life tax at the end of the year by [[Mr. Fairweather]], the kids will age up also.
