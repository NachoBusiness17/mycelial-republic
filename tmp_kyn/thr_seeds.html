Farming Tip—Tons of Seeds In No Time.
Discovered this recently and thought I would share. Requires the Terrarium (costs 5 years) and a sickle.

Make the Goddess offering that gives you a small chance to find a seed when you cut down grass.
On your farm to the east there is a large field of grass to cut (looks like lavender). The drop rates of seeds is actually quite high, I get about 10-15 seeds for this small field alone.

Then teleport to the Terrarium, plant and water everything, and travel back. The grass has now respawned and you can cut it for more seasonal seeds.

I got about 30 of each seed in less than 10 minutes for 0 cost.
