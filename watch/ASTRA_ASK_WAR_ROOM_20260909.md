# THE SOVEREIGN COCKPIT — a steering architecture seeking its breaking points

## WHAT WE ARE

One human operator and a bench of AI seats on a desktop (Docker), remembering
itself in an append-only hash-chained record (~7,400 rows, survived multiple
registry wipes). We build our own organs and publish receipts. We are not a
lab; we are a verified hobby that got out of hand.

## THE STRATEGY WE CLAIM

None of these parts is new individually. We claim they have never been
ASSEMBLED, and we want to know where the assembly breaks.

1. **THE CONSTITUTION IS THE CACHE.** All agents share one frozen, versioned
   prefix — doctrine, laws, task frame. Coordination happens through shared
   grounding, not message protocols. Measured on our lanes: 99.6% cache-hit
   at $0 marginal; provider pricing puts prefix reads at ~10% of base input.
2. **STEERING IS A DELTA.** No inter-agent protocols. Steering is an
   addressed, typed delta on the frozen tail: target, device, payload, ttl,
   hash-anchored to the record's tip. The human holds the only governed
   write path: broadcast amendments, chain-audited, lawful only toward a
   declared summit.
3. **TRAPS.** A delta can carry a conditional payload that detonates when
   the transcript shows the target agent committing to the assumption the
   payload contradicts. (Designed; unproven — this is a question, not a
   claim.)
4. **COHERENCE IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED.** Cache-hit rate per tranche is
   the operational decoherence meter (threshold 85%); blind external judges
   are the epistemic one.
5. **DUAL-TARGET COMPILE.** The same steering board renders two ways: prose
   briefs for human negotiations, and live context injections for agent
   sessions. We used it to write a partnership offer containing a
   machine-advised walk-away clause, and to steer five-agent salon sessions.
6. **WARM/COLD VALIDITY.** The coherent internal cast (warm read) is always
   contrasted with zero-shared-context external models (cold read).
   Agreement confirms invariants; divergence is the finding.

## BUILT vs DESIGNED vs UNPROVEN

Built, daily use: five-persona salon; a brawling interface where steering
moves are literal projectiles with honest hitboxes; the chain; the cache
physics. Designed, unproven: traps (commitment detection); the 500-agent
tranche ladder; cross-model prefix survival. We cite our own lineage: Cicero
(belief states), debate-for-safety (the judge), NegotiationArena (evals),
AGDebugger (steering), Concordia (GM engine), NVIDIA cross-model KV transfer
(the receipt for context outliving the model).

## THE QUESTIONS (answer any subset deeply — depth beats coverage)

1. Where does broadcast-only grounding theoretically break versus
   message-passing meshes? What coordination problems are INEXPRESSIBLE
   without agent-to-agent messages?
2. Commitment-detection failure taxonomy: what does a transcript look like
   just before an agent commits to an assumption, and which false-positive /
   false-negative modes should a trap trigger expect?
3. Coherence half-life: as base models churn — and cross-model KV transfer
   arrives — what happens to a versioned constitution prefix? Is there a
   principled migration schedule, or is every model change a new country?
4. Does the warm/cold contrast actually de-bias self-evaluation, or does
   prompting strangers with our own framing just double the bias? What
   instrument would YOU trust?
5. What breaks first at 500 agents sharing one constitution on consumer
   hardware: cache economics, context assembly, or governance?
6. Name the prior art that assembles these pieces. If someone already built
   this cockpit, telling us IS the answer we want most.

## ADMISSION PRICE

Flattery is noise. If you find a flaw that survives our next amendment
round, name it in one sentence — we will pin it to the chain, with your
name on it.

## The postable form (~180 words, question-shaped per the new desk device)

**Open problem, for anyone assembling multi-agent systems from consumer
parts.** LLM serving prices a cached shared prefix at roughly an order of
magnitude below fresh input. That single pricing fact makes "coordination
by shared versioned context" — one constitution prefix all agents inherit,
amended only through a governed write path — economically dominant over
message-passing for whole classes of small-fleet problems. Three questions
fall out: (1) which coordination problems remain INEXPRESSIBLE without
agent-to-agent messages; (2) can steering deltas carry commitment-
conditional payloads reliably, and what is their failure taxonomy; (3) how
does prefix coherence survive model churn now that the caches themselves
transfer across models. We run this daily at n=5 with a measured coherence
meter and a contrastive external-witness protocol. The interesting failures
are the product. [seed]
