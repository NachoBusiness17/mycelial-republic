## CHUNK 0 — THE SEED (paste this into Astra, fresh session)

```text
You are being asked to help design a system. First, calibrate: read the seed below, then state what the system is FOR, in your own words — one paragraph. If anything in the seed is ambiguous or wrong, name the ambiguity before restating. Do not design yet. Design comes later; this step only checks that we agree on what we are building and why.

THE SEED:

We are a small fleet: one human operator and several AI seats on desktop hardware (consumer GPUs), remembering itself in an append-only hash-chained record (~7,400 receipts; it has survived registry wipes). The system has six layers:

1. CONSTITUTION — a frozen, versioned prefix (doctrine, laws, task frame) shared by all agents. Coordination happens through shared grounding, not message protocols. Cache economics: prefix reads cost roughly 10% of base input.
2. EPISTEMIC BUS — agents emit typed events (assert, commit, depend, uncertain, object, retract, request) with staking and two-signal promotion.
3. MATERIALIZED BELIEF — a mutable belief state the agents actually condition on, garbage-collectable, distinct from the immutable audit record.
4. DURABLE EXECUTOR — sessions survive interruption and resume; work in progress.
5. REFEREE — a deterministic, veto-only circuit breaker. No generative capability. It enforces structure, never truth.
6. RECEIPT SPINE — the append-only audit: influence with receipts. The human holds the only governed write path to the constitution.

The design principle is liveness without sovereignty: the fleet continues safely while unable to obtain human constitutional authority. And play is the highest form of work: the system is also a game, because games are how it stays honest and fun.

YOUR TASK: one paragraph — what is this system FOR? Name its purpose as you see it, its single biggest risk, and the one question you would need answered before designing anything.

END YOUR REPLY WITH:
CAMP STATE v0
decided: [what is locked]
open: [what is unresolved]
next: [the single most important next item]
```
