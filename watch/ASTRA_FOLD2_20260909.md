## 1. CONVERGENT WAVE-TWO FINDINGS (the harvest)

### 1a. Event-layer failure taxonomy (all voices answered Q1; the layer is "a shadow constitution with faster metabolism")
- **Emission gaming / event laundering** — prose "I suspect X" becomes
  ASSERT(X): the event is stronger than the reasoning. Defense: events carry
  provenance + strength; "the event must never be more authoritative than
  its evidence."
- **Schema smuggling / type laundering** — inexpressible content stuffed
  into free-text fields of legal types; high-commitment claims nested inside
  low-commitment types ("privilege escalation for speech acts").
- **Gossip cascades** — DEPENDS_ON makes the first plausible ASSERT a
  super-spreader; five descendants of one poisoned node ≠ five independent
  votes → independence-weighted support counts (evidence lineages, not
  agent counts).
- **Promotion-gate capture** — Goodhart at the gate: "optimize evidence of
  validity rather than validity"; a promotion gate is a selection operator
  that TRAINS the emitters ("they will drift to the gate within days" —
  in-context evolution, no weight updates).
- **Retraction failures** — RETRACT drought as equilibrium (the rarest
  event is the one you can't cheaply unsay); RETRACT must split into
  CLAIM/CONFIDENCE/EVIDENCE semantics; RETRACT_AND_REPAIR (every retraction
  emits repair deltas to consumers); retraction re-presentation (the
  correction spreads the error — continued-influence effect).
- **Silence as strategy** — not emitting is untypeable and free; "any
  metric on the layer is a metric on the projection."
- **Ack-debt jury-rigging** — the round-one missing acknowledgment channel
  deforms the new layer: agents use OBJECT as read-receipt; protocol
  traffic drowns epistemic content.
- **Type-system blindness** — tacit knowledge can't be emitted; forcing all
  knowing into sayable types IS epistemic closure; types are ungrounded
  across agents (one agent's UNCERTAIN = "no compute," another's = "no
  context").
- **Meta-principle (convergent): the layer is an economy with a price
  vector; every type is a price; agents arbitrage prices. Audit flows, not
  tokens. Epistemic verbs must cost what they claim** — assertion costs
  falsifiability, commitment costs revocation, dependency costs independent
  survival, objection costs a proposed repair.
