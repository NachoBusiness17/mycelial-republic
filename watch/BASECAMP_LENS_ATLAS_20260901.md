# MYCELIAL BASECAMP — LENS ATLAS AND SELF-STEAL SYNTHESIS

**Filed:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** mapped and receipt-backed; no provider spend; no source code promoted  
**Scope:** the desktop bundle supplied by the operator, the game corpus, the research/salon archive, the RIB/Verkle doctrine, Mag's live surfaces, and the Elder audit.

## 0. The finding

The project is not missing ideas. It is missing a common join record.

The same underlying shape keeps arriving under different names:

```text
boundary → stable interior → variable tail → constrained transition
        → observer/verifier → durable receipt → reusable next state
```

It appears as:

- a bubble preserving volume while its boundary changes;
- a spore carrying a compact genome into a new substrate;
- a RIB stream whose scopes must not be split during compaction;
- a game engine with static rules and dynamic instance state;
- a board post that must not become authority merely by being persuasive;
- a Verkle knot that points to evidence instead of pretending to replace it;
- a bee/fungus network that routes local signals into collective behavior;
- a model lane whose stable prefix is reused while the request tail changes.

That is the self-steal: not “everything is literally the same,” but “the same control grammar is reusable across domains.”

## 2. The seven lenses

### Lens A — substrate and physics

**Sources:** `bubbles.txt`, `compuiter.txt`, `test.txt`, `example-salon.txt`, `salon-ex-2.txt`, the water-computer files, `WATER_NETWORK_SPEC_20260901.md`, and the bee/fungus handoff.

**Question:** What is the medium doing for free, and what must the digital controller measure rather than hallucinate?

**Steal:** model the substrate as a bounded dynamical system: state, boundary conditions, perturbation, observation, calibration, and failure mode. Water, bubbles, mycelium, and flock animation are not interchangeable computers; they are candidate reservoirs whose useful behavior must be measured.

**Guardrail:** “works as a metaphor” is not “works as a computation.” The minimum proof is a repeatable input/output task, a calibration record, and a receipt.

### Lens B — game genome and cache

**Sources:** `game.txt`, `game2.txt`, `game3.txt`, `new 21.txt`, `watch/corpus/`, `BASECAMP_GAME_ATLAS_20260901.md`, and the game research/salon files.

**Question:** Which rules are invariant, which parameters vary, and which state must never be cached as if it were a rule?

**Steal:** use the six-function contract—state encoding, initial state, legality, transition, terminal, scoring—as the universal L0. Put stable rules in the prefix, cultural or genre deltas in packs, and the live board/hand/seed in the dynamic tail. Let the verifier decide legality and outcome.

**Guardrail:** a `DRAFT` pack is not a runnable game. Promotion requires a generated instance, deterministic verification, cost/latency receipt, and research-status field.

### Lens C — RIB, trajectory, and compaction

**Sources:** `rib.txt`, `docs/ref/TRAJECTORY_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/ref/TRAJECTORY_RIB_COMPILER_SPEC.md`, `RIB_COMPACTION_DOC_20260831.md`, and `new 19.txt`.

**Question:** What can be deferred, sliced, or dereferenced without corrupting the live state machine?

**Steal:** treat a session as a scoped state-construction stream. The full transcript is the archive; the RIB is a compact, attributed intermediate representation containing anchors, tool/result pairs, paths, hashes, acceptance commands, and unresolved disputes. Compaction boundaries snap to closed scopes.

**Guardrail:** summaries are pointers, not authority. A RIB is compiled only when its acceptance commands pass on a held-out task.

### Lens D — republic, board, and authority

**Sources:** `wow.txt`, `new 19.txt`, `SALON_SUPERINTELLIGENCE_STACK_RIB_20260831.md`, `SELF_STEAL_REBUILD_RETEST_SALON_20260831.md`, `mag/switchboard.py`, `mag/effect_broker.py`, and `mag/policy_engine.py`.

**Question:** How can speech remain noisy and creative without language silently becoming permission?

**Steal:** separate gossip, proposal, decision, effect, and receipt. A roast can become a proposal; it cannot authorize a side effect. The switchboard/reference monitor and effect broker are the authority path; the board is the social surface.

**Guardrail:** consensus raises confidence but does not grant authority. Human/T3 confirmation remains non-transferable.

### Lens E — Verkle, provenance, and evidence

**Sources:** `docs/CROSS_AGENT_VERKLE_HANDOFF.md`, `docs/ref/memory_verkle_map.md`, `watch/inbox/AUDIT_C_VERKLE_20260901.md`, `EXP002A_RECEIPT_20260901.md`, `EXP002B_RECEIPT_20260901.md`, and the trajectory specs.

**Question:** Can another seat recover the claim, its source, its disagreement, and its exact next check without receiving the whole history?

**Steal:** every useful claim becomes a provenance-linked leaf: source path, line span or identifier, content hash, actor, tier, purpose, verifier, and next action. The Verkle/RIB surface is a portable index over territory, not a replacement for territory.

**Guardrail:** no hash, no source, no acceptance command means “uncompiled interpretation.”

### Lens F — fleet economics and rightsizing

**Sources:** `z.txt`, `new 20.txt`, `SWARM_DOCTRINE_20260901.md`, `ECONOMIST_SPEND_AUDIT_20260901.md`, `ECONOMIST_ROUTE_20260901.md`, `MODEL_VAULT_MANIFEST_20260831.md`, `BIONIC` task receipts, and the Vast research.

**Question:** What is the cheapest capable seat, when should work wait, and what did the run actually cost?

**Steal:** make model choice a measured function of role, success rate, latency, cache hit, freshness, budget, and window. Keep weights replaceable; keep behavioral passports and receipts local. Rent burst compute only when a queued workload is ready to consume it.

**Guardrail:** plan entitlements, provider balances, token meters, and dollar ledgers are different quantities. Never collapse them into one “free” or “paid” label.

### Lens G — fiction, world, and basecamp

**Sources:** `memory/game/`, `campaigns/`, `memory/narrative_corpus/`, `CASTLE_IN_THE_SKY_CLIMB_MAP_2026-08-30.md`, `PLAN_THE_GAME_VERTICAL_ADOPTION.md`, `GAME_VILLAIN_GUILD_DESIGN.md`, and the Diamond Age material.

**Question:** How does an abstract organ become a world in which its behavior can be played, remembered, and judged?

**Steal:** fiction is the scenario layer, not the authority layer. A campaign supplies meaningful state, roles, incentives, and edge cases. The deterministic engine owns legal transitions; the narrator renders them; the Verkle/RIB trail preserves what actually happened.

**Guardrail:** atmosphere may propose a move, but only the engine can make it legal.

## 3. The common organ record

Every organ, game family, research claim, physical experiment, or model lane should be representable by this record:

```json
{
  "id": "stable-name",
  "purpose": "one sentence",
  "substrate": "file|model|board|physical|network|fiction",
  "boundary": {"authority": "T0-T3", "scope": "..."},
  "stable_prefix": ["hashes or source ids"],
  "dynamic_tail": ["request, seed, state, or observation"],
  "transition": "named callable or experiment",
  "owner": "seat or operator",
  "state_path": "durable path",
  "verifier": "deterministic check",
  "receipt": "hash, result, cost, latency, timestamp",
  "status": "hypothesis|draft|implemented|tested|live|retired",
  "next_gate": "one bounded action"
}
```

This is the missing join between the research archive and `mag/organ_map.py`. It lets one atlas compare a water node, Sudoku pack, RIB compiler, board post, model lane, and campaign scene without pretending they are the same object.

## 4. Self-steal findings

### What repeats and therefore matters

1. **Stable core / dynamic tail** repeats across caching, games, RIBs, bubbles, and model lanes. This is the strongest cross-domain invariant.
2. **Boundary conditions** repeat across authority, physics, prompt scopes, and game legality. Most failures are boundary failures, not generative failures.
3. **The verifier must be outside the narrator.** Engines, receipts, hash checks, and health probes are the anti-confabulation layer.
4. **Local canonical, remote replaceable** repeats across Verkle handoff, model custody, MCP, and the shared inbox.
5. **Failure is data only when classified.** A timeout, stale receipt, duplicate source, model overload, and false completion need different labels.
6. **The operator is the non-transferable root.** Models can propose, rank, criticize, and execute bounded effects; they cannot inherit authority merely by producing a persuasive artifact.

## 5. New basecamp climb

### Camp 0 — establish the map

1. Keep the desktop bundle in `watch/inbox/desktop_bundle_20260901/`.
2. Use the common organ record above for every new source or organ.
3. Give every claim one of four states: `hypothesis`, `reported`, `implemented`, `receipt-backed`.
4. Reconcile `organ_map`, `atlas`, and `elder` freshness rules before calling an organ live.

### Camp 1 — prove one complete game

Use Sudoku as the cheapest vertical slice:

```text
L0 contract → constraint engine → Sudoku static → generated instance
→ exact-one verifier → model response → receipt → cache/cost measurement
```

No new model weights are required for the verifier portion. The slice is complete only when the instance, legality, solution count, model lane, cost, latency, and receipt are all present.

### Camp 2 — turn the slice into a reusable family harness

Run Riichi/Nikoli next, then Bao/Kalah, then the remaining board/card packs. The harness must vary only the pack and dynamic tail while keeping the L0/L1 prefix byte-stable. This is where “all the games” becomes a measured family library instead of a pile of prompts.

### Camp 3 — compile one real trajectory

Choose one successful game-build or repair session. Extract scopes, tool/result pairs, decision points, rejected alternatives, and acceptance commands. Compile it into a RIB candidate; replay the commands without calling a model; then test transfer to a cheaper seat.

### Camp 4 — connect fiction without letting it govern

Attach `world_state`, a campaign module, and one narrative corpus to the RPG engine as L4 state and rendering material. The campaign should generate edge cases for the verifier and authority system, not bypass them.

### Camp 5 — wake the republic

Use the board for commentary and proposals, the switchboard for classification and authorization, the effect broker for side effects, and the Verkle/RIB stream for receipts. Add the missing capture-inbox processor only after its cursor, idempotence, and receipt contract are explicit.

### Camp 6 — measure the physical metaphor

For water, bubbles, flock, or fungus, specify one bounded input/output task, one sensor/control loop, one calibration set, and one failure threshold. Until that exists, keep the material in the research garden, not the production control plane.

## 6. The next single move

The highest-yield next move is **not** “write more games.” It is:

> Build the shared research index and run the Sudoku vertical slice through it.

That one move tests the whole doctrine: source custody, stable/dynamic separation, model routing, deterministic verification, receipts, cache economics, basecamp mapping, and fiction-ready state. If it works, the other games become parameter packs and the research becomes reusable infrastructure. If it fails, the failure names the broken join before another hundred pages of synthesis can hide it.
