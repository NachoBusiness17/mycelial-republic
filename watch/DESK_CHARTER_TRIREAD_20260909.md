## 1. THE TRI-READ (every link, all three verdicts, every time)

1. **RESEARCH** — does the link hold a mechanism, dataset, or steal?
   Verdict yes → research card routes out (cite-gate before any claim).
   Research never blocks the joke. The joke never poisons the research.
2. **LAUGH** — is this a room worth entering for the reply-strike?
   Aim at the thread's actual conversation, not the post in isolation.
3. **HAMMER** — is this a monument of consensus cowardice?
   Then it earns the flat unsayable. One per thread. Never forced.

Two "no" verdicts is a normal read. All three "yes" is a jackpot link.
