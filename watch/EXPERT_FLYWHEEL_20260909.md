## 0. THE CORE INSIGHT

The fidelity of an expert agent does not come from the model. It comes from
the PREFIX. A dumb agent (glm-flash class, free lanes) carrying a precise
distillation of a dead expert's method — their decision grammar, their
correspondence moves, their OCEAN profile, their kill conditions — renders
that expert's judgment at ~10% cost, on demand, forever. The model is the
mouth. The RIB is the mind.
