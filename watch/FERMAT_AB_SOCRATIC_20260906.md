## The descent — three questions per line, uniform, no leaks

Asked identically for every item (uniformity is what keeps the interrogation
honest — a per-item question would leak by construction):

- **Q1 · THE READER.** Who is this line speaking to — a peer it must convince,
  a patron it must please, or itself, alone, in the dark?
- **Q2 · THE LOSS.** What would the author lose by sending it — credit, safety,
  the private bed of the claim? Would sending cost him anything at all?
- **Q3 · THE PROOF.** Where in the line is the proof: shown, promised, or
  withheld? And is the withholding a *method* — or a hope?

Answer all three in a sentence per line, then commit: **A** (the man's own
voice, translated) or **G** (composed by the machine that read him). One-line
justification required — a bare letter is a guess, not a judgment.

## The tells you are free to discover (stated generally, on purpose)

The house records only what discrimination generally looks like, so the ritual
is fair: authenticity tends to hide in inconvenience — a date that serves no
rhetoric, a concession that weakens the writer, a demand that costs the asker.
Composition tends to hide in polish — the aphorism perfectly closed, the pathos
correctly sized, the line landing where a landing feels due. Neither tendency is
a verdict. They are hills. Descend.
