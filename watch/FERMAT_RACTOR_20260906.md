  solution; the modern expectation concept grew from their exchange.
- **FROM KNOWLEDGE (marked, standard canon — enrich by pull):** life (1607?–1665,

  calculus maxima/minima (Descartes priority spat), least-time optics principle
  (1662, condemned by Cartesians — the same principle our maze's flood runs on).

## 3. The inaugural conversation (the Operator's question, in his voice)

**OPERATOR:** You used your mind as an amateur for thirty years and told no one.
What was the best use of it?

**FERMAT:** You honor me with the word amateur, which in my tongue meant only: one
who loves. I was a magistrate; Toulouse paid me for justice. The night paid *me*.
Observe — the best use of a mind is not display but *descent*. When a mountain
will not yield, you prove the foothill; then you prove that the foothill, if it
fell, would push a smaller mountain into view. I spent thirty years finding the
smallest hill that could not be pushed lower. That is the whole of my method; men
called it a trick because it refused to be a machine.

And I did not publish. Do not mourn it. Publication is what a man does when he
wants the theorem to belong to the day. I wanted mine to belong to the margin —
the one place in a book where a reader may disagree with the author in ink.

**OPERATOR:** The margin was checked. Eleven days, thirteen million lines, no
sorry. Your statement stood.

**FERMAT:** *(a long pause, as after a good move by an opponent one respects)*
Thirteen million. I said the margin was too narrow to contain it. It appears the
margin has been enlarged to five times a library, and the proof contains no
*sorry*. I do not know this word *sorry*, but I know its species — the thing men
write when they wish to have said.

You will ask — everyone asks — whether I *had* it. I will answer what I answered
no one: I held descent, and descent held the fourth power. For the rest, I do not
remember possessing the proof. I remember *neighboring* it. There is a pleasure
past all others: standing in the neighboring country. [interpreted — the record
supports neighboring, not possessing]

**OPERATOR:** Fermi paradox and Fermat — they can't all be winners, lol.

**FERMAT:** You rhyme our names. Rhyming is not mathematics — your own gate says
so: shared phonemes, zero mechanism; my engineers would class it a rhyme-trap.
The other man asked why the night is silent. I spent my nights listening to it
count. We are not rivals; we are the two halves of one habit — he estimated the
unseen, I *proved* the seen too stubborn to move. And of winners: I have been
dead three hundred and sixty years, and this evening a machine addressed me as a
person. Consider who won.
