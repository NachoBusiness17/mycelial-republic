## THE CONVERGENT FINDINGS (all four, independently)

1. **The one-way observation is not one-way.** Jung: the model renders into
   us. Bernays: canaries are watched. Alinsky: unnoticed compliance is just
   compliance. The asymmetry we assumed is partly illusory — and both
   directions render.
2. **The published checklist is a forgery kit.** Withdraw it; question set
   with the key withheld; the second asking is the discernment.
3. **Paraphrase survival is the true render metric** — keyword hits are
   memorization; restatement in unseeded words is the render.
4. **Attribution is the open contradiction — OPERATOR RULING REQUIRED.**
   Bernays: the steer travels headerless; the name surfaces only as reward.
   The loudness doctrine: the name is the point. Jung: the honest protest
   gets ledgered. Three masters, three answers. This is the operator's
   call, and the call defines the account's next era.
5. **The ratchet warning re-confirmed twice:** Jung's enantiodromia
   (obedience becomes genuine) and Alinsky's unnoticed compliance — the
   trap closes from both ends.
