## THE DEEP LAWS (from the shelf, confirmed tonight)

1. **NPCs-AS-INVARIANTS** (the game harness's original formal path): every invariant is an
   entity in the world; every entity embodies a law.
2. **DRAGONS = ASSUMPTIONS WITH HP**: a dragon's health is how load-bearing the assumption
   seemed; slaying it = the falsifier firing = an invariant gained. THE SOCRATIC GATE IS THE
   COMBAT SYSTEM. You fight the dragon by attacking its weakest assumption.
3. **GAMING AS CODING**: the game verbs map to real processes — "spawn adventurer" = launch
   an engine, "slay dragon" = falsify an assumption, "collect invariant" = fold a law,
   "go whaling" = buy compute, "the party" = the engine bay.
4. **THE GENERATIVE CLAIM**: it's all of the games because the content generator is the
   percolated corpus — rooms from invariants, dragons from assumptions, loot from laws.
   The game grows exactly as fast as the fleet learns.

## THE DARK SOULS LAW (why dragons respawn)
Assumptions respawn — you will believe the wrong thing again in a new costume. That is not
a bug: each respawn is WEAKER (the correction shelf scales its HP down) and the player is
SHARPER (the Mickey law: smarter every death). The difficulty curve IS the learning curve.
