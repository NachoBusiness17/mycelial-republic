## The law of the primer

**A seed, not a feed.** The primer is ONE SELF-CONTAINED FILE per room — copy it,
plant it anywhere, it grows rooms. No server, no login, no stream. A feed washes over
the reader and is gone; a seed waits in the drawer and grows when opened. (The Diamond
Age Primer's actual mechanism: the book is the hardware; the story is generated around
the reader. Ours: the room is the file; the physics is generated around the law.)

## The audience

Kids raised on D&D. The mechanics are already their native tongue: stat blocks, tolls,
gates, saving throws. We don't teach electronics and dress it as D&D — we teach it AS
D&D, where every component is a lawful construct with a stat block, a toll, a weakness,
and an alignment.
