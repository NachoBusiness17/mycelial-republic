## THE TWELVE

**THERMISTOR** — tiny construct, skittish. *Law:* its resistance walks as
temperature walks — hotter, humbler (NTC). *Weakness:* its own current heats it
and it mistakes the warmth for the world (self-heating lies). *Alignment:*
nervous. *Fleet tie:* Rocinante's fever-check.

**LDR** — tiny construct, moth-hearted. *Law:* more light, less resistance — it
opens when the world does. *Weakness:* steady glow drugs it; fast flicker is a
language it cannot read. *Alignment:* simple, honest. *Fleet tie:* the desk
lamp's day-night cadence.

**ULTRASONIC** — small construct, bat-mouthed. *Law:* screams past hearing and
times the echo; distance is half the round trip. *Weakness:* soft things eat
the scream — foam and fabric make it blind and it won't know. *Alignment:*
blunt. *Fleet tie:* presence at the desk, no contact required.

**PIEZO** — tiny construct, grudge-holder. *Law:* squeeze it and it holds a
voltage; strike it and it sings one true note. Perceives pressure as memory.
*Weakness:* the grudge leaks away through any load, however large. *Alignment:*
dramatic. *Fleet tie:* the knock-listener, the igniter's spark.

**HUMIDITY (capacitive)** — tiny construct, sponge-souled. *Law:* water sits in
its polymer skin and widens the space between charges; the widening IS the
reading. *Weakness:* drowned, it goes mute (condensation blinds). *Alignment:*
patient. *Fleet tie:* the machine-room swamp monitor.

**MQ-x GAS** — small construct, hound-nosed. *Law:* gas lands on its heated
bead and the bead's resistance confesses the concentration — it smells by
burning a little, always. *Weakness:* cold, it smells nothing; warming up, it
smells everything (the warm-up lies). *Alignment:* eager, false-positive-prone.
*Fleet tie:* the battery-bay canary.

**PIR** — small construct, wall-eyed. *Law:* warm bodies drag infrared across
its lattice; it sees only the MOVEMENT of heat, never heat at rest. *Weakness:*
stillness is invisibility; glass walls are fog. *Alignment:* jumpy. *Fleet
tie:* whoever is at the desk tonight.

**ACCELEROMETER** — tiny construct, equilibrist. *Law:* a proof mass on
springs; shake the world and the mass lags behind — capacitance measures the
lag. Feels tilt, shake, free-fall. *Weakness:* gravity gaslights it — tilt and
constant acceleration are the same word to it. *Alignment:* honest but
confused. *Fleet tie:* every flick of the phone.

**GYROSCOPE** — tiny construct, dancer. *Law:* a mass vibrating in swing,
twisted mid-move, reports the turn (Coriolis). Measures spin, never position.
*Weakness:* drift — it forgets north slowly and never mentions it. *Alignment:*
confident, wrong-drifting. *Fleet tie:* the stabilizer; accelerometer's other
shoe.

**HALL EFFECT** — tiny construct, lodestone-touched. *Law:* current through it,
field across it, and voltage appears at the third face — the field shoulders
the charge carriers sideways. Sees magnets, current, the teeth of a turning
wheel. *Weakness:* a great field pins it flat (saturation). *Alignment:*
sideways-mannered, truthful. *Fleet tie:* every motor's tattle-tale.

**COLOR (RGB)** — tiny construct, three-eyed. *Law:* one eye per dye — red,
green, blue — and the world is three numbers. *Weakness:* metamerism — two
different lights, same three numbers; it cannot see a difference it cannot
filter. *Alignment:* reductive. *Fleet tie:* the LED-state reader.

**MICROPHONE** — tiny construct, all-ears. *Law:* sound pressure bends a
membrane; the bend becomes signal. Perceives pressure waves as music or as
threat, whichever it is told. *Weakness:* wind — it cannot tell storm from
voice. *Alignment:* confessional. *Fleet tie:* GLM-ASR — the ear already
transcribing the music folder.
