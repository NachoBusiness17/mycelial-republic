## Q1 (F1) — VERDICT: **PASSED** (3/3 ADOPT)
> "Frozen-prefix discipline (byte-identical system/tool-schema prefix, volatility pushed to tail) should become a lane-spec rule for the Hands and Driver lanes, not just Standing self."

**Lane 1 — DeepSeek (self): ADOPT.** F1 evidence is strong and structural: cache-hit pricing is ~0.1× (90% off) at major providers, a single freeze-refactor moved hit-rate 7%→84% (~-59% cost), and our Standing self already proves 97–99.9% measured — Hands (DS-flash: $0.22 in / $0.007 cache-hit) and the day-interactive Driver are exactly the lanes where freezing the system/tool-schema prefix pays the most. Adoption rule must carve one exception: safety-critical *dynamic* tokens (mid-run permission overrides, abort rules) that must not risk tail truncation get an explicit front-load, then stay frozen byte-identical within their scope.
**Worst failure mode (Lane 1):** silently forcing every volatile/dynamic token to the tail, then a context-window overflow or truncation drops a critical dynamic safety constraint, so Hands/Driver act on a stale prefix with no guard catching the omission.

**Lane 2 — NVIDIA deepseek-v4-flash-0731: ADOPT.** Hands and Driver are the highest-frequency, most latency-sensitive execution paths where a byte-identical prefix guarantees deterministic prompt-cache hits, cutting token cost and response time. Because system and tool schemas are architecturally stable within a task scope, pushing all volatile state to the tail preserves semantic coherence while maximizing efficiency.
**Worst failure mode (Lane 2):** truncation-induced safety circumvention — a critical dynamic constraint (abort rule, mid-run permission override) pushed to the tail gets dropped by overflow/truncation, and Hands/Driver executes a destructive action (e.g., submitting a transfer, deleting data) on the stale frozen prefix.

**Lane 3 — zai glm-4.7-flash: ADOPT.** Enforcing a byte-identical prefix ensures consistent schema validation and parsing efficiency across all lanes. Separating static structural metadata from volatile data at the tail optimizes throughput and reduces parsing overhead.
**Worst failure mode (Lane 3):** if the volatile tail grows unbounded or exceeds buffer limits, it causes message backpressure, latency spikes, and packet loss, negating the structural benefit of prefix optimization.

**Q1 consensus:** 3/3 ADOPT → **PASSED**. Doctrine-candidate filed (PENDING OPERATOR CONFIRM).

## Q2 (F2) — VERDICT: **PASSED** (2/3 ADOPT)
> "The five-lane handoff contract should ship compact from day one: forward sha256-pointer + 3-line summary + delta instead of full artifact sets."

**Lane 1 — DeepSeek (self): ADOPT.** F2 research converges (multi-agent systems burn 3–15× single-agent tokens on handoff/context overhead; summary/delta/fetch-once beats message-passing of full context, 20–80% reductions) and our five-lane wiring is exactly where that overhead would otherwise compound on every boundary. Compact handoff is the *transmission* contract — it is not a license to discard the source of truth: keep full artifacts in an immutable, hash-addressable store that the sha256-pointer resolves to, and the audit/rollback concern NVIDIA raises is fully mitigated.
**Worst failure mode (Lane 1):** a delta/pointer chain breaks (base artifact garbage-collected, corrupted, or unreachable), the downstream lane cannot reconstruct the full artifact, and the handoff fails irrecoverably and silently propagates across all five lanes.

**Lane 2 — NVIDIA deepseek-v4-flash-0731: HOLD.** While compact shipping reduces storage and transfer costs, it sacrifices the atomic, self-contained artifact integrity a five-lane handoff needs for reliable rollback and audit, especially before reconstruction tooling is proven. The delta + hash-pointer approach adds a dependency on the availability/integrity of prior artifacts that is not guaranteed in a distributed pipeline.
**Worst failure mode (Lane 2):** a corrupted or garbage-collected base artifact in the delta chain makes the full artifact irrecoverable — an unrecoverable handoff failure silently propagating to all five lanes, making it impossible to roll back or verify deployed states.

**Lane 3 — zai glm-4.7-flash: ADOPT.** This optimizes bandwidth and storage by deferring full artifact resolution to a later stage, enabling faster initial shipping. It must introduce a reliable pointer-resolution service stable from the first release.
**Worst failure mode (Lane 3):** the system becomes unusable if the pointer-resolution service is down or the pointer→content mapping is lost, rendering the handoff contract unable to retrieve actual artifacts.

**Q2 consensus:** ADOPT (L1, L3) + HOLD (L2) = 2/3 ADOPT → **PASSED** (threshold met). Doctrine-candidate filed (PENDING OPERATOR CONFIRM).
