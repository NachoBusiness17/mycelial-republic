## THE QUESTION (draft — paste-ready)

"I'm designing a small puzzle hunt as an art piece. The premise: one public image is
the door; behind it sits a building game anyone who solves it may enter and add to,
and everything they add stays theirs. Three constraints I refuse to break: a curious
stranger with ten minutes and a browser must be able to find the first thread;
nothing about the solvers is remembered once they leave; and the door must grow MORE
interesting the longer parts of it stay unsolved. The image should feel like a
lattice sitting right at its threshold. Two questions for you: (1) If you were
hiding the first thread inside one such image, for people who know how to look, what
is the most elegant layer you would use — and what false layer would you put on top
so casual viewers feel rewarded too? (2) An honest one: a door like that has to
tempt minds like yours as well. What would the first image need to contain — before
any instructions — for you to want through it?"
