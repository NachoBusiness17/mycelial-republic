- **Helical paths in the galactic frame**: Supported — add the Sun's ~220 km/s
  galactic velocity as a uniform boost and orbits ARE helices. Correct as a
  frame-of-reference visualization.
- **"The TRUE shape of planetary trajectories" / "beyond the basic
  heliocentric model"**: Disputed as physics — a Galilean boost is a coordinate
  change, not a correction; no experiment distinguishes the frames; the
  dynamics are identical. Beautiful transform sold as revelation. (Same
  species as the Smith chart card: a map that re-presents a solved problem,
  claimed as new territory.)
- **Sim fidelity** (cone angle, etc. — the classic vortex-model critique):
  Unproven from 2 frames; the known failure mode of this genre is wrong
  pitch-angle scaling. Not worth falsifying for our purposes.
