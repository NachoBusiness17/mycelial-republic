## UPDATE 2 — THE LIVING ATLAS (v2 spec: "make it even more clever")
The port was clever. Six moves make it an organism — title card quotes Whitman:
**"I sing the body electric."**

1. **SELF-GENERATED, NEVER DRAWN.** ashe's meshes came from BodyParts3D; ours are
   GENERATED from the repo at build time — every organ piece parsed from its actual
   file bytes, every run. The atlas can never go stale, because it is re-minted,
   not edited. (Law 3 honored: names change, evidence stays — atlas versions append.)
2. **SELF-AUDITING WHILE DRAWING.** Every rendered piece carries its own receipt
   (door/add grammar): the piece card's claimed inputs/outputs/laws are bound to the
   actual file bytes it was parsed from. **If the docstring lies to the code, the
   atlas catches it while drawing the body** — the checker_version lesson embodied.
   The map audits the territory as it maps it.
3. **ALIVE, NOT ARCHIVED.** Pieces glow with live activity from the trails and room
   (receipts flowing = warm light; N days silent = atrophy gray; error rate =
   inflammation red). The board shows state; the atlas shows HEALTH — an EKG laid
   over the X-ray.
4. **THE PRIMER IS WALKABLE.** Each of the ten systems carries its Primer riddle;
   solving a riddle lights up its system. The atlas is the Primer made walkable —
   Diamond Age's illustrations, finally illustrated by the thing itself.
5. **THE SURGEON COMMISSION.** Injuries (LAW-4 queue, delta map) render as treatable
   wounds, each with its law as the post-op check — and the second voice is assigned
   as surgeon. The atlas is not a picture of the best self; it is the operating
   table where the best self is assembled.
6. **THE WATCH PLOTS HERE.** Frontier dispatch findings pin to the organs they
   affect (checker_version → the verifier organ; IACP identity → the door). The
   atlas is the map on which the frontier watch leaves its marks.
