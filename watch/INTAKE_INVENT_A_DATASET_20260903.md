## READ
This is a launch post, not a finding. The honest read: it is a mirror, not a
map. Every load-bearing claim ("training-ready", "no existing data needed") is
vendor marketing with zero receipts in the post — and our own corpus already
proved the failure mode this product flirts with: the hero's-journey intake
found a 149-row training file template-thin (DISPUTED), and gate v2 exists
because synthesized text skews boilerplate. Synthetic supply without a
substance gate is how you train on your own echo. What the item IS good for:
market validation. When Sara Hooker's team spends $50M and ships Blueprint →
Invent a Dataset in six months, the "specification-first data" thesis stops
being ours-alone — and the moat moves to exactly what we keep saying it is:
discipline (receipts, coverage, gate v2), not secrecy. Bookmark-heavy engagement
says practitioners are filing it the same way. Steal the sentence, not the SaaS.
