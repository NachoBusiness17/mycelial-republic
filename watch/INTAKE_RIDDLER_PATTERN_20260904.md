2. **Translation-drift as a lens** — dual-use, both faces filed:

     that line held in the pasted exchange itself, and holds here.

     and falsifier briefs through translate→back, map where meaning drifts.
     Drift = fragility. Wherever our launch-law text or rubric axes drift

     fuzzy instructions is a seat that errs. Drift-map = hardening lens for
     fleet text. The operator's own use — rewriting asks more subtly — is
     this face.
