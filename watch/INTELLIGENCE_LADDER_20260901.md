# THE INTELLIGENCE LADDER — why intelligence does plumbable work, and how it stops (2026-09-01)

## The operator's question
Why does intelligence do work that fast API + tokens + mechanics could do? (The lenses — ponytail, Lessig — enhance asks; they don't execute them. Where's the boundary?)

## The measured answer from today's receipts
Of the estate's ~$0.06 API spend: nearly all went to *judgment-shaped* calls (scoring, distillation, the twin's reasoning, the senet tension). The mechanical work — hashing, dedupe, wave-running, ledger appends, the entire capture-net — cost **$0 in tokens** (node fs + fetch). The boundary held everywhere we built it, and broke everywhere we didn't (the 79M drift = intelligence doing a transcript-mirror's job).

## The deep self-analysis: WHY intelligence gets burned on plumbable work
1. **Trust asymmetry.** We ask models to do mechanical work because their output *feels* reviewed. The cure is receipts: mechanical-plus-verified beats intelligent-plus-assumed. (Today's proof: the hash-verified snapshot beat every "carefully written" summary.)
2. **Interface laziness.** The LLM is a universal interface — one prompt replaces five scripts. The hidden cost: intelligence spent on format-handling. The cure: shaped interfaces (registries, ledgers, fixed formats) that scripts can walk.
3. **The discovery premium is front-loaded.** Intelligence was genuinely needed to *discover* each pattern (the salon, the research, the reconciliation). Once discovered, the pattern is API-able forever. **Every discovery converts intelligence-work into plumbing permanently — that conversion IS the exponential curve.**

## THE INTELLIGENCE LADDER (the doctrine)
Every task migrates DOWN as it becomes known:
- **L0 FRONTIER** — novel judgment, no sea-shape (the ask, the arbiter, the designer)
- **L1 LENS** — distilled reasoning with citations (the salon seats, the twin's reasoning layer)
- **L2 ZOMBIE** — sea-retrieval + steered execution (memlance-piloted local model)
- **L3 PLUMBING** — pure API/script/hash (zero tokens, zero intelligence)

The fleet's compounding = the rate of migration down the ladder. Today's conversions: the battery (L1→L3, scripted), the zombie R0 (L0→L2, walked), the bubble-net (chat→L3), the scorer v1 (failed UP-migration attempt — the sea under-determines, some work can't descend yet: that's the mirror's cost, measured 0/5).

## Where intelligence genuinely stays
- **The ASK** — operator intent (the one thing that never descends)
- **The NOVEL** — situations the sea has no shape for (until they become shapes)
- **The ARBITER** — when mechanical outputs conflict (the elder's role)
- **The DESIGNER** — building the next rung

## The v5 statement
Intelligence is a *capital expense*, not an operating expense. Spend it on discovery, bank the pattern, execute with plumbing, and the bill for tomorrow's version of today's work approaches zero. The kingdom's law: **nothing that was intelligent yesterday should be intelligent tomorrow unless the sea says so.**
