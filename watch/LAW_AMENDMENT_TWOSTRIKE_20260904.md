# LAW AMENDMENT — TWO-STRIKE, NUANCED (2026-09-04, operator ruling)
> **[AUDIT-TYPED 2026-09-08]** status: LIVE · claims: two strikes signal a dead door to route around (SWITCH, not ABANDON) — bail on boredom, not cost — SUPPORTED (operator verbatim ruling + same-day Exhibit A misfire, third door read the image at zero cost) · falsifier: none stated in card

**Operator, verbatim:** "the two strike rule is good but it needs some nuance. abandoning
was to save waste we don't have that constraint anymore."

**THE OLD LAW (scarcity era):** two strikes on an approach → bail. Born when tokens were
the binding constraint (ghost-era OOM wars, quota walls, paid-lane famine). Bailing
saved the only scarce thing: compute.

**THE AMENDED LAW (abundance era):** two strikes is a SIGNAL ABOUT THE DOOR, not an
order to stop. The response switches from ABANDON to **SWITCH**: route around the dead
door and keep driving — third eye, other lane, different approach — because retries are
now near-free (unlimited flash in-ZCode, Lite windows, free lanes; economist receipted:
rented abundance; the binding constraints are FOOD, ATTENTION, and TIME, not tokens).

**What two-strike still protects:** the actually-scarce things —
1. OPERATOR ATTENTION (his time is quota),
2. JUDGMENT-SEAT CONTEXT (the 201M-token conversation shape),
3. CLOCK (day windows, Sep-20 proof deadline),
4. NEW INFORMATION: keep striking as long as each attempt teaches something; bail when
   attempts repeat without new signal.

**The test, restated:** *bail on boredom, not on cost.* Two identical failures with no
new information = stop. Two failures each naming a different dead door = keep going
through the third door.

**Exhibit A (the misfire it fixes, same day):** the seat hit two empty vision responses
and declared "vision is flaky — two-strike bail." The operator's rebuke: infinite eyes
exist (deepseek, 4_5v, grok, browser, codex). Third door read the image perfectly.
Token cost of the third try: zero. The old law would have left the operator's screen
unread to save tokens nobody was spending.
