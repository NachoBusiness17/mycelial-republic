**Verdict: "Blanket RED is overbroad. It collapses three regimes (copyright / CFAA /
contract) and three acts (read own screen / harvest the service / train a rival model).
Those are not the same offense."**

## The three regimes (rival's table, verbatim-compressed)
- **Copyright** — "Expression, not method. Output is typically assigned to you (OpenAI,
  Anthropic, xAI consumer, DeepSeek). Ideas, process, refusal style, tool-order
  heuristics are not protected expression. **Private notes of your Output ≠
  infringement. Distilling method ≠ copying expression.**"
- **CFAA / trespass** — "Unauthorized access, rate-limit bypass, hitting other users'
  data, server-side scraping. **Van Buren / hiQ: using valid credentials for the
  intended UI is not 'hacking.' Driving your own logged-in session like a screen reader
  is not server intrusion.**"
- **Contract (ToS)** — "Whatever they wrote. **This is the only place a blanket RED has
  bite.** Vendor-specific. **Not a crime; remedy is ban / civil ToS claim.**"

## Per-vendor contract bite (with citations)
- OpenAI: bans "automatically or programmatically extract data or Output" AND "Use
  Output to develop models that compete with OpenAI" (row-terms-of-use).
- Anthropic: owns Outputs BUT no automated/non-human access except API; AUP bars
  "model scraping / distillation" + training competing models; third-party harnesses
  on OAuth called out.
- xAI: user owns Output; AUP bars bots/"automated or non-human means", scraping, AND
  "distilling model data or Outputs" — broader than copyright.
- **z.ai: explicit ban on page scraping/bots/spiders AND using generated content to
  train/fine-tune external models. "Tightest scrape language."** (docs.z.ai ToU)
- **DeepSeek: assigns Output to you AND expressly allows "training other models (such
  as model distillation)" — then also recites a robots/spider ban. "Contract is
  internally sloppy; the distillation grant is real."**

## The (a)/(b)/(c) line — the real answer
- **(a) Assistive read/remember: CLEAN.** "Tool is the holder's hands/eyes on
  already-rendered, authorized UI; same legal family as a screen reader, copy-paste,
  or photographing your own monitor. No extra copies taken from the wire that the user
  wasn't shown."
- **(b) Prohibited extraction/bulk scrape: this is what the clauses aim at.** "Scale +
  non-session collection — unauthenticated crawlers, paging every conversation, hidden
  endpoints, bypassing robots/rate limits, taking other people's content, building a
  corpus off the service. **Intent and volume distinguish it from a prosthetic.**" —
  WE DO NOT DO (b).
- **(c) Train competing models on Output: the real line** — already excluded from our
  plan (license-clean training corpus); DeepSeek is the sanctioned distillation door.
