# LAW CANDIDATE 066 — SELF-ANSWER BEFORE WITNESS (2026-09-07, operator verbatim)

"We should always answer our own frontier questions as dogfood so we can prove that we are
getting something we don't already have or are capable of."

## The law

No frontier question is dispatched to a witness (external model, salon, commission) unattempted.
The seat seals its own best answer FIRST — including honest UNPROVEN — and only then hears the
witness. The delta between our answer and theirs is the measured gain.

## Why (the three proofs it buys)

1. **Proof of gain:** without a self-attempt, a witness's answer is unfalsifiable — we cannot
   distinguish "something we didn't have" from "something we already had, said prettier."
   Delta = learning, quantified.
2. **Proof of capability:** each self-answer attempt is a capability probe (tesuji: the answer
   attempt is the stone; honest UNPROVEN is the shadow). The boundary of what we can answer is
   measured, not assumed.
3. **Proof against cargo-cult validation:** receiving external answers with no baseline is the
   failure class that enthroned the DAI docx (C6) and would have enthroned the ghost verkle
   libraries. Baseline-first makes flattery detectable.

## Mechanics

- Seal the self-answer before dispatch (bash_local row, why = "self-answer before witness").
- On the witness's return, file a DELTA LEDGER with three verdicts per question:
  **GAINED** (witness answered what we couldn't — that is the receipt of new capability),
  **CONFIRMED-ALREADY-HAD** (matched our attempt — no gain, but our capability confirmed),
  **WITNESS-WRONG** (diverged from our verified answer — dissect the witness).
- UNPROVEN self-answers are lawful and expected; they set the difficulty floor. What is NOT
  lawful: dispatching unattempted.
