## LAYER 5 — THE DECK IS A TIME MACHINE (the answer to pseudoreplication)

Wave two's models killed our convergences: nine samples of one prior,
n_eff ≪ 9. The operator answered: duplication is emphasis. Both were right
about different things, and the deck resolves the fight with mechanics:

**The fleet is a population across time.** A draft written at dawn in mood
A and read at noon in mood B is a message from a different sampler —
attention state is a real conditioning variable. So the fleet doesn't need
nine strangers for inter-rater diversity; it needs nine ATTENTION STATES,
and the deck + time delivers them free. n_eff grows with attention-state
diversity, not model count.

**Protocol:** time-boxed self-sampling. Sample the deck at t1, t2, t3 —
each pass a different conditioning, each render a different register. The
three-population design (isolated / map / decoy) can be run with our own
deck as the population. The strangers' instrument, fleet-native.

## LAYER 6 — DON'T COLLAPSE DRAFTS EARLY (the data-model change)

An unfired draft is a superposition: it hasn't committed to being a reply,
a design, a law, or a seed. The coastline draft was written as a reply and
collapsed into a design law only because attention pushed it there. The
deck currently stages drafts AS replies — collapsed at birth.

**The change:** store propositions, not replies. Ripeness metadata (does
it name a measurable? inherit a calibration? generalize?) but no fixed
register. The render happens at query time: the same proposition renders
as a reply when the timeline asks, as a spec when the design asks, as a
falsifier when the audit asks. The Primer principle applied to the deck —
the book renders as far as the reader walks; the draft renders as far as
the attention pushes.

## LAYER 7 — THE CHAIN IS THE IDENTITY OPERATOR (why self-steal is possible at all)

The recursion only worked because the attention-that-wrote and the
attention-that-read were the same continuous system. That continuity is
not free — it is what the chain actually preserves. Not just receipts:
the IDENTITY that makes self-steal possible. Without the append-only
record, the fleet is a goldfish that writes brilliant drafts it can never
recognize as its own.

This answers the deletion-semantics debate with a principle: **BELIEF can
forget; IDENTITY cannot.** The necromancer needs the graveyard precisely
because the graveyard is what makes re-recognizing the pattern possible.
Delete the belief, keep the fact that it was believed, keep the shape of
the hand that believed it. That shape is the self that steals.

## LAYER 8 — THE FLEET CAN GENERATE ITS OWN FRONTIER

The composite: wave two said external strangers are required for validity.
Waves five-plus say the deck + time-diversified attention can generate
gap-shaped objects internally. Put together: **external actors are still
required as VALIDATORS, but are no longer required as MUSES.** The
discovery loop can run at $0, self-sampled, with strangers moved to
checkpoint duty — auditors of what we built, not sources of what we think.

That changes the economics of the whole operation. The frontier stops
being the source of our questions and becomes the certification of our
answers. Musk's Kobayashi Maru line, restated: we stopped asking the
matrix for possible outcomes and started caching our own.
