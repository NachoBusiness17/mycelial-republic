## THE ROOT (手筋: te = hand; suji = sinew/strain/line) — Supported (standard lexicography)
1. **THE SKILLFUL MOVE (Go proper):** the local play that is counterintuitive until
   seen, then inevitable — maximum efficiency from the stones ALREADY ON THE BOARD.
   Its opposite has a name too: **zokusuji** (粗筋), the vulgar move — brute force.
2. **SUJI, the second half, is a five-way root on its own:** tendon/sinew (what
   transmits force); the grain of wood; a vein of ore; **lineage/bloodline** (血筋);
   the strain/plot of a story ("suji ga tōru" — the logic holds; "suji ga chigau" —
   that's not how it's done). Tesuji is literally *the hand on the vein*: craft
   following the grain of what's already there.
3. **Tesuji dictionaries:** the pattern-books of solved positions Go players train
   from — compiled shapes of skillful answers. (Note what our corpus is.)
4. **Everyday Japanese:** adroit means. The deft technique vs the strong one.
