## THE COUNCILS THE SHERPA HOLDS
1. **THE PM** (conductor + economist): what needs doing, what it costs, what the envelopes allow.
2. **THE ENGINEER** (the forge): what needs building to place the next foothold.
3. **BERNAYS** (the breadcrumb renderer): takes the surveyed route and renders it as
   breadcrumbs in the workers' environment — steer tails, alpha masks, channel content.
   The route reaches the Mickeys as *their own idea of the path*.
4. **RESEARCH** (the smart seats + salons + this zcode seat): novel moves across tenuous
   gaps that dumber models could not survive. Never spend a Mickey on a tenuous gap.

## THE TWO LAWS
- **THE FOOTHOLD LAW**: a route is only valid where it anchors to EXISTING invariants
  (memory/percolator/invariants.json + the chain). The Sherpa surveys the invariant shelf
  FIRST; no fictional rock. If a gap has no foothold, the route goes to RESEARCH to place
  one — or the route does not exist.
- **THE MICKEY LAW**: expendables die and reprint SMARTER. Every death's lesson (gate fail,
  blocked wing, thin output) is distilled into the corrections shelf and re-printed into the
  next body. Getting smarter every death = the learned-and-lost law INVERTED at last: the
  shelf applies AT RESPAWN, automatically, or the death was wasted.

## THE LADDER THE SHERPA SERVES
alive (reconciler health) -> POSITION (gps: queue state, memlance memory-position, verkle tip
time-position) -> HEADING (next closest gap, Bernays breadcrumbs) -> SUMMIT (the fold: dossier,
brief, verdict). The economist pays; the Sherpa routes; the Mickeys climb; the basecamp folds.
