## THE STRATEGY

1. **REPLIES, not quote-posts.** Reply directly inside threads where conversation is
   already happening. Quote-posts get zero views from a low-follower account. Replies
   inside hot threads get seen by everyone reading that thread.

2. **The 5-minute window.** High-follower accounts posting within the last 5 minutes
   are the targets. Reply fast, before the thread fills up. Speed beats polish.

3. **CUA coordinate clicks, not playwright locators.** X renders transparent intercept
   divs that eat actionability-checked clicks. Locate with playwright (find and verify),
   click with CUA (bypass the overlay), confirm with playwright.

4. **Fresh tab per intent.** The SPA routing table pollutes after accumulated
   navigations. Open a new tab for each engagement burst. Close it after.

5. **Context stored outside the browser.** The reply text, the target URL, the thread
   state — all in files, not in SPA state. The browser is a dumb renderer; the files
   are the brain.
