## THE VERDICTS

### Top steals (ranked, cross-witness)
1. **THE DISTILLER — a compression model per lane-TYPE** (deepseek-pro #1). Our own
   trail law made lanes verbose BY DESIGN; the missing organ is the cheap model that
   ingests a lane's full trail and emits the 200-token state digest. Attacks
   context-discard (the field's loudest complaint) at the architectural layer. Feeds
   directly from the corpus library (fine-tuning = weights-day; the library's
   weights-eligible shelf is its training set + our own trails).
2. **GUARDRAILS AT THE HANDOFF BOUNDARY** (deepseek-pro #2; OpenAI pattern). We validate
   at review time, not transfer time. Amendment: every inbox drop gets ONE atomic gate —
   tri-state receipt + policy check + budget check — at the moment of handoff. Turns
   "no receipt = never happened" from logging convention into enforcement.
3. **TOOL-DESCRIPTION-TESTING AGENT** (both witnesses: ADOPT). Anthropic's receipt:
   one rewritten tool description cut task time 40% for every downstream agent. A lane
   that tests its own tool descriptions against usage logs and rewrites them = pure ROI.
4. **SCRIPT-AS-COORDINATOR for huge jobs** (cerebras #2; Claude Code dynamic workflows).
   For 500-file-class jobs, the coordinator is a deterministic script, not an LLM.
   Windowed until a job that size exists.
5. **TIME-TRAVEL REPLAY on the chain** (deepseek-pro #3). The verkle chain is immutable
   but not forkable; for post-mortems, fork at knot N and replay under a different
   policy. Cheap for us (chain exists) — turns audit trail into simulation engine.
6. **AGENT CARDS for discovery** — window/pilot (external seats publish capability
   manifests at URLs; our manifests already play this role internally).

### Protect with prejudice (ours, confirmed ahead of the field)
- **Singleton actuation** (one browser, one phone) — deepseek-pro: "Every multi-agent
  system in the field assumes parallel actuation is safe. You've correctly identified
  that shared real-world state is the actual bottleneck. This is your moat."
- **One scheduler organ owns all timers** — "no other system has made timer ownership a
  single point of architectural fact."
- **Chain + percolator as living memory** — findings FOLD back into the constitution;
  "audit log AND knowledge base — a duality no one else has."
- Tri-state receipts; constitution-as-cache (the resident prefix).

### Where BOTH sides are wrong (the blind spot — deepseek-pro's blade)
**Asking is treated as failure everywhere.** The field routes around humans or makes
them the sync point; OUR stall-ladder (stall→nudge→worth-eval→kill) treats asking as a
stall condition. TheAgentCompany's data: agents fail from NOT asking. Amendment:
**ASKING-AS-SENSING** — asking the operator/founder early is a positive action with its
own budget (a sensor for ambiguous intent), not an emergency override. Maps to A2A's
input-required as formal state; lands in our inboxes as a first-class row type.

## THE CHARTER AMENDMENT (what this comparison forces)
Add two clauses to the lane contract (v1.1):
- **Clause 9 — HANDOFF GATE:** every inbox drop is validated atomically at transfer
  (tri-state + policy + budget) before the lane acts on it.
- **Clause 10 — ASK BUDGET:** each lane holds an explicit ask-allocation; asking the
  operator early and cheaply is a sanctioned, receipted action — never a demerit.
Windowed builds: distiller (weights-day, corpus-fed) · tool-description tester (builder
next shift) · chain-replay (organ build) · script-coordinator (when a 500-file job lands).
