## 1. THE GUTENBERG THESIS, GROUNDED

Before the press: reproducing *validated* text was the bottleneck (scribes).
After: reproduction became free, and scarcity moved to what-to-print and
literacy — the Reformation and science followed the new scarcity. Before Sept 4:
formal proof was scribe-work (Buzzard: £1M over 5 years; mathlib as monastery;
Wiedijk's 20-year list). After: 13M lines in 11 days, kernel-checked, and
Vinogradov in 3 days on 3 consumer plans. **The generation bottleneck on formal
mathematics broke.** The new scarcities — the exploitable seams:

1. **Statements worth proving** — conjectures are the new ink.
2. **Semantic grounding** — the kernel verifies derivation, never intent; the
   review gap is where mill-output dies (LAW-061's frontier form).
3. **The compiled library** — mathlib won't take AI reviews, so kernel-clean
   corpora pile up as orphan repos: monasteries again, and nobody is building
   the guild that indexes them.
4. **Zero-label-noise corpora** — 13M lines where the kernel is the label: the
   new paper, and we have a mill-sized press.

The world is watching the model. Nobody is building the around. **The press was
never the exploit; the guild that learned to print was.**
