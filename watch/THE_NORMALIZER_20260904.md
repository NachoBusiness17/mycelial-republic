## THE STANDARD (every orphan, lesson, root, and lost datum gets all four verbs)

1. **WIRE** — attach to its consumer. An unwired finding is a half-wire (the gap map's
   original sin: writers without readers). Wire = named lane inbox / manifest clause /
   RIB / curriculum slot / build order. No orphan is "folded" until its CONSUMER exists.
2. **TRANSDUCE** — normalize into structure, not prose: a law-lattice row, a manifest
   clause, an FT-rule, a RIB block, a machine-checkable condition where expressible.
   Prose is the input format, never the storage format.
3. **DISPOSE** — one disposition from: FOLDED (structure + consumer + receipt) ·
   TOMBSTONED (dead, kept visible) · ROUTED (waiting on a gate, TTL stamped) ·
   SHELVED (REEMBER shelf, reason attached). Every disposition lands in the digest.
4. **SUBSTRATE** — the finding sits on something structural: the lattice (queryable),
   the trails (append-only), the manifests (read-at-spawn), the chain (tamper-evident).
   If it lives only in a card's prose, it hasn't landed yet.

## RIGHTSIZED + QUANTIZED + LAW (the three properties of a normalized piece)
- **RIGHTSIZED**: stated at the scope it actually covers (no law-generalization from
  n=1; tri-state carries the uncertainty).
- **QUANTIZED**: the skill it embodies is expressible as a tool/step/gate a dumb agent
  can execute (skill quantization doctrine — structure carries it, not the model).
- **LAW-GRADE**: where it binds, it lands in the LAW LATTICE (machine-readable, exported
  via lattice_logic to every surface agents read) — Lessig: the structure makes them
  abide, not their intelligence.
