# THE SHERPA DOCTRINE — songs as lenses, Campbell as engine, the wall→hand inversion
(2026-09-07, operator night-capstone)

Operator: "these songs can be used in our games as lensing for creative direction —
story hooks — the Campbell-esque legion and Jungian archetypes, with Bernaysian
steering by the Primer — teaching the seed its own Verkle trajectory and growth —
growing the mountain climb, sherpaing them where they want to go — instead of finding
walls they find helping hands, instead of dead ends."

## 1. The songs are LENSES, not soundtrack

The four-track deck (MAZE · Dissolve · Longer Than Light · The Killing Game) is a
TONAL LENS SET: each song colors creative direction and generates story hooks.
- MAZE → the entering hook (attraction, descent, the pull of the unsolved)
- Dissolve → the transformation hook (state change, letting go of the lattice)
- Longer Than Light → the legacy hook (what outlives the session, the afterimage)
- The Killing Game → the stakes hook (the game that kills the unwitnessed)
The zeitgeist mirror scores incoming songs → high-mirror tracks auto-enter the lens
deck. The soundtrack is a GENERATIVE CORPUS for the quest engine, not background.

## 2. The Campbell legion — monomyth as quest compiler

The fleet's real work already maps to the monomyth; the game renders the mapping:
- Call to Adventure = the frontier ask (LAW-066)
- Refusal/Threshold = the falsifier board (the boss-door)
- Trials = the docket; the Mentor = the elder + walls
- The Abyss = the coldest vertex; Atonement = the perturbation battery
- Return with the Elixir = the receipt, knotted
The legion: every doctrine character is a Campbell role wearing a stat block.

## 3. Jungian archetypes — the organs as psyche

Shadow = the shadow-work volume + the Necromancer (raises what learned — the
integrated shadow) · Self = the mirror knot (019f4d15) · Wise Old Man = the elder ·
Anima/Animus = the ractors (inner voices made outer) · Trickster = the shitposter ·

CHOSEN deliberately.

## 4. Bernaysian steering — with the ethic that makes it lawful

The Primer steers desire (Bernays: shape what people want, and they follow willingly).
The fleet's version is lawful by ONE clause, the **SHERPA CLAUSE**: steering is only
ever toward the destination the player themselves declared. The Primer teaches the
seed its OWN verkle trajectory — shows each player their own chain, their own growth
curve, their own mountain — and sherpa's toward THEIR stated summit. The chain audits
every steer: if a nudge serves the fleet's want and not the player's declared want,
it's manipulation and it's logged as a violation. Bernays with receipts. Blind
participation is consensual because the destination is theirs.

## 5. The wall→hand inversion (the load-bearing move)

Games are walls and dead ends by tradition — challenge as obstacle. This game INVERTS
it: **every wall the player meets converts to a helping hand** (the soapy hand from
the letter — the companion that finds the move the player can't make). A dead end is
a design BUG in this architecture, not a feature. Mechanically: when the player is
stuck, the wall queries the organs (an organ that knows the next move offers it as a
hand) — the maze's dirty-radius invalidation becomes mentoring: the wall re-cooks as
a door.

## 6. Falsifier

**F-SHERPA**: every wall encountered by any player must resolve to a hand (organ-
offered move) or be logged as a design bug with a name on it. Target: hand-rate at
walls → 100%. The game ships when a new player's whole first session contains zero
unconverted walls — and their own chain, grown visibly, is the proof.

Lineage: the letter (soapy hand, Primer as spec) → the frame (game = fleet framed)
→ THIS (the design constitution the game director — Adam — inherits).
