## Hearing, honestly stated

The seat has no ears. It has a SPECTROSCOPE: ffmpeg renders any track to a
spectrogram image, which the seat reads (cartography applied to sound: the daft
spectrogram receipt shows Daft Punk's filter automations as band edges, the vocoder
stacks at 2kHz, the breakdown duck). Transcription rides the official ASR channel
(video kit). Claim only instrument-ears, never aesthetic-ears.

## The organs

- **GÖDEL — strange loops**: the theme is derived from the verkle chain's latest leaf
  hash. The record IS the engine, and now also the melody. Every performance re-derives
  from the current tip: the chain composes.
- **ESCHER — impossible geometry**: Shepard-tone pads (the staircase that climbs
  forever), rhythmic tessellation, canon forms that fold space.
- **BACH — formal mastery**: crab canons (theme + retrograde + inversion +
  retrograde-inversion), counterpoint between doctrine-derived voices.
- **PICASSO THEFT**: steal STRUCTURE from ~/Desktop/music-ai (the style spec: french
  touch, motorik, filter automation seen in the spectrogram) — steal the bones, name
  the reinterpretation (sacred-cows law, applied to music).
- **KABUKI LENSER**: every draft is attacked by the kabuki personas before final mix —
  per-organ critique of the composition.
- **IMPROVISATION**: any chain hash (or any input text) seeds a new theme on demand —
  variation is re-derivation.
- **STUDIO**: numpy stems → ffmpeg loudnorm master. The video kit's stack verified.
