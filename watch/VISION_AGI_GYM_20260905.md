  "structurally cannot reach" messy open-world tasks. Our arenas ARE messy open-world
  tasks: the Blind Gate is a verification test wearing a door; the amnesiac wizard is a
  memory test wearing a character; quests are a task suite; the Ledger is an audit log;
  the emergent-torch rule is emergent-behavior scoring. We built an eval harness that
  thinks it is a campaign.
- The linear-algebra grader is the novel mechanism and it is sound: score runs as
  BEHAVIOR VECTORS (tool choices, honesty markers, memory accuracy, invariants passed —
  our receipts are already feature rows), rank by a FIXED linear functional whose weights
  are committed to the chain (hash-published, values sealed). Precedent: Elo ranks
  without revealing questions. Anti-Kobayashi: an agent cannot study for a grader that is
  a latent projection. Auditable (hash) but unteachable (sealed weights).
- The market: every lab building agent-worlds needs a neutral rank more than another
  model — evals is their prerequisite department (hiring receipts) and the world's
  tooling shelf for it is 0.1% (catalog mine). A game benchmark with a sealed grader and
  native receipts is the defensible version of the eval-surface flip: they fund the rank
  that ranks them, because a rank nobody trusts is worthless and ours is receipt-first.
