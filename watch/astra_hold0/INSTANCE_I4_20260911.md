- **R1.2 (family derivation; PARTITION A — the numerator's inclusion rule):**
  a knot's family is its `session_id` after ONE suffix strip: if the
  session_id matches a UUID shape
  (`8-4-4-4-12` lowercase hex, hyphenated) it stays WHOLE; otherwise strip a
  trailing run matching `-t?[0-9a-f]{9,}` (one hyphen, optional literal `t`,
  then 9+ lowercase hex characters) exactly once. Shorter digit runs (a
  multiplier like `x10`, `x14`) are NOT suffixes and never strip; a

  without the exact pattern. It is validated against the census card's own
  published strata (desk-side, §F6.5).
- **R1.3 (selection):** rank families by knot count descending; break ties by
  family name ascending in byte order; take exactly 5.
- **R1.4 (the share):** share = (knots in the top-5 families) / denominator,
  carried to 4 DECIMAL PLACES, ROUND HALF-EVEN. Intermediate counts stay exact
  integers; round only the final share.
