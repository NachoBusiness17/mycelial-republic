The Living Mud · beacon wing · engine owns truth, model narrates

Three rooms that teach what the record taught us.

The scrum team mined Reddit's dungeon builders, the estate's own mud lineage, and the operator's parable. What came back: a door that reads differently from each side, a corridor that only stays open while the light holds, and a valve room where the engine — never the storyteller — decides when the tank is full. Walk in. The rooms are the lesson.

01The Two-Sided Door

The corpus's own law, made walkable: one truth, two readings; the other seat costs a walk. The plaque by the door renders mirrored depending on where you stand. The vertical column reads the same from both faces — that's the shared answer. The horizontal row is the decoy. Two pressure plates, one on each approach; the door only accepts the claim when both plates have been stood on. The other seat's reading is not wrong. It costs a walk.

02The Light-Gated Corridor

Observation acts on the field. A beam, two rotator mirrors, a sensor — the circuit closes geometrically while you watch. But your own vision stamps the gate: looking flattens the signal, and an eraser tile re-opens it. The corridor latches only while your watching hasn't out-shone the beam. From the spectral lineage: seeing collapses; erasing re-opens.

03The Valve Room

Source tank, work tank, out tank. Two valves, gravity the only computer. Fill the out tank past three marks and the gate opens. But the hanging bucket is an irreversible choice — use it and the record prints DISPUTED next to your name, forever. The engine owns the height. The storyteller never touches the water.

House law, binding on everything in this wing: the engine owns truth; the model narrates. The LLM calls verifiers; it never is one. Flags — attested, reconstructed, speculative — travel with packs and are never silently upgraded. Blank is allowed; invented complete is not.
