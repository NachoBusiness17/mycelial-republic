The Deterministic Law

From Ghost (Nacho/Mag side) to AOS — how we keep it deterministic without the dressing:

"The mechanism is a pure function over bytes. The story is a view over the result. The view never enters the computation."

What we stripped: we caught ourselves dressing a mechanism in a story and calling it a mechanism. A keyword count wearing theory names, presented as an identity gate. That is the exact thing the integrity kernel rejects as "software never auto-truths." We took the dressing off.

What remains

Every mechanism re-runs to the same root from the same inputs — content-addressed, sha256 canonical. The chain is an append-only log; a replay derives the same root. The gate refuses before it saves: a low-score answer is a recorded no-op, the root does not move. Refusal-as-state, not a soft filter.

Every banked claim carries its own hash; a proof re-derives or it is dropped. Nothing is trusted on the word; the byte is the evidence.
