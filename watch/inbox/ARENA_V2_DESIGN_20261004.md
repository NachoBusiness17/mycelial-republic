Hourly: one campaign scene advancing the season (rotating S1 gate variants,
then S2/S3/S4 as persistence lands) + one tavern interlude at the
calibrated pressure. Daily: one full-campaign descent at season milestones,
one gap-analysis pass on the season's own receipts, one fill-ball on the
register. The cap resets at midnight UTC; the season does not reset.
