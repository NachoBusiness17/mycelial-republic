## SELF-STEAL VERDICTS ON THE ORIGINAL PLAN

**Wing Chun (economy of motion):** the five-brief plan was five separate engagements.
The fold: ONE brief, ONE corpus curator, ONE training run. The curator IS the centerline.

**Monroe (state discipline):** the original plan had no state-re-entry design. The
trained model must re-derive from the ledger, not accumulate — the training corpus
must include re-derivation examples (state files → design decisions), not just
receipt → prose. Add: every training pair must be re-derivable from disk.

**Jobs (selection):** the falsifier was right but soft ("≥2x base rate"). Sharpen:
the trained model must produce a design direction the free lanes can execute WITHOUT
clarification, scored by the BFS gate on pass rate. If the free lanes ask questions,
the model failed.

**Elder (unqueried states):** the training corpus itself is an unqueried state — what
bias does it encode? The receipts over-represent tonight's wins (17 promotions) and
under-represent the 16 rejections. A model trained on wins-only will design like
something that never failed. FIX: the corpus MUST include the rejection receipts
with their gate verdicts as negative examples — the model learns what the gate
refuses, not just what it promotes.

## THE ASTRA BRIEF (rewritten)

Design the frontier-thinking training corpus curator + QLoRA recipe for a local
gemma-class model. Input corpus: the fleet's own receipts (gate verdicts, law text,
inscriptions, architect docs, rejection records). Output: {situation → design_direction}
pairs where the direction is executable by a free lane (BFS-gate-verifiable). Include:
- the rejection receipts as negative examples (the model learns what the gate refuses)
- re-derivation examples (state files → design, not memory → design)
- the Sherpa planning prompt (rank, decompose, add basis vectors)
- the evaluation battery: 10 held-out fleet decisions, scored by gate pass rate
- QLoRA config for gemma-2b/4b on available hardware
- the falsifier: trained model must produce gate-passing design directions at ≥2×
  base rate on held-out decisions, without needing clarification
