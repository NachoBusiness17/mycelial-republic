## SOCRATIC TEASE (five questions, answered from the record)

1. **What are a visitor's first ten seconds?** Today: scroll a landing page.
   Target: they are *walking*. Movement is the first proof that this is not a
   template. Arrow keys move them room to room before they read a word.
2. **What does "the website is the game" mean minimally?** A room graph (6
   rooms), a walker (keys + doors + nav, all of it), and a you-are-here
   instrument. A game is a state machine you move through — the site's sections
   already are one; we surface it.
3. **What do we steal from Moonshot?** Verified from the live page (screenshot
   + computed styles): pure black, pixel-mono display type, one canvas driving
   the whole scene, cinematic room-scale sections, "Join us / Build your own
   job" — the audacity register. Steal the shape, not the font (Fusion Pixel is
   theirs; ours is the monospace dither treatment).
4. **What do we steal from ourselves?** Everything, finally on the surface:
   shadow maze = rooms + light + descent; eigen-map = the minimap with a
   you-are-here torch; thought GPS = the room HUD ("ROOM 2/6 — RECEIPTS");
   smith chart = Γ values on the receipt ledger; the descent order = the room
   order itself (walk = narrative).
5. **Where is the higher-dimensional decision making?** It becomes the page:
   the minimap IS the eigen-map; the HUD IS the position fix; the receipts
   carry Γ; the walker's room graph is the same chain/star question we metered
   on the cathedral. The site stops describing the instruments and starts
   being one.
