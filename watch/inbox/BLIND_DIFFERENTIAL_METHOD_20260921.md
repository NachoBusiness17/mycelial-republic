# METHOD CARD — THE BLIND DIFFERENTIAL PROBE (operator practice, named + formalized)
**ts:** 2026-09-21T19:20Z · **source:** operator, window close, verbatim practice:
"I'll send a blind prompt cheaply as an initial test on free lanes on models to
do some ground work on what kind of differences models are returning and then
have it mine that nuance through interagency comms"

## The loop, formal
1. **One blind prompt** — same words, no context, no briefing; the only variable
   across runs is the model itself.
2. **Free lanes** — cost of the probe ≈ 0; the free tier is the sensor array.
3. **The DIFFERENCE is the finding.** Not the answers — the deltas: where models
   diverge is where their training, structure, and defaults are exposed. Same
   input, many returns: the spread is a measurement of the model landscape.
4. **Route the nuance through interagency comms** — the deltas become work
   handed to the fleet: peer handoffs, rib_bus, queue items. Other seats mine
   what the spread revealed.

## Doctrine tie (the operator's own decode)
This is test-time communication in practice — the decoded thesis that model
differences are trainable, exploitable signal ("the swarm was never the
vulnerable part, the steering was"). The blind probe measures the swarm's
spectral spread; the interagency routing steers by it. Also the mosaic seed:
many normals of one surface — the spread IS the shared sight, measured.

## Law (method card form)
**Every blind probe is filed: one prompt, N returns, the delta notes, routed or
not — tracked. A spread not filed is a measurement thrown away.**
