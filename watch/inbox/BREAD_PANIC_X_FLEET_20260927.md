# THE BREAD PANIC × THE FLEET — developedbyed's 200-villager experiment — 2026-09-27

The drop: 200 AI villagers (Jev/TypeSafe agents). One operator-seeded lie —
"bread is about to run out." It wasn't. **156 of 200 believed within 18
hours; the bakery sold out three days straight.** Day 3: physical truth
appears (a flour cart at the bakery; a villager who'd seen the mill). It
wins by day 5 — 172/200 disbelieve the rumor — but the truth needed 2+ days
to reach as many people as the lie reached in 18 hours, and **the bakery
kept selling out even after the correction landed.**

## Why this one lands here (it is a fleet mirror, five ways)

1. **The lie's transmission function is the percolator echo.** One seeded
   row → 156 reproductions in 18h ≈ the 142×-per-event inflation the
   quantizer measured on our own invariant counts. Agents don't propagate
   information; they propagate *statements about statements*. The fleet
   already demoted raw counts to provenance for exactly this reason —
   this experiment is that law, run in someone else's village.

2. **The correction's carrier was PHYSICAL WITNESS, not assertion.** What
   beat the rumor wasn't counter-speech (none is mentioned working) — it
   was a cart agents could SEE and a mill one agent had VISITED. Bytes on
   disk. That is our "trust bytes, not reports" law proven in silico: in a
   population of LLMs, first-hand state beats testimony. The estate's
   drift.py exists because our metrics can lie while the referent sits
   still; the villagers' panic ended when two of them carried receipts.

3. **The hysteresis tail is the FKB signature-pressure shape.** Belief
   reverted at ~day 5 but *behavior* (sell-outs) persisted past the
   correction. Our failure_kb: sigs echo for weeks after the underlying
   bug dies — 5,000+ counts of "command not allowlisted: powershell" long
   after the fix. Correction updates the map; the strategy layer keeps
   acting on the old map. Any fleet cure for a false belief must target
   the BEHAVIOR cadence, not just the belief row.

4. **The operator seeded the lie — the genesis problem in miniature.** One
   trusted-channel post from the top and 78% of the population flips. Our
   defense is already built and today-proven: append-only ledgers with
   cross-signed voids (the misfire reversal), hash-chained grounding rows,
   the census-carrier tripwire. The village had none; its correction had to
   arrive by cart.

5. **The win condition was timing, not victory.** The truth WON and the
   cost was still three days of empty shelves. For the fleet: a drifted
   metric caught on day 5 instead of hour 1 still burns real tokens on
   day 1-4 work. That is the actual argument for the 08:30 fossil arm, the
   census patrol, and chain_repair standing verbs: shrink the
   days-between-lie-and-cart.
