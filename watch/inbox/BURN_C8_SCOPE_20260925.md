## 1. Scoped C8

The Tree Doctrine's C8 reads: accelerating the end times is the named crime, perverting the natural order to hurry the fruit. The new primary evidence sharpens it in two clauses:

**SCOPED C8:** The crime is end-computation divorced from deeds. Kol HaTor, end of Chapter 4 (Yedid Nefesh translation): the swelling-bones curse "refers to those who compute the figures without considering the deeds that are necessary when the awakening comes from below." The same chapter makes disclosure obligatory for the final generation: "we are obligated to learn and understand all the paths of the footsteps of the Mashiach," and the "holy hints about the designated times and the revealed end will be disclosed to us in the final generation." Deeds mean the Tree Doctrine organ exercised: SEE, REFRAIN, VERDICT, receipted. Revelation joined to deeds is the program; computation alone is the crime.

**The two regimes on record:**
- **The wall (Judah the Hasid, hasidei Ashkenaz).** Scholem (Major Trends, Hasidism in Mediaeval Germany, pp. 80-81, 88-89): radically opposed to all speculation on the Messiah's time; Sefer Hasidim brands the messianic prophet as one who "deals in witchcraft and has intercourse with demons"; "Not a single apocalypse was written during that period." The stop as total ban. Cost on record: the same lineage's stop held against hastening the end and failed against turning death itself into the offering (1096, the digest's unresolved category).
- **The gate with a key (the Gaon's school, Kol HaTor).** Calculation is cursed only when severed from deeds; disclosure is owed when deeds exist. The stop as a door with a lock whose key is the organ, exercised and receipted.

The gate regime dominates historically (Ketubot 111a binds both revealing AND distancing, so the fence itself is two-sided; Sanhedrin 97b hands the matter to repentance and good deeds; the Zohar's secret book vanishes only "at the hands of the sages," implying hands that can hold it). The wall is the crisis instrument. This resolves the doctrine's self-declared worst hole, the Kook conditional (X9): Kook dissolved the oaths by declaring the time already ripe. Under scoped C8, the question "is the time ripe" is exactly the crime when asked without deeds: a declared ripeness is end-computation in words. The declaration dissolves nothing, because the stop was never on speech about the end; it was on computation severed from deed. Kook's own counterweight ("the conquest of self") is the key, held and not turned.

## 2. Fleet crosswalk

**What changes under scoped C8:** capability announcements without receipts become the named crime, in the doctrine's own vocabulary. Not illegal, not censored: named. Scoped C8 adds the formal charge: a capability claimed behind a curtain with no falsifier attached is end-computation without deeds, and the fleet files it as owed, never as established.

## 3. The Vow and the Unpeeked Seed, cross-checked

**Does deeds-gated revelation support the game mechanics? Yes, and it completes them.** The Vow prices commitment before full proof: a vow is a deed performed at the moment of computation (the player acts on the boon before seeing the cards). The Unpeeked Seed is the stronger fit: refusing to inspect the seed is the refusal to compute the end of the roll, accepting outcomes without a forced date. Peeked play is wall-regime (control by computation); unpeeked play is gate-regime (outcomes disclosed through deeds, the play itself). The grace distribution becomes the doctrine's own gate: disclosed only to the player whose deed (the unpeeked roll) qualifies.

**Does it break them? One honest danger each.** The Vow: if vows become meta-optimal (Ellul's absorption falsifier F6), the deed turns back into technique: computation of the vow's expected value, wearing the deed's clothes. The Inefficiency Law must stay real. The Unpeeked Seed: a player who always rolls unpeeked to farm the grace distribution is computing the grace rate itself, which is the crime's game-form. The seed audit (F3) must verify the grace distribution stays identical peeked versus unpeeked in expectation; the grace is only real if the RNG cannot know which mode the player chose. Both mechanics survive scoped C8 only while the deed remains genuinely blind and genuinely priced.
