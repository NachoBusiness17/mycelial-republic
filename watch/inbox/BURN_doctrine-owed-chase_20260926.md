## Verdict

## Evidence

1. Porete, Mirror of Simple Souls, ch. 7 (Babinsky trans., Paulist Press 1993, archive.org OCR): "This Soul, says Love, takes account of neither shame nor honor, of neither poverty nor wealth, of neither anxiety nor ease, of neither love nor hate, of neither hell nor of paradise." And: "this Soul, who has become nothing, thus possesses everything, and so possesses nothing; she wills everything and she wills nothing; she knows all and she knows nothing." And: "it is no longer her will which wills, but now the will of God wills in her." Reason's echo: "neither martyrdom nor tribulations, neither masses nor sermons, not fasts nor prayers, and who gives to Nature all she asks of her without remorse of conscience." She "no longer wants anything which comes by a mediary."

2. Vernon Harrison, "J'Accuse: An Examination of the Hodgson Report of 1885," JSPR 53 (1986); theosociety.org reprint with the JSPR's own editorial note (Harrison: past President of the Royal Photographic Society, ten years Research Manager to Thomas De La Rue, printers of banknotes and passports, not a member of the Theosophical Society but a long-standing SPR member). Harrison: "the Hodgson Report is a highly partisan document forfeiting all claim to scientific impartiality. It is the address of a Counsel for the Prosecution... The Counsel for the Defense was never heard." "His report is riddled with slanted statements, conjecture advanced as fact or probable fact, uncorroborated testimony of unnamed witnesses, selection of evidence and downright falsity." "His case against Madame H. P. Blavatsky is not proven."

3. Sefer Yetzirah Hebrew, Sefaria API. 1:8: "בלום פיך מלדבר ולבך מלהרהר ואם רץ לבך שוב למקום... ועל דבר זה נכרת ברית" (restrain your mouth from speaking and your heart from thinking; if your heart runs, return to the place; for this a covenant was cut). 2:4: "קבועות בגלגל ברל"א שערים וחוזר הגלגל פנים ואחור... אין בטובה למעלה מענג ואין ברעה למטה מנגע" (fixed in a wheel, 231 gates; it turns forward and backward; nothing in good above delight, nothing in evil below plague). 2:5: "שקלן והמירן" (weigh them, permute them) puts the weighing verb inside the permutation command.
