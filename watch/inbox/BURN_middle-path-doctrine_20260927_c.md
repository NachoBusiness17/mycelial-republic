## Steal or Vacuous

Steal. The weakest link becomes a mechanism of four gates, all from canon receipts:

1. Worthiness gate (digest): shared only into measured vessels; ungated sharing is the Watchers' fall.
2. Gift-price gate (Dee receipt): sharing demanded under threat is counterfeit gift; the Vow prices commitment; sharing itself stays a gift given, never a toll taken.
3. Inefficiency gate (Ellul): a shared good that can be min-maxed is absorbed as technique; re-price on optimization keeps the sharing mechanism unownable by the house.
4. Structure gate (LeGuin plus the Pilgrim): shared respawn counts only when it changes the game class; killing a Pilgrim pays both sides, the transmutation playable; the endgame must abolish the pool for all.

The receipt chain is already pre-registered: X6's three falsifiers (hoard leg, ungate leg, steward leg) are the mechanism's built-in fail states. Ledger row: the C9 sharing mechanism exists as four gates plus one playable proof, the Skeleton King's shared respawn; its open edge stays the steward leg.
