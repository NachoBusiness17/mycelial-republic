## Steal or Vacuous

Four of five game-design laws adopt; none vacuous.

1. Settlement-at-death wallets: ADOPT. VM Virtue-Marks settle only at run end, forfeit on early leave; "so it goes" death text is the settlement screen. Bounds Vow farming and gives F6 a ledger.
2. Bounded interest: ADOPT the bound half. Vow-held VM pays faithfulness interest, hard-capped; the reward half is hostage to F6: meta-min-maxed Vow yield re-prices to zero (Ellul: faith must cost).
3. Prerequisite-pair graphs: ADOPT; the atlas names it "The boon-draft lane's direct blueprint." Twelve boons become a cross-run pair-gated codex; a pair needs boons from two gods; the named completion payout is a god's meter crossing tortoise-to-thunderbolt.
4. Care loops: ADOPT. The shared VM pool gets a care-debt meter: presence-locked feeding, neglect bleeds yield. The Omelas child becomes mechanical, so F7 abolition is real severance; the meter IS the Small Gods belief surface.
5. Wealth-targeted threat checks: ADOPT as a pre-patch gate. Any broadcast- or Vow-driven summon that can aim a threat at another faction's pool share ships with faction-scope gates and rate limits before F1 playtest.

Laws 6-8 are program laws (fetch, no-fabrication, salvage), not game design: not applicable; this receipt already honors them (thin search reported thin).
