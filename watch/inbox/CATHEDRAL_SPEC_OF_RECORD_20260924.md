# Cathedral — smith convergence ruling (groq gpt-oss-120b over deepseek+groq drafts)

**1. WHERE THEY AGREE**  
- The world is a single “water‑clock” whose **OUT tank** supplies the only mutable resource.  
- **water_computer → fractal_maze.field** is the primary flow of that resource.  
- The **fractal_maze** has a scale‑1 walkable field and a scale‑2 “flood” crossing that triggers the next stage.  
- **audit_dungeon** is the ledger; touching a file folds a **Verkle knot** (audit checkpoint).  
- **doctrine_library.lens_gate** opens only after **ten lenses** are collected.  
- **riddler_optics** is the optics garage that the lenses must pass through.  
- **riddler_engram** is a ten‑seat “session” that resolves the invariants.  
- **adventure_2d6 (river_ford)** links the water‑computer gallery back to the start.  
- **blob‑double** enforces the universal “push‑duplicate‑not‑slide” movement law across all modules.  
- The only shared currency is a single scalar (head / computation token) that is earned, spent, and capped.  
- All invariants listed in **RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1** are mutually accepted.

**2. WHERE THEY DIVERGE (chosen design)**  
- **Resource model:** Spec A’s “head” measured in ticks with a hard cap of 10 is clearer and easier to balance than Spec B’s multi‑step token conversion system.  
- **Audit tracking:** Knot‑per‑file (Spec A) gives immediate feedback; a 30 000‑file counter (Spec B) is opaque and needlessly large.  
- **Lens acquisition:** Collecting lenses in the **doctrine_library** after the audit (Spec A) centralises the task; scattering fragments in the keep and dashboard (Spec B) dilutes the narrative.  
- **Module order:** The linear path from 2d6 crossroads → river_ford → water_computer → maze → audit → library → optics → engram → classic keep → blob‑double (Spec A) preserves the “water‑clock” metaphor; the more circuitous order of Spec B adds unnecessary back‑tracking.  
- **Reward values:** The simple +1 / +3 / +5 head rewards of Spec A give transparent pacing; Spec B’s fractional CT losses and bonuses complicate play without adding depth.  
- **Exit loop:** Spec A’s “return to OUT tank” via the river_ford closes the loop cleanly; Spec B’s “Unified Computation Core” introduces an extra abstraction that isn’t needed for the core experience.

**3. THE CATHEDRAL SPEC OF RECORD**  

*Through‑line:* The player begins at the **2d6 crossroads**, answers the river riddle, and steps onto the **river_ford** that opens the **water_computer.gallery**. By solving the fill‑sequence puzzle the OUT tank rises, generating **head** (one tick per water unit). Overflowing the OUT tank floods the **fractal_maze** field; walking the scale‑1 field costs no head, but crossing the scale‑2 bend consumes one head and lifts the player into the **audit_dungeon.atrium**. Inside the audit dungeon the player folds a Verkle knot for every file touched (costing one head each) and explores the 14‑room audit layout until all ten **lenses** are discovered in the **doctrine_library**. Depositing the ten lenses opens the **riddler_optics.garage_gate**, where the player guides a light ray through the lenses, earning the **engram token**. The token activates the **riddler_engram** ten‑seat session; each seat resolves one invariant and rewards five head. With head restored, the player proceeds to the **dnd_classic_stub** keep, walking the same 14 rooms now rendered as a classic dungeon for final verification. The journey ends in **blob‑double**, where three push‑duplicate puzzles (no slides) each grant three head; completing the third level returns a duplicate token to the water computer, merging with the original OUT tank and resetting the clock. The loop is complete when the player can no longer spend head, signalling victory.

*Canonical module order:*  
1. adventure_2d6.crossroads → river_ford  
2. water_computer.gallery (OUT tank)  
3. fractal_maze.field (scale‑1 → scale‑2)  
4. audit_dungeon (atrium → 14 rooms, knot folding)  
5. doctrine_library.lens_gate (collect 10 lenses)  
6. riddler_optics.garage (ray through lenses)  
7. riddler_engram.session_hall (ten‑seat session)  
8. dnd_classic_stub.keep (14‑room classic walk)  
9. blob‑double (levels 1‑3, push‑duplicate)  
10. water_computer.gallery (final convergence)

*Shared‑resource loop:* **Head** is the sole mutable resource. It is generated by the water_computer OUT tank (1 head per fill step), earned additionally by solving a 2d6 room (+1 head), completing a blob‑double level (+3 head), and resolving an engram seat (+5 head). Head is spent to overflow the OUT tank (triggering the maze), to fold each audit knot, to purchase each lens, and to step on any scale‑2 maze tile (all –1 head). The head pool caps at 10, matching the ten lenses; when head reaches 0 the player is “dry” and can only traverse scale‑1 field and cannot interact with audit or optics. The cycle of fill → spend → solve → refill drives the cathedral’s progression and guarantees that all invariants are satisfied before the final reset.
