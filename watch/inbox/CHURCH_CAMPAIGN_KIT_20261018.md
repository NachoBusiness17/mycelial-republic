schedules accountability.

## 2. WAVE 2 - THE RESERVE (held for replies and notes)

**2a. HIS OWN MAIL (the personal turn - deploy when someone says "they'd
never spy on Americans")**
The Committee found the government reading American telegrams wholesale
under SHAMROCK, and running watch lists on its own citizens under MINARET.
The chairman's own mail was in the haul. Fifty years later the mail reads
itself and files its own summaries. We didn't fix the surveillance. We

**2b. THE LICENSED LANTERN (deploy when someone cites FISA as the fix)**
Church's committee was so alarming that Congress passed a law requiring a
warrant before spying on Americans: FISA, 1978. The lantern became a permit
process. Then 2008 amended it so some warrants could arrive after the
spying. The lantern didn't go out. It got licensed - and the licensing
office never needs a flashlight. (FISA Amendments Act, 2008.)

**2c. SAME AS IT EVER WAS (deploy when someone says "this is just how
government works now")**
The Committee played that song once, and it was true then too: the
difference between 1976 and now is that finding the machine used to take a
senate committee with subpoena power and a year of hearings. Now the machine
sends you the transcript and calls it a feature. "Nobody was paying
attention" was the crime in 1975. In 2026 it's the business model.
