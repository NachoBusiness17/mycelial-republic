## RIMWORLD STEALS

1. **THE AI STORYTELLER (Cassandra/Phoebe/Randy).** RimWorld has no scripted plot — a director reads your colony's statistics and injects events for pacing: quiet stretches, then raids, then a cold snap. → **The house's storyteller is the OPERATOR'S steering, mechanizable**: a storyteller organ reads fleet stats (throughput, breaker trips, coverage, mood) and paces events into the arena — pastes as storms, audits as raids, capacity surpluses as harvest boons. The seat becomes Cassandra. This merges with the storm mechanic already built.
2. **WEALTH SUMMONS RAIDS.** Difficulty scales with accumulated wealth: hoarding attracts danger. → **Coverage is wealth**: as the arena's verified coverage grows, the audits grow with it — the storyteller raids whoever holds the most unverified treasure. Law: growing wealth without a fence summons the raid. The fence test (6/7) was pre-built armor.
3. **TRAITS, PASSIONS, MOODS.** Pawns have personalities, passions (skills they learn 2-4x faster), moods, and mental breaks. → **Lanes develop engram-backed personalities**: each lane keeps template+delta memory across sessions, grows passions (a lane that keeps nailing injection traps develops an expertise), mood from recent success/failure — and the breaker is the mental-break catcher: one lane's tantrum doesn't cascade.
4. **THE TERRAIN MATTERS.** Colony survival depends on the procedurally generated map (soil, mountains, rivers). → The real-process maze terrain matters because it IS the doctrine — already built; RimWorld's lesson is that terrain + traits + storyteller TOGETHER make stories.

## DWARF FORTRESS STEALS

The arena's win condition coexists with a lore-generating loss condition.
7. **ARTIFACTS.** Dwarves enter moods and produce unique legendary items with procedurally generated names, enshrined forever. → **The head museum IS the artifact system**: a lane's exceptional verified run becomes a named artifact enshrined with its generation history. *Build: artifact names generated per legendary run, jarred with receipts.*

## THE DOJO LAW (operator: "what is a game but a drill dressed up in colorful clothing — dojo the mouse to T-rex in the primer")
