## THE THEFT IN ONE LINE

**Gärdenfors describes the geometry our corpus was already stumbling into:
information as points in dimensional quality spaces, concepts as convex
regions, similarity as distance — the missing middle layer between our
associations and our laws.**

## THE OPERATIONAL TAIL (what becomes buildable)

1. **Convexity check for families** — a family whose members scatter across
   the space is a mislabeled region; the graph's 19 families get their first
   geometric audit.
2. **Distance-ranked search** — FTS5 projection (UF-120 verdict: justified)
   ranks by bm25 + dimensional distance; prototype results, not just
   substring hits.
3. **The lens system gets a metric** — lenses (memory/lenses) are
   perspectives = projections of the space onto chosen dimensions; the
   elder-lens procedure becomes: choose dimensions, find the convex hull,
   ask about the outliers.
4. **Ghost sweep law, geometric form** — a reference is valid iff its target
   point still lies in the region the reference names. That is the
   computable version of "no reference is born unowned."

## STAYING IS THE GAME (why this book, why now)

Gärdenfors grounds persistence: a concept STAYS by being a stable region
while its members change. The fleet's doctrine says the same in files-plus-
chain. The amendments keep the adapters from pretending to be the region;
the geometry keeps the region honest about its members. Staying = holding
shape while replacing contents — which is exactly what a cathedral is.
