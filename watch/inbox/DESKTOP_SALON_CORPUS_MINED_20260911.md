## Cross-salon evolution finding — how the format grew its organs

The seven saves are a fossil record of the salon format converging on what the fleet later
formalized as the shape train:

1. **Stage 1 — raw ensemble paste** (example-salon, Sep 1 11:15): operator's own words + N
   model takes stacked; no structure. The takes ARE the salon. Genesis content (spore ledger).
2. **Stage 2 — artifact saves** (salon-ex-2, +85 min): saving the polished synthesis document
   instead of the conversation; salon products become transferable spores for the next model.
3. **Stage 3 — history dumps with the operator directing** (salon3 → salon4 → SALON-Salon,
   Sep 1–3): full sidebar ToC, newest-first, dozens of topics; the operator names the method
   ("summon the economist... the sherpa... the elder... sancho") and the models start returning
   formal **DECISION / FOLD / VERDICT blocks** with evidence tables and promotion tests
   (D-CHORD-v4). The salon grows verdict, fold, and ExperienceCandidate organs here.
4. **Stage 4 — single-thread deep transcript with provenance** (salon.txt, Sep 7): one
   conversation, URL + export-date headers, BEGIN/END wrapper, a fabricated citation caught
   honestly, ending in a falsifiable experiment proposal.
5. **Stage 5 — models auditing models** (cognition-salon, Sep 8): no artifacts, pure rounds of
   self-description correction (GLM debunking its own marketing in first person), ending in a
   taxonomy that feeds fleet organs (The Director ≈ RDE ≈ router).

The pivot is dated precisely: `SELF_STEAL_SHAPE_TRAIN_20260903.md` (S1–S7 shapes, gates,
folds) lands between SALON-Salon.txt (Sep 3, raw verdicts) and salon.txt (Sep 7, provenance
wrapper). The Desktop saves are the pre-canonical stratum the salon skill was distilled FROM;
the fleet skill did not invent the verdict/fold — it promoted them from these files.

Secondary observations: (a) saves accumulate by supersession — each history dump contains its
predecessor's ToC plus new titles, so md5-distinct files are mostly strict supersets of
conversation coverage; (b) both salon.txt and cognition-salon.txt contain internal re-paste
