# DRIFT, DON'T COIL — the lake parable (2026-09-11, operator, told from the shore)

The task: tie a new nylon rope to a seawall hook, run it 120 feet to a cement anchor in a barrel on the lake bottom. Cold water. The new rope was already tangled; dragging it in, it spaghettied at 50 feet — wet, the knots tightened past budging. Fixing inside the tangle made it worse. Then the algorithm: anchor the tip of one end, shake out what came loose, thread that segment, add its topology OUTSIDE the knotted mess, keep building the chain until the whole line ran. Then he wrapped it back up — TANGLE again. Final state: untangled, drifting flat in the water, 150 feet, it stayed solved.

## THE FOUR LAWS (from the lake)

1. **WET KNOTS DON'T UNTIE — CHANGE THE MEDIUM FIRST.** Nylon tightens when wet; the loop tightened when loaded. The remedy existed in dry source code since 2026-08-03, but the wet process held the old module. You do not negotiate with a wet knot; you get to the shore (stop the process, dry the state) and then it shakes loose. Fleet form: activation is part of the fix — a corrected document is a dry rope coiled in a ball.
2. **NEVER FIX INSIDE THE TANGLE.** Passing ends through knots created more tangle — "perplexed by making it worse while fixing." Fleet form: patching live state, editing prose holds, arguing inside the chat stream. The fix locus is always OUTSIDE the mess: at the seam, in the file, at the shore.
3. **TIP-FIRST, CHAIN OUTWARD.** Anchor one end; shake out what comes loose; thread that one segment; add its topology outside the knot; repeat until the line runs. Fleet form: this is the chain build itself — verify one smallest segment, commit it to the outside topology (the ledger, the vault), extend. Never re-enter the knot to "fix the rest": the rest comes loose as the chain grows past it.
4. **DRIFT, DON'T COIL.** The solved rope re-tangled when wrapped. Solved state stays solved only in the open configuration: 150 feet drifting flat, held by the medium. Fleet form: storage topology = solution topology. Flat files with a manifest (the vault born tonight) are drift; chat transcripts, Desktop coils, and loaded-process memory are coils — they re-tangle solved work on storage. Astra's 14 companions were a solved rope someone coiled back into a ball; tonight they drift flat in the vault.
