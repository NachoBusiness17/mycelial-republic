## 2. THE THEOLOGICAL MAP (three positions, not two)

**POSITION B — Traditional doctrine of the Fall (Romans 5; Augustine).**
Fallenness is a primordial condition of the human heart, prior to any
institution; agencies and empires do not *cause* the Fall, they *express* it.
Under B, the motion's arrow is inverted: blaming the CIA for fallen humanity
mistakes the symptom's newest costume for the disease. B's strongest text
against the motion is internal to Christ himself: "from within, out of the
heart, come…" (Mark 7:21–23) — the fall is *inside*, not upstream.

**POSITION C — The Powers (Wink, Stringfellow; empire-critical reading).**
Walter Wink's trilogy (*Naming the Powers*, *Unmasking the Powers*, *Engaging
the Powers*) reads the New Testament's "principalities and powers" (Eph 6:12;
Col 1:16) as *institutions that were created good but became idolatrous* —
"the powers are fallen, and are to be redeemed." Under C, the motion's *moral
energy* is orthodox: unmasking institutional deception is a Christian
obligation, and Jesus's own ministry is the prototype (the temple action was
an attack on an institution's extraction layer; Mark 7:13 — "you nullify the
word of God by your tradition" — is Jesus condemning *weaponized dogma* in
exactly the operator's register).

**POSITION α — The operator's operational thesis.** However humanity fell,
the *findings and avenues* now exist, are documented, are actively modernized
(attention and narrative as battlespace), and the teachings mandate engaging
them: John 8:32 ("the truth will set you free"), Matthew 10:16 ("wise as
serpents, innocent as doves"), Luke 12:2–3 ("nothing concealed that will not
be disclosed"), and the scholar's office itself ("a scribe trained for the
kingdom… bringing out treasures new and old," Matthew 13:52).

## 3. THE DEBATE, THREE ROUNDS

### Round 1 — Do the records support "avenues of attack"? (α wins)
Overwhelmingly. The programs are confirmed; the *subjects* were citizens; the
research object was the human mind; the modern form is open-literature
(narrative-as-battlespace; commercial adaptive-RAG is the same science
civilianized). Nobody familiar with 94-755 disputes this. **B and C both
concede the record.**

### Round 2 — Does ignoring them contradict the teachings? (α wins, decisively)
"Know the truth" (John 8:32) is not optional decoration; "wise as serpents"
(Matthew 10:16) is a direct command to model the adversary; the scribe of
Matthew 13:52 is commanded to bring out treasures *new and old* — the
declassified file is "new," the teachings are "old." A believer who refuses
the confirmed record on grounds of comfort fails the exact vigilance texts he
preaches. **B's own tradition concedes this**: Augustine and Aquinas both hold
that truth known by any means is of God (Romans 11:36; "all truth is God's
truth" as the later scholastic formula).

### Round 3 — Does the record explain "the Fall"? (α loses; β fails)
Here the motion overreaches. The confirmed programs begin in **1953**; the
doctrine of the Fall is attested in the **second millennium BC** (Genesis),
formalized by **Augustine in the 4th century**, and its core claim — that
humans *pre-date and originate* their own corruption — is precisely what the
programs *exploit*. Ellul's *Propaganda* (1965) makes the anti-motion case
from inside the critique: propaganda does not manufacture the will to believe;
it **harvests** a pre-existing will. The arrow cannot be reversed: men built
the avenues *out of* the fall; the fall was not built out of the avenues.
Even under Position C — where institutions are literally called "fallen
powers" — the fall of the powers is *downstream* of the human heart, which is
why Wink's method is *unmasking*, not scapegoating.

**The honest finding:** α's moral force survives without β. "Ignoring their
findings contradicts the teachings" is established. "The CIA caused fallen
humanity" is both unhistoriable (no as-of date, no pre/post state to compare)
and theologically backwards in every tradition at the table.
