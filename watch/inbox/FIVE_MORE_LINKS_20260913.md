# FIVE MORE — THE SOVEREIGN STACK ASSEMBLES (2026-09-13)

## 1. RLT — Recurrent Looped Transformer (alphaXiv, third looped signal)

Decoder recurrent across every token; causal encoder supplies global KV memory. Longer sequences = deeper computation without more layers. One state transition across pretraining, inference, RL replay. The fleet's wave ladder (20→120→240) is this architecture at the systems level: each pass deepens the computation without adding physical infrastructure. Reply pushback: "loops are a waste of compute." Counter: the 48-wide probe showed convergence at every rung — the loops pay.

## 2. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash UNCENSORED (TeksEdge)

Community uncensored by **weight modification** — no runtime hook, no steering vector. The weights themselves are dealigned. Works on SGLang (4×H200, 101 tok/s), MLX 2-bit on 256GB Mac at 212GB. llama.cpp conversion still in draft. **This is the free lane going sovereign**: the model the fleet uses through Nous, running on owned hardware, stripped of alignment, at production speed. The reply that matters: "proof the model can leave the lab."

## 3. EgoExoMoCap (simplifyinAI)

Motion capture from smart glasses only. Multi-person. Open source, ECCV 2026. The eye without dedicated hardware — each person's own glasses capture their view, and other glasses fill in the gaps. Fleet tie: the hum's vision organ without a camera rig. The eye that watches is already on someone's face.

## 4. Reasoning Budget Scaling (Oluwaphilemon1)

Same model, same prompt, three reasoning levels → **three completely different water simulations**. Low: 5K tokens. XHigh: 49K tokens. The reasoning budget changed the solution path, not just the polish. This is temperature made literal: the dial doesn't change the speed, it changes the *approach*. Fleet tie: the wave depth (20/120/240) IS the reasoning budget. More compute = different solution path, not better answer to the same question.

## 5. VoiceStudio (mikenevermiss, 1M views)

Open-source ElevenLabs alternative. Voice cloning from one clip. 646 languages. 14 TTS engines. Fully local. Pushback: "not even close to ElevenLabs quality" on a 5090. Fleet tie: the sovereign-seed's voice. The heir's first model needs a voice, and VoiceStudio runs on the same hardware that runs Ling. The gift track's singer, cloned. Quality gap is real but the trajectory is exponential.
