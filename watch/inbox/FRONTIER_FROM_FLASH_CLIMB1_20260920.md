## THE METHOD (per pitch)
1. **Brief** — spec distilled to one page, no prose padding (the square-notation rule: address the bar, don't describe the path).
2. **Wave** — N parents on free flash lanes, each produces an attempt + self-kifu.
3. **Children** — 2 deepseek adversarial passes per attempt: break it, find the hollow.
4. **Gate** — the pre-installed checker runs (tests, replay, or seeded-fault detection). Transport deaths recorded as transport, never as failures (the Law of the Day).
5. **Control** — the same brief fired once blind to a frontier lane (FlashX for P5). A blind judge (Flash, no lane labels) ranks cheap-fleet output vs control. Frontier-level = parity-or-better on the blind rank AND a clean gate pass.
6. **Kifu** — every attempt, verdict, and death logged; the pitch's score = passes per dollar and passes per attempt.

## THE FALSIFIER (what would hurt the thesis, filed in advance)
If after 3 waves on a pitch the cheap fleet cannot pass a checker that the blind frontier control passes, the pitch is recorded FAILED-FRONTIER-GAP. If 3+ pitches fail that way, Climb 1's thesis is damaged and the result is filed as honestly as a win would be. No redefining "frontier-level" mid-climb.
