### 1.1 Weekly-capped premium currency with a 3x sink (Don't Starve Together) [cs-02]

- Sink law: weave cost is exactly 3x unravel value at every tier. Unravel pays 5/15/50/150/450 (Common to Elegant), Heirloom at triple (Heirloom Elegant 1,350); weave costs 15/45/150/450/1,350.
- Income cap: 15 curios per week (daily gifts plus random drops), averaging 32.385 Spools per item, 485.775 Spools per week at the ceiling. The counter resets every Thursday around 1pm PST. Anti-idle clause: the player "has to be active in order to receive Curios".
- Drop rarity odds: Common 52.7, Classy 26.0, Spiffy 13.0, Distinguished 6.0, Elegant 2.3 percent. Trade Inn: 9 same-rarity skins for 1 higher.
- Price anchor: the premium character tier (Wortox, Wormwood, Wurt, Wanda) weaves at 2,700 Spools; 2,700 / 485.775 = 5.56 weeks of fully capped drop income. Cash chests $5.99 to $9.99 (Wanda Chest $5.99, Deluxe $9.99; Wortox, Wormwood, Wurt Deluxe $9.99 each on Steam). Refuter kept: the Wortox page groups all four at 2,700; only the Wortox and Wanda pages state their own price directly [cs-02 owed].
- Loss conversion: legacy XP is 20 per survived day, paid on the death or level-transition screen; unlock ladder 160/320/640/960/1280/1600 XP (8 to 80 days; full base roster at 80); XP caps 1600/1920/2560/3200 by DLC.
- Giant calendar (the raid-boss face of the same economy): Deerclops HP 4,000, spawn days 30.8/102.6/174.4, respawn 16.8 winter days (71.8 calendar days after a kill); it "prioritizes spawning on players with at least 5 structures within 20 Tiles" (wealth targeting, see V4); the Hostile Flare carries a 60 percent winter spawn chance and a flare kill resets the 71.8-day timer (the player-held trigger). Dragonfly HP 27,500, year-round at the nest, 20-day respawn; stun rule 1,250 damage within 5 seconds buys a 10-second stun, each later stun demands 250 more inside a 60-second lockout; she flies home with a full heal and reset state whenever the server resets (V1 evidence). Extended roster: Crab King 12,000 (the fight starts only when a player sockets 9 gems), Toadstool 52,500 (99,999 Misery), Antlion tribute decay (post-attack timer resets to 80 percent of the previous calm, tribute cap 6 days).
- Port shape: VM pays on the elimination screen exactly as DST pays XP on the death screen. ### 1.4 Shared-world boss scaling plus participation loot (Terraria) [cs-12 relay*, cs-29 full]
