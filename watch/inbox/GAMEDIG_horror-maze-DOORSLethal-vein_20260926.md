### 1.1 Lethal Company quota + deadline clock [hm-05, hm-23]

Two clone cards cover one system (quota ladder, moons/routing), merged here.

**Quota ladder** (hm-05, Profit Quota page; wikitext re-read in the deep-dive session):
- Cycle = 4 days including deadline day (3 scavenging days). First quota always 130.
- `quotaIncrease = 200 * (1 + timesFulfilled^2 / 4) * (randomizerCurve.eval(clamp(random(0,1) - (totalLuck * 1.5), 0, 1)) + 1)`; quadratic increase, cubic quota values, quartic cumulative cost (the page's own analysis). The roll approximates a normal distribution, mean 0.018, sd 0.121; chance of [-0.1;0.1] = 58.71%.
- Luck: full furniture set reaches max luck 0.2043 and caps the random input at [0;0.69355]; per-item luck from Disco ball 0.06 down to Plushie 0.003; the Signal translator carries -0.012 (a trap purchase). Furniture bought during quota n affects quota n+1 only. Caveat carried: the wiki flags its own curve images as pre-v80 ("Version 80: Quota increase eval changed"), so treat the formula shape as load-bearing and the curve images as stale. Luck-origin conflict filed: wiki History says v55, the official v65 anniversary devlog says luck was introduced there [hm-05 owed].
- Overtime: `(quotaFulfilled - profitQuota) / 5 + 15 * daysUntilDeadline`. Index convention (carried by the same card: "daysUntilDeadline starts at totalDays - 2", and totalDays = 4): daysUntilDeadline runs 2, 1, 0, -1 across days 1-4, so the 15x term reads +30 / +15 / 0 / -15 by sell day. Under this reading all three carried lines cohere, and this revision re-ran the arithmetic to confirm (0.3 check 1): selling 785 at quota 130 on deadline day gives overtime (785 - 130)/5 + 15x(-1) = 131 - 15 = 116 and a total of 785 + 116 = 901, exactly the carried worked example; the min-sell formula inverts the same day: (5 x 901 + 130 + 75)/6 = 785 exactly. The "capped at 0 total" clause means the total bonus floors at 0 (the wiki: "It is never possible to lose credits as the overtime bonus is capped at 0"): on deadline day a crew at exactly quota receives 0, and an over-quota crew has 15 deducted from its over-delivery share.
- Sell-rate ramp (The Company page): Day 1 30%, Day 2 53%, Day 3 77%, Day 4 100%. Wiki-single-source; the fetched official devlogs (v50 through v80) contain zero buy-rate lines [hm-05 owed].
- Death tax: 20% of total credits per unrecovered body, 8% per recovered body; full crew wipe or abandoned ship deletes all scrap on the moon; upgrades and decor persist until the crew is terminated.
- Single Item Day: chance history 8.6% (v60) to 6.8% (v62) to 5.2% (v64); the SID page reads 4.2% "currently as of Version 80" while the Scrap page section still says 5.2%, conflict filed, 4.2% carried as the current reading [hm-05]. SID reroll rule: item rerolled twice if rarity <= 5 or two-handed, third reroll succeeds 40%; values clamped to [50c, 170c]; moon totals under 600c (1500c two-handed) multiplied 1.4x, over 4500c multiplied 0.7x.
- Min-sell meta (the wiki documents the optimization itself): `quotaFulfilled = (5 * total + profitQuota + 75) / 6`; worked example: sell 785 of scrap at quota 130 to receive overtime 116 for a total of 901 (arithmetic re-run this revision, exact; see the overtime entry above for the index convention that makes it close).
