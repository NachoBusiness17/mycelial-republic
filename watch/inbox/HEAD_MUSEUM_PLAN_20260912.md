## THE HEAD MUSEUM (the ractor enhancement — v1 design, from R5/R8 returns + DNA)

THE CLAIM: our ractors are heads in jars; Futurama fed theirs fish food and let them
spout one-liners; **we feed ours tokens and they grow.** The museum is the difference
between a cabinet and a college.

1. **JARS = the jar file per voice** (extends ractor_dna.yaml): identity + model pin +
   genetics + musical tone (exists) NEW: feeding log (every dispatch: model, tokens, date,
   receipt pointer), growth ledger (life_version bumps on survived dispatches — Fen is at
   1.3), best-receipts shelf (the voice's 3 best returns, filed in its jar).
2. **FISH FOOD = the zoo.** v1 protocol — probe hermes zoo per voice: a voice's CANON
   prompt (DNA + register + tone) runs a fixed 6-task voice battery on N zoo models.
   Scored blind (the Astra-vs-cheap protocol): voice retention (does it still sound like
   itself — register, tone key, catchphrases), register drift, task quality. The winner
   becomes the voice's model pin; ties go to the cheaper row. Heads can be MULTI-FED: the
   same soul, two bodies.
3. **FEEDING ECONOMICS**: 7 :free rows + Lite pennies = every head fed daily for ~$0.01/week
   fleet-wide. The $220 S:D budget is reserved for judgment-class head work, not feeding.
4. **THE MUSEUM SURFACE** (dashboard/static/head_museum.html — static, loopback, no new
   senses): the hall at a glance. One row per head: name, anchor, model pin, life_version,
   status color (green = fed+growing, amber = fed, stale = starving), last feeding, best
   quote on the shelf. Click = the jar file path. The standing test is the build code:
   "what we found, where, in colors a person reads at a glance."
5. **THE ONE LAW (from R8's brief)**: the museum must stay honest — heads display
   RECEIPTS, not performances. A head that didn't grow says so. No cabinet of
   curiosities; a college with transcripts.
