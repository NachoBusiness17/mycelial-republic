## The harness ablation study (dair.ai, Zoom et al.) — our receipts, formalized

176 matched settings, four models, one-variable-at-a-time. Their findings are the
fleet's week, published:

- Context management pays off as windows tighten, mostly by preventing overflow
  failures rather than improving reasoning. (Our pack discipline: the kernel is
  stability, not cleverness.)
- Rule-based elision BEFORE LLM summarization won accuracy-per-cost. (Our
  DISTINGUISH instinct: narrow by rule before asking a model to compress.)
- Making elided content recoverable added machinery nobody used. (Our tombstone
  boundary caveat: the archive holds it; the active view must stay clean.)
- Planning changed role with model strength: weak models gained success, strong
  models gained cost. (Our tier packs: same semantics, coarser resolution.)
