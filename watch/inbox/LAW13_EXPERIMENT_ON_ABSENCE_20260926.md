# LAW-13: Experiment on absence (standing rule, operator, 2026-09-26)

Verbatim order: "when there's an absence of data we must design an experiment
that falsifies or enhances our knowledge as a standing rule no hallucination
or invention or hiding behind we do not know we have all arxiv papers to
theoretically map to."

## The rule

An absence of data is never a terminus. When a lane, receipt, or decision
lacks the data it needs, three moves are mandatory, in order:

1. **Name the absence precisely.** What datum is missing, which instrument
   or source would produce it, and why it is absent (unmeasured, gated,
   dead endpoint, nobody looked).
2. **Design the minimal experiment** whose result would falsify the current
   claim or enhance knowledge: pre-registered expected outcome, the threshold
   that inverts it, and where the result files. "We do not know" must leave
   behind a runnable question, never just a shrug.
3. **Map to existing theory.** arXiv (and the corpus) is the first place to
   check whether the absence is already answered somewhere: cite what is
   known, or file that the map was attempted and what it lacks.

Hallucination, invention, and hiding behind "we do not know" are all
forbidden forms of the same failure: treating an unknown as finished.

## Lineage

Extends the no-fabrication law (2026-09-19: never synthesize sources, file
as owed). This law adds the positive duty: every "owed" must carry the
experiment or theory-map that would close it. Applies to burn lanes
(receipt Falsifier sections), drift pairs (VACUOUS verdicts must birth
referent experiments), and research gaps (UF items carry experiment designs,
not just intents).

Falsifier for the law itself: if any future receipt files an absence without
either an experiment design or a theory-map citation, the law is being
ignored and the receipt is out of contract.
