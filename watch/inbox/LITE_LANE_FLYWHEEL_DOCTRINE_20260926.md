# LITE LANE FLYWHEEL DOCTRINE — RACTOR PANEL VERDICT — 2026-09-26

**Thesis (operator, from live use):** the lite/start lane works as the fleet flywheel: a cheap, fast-access staff lane for flash fan-outs that owns the concurrency spread without taxing the heavy lane. "Maybe that's what Hermes is. Run a ractor panel on it via our free lanes."

**Panel:** 7 seats, run through the free lanes themselves (OpenRouter ember-1 x6, DeepSeek rescue x1). Verdict pattern: 7 of 7 PARTLY-SOUND. Raw rows: logs/ractor_lite_panel_20260926.jsonl.

## The consensus verdict (all seats converge)

The lane WORKS as a staff dispatch surface; the FLYWHEEL NAME is wrong, and the panel agrees on why from seven directions: a flywheel stores and compounds energy; the lite lane stores nothing. What resumed the starved veins instantly was the CACHE (the organ), and the lane merely re-entered a stored program. Von Neumann's line is the kill shot: a lane that only replays cached state is not a flywheel, it is a READ HEAD. The flywheel is the fleet's organ state itself (ledger, receipts, falsifiers, saved workflows); the lite lane is the cheap bearing it spins on.

**Corrected doctrine:** the lite lane is the fleet's STAFF LANE (wide, shallow, cheap, for mass); the heavy lane is the JUDGMENT LANE; Hermes is the RESCUE ENGINE (deep completion under LAW-11, a different job from staff); the organs are the flywheel. Routing is per-run and per-organ, never per-subscription.

## The sharpest tears, kept

- **Ellul:** cheapness abolishes the cost that made triage real. Starvation was the tier ladder rendering judgment; a lane where feeding is nearly free bypasses the question "was this vein worth feeding." Guard: the manifest's due-order and PAT demotion stay the triage organ; cheap lanes widen capacity, never decide priority.
- **Pascal:** wide-shallow-parallel is the exact geometry of the hunt; cheap access lowers the cost of motion, not the cost of judgment, and motion without absorption is divertissement. Guard: every lite-lane wave still owes its fold (a receipt without a self-steal is chase, not hare).
- **Le Guin:** momentum you do not own is momentum you owe. The lane is a plan drawn by another's hand; success measured by resume speed is a metric of motion. Guard: sovereignty law unchanged (couriers never govern); anything load-bearing gets mirrored to fleet organs same-hour.
- **Hall:** the outer court was the most dangerous room: cheap, fast, wide enough for the multitude, and men camped there mistaking access for attainment. Guard: the lite lane is an initiation grade only if candidates GRADUATE; heavy-lane work must cite lite-lane receipts it built on.
- **Goedel:** owns-the-spread-untaxing is a universal no run of successes can prove; silent degradation is the unprovable edge. Falsifier below.
- **Crowley:** today's entry proves the cache and the router, not the flywheel; lust of result is the enemy. The Record demands rotation under load over weeks.
- **Von Neumann:** mixed-strategy optimum only while COST is the binding constraint; when the constraint shifts to judgment quality, the cheap lane stops being optimal. The router must know which game it is playing.

## Hermes vs the lite lane (the operator's maybe)

Settled: different jobs. Hermes = deep rescue/judgment-completion engine (burn credits to complete demanded work instead of failing; second-engine verification for cross-engine diffs). Lite lane = mass/staff. The flywheel feeling comes from the ROUTER: the discovery that veins move lanes mid-run and resume from cache is the real asset, and it is fleet-side, not lane-side.

## Adopted (what changes operationally)

1. The lite lane becomes the default fan-out substrate for wide-shallow waves (scout passes, triage sweeps, first-pass digs), reserving the heavy lane for judgment and synthesis. The router question per wave: is the binding constraint cost (lite) or judgment (heavy)?
2. Free API lanes remain overflow; Hermes remains rescue + second engine.
3. Every lite-lane wave lands in organs same as any wave: receipts, folds, PAT demotion. Cheap never bypasses.

## Falsifiers (pre-registered)

- **F-LITE-1 (rotation under load, Crowley+Goedel):** the staff-lane pattern holds only if over the next 14 days lite-lane waves show no silent quality degradation: lite-lane receipts' correction/overturn rate stays within 2x of heavy-lane receipts on comparable briefs. If lite-lane corrections exceed 2x, the lane demotes to scout-only.
- **F-LITE-2 (absorption, Pascal):** at least 1 in 3 lite-lane waves must fold a self-steal improvement into the manifest; if the fold rate collapses below that while volume grows, the lane is producing motion without absorption and gets re-priced (volume capped to fold capacity).
- **F-LITE-3 (ownership, Le Guin):** zero load-bearing artifacts may live only on a lane provider's surface; quarterly check that every lite-lane output the fleet still cites is mirrored in fleet organs.
