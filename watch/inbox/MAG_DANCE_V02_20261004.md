## What landed

### 1. Tempo-octave resolution fixed (the v1 defect, proven live)

v1 estimate_bpm preferred the double-period when its autocorr was 0.75x the
primary, but bounded the check against lags[-1] = the SIGNAL LENGTH, not the
[lo, hi] tempo window. Two failure modes: (a) a lock could report a BPM below
lo entirely, (b) resolution was one-way (only ever doubled, never resolved a
half-tempo lock up).

v2 rule (tools/beat_map.py estimate_bpm): candidates b0/2, b0, b0*2 clipped to
[lo, hi]; each scored by _grid_score = mean onset z over its own beat grid
(the alignment test); walking slowest -> fastest, adopt the faster candidate
only while it keeps FAST_KEEP (0.7) of the slower's score. True octave-up
grids land every beat on a real onset (score holds); ghost octave-ups drop
half their beats into silence (score collapses).

LIVE PROOF in the estate's own library: flat_beat_loop.mp3 read 64.6 BPM under
v1 - below the lo=80 window, the defect class exactly - and reads 129.20 under
v2, the true Flat Beat tempo, exactly double. dance.mp3 held its validated
123.05 (v1 was already right there).

### 2. THE DAMAGE MAP CONTRACT (tools/beat_map.py damage_map)

Every beat banks one hit on the skeleton; the beat grid IS the combat round
tick grid. Row shape: {t, zone, hp, imp, downbeat, strength}.

- downbeat: heavy zone, head/torso alternating per measure ((k//4)%2),
  hp = 3 + int(clip(strength, 0, 9))
- off-beat: limb rotation (k%4 + k//4)%4 over armL, armR, legL, legR,
  hp = 1 + int(clip(strength, 0, 9)) // 2
- imp = impulse vector, magnitude = hp, direction = golden-angle walk
  (k * 137.50776405 deg mod 360) - deterministic, no RNG import, rerun-byte-
  identical (dice-law discipline: outcomes live in the carrier)

Zone keys match dashboard PARTS exactly. Consumers: the dashboard flash (now),
the Mountain brawl / game_brawl pressure line (owed wiring), the physics rig
(MuscleMimic anchor, when layer 3 grows a runtime).

DESIGN BUG CAUGHT BY THE CONTRACT SMOKE: first rotation used plain k%4, and
since k%4==0 is always the downbeat, armL was NEVER a target (census on
dance.mp3: 144 armR, 143 legL, 143 legR, 0 armL). Fixed to the measure-stepped
rotation; all six zones now hit on every track (8/8 verified 6/6 zones).
