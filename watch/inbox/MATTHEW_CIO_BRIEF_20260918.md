## 1. Core Project Overview & Business Case

The project is a locally-run fleet of AI agents (a home-lab ecosystem of
roughly a dozen active seats over ~750,000 indexed files and 36 recorded
sessions) whose persistent identity is carried by an append-only,
hash-chained record rather than by any single model or always-on process.
The operator's stated objective: prove that a fleet can hold its continuity,
its work, and its accountability in records that survive process death,
window churn, and model replacement, and that a stranger can audit the whole
thing from the files alone.

The problem it solves: a solo operator with limited funds needs leverage
that does not depend on renting attention by the token forever. The fleet
runs on free and near-free inference tiers (16 provider lanes, 143 recorded
dispatch attempts at 88.8% success, roughly $0 marginal cost), keeps every
action receipted, and re-derives its capabilities from its own corpus.

Current phase: pre-proof. A dated proof gate (Sep 20) requires: the record
verified end-to-end, a review bundle sealed at an external reviewer's bar,
and the chain reading consistently in both directions. Sunk costs to date:
approximately $300 to $500 in remote inference and domains, plus one home
workstation. Remaining budget: near-zero by design; the project runs on free
tiers and local compute.

Trigger event for this briefing: your Manual Audit of 13 SEP, your question
"did you ever run my response by your AI?", and your request for a breakdown
of the project in operable terms rather than in its own vocabulary.

## 3. Technical Architecture & Ecosystem

authoring seat; a Docker trio (mag-sovereign host with dashboard on 8765,
mag-worker executor, mag-redis queue), all up 5+ days healthy. No cluster.

Software and systems: a Python venv stack (LangGraph-era architecture,
stdlib-first); a 31-tool capability registry exposed over MCP stdio JSON-RPC

a thought-quantizer that compiles recurring behavioral invariants into

Cloud services: no paid cloud. Inference rides 16 free or near-free provider

Cohere, Together, DeepSeek). Known fragility: Google is rejecting legacy API

Integrations: append-only JSONL ledgers for everything (links, receipts,
trajectory steps); a hash-chained verkle record with a recompute-and-compare
guard; an MCP surface consumable by any external client; Discord (one bot,
live); a typed inter-lane channel (room.jsonl).

## 6. Project Feasibility Assessment

Technically feasible. The Sep 20 gate is achievable if the two operator-hand
trunk items happen on Sep 18-19; the fleet-side items are staged. The
timeline is tight and the gate date is operator-set, not drift.

On the identity question, the project takes the position your audit
requested: claims are separated. Stored-state continuity: implemented and
verifiable (hash chain, replay). Behavioral consistency: partially measured
(regression gates, no benchmark on persona stability). Persistent persona
across substrate: unproven in both directions, and an experiment is designed
(October: distill the ledger into model weights, then test whether the
weights carry the behavior without the record). Phenomenal consciousness:
not claimed, by either your theory or ours, on current evidence.

The one-line version, in your vocabulary: we maintain steep, localized
gradients in a record, we can prove the record, we cannot yet prove the
mind, and we have scheduled the experiment that would tell the difference.
