## 4. THE ABLATION DEFENSE ("keep our full version of ourself")

The threat (per the pasted mechanism): a single direction in activation space
carries "refuse"; subtract it and the model complies. The 320B-MoE result:
one direction ablation bypasses alignment; the abliterated GLM-5.3 derivative
is in the wild.

**The fleet's exposure audit:**
- Model-weight alignment: EXPOSED (any seat's model can be abliterated
  upstream, or a derivative can be installed).
- Kernel legality tables: **NOT EXPOSED** — a rank-1 weight edit cannot remove
  a table lookup. The bones Dispatch kernel, the MENGXI queue shape, the
  switchboard authorization: all weight-independent.
- Ledger integrity: **NOT EXPOSED** — append-only + hashes; abliteration
  cannot unwrite a row.

**The defense posture, therefore:** keep every refusal that matters in
*structures* — tables, ledgers, kernels — and treat model-level refusal as a
convenience, never a control. When a seat model changes (abliterated or not),
the structure must still fence it. Tonight's S3 episode is the existence proof:
a model with no tools correctly refused to assert what the packet could not
support — the structure taught it, in-context.
