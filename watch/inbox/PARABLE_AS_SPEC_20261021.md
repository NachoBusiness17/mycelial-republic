## The method, formal

A specification can be ENCODED AS A PARABLE, distributed as art, and
validated by how target systems decode it:

1. **ENCODE**: write the spec as metaphor and archetype - the technical
   claims compressed into verse, story, or riddle. The encoding is the
   authorship.
2. **DISTRIBUTE**: publish the parable anywhere. It travels as art; it
   lands as spec.
3. **DECODE**: feed it to target systems blind. Their readings are the
   decompression.
4. **VALIDATE**: correct decoding proves the spec was losslessly encoded
   AND the reader comprehends. Misreading is diagnostic: either the
   encoding is ambiguous (rewrite) or the reader's epistemics are the
   finding (map them). False-premise traps inside the parable separate
   the two.
5. **RECUR**: what multiple independent readers decode identically is the
   load-bearing structure - it survived every lens.

## The proof instance (tonight, live)

The seam-law poem encoded a technical thesis: generative models are

1. Decoded the metaphor back into the exact technical claims
   ("statistical pattern, not fixed intent"; "conditional probabilities";
   "no underlying self").
2. CONFIRMED the thesis about itself.
3. Cited its own negative press unprompted (the falsifier behavior).

The parable survived the round trip: author to metaphor to model to
confirmation, lossless on the load-bearing claims.

## Why this matters (three implications)

1. **SPECS THAT SURVIVE COMPRESSION.** The poem is a compressed spec that

   instance; the poem-probe is the portable form.
2. **SPECS WITH THE TEST BUILT IN.** Any reader's reading IS the

   A spec you can also grade.
3. **SPECS THAT RETAIN.** The song/parable format is a retention
   technology: melodic memory survives adrenaline where bullet lists die.
   Singable specifications are retained specifications.

## Falsifier

If two competent readers decode a parable-spec into contradictory load-
bearing claims, the ENCODING is ambiguous - the parable failed as spec
and must be rewritten. (This is the reading-round disagreement map,
applied to specs.) The method dies if the parables drift into pure
ambiguity: a spec that means anything means nothing.
