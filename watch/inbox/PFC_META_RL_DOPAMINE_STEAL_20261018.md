## 1. Verbatim Intel: The Nature Neuroscience Meta-RL Framework

The DeepMind neuro-AI framework (Wang et al., 2018) resolves the long-standing debate between dopamine (striatal model-free RL) and prefrontal cortex (PFC model-based executive control) by unifying them into a **two-timescale meta-reinforcement learning system**:

1. **The Traditional Dopamine Model:** Canonical RL models propose dopamine (DA) signals reward prediction errors (RPEs), slowly modifying synaptic weights to associate states, actions, and rewards.
2. **The PFC as an Autonomous Learning Engine:** The PFC maintains representations of action values, reward histories, and upcoming choices in its recurrent activity, demonstrating internal learning computations independent of immediate synaptic plasticity.
3. **The Meta-RL Resolution:** Dopamine does not directly execute every task-level decision. Instead, **dopamine trains the recurrent prefrontal network over slow evolutionary/developmental time to implement its own fast, flexible learning algorithm in recurrent activations.**
4. **Two Operating Timescales:**
   - *Slow Timescale (Synaptic Plasticity):* Driven by dopamine across thousands of episodes, shaping the structural network weights.
   - *Fast Timescale (Recurrent Activation Dynamics):* Once trained, the PFC's recurrent dynamics support instantaneous in-context adaptation to unfamiliar tasks, even while synaptic weights remain completely frozen.
5. **Emergent Model-Based Behavior from Model-Free Training:** When a recurrent network is trained via model-free RL on diverse multi-task distributions, its internal activation trajectories spontaneously exhibit model-based structure learning and inference without requiring an explicitly hand-crafted world model.
6. **Adaptive Learning Rates:** The network automatically accelerates its learning rate when the environment is volatile, and stabilizes when conditions are stationary.
