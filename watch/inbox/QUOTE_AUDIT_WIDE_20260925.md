## Method and denominators

- Normalization (MICKEY-17 verifier-lane convention): `norm(s) = re.sub(r'\s+', ' ', s).strip()`
  on needle and haystack. Case-sensitive. No other equivalence (a curly apostrophe is NOT
  equal to a straight one; that is why some near-misses below exist).
- HIT = normalized quote appears inside the normalized claimed lane row's `quote` field, or
  it minus/plus a scope trim within a 0.6 length ratio; partial = longest shared token
  window >= 8. Wider haystacks in priority order: any lane row quote field (claim fields
  marked secondary), `memory/runs/mickey17_v2/**` concat, `memory/research_packs/**` concat,
  `watch/inbox/**` + `watch/*.md` concat (25.9 MB), canon/ + root `*.txt`, fallback
  `reference/` grep only. `memory/` is 5 GB and `corpus/` 6.8 GB estate-wide; those trees
  were NOT greppable in full and every miss says so.
- Snapshot first: both dirs copied to `memory/runs/quote_audit_wide_20260925/snapshot/`
  with sha256 + double-read stability. All 24 files stable; live re-hash at audit end: 0 drift.
- Concurrency disclosure: `corpus/steal/MASTER_MICKEY17_V2.md` was rewritten by the v2.1
  boot-lane fold DURING this audit session (v2 content observed early, v2.1 later; the file
  even changed between two of my reads). Audited bytes = the v2.1 fold snapshot,
  sha256 `94c18694fd5c489a...` (full hash in results json). The 0924 MASTER is
