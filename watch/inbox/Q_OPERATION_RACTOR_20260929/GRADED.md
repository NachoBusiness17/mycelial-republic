## 4. TENSION MAP

Collision 1: what does the operation assert? East: nothing checkable; it arranges a mirror, three dated claims in 4,705 posts being the measure of the refusal. West: it asserts a revelation, a hidden order, an end whose decoding key is always later; the assertion is real but scheduled past every verdict. Game theory: it asserts an omniscient ally, a claim whose function is to substitute for proof because proof would have to be costly.

Resolution: all three are true of one machine at three levels. At the level of checkable propositions, the East is right: near-zero assertions, and that is measured. At the level of genre claims, the West is right: the frame asserts a plan and an ending, and the genre's trick is that these assertions are unfalsifiable by construction, which is exactly why the East's count can be near-zero while the West's revelation stays loud. At the level of function, the game theory is right: the assertions that exist exist to coordinate, not to describe. The unified picture: the operation sells a non-falsifiable frame (West's sealed text) whose propositional content is near-empty (East's mirror) so that its coordination value stays intact (game theory's Schelling point). "Trust the plan" is simultaneously an instruction to keep practicing (East), the seal's own promise that the key turns later (West), and a mutual-assurance contract with no enforcement clause (game theory). A synthesis that picks one level over the others fails the corpus.

Collision 2: what does it WANT? East: querents; the consultation itself; "The machine's product was never the future. The product was the crowd, synchronized." West: gathering, frame installation, and by the third wave revenue; "the collection plate, deferred to the third wave, arrived as a card." Game theory: attention, participation, then payment, by revealed payoff; intent Unproven.

Resolution: the three wants are one want measured at three waves with three instruments. East's "querents" and game theory's "participation" are the same state (synchronized believing) named by an observer of form and an observer of incentives; West's "gather" is the genre's word for the same crowd-state; all three converge on the third wave's monetization with the same numbers (card plus medbed x22). The real disagreement is about what the crowd gets. East says certainty-as-practice plus each other; West says the opus transforms them (the measure is what the readers become); game theory says the product they consume is knowledge-of-others'-belief and the labor they pay is decoding. The synthesis should rule: first-order belief about the world was the cheap raw material, second-order belief (who else saw this) was the product, and the apocalyptic content (West) was the directional payload that told the synchronized crowd what the synchronization was about. A mirror alone synchronizes nothing; scripture alone cannot scale; the empty frame plus the directional content is the working composite.

Collision 3: why did it survive, design or selection? East: design, in the mantic sense; the refusal to date is "not cowardice; it is design." West: ambiguous on purpose; the operation "learned, or was designed, to stop dating" after the first drop broke. Game theory: explicitly Unproven, and it files the third option that absorbs the other two: "The equilibrium does not need a genius to produce it. It produces the appearance of one."

Resolution: game theory's selection story is the umbrella. The lineage demonstrably kills dated senders (Pool 16 of 16 BROKEN, filing through expiry, no retraction, and the register drift toward finance and urgency), so any wave-5 survivor is enriched for low specificity regardless of anyone's intention. The East's mantic refusal is the behavioral rule the selection enforces; the West's genre is the cultural toolkit that packages the survivor. Design intent stays Unproven, and the synthesis should say so once and stop.

Collision 4: what is the archive? Game theory: the corpus was the movement's mint, "its treasury," revalued after every news cycle under the "future proves past" license. West: the archive is the bequest, "a corpus can be instrumented," the operation's own texts turned against it. East: the corpus is the inexhaustible mirror, "6,242 posts are too many for any reader to hold, so the mirror always has another panel to consult."

Resolution: no collision, three receivers. The same bytes are a treasury for adherents, a mirror for querents, and a dataset for the estate. The unified machine is a text whose value is settlement-layer-dependent, which is the estate's whole point and should be the synthesis's.

Collision 5 (the quiet one): the audience's condition. West's alchemy says the opus succeeds and the readers are transformed ("The measure is what its readers become"). Game theory says the readers are damaged inventory ("A population trained to read disconfirmation as depth"). East says they are a trained crowd, "a crowd that has practiced synchronized decoding for years and can be re-aimed at any feed." Same fact, three valences. The synthesis should carry the game theory's ledger framing (what happened to them is measured in labor and dollars) with the West's mechanism (how the transformation was performed, stage by stage) and the East's warning (the capability persists after the operator leaves).

Where the lenses disagree about GOING: East says the form goes commercial and then pure-symbol, ending in "the mirror with a ledger." West says the vector is secularization plus monetization, ending in the card and whatever audience next takes the bargain. Game theory says the gradient is less specific, more urgent, tighter monetization, and predicts "the next wave will be harder to score than Pool." These are compatible; the synthesis should note that only the game theory's version makes a scoring-relevant claim (harder to score), and that the East's "divination with a ledger" and the game theory's "keep the instrument pointed at itself" are the same bet stated in two vocabularies, with the Oct 4 row as its test.

## 5. VERDICT

Strongest as written: LENS_GAMETHEORY.md. It is the only essay with zero verification defects beyond a single tri-state slippage, it contributes the most beyond-digest measured content and every added number checks out at source, and it is the only lens whose explanation of the operation's survival is causal rather than formal or generic. LENS_WEST.md is the best written and the most complete answer to the five-question charge, and it loses to the game theory only on the unverifiable "before the site surfaced" clause and the weak footnote domains. LENS_EAST.md is the most distinctive and the most honestly self-limiting, and it carries the one defect that its own genre cannot afford: a misattributed sacred-text quote stamped "Verified."

What each lacks:

- East lacks content, and it says so; the synthesis must borrow the West's account of what filled the mirror, and the game theory's account of why unowned frames coordinate. It also lacks any statement of the selection pressure that makes never-dating stable, which its goose metaphor points at without proving.
- West lacks the incentive spine: it observes that the operation stopped dating and leaves "learned, or was designed" hanging, where the game theory's selection equilibrium closes the question. It also lacks the audience's labor economics; "paid them in engagement" gestures at what the receiver-cost inversion actually computes.
- Game theory lacks form and content: its coordination machine would run on any Schelling point, and only the West explains why this genre and only the East why this shape (unowned, flat, traceless). It also lacks any account of why anyone wanted the product, which the East's certainty-as-practice and West's identity offer supply.

Single sharpest sentence across all three: "The plan cannot fail. It can only be deeper than you thought." (LENS_GAMETHEORY.md, section 4). It compresses all three lenses into ten words: unfalsifiability (the East's mirror), the deferred decoding key (the West's seal), and the dominant strategy (the game theory's never-settle). Runner-up, for the synthesis's own self-description: the East's "do not stand, keep."
