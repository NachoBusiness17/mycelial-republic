## 1. One machine, three levels

The East lens was right about the operation's FORM and blind to its content. The
West lens was right about the CONTENT and had no incentive story. The game theory
lens was right about the FUNCTION and indifferent to why anyone would want the
product. All three are true of one machine at three levels, and the tension map's
resolution is simply to stack them:

- **Form (East):** a mantic machine. It asserts nothing and arranges a mirror.
  Measured: Q made exactly 3 forward-dated claims in 4,705 posts. A mirror does
  not need to be right; it needs to be available.
- **Content (West):** apocalyptic revelation. Sealed text, deferred key, the wise
  understand and the wicked do not. "Future proves past" is Daniel 12 with a
  server. The message is ancient; only the distribution is new.
- **Function (game theory):** a protocol for manufacturing common knowledge
  without paying for it. Information requires accuracy. Common knowledge requires
  only publicity. The operation sold the second good, which is why disconfirmation
  never touched it: disconfirmation attacks the information good, and there
  almost was none.

"Trust the plan" is the three levels in one speaker line: practice-instruction
(the mirror's ritual), seal-promise (the deferred key), and an unenforced
mutual-assurance contract (the coordination good).

## 2. The one genuinely new thing

Apocalyptic literature before 2017 was written by a seer FOR a congregation. The
Q operation was written by a seer THROUGH a congregation. The congregation
acquired a terminal and the revealer acquired a reply button. Daniel's seal kept
the meaning from the unworthy; Q's version keeps the meaning in the future and
lets the whole crowd dig for it. Distribution without decryption, at scale, with
the decryption outsourced to the audience as free labor. Cicada 3301 proved the
labor model works: Liber Primus, 12,140 runes across 27 types, top rune only 4.1
percent, unsolved since 2014, and the crowd has never stopped digging, because a
flat text regenerates its own mystery on demand. The koan does not convey. It
generates labor, and the labor is the payload.
