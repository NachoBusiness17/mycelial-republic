four engine stages, so the steps ride inside the workflow instead of riding

## The swarm design

The swarm is not a fixed crew - it is a lens library plus a composition
rule: every mission selects its lenses, every lens is a standing persona
(same brief, same founding law, fresh context per mission), the hostile
pass is the swarm's immune system, and the synthesis voice is the only
one that speaks to the operator. Composition examples fired tonight:
ractor50 = 7 doctrine lenses; vonnegut-final = jung + bernays + archaeology
-> one triangulator; the music program = audio-dna + remix-mechanics + two
cutters; this engine generalizes all of them.

Standing rule (ractor50's own finding, now structural): every lens finding
survives a hostile pass, weakened findings steer back once, and nothing
speaks to the operator that did not survive. The swarm rides; the fold
talks.
