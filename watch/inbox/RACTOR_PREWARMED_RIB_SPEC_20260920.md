## WHAT A RIB IS

A per-chair portable bundle in the travel-kit pattern (build_travel_kit.py lineage: self-verified, hash-manifested, over-cap by pointer): **the pre-warmed, boot-ready context of one ractor**, so a summon pays the cold-start once and rides the warm prefix after (the cache law applied to chairs — the answer to stochastic-side bugs).

## LAYERS (per chair)

- **L0 — KERNEL (never scored, never compacted):** identity card, the figure's documented METHOD (steps-of-the-mind), OCEAN v2 measured-from-corpus (not asserted), the refusal path (Diogenes' law: a chair may refuse; refusal is a fidelity signal), the Bernays label (authority = operator's projection over a corpus — printed on the tin).
- **L1 — WORKS:** life works / essays / novels, digested to size per **Euler's saturation experiment** (nested 2K→50K subsets, blind classification + decision-alignment on held-out known cases; build the curve BEFORE building big).
- **L2 — VOICE:** correspondence, letters, quotes, interview/podcast transcripts — the first-person material, cited verbatim where it binds.
- **L3 — SHADOW SHELF (Jung's amendment):** the figure's documented oppositions, refusals, reversals, unwritten positions — negative space as data. Freud's amendment feeds the evals from here.
- **L4 — CASE LAW (the operator's self-steal, NEW):** the chair's own prior salon testimony and verdicts, indexed by topic. Every new ruling must cite or explicitly overrule its own precedent — silent contradiction of one's own case law is the new silent-agreement-illegal.
- **L5 — CONTEXT DOSSIER:** the zeitgeist rows + current-question brief (rebuilt per summon, never shipped warm).

## V3 AMENDMENT — THE LIVING LENS (operator, 2026-09-20: "an open-ended RIB of the mind for each, constantly evolving like us, reusable and interchangeable as a lens in which to view the world, unique to the minds instrumental in its generation")

The RIB is not a frozen pack. It is a LIVING LENS:

1. **OPEN-ENDED**: no terminal size. The Euler saturation curve sizes the *works* layer, but case law, shadow entries, and invariants append indefinitely — the lens accumulates rulings the way the fleet accumulates LAW rows.
2. **CONSTANTLY EVOLVING, LIKE US**: every folded testimony appends to the chair's case law; every overruled precedent is marked, never deleted (chain law). The lens sharpens with use — and the evolution itself is measured (does ruling N+1 cite deeper precedent than ruling N? that curve IS fidelity-over-time).
3. **REUSABLE AND INTERCHANGEABLE — THE LENS SOCKET**: one uniform interface, `view_through(chair, object)` where object ∈ {question, corpus, artifact, decision, another chair's testimony}. Any chair fits any object; any salon is N lenses on one object; a chain of lenses (chair A views chair B's testimony) is legal and is how cross-examination formalizes. The socket is the salon protocol's summon step, made general.
4. **UNIQUE TO ITS GENERATIVE MINDS**: every RIB records both parents — the figure's corpus AND the fleet's own history with the chair (the salons where it drew blood, the rulings it overruled). A Bernays-lens rebuilt from the same texts elsewhere would NOT be this lens: its case law carries this fleet's questions. The lens is a hybrid organism — figure's skeleton, fleet's scar tissue. That uniqueness is stamped in the manifest (provenance = corpus hashes + case-law chain range).
