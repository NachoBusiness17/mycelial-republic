## 2. The lens (one sentence, then the instruments)

**Render the invisible system through its visible record; lift what is nonlinear into the
space where it becomes linear; and meter the advantage honestly, because it is always
bounded.**

- **Lyapunov (src 1):** never solve the trajectory — find the energy that always falls. Certificates over solutions; cross terms make the certificate strict.
- **Chladni (src 6):** dynamics you cannot see leaves geometry you can. The sand gathers where motion stops — the nodal pattern is the Lyapunov level set made visible. And the λ cross term reshapes the entire pattern **without changing the pitch** — cross terms are design parameters, not decoration.
- **Phase portrait (src 4), 4D shadows (src 5):** the same instrument again — draw the flow, not the solution; show the shadow, not the space.
- **Carleman / lattice-Boltzmann (src 10):** the dimension-lift done with full honesty — lift the nonlinear system into a higher-dimensional linear one, pay the condition number, and the audited result is: **bounded, modest advantage, on selected observables, in the high-error-tolerance regime.** "Small, but nontrivial." The hurdle list (convergence, condition number, data extraction) is a reusable risk checklist for ANY lift — including ours.
- **Unfolding (src 8):** before comparing spectra, unfold each to its own density. And the deep cut: some statistics survive a bad unfolding, others silently don't. Every meter is an unfolding choice; report the statistics that are robust to the choice and name the ones that aren't.
- **Entanglement distribution (src 7):** one source, many noisy channels, receivers at the far ends. The open question is never the source — it is whether correlation survives the channels.
- **SciML book (src 2):** simulation and learning share one gradient. Models simulate; meters validate. (Photoelasticity law, third independent confirmation.)
- **mimik (src 9):** a tool that renders behavior — workflows captured as annotated steps.
- **Named reactions (I1):** a mature discipline names its verified transforms with reagents and conditions. That is what a finished organ catalogue looks like.
- **Logistic regression (I2):** the calibrated baseline. Any score → probability (sigmoid); honesty about confidence (log loss). Every router must first beat this floor.
- **P = F/A (I4):** pressure is force over area. Our force is bounded — one operator, cheap lanes, the $250 iron circle. Every strategic lever we own is area reduction.
