# Roblox Seasonal / Halloween Window — Research Note (2026-09-24)

Prepared for: small-studio spooky skeleton maze dungeon launch, Halloween 2026.

## 1. Do seasonal games spike-and-die, or become evergreen?

- **Halloween is a proven launch/growth window for horror.** DOORS (LSPLASH, launched Mar 2021) built its base on horror replay loops plus seasonal re-engagement; it sits at **7.7B+ visits** with a record **~381,720 CCU** around a major update ([Rolimons/DOORS wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com), [update log](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Logs)). Its 2024 "The Haunt" Trick-or-Treat event (limited badges, reworked lobby) was designed as a lapsed-player re-engagement hook ([event wiki](https://doors.miraheze.org/wiki/2024_Trick_Or_Treat_Event)); third-party trackers logged a **+79% month-over-month activity lift** during a recent Halloween window ([howmanyareplaying](https://howmanyareplaying.com/roblox/experience/2440500124)).
- **Pattern:** spike-and-die happens to games whose *only* content is the seasonal skin. Evergreen conversions (DOORS, Pressure, Forsaken — horror titles carried by platform horror demand in 2024–25) happen when the seasonal window imports players into a core loop with regular non-seasonal updates after.
- Genre tailwind: Roblox's official horror hub prominently features multiplayer horror year-round, spiking each October ([roblox.com/discover horror](https://www.roblox.com/discover)).

## 2. Window mechanics

- **Platform event timing:** Roblox's official **Halloween Spotlight ran Oct 23 – Nov 3, 2025** — a hub ("Cursed Clearing") linking ~11 featured games plus 30+ Halloween updates, with quests rewarding runes/keys for avatar items ([Sportskeeda, Oct 24 2025](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox/roblox-halloween-spotlight-items), [Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Halloween_Spotlight)). Note: big games drop Halloween content across all of October; the *official* platform event is late-October.
- **2026 status:** as of 2026-09-24 **no 2026 Halloween platform event is announced**; the Roblox Wiki notes Halloween Spotlight was the last official platform event, with no Hunt since ([Roblox Wiki — events](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Event)). Plan as if **developer-run events + free limited UGC are the 2026 lever**.
- **November cliff:** limited-time events produce roughly **2x engagement during the window** and a **15–30% retention drop after they end** (dev-community rule-of-thumb, not Roblox-official; [Roblox Studio Tips Monthly](http://escueladeugc.com)). Platform-level, Q4 is Roblox's engagement peak (holidays) but a game that rides *only* Halloween sees its cliff in the first half of November — after Nov 3, seasonal search/discover traffic collapses.

## 3. Platform seasonality

- Roblox engagement builds through October, peaks late Oct (Halloween) and again at winter break; Q1 dips are seasonal, not demand decay (pattern per Roblox quarterly reports/10-Q seasonality discussion, e.g. [ir.roblox.com](https://ir.roblox.com)). Platform scale: ~144M DAU ([Yutori Scouts, Gen Alpha gaming trends](https://yutori.com)).
- 2025's biggest events were independent of Halloween (Grow a Garden's mid-2025 CCU records), but October is still horror's month on-platform.

## 4. Monetization in seasonal windows

- **Free limited UGC (1 per user) is the proven acquisition hook** — devs run their own "mini-events" handing out capped free items; paid variants/bundles convert the engaged ([Roblox Wiki — Event](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Event), [DevForum: in-experience UGC](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/so-how-do-you-create-in-experience-ugc/2660028), [Rolimons free limiteds](https://www.rolimons.com/free-roblox-limiteds)).
- **In-experience bundles** are now a first-class lever ([GameDeveloper: Roblox lets creators list bundles](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/roblox-will-soon-let-creators-list-item-bundles-to-grow-their-earnings-)).
- Standard seasonal playbook: **event currency** (e.g., Black Hole Core's "Spirits" in Halloween 2025) earned by playing, spent on limited-time skins; event pass with free+paid tracks; everything time-boxed so scarcity does the conversion. Typical event windows: 2–4 weeks; scarcity + FOMO is the lift, not price.

## 5. Concrete launch calendar (skeleton maze game)

| Date | Move |
|---|---|
| **Sept 29 – Oct 2** | **Soft launch** (no fanfare): fix crashes, tune maze difficulty, seed first ratings. Never launch cold on Oct 31. |
| **Oct 9 (Fri)** | **Full launch + v1.0.** Core maze polished. Free limited UGC skull (1/user) as the hook; paid cosmetic bundle live. |
| **Oct 16** | Mid-season patch: new maze wing, first speedrun leaderboard event. |
| **Oct 23** | **Halloween update (the reserved content):** limited-time haunted zone, event currency ("Bones"), event pass free+paid, 2nd limited UGC. This lands exactly where the 2025 platform event sat (Oct 23). |
| **Oct 30–31** | Finale weekend: final boss / rare-drop window; end all timers visibly. |
| **Nov 6** | "Aftermath" update within 1 week: remove dead event UI, convert a trimmed version of the haunted zone to permanent content, tease a November roadmap. This is the anti-cliff move — the games that survive are the ones with content dated *after* Halloween. |

**Reserve for Halloween vs launch day:** launch = core maze + monetization skeleton; Oct 23 = ~40% of spooky content (event zone, currency, pass, finale). Endings must be announced in advance so FOMO converts.
