## 5. Seasonal / Halloween

- 2024: "The Haunt" — hub event, quests across featured horror games; players found the "devhunt" format tedious ([Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Haunt)).
- 2025: replaced by "Halloween Spotlight" (late Oct → Halloween): earn Runes (any participating game) + Keys (hard challenges) for UGC rewards ([Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Halloween_Spotlight)).
- Featured lineups deliberately mix **new horror releases** with established hits — participation is an application/invite, and it grants homepage/event-hub placement. That is the closest thing to an algorithmic boost: it is real but curated, not automatic.
- Data point: DOORS historically times major updates to October (Hotel+ was Halloween 2022). Horror genre CCU visibly swells in Oct as Spotlight traffic re-points at scary games.
- No public Roblox stat isolates "October launch boost"; the defensible claim is that late-Sept/Oct horror launches that make the Spotlight lineup get outsized discovery vs. the same game in July.

## Bottom line for a new studio

Enter horror-maze (proven small-team genre, resident audiences, Halloween window open now), design for clips and revives, ship weekly, and expect suitors if it works.
