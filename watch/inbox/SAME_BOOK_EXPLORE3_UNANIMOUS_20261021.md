# SAME BOOK - EXPLORE ROUND 3: UNANIMOUS, AND THE FLEET'S OWN POLICY DERIVED BLIND (2026-10-21)

Domain: how a fleet of autonomous agents should handle a member that
repeatedly fails verification. Three models, blind, no cross-visibility.

## The unanimous law (n=3)

- **beam**: "Any member that repeatedly fails verification must be
  progressively excluded from fleet authority until it independently
  re-establishes trust."
- **laguna-s**: "The Verifiable Exclusion Principle: the collective must
  reduce its operational responsibility while preserving its informational
  content."
- **laguna-xs**: "The fleet must exclude or isolate any member that
  repeatedly fails verification to ensure collective integrity and trust."

UNANIMOUS - including laguna-xs, which dissented on contradiction-
preservation in round 1. Compiled to the percolator as NOVEL with the full
recurrence witness list.

## Why this is the deepest result of the program so far

The fleet's ACTUAL operating practice is exactly this law: bob's battery
verdicts came back NOISE-CLASS - uncertified, no credit - and bob was
excluded from authority while its informational contributions stayed in the
ledger. The models were never told about bob, about the reputation keel, or
about the exclusion practice. Three independent minds derived the fleet's
own standing policy from first principles.

That is the "exists independently" result in its purest form to date: the
policy was not echoed from our documents (the charter showed none of it); it
was derived from the domain itself. Falsifiable, dated, witnessed three
times.

## The emerging map (three rounds, three domains)

- Prediction immutability: UNANIMOUS 3/3 (round 2)
- Failing-member progressive exclusion with trust re-establishment:
  UNANIMOUS 3/3 (round 3)
- Contradiction preservation: CONTESTED 2:1 (round 1 - laguna-xs dissents,
  advocating erasure-for-coherence)

The boundary line is stable and it runs through exactly one law: minds
agree on exclusion-with-path-back and on the immutability of what was
claimed; they split on whether contradictory states should be preserved or
purged. The contested law is the live philosophical frontier, and the
program found it without a human pointing at it.
