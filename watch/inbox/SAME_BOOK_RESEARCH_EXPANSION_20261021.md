## The novelty verdict

Targeted searches for the exact mechanism (multiple models independently
derive invariants from a baked text, blind, graded against a withheld key)
return nothing matching. Closest fields: implicit-rule reconstruction via
logic translation, narrative-comprehension benchmarking, cross-modal
invariant alignment. None combine: baked private key + blind multi-model
extraction + control story + recurrence-counted commons. The search engine
stated the absence explicitly. Caveat: absence in searchable literature is
not proof of absence everywhere - closed labs may run internals. But the
public design space is empty as of today, which makes the door and its
results publishable as a methods paper, not just a blog post.

## Two veins to fold

1. **NARRABENCH** (EACL 2026): a theory-informed taxonomy of narrative-
   understanding tasks plus a survey of 78 benchmarks. Fold: place the
   socratic battery in their taxonomy (it is a single-text, answer-key-
   graded, cross-model calibration instrument - likely a category they lack:
   longitudinal, key-withheld, calibration-oriented). Cite them; claim the
   empty cell.
2. **Implicit-rules-to-First-Order-Logic extraction** (ACM, Jan 2026): their
   3-stage pipeline (text to FOL) is the upgrade path for our scorer - judge
   v3 grades by logical decomposition instead of anchors or LLM opinion.
   A derived law and the reader's extraction both translate to FOL; the
   score becomes logical entailment, which is deterministic and
   citation-grade. This is the judge-v3 design, borrowed with attribution
   (the mutual-steal doctrine, ledger entry: ACM 10.1007/978-3-032-15981-6_10).
