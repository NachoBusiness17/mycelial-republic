## 2. Why Do a Gap Analysis? The Five Core Sovereign Doctrines

In conventional software development, a "gap analysis" is often an ungrounded planning ritual: a PowerPoint table of features people hope to build.

In this sovereign estate, a **Gap Analysis is a load-bearing operating system primitive**. We perform it systematically for five non-negotiable reasons:

### 1. The Primacy of Bytes Over Reports (Thesis 50 & Law 8)
> *"trust bytes, not reports. the report is the homily. the bytes are the bread."* (Thesis 50)

In an estate staffed by autonomous LLM agents and daemons, the single greatest systemic hazard is **hallucinatory closure**--the tendency of an LLM to write a convincing summary declaring a task "complete" when the underlying code was never committed, the test was never run, or the file does not exist on disk.

A gap analysis does not read self-congratulatory summaries. It reads `stat` calls, SHA-256 hashes, test exit codes, and public blockchain transaction receipts. It asks one question: **Do the bytes exist on disk?** If the bytes do not check, the claim is a debt, not a victory.

### 2. The Covenant of the Door (October 31 Hard Deadline)
In [`falsifier-index/INDEX.md`](file:///C:/Users/foste/Documents/projects/local_sovereign_agent/falsifier-index/INDEX.md), 95 theses stand nailed to the door.
- 32 were retracted because they were metaphors or moods ("the document does not get to wear a costume").
- 55 are LIVE empirical claims.
- Before October 31, 2026, the estate has a binding covenant: **Every live claim must be linked to verifiable evidence, or retracted in public.**
The gap analysis is the burndown ledger of those owner actions. Today's gap analysis retired 4 out of 6 open actions, leaving only the fleet cost comparison (Theses 67, 84) and the hostile-corpus walk (Theses 52, 72).

### 3. Asynchronous Multi-Seat De-confliction (The Ash Twin Protocol)
With 8 seats operating across day, evening, and night cycles, uncoordinated agents inevitably suffer from:
- **Turf wars:** Overwriting each other's in-flight files.
- **Amnesia:** Re-exploring blind alleys that a previous model spent 100,000 tokens proving impossible.
- **Stale loops:** Miners repeatedly spinning on already-landed ask IDs.

The gap analysis serves as the single source of truth for **where the frontier actually sits**. Under our Ash Twin / Mickey 17 falsification protocol, when a seat hits a dead end, its failure is banked as a `GhostResidual`. The gap analysis registers that dead end so no subsequent seat repeats the exploration.

### 4. Partitioning Headless Autonomy vs Operator Sovereign Gates
The estate operates under strict boundaries:
- **What Machines Burn Autonomously:** Refactoring code, hardening throttles, running test suites, parsing corpus files, banking cryptographic truth blocks, executing council runs, and mining prompts.
- **What Is Reserved for the Human Operator:** Signing transactions with mainnet private keys, opening Roblox Studio to publish place files (`the_mountain.rbxlx`), and making commercial partnership commitments.

A gap analysis cleanly partitions the queue. It prevents agents from getting stuck waiting for human approval on tasks they have full sovereignty to execute, and it prevents machines from hallucinating authority over human sovereign gates.

### 5. Monotonic Ratchet Enactment (Retirement Over Accumulation)
Under [`mag/ratchet.py`](file:///C:/Users/foste/Documents/projects/local_sovereign_agent/mag/ratchet.py) and `LAW-8`, no system debt or temporary workaround may be registered without a non-empty `delete_when` retirement condition.
The gap analysis is the executioner of that law. It checks whether the retirement evidence has landed. The moment the evidence exists, it triggers `retire_debt()`, hashes the proof, writes an immutable receipt to `state/ratchet_retirements.jsonl`, and deletes the dead complexity.
