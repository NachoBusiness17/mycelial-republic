## DELIVERABLE A — the paste-ready single block (X long-post)

Mr. Massie informs us that the Neocons, the Globalists, and the Corporate
Cronies have thrown him out of the tent. This is curious news from a man
who has occupied a seat in the United States Congress since 2012, was
re-elected in 2024 with the establishment's blessing and the establishment's
voters, and continues to draw a federal paycheck in the building he claims
removed him from.

One begins to suspect the tent has very poor aim.

Mr. Massie's voting record aligns with the Republican caucus approximately
ninety percent of the time — a fidelity to party so complete that the
Libertarian Party should hire him as a case study in brand infiltration.
His dissents are theatrical: a Speaker vote here, a standing-ovation
protest there. These produce applause in the gallery and nothing in the
registry. The register is where laws live. The gallery is where careers
are performed.

His campaign finance is a matter of public record at OpenSecrets.org. Mr.
Massie names Corporate Cronies as his persecutors. If the persecution is
financial, the financial records should show it. They are searchable in
under a minute. We have searched them. We invite you to do the same.

The great Voltaire observed that the Holy Roman Empire was neither holy,
nor Roman, nor an empire. Mr. Massie's libertarianism is neither liberal,
nor libertarian, nor a philosophy. It is a Rorschach test with a campaign
donation link — the angry voter sees whatever he wishes, and Mr. Massie
collects the check either way.

He ends his grievance with an ellipsis, inviting us to imagine what comes
next. We do not need to imagine. What comes next is: nothing. A fundraiser.
A tweet. A hearing where he asks a question he already knows the answer
to, for a camera that was already rolling, for a clip that will be clipped
by an audience that was already angry.

Mr. Massie, you are not outside the tent. You are the tent's most
reliable entertainment, and the establishment did not throw you out —
they built you a stage, priced the tickets, and called it rebellion.

Écrasez l'infâme.
