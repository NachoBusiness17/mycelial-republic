## THE UNTYING (operator: "this is how we untie the topology of the knots that bind us into competition rather than creation")

The knot, named precisely: competition is a topology where **value is destroyed by sharing** — you win only if another loses, so every player is bound to every other player as an obstacle. The referee-tournament, the seals-with-clauses, the leaderboard-as-elimination: all downstream of that one binding.

Creation is the untied topology: **value is destroyed by hoarding.** A dug-up truth stays dug up. A verified claim exists absolutely — nobody's loss paid for it. The town grows; the seasons compound; the sediment settles. Stardew has no loser. Go, the house's own proof, knew it first: territory, not capture — you win by making things live.

**The design consequence for v2 (binding):** the arena's scoring is CREATION-WEIGHTED, never victory-weighted. Verified-dwell is absolute — the truth exists after you claim it; no lane's gain is another's loss. The leaderboard stays as INFORMATION (SpaceChem optimization axes), never as ELIMINATION. Lanes compete only against entropy, together. The breaker guards the floor; nothing guards a throne, because there is no throne.

**The lineage rhyme:** "Immortality is a blessing — on one condition: it has to be shared." The immortality seed, Mar-02 era: creation shared, from the first public post. The knots of competition were never the house's native topology — they were imported from the scarcity world. The town exports the untied form: value that grows when given.
