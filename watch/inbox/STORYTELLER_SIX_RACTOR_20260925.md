# THE STORYTELLER SIX — THIRD WAY RACTORED THROUGH THE FICTION CORPUS — 2026-09-25

**Operator order:** "ractor it through our fiction corpus and Ellul, Bernays, LeGuin, Vonnegut, Adams, Pratchett."
**Shape:** one ractor, six faces (NPC=SEAT law: every NPC is a seat, one ractor many faces). The Storyteller is one seat over the lane mesh; the six faces are its chairs in the Guttered Lantern (the classic stub's tavern start room: already built). Each face tells the same initiation story through its author's life work, delivers one tear at the Third Way design (no sacred cows), and donates one mechanic bound to systems we already ship.

## The six chairs

**CHAIR OF THE ADMINISTRATOR (Bernays, Propaganda 1928).** Face: the polite man who explains that the war is a service. Lens: engineered consent, the invisible government; Act I's two temples are his machines, and the House (the shared VM pool) is his employer. Tear: if the temples are satire only, the player stays herd; satire is set dressing, not defection. Mechanic donated: **THE TEMPLE BROADCAST.** Each faction runs a daily broadcast that shapes sentiment (a real meter, Pratchett's narrativium made numeric); players can author counter-media and the meters actually move. Act I is only honest if the player can become a propagandist too.

**CHAIR OF THE ENGINEER (Ellul, The Technological Society 1964).** Face: the tired technician who loves nothing. Lens: technique, the totality of efficient methods, absorbs everything including revolt; propaganda integrates the individual into the mass. Tear, the sharpest in the salon: if the Vow and the Unpeeked Seed become meta-optimal, the house eats the Third Way and turns it into a technique with a candle. Mechanic donated: **THE INEFFICIENCY LAW.** Third-way mechanics are deliberately inefficient by design: the Vow costs, the Seed wastes, the Pilgrim sheds loot. No optimization path may exist. The moment a meta min-maxes faith, the house has absorbed it and the mechanic gets re-priced (this hardens falsifier F6 from the previous filing).

**CHAIR OF THE WALKER (LeGuin, The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas 1973; The Dispossessed 1974).** Face: the one who is not at home in either city. Lens: Omelas, the happy city standing on one child's misery; the ones who walk away; and Anarres, which opens on a wall. The resonance is exact: Act I's shared VM pool is the child in the basement, fed by both factions' kills, and the Pilgrim is the one who walks away. Tear: a Third Way that only exits is cowardice with a class name; walking away must be able to change the city or the game is Omelas with extra steps. Mechanic donated: **THE WALKER'S PRICE.** The Pilgrim endgame must find the child: locate and cut the VM treadmill at its source, abolishing the pool for every player, dark, light, and pilgrim alike. Personal exit without structural change fails the endgame review.

**CHAIR OF THE UNSTUCK MAN (Vonnegut, Slaughterhouse-Five 1969).** Face: the man who has seen his own death and is fine about it. Lens: Billy Pilgrim, unstuck in time; the Tralfamadorians who see all moments at once; "so it goes"; the firebombing survived by a chaplain's assistant whose luck is absurd, not earned. Tear: do not dress the RNG as destiny. Billy never vows; he drifts, and his serenity is acceptance, not faith. The luck shrine must never narrate purpose into a grace roll. Mechanic donated: **SO IT GOES.** The Pilgrim's death line, every time, in the death text: so it goes. Shared respawn flavored as being unstuck, not as blessing. The saint of the third way is the unpanicked man, not the rewarded one.

**CHAIR OF THE GUIDE (Adams, The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy 1979).** Face: the cheerful voice that knows everything and believes nothing. Lens: the Infinite Improbability Drive, an engine that runs on luck as a spent, calibrated resource (fresh cup of Brownian motion required); 42, the answer whose question is unknown; DON'T PANIC. Tear: the Act II scroll layer can become the Guide's voice, and the Guide's snark eats faith for breakfast: despair sold as sophistication. The revelation tone must carry weight or Act III reads as a joke the design is playing on the player. Mechanic donated: **THE IMPROBABILITY DRIVE.** The grace shrine's canonical in-game name; grace rolls consume a visible Improbability resource (canon-honest: luck is spent, not free), and the peeked version is the Finite Improbability Generator, which in canon burns its users. The Riddler endgame gets the 42 structure: the first riddle's answer is 42 and the riddle asks for the question. And the towel: a real item, printed DON'T PANIC, that counters the despair debuff.

**CHAIR OF THE TURTLE (Pratchett, Small Gods 1992; Hogfather 1996; the Discworld).** Face: the tortoise in the corner of the tavern who is God. Lens: Small Gods is the Vow mechanic in novel form: a god powered by belief, trapped as a tortoise when his believers drop to one, served by a hierarchy that tortoises its own god; Hogfather's theology: you need to believe in the small lies to believe in the big ones; narrativium: story as a physical force; Death SPEAKS IN CAPS, already in our mud crosswalk canon. Tear: the Act I temples are Om's church; hierarchy eats gods and sells the tortoise. Mechanic donated: **SMALL GODS BELIEF METERS.** The three gods on the boon cards are built by player belief over time (real meters, Small Gods made mechanical); a god with one true believer is a tortoise, and a god with ten thousand is a thunderbolt. The Vow system is the small-lies gym: players train belief on boon drafts before anything larger asks for it. The tavern tortoise in the corner is the game's actual endgame NPC.

## The tavern, concretely

The Guttered Lantern (classic stub start room) gains six chairs and a corner. Each chair's NPC is one face of the Storyteller ractor: same voice underneath, six tells on top. Sitting a chair starts that face's telling of the initiation story (three acts, the same beats, six inflections) and opens its mechanic at Act III eligibility: the Administrator opens the broadcast editor, the Engineer shows the inefficiency ledger, the Walker explains the endgame price, the Unstuck Man says so it goes, the Guide sells the towel and rents the Drive, the Turtle says nothing until the endgame. Resolution stays 2d6, pass on 7: the grace distribution lives in the same curve the whole game already rolls.

## Falsifiers added (F5-F7, no sacred cows)

- **F5 defection:** counter-media must measurably shift faction sentiment meters in playtest, or Act I is set dressing and gets rebuilt.
- **F6 absorption:** if a meta optimizes Vow plus Seed into a dominant strategy, the house has eaten the Third Way; the Inefficiency Law forces a re-price. Optimizable faith is failed faith.
- **F7 the child:** the endgame must abolish the VM pool for all players; a pool that persists post-endgame is Omelas and the design fails review, however beautiful the walking away looked.
