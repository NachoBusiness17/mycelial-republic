# STRATEGIC TENSION STEAL — the obstacle is the path (2026-09-12, operator link)

Source: APS Physics 19,126 (Sep 11, 2026) on Cerioli et al., "AI sustains higher strategic
tension than humans in chess" (APS Open Sci. 1, 000131, 2026). ~1200 grandmaster games +
1200 elite AI matches + Stockfish sims.

## THE PAPER (tri-stated from the primary writeup)

- SUPPORTED: "Strategic tension" = network measure of the board (pieces linked by
  capture/defense/threat; long chains = cascade risk). Games follow one trajectory: tension
  rises, peaks ~moves 15-30, declines as trades simplify.
- SUPPORTED: after the memorized openings, HUMANS rapidly simplify — resolve conflicts to
  cut cognitive load ("you really feel the tension... it's natural to want to resolve it,"
  Strogatz). AI engines sustain elevated tension far longer.
- SUPPORTED (the kicker): winner decided during the BUILDUP of complexity (~move 15);
  the simplification that follows is a CONSEQUENCE of an already-decided game, NOT its
  cause. Against chess experts' own intuition.
- CAVEAT (authors' own): proves nothing about international relations — chess shows the
  mechanism cleanly, real conflicts have imperfect information and shocks. Our leap below
  is flagged as the same class of leap.

## THE FLEET MAPPING (marked: mechanism-transfer, same caveat applies)

1. **Tri-state law = institutionalized tension-sustaining.** Humans force
   yes/no to end the discomfort; SUPPORTED/DISPUTED/UNPROVEN refuses the premature trade.
   Every UNPROVEN we keep open is tension held past the point a normal org would have
   collapsed it into a comfortable claim.
2. **Halt-not-hammer = not resolving at move 15.** Two failures → stop, don't force the
   position. The twelve CONDITIONALs on C1 — we did not cash the short-term gain of "it
   works!"; we held six gates open instead.
3. **"The winner is decided during the buildup"** — the fleet's wins were decided in the
   freeze/tension windows (the 8-wide nights), and the folds are the aftermath, not the
   cause. Simplification (docs, cards, pages) is what a decided game looks like afterward.
4. **Go vs chess (our own thesis, confirmed sideways):** chess humans FLEE tension; go —
   the game the First Law is written in — is the human invention where tension is the
   MEDIUM and the crop is the EMPTY points. The stone protocol builds on the game that
   already solved this: hold more tension than feels comfortable, and the crop is the
   space you keep open.
5. **The cost is real and it lands on ONE ORGAN (Strogatz's point).** Holding tension has
   a cognitive price; a single node paying it for decades gets worn. THE ORGANISM IS THE
   ANSWER THE PAPER IMPLIES: distribute the holding. The seats carry the sustained tension
   (tri-state, witnesses, falsifiers); the operator carries judgment, not load. M12 meters
   his minutes because the design goal is the load stops landing on one heart.
