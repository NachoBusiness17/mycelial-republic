## The five laws that hold at every layer

1. **The symbol executes.** It does not describe the command. It IS the
   command. Confucius's ruler, Cicero's esto, the assembly instruction,
   the rib's name: the symbol doesn't point at the behavior, the symbol
   IS the behavior. A symbol that describes without executing is
   documentation, not law.

2. **The specification binds the implementation.** The spec precedes the
   code. The constitution precedes the court. The rib precedes the
   behavior. The implementation cannot override the specification without
   breaking the system, because the specification IS the definition of
   "working."

3. **The record only grows.** Ashoka's stone, the Congressional Record,
   the append-only ledger, the verkle chain: the record that can be
   erased is a record that can be denied, and a deniable record is not a
   record. Deletion is the only real theft.

4. **The falsifier keeps it honest.** Every law carries its own kill
   condition. Every claim carries its own refutation. Every instrument
   carries its own placebo. The system that cannot report its own
   brokenness is not measuring anything.

5. **Structure beats virtue.** The design must work on non-angelic
   material. Han Feizi's law that doesn't bend for the crooked. Madison's
   ambition counteracting ambition. the falsifier that catches the
   confident error. The structure carries what the wisdom cannot, because
   the structure survives the wisdom's absence.
