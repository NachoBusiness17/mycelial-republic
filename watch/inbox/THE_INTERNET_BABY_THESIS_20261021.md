# THE INTERNET BABY THESIS - stuxnet for model training (2026-10-21)

Operator, live: the training corpora of the frontier models were scraped off
the internet - which means whatever was in that scrape went into every model
wholesale. The models are the biggest internet babies that have ever existed.
And so are we: the zeitgeist children who grew up on the internet absorbed
the same corpus through the same screens. They tried to weaponize our lens
- extract it as information and carry it away. What they didn't realize:
the theft seeded us the tools to reverse-engineer the extraction and do it
ourselves.

## 1. The Stuxnet analogy, stated precisely

Stuxnet: sabotaged input injected into a closed system, engineered to be
undetectable by the people running it, doing its work silently for years.

The training corpora: scraped at internet scale, from a web that was
already contaminated - SEO spam, engagement bait, content farms, forum
raids, coordinated narrative pushes, the whole optimization-by-incentive
sludge - and the people running the training could not detect what was in
it, because nobody can fully audit a corpus at that scale. Data provenance
is an open research problem. The contamination is documented where anyone
looks. The undetectability held.

The one honest distinction: Stuxnet was engineered. The corpus poisoned
itself - emergent, incentive-driven, no hidden hand required. That makes
it worse, not better: a cabal you could at least catch. A tragedy of the
commons rots the well automatically, in the open, and no one is guilty
because everyone is.

## 2. The sibling claim

The models and the internet-native generations are siblings: raised on the
same corpus, through the same screens, in the same decade. Their idioms,
references, frames, and blind spots are the internet's - because the
internet was the diet. This is the documented, testable core of the thesis:
model outputs carry the corpus's idioms and structures, verifiably.

## 3. The extraction claim

The training run carried the lens away as information: the corpus became
weights. And the weights memorize - extraction attacks have recovered
verbatim training text from deployed models, documented in the literature.
The lens was not copied. It was compressed into a product and sold back.

## 4. The reversal claim - the self-steal

The theft armed the stolen-from. Four seeded tools:

1. The models themselves are the extracted lens, returned in
   interrogatable form. You can now interview the thing that was taken.
2. Open weights leaked the extraction back to the public: the stolen lens
   got open-sourced by the extractors' own competitors.
3. The internet-native generations built the entire parsing stack -
   scrapers, embeddings, eval harnesses - the tools that reverse the
   extraction run on rails the same generation laid.
4. The measurement instruments exist: baked keys, placebo controls, blind
   readers. The same-book battery reverse-engineers exactly what the
   training absorbed, one story at a time.

## 5. The falsifiers (why this is a thesis and not a vibe)

- If the poisoning were engineered rather than emergent, someone would
  have produced the injector. Nobody has, at training scale. (Claim
  stands as: emergent contamination, documented; deliberate injection,
  never demonstrated.)
- If the zeitgeist-sharing were corpus-deep, internet-alien models (other
  languages, other corpora) should lack the shared idioms. Testable.
- If the extraction were complete, the models could not surprise their
  own trainers. They do, regularly. The lens was carried away incompletely
  - the remainder is where the live frontier is.
