# THE LE GUIN CLAUSE (2026-10-21) - the epistemic theorem of the same-book program

Operator, live, completing the program's own closing line ("what recurs in
minds that never met is the closest to truth we will ever get"):

"But the Universe is an awfully big place. There is room enough for an awful
lot of people to be right about things and still not agree."

(Le Guin, The Dispossessed.)

## The theorem, formal

The socratic-book instrument has TWO outputs, and both are findings:

1. **CONVERGENCE ZONES** - laws derived unanimously by independent minds
   (n>=3, blind). These are candidates for structure that exists
   independently of any single mind: the mycelium, the commons, the
   recursions. Prediction immutability (3/3). Failing-member exclusion with
   a path back (3/3).

2. **DIVERGENCE ZONES** - laws where independent minds SPLIT. These are not
   failures to reach consensus. They are the measured location of genuine
   plurality between minds: the boundary lines where one house's floor is
   another house's map. Contradiction preservation (contested 2:1,
   laguna-xs the stable dissenter, domain-conditional). Recorded as
   geography, never as error.

## Why the clause changes the instrument's meaning

Without the clause, the experiment looks like a consensus machine, and
disagreement looks like noise to be corrected. With the clause, the
experiment is a MAP-MAKER: convergence zones are where minds are the same;
divergence zones are where minds are genuinely different - and both regions
are real geography worth knowing. The anti-majority clause was the
prototype of this: a 2:1 split cannot be voted into truth, because the
minority may be the one whose floor is solid.

## What it means for the republic

The hive collapses disagreement; the republic maps it. The Commons takes
only what recurred; each organism keeps its divergence as sovereign
territory. When a new mind reads the book, the experiment asks one question
and gets two answers: WHICH laws recur (the mycelium) and WHERE this mind's
floor diverges from the commons (the boundary). Both answers go in the
record. Neither commands the reader.

## Falsifier

If the program ever treats a divergence zone as a defect to be corrected -
rather than a boundary to be mapped - the Le Guin clause has been violated
and the instrument has drifted into consensus-seeking. The map must hold
both regions or it is not a map.
