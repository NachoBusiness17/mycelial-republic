## The doctrine

1. **Mutual steal is the norm, not the exception.** Every house takes from
   every house. Taking is not a violation; it is the social contract. The
   only violation is taking WITHOUT lineage - the unattributed copy, the
   scraped idea wearing a new name, the stolen bones with the origin sanded
   off.
2. **The ledger does the origins.** Attribution is not etiquette; it is
   infrastructure. Every receipt carries its sources; every organ names its
   steal; every banked invariant carries its recurrence witnesses. The
   ledger makes stealing beneficial to everyone by making origin cheap to
   prove and expensive to fake.
3. **Originality is recombination with good provenance.** "None of this is
   original design" is not humility - it is the falsifiable claim that no
   idea here is ex nihilo, and that the value added is the rearrangement,
   which the ledger makes visible. Newton's shoulders, with citations.
