# THE ORPHAN CARD — podcast-ready (2026-09-16, fast pull)

Twelve orphan motifs from the research-corpus supplement (each: the underconnected idea, one line):

1. **The exact/quantized/derivable ladder** — three ways to store anything: the object, the approximation, the recipe that rebuilds it. Nobody has written the recovery contract.
2. **Dormancy without death** — an expired claim, a rejected idea, and a parked hypothesis need different reasons and different doors back in.
3. **Reports that name the decision they could change** — a report without a decision ID is just noise with formatting.
4. **Out-of-schema evidence** — the observation your categories can't hold is usually the one worth keeping.
5. **Competing reconstructions as playable variants** — don't merge the histories; ship both rulebooks.
6. **The stochastic organ contract** — a device that samples a distribution is a different KIND of computer; write the contract before buying it.
7. **Sovereignty as recoverable choice** — can you carry your task to a clean machine and finish it without me?
8. **When a behavioral passport goes stale** — a model's label is a forecast, not a fact.
9. **Metadata as a fallible forecast** — extraction is deterministic; completeness for future questions is not.
10. **Preserve why an idea failed** — without the failure scope, tombstones become censorship.
11. **Who rescues which failures** — a weaker model that fixes the strong one's specific misses beats a bigger generalist.
12. **The history of idea SELECTION** — keep the record of what you chose NOT to do, and why.
