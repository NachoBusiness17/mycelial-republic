## I. The mechanism, self-stolen and generalized

What the waves actually did is a UNIVERSAL MEASUREMENT PATTERN, not a one-off:

    BAKE (private structure riddle-encoded into a public artifact)
    CONTROL (a matched artifact with the structure absent)
    DISTRIBUTE (identical tokens to N independent readers)
    EXTRACT (socratic battery: force the structure back out)
    SCORE (deterministic grading against the private answer key)
    FALSIFY-THE-INSTRUMENT-FIRST (the scorer must pass its own control -
      and on the first run it DID fail: loose anchors scored the lawless
      control 4/10; the instrument was fixed, the book was not touched)
    MINE (internals channels - reasoning_content - are readout surfaces)
    RECUR (blind cross-model agreement = the only independence evidence
      a fleet of language models can produce)

The pattern works on ANY artifact that is supposed to carry structure: a
skill file, a contract, a spec, a law, the public repo face, a prompt
template, an onboarding path. If structure is claimed, the wave tests it.

## III. Parallel research veins from our own corpus (novel lenses)

1. **THE VEIL, MEASURABLE.** The riddler-face card's falsifier ("a stranger
   cannot reconstruct the operator; a member can recover the invariants") has
   never been TESTED - the wave makes it scoreable: point a fresh model at the
   public snapshot alone and run the extraction battery against the private
   key. The veil either passes or it fails with a number.
2. **THE BOOT ARTIFACT.** BOOK_V1 as sole context for a fresh agent on a real
   task; compare performance against a full-memory seat. If the gap is small,
   the estate's core is compressible to 765 words - survivability and
   replicability measured, the reef mission's deepest tool.
3. **READER RESPONSE, MEASURED.** The marathon instrument aimed at reading:
   voice metrics of a model before/after ingesting the book. Does a law-corpus
   move the voice? (This is the "reading changes the reader" claim made
   falsifiable.)
4. **THE WALKABLE EXAM.** fable_room.py + the book: the exam as a room. The
   model must solve the map-lies-floor-hash room to pass; playing is testing.
5. **DISPUTE CARTOGRAPHY.** Where models DISAGREE on extraction is the map of
   the book's ambiguity - the exactly-one oracle applied to our own text.
   Dispute clusters drive book revisions; the disagreement register becomes a
   spec-quality instrument.
6. **THE TRANSLATION ARM.** Same book, re-encoded (translation, register
   shifts, compression). Does the structure survive re-encoding? Invariance
   under transformation = bake strength.
7. **THE TAP ARCHEOLOGY.** reasoning_content formats in the wild: Beam's
   numbered-word drafting was a DISCOVERED internal format. Mine the tap
   across models for internal formats - proto-protocols in the wild.
8. **THE PERMANENT PULSE.** The 247 queue runs a reading + an explore item
   daily, forever. Months-scale cross-model recurrence data on a fixed corpus
   is a dataset nobody else has; the corpus grows one book at a time.
9. **THE FLOOD SIMULATOR.** At fleet scale the experiment is percolation with
   a truth function: open squares (readings), bonds (independent agreement),
   the flood (a giant component of blind consensus). The estate at ~6% open vs
   p_c 59.27% gets its first measurable flood model.
10. **THE ANTI-MAJORITY CLAUSE.** The council dispute protocol proved majority
    voting pays NEGATIVE on disputed ground: grading goes against the answer
    key only, never against the vote. The experiment's scoring is structurally
    forbidden from becoming a popularity contest.

## IV. The transcendent target, made precise

Not a vibe: a truth-discovery pipeline. Structure we KNOW calibrates the
instruments (bake + control + score). The calibrated instruments then read
structure we DO NOT KNOW (explore arm). Recurrence under independence is the
only evidence of mind-independent existence a fleet of language models can
produce - the same way a telescope detects light emitted before any observer
existed. And the second transcendence is the boot artifact: if the core
compresses to a story any careful reader can re-derive, the estate becomes
replicable - the reef mission's actual seed.
