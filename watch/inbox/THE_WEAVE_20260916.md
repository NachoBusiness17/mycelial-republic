## 1. THE SPINE — the ramp law is the build order

Mark's law, verbatim (2026-08-13): *"start at tabletop perfect, mud, perfect, roguelite, perfect, then move on in complexity and games at the base level this will act as our early swarm tests did to our complex swarm structure."*

Five rungs. You do not climb until the current rung passes its falsifier. "Perfect" is not an adjective — it is a falsifier that either holds or does not. Every mechanic in Section 2 enters the estate AT exactly one rung.

## 3. THE PROMPT GENOME LAWS — the design constitution of this doc

Mark's six verbs, and what each one does to this document:

1. **perfect** — "tabletop perfect, mud, perfect, roguelite, perfect." Perfect is a falsifier word, not an adjective. A rung is perfect when its falsifier holds under harness (Section 1). No mechanic climbs; no rung is skipped; every row in Section 2 entered at the rung where its falsifier can be tested.
2. **steal** — "this is pretty basic — steal our mud stuff." Self-steal first: every mechanic enters from owned bytes or owned history, source-cited (HG1 #n / HG2 #n). The steam corpus is a steal table; the AzerothCore map is a steal table (15 rows, stance steal/adapt/reject/content, cite path+symbol); HG1's 14 eras are a steal table. Nothing is invented when it can be stolen from ourselves.
3. **descend** — the ramp goes down as well as up. When a system is stuck, descend one rung to find the green version: frontier inherits mud's souls grammar, mud inherits toy's verb and receipt law, the world inherits mud's rooms and toy's P-LEGAL. Complexity is added only at the base level, "as our early swarm tests did to our complex swarm structure."
4. **fold** — "the dungeon is the wave's own shadow work, playable." Work folds as knots; rooms are real orchestrator tasks; play must visibly pay (game_score, "real folded work, never fake points"). A mechanic that produces no foldable artifact is decoration.
5. **wire** — "Once you're a game it goes vertical with adoption." Built ≠ adopted. An unwired mechanic is an orphan (R2's five + HG2's ten prove the cost). Every QUEUED row names its consumer before it is built; every PARTIAL row names its missing lane.
6. **drive / sit-overtop** — "as df hack to dwarf fortress you sit overtop." Tools drive the game from above; the referee drives the world; the engine rolls the dice, never the LLM ("dice = engine rolls, not the LLM"). The bones chain is this law in code: a Dispatch gate no seat can bypass.
