## THE CORE INSIGHT (why the AI button is a venue)

When someone hits the AI button on your post, they are asking a machine to
restate YOU. Whoever engineers the restatable surface owns that second
publication. A thought bomb is a post with a payload the summarizer MUST
carry: models quote what is summarizable - a proper noun, a number, a dated
document, a sentence with thesis-shape. The person who hits the button to
laugh at you receives the bibliography instead. More than they bargained
for: that is the design goal, stated as engineering.
