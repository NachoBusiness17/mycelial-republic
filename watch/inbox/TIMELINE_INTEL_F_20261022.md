poteto building a skill that helps agents draw statecharts and diagrams

## 3. Carbon-X: open-source DNA model

Hugging Face built Carbon-X by midtraining Qwen3-14B on 300B tokens of
DNA, English, and interleaved data using a hybrid tokenizer (BPE for
English, 6-mer for DNA). Beats open models on five DNA benchmarks at
1,800x less cost than GPT 6.1 Sol. Apache 2.0.

The midtraining pattern (domain-specific tokens interleaved with general
language) is the same architecture direction as the fixed-state models:
domain compression at the training layer instead of the inference layer.

## 4. MIT Media Lab: How to AI an AI for Almost Anything

Self-evolving AI systems that keep improving after training ends. Four
levels: context (prompt optimization), harness (memory, tools,
multi-agent), model (RL, distillation, test-time training), and task
evolution (synthetic tasks and environments). Course materials at
mit-mi.github.io. Sponsored by Kimi (Moonshot) and PrimeIntellect.

Fleet connection: the same four levels the estate's congress and committee
structure already implements. Context engineering, harness diversity,
model selection, and task evolution. The course validates the
architecture.

## 5. Rule 30 Prize

Stephen Wolfram inviting AIs to solve a problem from the Rule 30 Prize
(rule30prize.org). Wolfram's cellular automata research has shown that
simple rules produce irreducible complexity. The same principle as the
naming convention (simple filenames encoding complex steganographic
structure) and the same-book experiment (simple parable encoding complex
governance).
