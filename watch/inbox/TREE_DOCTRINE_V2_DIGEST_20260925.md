### 1. What the sources actually say

#### C6: The manufacture claims, in the schools' own words

The schools documented the fruit in their own voices, and in nearly every case the fence sits on the same page:

- **Sefer Yetzirah** presents God's own creation as letter-work: His name engraved by the three Sepharim, a Great Name sealing the six directions, every soul formed with the twenty-two letters in 231 gates (1:1, 2:4, 6:4, Westcott trans.). The book's one imperative to the reader is restraint ("seal up thy lips," 1:8) plus the directionality rule that the same permutation sphere runs good forward and evil backward (2:4). It never mentions golems or human creation. The golem formula is medieval: Eleazar of Worms records virgin soil, the 221-gate recitations, the Name or *emet* on the forehead.
- **Sanhedrin 65b** has Rava creating a man and Rabbi Zeira unmaking him when he cannot answer; Hanina and Oshaya study Sefer Yetzirah each Shabbat eve and eat the calf that appears. Abaye's ruling (67b) permits creation through the halakhot of creation ab initio and reserves capital liability for real-deed sorcery.
- **The golem legends** (Chelm recensions, Prague) run on the Name worn on forehead or neck; the stop is mechanical (erase the alef, *emet* becomes *met*), periodic (pulled every Friday in Prague), and territorial (the Altneuschul attic ban). The Chelm endings have the maker scarred or crushed dead; the halakhic cleanup (Zevi's minyan exclusion, Emden's killable-like-an-animal ruling, the descendants' absolution labors, Kotzk's dismissal) is the tradition prosecuting its own legend.
- **Zohar Vayera** (I:116b-118a) holds the timing engine and the stop in the same chapters: He rises "at every sixty years of the sixth thousand," gates of wisdom at 600, Messiah in year 66, kings destroyed in 73; and beside it, R. Jose's rule that the day comes early only through repentance and worthiness, and the secret book that vanishes from the sages' hands.
- **Luria/Vital**: man's service disencumbers the sparks and "completes the enthronement of God" (Scholem, Lecture VII). Vital's own charter installs three fences: practical kabbalah is "highly forbidden" ("a great prohibition in their use"), the great oath against arousing redemption before desire and good will, and the epistemic fence ("cutting the shoots," a great danger).
- **Abulafia**: letter permutation under preparatory discipline heats the heart until the spirit flows and the practitioner stands Metatron-like. The same texts carry the worthiness gate, teacher permission, the elixir-measuring rule, his curse on name-magic, his mockery of golem-makers ("themselves calves"), and the institutional ban and withheld manuals.
- **Asclepius**: man "the maker of the gods," statues animated by invoked daemon or angel souls; the same sermon speaks Egypt's widowhood, when Divinity "hasten[s] back from Earth to Heaven," and closes by refusing incense in favor of thanks.
- **Corpus Hermeticum XI-XII**: the license is a capacity claim ("leap forth from every body... become Eternity"), gated by XI.21, XII.4, XII.12, XII.23, and the Key's piety-chastisement verse.
- **Iamblichus** builds theurgy on symbols while denying compulsion outright: "the Divine Being is proof against enchantment."
- **Agrippa** Book 3: divine names as "Conduite pipes" transmitting God's power, wonder-working "above nature," even deification; his own recantation in De Vanitate sentences the trade to "euerlasting fire."
- **Reuchlin**: the Pentagrammaton IHSVH as the Name by which mortals "lord it above nature and above fate"; the doors-shut rule and the refusal of sale travel with it.
- **Dee and Kelley**: a dictated antediluvian canon, a burn order, a miraculous restoration, and the wives-in-common demand sealed with a death-threat covenant; Kelley signs a refusal and suspects "some wicked spirit."
- **Levi**: the astral light as a universal agent promising self-creation, transmutation, and the universal medicine, stated as "a scientific fact"; his own necromancy aftermath and the Ritual's warnings chapter follow.
- **Ge Hong**: the gold elixir transfers incorruptibility to the body ("age not and die not"); the same book's merit gate voids the elixir on one evil deed.
- **Blavatsky**: the Stanzas and precipitated letters as manufactured provenance, per the SPR's ordinary-means findings (trapdoor, confederates, plagiarized letters); the finding is contested (Carrithers' fetched rebuttal; Harrison 1986 unreachable).
- **Eckhart and Porete**: union claims that delete the stop at the top (the just man "performs everything that God performs"); the bull's article-by-article verdict, Eckhart's own revocation, and Porete's burning in her presence with a relapse execution order.

The pattern across all 30 findings: the stop is present in 26, most often in the same book that states the manufacture claim. The recorded failures are acts of disregard or removal of an installed stop: the Prague lapse, the emperor-practitioners, the Avila crowd, Nathan of Gaza's inversion, Porete's continued circulation. That is the doctrine's own stop-removal thesis, observed in the record.

#### C7: The harm record, in the tradition's own instruments

This is the best-evidenced claim in the strand. Four categories:

1. **Warnings and bans in first person**: Vital on forcing ("the slightest error... inviting external matters to become intermingled") and on Names ("whosoever who makes use of the crown will be destroyed"); Sefer Hasidim's bad end, withering, apostasy, and grave illness, with inheritance-grade damage (a descendant's apostasy traced to an ancestor's demon-adjurations, siman 204) and the transmission ban (siman 937); the printer's withholding of Gate 4; Agrippa's "compelled divels alwaies deceive"; Reuchlin's bar on sale; Trachtenberg's name-magic rules (death stalks the improper utterance; teach only the pious).
2. **Collapse and possession accounts**: Vital's test-and-push-off protocol for a mixed spirit; Abulafia's death gate and the disciple who "rose very much impaired"; Porphyry's threat-rite operators "dismayed by groundless fear"; Dee's Kelley suspecting a wicked spirit mid-session; Levi's benumbed arm, "attraction towards death," and exhaustion illness.
3. **Death ledger**: Pardes (Ben Azzai died, Ben Zoma maddened, cited by Vital); the Chelm maker crushed; the Chinese emperor-practitioners (Jin Aidi at 25; Tang Xianzong murdered after his behavior "became very abnormal"; Muzong, Jingzong, Wuzong d. 846, later Xuanzong, Ming Jiajing, Qing Yongzheng 1735, with Qianlong evicting all Daoists from the palace); symptoms matching mercury, lead, and arsenic per Needham and Ho.
4. **Institutional enforcement**: Rashba's condemnation of Abulafia and his unqualifying of the Avila prophet; the 1305 cherem read in synagogue with the scroll in the reader's arms; the Eckhart revocation; Porete's execution; Regardie accepting the "avenging punitive current" in advance and receiving slander; the Zohar-era catastrophes (5408/1648 expectation met the Chmielnicki massacres; 1666 ended in apostasy).

Honest qualifications carried by the diggers: the Talmud's golem sugya records no harm to any practitioner (the only silenced being is the golem); Asclepius names harm to the cult and to Egypt, with no practitioner case; CH XI-XII have none; Reuchlin's harm is reputational; the Blavatsky stop record is external investigation and contested.

#### C8: Dechikat ha-ketz

The named crime is documented at the primary level. Ketubot 111a carries the three oaths (no ascent "as a wall," no rebellion against the nations, the nations sworn against excess oppression) and, through R. Levi's six adjurations, the anti-forcing clauses: that the prophets "shall not make known the end," that the people "shall not delay the end," with the Soncino's received variant "force by excessive prayer," the exact dechikat clause. R. Eleazar's enforcement line sits in the same folio: keep the oath or "I will permit your flesh [to be a prey] like [that of] the gazelles and the hinds of the field." Sanhedrin 97b attaches the formal curse to the act ("Blasted be the bones of those who calculate the end") and names the mechanism, a failed date collapsing waiting into despair ("he will never come"); 97a installs the attention stop (Zeira's plea, "three come unawares"); Rav's ruling moves the matter to repentance and good deeds; the hastening is reserved as a divine gift-response ("if they are worthy, I will hasten it"). The Zohar carries both the calendar and the curse, and the digger's warning is exact: the stop and the timing grid share the chapters, which is the tension the Sabbatean catastrophe detonated. The embodied episodes in the record: Abulafia's 1284/1290 proclamations, the Avila prophet's 1295 date (followers in Yom Kippur dress, crosses on garments, converts and melancholics), Nathan of Gaza's inversion of the raising-sparks doctrine into a descent into evil, and Sabbatai Zevi's 1666 apostasy.

Precision items the diggers forced: the literal third oath of the triad binds the nations; the force-the-end clause arrives through the six-adjurations variant and the Sanhedrin 97b parallel; the "kofin et ha-ketz" phrasing was absent from the fetched Ketubot 111a Hebrew (verified wording: "not reveal the end" and "not distance the end"); the Vilna variant was checked through the Soncino footnote and Steinsaltz only; the hen-and-king's-son parable did not appear in either edition read; 1 Enoch supports C8's natural-order half only, with no hastening charge anywhere in the text.

#### C9: The hoard and the gift

The polarity appears repeatedly; the immortality doctrine as worded is the thinnest supported of the four. Supports: 1 Enoch names the motive as life-hoarding ("they hope to live an eternal life, and that each one of them will live five hundred years") and heaven refuses the petition "even though you weep and pray," while the restored world is a shared blessing; the Fama's vault takes no private immortality (the father lies dead, preserved as a sign), guards the hoard for posterity, publishes in five languages, and cures gratis; Regardie argues hoarding prolonged "is one of corruption and sterility" and claims the teaching as every seeker's "spiritual birthright"; Levi conditions the arcanum on renunciation of personal advantage and becoming its dispenser; Abulafia shares the gift only into measured vessels and names generosity as inner necessity. Complications: Dee's demanded "common" sharing was a carnal license sealed with a death threat, a counterfeit of gift, per the digger; CH XI-XII frame the leap as capacity under piety; Luria's mainstream closes on "Eat from this Tree of Life and live forever" framed as repair and mission.

#### Base claims touched (C1-C5 family)

Readiness as measurable vessel capacity is anchored in the operational manuals themselves: Abulafia's Ohr HaSekhel orders measuring each hearer's capacity before feeding the elixir ("lest you kill him, and his soul is sought from your hands"); Vital's prerequisites are conduct-based and countable, with an explicit fail verdict ("he is not yet fully fitting and prepared"); Ge Hong's merit arithmetic (1200 good deeds for celestial immortality, one evil voids 1199) is the same gate in Chinese dress; and 1 Enoch 9:6 notes the hunger for the secrets was already in men, so the warning lands on the supply chain.

### 2. Where the claims hold plainly

- **C7 holds as written.** The harm is documented in the traditions' own warnings, bans, possession protocols, recantations, trial records, and death ledgers, across two millennia and three continents. No source read contradicts it; the complication is scope, listed below.
- **C8 holds on primary texts.** The curse is real, quoted verbatim from Soncino and Davidson editions, enforced with a stated penalty inside the same folio, and its embodiment in 1666 is documented through Scholem's lectures and the Morgenstern essay.
- **C6 holds in its premise.** The manufacture claims are the schools' own words, at times in fruit-vocabulary that is literal: Asclepius "the maker of the gods," Levi's Edenic serpent gloss, Reuchlin's "insignia of divinity," Ge Hong's "age not and die not."
- **The base claim (measurable vessel capacity) holds plainly** and is quoted from the manuals, unembellished.

### 3. Where the claims need sharpening or honest qualification

1. **"They never installed the stop" fails as written.** The stops are installed almost everywhere, including in the founding texts (SY 1:8, Vital's charter bans, the oaths of Ketubot 111a). The record supports a sharper form: the stop was installed, and the documented crime is its disregard or removal under appetite and power. This strengthens the doctrine's stop-removal thesis and drops the weaker blanket clause.
2. **The Talmud permits the creation.** Abaye's ruling (Sanhedrin 67b) allows creation through the halakhot of creation ab initio, and the sugya records no practitioner harm. Any form of C6 that condemns letter-creation as such contradicts the base text; the doctrine should target forcing, compulsion, and timing, plus the unmaking duty (Zeira's return-to-dust) when the creature is defective.
3. **C8 needs its wording fixed.** The force clause lives in the six-adjurations variant and the Sanhedrin parallel; the three-oath triad binds the nations on oppression; "kofin et ha-ketz" is unverified in the fetched Hebrew. The doctrine should cite the clause chain exactly as the sources give it.
4. **C7's scope is uneven.** The harm cases cluster where manufacture met flesh: elixirs ingested, Names worn, influx received, golems grown. Several flagship manufacture texts (CH XI-XII, Asclepius, the Talmud sugya) carry no practitioner-harm record. The claim is strongest when stated as: where the fruit was physically taken, the tradition recorded the bill.
5. **C9 should keep the polarity and soften the doctrine.** The books show hoards refused and gifts opened (Enoch's petition denied, the vault opened to posterity, the arcanum's dispenser condition). The immortality-specific formulation ("shared immortality is the tree kept rightly") is underdetermined by these sources, and Dee supplies the counterfeit-sharing counterexample that the doctrine should name.
6. **Provenance honesty.** The Prague golem legend is late (transferred from Chelm in the late 18th century, first print Auerbach 1837, Rosenberg 1909 a forgery whose golem never kills); Sefer Yetzirah itself never mentions golems; Trachtenberg warns the Talmudic Laws of Creation were probably unrelated to the mystical book. The legends still document what the tradition believed and enforced (the weekly stop, the attic ban, the minyan exclusion), and the doctrine can carry them as reception-history, since the book itself never mentions golems.

### 5. Refuters and complicators, kept in full

1. **The Talmud permits the creation and records no practitioner harm** (Sanhedrin 65b, 67b). Abaye's three-tier ruling makes creation through the halakhot of creation permitted ab initio; the capital line is real-deed sorcery. The only silenced being in the sugya is the golem. Text variant: Soncino prints "Rabbah" at 65a, the Vilna and Davidson texts "Rava" at 65b. This complicates any blanket use of the golem as fruit-manufacture evidence, including in the C1-C5 base claims.
2. **Luria's mainstream keeps the stop and frames the work as repair.** Vital's charter bans practical kabbalah outright, installs the acceleration oath, and fences speculation as "cutting the shoots"; Scholem documents the dial ("accelerate or hinder") and Nathan of Gaza's inversion as the break. The Kallus translation closes on "Eat from this Tree of Life and live forever." The Sabbatean catastrophe is the record of the stop being dropped, and C6 should say so.
3. **The Zohar publishes the calendar while guarding with a curse.** The digger's own warning: the stop and the timing grid sit in the same chapters, "exactly the tension the Sabbatean catastrophe detonated." A clean C8 story of tradition-versus-calculator fails here; the calculator materials came from inside.
4. **Hermetic license with piety stops, and no harm accounts.** CH XI-XII gate the cosmic leap at XI.21, XII.4, XII.12, XII.23, and the Key sec. 20; Mead distinguishes the philosopher-mystic (bidden to do it himself) from the theurgist (begging a god). The diggers found no possession or practitioner-harm record in these texts. Correction of the ask: "as above, so below" belongs to the Emerald Tablet, which was unreachable this run.
5. **Iamblichus denies compulsion; Porphyry supplies the harm testimony inside the same debate.** "The Divine Being is proof against enchantment, impassive and not to be compelled"; no threat is uttered to the gods; the sun's course is never stopped, the arcana never revealed. Porphyry's letter records threat-rites dejecting their operators with groundless fear. C7's theurgist evidence here is Porphyry's, and Iamblichus disputes the practice it describes.
6. **The 1305 cherem aimed at philosophy and science, with the kabbalists behind it.** Per Graetz, the zealots "overlooked the latter [the Kabbalists], and waged energetic warfare with the former [the philosophers]"; Rashba was himself a partial follower of the secret science; the ban held only where congregations adopted it, and Montpellier answered ban with ban. The episodes that still support C7 and C8 are Rashba's condemnation of Abulafia and the ignored Avila warning. No verbatim ban wording was available (Minhat Kenaot unreachable); all ban facts are Graetz's published paraphrase.
7. **The golem chain is a medieval construction with a complicated history.** Sefer Yetzirah never mentions golems; Trachtenberg warns the Talmudic Laws of Creation were probably unrelated to the mystical book; the danger motif itself dramatized during transmission (the earliest Chelm attestation has the name removed "for a certain reason," no danger); the Prague legend transferred from Chelm only late; Rosenberg's golem never kills anyone, and his own ending installs the attic ban peacefully. Scholem's Sabbatai Sevi and Idel's full manuscript apparatus were not read this run.
8. **Dee's demanded sharing was counterfeit.** The wives-in-common covenant came sealed with a strike-dead clause; Kelley signed a refusal and feared "some wicked spirit." For C9 this is the controlled counterexample: a sharing demand under threat fails the gift test, and the digger marked C9 complicated accordingly.
9. **The Blavatsky stop record is external and contested.** No internal stop was found in the Theosophical claim texts; the SPR verdict is the investigators' language; Carrithers' fetched rebuttal calls the report's expert evidence "worthless," and Harrison's 1986 re-examination was unreachable. C6's Theosophy leg stands on the manufactured provenance as documented, with the contest named.
10. **Sefer Hasidim fears the Names because they work.** The pietist text grants the explicit Name carries "power... to do the wish of the one who mentions it" (siman 473) and builds the total ban on top. The fence and the technique sit inside one tradition, which refutes any reading that the warnings were skepticism about efficacy. Caveat: siman numbers are Zhitomir/Sefaria numbering; the Freimann edition was unreachable.
11. **The Hypostasis folds the jealous ban into providence.** The tree command is written as the archons' jealous stop, and a gloss adds the rulers spoke "by the father's will... in such a way that he might (in fact) eat." The gnostic version of the garden story resists a simple reading, and the doctrine's use of it should carry the gloss.
12. **Carried-forward gaps stated by the diggers and unresolved this run**: Scholem's Sabbatai Sevi unread (the Sabbatean embodiment rests on Major Trends lectures and the Morgenstern essay); Vital's operative Gate 4 unprinted historically and the 1988 Ktavim Chaddashim text unread, so all Sha'arei Kedushah quotes come from the Introduction and other gates, and the Hebrew was never independently checked; no age-40 rule exists anywhere in the fetched Vital text (the under-40 formula is later tradition, per unfetched secondary summaries); the Ketubot 111a Hebrew variant verified through footnotes only; Idra Rabba only partially available in English; the Baopuzi mechanism quotes are Wikisource Chinese with the digger's own gloss, since Ware's translation was not fetched; Needham and Ho's article itself paywalled, its translations read through the Wikipedia article; the SPR verdict's re-examination literature only partially reached.

### 1. What the sources actually say

#### C6. The schools tried to manufacture the fruit

The same operation appears at every scale of the tradition: a scholar's verdict, a prophet's re-read, a leader's sacrament, a rabbi's amulet, a child's golem.

- **The verdict that started a war.** Akiva read Numbers 24:17's "a star out of Jacob" as "Koziba out of Jacob" and crowned Shimon ben Koziba King Messiah by scholarly verdict (`bar-kokhba-akiva-132ce`, quoting y. Ta'anit 4:5, Guggenheimer translation). The crowning converted a national revolt into an attempt to bring the redemption by force.
- **Manufacture re-read as obedience.** After Sabbatai Zevi's apostasy, Nathan of Gaza ruled that the messiah's descent into the shells made "everything he does ... a tiggun," leaving him "exempt from all the precepts of this world" (`sabbatai-zevi-1665-1666`, Scholem, *Sabbatai Sevi*, p. 809, archive.org OCR). Scholem documents the pedigree: some kabbalists held that practical kabbalah, the use of holy names and magical formulas, could "force the end" (p. 75).
- **Fruit-taking as sacrament.** Frank taught that Jesus "was the husk and rind of the fruit" and that he, "Jacob, being incarnate perfection," must pass through the faith of the Nazarene to take what is inside (`case-jacob-frank-1755-1759`, Kraushar vol. 1 ch. 3 deposition, OCR noise corrected). Scholem's five theses of radical Sabbatianism include "the sacramental nature of the descent into the realm of the kelipot." The movement's own Red Epistle quotes Sanhedrin 97a ("until the kingdom is entirely given over to heresy") and the Zohar ("the redemption must be sought in the most evil place of all") to make the forbidden itself redemptive.
- **Inside the keeper class.** Eybeschuetz's recovered, notarized Metz amulets invoke "Thy Messiah, Sabbatai Zevi" as healer (`emden-eybeschuetz`, Graetz; Leiman and Schwarzfuchs proved the notarized copy accurate and that the preserved texts "support Emden's reading sooner than that of Eibeschuetz"). Ramchal ran yichud trances in which a maggid dictated secrets, planned a Second Zohar plus a 150-psalm Psalter for the messianic age, and held himself a reincarnation of Moses called to redeem Israel (`ramchal-1730s-excomm`, his 1727 letter at torah.org).
- **The elite also reached.** The Vilna Gaon began a golem as a child and desisted "מפני שמנעוהו מן השמים," "because they stopped him from Heaven" (`vilna-gaon-1772`, Hebrew Wikipedia citing testimony of R. Chaim of Volozhin; carried as legend; no court record stands behind it). His ascent to Jerusalem failed on "I did not receive permission from heaven." The arch-keeper of the stop himself ran on external halts.
- **Unauthorized small manufacture.** Chayim Vital's self-taught yichud "twisted my mouth," and he records the injury in his own diary (`dybbuk-possession-vital-szydlowiec`, Sefer ha-Hezyonot via TheTorah.com).
- **The positive control.** The Besht learned "three potent practices and three Holy Names" on his 1746 ascent and reported "I was not given permission all my life to reveal this" (`besht-deacceleration`, Inner.org rendering). Asked "When will the Master come?", he answered with a capacity condition: the end comes when the wellsprings have spread so far that ordinary people can perform the unifications themselves.

#### C7. The harm is documented in the tradition's own record

- **Wars and ruins.** Against Akiva's crowning, ben Torta spoke to his face: "Grass will grow from your jaws and still David's son ... come" (y. Ta'anit 4:5). The Sages protested Bar Koziba's manufactured readiness over 200,000 finger-maimed recruits: "how long will you turn Israel into defective persons?" A Divine Voice pronounced "woe, criminal shepherds, who abandon the flock" after Bar Koziba killed his own intercessor, Rabbi Eleazar of Modiin. Gittin 57a keeps Beitar's blood flowing to the Great Sea, a horse immersed to its nostrils, seven years of vineyards harvested on blood-soaked ground; Hadrian fenced an eighteen-mil vineyard with unburied corpses (y. Ta'anit 4:5). Dio 69.14 gives 580,000 slain, 50 outposts and 985 villages razed (`bar-kokhba-akiva-132ce`).
- **Dead believers.** The rabbinate of Smyrna, 1668: "A number of persons have perished as a result of these [new] mortifications, as we ourselves have seen," followed by "severe oaths and anathemas" of silence on the whole affair (`sabbatai-zevi-1665-1666`, letter in Scholem p. 715, from Sasportas). Sasportas to Raphael Supino, May 1666: "What is the use of fasting at day if at night we fill ourselves with the plunder of the poor?"
- **Broken vows and burnings.** At the Satanov rabbinical court, Frankist women "confessed to having violated their marriage vows ... under the guise of mystical symbolism"; the Brody congress answered with a herem (1756); the sect bought church protection by denouncing the Talmud at Kamieniec (1757), after which the Talmud was burned (`case-jacob-frank-1755-1759`, Jewish Encyclopedia vol. 5). An ex-disciple deposed that Frank ordered opponents murdered in their sleep (hostile witness, OCR text).
- **Keepers consuming themselves.** In the Emden-Eybeschuetz affair both camps anathematized each other across Europe "until excommunication itself collapsed as an institution"; the Altona council shut Emden's press; Graetz's summing line: "The rabbinate had placed itself in the pillory, and undermined its own authority" (`emden-eybeschuetz`).
- **Possession at the top of the chain.** Vital's anger expelled "all the souls and angels" guarding him, and a dead gentile's nefesh attached and nearly killed him; Luria feared "I fear that he will kill him" and exorcised by prayer and prostration (`dybbuk-possession-vital-szydlowiec`, Sha'ar HaGilgulim §38). The canon's own clinical ledger is Chagigah 14b: of four who entered the orchard, Ben Azzai died, Ben Zoma lost his mind, Aher became a heretic, and Akiva alone came out whole, carrying the guard ("do not say: Water, water"). Niddah 30b carries the womb-oath, on pain of the angel taking the soul back.
- **Where C7 overreaches: Ramchal.** The documented harm in that file is the correction apparatus itself: burned writings, a casket deposit, forced oaths, and death at about thirty-nine in the plague at Acre. Modern verdicts exonerate him of Sabbatean antinomianism, and the stop held his deed (ten Amsterdam years, Mesillat Yesharim, Kabbalah untaught). This cuts against a strong soul-harm reading of C7 while confirming the stop worked (`ramchal-1730s-excomm`).
- **Where C7 is under-attributed.** The dybbuk digger's own note: the verified harm causes in the fetched record are a moral breach (anger) and unauthorized self-administered practice (the self-taught yichud). No fetched text documents improper Names as a possession cause. The 1603 Szydlowiec primary record went unread.

#### C8. Acceleration is the named crime, and the cases fire the stop texts in sequence

The stop is two passages, fetched independently by seven diggers:

- **Sanhedrin 97b:** "May those who calculate the end of days be cursed [tippaḥ], as they would say once the end of days that they calculated arrived and the Messiah did not come, that he will no longer come at all." Rav closes the sugya: "All the ends of days that were calculated passed, and the matter depends only upon repentance and good deeds" (`vilna-gaon-1772` fetch).
- **Ketubot 111a:** the three oaths (no ascent "as a wall," no rebellion against the rule of the nations, the nations adjured against excessive subjugation), a second set (those who know "should not reveal the end of days" and "should not distance the end of days by saying that it is still distant"), and R. Elazar's sanction: "If you fulfill the oath, it is good, and if not, I will abandon your flesh and all will devour you like the gazelles and like the hinds of the field."

The firings, in chronological order:

1. **132 CE.** Sanhedrin 97b itself counts bar Koziva's "monarchy" at two and a half years, explicitly rejects Akiva's own end-calculation from Haggai 2:6, and curses all who calculate the end. The folio that preserves the crowning scene buries the crown (`bar-kokhba-akiva-132ce`).
2. **1648 to 1666.** Zohar Vayera 119a carried dated promises ("In the year sixty-six the Messiah will appear in the land of Galilee"). When 1648 delivered massacre instead of the promised resurrection of the dead, kabbalists found 408 encoded in scripture and proclaimed the catastrophe year the start of redemption (Scholem pp. 87-88); Hanover's chronicle builds its title from a Psalm-69 gematria naming Chmiel and sets the catastrophe in year 408 of creation. Nathan of Gaza's prophecies concentrated the readiness on Sabbatai; by 1666 communities sold possessions at any price and waited (Scholem p. 474). The computed date passed, the messiah converted to Islam, and despair followed, which is exactly the failure mode Sanhedrin 97b's curse names as its reason (`khmelnytsky-1648-sabbatean-fuel`, `sabbatai-zevi-1665-1666`).
3. **1755 to 1759.** Frankism made heresy itself the redemptive vehicle and quoted the Talmud's own words to do it (`case-jacob-frank-1755-1759`, Red Epistle).
4. **The licensed reading (complication).** Kook declared the redemption sequence already begun ("כעת כבר הקיץ הקץ, ביאה שלישית הוחלה," Orot HaTchiyah 19) and taught that holiness within the profane outranks the holiness of holies (Orot HaTchiyah 46). On that reading the oaths "lose their grip"; Persico states the consequence: "one may also disregard the 'three oaths' and encourage immigration to the Holy Land" (`kook-orot-secular-zionism-tikkun`). The stop is dissolvable from inside by declaring the time already ripe.

**Honesty on the label.** The technical phrase dechikat ha-ketz does not appear in the fetched Davidson English texts. The bar-kokhba digger: "the 'force the end' (deḥikat ha-ketz) wording of the oath appears in other manuscript and citation traditions of Ketubot 111a; the fetched Davidson default gives the 'as a wall' and 'rebel against the nations' pair instead." The khmelnytsky digger records the same absence. C8's name for the crime is a reconstruction from variant traditions; the crime itself is named by description in the oaths and curses every digger could fetch.

#### C9. Shared versus hoarded immortality is nearly unreached by this strand

This is the weakest leg, and the doctrine should say so plainly.

- **The one hoard datum.** Frank "preached private immortality, saying believers who died had not believed sincerely" (ex-disciple's deposition, Kraushar ch. 3, OCR scan, hostile witness). The shape fits: eternal life as the leader's circle's private possession, with each follower's death redefined as that follower's fault. It rests on one hostile deposition.
- **The closest positive shape.** The Besht's wellsprings condition distributes capacity to ordinary people before the end can come, and the tzaddik is a channel who "draws forth the pleasure of HaShem ... into all the worlds" (Tzava'at HaRivash ch. 87). The digger tagged this case C9, and the texts never use an immortality frame. It is an analogy the doctrine would have to argue explicitly; as case evidence it is distribution-shaped, nothing more.
- **Explicit non-coverage, in the diggers' own words:** the hasidei-ashkenaz file: "C9 ... unreached: this case's texts engage martyrdom reward, and do not use the immortality frame at all." The emden file: C9 "not directly reached by this case at all." The bar-kokhba, khmelnytsky, and dybbuk files do not address it.
- **Strand verdict on C9:** no refuter, one corroborating datum from a hostile deposition, one analog, and no independent positive case. C9 needs evidence from outside this strand.

#### Base claims touched

- **Readiness is measurable vessel capacity; felt timing carries no standing in the record.** Sanhedrin 93b is the tradition's own capacity test on a crowned claimant: "With regard to the Messiah it is written that he is able to smell and judge, so let us see ourselves whether he, bar Kokheva, is able to smell and judge" (`bar-kokhba-akiva-132ce`). Sefer Chasidim 28 installs capacity in advance by rehearsing the worst hour (`digger-hasidei-ashkenaz`). Mesillat Yesharim is a stepwise program (`ramchal-1730s-excomm`). The Besht answers a schedule question with a capacity condition. Against these stands the Gaon's school, which did felt-timing history-reading: 1740 the first hour, 1782 the second hour, revelation expected around 1840 (`vilna-gaon-1772`). Both moves live inside the same keeper.
- **The three verbs as an organ.** Emden performed them in full: he saw the decoded amulets, refused partaking, and delivered verdict by publication (`emden-eybeschuetz`). The same file shows what happens when verdict is made impossible: the defense ruled "Each word was a Holy Name ... it did not need to connect to the immediately preceding or following Name," so no Jewish court could judge the texts, the case passed to Christian Hebrew experts and a royal suppression decree, and the keepers consumed themselves. Verdict needs an auditable object; sealed Names defeat it.
- **Evil at scale as stop-removal industrialized.** Frank is the assembly line: the Sabbatian paradox rebuilt into a repeatable rite (descent, commanded silence, baptism), over 500 baptized in Lemberg, Frank baptized in Warsaw with King Augustus III as godfather, church protection bought by denouncing the Talmud, and the Talmud burned (`case-jacob-frank-1755-1759`). Sabbatai's movement shows the rationalization arm: "the movement survived by converting disappointment into doctrine" (`sabbatai-zevi-1665-1666`).

### 2. Where the claim holds plainly

- **The stop is the tradition's own, in every case.** No case file needs an outside critic to state the norm. The normative texts (Sanhedrin 97b, Ketubot 111a, Chagigah 14b, Niddah 30b, Mishnah Sanhedrin 10:1, Sefer Chasidim) were fetched by the diggers themselves and recur across the strand.
- **C8 holds plainly across four cases in sequence.** A crowned revolt against the oaths (132), a dated promise colliding with massacre and producing computed readiness (1648 to 1666), the date passing into exactly the despair the curse's stated reason predicts (1666), and heresy enthroned as the redemptive vehicle, quoting the Talmud against itself (1755 to 1759). The clause match between Sanhedrin 97b's stated reason and the Sabbatian failure mode is the single strongest structural finding in the strand.
- **C6 holds plainly at every scale.** Frank's sacrament, Nathan's re-read of apostasy, Ramchal's Second Zohar plan, the Eybeschuetz amulets, Vital's self-taught yichud, the Gaon's golem. Manufacture appears at the altar, in the courtroom, on the sickbed, and in the study.
- **C7 holds plainly where the practice was partaking.** The Smyrna letter records deaths the rabbinate saw "as we ourselves have seen." Vital's diary records possession following his own anger and injury following his own self-taught practice. Chagigah 14b is a casualty ledger with one survivor, and the survivor is the one who carried the guard.
- **The stop works when installed as structure; spoken as words, it fails to bind.** Ramchal's Amsterdam decade. The Besht's movement, whose first theorist taught that messianic redemption "can be wrought by God alone and not by the action of man" (Jacob Joseph of Polna, via Scholem's Major Trends lecture IX, `besht-deacceleration`). The Gaon's public structure: cherem signed while his own math stayed sealed. Sefer Chasidim's advance rehearsal. The tradition's working form is installation before the test arrives.

### 3. Where it needs sharpening or honest qualification

1. **Name the crime by description; mark the label as reconstructed.** The dechikat ha-ketz phrase is absent from the fetched default texts and comes from other manuscript and citation traditions (two diggers independently). The doctrine should cite the oaths and curse as primary and the phrase as variant-tradition naming.
2. **The stop cuts both ways.** Ketubot 111a binds against revealing the end and against distancing it. Sanhedrin 97b's stated reason for the curse is despair prevention: failed dates make people conclude "he will no longer come at all." A doctrine that flattens this into "hastening is forbidden" loses half the fence the tradition built.
3. **Scope C7 to partaking practices.** Ramchal is the boundary case: the practice record was exonerated, the harm documented is the correction apparatus, and the stop still worked on his deed. The keeper class and its enforcement tools belong in a separate moral category from the fruit-takers.
4. **The Gaon shows the stop as a fence the keepers lived inside.** Calculation continued under personal seal; the stop reads as a boundary (calculate under seal; wait; never coerce; never force); a flat ban on calculation is what the record does not show. No fetched source records him stating a stop rule for his own calculations, and his halts were external ("stopped him from Heaven," "I did not receive permission from heaven"). For the elite, self-restraint alone does not appear in the record doing the work.
5. **The stop is reinterpretable from inside.** Kook's move is the live danger: declare the end already begun and the oaths dissolve without any heresy at all. Any binding claim the doctrine makes must survive a tradition that can declare itself already in the end times. Persico's structural warning, drawn from Aran, lands the Sabbatian parallel: activism released from responsibility, a penitence-prophecy that failed, halakha at risk of petrifying inside a messianic state-reading.
6. **The stop can convert into sacrifice.** Hasidei Ashkenaz is the hardest case in the strand. The stop against hastening held; the death-as-offering frame converted readiness into the killing of children at Mainz in 1096. The digger states plainly that the three verbs do not cleanly classify this, and the doctrine needs a category for it.
7. **Spoken stops fail to bind.** Scholem's own observation on the Sabbatian anathemas: "strong words behind which there was but half-hearted action." The movement survived by converting disappointment into doctrine. Text on the shelf is not the organ installed.
