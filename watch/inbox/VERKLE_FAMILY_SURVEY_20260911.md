## 1. The surfaces

### 1.1 dashboard/static/verkle_pyramid.html — "the chain as a place" (hologram zone two, 2026-09-08)

- **(a) Renders:** a raymarched neon SDF pyramid (raw WebGL fragment shader, 400-step
  raymarch, no three.js). The operator's Shadertoy pyramid promoted to the chain's
  self-portrait: big pyramid = the verkle structure; small cell pyramids stacked inside it
  = knots; a rainbow laser that descends level by level = the audit walk; spark particles
  at the base = events. Ambient monument, permanently animating.
- **(b) Data:** `fetch('verkle_pyramid_seed.json')` → served copy of
  `state/verkle_pyramid_seed.json` (both copies exist; static copy verified identical, 137
  bytes). Seed = chain FACTS ONLY: `{knots: 7377, tip_leaf, tip_ts: 2026-09-06T23:08Z,
  tip_num: 5346, prev_num: 88794}`. Only two uniforms are data-driven: `uKnots` (unused in
  the shader math beyond plumbing) and `uTip = tip_num/100 = 53.46`, which seeds the laser
  path's hash. The visual is ~99% procedural; the data touches pathSeed and the HUD text.
  On fetch failure it falls back to hard constants `{knots:7377, tip_num:5346}` and keeps
  rendering.
- **(c) Visual language:** raw WebGL, SDF raymarch, neon rainbow-on-void (#050508),
  monospace HUD with amber accents (#e8b45a), mouse-drag = camera orbit (mouse fed as
  iMouse uniform). Shader-compile error trap: `document.title='ERR: …'` + console.error,
  page stays blank rather than lying. One frame is drawn immediately before the rAF loop —
  explicit comment: "hidden panes throttle rAF, but the pyramid must exist" (the Fleet
  Console peeks pages inside a hidden-ish iframe).
- **(d) Gap:** shows STRUCTURE as a monument — no per-knot data, no themes/families, no
  time axis, no history. The chain could be 10 knots or 10,000 and the picture is the same
  pyramid with a differently-seeded laser. It answers "what shape is the chain" and never
  "how did it get built."

### 1.2 dashboard/static/verkle_knot_wave.html — "the Republic's genome, as a body" (2026-08-15; root copy `verkle_knot_wave.html` byte-identical)

- **(a) Renders:** a 2D phyllotaxis spiral (golden angle 2.39996323) of the chain's knots:
  each knot is a themed dot oscillating as a standing wave (sine per index), sized by
  `tension`, glowing with a halo when `residual > 0.3` ("it left residue"); a green radial
  wave-packet travels knot-to-knot = "an idea propagating via steal"; a white double-ring
  dot at the spiral end = the merkle tip (the head). Faint blue chain links connect
  consecutive knots.
- **(b) Data:** embedded via `<script src="game_verkle_knots.js">` — the global
  `VERKLE_KNOTS` array + `VERKLE_TIP`. No fetch; static snapshot regenerated by
  `mag/verkle_render.py` (schema `verkle_render.v1`, "Real bytes, not invented").
- **(c) Visual language:** Canvas 2D. Warm parchment palette (Georgia serif, gold #e6c07a
  on dark brown) — deliberately anti-cyberpunk, the "room that remembers" register.
  Per-knot colors come from the DATA (theme→color baked in by the generator). Hover =
  nearest-point lookup → readout card with the knot's REAL identity: theme, leaf hash,
  timestamp, session, tension, dirichlet, Q proxy, residual. Legend auto-built from the
  themes present. No error banner; no pause-on-hidden.
- **(d) Gap:** the spiral index masquerades as sequence but carries no readable TIME —
  dates exist only in the hover readout. Eats only the 260-knot snapshot, which spans
  **Aug 3 → Aug 15** (see 1.4) — it cannot see the last three weeks of the chain at all.
  No families-as-structures (colors are per-dot only), no era bands, no silence, no
  aggregation. It answers "what does the body feel like" in a permanently frozen present.

### 1.3 viz_3d_verkle.html — "the chain in 3D" (2026-08-15; dashboard/static copy byte-identical)

- **(a) Renders:** the same 260 knots as emissive spheres on a 3D golden-angle helix
  (radius slowly shrinking, y = index), linked by a line, white tip sphere at the top, a
  green traveling pulse sphere = the steal-idea, and the knots "breathe" — oscillation
  whose FREQUENCY is live system CPU and whose AMPLITUDE is our LLM processes' CPU, polled
  every 2s from `http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/v1/local-pulse` (the loopback fleet pulse).
  The chain's motion is literally driven by the machine's current workload.
- **(b) Data:** embedded `game_verkle_knots.js` (same 260-knot snapshot) + live pulse API
  (graceful: `live.ok=false` → "(pulse offline)", defaults freq/amp continue).
- **(c) Visual language:** three.js r170 via jsdelivr CDN importmap + OrbitControls (drag
  orbit, scroll zoom, damping), raycaster hover → the same identity readout as knot_wave
  (theme/hash/ts/session/tension/dirichlet/Q/residual), theme legend. Dark slate palette
  (#0a0e14), standard material with emissive glow, ambient+directional+hemi lights.
- **(d) Gap:** same frozen-snapshot problem as knot_wave (Aug 3–15 only), and the layout
  is decorative — the helix encodes index, not time, not family structure. It adds LIVE
  machine-state coupling (which is about NOW, not history) and a CDN dependency that fails
  offline. Families are per-dot colors, never structure; no eras, no silence, no
  construction narrative.

- **(c) CRITICAL for the new room:** ts span is **2026-08-03T17:19 → 2026-08-15T10:11**.

## 2. Gap analysis → the construction lens room

### 2.1 What the family already shows (so the room must NOT repeat it)

| Surface | Axis | Shows | Never shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| verkle_pyramid | space | structure as monument, audit-walk light | per-knot truth, time, families |
| verkle_knot_wave | spiral index | body, breathing, steal-idea, per-knot identity | readable time, eras, aggregation |
| viz_3d_verkle | helix index + live CPU | body in 3D, machine coupling | history, family structure |
| (chain jsonl) | — | the bytes themselves | any rendering at all |

**The unowned dimension is TIME-AS-STRUCTURE: days as places, construction as visible
labor, families as populations changing over time, silence as wounds, castle-vs-foundation
as a judgment about where the mass landed.** No existing surface aggregates, buckets,
compares, or periodizes. That is the whole room.

### 2.2 REUSE (do not reinvent)

1. **Seed-loading pattern (pyramid):** tiny facts JSON fetched at load, hard-coded
   fallback on failure, HUD keeps counting while the room still renders. For bulk data,
   use the **game_verkle_knots.js pattern**: a regenerable JS-const file from a mag organ,
   schema-versioned header (`verkle_render.v1` convention → e.g. `verkle_build.v1`), "real
   bytes, not invented" contract, copied into dashboard/static (both copies, like the
   existing twins).
2. **Color palette constants:** `THEME_COLORS` from `mag/verkle_render.py` is canon. The
   new room MUST consume the same mapping (ideally by emitting colors in the generated
   data file, exactly as verkle_render does) so biography stays #d8cfc0 across every room.
   New families invented for the room (era bands, castle/foundation classes) need their own
   named constants in the generator, not ad-hoc hex in the HTML.
3. **Error-trap pattern:** the errbanner fixture (arrakis/eraser_maze/bad_apple_field
   precedent — a fixed red banner div + `window.addEventListener('error'/'unhandledrejection')`)
   plus the pyramid's shader-compile trap (surface the error in `document.title`, fail
   blank, never fail by lying). AGENTS.md: the fixture was added after the maze/arrakis
   birth-defect night; the new room should be born with it.
4. **Pause-on-hidden / peek-survival pattern:** the pyramid's one-immediate-draw before
   rAF (so the Console's peek iframe paints) PLUS wipeout_lightmaze.html's
   `visibilitychange` handler (pause on hidden, resume on click with
   `last=performance.now()` so no time jump). The existing verkle surfaces have NEITHER
   fully; the new room should have both from line one.
5. **Hover-identity readout:** nearest-point (2D) / raycast (3D) → real knot fields card,
   as in knot_wave and viz_3d. Reuse the readout's exact field set for per-knot inspection;
   add day/era context.
6. **Console registration:** one VISUALS row in dashboard/static/index.html.
7. **Hologram law:** byte-driven or it fails (THE_VERKLE_HOLOGRAM law section) — the room's
   data file must be generator-emitted from the chain/topic_evolution bytes, with the
   generator named in the file header, same as verkle_render.py's contract.

### 2.3 DO DIFFERENTLY (the non-duplicate clause)

1. **Time is the primary axis, not decoration:** x = day (Aug 10 → Sep 6), one column or
   band per day; knot count per day as mass. The spiral/helix/pyramid already own
   index-as-shape; the room owns days-as-places.
2. **Families as POPULATIONS, not dot colors:** stacked/flowing shares per day, or swarm
   clusters that grow/shrink — theme evolution over time is unrendered anywhere.
3. **Era bands as first-class geometry:** named periods (biography-vom -> hands era, the
   Sep 5–6 concurrency wave, etc.) as background strata the knots land in. No surface has
   any period concept.
4. **The silence wound:** days with zero knots render as explicit gaps (dark, scarred,
   labeled) — this is F-H3's gaps-as-unwritten applied to the calendar, and the room's
   most distinctive honest feature. Do not smooth over empty days (that would be
   hand-modeled decoration under the hologram law).
5. **Castle-vs-foundation:** a comparison view (early mass that everything stands on vs
   late decorative/tip mass) — no surface distinguishes position-in-history semantics at
   all. Keep it derived (e.g. knots later depended-on vs leaf-tip-only), not vibes.
6. **No CDN dependency:** viz_3d dies offline (jsdelivr). Build the room on Canvas 2D or
   raw WebGL like the pyramid/knot_wave so it renders with the network flat.
7. **Generate a new data file, do not stretch the old one:** `game_verkle_knots.js` caps at
   260 knots and ends Aug 15. The room needs a new generator pass (daily buckets Aug 10 →
   Sep 6) from `memory/biography/verkle_chain.jsonl` (Aug 12 → Sep 6, 7,377 rows) plus the
   topic_evolution lineage for Aug 10–11 (chain jsonl starts Aug 12 — the window's first
   two days need an explicit source decision; flag to the builder before building).
8. **Name the zone:** declare it hologram zone three in the header/comment and inherit the
   falsifiers (byte-driven, gaps honest) so the family stays one program, not three toys.
