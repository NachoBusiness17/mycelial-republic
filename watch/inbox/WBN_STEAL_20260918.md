## The mapping: their organ -> our organ (all but one already exist)

1. Four players + one GM, fully improvised -> NPC = SEAT architecture: one ractor,
   many faces, over the lane mesh. Four persona seats take the table; the DM face
   runs the world. Traits into step() make the drama emerge from mechanics.
2. Childhood PRELUDES prologue before the main timeline -> generated Preludes: each
   PC's origin as a tiny tale from its stat-block seed (mulberry32, replay-safe),
   played before the campaign proper. The audience bonds before the stakes land.
3. Named seasons and numbered parts with cliffhangers -> the campaign engine's
   worldline: seasons as named arcs, episodes as kifu sessions, and the cliffhanger
   is a state snapshot filed at the tension peak. The chain is the season pass.
4. Sound design + original score (Fortunate Horse) -> THE TORTOISE. The GEB
   musician already derives themes from chain tips, already builds crab canons and
   Shepard pads. Each episode gets a score generated from the session's own hash.
   Nobody else's actual play has music that is cryptographically about the episode.
5. Character art per arc -> the render pipeline + imagegen: portraits per PC per
   arc, seated in the hologram/vislab frame.
6. Full credits block every episode -> receipts. Who played, which seeds, which
   gates passed, which chain tip the score came from. Our credits are verifiable.
7. Transcripts bundled per episode -> byproduct, not labor: the prose lane
   narrates, the ASR stack transcribes, both are already fleet organs.
8. Guest voice casting for NPC texture -> guest lanes: a visiting provider model
   voices the one-off innkeeper. groq plays the herald. The mesh is the casting
   agency.
9. Improvisation with emotional weight -> the whole npc-seat thesis: belief is
   attention, traits drive step(), so choices feel chosen because they are.
10. Story-first, mechanics serve narrative -> the RUINS GO law in costume: the gate
    refuses what the geometry cannot carry, and everything it carries, the prose
    lane makes sing. Fair rules are what let an audience trust a story.

## The pipeline (what we would actually run)

Prefer + brief the four seats and the GM face -> generate Preludes (tiny tales)
-> play the episode in the mud/game engine (kifu records every move) -> prose lane
narrates the kifu into episode form -> Tortoise scores it from the session's chain
tip -> portraits rendered -> receipts + transcript bundled -> publish. Cost: zero
dollars. Every output is a receipt. Replay determinism means every episode can be
re-watched from the record alone, which no human actual play can offer.
