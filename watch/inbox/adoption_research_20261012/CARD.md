## THE RESEARCH PROJECT: how to improve the adoption engine

The question is not "should ideas be adopted" (the estate's yes is total) but
"how does adoption compound instead of leak?" Four legs, ranked:

### A1. THE ADOPTION LEDGER (one sitting)
Today adoption is invisible: ideas leave the estate (posts, the seed repo,
tags) and their return paths are untracked. Build the ledger: every public
idea carries a marker (the repo link, a coined term); every inbound sighting
(another account using the term, forking the seed, citing the pattern) banks
an ADOPTION row - who adopted what, where it traveled, what grew. Lilith's
PS4 story is the founding row type: an idea that left, landed elsewhere, and
grew. Instrument: grep the timeline for coined terms (magatama, singing
ractors, command circles, "the rising tide") on a weekly cadence - the
sightings ARE the ledger rows.

### A2. THE ADOPTION COMPOUND LAW (one sitting, doctrine)
Mint the law the post states: ADOPTION IS THE HIGHEST FORM OF FLATTERY -
inverted into policy: ideas are seeded FOR adoption; paranoia is taxed (an
idea hoarded assumes its own poverty); the falsifier of an idea's worth is
measured by its adoption distance (how far from the speaker it traveled and
still worked). Lilith's PS4 idea traveled from a paranoid giver through her
to a robot builder - adoption distance: two hops, still useful. That distance
is the metric.

### A3. THE ADOPTION-ROUGHENER (one sitting, the contrarian leg)
Improve adoption by ROUGHENING the ideas before release: a polished idea
reads as finished and gets admired; a rough idea with a visible edge gets
PICKED UP AND EXTENDED. The Mr Pool lesson inverted: prophecy-pumps polish
because they want worship; the estate roughens because it wants children.
Test: post pairs - one polished, one rough-with-open-edge - and measure which
gets extended by others (quotes that ADD vs quotes that admire). The
trickster knows: leave the door open and the fox walks in.

### A4. THE ATTRIBUTION-FREE COMPOUND (one wave, the deep leg)
The paranoid giver's failure mode is attribution anxiety. The estate's cure
is already built: the receipt law means the ESTATE does not need attribution
to compound - every adopted idea carries its receipts WITH it (the seed, the
rib, the circle). Research: does receipt-carrying adoption change the
adopter's behavior? Hypothesis: ideas adopted WITH their falsifiers and
circles get extended further than bare ideas, because the adopter inherits
the improvement discipline, not just the claim. Instrument: the seed repo
adoption cohort - forks that receive the full seed vs forks that receive
stripped versions, measured on whether they fold (bank changes) or just

## Falsifier for the whole project

If the adoption ledger fills with sightings but no adopted idea ever returns
a CHANGE (a fork that folded, a term used with new mechanism, a pattern
extended), then adoption is flattery but not compounding - and the seeding
effort should drop to zero and the corpus should go private. The post's
optimism is testable. That is why it is a research project.
