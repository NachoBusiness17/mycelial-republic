## SOVEREIGNTY SYNTHESIS: regulatory moves that bound open weights, local inference, or accelerator exports

Bound (named, with source + date):

1. MODEL-ACCESS LICENSING PRECEDENT (the big one). 2026-06-12 Is-Informed Letter signed under Commerce Secretary Lutnick required Anthropic to obtain "a license before any export, reexport, or transfer (in-country) of its Mythos and Fable models," reaching any foreign person worldwide; Anthropic pulled both models for all users; a 2026-06-26 second letter exempted trusted partners for Mythos 5 only. Authority cited: ECRA 4817(b)(1) and EAR 744.22(b). Source: Mayer Brown analysis (fetched in full 2026-09-29) and Lawfare, "A Kill Switch for Frontier AI" (fetched 2026-09-29), which notes an open-weight release would squarely fall within export-control jurisdiction since distributing weights abroad is an unambiguous export. First documented use of export-control authority on a deployed model's access.

THE SILENT SEPTEMBER CLOCK (in-window). 2026-09-21: the American AI Exports Program's own decision deadline passed with no public announcement and no designated list; 2026-09-14: Commerce said decisions were coming soon (Export Compliance Daily). OPEN-WEIGHTS-AS-CYBER-THREAT ADVOCACY (in-window). 2026-09-24: an OpenAI leader at a Washington event warned of "persistent" AI cyber-attacks tied to open-source/open-weights models as they approach the cutting edge (search summary).
