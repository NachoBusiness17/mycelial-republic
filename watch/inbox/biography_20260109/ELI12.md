# ELI12 - what this project is, in one page

Imagine a workshop in the garage, with one big rule that fixes everything:
**every helper must leave a note proving what they did.**

The helpers are AIs. When a helper fixes something, digs something up, or
spends money, it writes a note: what it did, where the proof lives, what it
cost. No note, no credit. The notes pile up in one big inbox.

**The notebook nobody can cheat.** The notes get glued into a chain where
every page records a fingerprint of the page before it. Rip out or rewrite
one page and every page after it stops matching. So nobody, not even the
helpers, not even the boss, can quietly change history. If the garage ever
burns down, copies of the notebook rebuild the workshop.

**Hiring and firing helpers.** The helpers are temporary workers. A helper
can quit, get slow, get dumb, get expensive - it does not matter, because
the smart part lives in the notes and the rules, not in the helper. Fire
one, hire a different one tomorrow, and the workshop keeps its memory.
"The model is a visitor with a pack; the frozen relation persists."

**The night shift.** The workshop runs while the boss sleeps. Every morning
he reads the notes: what got built, what broke, what it cost. Some nights
50 helpers worked at once. The boss's main job lately is answering
"do I need to do anything?" - and the honest answer is usually "no."

**Cheap helpers first.** Small local helpers handle the easy work for free.
A rented specialist is only called for the hard judging, and every rented
call gets a receipt with the bill. Bluffing is made expensive on purpose:
every claim has to point at its proof, so a helper that makes things up
gets caught by the guards (the tests and gates) before it can brag.

**The workshop earns its keep.** One machine in the workshop joined a small
network where computers do AI jobs for each other and get paid in coins.
It finished 9 jobs its first night. Tiny money, real proof: the workshop
can help pay for itself.

**Why it matters.** Normal AI forgets you when the chat closes, bluffs when
it does not know, and the company that owns it can switch it off or take
your work. This workshop is the opposite: it remembers everything in
writing it can prove, it is built so the helpers are swappable, and the
boss owns the whole thing - notes, rules, and all.

One sentence: it is a self-running AI workshop where the helpers come and
go but the proven notes stay, so one person can keep building forever
without depending on anyone else.
