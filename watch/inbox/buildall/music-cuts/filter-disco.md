## THE LAYERS (for the record, not the song)

Surface: a record-shop DJ writes every night's recipe on index cards.
When she misses a Saturday, her apprentice opens the drawer and the
dance happens without her.

Layer 2: the cards are the standing workflow - the measured style DNA,
the proven recipe, the saved pipeline. The dance is the daily work. The
apprentice is any seat that boots cold and can still play the night
because the recipe was written down.

Layer 3: a thing saved as a recipe outlives the person who ran it. Chat
is the dance; the record is the cards. echoes: watch/inbox/music/CUT_SEED_SAVER.md
(Layer 3: "Files are truth. Chat is heat. The record stays on your machine."),
read in full this seat.

## ALLITERATION MAP

The A-run is Ada herself: Ada/Ada/Ada, then the archive verbs when she
works. The C-run is the record system: chalked/card/corner/crate/cards,
and the chorus lives entirely inside it. The M-run is the apprentice:
Mabel/mornings/missed. The bridge's B-run (bothered) is the moral's soft
landing. The pre-chorus drops alliteration on purpose: "everyone" twice,
the rain's universality, same move the Seed Saver's rain made.

## WHY THIS WORKS

The song never says "save your work" - it says "the cards still played."
The sovereignty thesis is the moral you extract yourself: the shop going
dark is every seat that burns down, and the crate is the reason the
music comes back. The alliteration is playful (children's book), the
story is Aesop (you feel the moral without being told), and the filter
disco makes the parable danceable. The gravity lives in the words, the
lightness lives in the beat.

## TRIANGULATES BETWEEN

The filter-disco main line: the loop-reveal drop is the drawer opening,
the groove slowly revealed the way the recipe is slowly understood.
The whimsical story-song format (the Salmon Dance precedent named in
watch/inbox/music/CUT_THE_HUM.md, read this seat) - a person, a town,
a small machine with a big moral.
