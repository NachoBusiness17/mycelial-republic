## 1. Diagnosis: a command economy that already knows prices exist

Production is vast and measured. The learned unit-cost store prices every recent job the same: all 87 rows of `memory/learn/sizing.jsonl` resolve to `flash` / `deepseek-v4-flash`, including the three freshest rows from 2026-09-28 (`sizing.jsonl:85-87`).

The estate has exactly one price system: the tier ladder, now codified as funding truth. "The $180 in Hermes credits ... is the ONLY funded tier"; free stealth lanes are "not funded at all" (`memory/rib/RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:9-13`). And it already has a written trade policy in its constitution: "Markets: Heuristic residual free; don't rent memory from a remote seat" and "Private residual never remote" (`docs/DNA.md:130-132`). That is an export-control regime (capital controls on the core) with no domestic market underneath.

One vocabulary finding: the file named `allowance` (`memory/frontier/allowance.jsonl`) contains no allowance at all; it is a frontier-council dispatch log of prompts to chatgpt/grok_web/deepseek surfaces (rows at lines 1-207 and today's tail). The estate's one file named after a budget concept is actually a dispatch log. The economic vocabulary is unclaimed because the economy is unbuilt.

## 3. The buyers

1. **Future sessions are the largest buyer.** Every receipt is purchased with the re-derivation it saves; AGENTS.md prices this directly ("Pay the context tax once"). A receipt is economically a bond: principal = the work, coupon = tokens it costs each future session to load, yield = avoided re-derivation. The census-carrier tripwire (B2-215, carried in AGENTS.md clippy) is what happens when a bond's stated claim rots: the carrier is flagged, not silently defaulted.
2. **Sibling lanes**, via pass-off: the workflow gate institutionalizes demand with check G2, "an earlier actor's output (text/json/path) feeds a later ask" (`memory/workflows/REGISTRY.jsonl` rows, G2-passoff, all PASS).
3. **The operator**, the sovereign buyer whose consent is the scarcest good: the owed list in `state/CURRENT.md:19-22` is an order book of jobs waiting on the only customer who can authorize (seed stranger verify needs "operator hand", `state/CURRENT.md:40-43`).
4. **External buyers: none, honestly.** The speculative market is data-as-labor: seat session tapes as training corpus (the literature is real: "Should We Treat Data as Labor?", AEA Papers and Proceedings 2017, Posner/Weyl and coauthors, verified by search). The estate has inventory and no channel, no license clarity, no counterparty. Any revenue claim today would be fabrication.

## 4. The currency: tok-hour base money under an unforgeable supply cap

The sibling flash50 item `deep-attention-market.md:34-38` proposes RESID, an asset currency minted by downstream consumption of verified work. Cathedral-market supplies what RESID lacks: a numeraire. The composition: RESID is the asset layer, priced in the base money below.

**Base money is the funded-account tok-hour.** Its decisive property: scarcity is enforced exogenously. The provider's rolling-throughput limiter is a central bank no lane can corrupt; every 429/1302 in the holds log is the bank refusing to print (`BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:926-975`). Internal point economies usually die of forgery (points inflate); here the money supply is outsourced to a counterparty with a hard cap that measured clean at width 10 and surfaced the wall by 24 (holds entries at :941-945 and :962-975; the 109.4M tok/h figure and maxAttempts asymmetry are carried in AGENTS.md's fan-out width law, whose primary receipts I did not open this session).

**The FX regime already exists and has teeth.** The driving ladder's climb rule ("sensing may feed judgment, but sensing is never substituted for it", `RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:20-23`) is a convertibility restriction: free-lane tokens are debased coinage, legally non-convertible into "done". That is a deliberate Gresham guard (Gresham from training knowledge): without legal overvaluation of verified output, cheap free-lane answers would drive verified ones out of circulation.

**The credit layer already exists too.** Verdicts and invariant counts are credit histories; `mag/one_sizer.py:62-81` resolves "cheapest capable tier" by mode-vote over landed ok-rows, which is revealed-preference unit-cost discovery. And here is a live finding: the monetary regime changed on 09-28 (DeepSeek unfunded, Nous funded, RIB minted 09-27/28), yet the one-sizer's freshest rows from 09-28 17:03-17:43 UTC still price work at `deepseek-v4-flash` (`sizing.jsonl:85-87`). The learned unit-cost store is stale against the regime change. In market terms: the price list was not repriced after the currency reform. This is exactly the class of drift a real market exposes and a command economy hides.

## 5. Four markets to open, each pinned to existing code

1. **Lane-time auction.** Daily sealed bids for share of the measured envelope; clear at the margin, queue losers to offpeak. Hanson's futarchy split (vote on values, bet on beliefs; training knowledge) maps cleanly: the operator keeps values (the laws, non-negotiable), lanes bet beliefs (which brief lands green). Instrument: bid fields in the BURN dispense records. The hard part of any internal market, delivery-versus-payment settlement, is already solved: "landed array re-derived from the LANDED RULE (B2_NN_*.md on disk ...), not self-report" (`BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:1067`).
2. **Debt bonds.** `configs/violation_baseline.json:2-16` registers accepted debt with file, rule, and acceptor, but admission is free while the cost is externalized onto every future session as context tax. Price it: each accepted row carries a coupon (estimated added bytes to pre-push and session loads); the registrar pays from its lane allowance, and `prune` recovers principal (AGENTS.md ratchet law). Debt becomes priced at origination instead of invisible forever.
3. **Free-lane options book.** The freelane board already prices liveness like a trading desk: burns LEDGER tail rows tick "DeepSeek 401-alive; OpenRouter 460 vs 458; Featherless 22,059 vs 22,054" and close dead lanes "FULLY DEAD alongside Chutes/SiliconFlow/Scaleway". A free lane is a free option with default risk; the probe cadence is the premium already being paid. Formalize the book: strike, expiry, probe age per row.
4. **Pass-off spot market.** Gate check G2 pass-off today requires a double coincidence of wants (Jevons 1875, training knowledge): a builder lane needs exactly what a reviewer lane offers, simultaneously. A numeraire removes the coincidence: reviews pay tok-hours, builders bid. The gate (`memory/workflows/REGISTRY.jsonl` G1-G7) stays the non-tradeable compliance rail.

## 6. The unpriced core: why a cathedral, not a bazaar

Raymond's 1997 contrast (verified by search) is closed central design versus open market production. The estate inverts him: it already runs bazaar-scale production, 553 numbered lanes dispensed in one window (with malformed duplicate rows at `BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:421-435` proving even the ledger has warts), under cathedral law. The design answer is not to choose: **price the periphery, never the core.** Operator GO, the no-fabrication law, VOID authority, and receipt truth must never carry a price, because a market that priced truth-claims would let lanes buy laws (Goodhart, training knowledge: measure it and it stops measuring). The scarcest good, the sovereign's consent, is correctly rationed by queue, not by sale: the owed list and the "operator-gated" crons law (`state/CURRENT.md:19-22`) are the queue.

Ostrom's eight design principles (verified by search) show the estate already governs its commons without prices: clearly defined boundaries (`watch/inbox/SHARED_SURFACE_CONTRACT.json:13-21`), monitoring by accountable peers (drift patrols, census patrol, AGENTS.md clippy), graduated sanctions (exit-2 blocks at pre-push, dispatch holds), low-cost conflict resolution (escalate), nested enterprises (lane, wave, estate). Ostrom's cases managed alpine pastures and lobster fisheries for centuries with monitoring and sanctions instead of markets. The estate's missing layer is not governance; it is relative prices for allocation, the layer Hayek named: the price system communicates dispersed knowledge so that coordination "might have been arrived at by one mind possessing all the information" (Hayek 1945, AER 35(4):519-530, verified by search). No one mind, operator included, knows whether the next tok-hour is worth more in Q-028 debt, FW-45 widgets, or another burn wave. Prices are how the fleet finds out without asking.

## 7. Can the estate sustain itself economically? The honest verdict

**Money revenue today: zero.** Every input is subsidy: the account envelope this session itself runs on, the $180 Nous credits named "the ONLY funded tier" (`RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:9-11`), operator electricity for the ollama janitors, and operator attention. The estate cannot sustain itself in money terms, and this item records that as fact, not aspiration. Break-even stays symbolic on purpose: at monthly subsidy S and external price P per verified unit, break-even requires N = S/P units; both S and P are OWED measurements, unknown here and not guessed.

**Use-value economy: real and closed.** The only sustainability metric measurable today is value per tok-hour, trending through instruments that already exist: the green-rate of burn verdicts, the ratchet debt trajectory (register vs prune), and receipt reuse through the receipt-body FTS index (2,229 files, `state/CURRENT.md:12`). External paths, ranked honestly: (1) data-as-labor sale of seat tapes: real literature and real inventory, zero channel or license, OWED everything; (2) open-source reputation of the gate and patrol tooling: converts to goodwill, not income; (3) the tithe model: cathedrals were financed across generations by tithes, and the estate's tithe is the subscription. A cathedral that cannot pay rent is still economically rational if each stone raises the value of the nave, and the nave here is measurable.
