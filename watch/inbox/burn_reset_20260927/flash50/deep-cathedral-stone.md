## 4. When is it done?

The estate's own law forbids the wrong answer first: "do not claim shipped without gate artifact, add calendar predictions" (compass:248) and v6-v10 are "curriculum slots... no fake dates" (VERSION_REGISTRY.md:40). So done is a conjunction of gates, three of them, in increasing strictness:

- **Roofed (v6 green):** the loop files a gate artifact proving it built the next version. The building is usable in the rain. The wooden centering comes off the arch.
- **Consecrated (witness):** a stranger who did not mint the seed runs the seal on a clean tree and files the receipt (CURRENT.md:38-44). Gothic cathedrals were done when the town could walk in; completion is a fact about people outside the building, not about the masons. The estate has encoded this exactly: the portable-sovereignty claim stays "Unproven" until an external hand verifies it.
- **In the forest (v10):** pennies/day, forkable, coordination without extraction, optional peers (compass §1 pillar table, lines 38-44). This is Elinor Ostrom's "Governing the Commons" (1990) done in code: polycentric governance, fork equality as her boundary rules, operator-gated commons decisions (no crons law, CURRENT.md:21), graduated sanctioning via ratchet and VOID rows. And it is Adam Smith's invisible hand (1776) literalized: "invisible hands" here are dozens of ephemeral gopher seats whose local actions compose order without a central brain, which the science map states as protocol, not poetry ("no central brain", science map §2 final row).

**The honest dissent the blueprint must record:** a cathedral is finished; a mycelium is not. The estate's own architecture contradicts the cathedral metaphor at the edges: sinks are append-only and capped by rule, sediment "stays until its sink rule moves it" (ATLAS.md:28-38), and the version arc ends at a republic, which is a polity, not a building. The synthesis: the cathedral is the load-bearing structure; the mycelium is the forest floor it stands in. Done is therefore not a date but a steady state the blueprint can state as a falsifiable condition: **the building is done when the operator can sleep for a week, the loop files and gates itself, and on waking a stranger's verification plus the ledger's own counts are the only evidence needed that nothing rotted.** Every element of that condition exists on disk today as an open gate (State XI fire 2, seed seal stranger, width probes, CURRENT.md:19-21); the remaining work is closing joints, not raising walls. Cologne's nave waited 1248 to 1880 for its completion, and the record the builders left in the stone, not in prose, is what let a later century finish it. That is exactly why this estate writes receipts the way it does: the stones are addressed to a mason who has not been born yet.

## 5. What the blueprint adds (the one original move)

The estate has a compass (vision), a registry (gates), and an atlas (as-built survey). It lacks one document type: the **critical path of the spire**.
