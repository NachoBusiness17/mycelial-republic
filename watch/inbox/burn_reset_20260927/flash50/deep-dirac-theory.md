## 1. Layer one: algebraic topology (a graph is a space)

Treat the graph as a 1-dimensional CW complex: vertices are 0-cells, edges are 1-cells. The chain groups are C0 (formal sums of vertices) and C1 (formal sums of edges, with orientation), and the boundary operator d1 : C1 -> C0 sends an oriented edge to (head - tail). The first homology is H1 = ker d1 / im d2. For a bare graph there are no 2-cells, so im d2 = 0 and H1 = ker d1 exactly: the cycle space. This is the classical fact (Veblen's early-1900s work on modular cycle spaces; Hatcher's Algebraic Topology, ch. 2, treats it as the first worked example of homology), and it is the reason the estate's rank formula is topology and not just arithmetic:

- dim H1 = m - n + c, the circuit rank / first Betti number. Equivalently the Euler characteristic chi = V - E equals beta0 - beta1 = c - beta1. The estate computes this at `mag/dirac_loop.py:263` and its tests verify it by hand on a square (beta1=1) and a figure-8 (beta1=2) (`tests/test_dirac_loop.py:26-43`).
- A "closed loop" in the operator's sense is exactly a homology class: two walks around the same holes with different rhythms are the same class. What is invariant is the hole, not the walk.
- The number 8 (operational scope) is therefore a topological invariant of the declared lifecycle graph: it is unchanged by re-spanning, re-rooting, or re-ordering the DFS. It would only change if an edge or vertex were added to the transitions tuple at `mag/organ_map.py:650-674` or a component were merged.

The homology reading also explains the estate's core discovery of 09-27, stated at `mag/dirac_loop.py:200-202`: adding edges never closes loops, because each new non-tree edge is a new H1 generator. In topological terms: you cannot kill a hole by adding a path through it; you can only fill it with a 2-cell. The estate's 2-cell is the banked receipt (section 4).

The multigraph subtlety (Finding 1): homology over a multigraph counts parallel edges as independent 1-cells. A pair of antiparallel edges u->v and v->u has boundary (v-u) + (u-v) = 0, so it is a genuine independent cycle of length 2. The estate's rank counts these (m is the edge-list length, `mag/dirac_loop.py:261-263`); the estate's test even pins a dangling 2-cycle at beta1=1 (`tests/test_dirac_loop.py:106`). But the enumerator cannot see them, as Finding 1 shows.

## 2. Layer two: the fundamental cycle basis (the algorithm and its price)

The fundamental cycle basis is the standard deterministic construction (Kirchhoff 1847 introduced loop currents and mesh analysis; the fundamental-system construction is classical, cf. Veblen): take any spanning forest T; for each non-tree edge e = (u, v), the unique tree path P_T(u, v) plus e is a cycle; the resulting m - n + c cycles form a basis of the cycle space. The estate implements exactly this (`mag/dirac_loop.py:168-182`) in O(V + E).

Three properties matter theoretically, and all three are visible in the estate's own design:

1. **The basis is a choice; H1 is the content.** Different forests give different bases of the same space. The estate's determinism (sorted vertices, adjacency order) makes the basis reproducible, which is an engineering virtue, but nothing in the closed/open classification may depend on the choice. It does not: the invariant hashes the sorted label SET of the loop (L185-188), and banking stores that. Re-spanning the graph cannot reclassify a banked loop. This is why closure survives the 09-27 -> 09-28 graph state: the classification is a function on H1, not on the basis. (I verified the mirror pair's shared invariant from both orientations during this ask: both directions hash to 1992f396..., check 6.)
2. **The basis is local and cheap.** Each fundamental cycle is the unique minimal certificate for its generator: one edge plus one path. This is the workflow analog of the verkle root: constant-size proof replacing full replay. The estate runs the same idea at two scales: loop invariant for the organ graph, chain root for the corpus (`mag/manifest.py:115-116` anchors the manifest hash into the verkle chain, deduped).
3. **The basis leaks orientation into the count.** Finding 1 (next section) shows the frozenset keying at `mag/dirac_loop.py:131,144,177` makes the enumerated loop count basis-dependent while beta1 is not.

## 3. Layer three: discrete exterior calculus (the graph as a manifold)

Discrete exterior calculus (DEC; Dodziuk's 1976 discrete Hodge theory, Eckmann's 1944 combinatorial Laplacian, and the Desbrun-Hirani-Leok-Marsden DEC framework) promotes the graph to a manifold-with-boundary on which the exterior calculus runs verbatim:

- A 0-cochain is a scalar field on vertices (the estate: organ states, e.g. the admission pressure in `mag/context_admission.py`).
- A 1-cochain is a field on edges (the estate: transition receipts, edge labels, the `evidence_required` receipts at `mag/organ_map.py:675-676`).
- The coboundary d : C0 -> C1 is the edge difference operator (f[e] = f[head] - f[tail]); its adjoint delta (the codifferential, with respect to the Hodge star weights) is the discrete divergence. The Hodge Laplacian is D = d delta + delta d.
- The discrete Hodge theorem (Eckmann; Dodziuk): harmonic 0-forms (kernel of D on C0) are the constant functions per component, so their dimension is beta0 = c; harmonic 1-forms (kernel of D on C1) have dimension beta1 and represent H1. This is exactly the two numbers `mag/dirac_loop.py` reports: `spectrum` returns kernel_dim = c (L287-289) and beta1 = m - n + c (L290-291). The note at L285 admits the kernel is computed by BFS, not by eigendecomposition; that is honest, and it means the DEC program (actually building D on C0 and C1 and diagonalizing) is unimplemented in the estate today. I did not run any eigensolver in this ask either; the spectrum claimed here is the two kernel dimensions, which are exact by the Hodge theorem.

Kirchhoff's laws are the classical statement of this layer and the estate already runs them implicitly:

- KCL (nodal): flow in = flow out at each vertex. In agent terms: conservation of work tokens at every organ. A violation is a leak (work enters the pack and never exits as a receipt).
- KVL (loop): the sum of potential differences around any cycle is zero for a consistent static field. On the knowledge graph, the "potential" is the accumulated state transform along each transition verb; a nonzero loop sum is a contradiction: A says B says C says A, and the cycle disagrees with itself. This is the theoretical basis for the claim that an acyclic knowledge base cannot even express self-contradiction, and therefore cannot detect it: on the knowledge scope, beta1=0 (live check 4), there is no loop around which consistency could be tested. The estate's own deep-dive said it plainly: the knowledge graph is a TREE, all the loops live in the operational graph; docs-as-graph cannot loop, practice-as-graph does (`watch/inbox/SELF_STEAL_DIRAC_MEMLANCE_20260927.md:30-32`).
- Mesh analysis: any current distribution decomposes uniquely into tree flows (determined by sources/sinks) plus one free loop current per fundamental cycle. The free parameters of the estate's workflow dynamics are exactly its beta1 loops. An operator reading `rank` is reading the dimension of the space of unconstrained circulating behaviors.

The Hodge decomposition (any 1-cochain = exact + co-exact + harmonic) is, I claim, the missing attention-allocation theorem for this estate:

- exact part = gradient of a vertex potential: work that flows downhill from sources (goals) to sinks (receipts). Healthy, scaffolded effort.
- co-exact part = divergence-free circulating current: effort that enters and leaves every organ at equal rate, touches the whole cycle, and deposits nothing. This is the formal shape of busy-work: loops that run forever with no source and no sink. A divergence-free detector on the transition receipt stream would be a garbage-work alarm grounded in a theorem, not a heuristic.
- harmonic part = the permanent loop structure itself: beta1 classes that neither source nor dissipate, the estate's fixed topology.

## 4. Layer four: the Dirac operator (physics, and why the name is right)

In the continuum, the Dirac operator is the square root of the Laplacian: D_gamma^2 = -laplacian + curvature coupling, and the particle it propagates is a spinor. On a complex, the analogous operator is the Kahler-Dirac operator d + delta (Kahler's 1961 inner derivation; on lattices it is the Kogut-Susskind staggered fermion construction, 1975). Its square is exactly the Hodge Laplacian, and its zero modes split by degree into the Betti numbers: the number of independent fermionic species equals the number of homology generators. This is the precise sense in which the estate's phrase "Dirac loop" is licensed:

- Each H1 generator is a zero mode of the first-order operator, an independent species that cannot be removed by any deformation of the graph that does not change the cell structure. beta1=8 means: the operational organism has 8 fermionic degrees of freedom, i.e., 8 independent ways to circulate.
- The gauge-theoretic layer: in lattice gauge theory (Wilson 1974), the dynamical variable on each link is a group element (a phase), and the gauge-invariant observable attached to a loop is the Wilson loop: the ordered product of link variables around the cycle. Physically it is the Aharonov-Bohm phase (Aharonov and Bohm 1959): the loop's holonomy measures the flux threading it, and it is blind to the path, sensitive only to the hole. The estate's `loop_invariant` is precisely a Wilson-loop functional with the structure group abelianized to its furthest degree: order ignored (sorted), multiplicity ignored (set), direction ignored (frozenset tree keys). What survives is the only gauge-invariant content of a Z2 connection: WHICH cycles exist. The estate measures the topology of its own connection and nothing else, which is exactly enough for its purpose (closure detection) and nothing more (Finding 2).
- Banking as sector selection: in the ledger (`watch/inbox/DIRAC_BANKED_INVARIANTS.jsonl`, 6 rows), a loop's holonomy value plus a receipt is recorded, and `scan` classifies any loop whose invariant is in the banked set as closed (L295-312). In physics language: the unbanked loops are the live sector (must be re-derived by replay every time: expensive, interferes), the banked loops are the frozen sector (verify by hash comparison: O(1), no interference). The tesuji phrase verify-without-replay is a statement about topological protection: an invariant that depends only on the homology class cannot be spoiled by continuous deformation of the path (re-spanning, re-ordering, refactoring the walking code), only by crossing a banked boundary (editing the loop's label set, which requires a new receipt).
- The aromatic analogy, one line: a banked loop is like a Huckel/London aromatic ring (1931/1937), a cycle whose circulating current is stable and energetically preferred over the open-chain alternatives; the estate's closed loops are the aromatic sextets of its organ graph.

## 5. The correspondence theorem: when is a knowledge-graph cycle a physical Dirac loop?

Synthesizing the layers, a KG cycle is a merely combinatorial cycle (homologically real, physically inert) unless ALL five of the following hold. I state them as criteria, each with the estate's witness or counterexample:

1. **Transport (fermion support).** Vertices hold state and edges MOVE it: each edge carries a transition verb, not an association. Witness: the transitions tuple at `mag/organ_map.py:650-674` (preflight, repack, enqueue, commit, authorize_effect...) and its docstring stance that edges are NOT marked verified merely because endpoints exist (L640-646). Counterexample: source-derived association edges of the knowledge graph, whose scope has beta1=0 anyway: nothing circulates in a tree.
2. **Independence (mode count).** The cycle is a generator of H1, i.e., it attaches to a non-tree edge; dependent cycles are superpositions (mesh combinations) of basis loops and carry no new conserved quantity. Witness: `mag/dirac_loop.py:200-202` states it as law. Caveat from Finding 1: on a multigraph, rank counts generators the enumerator cannot name; an unnameable generator is a dark mode: real, conserved, and unobservable by the current sensor array.
3. **Holonomy (observability).** A gauge-invariant functional of the loop exists, is computable from the loop alone, and is stable under deformation. Witness: `loop_invariant` (L185-188), pinned order/duplicate-independent by test. A loop with no computable invariant cannot enter the closure ledger and remains permanently open-noise.
4. **Sector choice (banking).** The holonomy value is banked with a receipt (`bank`, L231-254). This is the act that converts a hole into a filled 2-cell: after banking, the loop is no longer a question the graph asks (open gap, swarm work order) but a fact the ledger holds (verify-without-replay). Witness: the six ledger rows, each carrying the code-level receipt (e.g. row 4: water proof anchored via `anchor_proof` at `mag/water_computer.py:227-239` pinning sha256 of claim+graph_spec+volumes as a chain leaf, then refreshing `mag/manifest.py:115` anchor).
5. **Conservation (admissible moves).** The invariant is unchanged by the estate's legal transformations: amend-not-rewrite, re-spanning, re-ordering, refactoring the walker. Verified in practice: beta1 stable at 8 across 09-27 -> 09-28, all six closures persisted across a day of heavy sibling-lane landings (scan today reads closed_solved=6).

Corollary taxonomy of inert cycles: an association cycle in a doc graph (no transport); a dark mode under a frozenset enumerator (no observability instrument, Finding 1); an unbanked loop (no sector choice: it is a gap, which is exactly the estate's semantics of open = work order); an invariant that mutates under refactor (no conservation: bookkeeping, not physics). The six operational loops today satisfy all five and are closed; the two dark loops satisfy 1, 2 (rank), and 5-trivially but are blocked at 3-instrumentation and 4-access.

The self-referential coda: every one of the six loops routes through the dirac_loop organ and self_audit (`watch/inbox/SELF_STEAL_DIRAC_MEMLANCE_20260927.md:20-25`; visible in the scan paths, e.g. pack -> admission -> dirac_loop -> self_audit -> worker -> surface). The organ sits inside every hole it detects: the measurement operator is a component of the system it measures. In the Dirac formalism this is not a paradox but the norm (the operator carries its own charge); operationally it means the estate's audit lane can never be a neutral outside observer, and the closure of the LAST unbanked loop is the fixpoint where the organ's own membership is what remains unverified.

## 6. Implications for autonomous agents

1. **beta1 is the budget of unfinished business.** The circuit rank is the number of independent, non-decomposable work orders the org chart can hold. The estate's practice matches: each open loop got a named closing action and an owner lane (`watch/inbox/lens_20260927/DIRAC_GAPS_READ.md` verdict table), and the fan-out width laws of this estate (workflow children, Task agents) are constrained by a different envelope; loop count is the WORK envelope, width is the TRANSPORT envelope. Scheduling heuristic grounded in the mesh theorem: a fleet should hold open loops roughly equal to its parallel capacity, because each fundamental cycle is one free mesh current (one unconstrained degree of circulating effort), and extra open loops beyond capacity are standing torsion (unpaid contradiction debt).
2. **Closing loops compiles runtime knowledge into O(1) verification.** The measured payoff in-estate: on 09-27 the scan could never report closed (the invariant sets never met, `watch/inbox/lens_20260927/DIRAC_GAPS_READ.md:9`); after the banking act (the L191-225 fix plus six receipts), the same organ reports 6/6 closed in one command. General law for agent estates: every repeated verification (re-reading a file to check a claim, re-running a suite to trust a surface) is a loop walking a holonomy that could be banked once. An agent that banks holonomies converts O(n) replays into O(1) hash checks, and the receipt column is the audit trail that keeps the hash honest.
3. **Trees are blindly consistent; agents need holes to self-audit.** The knowledge scope's beta1=0 (live check 4) is a diagnosis, not a virtue: an acyclic corpus cannot express a contradiction among its own claims, so no amount of reading it can falsify it. Falsifiability requires cycles plus observables. Estate-prescriptive: keep at least one deliberate cycle among doctrine artifacts with a measured invariant (e.g. claim -> receipt -> claim), or the doc layer is epistemically dead weight. This reframes the estate's own finding that docs-as-graph cannot loop: not a limitation of documents, but the absence of any KVL surface in prose.
4. **The Hodge decomposition is a garbage-work detector.** Divergence-free currents (co-exact flows: equal in/out at every organ, no source, no sink) are the formal signature of churn. An instrument that classifies the transition-receipt stream by Hodge component would separate goal-driven effort (exact), structural circulation (harmonic), and busy-work (co-exact) with a linear-algebra criterion rather than a judgment call. Not built in the estate today; proposed here as the natural next organ.
5. **Dark modes are the audit gap class.** Finding 1 generalizes: any sensor that keys on canonical identifiers (frozensets, sorted sets) is blind to the distinctions its key collapses. In agent fleets the analog is quiet: two seats doing reciprocal handoffs (A delegates to B, B delegates back) form an antiparallel pair that a deduplicating tracker will count as one relationship and zero loops, while rank-style accounting knows there are two. The prescriptive: instrument rank (multiset truth) AND enumeration (nameable modes) side by side and treat any divergence as an inventory of dark modes, exactly as the scan's betti1=8 / n_loops=6 pair already does without naming it.
6. **The first-order principle.** The Dirac operator is first-order; the Laplacian is its square. The estate's dynamics are already first-order in time (each tick applies verbs once), but its verification is second-order (scan walks the whole basis). The Kähler-Dirac view suggests the compact invariant: verify the SPECTRUM (the two kernel dimensions beta0, beta1) rather than the basis (the enumerated paths). The spectrum is a handful of integers; a change in them is a topological event that deserves a receipt in its own right. Concretely: log (n, m, c, beta1) on every organ_map edit; a delta in beta1 without a paired bank/void row is an unlogged topological mutation.
7. **Spinor flavor, flagged as flavor.** The operator's belt-trick shader lore (the GLSL Dirac-belt-trick lattice parable recorded at `watch/inbox/FLEET_IMPRINT_20260917.md:1415`) plays on the SU(2) double cover: two full twists of the belt are trivial, one is not. The estate's Z2-valued closure (banked or not, closed or not, nothing in between) is likewise a two-state invariant that ignores continuous deformation, and the mirror-loop degeneracy of Finding 2 is a double-occupancy of one value. I claim no theorem here: only that the estate's operator intuiited the right algebra a year before the organ formalized it, which is the usual direction of discovery in this house.
