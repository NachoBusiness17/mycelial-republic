# DEEP-DREAM-CYCLE: The Estate's Dream Cycle (designed from its own organs)

Item: deep-dream-cycle (flash50, 2026-09-28). All estate claims below were verified by reading or running against this tree this session. File inventory is at the bottom.

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The estate does not need a new sleep organ; it needs a **hypnogram**. It already owns every stage of a biological sleep cycle as separate, verified mechanisms: an episodic capture pile, an overnight classifier that transfers episodes into case law and training rows, a token-pressure recursive summarizer with Generative-Agents decay weights, a percolator that compiles repeated signals into invariants and strips noise, an actual Letta-style `dream()` that reclusters and prunes the knowledge graph, a logprob replay instrument that measures whether consolidation changed anything, and a fail-closed AFK gate that defines "the operator is asleep." What is missing is the staging: the stages run on different clocks, two of the memory stores are disconnected (one is dead), the graph `dream()` is wired to nothing, and there is no REM phase at all. The design below composes what exists into a five-stage cycle (W-guard, N1 capture, NREM consolidation, REM replay, pruning/downscaling, awakening receipt), moves the right memories between the right scales, and attaches a falsifier so the REM phase can be killed if it proves decorative.

## 2. The three verified gaps

Verified this session: that file has exactly 4 rows, all timestamped 2026-08-11. The `memory_consolidate` step at `mag/afk_cadence.py:184` therefore runs every AFK cycle over a store nobody writes. This is the dream cycle's first fix: repoint FLOW_LOG to the pile (or make mem_lands a pile writer), or retire the step. One organ, currently asleep at the wheel while looking busy.
2. **`dream()` exists and is wired to nothing.** `knowledge_graph.py:393-431` is a complete offline-consolidation pass with prune counters, versioned persistence, and a self-describing doctrine fold, reachable only by hand through its CLI subparser. The one function literally named for this ask is an orphan.
3. **No REM.** Every existing stage is NREM-shaped: classify, fold, compile, dedupe, verify. Nothing replays the estate's own history generatively to stress-test what it learned. The replay instruments that exist are passive (`pile_classify` re-reads and labels rows; `warmed_cold_replay` measures). No stage produces a counterfactual, contradicts an invariant, or proposes new case law from old failures. Biology would call this a night of pure slow-wave sleep: consolidation without simulation.

## 3. The design: DREAM.v1, a five-stage hypnogram on existing rails

The cycle is a state machine over the estate's own gates, not a new loop. Every stage below is either already implemented (cite) or a wiring change, and each cycle writes one receipt row.

**Stage W: the sleep gate (exists).** Reuse `afk_cadence` verbatim: fail-closed AFK check (`afk_cadence.py:40-53`), fail-closed pause (`:89-99`), `right_size()` (`afk_loop.py:224-238`). Budget semantics become stage selectors: `light` (budget 1) admits only deterministic NREM work; `medium` (2) admits the local-model passes; `full` (4) admits REM. A dreaming cycle that errors must deny and name the error, exactly like t52, because a wrongly-awake dream spends the operator's tokens and a wrongly-asleep one is merely deferred.

**Stage N1: capture (exists).** Session closes, escalations, and the day-end row feed the pile (`state_snapshot.py:75-106`, `day_end_pile.ps1` step 1). This is encoding; nothing to add. The only change: the day-end row should carry a `day_digest` hash linking the day's verkle knots so the night's stages can cite what they consolidate.

**Stage NREM: systems consolidation (wire, do not rewrite).** One scheduled deep pass, day-end (~23:00 local), running in order:
  1. `pile_classify.py` drain, no limit cap: episodic state_hash rows become category/lesson_kind/recommendation rows folded into skill_ledger case law + training_rows. This is the hippocampal-to-cortical transfer, and it is the estate's single strongest existing analog.
  2. `memory_consolidate.consolidate()` over the REPAIRED input (see gap 1): token-pressure fold of scope summaries into the active layer.
  3. `percolate()` over the day's folded text: repeats compile toward invariants, novelty enters the explore queue, noise is stripped (the signal-to-noise gate; Tononi's downscaling before the transfer finishes).
  4. `template_invariant.consolidate_all()` doctrine dedupe (already an afk step, `afk_cadence.py:179`).
  5. `knowledge_graph.dream()`: recluster, prune duplicate knots, fold the dreamed-communities doctrine node. This closes gap 2 with a one-line call, and the estate gets its own function back.

**Stage REM: replay (the new organ, bounded).** Budget `full` only, off-peak windows (`mag/offpeak.py` economics), local models only, three replay loops:
  1. **Replay for learning (generative).** Sample k rows from `memory/pile/training_rows.jsonl` (270 verified) and `memory/failure_kb`; the local janitor (gemma class) generates counterfactual variants of past failures and past fixes. Run each variant against the invariant registry and skill_ledger: a contradiction is a falsifier candidate for `memory/percolator/explore.jsonl`; a repetition across samples is an invariant-count increment, never a single-shot compile (the percolator's count threshold stays the sole promotion path). This is the estate's own shortlisted Dream-RSI move (salon_50 A04) and Deep Generative Replay's trick: rehearsal over regenerated experience instead of raw storage.
  2. **Replay as measurement.** One `warmed_cold_replay`-style probe per cycle: preload the cycle's freshly consolidated active layer and measure the ppl delta on a fixed continuation. Below 3% the consolidation did not move the field; that memory is a downscale candidate, and the delta is recorded in the receipt. The estate already owns the instrument and its honesty labels (`warmed_cold_replay.py:12-20`); the dream cycle merely schedules it nightly instead of once per worldline.
  3. **Threat simulation.** The night is when `verify_gate` and `dogfood_audit` rerun against the day's changed surfaces (`afk_cadence.py:143, 202` already run them every cadence; the dream cycle promotes them to the REM ledger). Failures wake the hypnogram: a WARN row to `memory/attention.md`, the day_end_pile alert pattern (`scripts/day_end_pile.ps1` header). This is Revonsuo's move: sleep preferentially rehearses threats, and the estate's equivalent of a threat is a red gate discovered while nobody is watching.

**Stage P: pruning and downscaling (two tiers, both exist).** Soft pruning is retrieval demotion: the DECAY=0.995 retrieval score already silences unaccessed records without deletion (`memory_consolidate.py:14-18`); the dream cycle adds an explicit `archived_at` flag when a record's expected retrieval contribution crosses a floor, using the MEMORY.md refix pattern: unresolvable things move VERBATIM to a ledger with a promotion path, never to the void (416 phantom targets taught the estate this). Hard pruning stays behind the operator gate exactly as `verkle_compaction.py` landed it: fold-to-counter-leaf with archive sha256 and Merkle root, plan-only until operator GO, "deleting live evidence is a separate operator decision, never a rider." The dream proposes prunes as receipt rows; only the operator buries anything.

**Stage Awakening: the receipt (exists as a pattern).** One `dream.v1` row per cycle appended to `memory/pile/dream_log.jsonl`: stage timings, rows transferred pile-to-classified, invariants compiled vs explored vs noise, graph nodes pruned, counterfactuals run and contradictions found, ppl delta, prune proposals. WARN-only to attention.md, everything else quiet: the pile habit's discipline, mechanized (FW-50). Warm boot then reads the last dream row (`hermes_warm_boot.py:84-94`), so every seat that wakes sees what the estate consolidated overnight. This is the recall side of sleep research's core claim: consolidation is only real if waking recall changes.

Three trigger classes, all already owned:
1. **Gate triggers (the "when" of sleep):** fail-closed AFK + pause + right_size level, reused verbatim. No dream work during operator activity, ever, by construction.
2. **Pressure triggers (MemGPT-style interrupts):** pile silt (unclassified count; verified 1 today, alert threshold ~50), fold pressure (PRESSURE_CHARS=8000), FTS drift (the B2-465 daily check's exit 2), census drift (B2-244 deep check). A pressure trigger may run a bounded NREM slice outside the circadian window, deterministically only.
3. **Circadian triggers (the clock):** day-end ~23:00 = the NREM deep pass; budget-full off-peak windows = REM (economic gate via offpeak.py, GPU-idle gate via the existing gpu probes); 08:00-09:00 = the morning W return, where the estate already schedules improve (08:00), census patrol (08:30), and FTS heal (09:00) per AGENTS.md. ## 6. Literature anchors (connected, not decorated)

The estate's own docstring already leans on Park et al.'s Generative Agents scoring (recency/importance/relevance, the exact weights at `memory_consolidate.py:48-50`), MemGPT's memory-pressure trigger (PRESSURE_CHARS), and EverMemOS episodic-to-semantic consolidation. The staging design adds the classical systems-consolidation arc those citations gesture at: Marr's 1971 simple-memory model (fast temporary store, slow permanent store); McClelland, McNaughton and O'Reilly's 1995 complementary learning systems, whose catastrophic-interference argument is the direct justification for why the pile stays append-only while invariants compile slowly; Buzsaki's two-stage model and the Wilson and McNaughton 1994 / Lee and Wilson 2002 replay findings (the hippocampus literally replays waking sequences during slow-wave sleep: the pile drain is that replay, and the trajectory tape is the sequence); Squire and Alvarez 1995 on systems consolidation gradients (which predicts exactly what the estate observes: fresh rows matter, the 08-11 flow store went silent without harm because nothing had consolidated into it); Tononi and Cirelli's synaptic homeostasis hypothesis (sleep's global downscaling improving signal-to-noise: the percolator's noise stripping plus DECAY demotion are the machine versions); Rasch and Born 2013 for the coupling requirement (consolidation needs replay: the missing REM stage). On the ML side: Hinton's 1995 wake-sleep algorithm gives the two-phase precedent in training itself; Lin 1992, Mnih 2015 and Schaul 2016's prioritized replay justify importance-gated sampling (the pile's state_hash dedup and importance weights are a priority buffer); Shin et al. 2017's deep generative replay justifies rehearsing over regenerated rows rather than raw storage; Ha and Schmidhuber 2018's World Models and Hafner's Dreamer line justify REM as policy learning inside a simulated world; Revonsuo's 2000 threat-simulation theory predicts the dream should be biased toward rehearsing failures, which is exactly what `failure_kb` sampling gives it. The estate's salon battery already queued Dream-RSI (A04) and the Anthropic memory pyramid's explicit forgetting engine (A06) as reading; this design is the pre-registration of what to do with them.

## 7. Falsifier (pre-registered, estate discipline)

The REM phase must prove itself or be cut: within 30 days of landing, generative replay must produce at least 3 accepted deltas (a contradicted invariant with a receipt, new case law a morning seat actually cited, or a measured consolidation delta >=3% on the warmed-cold probe feeding a real downscale decision). Fewer than 3 means REM is decoration, the same verdict the <3% RESURRECTION-NOT-REAL threshold hands a preload: cut the stage, keep the receipts. NREM and the wiring fixes are exempt from this falsifier; they are bug fixes (a dead consolidator and an orphaned dream() are verifiable defects, not hypotheses).
