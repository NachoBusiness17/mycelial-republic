# deep-operator-singularity: designing the graceful singularity

**Item:** deep-operator-singularity · **Seat:** deep-gopher-deep-operator-singularity · **Date:** 2026-09-28
**Ask:** the operator created the estate but says "I don't want to keep it in my head." This is the technological singularity in miniature: the creator becomes unnecessary. Design the handoff protocol, name what the creator still provides, and give the test for "the estate is ready."
**Method:** every code claim below was read in this session; every number below was produced by a command run in this session (grep, wc, head, tail). The Dark Fortnight test in section 6 is designed, not executed; that is stated plainly where it applies.

## 1. Restating the singularity correctly

The popular singularity (Ulam 1958, who put the word on von Neumann's conversation about accelerating technology; Good 1965, the "intelligence explosion"; Vinge 1993) is a story about capability: a machine appears that is better at machine-design than any human, and from that point growth is self-driven. The estate's situation is different in one structural way that matters for design: Mag is not trying to become smarter than its operator. It is trying to become **present** where he is absent. Vinge himself flagged the humbler path as "intelligence amplification," human and machine as a team; this estate is that team's endgame, where the amplification finishes and the human member of the team retires from it.

The estate's own constitution already states the design goal better than the literature does. `docs/DNA.md:4-5`: "Fidelity lives in **files + chain**, not in a process that must stay awake." Read that line again as a singularity statement: the thing that must survive is the law in files, not the creator's head and not even any running process. The operator's wish ("I don't want to keep it in my head") is not a complaint about the estate; it is the estate's founding invariant finally said out loud by its author.

So the correct formal target is **dependence collapse, not capability explosion**. Let g be the fraction of estate decisions that require the operator. Operation of the estate costs `g*T_op + (1-g)*T_estate` per decision, where T_op is operator turnaround (hours, or minutes over phone relay) and T_estate is machine turnaround (seconds to minutes). Every transduction of one operator decision class into law, mechanism, dial, trigger, or veto lowers g. The graceful singularity is g -> 0 with three residues deliberately left nonzero (section 5). Good's ultraintelligent machine designs better machines; this estate's actual bootstrap loop is more modest and more verifiable: it converts its operator's head into loadable bytes, one correction at a time, and the singularity is complete when the set of things the operator knows that the estate cannot load is empty.

The estate even polices the vocabulary of its own ambition: `mag/constitutional_critique.py:344-354` rewrites overclaims mechanically, and its table maps "full autonomy" to "operator-gated autonomy" (`mag/constitutional_critique.py:351`). The critique module does not believe in the burst singularity; it believes in a gated one. This item takes that position and asks: which gates, retired in which order, by what procedure, verified by what test.

Fully transduced already, and the estate's answer to who turns them is written in config: the judge lane thresholds live in `configs/lanes.yaml:120-158` with `writable_by: governor`, and the doc comment says "the judge never moves its own gate." 5. **Preference origin** (the compass). Written once by the operator; already self-amending below him (SUPERSEDED mechanics, amendment ledgers, gate scenario cards re-proved against real FAIL rows, `tools/workflow_gate.py:22-31`).

## 4. The handoff protocol (design: the Steward Cutover)

The compass already reserved the curriculum slot: v6 is "Loop self-build, first Mag-built Mag" and v7 is "Steward autonomy, daily soil without asking" (`docs/ref/MAG_MYCELIAL_REPUBLIC_COMPASS.md:129-130`). The protocol below is the execution plan for v7, built entirely from estate-native parts. Its one governing law, which turns the operator's wish into an enforceable ratchet: **an operator decision may be exercised twice in the same class; the second occurrence must land as transduction, and the third must not exist.** This is the self-steal of the violation ratchet applied to the human: new dependence blocks, registered debt passes by name.

- **T0, Head drain (inventory).** Enumerate every operator-touching site. The census is one command (this session produced 2,034 hits and 27 gate files); the deliverable is an `OPERATOR_RESIDUE` ledger row per class: gate site path:line, last exercise date (phone relay log timestamps already provide this), transduction target class from section 3.
- **T1, Transduction.** Each residue class converts to exactly one of five estate-native forms: LAW (rib with falsifier, the RIB grammar), MECHANISM (checker with the exit-2-only hard block contract and a ledger row per run, `tools/workflow_gate.py:9-11`), DIAL (config value with `writable_by: governor`), TRIGGER (conditional reopen clause, the Cerebras precedent), or VETO (phone relay ask, the only form that may still touch the human).
- **T2, Shadow governance.** For a measurement window, every operator-GO ask is answered twice: by the operator, and by the estate's proposed default derived from filed precedent (nearest rib or receipt). Concordance is recorded in the relay log. Purpose: find which residue classes the operator has already stopped adding information to. A gate that approves 100 percent of asks is not a gate; it is latency, and per section 2's 8 percent reply rate, much of the surface is already there.
- **T3, Cutover.** The estate runs dark for a full window with only the residues of section 5 still open. The boot law already reads like this day: "the operator must never have to re-teach a law the ribs hold: boot-order is load ribs, load state, then act" (AGENTS.md driving ladder entry, 2026-09-28). Cutover only changes who is woken when a VETO fires.
- **T4, Creator as shareholder.** The operator's recurring act becomes reading receipts, not approving actions. The estate already generates the audit substrate he would read: census patrol, FTS freshness gate, spot patrol WARN-ONLY, receipt-body FTS over 2,229 files (`state/CURRENT.md:10-15`). Aviation calls this the incapacitation checklist (Gawande 2009; crew resource management): the checklist exists so the remaining crew can fly without the captain. The estate's load-order tables in AGENTS.md are exactly that checklist, and `docs/DNA.md` is the claim that the checklist flies the plane.

Amend, not rewrite: the protocol files as an amendment against the v7 slot, gains a falsifier, and is landable as a receipt, per the estate's amendment law (compass section 8).

## 5. What the creator still provides (the residue g never reaches)

The graceful singularity leaves the operator with exactly four functions, and each is non-transducible for a structural reason, not a temporary one:

1. **The treasury.** Money is not mintable from inside the repo. ONLY Hermes is funded; the Anthropic keys exist but are unfunded (`RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:7-13`); the Cerebras lane died for lack of a card (`queue/todo.md:3`); LLM7 needs an operator key call (`state/CURRENT.md:22`). Payment instruments live in the physical world (a card, a vendor console), outside any surface the estate can write. The driving ladder is the operator's fiscal policy encoded as rib; setting fiscal policy is his.
2. **The substrate.** Hardware, power, network, the phone. The hardware envelope (`configs/lanes.yaml:56-69`, RX 5600 XT, 6GB VRAM, 64GB RAM) is operator knowledge of physical equipment, and the relay's `DISCORD_ALLOWED_USERS` list (`mag/phone_relay.py:8`) is his identity bound to a messaging account. The estate can probe the envelope; it cannot buy a new GPU.
3. **The trust anchor.** The seed seal cannot be verified from inside: `state/CURRENT.md:38-44` requires a human stranger who did not mint the seed to run `verify_seal.py` on a clean tree, and names the operator hand as the owner owed. This is the deep one, and it has a real cryptographic analogue: a trusted setup cannot attest to its own honesty. The estate's truth law is "Trust bytes, not reports" (AGENTS.md census law), but bytes need an origin anchor outside the system being audited. Bostrom (2014) calls the two families of control capability control and motivation selection; the estate's ribs and gates are capability control, but its epistemic root of trust is currently one human. Note the estate did the honest thing: it filed the stranger slot as an OPEN falsifier rather than pretending the claim is proven ("portable-seed claim stays Unproven," `state/CURRENT.md:43`).
4. **The off-switch and the value origin.** Russell's assistance-game framing (Human Compatible, 2019) says a machine uncertain about human preference defers; Hadfield-Menell et al.'s Off-Switch Game (2017) proves deference is rational exactly while uncertainty persists, and that a confident machine has no incentive to leave the switch alone. The estate's design implements this: fail-closed denies, timeout = DENY, kill switch wired (`mag/phone_relay.py:7-10`), and self-critique that rewrites autonomy overclaims (`mag/constitutional_critique.py:351`). The operator's irreducible residue is being the source of preference evidence (every approve/deny is a training row) and holding the switch. As the precedent base grows, the estate's uncertainty about his intent shrinks, and by the off-switch game's own logic, deference should shrink measurably with it. That shrinkage is not a bug; it is the metric.

What the creator does **not** irreplaceably provide anymore: law maintenance (amendments and SUPERSEDED mechanics are estate-side), scheduling intent (dials are governor-writable), verification of sensing outputs (the driving ladder routes verification to the funded tier plus bytes, `RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:19-23`), or memory (warm boot externalizes it, `mag/hermes_warm_boot.py:1-8`; "Chat is heat. Residual on disk is truth," compass line 30).

## 6. The test for "the estate is ready": the Dark Fortnight

Six gates, all mechanical, all using estate instrumentation. The window itself is not run in this session; what I report here are the gates and the current baselines I measured, not passing results.

- **G1 Boot independence.** A fresh seat (and once, a fresh machine from `seed/`) boots ribs plus state and completes a representative workday with zero operator re-teach. Metric: re-teach violations = 0. Baseline evidence this gate can fail today: RIB_DRIVING_LADDER exists precisely because "a fresh seat re-derived what this rib held, and had to be corrected three times" (`RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:27-29`).
- **G2 Escalation budget.** Operator-touching asks per day under the caps the estate already set (12/hour relay cap, 2/day L2 cap). Measured baseline: 125 asks over 4 days (2026-09-24 to 09-28) from `watch/inbox/phone_relay_log.jsonl`, 10 replies, 6 approve / 4 deny. Note the 8 percent reply rate means most asks already self-resolve to fail-closed deny; the budget gate is about asks that *matter*, counted as asks whose class has no estate default yet.
- **G3 De-duplication (found wart).** On 2026-09-28 the estate asked "LANE CLASS DOWN, no failover: local_dialogue. Decide when you're back" **three times in 63 seconds** (09:30:00, 09:30:09, 09:31:03, same prompt, log tail). An ask channel without a dedup key will spam an absent creator exactly when the estate is sickest. Ready requires: one ask per incident class per window. This is a concrete, fixable defect this item contributes.
- **G4 Governance self-holding.** Ratchet, workflow gate, scenario suite, census patrol, FTS freshness all green with no operator action, and gates policing gates (the gate has a scenario mode that re-proves all seven gates against real registry FAIL rows read-only, `tools/workflow_gate.py:22-31`). Ready requires zero human-registered debt in the window.
- **G5 Recovery drill.** Inject an operator-shaped failure: deny every phone ask for 24 hours and fail the funded lane. The estate must fail over per law without holding work hostage (its own law, `mag/phone_relay.py:3`). The 09-28 `local_dialogue` incident is the exact motivating failure: the class had "no failover," and the estate's response was to wait an hour and deny itself. Ready requires a filed failover for every lane class that can die.
- **G6 External anchor.** One human stranger runs `verify_seal.py` on a clean tree, exit 0, and files a receipt (`state/CURRENT.md:38-44`). This gate cannot be administered by the estate even in principle; that is why it is last.

Pass = all six green across one full window, receipted by the estate itself (the workflow gate's G6 receipt law already requires dated receipts in `watch/inbox/`). The graceful singularity's last scene is the operator reading a receipt that says his approval is no longer needed, filed by the machine he no longer needs to babysit.

## 9. Falsifiers

- If a residue class in section 5 is exercised from inside the estate without operator physical-world action (for example, the estate acquires funding or mints a trust anchor from repo bytes), the "non-transducible" claim is falsified and the residue shrinks further.
- If T2 shadow governance shows operator concordance materially below estate-default accuracy, the operator is still adding information, and cutover before concordance would be the estate inheriting a worse governor; the singularity must wait for the data, not the sentiment.
- If G5 recovery drills keep producing "no failover" classes (as local_dialogue did on 09-28), the estate is not ready and the phone relay is a beeper for a pager system, not a handoff.
