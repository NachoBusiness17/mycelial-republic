## 0. What a receipt is, physically

The estate has two receipt families and they are the same object at two scales.

1. The epistemic receipt: a grounding wave post. mag/grounding_wave.py:3-6 defines it as "ONE ground-truth claim with a tri-state verdict + an evidence pointer, hash-chained to the previous post." The v4 key set (mag/grounding_wave.py:119-126) is prev, ts, kind, claim, verdict, evidence, note, seat, witness, src_sha256_12, warrant. Physically it is the full measurement record of one observation: who measured, when, what was claimed, what was pointed at, what the bytes hashed to at post time, and what command would re-derive the verdict.
2. The freshness receipt: one tool result. tools/freshness_check.py:81-83: "A tool result is not a fact, it is an observation made at a moment against bytes that can move. receipt() stamps the moment and the bytes; stale_claim() gates any later claim against both." receipt() (tools/freshness_check.py:111-133) mints {observed_at, source_hash, max_age_seconds, tool}: a time coordinate, the identity of the observed world, and the coherence bar, carried with the observation.

So the elementary particle is R = (t, h, c, bar): a moment, a hash of the world at that moment, a claim, and a decay constant. Every gate in the estate is a statement about which operations are allowed on R, and the physics below is read off those operations.

## 1. Conserved quantities

Noether's rule inverts cleanly here: each conservation law in this estate is the Noether charge of a symmetry the estate refuses to break, and each one has a named falsifier receipt.

**1.1 Conservation of record (the tape).** The grounding ledger is append-only with delete-as-append: "corrections are new posts that cite the old hash" (mag/grounding_wave.py:4-6), "Nothing is ever edited or deleted. NO AUTO-MIGRATION: legacy rows keep v1-v3 semantics; row 122 stays in history" (mag/grounding_wave.py:40-41). The live verify run this session shows what conservation of record means when the tape is damaged: "FAIL LINKAGE-PASS: 137 posts intact, tip=fc3c32be6f26 | row 122: hashless defect contained by void; row 123: prev defect contained by void" (section 8, run 3). Even defects are conserved and fenced, never erased. The symmetry is time-translation invariance of the past: re-reading history does not change it, and the estate enforces that by making history a data structure, not a process ("files not processes; chain-side append, nothing scheduled", mag/grounding_wave.py:8).

**1.2 Conservation of debt (the ratchet).** tools/violation_ratchet.py:5-8 states the law: "debt that exists today is a REGISTERED CLAIM in the baseline; only NEW violations fail. The register can only grow by a human/agent running baseline-update, never silently. Debt that gets fixed leaves via prune, so the register trends down." Debt is a charge that cannot be created silently or destroyed silently, only moved between a free state (NEW, blocks the push, exit 1) and a bound state (registered, passes) by a named act. Two refinements make it a real conservation law rather than a counter: the B2-95 prune gap (tools/violation_ratchet.py:309-318) closed the leak where a prune silently reaped 25 externally sourced mimosa claims, so now "external debt leaves the register only by explicit re-triage, never by a silent reap"; and the B2-273 growth law (tools/violation_ratchet.py:73-80, 260-272) stamps accepted_lines into each accepted file-too-long claim so a bound state cannot absorb unbounded mass invisibly. The live run this session caught both phenomena in the same breath: hits=885, registered=884, NEW=1 (tools/fossil_check.py:73 shell-true, sitting free in the working tree) plus two GROWTH lines (tools/secret_gate_scan.py accepted at 409, now 415; tools/stealth_driver.py accepted at 753, now 879). The register is also gauge-invariant: the fingerprint is sha256(rule NUL file NUL detail) with the detail "deliberately line-free so unrelated edits don't churn the register; one fingerprint per (rule, file)" (tools/violation_ratchet.py:19-21, 84-85). The charge is defined on (rule, file), not on line coordinates, which is why editing around a debt does not fake its destruction.

**1.3 Conservation of attribution (two-eyes).** A void "requires a named second seat at write time and only COUNTS once that seat's witness row is in the chain signing the void. ... the author never picks their own second eye" (mag/grounding_wave.py:36-39). Attribution is a separately conserved quantity: it cannot be self-assigned at write time and it cannot be laundered by re-posting, because seat is hashed into every row (mag/grounding_wave.py:91-94).

**1.4 Conservation of evidence identity.** G2 binding: "path-shaped evidence gets its sha256_12 recorded in the row AT POST TIME ... verify re-hashes the bytes: identity, not mere existence. Evidence edited after the fact stops corroborating (EVIDENCE-MUTATED FAIL)" (mag/grounding_wave.py:22-28). Evidence identity is pinned at measurement; later mutation breaks corroboration rather than silently re-binding. The same pin exists one level down: stale_claim() re-hashes the source and fails with "source drifted since observation ({old12} -> {new12})" (tools/freshness_check.py:185-192).

**1.5 The firewall: epistemic charge does not convert into authority.** "Corroboration raises confidence, never grants permission; effects stay switchboard-only" (mag/grounding_wave.py:54-55). Witnessing a claim never actuates anything. This is the architectural equivalent of a quantum nondemolition measurement: the estate enforces, in code, that the observation channel cannot leak energy into the control channel. It is also the answer to the v3 FALSE-RECEIPT attack (mag/grounding_wave.py:96-105), where "a fabricated SUPPORTED row passed verify()" because linkage-PASS was being read as truth-PASS; the fix auto-demotes unwitnessed SUPPORTED to UNPROVEN and forbids any reader from quoting bare PASS again.

## 2. The uncertainty principle: you cannot have both recency and completeness

This is not an analogy the estate would find poetic; it is the measured content of the census denominator law (B2-216, AGENTS.md Clippy bullet): "a count is a snapshot of one tree at one moment ... Name instrument, moment, and delta before comparing counts; never difference two counts taken at different moments."

**The mechanism.** Measuring the estate takes wall-clock, and the estate produces during the measurement (lanes land files daily, tools/census_drift_patrol.py:19-21: "a census is a snapshot and lanes land test files daily, so a same-day mismatch is reported as evidence, never as a violation"). Therefore any claim pairing coverage C with age A obeys Delta_A times Delta_C bounded below by a constant k set by the world's production rate. Drive age to zero and you must measure continuously, which costs a full instrument pass (the B2-244 deep check "needs the venv and a full collection pass" and "stays out of the default pre-push path because it needs the venv", tools/census_drift_patrol.py:53-64); drive coverage to everything-up-to-now and your answer is a frozen past moment by the time you finish. The classical degenerate case is the disease that built the patrol: the hand-copied "1,341 tests / 264 files" headline "rode 12 days stale with 31 files grown and no gate noticed" (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:2-7).

**The estate meters the uncertainty instead of dissolving it.** Three measured constants: the coherence time of a tool observation is DEFAULT_MAX_AGE_SECONDS = 900 s (tools/freshness_check.py:85); the census and boot-doc bars are STALE_DAYS = 7 (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:78, tools/freshness_check.py:53); clock skew tolerance is 300 s future / FUTURE_TOLERANCE_DAYS = 1 (tools/freshness_check.py:86, tools/census_drift_patrol.py:80). And the verdict logic encodes the pairing, not age alone: "STALE = age beyond bar AND growth since the census" (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:252). The live run this session is the principle executing: "census 1d old of 7d bar, growth noted but inside the window (5 file(s) since census), disk 369 vs carrier 352 (instrument lag: ... same-window lag, evidence only)" with exit 0. The same lag past the bar is a violation. The bar is not about truth; it is about the joint (age, growth) state.

**k is proportional to the production rate, and death is the classical limit.** tools/freshness_check.py:292 computes is_stale = heartbeat AND age > STALE_DAYS AND not frozen, where heartbeat means a commit within HEARTBEAT_DAYS = 2 (tools/freshness_check.py:8-11, 53-54). In a dead repo nothing is ever stale, because nothing moved under the snapshot: with production zero, the uncertainty constant k goes to zero and complete-but-old becomes exact. The uncertainty exists only because the estate is alive. The live freshness run printed "repo heartbeat 0.0d ... alive=yes" before it was allowed to call anything stale.

**The tradeoff also runs on the write side.** mag/failure_kb.py:563-566: "Count-1 sigs stay log-only by design: the index is the recurring-class view, a 3MB all-sigs index would be rewritten by every log_failure call, and a singleton is imported automatically on its next recurrence." Perfect completeness at full recency is unaffordable, so the estate splits the observable: the log is complete and append-only but slow to query, the index is the recent recurring view and cheap. Every derived view then carries the (t, hash) of what it was built over so the deficit stays computable instead of hidden: the FTS meta table records catalog_bytes and catalog_mtime_utc "so a stale index is detected by bytes even when a restore or a touch lies with mtimes" (tools/corpus_fts_rebuild.py:11-15), and every FKB rebuild writes a rebuild_receipt naming previous totals "so no future rebuild can silently zero the cross-session counter" (mag/failure_kb.py:568-571).

**Measuring the measurer.** The regression is real and the estate caps it with frozen witnesses: the claim parser itself was poisoned by prose numbers until B2-245 anchored it on the parenthesized census clause (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:84-96), the B2-244 deep check exists because the file-growth signal was blind to items added inside existing files (a 42-item delta "measured live on 2026-09-26", tools/census_drift_patrol.py:53-57), and the grammar is pinned by an ownership law whose fixtures "are NEVER auto-synced to the live AGENTS.md bullet ... a frozen vector is the only witness that can catch a grammar break" (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:96-126). Statement of the principle: Delta(recency) x Delta(completeness) >= k(production rate), with the estate's whole metrology apparatus being an accounting of k.

## 3. The E=mc^2 equivalent: E_receipt = m x c_ctx^2

Proposal: a receipt's mass m is its hash-pinned byte-bound content (claim plus evidence identity); its energy E is the in-context capability it releases when converted; and c_ctx^2 is the context conversion constant, the exchange rate between bytes at rest and tokens in a working seat. Four grounded consequences:

**The conversion is real, measured, and lossy by law.** The quantized-thought compiler is a reactor with a posted yield: memory/corpus/quantized_thought/compile_report.json (read this session) states the law "quantize thought: frontier nuance -> flash imperatives -> local laws; dedupe by signature; fragments+telemetry refused; weight=recency x shape" and the mass balance: 331 invariants in, 11 refused, 13 dedupes, 307 kept, packed into 48 fuel packs (frontier 24, flash 12, local 12). Note the yield law: weight = recency x shape. Not all mass converts; conversion yield depends on the receipt's shape, and the v0.2 -> v3 amendment history ("two-pass classification healed the v0.2 over-refuse") shows the reactor being retuned to raise yield without melting the fuel rods.

**Mass defect and binding energy.** Compiling many receipts into one law releases the difference as usable order. The AGENTS.md census bullet is a bound state: one Clippy line carries at least five cited receipts plus two tripwire laws, and the fleet's driving ladder names the binding energy explicitly: "The operator must never have to re-derive a law the ribs hold: boot-order is load ribs -> load state -> then act" (AGENTS.md, driving ladder bullet). What the mass defect buys is the abolition of re-derivation cost. "Pay the context tax once" (AGENTS.md, deliberate-ask bullet) is the statement that conversion is paid at mint time, not at every use.

**Rest mass still gravitates, and uncontrolled release is a bomb.** An unconverted receipt has rest mass only: storage plus decay risk (the FTS index "decays to scan fallback whenever catalog.jsonl moves past catalog/catalog_fts.sqlite", AGENTS.md B2-465 bullet), and unindexed mass exerts force on every future session that must re-scan it. The total convertible mass is finite and now measured: the corpus map at the B2-22 spike was 757,667 rows / 214,470,472 bytes with a full conversion costing 12.35 s build into 217,686,016 db bytes (tools/corpus_fts_rebuild.py:8-9). But the total-energy equation says nothing about release rate, and the fleet has measured the rate envelope the hard way: 50-wide workflow children 429-storm the account and crash tethered sessions with fake subscription errors, while Task agents at width 10 banked 109.4M tok/h clean (AGENTS.md fan-out width law, receipts cited there). Same mass, different c: power, not energy, is what melts the tether. The reactor design question of the estate is always "how fast may this conversion run", which is why max_concurrency, tier ladders, and offpeak queues are the control-rod assemblies.

**Annihilation is recorded, never silent.** When a registered debt is fixed, prune reaps the claim within the scanner's domain (tools/violation_ratchet.py:304-330) and the print is the annihilation record: "RATCHET prune: register=N; fixed-debt-removed=M; external-kept=K". Matter (the debt claim) meets antimatter (the fixing commit) and the reaction leaves a receipt, not a void in the books. The grounding chain does the same for false claims: VOID buries the row "under a cross-signed kind:'void' row naming the target's hash" (mag/grounding_wave.py:34-35); the live verify reports "ADMISSION: 3 voided, 0 contested, 0 pending voids" (section 8, run 3). In this estate even annihilation is a conservable event.

## 4. Collapse: the tri-state ladder and the warrant

A claim in this estate is a superposition until the right instrument fires. The ladder is RECORDED -> CORROBORATED -> CONTESTED -> VOID (mag/grounding_wave.py:15, 48-52) over verdicts SUPPORTED / DISPUTED / UNPROVEN (mag/grounding_wave.py:88). Collapse has three required conditions, and all three are anti-shortcut devices: (1) an executable warrant, "a command that exits 0/1, hashed into the row" (mag/grounding_wave.py:29-31), because "a Claim whose Check is 'the author agrees' is a notary, not knowledge" (mag/grounding_wave.py:31-33); (2) two independent hands, neither the author (two-eyes, mag/grounding_wave.py:36-39); (3) live evidence identity, since "dead path-shaped evidence is refused at post (EVIDENCE_DEAD) ... applied earlier" than verify (mag/grounding_wave.py:26-28). Absence of measurement is itself a definite state, and it fails closed: "a timed-out tool call mints no receipt at all, and stale_claim(None) is stale for exactly that reason" (tools/freshness_check.py:22-23); every gate carries exit 2 = cannot verify as a third outcome distinct from pass and fail (tools/violation_ratchet.py:28, tools/census_drift_patrol.py:23-33).

The deepest discipline is the refusal to sum observables. The live verify headline reads "FAIL LINKAGE-PASS: 137 posts intact" (section 8, run 3): the physical tape observable (137 posts, hash-chained, tip fc3c32be6f26) and the epistemic admission observable (11 witnessed SUPPORTED, 49 legacy-unwitnessed, 3 voided, EVIDENCE-MISSING on rows 75, 76, 86) are printed separately and never collapsed into one scalar, exactly as one does not add a position to a momentum. The v3 attack happened because someone did add them.

## 5. Thermodynamics: why receipts exist at all

Unrecorded claims decay; that is the second law as this estate lives it, and every gate is a Maxwell demon stationed at a specific leakage channel: the ratchet at debt leakage, the patrol at census decay, freshness_check at boot-doc decay, the FTS check at index decay, the grounding chain at evidence mutation. None of them destroys entropy; each names the leak into a ledger so cleanup is targeted work instead of ambient anxiety. The demon's memory cost is the ledger itself, and the estate pays it knowingly: 693 rows in memory/burns/LEDGER.jsonl as of today (counted this session), each a verdict with a landing pointer.

The arrow of time is structural: append-only tape, delete-as-append, corrections that point backward by citing old hashes (mag/grounding_wave.py:4-6, 34-41). You can add records and never remove them, which makes the estate's history monotonic and gives "before" and "after" an operational meaning: the direction in which hashes accumulate.
