# deep-prompt-compiler: a Picasso-compiler for verbose prompts, and the measured proof that the estate's real prompts barely compile

Date: 2026-09-28. Lane: flash50 deep item. Every number below was computed this session from bytes on disk or from commands I ran; every citation names a path (or a fetched source). No em-dashes anywhere, per law.

## 0. What was asked and what was built

The ask: design a prompt COMPILER that takes a verbose prompt, applies reduction rules (strip redundancy, merge overlapping sections, preserve load-bearing constraints), and outputs the minimal equivalent, then test it on 3 real prompts from the repo, grounded in the State XI Picasso-reduction (the burn receipt that went from the full contract to verdict + one quote + falsifier + closed bibliography + headers).

Built: `tools/prompt_compiler.py` (new this session, stdlib-only, 315 lines, deterministic, no LLM in the loop, no subprocess). Passes, in order:

- **P0 dashnorm** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:105`): em/en-dash to " - ". The compiler itself enforces the house law, so restored pins can never carry an em-dash back into a compiled output.
- **P1 linededupe** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:110`): exact duplicate lines dropped, first occurrence kept (common subexpression elimination; dedupe key = the `signature()` 60-char normalized prefix idea already shipped in `tools/thought_quantizer.py:103-107`).
- **P2 neardedupe** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:123`): later line dies when its token-set Jaccard against any kept line is >= 0.72 (dead-code elimination). Pinned lines are exempt.
- **P3 peephole** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:143`): zero-instruction filler rewrites ("in order to" -> "to", "please ", "it is important to note that", "due to the fact that" -> "because", ...).
- **P4 sectmerge** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:155`): markdown sections merge when headers share a normalized signature OR same header level with content-token Jaccard >= 0.55; unique lines fold under the first header, the rest drop.
- **P6 evidence** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:212-250`, release builds only): forensic provenance (paths, dates, shas, counts) on NON-PIN, NON-IMPERATIVE lines is bounded to a pointer ("[evidence: N refs elided]"). This is the debug-vs-release build distinction; lossy by design and falsifiable because the elided refs resolve from the compile source (`sha_in`).
- **P5 backpatch** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:259`): every pinned constraint line (PIN_RE at `tools/prompt_compiler.py:49-55`, shaped after `LAW_SHAPE_RE` in `tools/thought_quantizer.py:48-50`: must/never/always/law/owed/falsifier/exit N/no git/verbatim/...) is verified byte-present; a lost pin is restored verbatim and logged in `restored_pins`. The compiler never papers over a violation; it reverts and confesses.
- **Gates** (`tools/prompt_compiler.py:274, 295-297, 312`): `factgate` requires every file-path and hex token in the source to survive (or be accounted for by a P6 elision); `idempotency` measures residual shrinkage of compile(compile(x)); exit 1 on factgate FAIL.

The compile report is the debug-symbol table of the build: bytes in/out, per-pass fired counts, pin manifest with preserved counts, fact-token census with lost/unaccounted lists, `sha_in`/`sha_out` (16-hex), so any output byte is traceable to source and any deletion is auditable. This is the mechanized form of the State XI receipt law: "deletions and assessment live in the annotation", and a deletion whose loss would change a decision was load-bearing (`watch/inbox/BURN_middle-path-doctrine_20260927.md:31-33`).

Every pin survived verbatim (pins_preserved = pins, restored 0). Every output has zero em-dashes (checked programmatically over all five outputs).

**The headline result is negative and it is the finding: the estate's real prompts are 99.1 to 99.9 percent irreducible under mechanical reduction rules.** The ask's premise (verbose prompts full of strippable redundancy and overlapping sections) is false for the three real prompts tested. Three independent measurements explain why:

1. **Pin density.** 3 of 9 non-blank tide lines are pinned (33 percent), 11 lines in the RIB, 6 of 12 clippy lines (50 percent, verified by re-running PIN_RE over each source). Every clippy bullet is itself a law ("Census denominator law (B2-216)", "Max-concurrency standing law") with its receipts inline. A compiler that refuses to touch pins has almost nothing left to refuse to touch. The estate does not write prompts; it writes executable law, and law does not dead-code-eliminate.
2. **Evidence interleaving.** Receipt-token census (banked instrument `flash50/_prompt_census.py`): 13.6 percent of clippy chars are path/date/sha/count tokens (49 spans), 5.2 percent for tide, 0.6 percent for RIB. But P6 fired 0 on all three, because the evidence rides on pin lines or imperative lines, which are protected by design. I proved P6 is live with a synthetic declarative-provenance input (fires, elides 4 refs, factgate PASS with the losses accounted as elisions, output line: "Background: the packs were compiled [evidence: 4 refs elided]"). The real prompts contain almost no purely declarative provenance to elide: the paths ARE the instructions ("Read watch/inbox/...", "run mag/lite_guard.check").
3. **Redundancy is thesis-level, not surface-level.** A sentence-level diagnostic over both long prompts found ZERO clippy sentence pairs at Jaccard >= 0.45 despite 88 percent of its char mass sitting in long sentences, and 3 pairs in the RIB at 0.47 to 0.57: the same thesis ("K8s is valuable because it is the substrate that makes emergent design correct") restated with different evidence attached. Thesis-DCE at that threshold would remove only 2 of 94 long sentences, 175 chars (1.4 percent of the RIB). "Same law, new receipt" is not duplication: the fresh receipt path is new information. A claim/evidence two-channel compiler can dedupe the claim and must keep both receipts, which is nearly a no-op in bytes.

## 2. Grounding in the estate's own antecedents (what I read)

- The row-level compiler already exists: `tools/thought_quantizer.py` v0.3 compiles `memory/percolator/invariants.json` into the tiered packs, refusing fragments (<25 chars) and pure telemetry while keeping imperative-shaped error-quoting laws (`classify`, `tools/thought_quantizer.py:57-79`); its live report `memory/corpus/quantized_thought/compile_report.json` reads: in 331, kept 307, dedupes 13, refused fragments 9 + telemetry 2, error-quoting laws kept 37, packs frontier 24 / flash 12 / local 12. My tool is the document-level sibling: it compiles whole prompts where the quantizer compiles law rows. The sibling flash50 item `flash50/_quant_measure.py` measures the tier chain's distortion; this item measures the compile floor of whole prompts.
- The State XI receipt is the hand-compiled specimen this tool mechanizes: the load-bearing minimum is "verdict word, one quote with path, falsifier line, closed bibliography, headers retained" (`watch/inbox/BURN_middle-path-doctrine_20260926_g.md` line 26, pre-registering a 20-fire A/B arm with rollback to a State IV floor), executed as fire 1 in `watch/inbox/BURN_middle-path-doctrine_20260927.md`. That receipt's own honest assessment names exactly the risk my report table prices: "_f was auditable because its counts pinned bytes (505 rows, 268/237, 93 percent); a State XI fire whose verdict rests on measured data loses that auditability" (`BURN_middle-path-doctrine_20260927.md:33`). So the compiler emits byte-pinning counts on every run: compile reports are how a reduced prompt stays auditable.
- The estate's one real prompt-amendment went the OTHER direction: the tide v1 baseline (2,316 chars on disk, 2,328 as I read it with trailing newline) was healed by AMENDMENT, growing to 3,101 chars (commit f737ee453 in the repo log: "live automation-b0ebbbb9 prompt amended 2316->3101 chars ... with v1 baseline stashed"). The missing constraints (empty-retry, UNAVAILABLE marker distinct from EMPTY, replace-not-stack) were load-bearing additions. Fidelity beats brevity: a prompt compiler's first duty is the pin surface, and when the pin surface is incomplete the correct compile GROWS the prompt. The compile report should carry an OWED when a pin is discovered missing; v1 of this tool records pins it finds and preserves them, it does not yet detect pin-shaped holes (that is the tide lesson and is left as the named follow-up).
- The no-import law ("Picasso law: recreated from shapes in its own hand", `corpus/steal/ZCODE_REPO_STEAL_MAP_20260924.md:42,185`) is why the compiler is stdlib-only and borrows only regex-shape ideas from the estate's own quantizer rather than importing a compression library.

## 3. The literature, verified this session

- **LLMLingua** (fetched `arxiv.org/abs/2310.05736` this ask): Huiqiang Jiang, Qianhui Wu, Chin-Yew Lin, Yuqing Yang, Lili Qiu, "LLMLingua: Compressing Prompts for Accelerated Inference of Large Language Models"; abstract claims it "allows for up to 20x compression with little performance loss" via perplexity-scored token dropping with a small LM. This is the maximal-compression pole, and the estate's law forbids its trade: perplexity scoring happily drops the paths, hashes, and exit codes that make estate receipts verifiable. My `factgate` is precisely the anti-LLMLingua: those tokens are the semantics.
- **Gisting** (verified via search this ask): Jesse Mu, Xiang Lisa Li, Noah D. Goodman, "Learning to Compress Prompts with Gist Tokens", NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2304.08467: learns to rewrite a prompt into a handful of gist tokens (below 1 token per word at 2,000x on some settings). Requires per-model training and produces an OPAQUE compressed form: no byte-verifiable mapping from output back to constraint. Incompatible with a receipt culture that resolves disputes by quoting bytes.
- **Compiler theory**: the passes are the classical ones (Aho, Sethi, Ullman, *Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools*, 1986: dead-code elimination, common subexpression elimination, peephole optimization), which are safe because "semantics" is exactly defined as observable I/O behavior. The deep point of this item: for prompts, semantics is (pinned constraints + citable fact tokens + imperatives), and outside that set semantics is NOT well-defined, so a safe compiler must refuse there. That is the same move as the quantizer's refusal classes, and the same move as the State XI falsifier: define the boundary of what you may delete, then price everything beyond it in fires.
- **Minimal description length**: the minimal equivalent prompt is the Kolmogorov-minimal form and is uncomputable; what a compiler gives is a constructive upper bound plus an audit trail. The measured floor for this estate's prompt genre is now on record: >= 99 percent of bytes are irreducible under semantics-preserving rules, because the genre is law-plus-receipts, not prose.
- External convergence already cited in-repo: NLM's structured-abstract definition ("distinct, labeled sections ... for rapid comprehension", no word minimum), fetched in the State XI fire (`BURN_middle-path-doctrine_20260927.md:13,26`): headers are the irreducible core; prose volume is free. My P4 agrees and my P2 refuses to touch headers as pins.

2. **Debug and release builds for prompts.** Debug build (estate default): evidence inline, fully citable, 0.991 to 0.999 ratio. Release build (`--release`): declarative provenance bounded to pointers, measured elidable ceiling of about 13.6 percent on clippy IF its evidence were declarative (it is not; it is pin-borne, so realized release savings on the real three: 0). The honest statement: the release pass exists, is proven on its input shape, and the real prompts contain almost none of that shape.
3. **The falsifier** (per house law, a reduced form without one is a silent skip): if any future seat, given ONLY a compiled output from this tool, fails a task that the full source prompt completed, or must reopen the elided/merged text to decide, then the deleted bytes were load-bearing: log an XI correction event against this item's report and tighten the pin regex or lower the overlap thresholds. Conversely, 20 seat-tasks completed on compiled forms with zero reopens would price the deleted surface at zero, the same A/B shape the `_g` pre-registration runs for receipt forms.
