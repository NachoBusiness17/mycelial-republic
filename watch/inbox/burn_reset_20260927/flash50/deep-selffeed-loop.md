## 0. The mechanism, as built

Not a repeat" (lines 798-804). The proposal is regex-extracted JSON (line 811, `\{.*\}` DOTALL) and appended to `state/fuel/stealth_work_queue.jsonl`. `t_work()` (line 321) later pops the first unfinished queue row, dispatches it across the free lanes under MINING_PREAMBLE (line 143) with the no-fabrication WORK_LAWS (line 128), and appends the answer back into MINED_FILE (line 480). Findings feed proposals feed findings: that is the whole loop. The docstring celebrates the growth: the queue "never drains" (line 787; the original docstring bytes also carry an em-dash in that sentence, not reproduced here).

## 1. Verdict: neither convergent nor divergent today. Degenerate. And the designed dynamics collapse toward convergence.

The question asked: convergent (findings narrow) or divergent (findings broaden)? The honest answer has three layers.

**Layer 1, as designed: divergent by intent.** Append-only queue, one new item per cycle, ids minted by the model, no capacity bound, and the integration test canonizes growth as the pass condition: tests/test_selffeed_integration.py:49-51 fails if `post_count <= pre_count`, i.e. the test asserts the queue grew and checks nothing about content, dedup, depth, or parentage. Growth is the only formal contract the loop has.

**Layer 2, as designed dynamics: the proposal operator is mode-seeking, so it converges.** The proposal is a Markov chain: next topic = LLM(window of last 4 findings). Two attractors follow from that shape. (a) Collapse: the prompt says to pick "the most interesting finding" of the fed window, which is an unreproducible argmax; models pick the salient item repeatedly, so a uniform window feeds a uniform proposal and depth-recurses one branch. (b) Escape: when the window is uniform and the model tires of it, it jumps sideways with no evidence gradient. Both moves happened, in order, on the only two successful selffeeds: m1 (selffed_ts 2026-09-28T17:06:03Z, state/fuel/stealth_work_queue.jsonl) took the fed Belady context deeper into estate forensics ("grep mag/.mimosa/hook-state/ for session autonomous-auto-fkb-183618... test the hypothesis that the Hermes autosave compactor truncates commit SHAs"); m002 (17:17:23Z) escaped to an unrelated item (Working.md corrupted by a 17x duplicated block, with a proposed collapse recipe). Branching factor observed: 2 proposals from a homogeneous Belady window, one depth move, one lateral jump. That is divergence without a reward signal, not exploration.

**Layer 3, as actually run today: degenerate.** The loop adds storage without content. The receipts: `watch/inbox/STEALTH_MINED_FINDINGS.jsonl` holds 124 rows spanning 2026-09-28T00:37:48Z to 19:34:23Z, and 111 of the 124 (89%) carry `work_id: lib-belady-1966`, the same ask re-sensed across lanes (openrouter 60, hermes 29, nous 22 rows overall). Cause: `t_work` marks items `status: "sensed"` (line 483-489) but never writes `done_ts`. The comment at lines 486-489 claims "The row flips to done only when the funded tier verifies it": no such flip exists anywhere in the driver; the only `done_ts` writers in the repo are the selfimp queue (stealth_driver.py:740, a different queue) and `mag/boot.py:183` (an unrelated sancho_boot report dict). So `_next_work()` (lines 295-298) returns the first row lacking `done_ts`, which is always `lib-belady-1966`, every cycle. The queue head is immortal: head-of-line blocking. One queue row even carries `"done_ts": null` (the only occurrence of the key in the file; zero rows have a truthy done_ts). Both selffed items (m1, m002) sit unstarted behind the immortal head. Learning per cycle is currently exactly zero marginal bits.

## 2. What prevents infinite recursion? In code: nothing. Three accidental brakes and one operator word.

## 3. The literature frame

The loop is an open-ended-generation system, and the field has already mapped its failure modes. Self-consuming generative pipelines collapse toward mode without fresh grounding: Shumailov et al., "The Curse of Recursion: Training on Generated Data Makes Models Forget" (arXiv 2305.17493; the Nature 2024 "AI models collapse" paper) and Alemohammad et al., "Self-Consuming Generative Models Go MAD" (arXiv 2307.01850, ICLR 2024) show iterative self-consumption loses the tails; Gerstgrasser et al., "Is Model Collapse Inevitable?" (arXiv 2404.01413) show the collapse is avoided by ACCUMULATING fresh, non-duplicated data with real grounding, which is precisely what this queue fails to do (it accumulates 89% one duplicated item, and its only ground-truth hook, the ponytail audit, is dormant). The over-convergence attractor is also the novelty-search literature's starting point: Lehman and Stanley, "Abandoning Objectives" (Evolutionary Computation 19(2), 2011) replaced objective argmax with novelty because objective-seeking collapses; but pure novelty diverges pointlessly, which is why Mouret and Clune's MAP-Elites (arXiv 1504.04909, 2015) fixes an archive geometry: you may only be novel inside the cells. "Branch into the adjacent unknown" is Yamauchi's frontier-based exploration (CIRA 1997): propose only at frontiers, the boundary between verified and unverified, never in the interior of an exhausted cell. The "which cell next" choice is a bandit: Auer, Cesa-Bianchi, Fischer, UCB1 (Machine Learning 47, 2002). The value signal that should steer it is learning progress, not interest: Schmidhuber's compression progress (arXiv 0812.4360, "Driven by Compression Progress", 2008; the estate already has this queued as its own investigation, `w-schmidhuber-estate` in the work queue, asking where in the percolator/quantizer/fossil pipeline the compression-progress reward should live; this section is a partial answer: it lives on the verified-yield of descendants) and Oudeyer and Kaplan's intelligent adaptive curiosity (2008). Staged evidence bars are curriculum learning (Bengio et al., ICML 2009) crossed with active learning's cheap-to-expensive query scheduling (Settles, 2009 survey). And for stopping multi-agent self-talk, CAMEL (Li et al., NeurIPS 2023) learned the same lesson this loop needs: role-play without a task-specifying, code-checked termination condition rambles; the stop must be enforced by the harness, not requested from the model.

## 4. The bounded-exploration curriculum (design)

Principle: the loop is a bandit over an implicit topic tree; make the tree explicit, stamped, and budgeted, and make verification the only thing that closes a branch. Seven mechanisms, each wired where the code already has a seam:

1. **Generation stamp + depth cap (the recursion bound).** Every queue item gains `gen: int` and `parent_id`. Human seeds are gen 0 (the `lib-*`/`w-*` rows). `t_selffeed` stamps the fed window's max gen + 1 and refuses (fail-closed, like the ratchet) to append at `gen > MAX_GEN` (6 is a defensible start); at the cap it must instead propose a gen-0 seed drawn from `t_backlog` (line 686, which already counts open rows on the B2 board and queue index). Depth becomes measurable and finite. This is the CAMEL/ToT lesson expressed as the estate's own gate shape.
2. **Dedup gate before append (the collapse prophylaxis).** The driver, not the model, owns ids: mint `m<NNN>` monotonically from WORK_STATE and ignore the model's id (the m1/m002 drift is the proof). Before appending, match the normalized ask against the existing queue and MINED_FILE via the estate's own corpus FTS5 surface (tools/corpus_fts_rebuild.py feeds corpus_search; a near-dup hit above threshold returns "skip: duplicate of <id>" and the cycle banks nothing). Gerstgrasser et al. is the receipt that accumulation-without-duplicates is what breaks the recursion crisis.
3. **Archive with capacity instead of an append-only queue (the divergence bound).** Replace the JSONL queue's implicit infinity with fixed cells: topic label from a FIXED taxonomy the proposer must choose from; the estate already has one, the TOPICS list at lines 826-839 plus the work families (drift, fanout, backlog, chain, fkb). One open slot per (cell x stage); a new proposal displaces a slot only by score. You cannot diverge outside the geometry of the archive.
4. **Learning-progress as the keep signal (the value).** Score a lineage by the verifiable yield of its descendants: ponytail audit pass rate, finish-reason health, funded-tier verify. A lineage whose last two children produced empty/OWED/unverified rows is pruned and its cell is marked exhausted. This kills the observed pathology directly: 111 re-senses of one item is a lineage earning forever while producing zero information.
5. **Wire the missing done-flip and cap re-senses (fix the two live bugs; the curriculum is unreachable without them).** (a) The funded-tier verification step must call `_mark_work(id, {"done_ts": ..., "status": "done"})`; the comment at 486-489 already promises this; (b) `_next_work` must rotate or skip rows already `sensed` more than MAX_SENSES (2) lanes instead of always returning the head (lines 295-298); (c) `_dispatch_hermes` must resolve the Hermes interpreter from HERMES_HOME or explicit config, never `sys.executable`, with a boot-lane smoke assert. Fixing (a)+(b) converts the 111 duplicates into at most 2 per item.
6. **An explicit explore/exploit scheduler (replace "the most interesting finding").** After each verified finding, code picks the next proposal's mode: with probability rho (decaying from ~0.7 to ~0.3 over cycles) EXPLORE (propose in the cell with fewest probes; UCB1 tie-break), else EXPLOIT (deepen the cell with the highest verified yield, frontier-only per Yamauchi: a cell is deepenable only while its findings are verified-but-incomplete). The prompt then names the mode and the parent row; "most interesting" is removed from the model's discretion because an unreproducible argmax cannot be audited, and this estate audits everything.
7. **Staged evidence bars (the curriculum proper).** Stage A, gen 1-2, explore-heavy: citation and definition hunts (the belady-class work), verify = source exists or honest OWED. Stage B, gen 3-4: mechanism questions on estate evidence, verify = named path:line plus a non-empty ponytail audit. Stage C, gen 5-6, exploit: interventions (selfimp-class), verify = tests green plus receipt, funded tier only, per RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928 (a free-lane dispatch is SENSED, never DONE). Each stage raises the cost and the evidence bar, so the loop graduates from findings to landed changes, and "learning per cycle" becomes operational: verified, non-duplicate claims accepted per cycle, minus duplicate re-senses. Today's loop scores zero on that metric (0 verified flips, 0/0 audits, 111 duplicates, both selffed items unstarted).

## 6. One-paragraph answer

The selffeed loop is divergent by design (append-only, "never drains", its own integration test fails unless the queue grows) and convergent by dynamics (a recency-4 window plus "the most interesting finding" is a mode-seeking Markov chain; empirically 111 of 124 mined rows are one item). Nothing in code prevents infinite recursion: depth is unmeasured, ids are model-minted and drift (m1, m002), dedup does not exist, and the verification flip that was supposed to ground each branch is a comment without code, while the ponytail audit it leans on has fired 0 times in 124 rows; today the recursion is additionally broken by head-of-line blocking (sensed items never complete) and by a hermes spawn resolution bug (15 of 16 selffeed cycles errored), so cycles add duplicate storage and no content. The bounded-exploration curriculum that maximizes learning per cycle: stamp every item with generation and parent and cap depth in code, own the ids and dedup via the estate's FTS surface before append, replace the infinite queue with a fixed-cell MAP-Elites-style archive, score lineages by verified descendant yield (Schmidhuber's compression progress made mechanical via the ponytail audit and the funded-tier flip), schedule explore/exploit by UCB over cells with proposals only at frontiers, and stage the evidence bar from citation hunts to estate interventions so the loop's terminal state is landed, verified changes rather than an ever-growing pile of near-identical findings.
