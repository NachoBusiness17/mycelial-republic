## 1. Decompose the complaint: STATE versus AUTHORITY

"I don't want to have to keep the entire estate in my head" carries two different burdens that the design must not conflate, because the estate can retire one almost completely and can only shrink and batch the other.

- **STATE**: knowing what is true. What landed, what is owed, what is stale, which law covers which act, where the receipts are.
- **AUTHORITY**: the power to make binding decisions. Spend, sign, publish, rewrite the evidence chain, promote a candidate, flip a gate from warn to block.

The estate has been at war with the STATE burden for its whole life and is close to winning it. The machinery is already there: the boot-order law (ribs then state then act, `memory/rib/RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:25-32`, invariant 3 "REMEMBER BEFORE RUNNING" exists precisely because a fresh seat re-derived what a rib held), the census-carrier laws (B2-215/216/244/246/249 in AGENTS.md: a count is a snapshot of one tree at one moment, never a constant), the receipt discipline, the FTS freshness gate (B2-465 in AGENTS.md: "routine stale-then-fresh needs no operator"). The AUTHORITY burden is the actual residue, and zero-operator mode is a design for it, not a wish that the residue would dissolve.

The estate's own native doctrine already says what zero-operator mode is: `mag/law.py:1-6` opens "the CODE-AS-LAW enforcement registry (Lessig: architecture regulates more than content)" and records the operator's own doctrine that behavior should be "enforced by STRUCTURE (rails), not by asking an LLM for direction or by narration." Zero-operator mode is the completion of that program: from laws the operator writes, to laws that run the house in his absence, to a precise statement of which powers never compile.

## 4. The design: three tiers by verifiability, reversibility, legitimacy

Classify every decision by three properties: can the correct answer be derived from bytes (verifiability), can a later session undo it (reversibility), and does the law's own source of validity require the one human with personhood (legitimacy). This is my synthesis of Parasuraman, Sheridan and Wickens' levels-of-automation model (IEEE Trans. SMC-A 30(3), 2000: automate per decision stage, information acquisition to action implementation), Snowden's Cynefin (HBR 2007: only ordered domains proceduralize; complex domains get probe-sense-respond governance; chaotic domains need direct authority), and the estate's own reserved list.

**Tier LAW (delegate to the law system): verifiable, reversible, no legitimacy dependence.** The estate's test case already exists: `mag/cost_governor.py:1-12` compiled an operator sentence ("stop ourselves from blowing 50c on a FAILED task; allow ourselves to spend a dollar on a USEFUL one") into two constants, SAFE_CEILING_USD = 0.50 and USEFUL_CEILING_USD = 1.00 (`:46-47`). That is the pattern: speech becomes ceiling.

1. **The flip law.** The single highest-leverage change. Replace every TODO-FLIP-TO-ENFORCE operator decision with a soak rule: a warn-only gate flips to enforcing after N consecutive green observations, logged per gate, with the count measured by the same gate output contract the B2-338 first-line convention already fixed (`githooks/pre-push:67-75`). This is mechanical, it is auditable, and it honors t40's original fear (lockout) by making the flip conditional on evidence instead of on nerve. The scenario-suite block (enforcing since landing, `:280-306`) is the existence proof that a gate can hold enforcing power without an operator wakeup.
2. **Incident triage laws.** Today's operator-response clauses (census STALE: "the operator refreshes this bullet's claim from a fresh pytest --collect-only -q and names the landings that moved the count," AGENTS.md B2-246; FTS exit-2-after-rebuild: "an incident (inspect the log JSON note...)", B2-465; the standing verkle backfill warn, `Q-001.md:24`: chain rows 8212 vs knot files 24686) become bounded self-heal laws with attribution requirements, in the pattern the FW-03 grounding verify already proved: classify, contain, never edit evidence in place.
3. **Task lifecycle laws.** The bounded-task pattern (`sanctioned_scheduled_tasks.yaml:54-61`, paired create/delete with "remove this row at close") becomes automatic: a task whose bounded date passes closes itself and files the row, no human habit required.
4. **Budgeted spend.** Within the standing ceilings (governor SAFE/USEFUL, the funded-tier ladder: only Hermes funded, `RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:7-13`), spending is already law-governed. Only changing the cap is a Tier C act.

**Tier QUORUM (delegate to the fleet's own adversarial process): judgment needed, authority not, reversible with a veto window.** Today these are the decisions that die as "plan-only, OWED operator GO." The estate already owns every component: the workflow gate's seven checks G1-G7 (`tools/workflow_gate.py:45-80`: per-item named actors, passoff, steering, salvage, deliverable, receipts, laws), the judge-independence stamp (B2-60, `:96-114`), and the succession contract's 48h veto window (`configs/succession_contract.yaml:62-84`). The design: a three-seat adversarial quorum (proposer, fresh-reader judge on a cross-family model, red-team) can record a PROVISIONAL-GO row in `OPERATOR_GO.jsonl` with a `proxy: true` field, executing only reversible halves of a gated decision, every such row presented for veto on return. Two hard honesty laws bind it. First, vocabulary: `Q-001.md:5` ruled "a workflow dispatch is NOT operator GO," and that ruling survives zero-operator mode: proxy GO is a different schema value, never laundered into the operator's. Second, reach: the irreversible half still queues. The chain-tip rewrite's plan can run and prove itself (Q-001's plan-only half did: 9 tests passed, point-in-time roots recorded, `Q-001.md:20-24`); the apply(execute=True) stays in HOLD.

**Tier RESERVED (genuinely needs the human).** The estate answered this itself in Article 2: spend authority, credential custody, legal personhood, the public voice, the beacon (`SUCCESSION_CONTRACT_20260912.md:13-15`). Two more come from the code: chain-tip rewrites, because the no-fabrication law depends on the evidence substrate being trusted and that trust is anchored in him (`Q-001.md:5-12`); and the meta-law layer, because the succession contract's signature line is blank (`:37`) and "Signing is the one authority that stays with the Operator, forever" (`:5`). Aim-setting belongs here too: the fleet is ask-driven, and who writes the asks is the irreplaceable function. Note the shape: for 30 days, RESERVED does not mean these decisions get made; it means the estate is designed so that NOT making them is safe (HOLD accumulates, nothing decays, nothing silently executes).

## 6. The protocol

**Phase 0 (before silence, about a week):** sign the contract and pass the drill once (Article 5: "a succession plan that has never rehearsed is prose, not plumbing," `SUCCESSION_CONTRACT_20260912.md:33`); arm dead_hand at 168h (`dead_hand.py:310` accepts 48/72/168); repair the pulse channel by fusing it with order transcription; escrow the custodian keys; land the flip law and the incident-triage laws; confirm the funded envelope (Hermes credits are the only funded tier; the width law's measured shapes keep the fleet inside the rolling-throughput limit).

**Phase 1 (days 0-7):** the LAW tier runs the house: scheduled tasks, gates, budget governors, census and FTS self-heal. Nothing here ever needed him daily; the receipts will show it.

**Phase 2 (days 7-30):** the QUORUM tier handles GO-shaped work at PROVISIONAL-GO with veto-on-return; everything irreversible accumulates in HOLD oldest-first. The dead-hand watchdog, armed, is the tripwire if even the quorum process itself goes dark (its liveness check, `dead_hand.py:57`, WATCH_EVERY_H = 1).

**Phase 3 (return):** one bounded session. The design target is not "the estate needs nothing from him." It is: the estate never requires him to HOLD STATE, only to HOLD AUTHORITY, and it presents authority as a single oldest-first queue. Memory lives in the ribs, the chain, and the receipts; power stays with the keys. That is the direct answer to his sentence: the estate gets out of his head and into the law, and what remains of him is not memory at all.

## 7. Literature grounding (named as literature)

- **Bainbridge, "Ironies of Automation" (Automatica 19(6), 1983):** automating operations leaves the human called on precisely at the moments automation cannot classify, while removing the practice that kept him able to intervene. The estate's drill (Article 5) is the standard countermeasure: keep the human rehearsed in the failure path, because a 30-day-absent operator is exactly Bainbridge's deskilled supervisor. This is the strongest argument that Phase 0's drill is not ceremony.
- **Kephart and Chess, "The Vision of Autonomic Computing" (IEEE Computer 36(1), 2003):** self-configuration, self-healing, self-optimization, self-protection over a MAPE-K loop (monitor, analyze, plan, execute over shared knowledge). The estate's scheduled-task set plus its laws is recognizably the monitor and execute halves; the flip law and triage laws proposed here are the missing plan half, which is why they are the highest-leverage gaps.
- **Parasuraman, Sheridan, Wickens (IEEE Trans. SMC-A 30(3), 2000):** automation is per-stage and per-level; the right question is never "automate or not" but "which stage of which decision." The three tiers are exactly that allocation, with the estate's reserved list fixing the ceiling.
- **Beer's Viable System Model (The Heart of Enterprise, 1979):** variety engineering. The estate already attenuates environmental variety with law (gates, ratchets, budgets); zero-operator mode extends attenuation so that only the varieties that genuinely require personhood (legal, financial, cryptographic) reach the human channel.
- **Google SRE (Beyer et al., O'Reilly 2016):** toil is manual, repetitive, automatable work that scales with service growth; error budgets convert operational judgment into arithmetic. The flip law is an error budget for gate strictness; the OWED pile is the estate's toil backlog, and the census-carrier laws are its SLOs.
- **Ostrom, Governing the Commons (1990):** already inside the code (`mag/law.py:78-87`, LAW-7/8/9). Her collective-choice principle is also the honest limit: rules about rule-making need the consent of the governed, and in this estate one of the governed holds the personhood. That is why the meta-law layer stays RESERVED no matter how good the quorum gets.
- **Two-person rule and dead man's switch (security engineering, e.g. Anderson, Security Engineering, 3rd ed.):** `scripts/dead_hand.py` is literally the latter, and the 2-of-3 witness quorum (`dead_hand.py:55-56`) is the former. Zero-operator mode does not invent new authority shapes; it wires the ones security practice already validated.
