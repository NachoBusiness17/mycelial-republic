## 2. The move: flat JSONL is already a sparse tensor in COO disguise

A row of any S1-S5 carrier is a coordinate list: field names are indices into per-producer vocabularies, values are cells, absence is structural zero. The COO sparse format (coordinate list; Bader and Kolda's tensor toolbox made it the standard exchange form) stores exactly this: (index tuple, value) triples. So tensor receipts are not a format change. They are three declarations added to what the rows already are:

1. Declared axes with declared bases. Each carrier names its modes and the allowed coordinate sets: instrument in {pytest.collect-only, pytest.run, trajectory-tape, workflow-gate, gap-scanner, ...}, moment as UTC day bucket, tree as git tip prefix, actor as seat or lane id, verb from the standing-verb census (steal, strike-the-chord, fix-debug, iterate, moonshot per the oral-01 commit 4a48cd205 in the environment git log), claim-kind, surface, width, warrant grade.
2. Relationships as structure: a receipt's position in the space IS its relationship to the estate's measurement apparatus. Receipt-to-receipt edges stay in the sibling's graph tensor; the two compose because my cells' receipt ids are their graph's nodes.
3. A query algebra over contractions (section 4) instead of one bespoke reader per surface, which is today's state: `mag/vertical_meter.py:9-14` hand-rolls the day marginal for one carrier, my probe hand-rolls three more, parse_carrier hand-rolls a prose extraction for a fourth.

The physics-of-receipts sibling's elementary particle R = (t, h, c, bar) is one cell of my field: t and h are the moment and tree coordinates, c is the value slot, bar is a validity predicate. The design is the same object seen from the side: they axiomatize the cell, I index the cells.

## 3. The dimensions (question 1)

Nine modes, each justified by an estate cost already paid. The restraint rule is the sibling's: every mode must pay for itself in a named query; a mode that never enters a contraction is a field looking for a query.

- a1 instrument. The AGENTS.md B2-216 law says name instrument, moment, and delta before comparing counts, and never difference counts from different instruments. That law exists because the format carries no instrument coordinate. Measured live (probe C1): the carrier cell reads collected=2303, files=352, moment=2026-09-27 (extracted by the patrol's own parse_carrier, `tools/census_drift_patrol.py:148-170`, from the AGENTS.md bullet), while the tape cell reads steps_total=32,870, moment=2026-09-29. Differencing those numbers is category error today and a shape error in the tensor: subtract M[i1, t1] from M[i2, t2] and the query engine refuses on the instrument coordinate before the numbers ever meet.
- a2 moment. Between my two probe runs, minutes apart, the registry went 30 -> 31 rows and the tape 32,778 -> 32,870. The B2-249 carrier shape law forces the census date into a parenthesized clause purely so the patrol can re-derive the moment coordinate by regex. In a typed tensor the moment is a coordinate, and the "claim goes stale at >7d" freshness rule (`tools/freshness_check.py` doctrine, per the physics sibling's section 0 read) is a distance query on a1-typed coordinates, not a scheduled patrol.
- a3 tree. The s2-06 commit (environment git log) records the gap census running while "tracked grew 47528->47593 mid-census from sibling lanes": the tree moved under the instrument mid-measurement, and the receipt can only say so in prose. A tree coordinate (tip prefix per cell) makes "same tree" a filter that any cross-receipt comparison must pass; s2-06's honest prose is the manually-written version of this coordinate.
- a4 actor. The registry carries name; the tape carries session_id; the index events carry writer on 45.1 percent of rows. The headless majority (3,141 events) is the strongest argument for a declared actor basis with absence as a typed NULL: today "who wrote this" is schema archaeology per event; in the tensor it is one marginal and the empty bin is visible in every query that touches it.
- a5 verb/kind. The tape's face field has exactly two values in 32,908 rows: bash (32,886) and milestone (22). The face axis is already load-bearing: it is how I isolated the milestone defect below.
- a6 claim-kind and outcome. The registry's nested checks array is a claim-kind x outcome fiber per row. ## 4. How you query (question 2)

Six query shapes, cheapest first, each one a real hand-rolled reader retired.

Q1 slice and dice: M[instrument=pytest.collect-only, day=:] is the census series; M[gate=G3, outcome=FAIL] is the steering-failure set. My probe printed both as tables; the gate x ok marginal above took 6 lines of python that exist nowhere in the estate today.

Q2 marginal (contract an axis): sum over day to get per-actor totals, over actor to get per-day totals. `mag/vertical_meter.py:36-48` is this query, hand-written for one carrier, with a wall target (100 sealed steps/day). Note the meter reads memory/runs/trajectory/steps.jsonl (`mag/vertical_meter.py:12`) while the live tape I verified is memory/trajectory/steps.jsonl: two carriers, same row schema, one meter looking at the quieter sibling. An axis-declared field makes that divergence a join query instead of a silent fork.

Q3 the comparison-as-shape-check. The deep one. M[t1] - M[t2] is legal only when the instrument and tree coordinates match; M[i1, t1] - M[i2, t2] with i1 != i2 is a broadcasting error. B2-216's "never difference two counts taken at different moments" and B2-249's grammar pin both stop being operator discipline and become type errors the query engine raises. COLLECT_DRIFT_TOLERANCE = 20 (`tools/census_drift_patrol.py:124-125`) becomes an assertion evaluated at query time between same-coordinate cells across moments. This is the single highest-leverage change: the patrol stack (B2-215, 244, 246, 249: four laws in two days) exists to guard ONE prose claim against exactly the comparisons the format cannot forbid.

Q4 diagonals and equality structure: twins. Build the artifact x artifact equality slice; the estate's twin convention (byte-identical files banked under two names) is its diagonal. Measured (probe T1): s2-06.md vs secret-gate-gaps.md identical=True, 16,725 bytes, sha256[:16]=4560df0720f8b8bb equals the commit's claim; s2-24.md vs decorator-census.md identical=True, 15,107 bytes, sha256[:16]=446f921b1a58f0d6 equals the commit's claim. The commit-message receipt (S5) survived verification against the artifacts: the rank-1 projection was faithful on every checkable coordinate. This document plus its twin is the third pair; the estate has now reinvented the diagonal three separate times because the structure was demanded by practice before anyone named it.

Q5 chain queries on the receipt graph: hand the receipt-id mode to the sibling's edge tensor and contract. Two algebraic flavors earn names: max-plus (tropical) contraction over dependency chains gives the critical path of a wave, which gates what (the drift patrol gates push, the gate gates CreateWorkflow: those are min-plus constraints in waiting; the max-plus algebra is standard for such schedulings, see the literature anchors). And k-hop reachability via adjacency powers answers "which receipts does this void contaminate," where the sibling measured depth-2 propagation flags.

Q6 the integrity marginal, discovered by this probe, not designed: contract the tape over (day, hash-clean). Measured: 822 of 32,908 rows fail the step_hash recomputation (2.5 percent), and the sanctioned verifier at its own moment agrees: 32,087/32,939 clean. The estate owns a verify CLI (`mag/trajectory_step.py:114-134`) and a falsifier triple (COVERAGE, SCHEMA, INTEGRITY, :29-32), but nothing schedules the INTEGRITY check, so a 2.5 percent rot band has been riding invisible in a git-tracked receipt of record. One marginal, run daily next to MagFtsIndexDaily, banks it forever.

## 5. Patterns that emerge (question 3)

P1 G3-steering is the estate's hard verb, and the-50 is its fossil. The gate x ok marginal fails G3 on 10 of 31 rows; the FAIL-name census shows `the-50.dwf.ts` failing five of seven gates at 05:30 and 05:32 UTC on 2026-09-26 with the IDENTICAL digest 0937eff9fdda on both rows. Same digest = unchanged script bytes re-submitted: a retry-without-amend, visible only because the digest coordinate is structural. The amend-not-rewrite law (ask's workspace context) is the fix for precisely this cell pattern, and the tensor makes it a query: two rows, same digest, both FAIL, dt <= minutes.

P2 the rot band, characterized not diagnosed: dirty rows span all 25 UTC days of the tape's life (2026-09-05 through 2026-09-29, 20 to 101 rows/day, no dead-zone), concentrate by session (top session 118 dirty rows), and the milestone face is dirty on 22 of 22 rows while bash is dirty on 800 of 32,886. The milestone producer's basis composition has diverged from make_row's recipe since birth (`mag/trajectory_step.py:61-73`); the face axis isolated it in one query. Root cause not diagnosed in this session; the finding is banked as OWED for the tape's owner.

P3 every count is a cell, and siblings prove it live: registry 30 -> 31 rows and tape 32,778 -> 32,908 -> 32,939 across my three reads in one hour, mirroring the s2-06 commit's mid-census growth note. The B2-216 moment law is not paranoia; it is the observed physics of this estate at 50-wide concurrency.

P4 headless writers are the norm, not the exception: 54.9 percent of index events carry no writer coordinate, and the fold consumer already complains (nonce-gap fold_warnings in INDEX.json). A declared actor basis converts that from archaeology to a NULL filter, and the nonce-gap warning becomes what it mathematically is: a sequence-axis discontinuity check.

P5 the rank-reduction is faithful where it is checkable: the s2-06 and s2-24 commit HEADLINEs' byte counts and sha prefixes all verified true against artifacts. The general pattern: a commit message is a hand-made rank-1 projection of the session's cell set, and the estate's habit of embedding 16-hex prefixes makes the projection verifiable coordinate by coordinate. Tensor receipts do not replace S5; they give S5 a source to be checked against.

P6 schema discipline works when declared: trajectory_step.v1's mandatory schema_version measured 100 percent compliance over 32,908 rows, while the undeclared surfaces (index events: 30-field union; REGISTRY: 6 keys but nested ragged checks) drifted. The estate's one mandated axis held; the undeclared ones roved. That asymmetry is the empirical case for axis declarations over yet another format.

## 7. Restraint, cost, falsifiers

No format migration: the rows stay; each carrier gains one axis-declaration line and, where a consumer exists, the derived sqlite view. No dense materialization, no embeddings on the permission path, no new registry beside the existing ones (the worldline registry at `mag/seed_registry.py:83` and the workflow registry stay where they are; they gain coordinates, not a parent). What tensors do not fix: a well-formed tensor of lies passes every shape check; shapes make comparisons LEGAL, grades and hashes make them TRUSTWORTHY, and `mag/bash_local.py:4` already declares the most-axed tape an untrusted sidecar. Falsifiers, banked with the design: (a) if no marginal or slice from section 4 changes a real decision within one full wave, tombstone the axis declarations and keep the patrols; (b) if the derived view's rebuild over S3 (581,477 rows) cannot stay under the FTS5 daily-writer budget, the view owes an invalidation law before it ships; (c) if declaring bases produces a fourth headless-writer population instead of eliminating the existing one, the actor axis is mis-designed and should shrink to the two writers who actually matter (seat, lane).
