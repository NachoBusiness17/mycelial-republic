The estate believes all three. It practices them artisanally.

## 5. What must not be automated

No detector mutates itself from model output without the operator flip. The t40 WARN-ONLY-first convention and the flip-to-enforce operator decision are the thymic checkpoint: where autoreactive receptors (rules that punish honest work) are deleted before they reach the blood. A self-modifying immune system without that checkpoint is the autoimmune disease with extra steps, and the estate already states the higher-order version: model text can propose; only the switchboard/effect broker authorizes effects (AGENTS.md shared-artifact boundary). The adaptive arm proposes antibodies; the operator class-switches them. The second hard limit is the falsifier discipline: every new receptor ships with its frozen vector and its falsifier (the B2-279 refold lesson: the stability pin alone passed on the mutated comparator; only the full pin set named it, pre-push:168-195), because a receptor that cannot name its own escape is a decoration.
