# w4-28: The Nuremberg funnel, measured (raw logs to context pack)

The ask names the funnel after the Nuernberger Trichter, the 16th-century woodcut of wisdom
poured into a student through a drinking horn. The myth of the funnel is that learning is a
pouring problem: push enough text through a narrow neck and the head fills. The estate's
machine looks like that funnel and is not one. This card measures the real thing stage by
stage with this session's commands, builds the loss taxonomy out of `mag/context_pack.py`,
answers the "total information loss" question (the answer is a rate, not a stock), and
proposes the optimal compression per stage. Every number below was produced in this session.

is aperture.

## 2. The inversion: loss lives at read, not at write

The single most important structural fact, visible in the code: the estate is lossless on
the write side and lossy only on the read side. Receipts, JSONL ledgers, and the verkle
chain are append-only; `memory/biography/verkle_tip.json` right now commits to 8,278
leaves under root e55ffb5a393b. Nothing upstream of the pack is destroyed by the act of
compression downstream. The classic data processing inequality (Cover and Thomas: for a
Markov chain X to Y to Z, no transform of Y recovers what X to Y dropped) does apply, but
per read: each boot pays the loss again from the intact archive, and a seat that needs a
dropped fact can re-read the source. Loss is therefore not accumulated property of the
estate. It is a per-boot rate: (bytes shed per render) times (renders per day). The funnel
metaphor fails because a funnel destroys nothing; the estate's true shape is a lossless
ledger plus a metered, re-incurred aperture. That is why the correct engineering target is
the read policy and the loss accounting, never the archive.

## 3. Taxonomy of the eight distortion operators (all in `mag/context_pack.py`)

1. Prefix truncation: `_clip` (:277-280) takes `text[:n]`. Oldest survives, newest dies.
   Chronology-inverting by construction.
2. Sandwich elision: `_head_tail` (:297-302) keeps 72% head plus a tail and admits it
   in-band: "(full-context ceiling; middle omitted)". The only operator that announces
   exactly what it dropped, where it dropped it.
3. Schema projection: `_rib_doctrine` (:305-340) keeps title line plus goal line per rib,
   capped; measured 4,235 chars for 43 ribs. Body dropped, pointer preserved.
4. Semantic quantization: the thought quantizer compiles 331 percolator fragments into
   24 frontier and 12 flash laws (flash imperatives <=220 chars by design,
   `instructions_flash.jsonl` is 4,914 bytes). Its compile_report carries counters and
   verdicts like "inflated echo - provenance only" against raw_count=232,214: the stage
   distrusts its own inputs.
5. Relevance sampling: resonance `top_cards(goal_hint, n=3)` (:649-663), proven_moves
   limit 8 (:394-406), trail 12 events / 1,600 chars (:676-682), skills_for_job 600 chars
   (:684-691), ijl beads 500 chars keyed on a soft goal (:693-707). Scored, query-aware.
6. Aggregation-to-count: `dig_edges_n`, `n_leaves`, dirac closed/total (:19-31, :711-730).
   Zero-content badges that prove the estate exists without carrying it.
7. Pointer commitment: verkle root (64 hex chars), frozen-prefix sha256 (:452-489),
   trail base_id in the worker preamble (:1317-1335). Lossless by construction: 32 bytes
   that address the whole archive. This is not compression of content, it is an address.
8. Metered shedding: the clip ladder (:1238-1278) drops whole layers in order (stage 1 L4
   heat, stage 2 compact L3, stage 3 L0d/L0f context blocks, stage 4 hard clip) and every
   stage appends a `pack_offload.v1` row via `_emit_pack_offload` (:34-49).

The ladder is a hand-derived distortion schedule, an ordered priority kernel: heat is
least valuable, then task context, then context blocks; the worker contract (frozen BUILD,
scope card), directives, compiled laws, and the standing objective are near-sacrosanct.
That ordering was not decreed, it was corrected by failure: the comment block at :1081-1090
records that quantizer landing 3eb6dc251 (2026-09-27) pushed the L0 tier past the whole
budget in build mode so the final hard clip truncated the frozen BUILD contract, "the exact
discard the clip ladder's own rule forbids," and the ladder was reordered so only
contextual history sheds (:1261-1268). This is rate-distortion optimization by feedback,
where the distortion measure is observed task failure, approximated offline.

## 4. The loss ledger, counted

`watch/inbox/PACK_OFFLOAD.jsonl`: 978 rows. Stages: 1:251, 2:251, 3:251, 4:248. Modes:
full 832, build 88, janitor 52, route 12, plan 9, audit 8. So 248 renders (one in four
overflow events) end at stage 4, the arbitrary byte cut with a 20-char reserve, and 85% of
all shedding happens in full mode, the default seat aperture. The last three rows at the
tail of the file are a live specimen: mode full, gathered 26,183 chars, shed L3 compaction,
then L0d/L0f, then hard-clipped to 4,500 (17.2% survival). This is, as far as I know, a
genuinely unusual artifact: a lossy channel that files a receipt for every loss. The
house style elsewhere (staleness debt pointers at :376-378 that hide an uncompiled law
and say so; "counts are provenance only" in the quantizer; in-band clip markers) is the
same instinct: visible loss is the opposite of hallucination, because a seat reading a
clipped pack knows the entropy is still out there.

## 5. Where the funnel actually loses (unaccounted loss, measured)

- Measurement debt: `_residual_census` (:531-568) counts only dig-flavored kinds
  ("dig_leaf", "dig", "corpus_leaf", :556-558) and cached the result in
  `memory/working/residual_census.json` as {"dig_edges_n": 0}. My kind census over
  `memory/biography/residual/` (4,003 files) found local_doc (68,051 occurrences), paper
  (20,015), skill (8,006), framework_seed (8,006), steiniger_author, framework,
  chord_commitment, author_site (4,003 each) and zero dig-flavored kinds. The badge is
  accurate about an extinct species and silent about eight living ones; every pack that
  prints dig_edges=0 over a 4,003-file residual estate is a confident wrong-shaped number.
  Aggregation-to-count without a schema check is loss wearing a true-looking face.
- Semantic-stage refusals are counters, not rows: compile_report records 2 + 9 refusals
  and 13 dedupes as integers only. Unlike the render stage, the quantization stage does
  not file per-item loss receipts. The inputs survive in invariants.json (366 rows), so
  they are re-derivable, but nothing today can answer "what did the quantizer refuse."
- Stage 4 hard clips (248 recorded) cut arbitrary bytes; the offload row records the fact
  and the char count but the cut is content-blind. Recoverable by re-render only if the
  gather is reproducible; time-dependent fields (ts, health) are not.
- The 7-day staleness gate (:369-378) hides compiled law entirely when stale. Visible
  debt, but a seat booting in that window is lawless by design; that is a scheduled total
  loss of the laws layer, re-incurred daily until recompile.

## 6. Optimal compression, stage by stage (the design ask)

- Logs to receipts: do nothing. Write-side losslessness is the estate's crown jewel; any
  summarization here converts a reversible rate into an irreversible stock. Keep the hash
  spine every receipt already carries.
- Receipts to findings: stop summarizing, start indexing. The funnel's only query-blind
  stage is batch mining into prose banks (STEALTH_MINED_FINDINGS at 0.2% of receipt bytes
  is a 99.8% pre-commitment to a guess about future questions). The B2-465 FTS5 daily
  index organ (tools/corpus_fts_rebuild.py behind MagFtsIndexDaily) already proves the
  pattern: compress at read by retrieval, not at write by summary. Extend index coverage
  to receipts; findings banks become caches of past answers, not the interface.
- Findings to laws: keep the quantizer (331 to 12 flash is the highest-quality 98% loss in
  the estate, judged by shape and dedupe, and the flash tier is deliberately tiny), but
  add a refusal ledger with row-level records, symmetric to pack_offload.v1, so the
  semantic stage's discards become queryable the way render losses already are.
- Laws to ribs: no compression. Ribs are the canonical form; the doctrine caption
  (title + goal, 98 chars per rib) is correct because ribs are pointers by design. One
  upgrade: commit each rib body's hash into its doctrine line so a caption can prove the
  body it points at is unchanged, verkle-style. Trust-by-caption should become
  trust-by-commitment.
- Ribs to pack: replace ladder stage 3's all-or-nothing context block drop with
  query-conditional selection inside the same budget. The estate already owns the two
  query-conditional compressors (resonance top_cards, mirror voice goal-token scoring,
  :193-241); the doctrine is deterministic and $0 by its own docstring, so scoring 43
  captioned ribs against the goal is cheap. Measured 4,235 chars of doctrine shedding
  wholesale in 251 recorded stage-3 events is the largest recoverable inefficiency in the
  funnel.
- The pack itself: under prefix-cache pricing the A2 frozen-prompt law (:430-438) is a
  rate-distortion inversion worth naming: the objective is not minimal context but maximal
  prefix stability, because byte-identical prefixes re-price the session to the tail. The
  optimal compressor is memoization, not minimization; a slightly larger pack that never
  changes beats a smaller one that churns.

## 7. The answer to "total information loss"

Write-side total: zero, measured (append-only ledgers, 8,278-leaf verkle chain, 26.4 MB of
30d receipts). Read-side total: about 99.99975% per full-mode boot (1 part in ~406,000),
re-incurred every boot, and almost entirely recoverable by re-read or re-derivation from
the archive the pointers commit to. The non-recoverable residue is small and specific:
any free-lane sensing output that was never byte-backed (per RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928
those are findings-only by law anyway), pre-ledger-era decay, and time-dependent render
fields that carry no estate information. The correct management variable is not a loss
budget but a loss-visibility budget: the funnel is optimal exactly insofar as every shed
byte is either re-derivable, pointed at, or receipted, and the two places that fail that
test today are the dig_edges badge (untrue-shaped) and the quantizer refusals (unreceipted).

Not run: pytest (analysis only, no code changed); live network probes; nothing else owed
by the ask. The commands cited in section 1 and 4 are the checks for this item.
