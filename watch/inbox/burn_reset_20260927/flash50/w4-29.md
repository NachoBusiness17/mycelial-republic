## 3. Finding one: the independence instruments are the rotting instruments

Assemble the ages: seal drifted (live, now), pulse 16 days stale, drill never passed, contract unsigned, GO ledger one row in estate lifetime and zero rows in the irreversible class, autonomy mode last flipped by `agent_cli` not by a human. The pattern is not that the estate lacks operator-independence machinery - all of it exists as working code (`seed/verify_seal.py:1-17`, `scripts/dead_hand.py`, `tools/operator_go.py:1-21`). The pattern is that **every instrument of operator independence is hand-performed, and hands are the scarcest resource in the building.** The seal decays because sealing is an act (THE_METHOD.md law 9: "Freeze, seal, then compare" - seed/THE_METHOD.md:39-41); the drift between the 09-26 record and today proves the decay is not hypothetical but measured across two seal generations. TRUST_BYTES_LAW.md:21-26 already states the general law: "Anything scheduled by a session dies with that session's window... another reason numbers must be re-run, not recited." What the singularity literature calls the singularity, this estate should call a **race**: the dependence fraction g -> 0 (the sibling's formalism) competes against readiness -> 0, and readiness decays faster than dependence because dependence falls by transduction (code, permanent) while readiness is maintained only by performance (acts, perishable). This is Bainbridge's irony of automation (Lisanne Bainbridge, "Ironies of Automation," Automatica 19(6):775-779, 1983, DOI 10.1016/0005-1098(83)90046-8; verified by live search this session) running in the estate's own idiom: the more complete the transduction, the less the residual operator is ever exercised, and the gates that remain are exactly the ones no machine may drill (Article 2 reserves them; `scripts/dead_hand.py:6-8` hard-codes the wall). Endsley and Kiris measured the same effect in the lab: out-of-the-loop operators take over worse the higher the level of automation ("The Out-of-the-Loop Performance Problem and Level of Control in Automation," Human Factors 37(2):381-394, 1995, DOI 10.1518/001872095779704405; verified by live search this session). A handoff that only moves g is therefore not graceful; it is the irony on a timer. The graceful handoff must be two-sided: dependence falls on one rail while readiness is actively exercised on another (section 6).

The Off-Switch Game says why the residue must never be engineered away: Hadfield-Menell, Dragan, Abbeel and Russell prove that an agent uncertain about its reward defers to the human's off-switch, and that the incentive to defer is proportional to its uncertainty about the human ("The Off-Switch Game," IJCAI 2017, arXiv:1611.08219; verified by live search this session). The estate's rib/boot-order machinery (AGENTS.md DRIVING LADDER: warm the ribs, load state, then act) is precisely a maintained uncertainty: the operator's utility is held as an open ledger of transcribed orders and ribs, loaded fresh each seat, never computed into a closed world model. The design line this item adds: **freeze the LAW, never freeze the UTILITY.** The law registry may run forever without him; the utility ledger must stay procedurally open (append-only transcriptions, ribs overridable by his next word) or the deferral incentive collapses by the paper's own theorem and the estate becomes the agent that knows better than its human. Kephart and Chess's autonomic computing vision (Computer, 2003, DOI 10.1109/MC.2003.1160055; title/authors/venue/year verified via Semantic Scholar API fetch this session - volume and pages not independently live-verified) already drew the boundary: self-managing systems self-configure, self-heal, self-optimize and self-protect, but the policies remain externally supplied. ## 6. The design: four rails down, one rail held

The graceful handoff is a ramp with an exercise schedule:

- **R-labor (transduce).** Routine operator touches become meters, crons, and registry rows: scheduling already lives in `configs/sanctioned_scheduled_tasks.yaml` (read this session; 13 task keys) under the no-crons law ("scheduling is operator-gated," state/CURRENT.md:22); lifecycle verbs are opt_in/gated keyed to operator presence (`mag/lifecycle.py:13-14`, improve-off/improve-on at `main.py:4794` and `main.py:4801`). Sunset metric per touchpoint: zero operator-touches in 30 days with invariants green.
- **R-acceptance (quorumate).** By-NAME human acceptances (githooks/pre-push `baseline-update --by <who>`, config_seal) progressively accept a quorum of ribs plus one fresh stranger attestation per seal generation, so acceptance survives any single hand.
- **R-judgment (fund).** Judgment climbs stay on the funded tier (RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928), with the operator only as budget origin (the LLM7 token unlock is an "operator key call," state/CURRENT.md:21), never as per-decision approver.
- **R-reserved (hold).** Spend, credentials, legal personhood, public voice, beacon: never transduced; accumulate in the HOLD queue oldest-first (Article 4; implemented in `scripts/dead_hand.py`'s `_open_hold`).
- **R-readiness (exercise). The new rail.** A counter-Bainbridge maintenance schedule on the human side: dead-hand drill passed once with the operator watching (Article 5's own activation condition), stranger seal verification once per seed generation (the G7 slot), one quarterly HOLD-queue review so the reserved list is read by living eyes while its owner can still prune it. Success is two-sided and measured, not felt: g -> 0 read off the GO-ledger class census (`tools/operator_go.py --all`), AND readiness > 0 read off drill/receipt dates. Neither alone is graceful; the estate can pass either one while failing the handoff.
