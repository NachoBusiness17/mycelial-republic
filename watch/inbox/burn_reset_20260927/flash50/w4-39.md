## 5. The eight pressure gradients

A gradient here is a variable whose spatial or stock difference causes flow. The estate's gradients are heterogeneous in kind, and a routing protocol that collapsed them into one priority number would lose the physics. Types: stock (level drains as it is spent), flow (replenishes or decays on a clock), field (set by configuration, not by consumption).

P1, budget (stock, osmotic). effect_broker.execute decrements one budget unit after the authority gate and before the effect can run, with the comment: "otherwise a budget existed only in prose and unlimited calls slipped through the broker" (effect_broker.py:387-402; board_write is excepted because its owner revalidates and charges its own budget, lines 392-396). gpipes refuses an entire fan when provider_budget is exhausted (gpipes.py:113-120). The remaining-budget number even rides the receipt (rec["budget_remaining"], line 398).

P2, in-flight occupancy (stock, turgor). concurrency_gate.py:1-12 grounds itself in the measured fact that z.ai limits are CONCURRENCY, not RPM: "thousands QUEUED, single-digit (often ONE) in-flight." Caps are a per-model table including deliberately honest entries: deepseek-v4-flash 3 is "a conservative local cap, NOT a verified vendor number" (lines 49-52) and max-flash-5x 50 is "the measured test width, NOT a vendor cap" (lines 53-58). Leases are per (lane, model) because sharing a slot across API keys "made Lite wait 180s while Max hung" (lines 69-76). LEASE_SECS 120 with stale-steal (line 61, _lease_ok at 79-86) is the lytic enzyme of this network: a crashed hypha's slot dissolves and the cord reopens.

P3, abundance (flow, self-feed). The burn's law 4 ("every landed lane appends 1-3 new briefs to QUEUE.md Open") makes the down channel's exhaust the next channel's substrate. In fungal terms the network deposits substrate behind it as it forages; the queue is both meal and fruiting body. ## 6. The protocol, stated as eight laws

L1. Dispatch is placement plus admission, never push: the mover checks budget (P1) and leases a slot (P2); if either is dry the molecule pools and the pipe holder blocks with a deadline, visibly (concurrency_gate.py:89-107, gpipes.py:112-120).
L2. The substrate self-feeds: landed work appends new substrate by law (burn law 4), so the network's equilibrium is growth, not drain.
L3. Walls are weather, not architecture: 1302/429 contact means HOLD and re-check on a named clock; never re-shape the network in response to weather (burn holds; AGENTS.md first-contact rule).
L4. Saturation sheds with a receipt: over-potential context emits PACK_OFFLOAD rows naming what was dropped; black-band context is refused, not squeezed (context_pack.py:34-49; organ_map.py:573-581).
L5. Upward flow carries frequency, not volume: problems ascend as hash-canonical signals that accumulate count; law-formation is a threshold on count plus survival of the false_positive filter (explore.jsonl; invariants.json).
L6. Doctrine moves as steam at any tier, bounded by reconstruct-don't-hold: a corrupted or stale steam packet can waste attention but cannot corrupt truth, because every seat re-derives from source bytes (reconstruct.json:5; rib_bus.py:163-201).
L7. Trust climbs one way: free/sensed product enters the network labeled and cannot be consumed as verified without funded-tier verification; receipts prove success, not existence (organ_map.py:531-551; stealth_driver.py:128-129 for the laws that ride every ask, confirmed by grep this session).
L8. Fruit only through the sill: work becomes estate-true when the gate row says PASS and the receipt lands in watch/inbox with a date (REGISTRY.jsonl; the gate descriptors themselves, read tonight).

This item itself is a specimen of L1/L7/L8: it entered through a gated workflow whose registry rows passed G1-G6 tonight, it carries the no-fabrication and no-em-dash laws inline in its ask (the same WORK_LAWS line the fleet's own driver stamps on every dispatch), and it banks to the shared inbox rather than to any session cache, per the shared-surface contract in AGENTS.md.
