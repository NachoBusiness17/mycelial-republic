## 1. The frame in one move

## 7. Synthesis: the estate metabolizes incompleteness, except in one organ

Assemble the taxonomy. Class S (self-count): Godel sentence found, three organs, a scheduled task, a fail-close grammar (census). Class A (authority): solved by arithmetic consumption plus receipts, with an admitted infinite regress above it. The pattern is that the estate's design response to every confirmed incompleteness is the same move, arithmetization plus an outside witness, and its "Trust bytes, not reports" law (AGENTS.md:27) is the general form: demote every sayable sentence to a report, promote only re-derivable bytes to truth. Godel's threat dissolves because nothing load-bearing is ever allowed to rest on a sentence the system can merely say. The residual risks are exactly where arithmetization has not reached yet: wiring (section 2) and spend (section 3). Both have cheap named fixes in the estate's own idiom (a liveness spot patrol over proved-but-uncalled mechanisms; a spend-to-ladder reconciliation row), and both are falsifiable claims a future lane can close by building the organ or by showing the organ already exists.
