## 2. The move

A JSONL row is already a sparse COO entry (coordinate list, per Bader and Kolda's tensor conventions): field names are indices, values are cells, absence is a structural zero. Tensor receipts are therefore not a format change. They are three declarations on top of the rows: declared axes with declared bases; relationships as coordinates in those axes rather than prose; and one small query algebra replacing the per-carrier readers. Physical layout stays append-only JSONL plus, where a consumer exists, a derived sqlite COO view rebuilt by one sanctioned writer, the exact contract `tools/receipt_body_fts.py:11-16` already runs and the B2-465 daily-writer pattern in AGENTS.md.

## 4. The query algebra (question 2): six operators, each defined and each executed

Q1 slice: fix all modes but one. Executed: burns verdict slice tops out LANDED 310, steal 200, green 21; wave kind slice claim 127 / void 5 / witness 5; the registry's nested checks fiber gives the gate x ok table: G1 31/2, G2 29/4, G3-steering 23/10, G4 28/5, G5 30/3, G6 29/4, G7 29/4 (ok/fail at this moment). Q3 contraction (join): the algebraic identity the whole design rests on is that a relational join IS a tensor contraction on the shared index: natural join of R(a,b) with S(b,c) equals einsum('ab,bc->ac'), Codd's join primitive (1970) written in Einstein notation. Two executions:

- Burns self-join on slug ordered by ts: 223 implicit chain edges over 470 slugs, chain depth histogram {0: 449, 1: 5, 2: 2, 10: 3, 18: 2, 34: 1}. The depth-34 chain is free-resource-landscape (35 rows, 2026-09-25T10:05:50Z to 2026-09-28T13:20:10Z). This is the edge-tensor sibling's "supersession depth as amendment smell" metric, measured on chains that today exist only implicitly. The 223 also independently reproduces that sibling's re-post count from a different probe.
- Registry self-join on digest: 33 rows over 23 digests, 4 digests repeated, multiplicities {2, 2, 4, 6}. The 6-fold digest d2a6783b55be was submitted under TWO names: tree-doctrine-dig.dwf.ts five times and law12-genesis-repass once (timestamps 05:30:45Z to 21:19:55Z on 09-26). And digest f72e86015d02 (game-mechanics-deep-dig) went FAIL at 22:18:50Z and PASS at 22:19:57Z on identical bytes, 67 seconds apart.

Q4 negation (querying the zeros): the operator neither sibling defines. A log represents only positives; a tensor over a DECLARED index set makes absence queryable, which is the closed-world assumption of databases (Abiteboul, Hull, Vianu 1995, stratified negation) as opposed to the open world of RDF. The estate's entire patrol stack (B2-215, B2-244, B2-246, B2-249) is closed-world probing bolted onto an open-world store, by hand, one law at a time. Three executions:

- Dangling object pointers: 3 of 693 landing coordinates resolve to no file, and the 3 split three ways, measured component by component this session. One cell concatenates five paths with " + " separators and every component exists on disk (scripts/index_fold_host.mjs, mag/shared_surface.py, both test files, the law file: artifacts real, coordinate unresolvable, zero data loss). One embeds a prose clause inside the path cell and no B2_523-named file exists under any glob: pollution and loss together. One is cleanly named and simply absent (ORGAN_CENSUS_20260927.md, zero glob matches). Negation queries need clean coordinates before they need an index; this is the write-time argument for declared bases, measured.
- Interior silence for the five largest slug streams: zero silent days in every span (the wave poured continuously 09-25 to 09-28). A negative result is still a measurement; it certifies continuity rather than finding a gap.
- Completeness of the nested checks fiber: 33/33 registry rows carry exactly 7 checks, zero incompletes. The one structure in the estate with declared per-row shape is also the only one at 100 percent presence, which is P4's case in miniature.

Q5 liveness (the admission fixed point): the non-monotonic closure the edge-tensor sibling formalizes (void-of-void reversal, view-not-mutation). I did not reimplement it; I ran the wave's own verifier, which computes the admission view: "FAIL LINKAGE-PASS: 137 posts intact, tip=fc3c32be6f26 | truth-gate: 11 witnessed SUPPORTED, 49 legacy-unwitnessed | row 122: hashless defect contained by void; row 123: prev defect contained by void; row 129: re-anchored past voided stretch; row 130: re-anchored past voided stretch; row 131: prev defect contained by void; row 132: re-anchored past voided stretch | ADMISSION: 3 voided, 0 contested, 0 corroborated, 0 pending voids, 3 propagation-flagged | EVIDENCE-MISSING row 75:swarm_20260912/smoke_driver.js, row 76:suno/flat_beat_loop.mp3, row 86:flygomoku_20260912/hermes_mirror_summary.json", EXIT=1 by design. My probe's own hash-based edge resolver adds one finding: 1 of 10 act edges is unresolved, the void posted 2026-09-27T18:33:50Z whose object coordinate is the string "line:122". Two address spaces (64-hex row hashes and line numbers) share one coordinate slot; the wave contains line-anchored defects forever rather than refusing them at the gate. A typed tensor refuses a non-hash value in a hash mode at write time; the contained-defect class it leaves behind (verify names rows 122, 123, 131 on every run) is the ongoing invoice for not doing so.

Q6 grade-gating (the filter law): every query that feeds permission must filter k >= 2, because "corroboration raises confidence, never grants permission" (`mag/grounding_wave.py:54-55`, contract :91). Everything this item measured is k=0 analytics; none of it can authorize an effect. Stated as the algebra's type discipline: analytics reads any grade, permission reads only witnessed grades, and nothing auto-upgrades a grade.

## 5. Patterns measured here that neither sibling saw

P1 the verdict basis is collapsing. 114 distinct verdict strings across 693 rows, including case-duplicate pairs (LANDED 310 and landed 19; ERRATUM 7 and erratum 3) and one verdict cell that is a 1,198-character landing report. A coordinate slot absorbing prose splits every marginal that touches it and defeats the absence query (is a verdict "LANDED"? sometimes not even its own writer knows). Fix at write time: declared basis with a closed core {LANDED, OWED, ERRATUM, CORRECTION, REFUSED, CONFIRMED, BLOCKED, PARTIAL}, long text exiled to the note mode.

P2 verdict is not a pure function of script bytes. The 67-second FAIL to PASS flip on identical digest f72e86015d02 proves the registry verdict reads workspace state the row never records (between 22:18 and 22:19 the world changed: receipts land in watch/inbox, gates that check them flip). The w4-18 field design names the tree mode (its a3) from census prose; this is the first measured instance where a missing mode makes a VERDICT misleading rather than merely incomplete. Declare the workspace coordinate or verdict analytics lie by construction.

P3 digest aliases. Six submissions, one digest, two workflow names: the identity chain (name mode) and the content chain (digest mode) disagree, and only a contraction on the content mode can see it. Whether rename or copy, the receipt should carry the edge, and today it cannot.

P4 ragged modes are the default; gates make presence. landing 693/693 (gate-fed), lane 88/693, failure_class 2/693, registry actor absent entirely, wave authority in prose. Nobody decided these densities; they are the sediment of which writers felt like writing which field. Declaring modes with presence gates is how a schema stops being a suggestion.

P5 the structured ledgers are the unindexed half of estate memory. receipt_body_fts serves prose receipts only, by its own scope law (:25-28); the effect broker answers only event_id lookups by scan (:437-453). Every operator in section 4 was run by a throwaway probe because no standing surface can run any of them. That is the niche: tensor receipts are the query layer for exactly the surfaces the FTS lawfully refuses.

## 6. Physical shape, restraint, cost

No format migration: rows stay append-only; each carrier gains one axis-declaration header (the trajectory_step.v1 discipline, whose 100 percent schema_version compliance w4-18 measured across 32,908 rows). A derived sqlite COO view per carrier, rebuilt by one sanctioned writer with the receipt_body_fts build/check freshness contract (:11-16), is the entire query substrate; a thin CLI (`python -m mag.receipt_tensor slice|marginal|join|absent ...`) retires the hand-rolled readers. All operators are O(nnz); measured nnz today: 693 (burns) + 231 (registry checks) + 10 (wave edges). Density 0.32 percent on the burns frame: the dense tensor is never materialized, COO from birth. The k and A modes ride the rows as ordinary columns; the edge mode r stays in the sibling's edge log; my registry joins its receipt-ids to that graph when it ships.
