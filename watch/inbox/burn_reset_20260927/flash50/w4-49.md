## 1. Two laws in tension, and the machine that resolves them

Korzybski (Science and Sanity, 1933): the map is not the territory; a healthy structure must preserve relations without confusing levels. Conant and Ashby (1970) proved the control-theoretic inversion: every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system. Regulation dissolves the warning. If an organ steers the territory, it IS a map, embodied, executing; there is no steering without an internal copy of what is steered.

The estate's emblem for this tension is its own water computer. The docstring opens with a denial: "Isomorphism, not identity. Engine owns tank heights + valves. Pieces (pumps) may tick. They may not pour. Bucket into OUT is a cheat, not a win" (mag/water_computer.py:3-4). And yet seed() writes the state as literal flag strings, water:source=3, water:work=0, water:out=0, valve:source_work=closed (mag/water_computer.py:57-69), and heights() reads those flags back as the entire state of the world (mag/water_computer.py:89-100). There is no water. The map fields are the territory. The docstring's "isomorphism, not identity" is itself a map claim about the model, honored by one convention: the flags are the only medium, so pouring outside the flag grammar ("bucket into OUT") is defined as cheat, not win. The estate's answer to Korzybski vs Conant-Ashby is not a metaphysical position but a medium discipline: identity is allowed exactly as far as the medium runs, and the cheat condition is named in advance.

## 3. What the pipeline forbids: unlicensed fusion

The pathologies in the estate's record are not random drift. They are all one crime: description acquiring causal force without passing through a site.

1. The hand-copied census. The "1,341 tests / 264 files" headline rode 12 days stale while tests/ grew 31 files, "because the boot freshness gate watches boot docs and no gate watched the test-count carrier" (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:2-7). A map claim was doing the work of a measurement. Nobody lied; the crime was structural, description flowing into decisions with no instrument in the loop.
2. The warm-at-boot fiction. Commit 6a6e35b59 (ladder-02, repo log) audited tonight's sessions against the doctrine that ribs are warm at boot: "30 steps.jsonl filename hits all directory listings, 12 transcript mentions all tool-result listings, zero content reads". The map described a practice; the practice never enacted it. Doctrine as aspiration is unlicensed fusion in reverse: a map that no territory matches.
3. The over-claim asymmetry. The drift core is symmetric on purpose: "an under-claim is the B2-244 disease (items grown inside existing files while the carrier froze), an over-claim is worse (tests removed or a number invented)" (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:197-216). This is the estate's theory of the two residues: honest fusion leaves lag (tolerable inside a window, "evidence only"); unlicensed fusion leaves fiction (fail, ratchet, quarantine). The 4,027-count powershell error invariant shows failure mapped to remedy, while the 13 false positives in the same file show that even the map of failures keeps a map of its own mistakes.
4. The frozen witness. The B2-274 ownership law freezes the regression vectors and forbids auto-syncing them to the live carrier, because "a frozen vector is the only witness that can catch a grammar break" (tools/census_drift_patrol.py:96-116). This is second-order cybernetics made operational (von Foerster: the observer is inside the observed): the census patrol reads the constitution that commands the census patrol, so the loop could rewrite itself, and the only thing that stops a self-model from reprogramming its own checker is a fixture outside the loop. Godel's shadow, in miniature: no map certifies its own currency; certification must come from an instrument the map cannot edit.

## 4. The residue: four leftovers of fusion

R1, the lag. No map is simultaneous with its territory. The instruments encode simultaneity as a budget: LIVE_MAX_SECONDS = 24 hours (mag/organ_map.py:42), the 7-day census bar, "same-window lag, evidence only". The live organ reading makes the lag a number: 38 organs VERIFIED, 4 LIVE, so 34 verified organs had no successful receipt inside the 24-hour liveness window at run time, and 0 GHOST because a declaration that cannot show evidence gets the ghost label and the registry "must say what evidence would make its claim true" (mag/organ_map.py:44-45). Rosen's anticipatory systems and the incompleteness theorems both predict this can never close: tests landed between the 09-27 census and my 09-28 patrol run (5 files, named in the output), so the map was already behind the territory while being verified against it. The lag is not an error to eliminate. It is the phase of the loop, and the estate's health metric is the size of the NAMED lag, never its absence.

R2, the discard. What compression sheds is recorded, not lost: 1,005 pack-offload rows naming the shed layers, 24 shed events of each kind per the variety counter, the quantizer's 13 refusals and dedupes and its healed over-refuse. And the constitution names itself after this residue: "Mag DNA -- residual constitution", commitment dna-residual-003, "Fidelity lives in files + chain, not in a process that must stay awake" (docs/DNA.md:1-4). A day is filed as residual + registry + leaf. The discard ledger is where the next surprise lives: everything the current map could not hold is precisely the territory the next map must grow into. The sibling item's census (3.8:1 map mass over machine, 17.33 GB record) reads differently from this angle: the estate over-saves because the discard, not the kept, is the risk surface.

R3, the signature. As fusion completes, verification shrinks from reading the map to checking a witness the map cannot forge: the broker receipt with pre-state, post-state and postcondition evidence (mag/effect_broker.py:406-432); the verkle commitment behind Clippy's "The rope is verkle-committed" (AGENTS.md); organ_map's refusal to let "a verified import" pass for "a connected organ" (mag/organ_map.py:643-645) and its graph note "edge liveness is unknown until a transition receipt exists" (mag/organ_map.py:725). The signature is constant-size; the territory it witnessed is gone; this is the only part of the fusion that outlives both map and territory, which is why fabrication, not failure, is the estate's unforgivable crime: a forged signature occupies a causal slot in every future boot.

R4, the counterfactual. A map that fuses with the territory makes claims about territory that does not exist yet. The estate scores them: w4-02-prediction-ledger.jsonl (same bank) holds 8 rows, each a dated prediction with speaker, decision class, confidence and evidence; row W4L-A4-LLM7 has mirror-L2 granting 0.3 confidence that the operator unlocks the LLM7 token by 2026-10-28, with a Brier calculator banked beside it (w4_02_brier_calc.py). Rosen called a system containing a predictive model of itself an anticipatory system; the residue of anticipation is the score. An unscored prediction is decoration; a scored one is the only test of whether a map was knowledge.

## 5. Synthesis: identity is an audited gradient, and the residue is the bookkeeping

Put the pieces together and the ask's two questions get precise answers. What happens when map becomes territory? It happens through licensed sites, in this order: the map is compressed (Site 1), distilled (Site 2), authorized (Site 3), sedimented into law by counted confirmation (Site 4), and held to a dated differential forever after (Site 5). Completed fusion is not a state but a closed loop with self-reference at the top: the constitution contains a sensor reading of the codebase, and a program holds the sentence to account while frozen fixtures stop the sentence from redefining the program. The estate does not believe the map is the territory. It spends instruments enforcing the fusion that the theorem (Conant-Ashby) requires of any regulator.

What is the residue? Four things, one per flow the fusion interrupts: the lag (the phase difference no instrument can close, named and budgeted), the discard (the shed layers and refused fragments, ledgered because they are the growth surface), the signature (the constant-size witness that survives both parties), and the counterfactual (the scored prediction, the map's debt to a future territory). Their common form: the residue of fusion is the record of the conversion itself. A conversion without its record is the disease the estate patrols (the 12-day headline, the warm-at-boot fiction); a conversion with its record is the whole machine. Korzybski's inequality survives, but inverted into an instrument specification: the map is not the territory, therefore every site where it becomes territory must leave a receipt, and every receipt must be younger than its liveness window. The map never becomes the territory; it becomes a territory, a thing other instruments measure, with its own drift alarms pointed back at it. That recursion, not the identity, is the estate's actual invariant: dna-residual-003, the constitution that files its own leftovers as law.
