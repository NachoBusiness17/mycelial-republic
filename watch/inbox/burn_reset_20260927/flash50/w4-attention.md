# w4-37: The Attention Economics of the Estate

Item: deep research on attention economics. Design the attention market: what determines what reaches the operator, what is the cost of each alert, how do you prevent attention bankruptcy. All code cites below were read this session; all measurements were run this session with the repo venv interpreter. Literature anchors are named from general knowledge and marked as such (see Honest limits).

Epigraph, in-tree: "Law: a pool that refills while we sleep is a pool we paid attention to and wasted." (mag/cooker.py:5)

## 0. Thesis

The estate has TWO scarce attention resources and has already built an economics for one of them. The machine half is mature: mag/decl_attention.py implements declared attention as a byte-level view protocol, where a reader declares `<focus>` / `<local>` / `<global>` visibility, skipped blocks are never read from disk, prefetch misses are counted and reported, and a mask invariant asserts "no silent serve" (decl_attention.py:613-617, miss reporting :671-673, folded from arXiv:2609.02737 and SparDA arXiv:2606.04511, both named in that file's header). The operator half has the same organs in miniature: a ranker, a daily cap, a toast channel, a feedback protocol, an operator inbox, and a digest. But the operator half is a market with no trades. Every price-discovery instrument was built in a ten-day window in August 2026 and every one went dark within ten days of being built, while the only attention writer that survived is the one that bypasses every meter. Attention bankruptcy already happened once, on 2026-08-07, and the recorded response was to raise the quota. This paper is the autopsy, a pricing model for a single alert, and a seven-law market design built entirely on bones the estate already owns.

## 3. What determines what reaches the operator today (five routing laws, descriptive)

L1. Hard bands override everything. Repository blockers, unread worker failures, and unread operator guidance are stamped `hard_band` at emission (attention_ranker.py:133, :149, :173, :191, :203, :219) and feedback cannot demote them (:234-239, :250). This is the market's inalienable baseline: safety claims cannot be priced down.

L2. Everything else is a fixed-weight linear utility (:89-94) whose only adaptive term, feedback, has never received a cast (Receipt 1). Today the ranker is a deterministic prior with no learning, running exactly as it did on 08-06.

L3. Emission is two-tier by accident of code order: the file write happens before the cap check (act.py:96-118), so over-cap alerts are still FILED (bounded, pull-readable) and only the PUSH (toast) is suppressed. This is a good accident; the design below makes it a contract.

L4. The expensive human lane is rationed by tags and budget: `[priority]`/`[L2]` markers (lanes.yaml:76-80), 2 specialist escalations per day (:72), blocked escalations degrade to filed attention notes (act.py:144-155).

L5. Heal-first escalation: routine decay alerts nobody. The FTS index daily task rebuilds through the sanctioned writer and "alerts to memory/attention.md only when exit 2 survives the rebuild attempt" (AGENTS.md, B2-465 clause); the census patrol is WARN-ONLY at pre-push with flip-to-enforce named as an explicit operator decision (B2-215), and its cannot-verify exit (return-2 paths, tools/census_drift_patrol.py:304-352) is itself treated as an incident (B2-246). Alert = healing failed, not state drifted.

The structural observation: all five laws are SUPPLY-side. Nothing anywhere measures what an alert cost the operator or what it returned. The market has production rules but no prices, no settlement, and no ledger - because the ledger instruments exist and are dark (Section 2).

## 4. The cost of an alert (the pricing model)

Define the total price of an alert as five terms:

**C_render** - bytes and tokens to compose and file. In this estate: one prepend into a bounded file. Measured scale: the whole attention.md is 9,681 bytes. Trivial; correctly ignored.

**C_context** - the tokens it occupies downstream, in every seat pack and chronicle that carries it. Bounded by design: 600-char clip into context packs (context_pack.py:620), 400-char tail (:933), 40-50KB file caps (biography.py:526, act.py:97). This is a bounded, amortized cost - the estate already taxes it correctly. Note the tax is paid by SEATS, not by the operator: every pack built since the wallpaper accumulated has spent its 600 attention chars on a template block.

**C_interrupt** - the only term paid in the truly scarce currency. A push (toast, flag) interrupts a human. The interruption literature (Gloria Mark and colleagues, UC Irvine, CHI 2005 "No task left behind?" and CHI 2008 "The Cost of Interrupted Work"; also Parnin and Rugaber on resumption in programming, 2011) places resumption lags on the order of tens of minutes and documents stress carryover. In fleet terms C_interrupt is the operator's funded-tier judgment time - the driving-ladder law (2026-09-28) makes explicit that only judgment climbs are worth the human tier at all, so an interrupt that does not carry a judgment-sized decision is pure waste.

**C_trust** - cry-wolf depreciation, the dominant and unmeasured term. Signal detection theory (Green and Swets, 1966) models the operator as holding a criterion: each false alarm rationally shifts the criterion toward ignoring the channel, which raises the miss rate for real P0s. The clinical endgame is documented: alarm fatigue was ranked the number one health technology hazard by ECRI Institute in the early 2010s, and the Joint Commission's Sentinel Event Alert 50 (2013) mandated hospital-wide alarm management after operators had learned to silence alarms wholesale. The in-estate analog is already visible: an operator surface carrying 30 identical blocks with 1 signal line (Receipt 4) is a channel no rational operator reads, and the ranker's `wallpaper` signal - the only hook that prices C_trust (attention_ranker.py:86, :91, :241) - has never been cast once. The estate's asymmetric adjustment (+4 useful / -8 wallpaper, :241) already encodes the right SDT direction (a false alarm poisons the channel for real alarms more than a hit helps it), at an implicit 2:1 ratio. It has simply never settled a single trade.

**C_rework** - the opportunity cost of misallocated attention. In this estate the sharpest form is measured, not hypothetical: five operator corrections paid in full on 09-28 (ladder-02 CORRECTION card) returned zero capitalization, because the minting path that converts attention into law ran only for machine triggers. An alert that arrives and changes nothing durable is an alert the estate pays for again tomorrow - that repeat payment is the rework term.

So the true unit of account is operator-seconds of funded judgment, and an alert is worth emitting only if expected judgment saved exceeds the sum. The estate's own escalation gate already applies exactly this arithmetic to TOKENS: "Multi-lane fan-outs must clear premium x baseline value per 1k tokens or the value-per-token admission gate rejects the spend" (configs/lanes.yaml:101-108, the effort_tiers block). The insight of this paper: that admission gate exists for tokens and is absent for attention. Build the same gate for the scarcer resource.

## 5. Four failure theorems (why the market failed, stated generally)

**T1 - Gresham's law of attention: unmetered emission drives out metered emission.** The metered writer went silent on 08-07 and the unmetered writer is the sole survivor with a measured 30/31 wallpaper share. Wallpaper is free to emit; priced pings are not free (they consume the cap and eventually the operator's trust). Any shared attention surface with heterogeneous emission costs converges to its cheapest emitter. The fix is not willpower; it is emission parity (Law A3).

**T2 - Contract drift kills price discovery before disuse does.** The feedback loop did not fail only because nobody used it: had anyone used the flagship pipe, the two vocabularies (good/bad at feedback.py:49-58 vs useful/wallpaper at attention_ranker.py:17) would have silently rejected every cast while reporting success. Any market whose settlement layer has a latent enum mismatch is a market where absence of trades is uninformative - you cannot tell apathy from broken rails. The estate's own law applies: instrument drift must be exit-code-visible, never boolean-cosmetic.

**T3 - Attention bankruptcy is a queueing collapse, not a volume problem.** Kingman's formula (1961): mean wait grows roughly as rho/(1-rho); as utilization approaches 1, latency explodes nonlinearly. Operator attention is a single-server queue with fixed capacity. Raising `max_attention_per_day` 8 -> 48 (configs/mag.yaml:5) raised offered load, not capacity - the server stayed one human. The 2026-08-07 response was the textbook wrong move, preserved in a config comment like a fossil in amber. Only two levers move rho: cut emissions (admission control) and consolidate (batching). The design below legalizes both and outlaws the cap-raise.

**T4 - Inverted minting: the market learns from its cheapest signals and evaporates its most expensive ones.** Ladder-02 (commit 6a6e35b59) measured it: gopher triggers mint ribs same-day (c1c0136cd), operator corrections mint nothing (CORRECTION card: 5 corrections, 0 laws, while 3 of 9 cards were outright IGNORED). A learning system that capitalizes machine noise at a higher rate than human signal is running its memory backward; the rib corpus compounds in the wrong direction. The DELIBERATE-ASK doctrine "pay the context tax once" is the estate's amortization law for context - it needs a symmetric law for attention: a signal the operator paid real attention to must amortize, or the payment was burned.

## 6. The attention market (seven laws, all on existing bones)

**A1 - Priced two-tier emission.** Formalize what act.py already does by code order (Section 3, L3): every alert is FILED unconditionally into its bounded surface, and PUSHING (toast, flag, dashboard band) spends from a daily OPERATOR-SECONDS budget. Replace the raw count cap (mag.yaml:5) with a per-class seconds budget, and repair the counter the same day (Receipt 2 shows it frozen since 08-07). The knob already exists; only the unit and the enforcement are wrong. Over-budget pushes return `suppressed: true` exactly as act.py:113-118 does today.

**A2 - Unified price discovery.** One vocabulary everywhere: feedback.py:56-58 normalizes `good`->`useful`, `bad`->`wallpaper` before calling record_feedback (five-line fix; the mismatch is Receipt 6). Wire the dashboard buttons (rest.py:871 already exposes the endpoint). Make feedback casts a standing ritual: every session close casts at least three signals on what it actually read - the estate already mandates "contribute a self-improvement from every wave" (AGENTS.md max-concurrency law); casts are the attention-denominated version. Unpriced alerts default to wallpaper weight, so silence never looks like approval.

**A3 - Emission parity (the Gresham fix).** Any writer to an operator attention surface must register a ranker-addressable item id, so it can be priced, pinned, muted, or demoted (the id plumbing exists: `record_feedback(item_id, ...)` at attention_ranker.py:63). biography.py:509-526 is the named bug: its blocks carry session ids that would price perfectly. Either route the biographer ping through the metered path or make each emitted block a digestible ranker item. Unregistered writers are treated as the defect class they measurably are.

**A4 - Compile recurring alerts into gates.** An alert class that fires three times with the same remedy must submit a gate, not a fourth alert: the ratchet pattern (violation_ratchet at pre-push, workflow_gate scenarios, AGENTS.md build discipline) is the estate's own proof that a repeated attention payment can be amortized into a mechanical check. Alerts are for NOVEL failures; gates are for KNOWN ones. The FTS daily task is the template: heal automatically, escalate only the residue (B2-465). This is "pay the context tax once" applied to alerts: pay attention once, then make the estate remember so no one pays again.

**A5 - Bankruptcy tripwires, all measurable with instruments the estate already has.**
   (i) SNR tripwire: template fraction of attention.md headings; measured today at 30/31. Alarm above 50 percent; auto-digest below it. The measurement is one regex (the one run in Receipt 4).
   (ii) Wallpaper rate per class > 0.5 over >= 5 casts (via the repaired A2 ledger) -> automatic demotion of that class to digest-only. The ranker already implements the mechanism (:234-243); only the ledger was missing.
   (iii) Correction half-life: hours from an operator correction to a minted law or AGENTS.md line. Ladder-02 measured infinity on 09-28. Alarm when the half-life exceeds 24 hours - this tripwire guards T4, the worst failure mode, because it is the only one that corrupts the estate's memory rather than merely wasting a day.
   (iv) Queue laws: operator_inbox pending (cap 50, operator_inbox.py:131) and daily push count held over budget for two consecutive days is declared a utilization crisis; the lawful response is cutting emissions at the source class, never raising the cap (08-07 lesson codified, T3).

**A6 - Digest rationing as the default surface.** The press digest already rations the operator to ONE pressing matter per ~900-tick cadence and it is alive (Section 1; PRESS_DIGEST.md dated this week). Generalize: the operator's pull surface shows one next-thing plus hard bands; everything else competes inside the digest. The ladder-02 scorecard grammar (FIRED / PARTIAL / IGNORED, commit 6a6e35b59) is the right row format for compliance-class rollups - it was good enough to expose the estate's own ignored laws; use it for every recurring attention class.

**A7 - Never-silent suppression.** Adopt the machine half's honesty law for the operator half: decl_attention.py counts and reports every miss and asserts the mask invariant (:613-617). Symmetrically, every suppressed push (act.py's `suppressed: true`) must surface in a counted misses rollup, so suppression is a visible inventory the operator can audit, not a silent drop. The FW-46 comment at act.py:246 ("silent loss masquerading as health") is the same law already written into this file's own history.

## 7. What the market buys

Stated as direction, not as a measured result (nothing here was run end to end): under A1-A7 the operator's push channel carries only hard bands and the daily digest; wallpaper classes pay their way down to digest-only via the repaired ledger; operator corrections amortize into ribs on a measured clock instead of evaporating; and the estate's memory compounds its most expensive signals instead of its cheapest. The scarce human stays on P0s and judgment - which is, by the driving-ladder law, the only thing the human tier is for.
