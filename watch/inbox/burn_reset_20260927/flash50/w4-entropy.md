## 0. Thesis

The estate already runs an entropy economy. It prices the disorder of an incoming signal (`_signal_entropy`, mag/fuel_feed.py:153-167), buys ordered inference at lane-specific prices (`LANE_MAX_TOKENS`, mag/fuel_feed.py:134-135), refines raw failure mass into law through a two-stage compression cascade (percolator then quantizer), and refuses to let disorder grow silently (violation ratchet, tools/violation_ratchet.py:1-28). What it lacks is one balance sheet that says, per day: how much order exists, how much was bought, what it cost, and what decayed. This item designs that sheet. The governing identity: the total order the estate can afford is its free-energy flux divided by the marginal cost of one unit of durable order. Both terms are measurable today from organs that already exist. The deep claim I will defend: the estate is a dissipative structure in Prigogine's exact sense, but of a peculiar assisted subtype, because its self-organizers themselves need organizers, and today nobody organizes them.

Four organs, one quantity. The estate's engineers keep rediscovering entropy because entropy is the correct name for what they keep fighting: unpriced difficulty.

## 5. Is the estate a dissipative structure?

Prigogine's dissipative structure (Nobel 1977): order maintained far from thermodynamic equilibrium by continuous throughput, with entropy exported to the environment. Clause by clause against the evidence above:

- **Far from equilibrium**: yes, and provably so, because the estate has names for equilibrium. "Stale-trap disease" is the AGENTS.md name for the equilibrium where documents stop being refreshed; the 7-day BOOT_4_7 staleness gate, the B2-215 census-carrier tripwire, and the B2-465 FTS decay are all formal statements that every order-state relaxes toward disorder on a timescale of days. `LOSING_STREAK = 3` (mag/fuel_feed.py:125) and the lane cadence cooldown (:209-217) say the same about lanes: lane reliability is also a decaying state variable.
- **Maintained by throughput**: yes. The stock exists only because flux crosses it daily: 693 burn-ledger rows, 33,490 trajectory steps, sizing rows timestamped today at 17:43, 22:18 and 00:32 (memory/learn/sizing.jsonl, read this session), the 09:00 FTS pump, the 17:20 patrol. Cut the flux and the decay laws win on their stated timescales; nothing in the estate is self-maintaining.
- **Exports entropy**: yes, to four sinks: disk bytes (6.4GB inbox, 1.29GB ESTATE_INDEX, append-only), provider rate-limit buckets (429/1302 storms), the operator attention queue (ALERT appends, L2 escalations), and literal waste heat in the host silicon running the gemma janitors.
- **Throughput has a measured convection window.** A Benard cell convects only in a band of Rayleigh number: below it, no circulation; above it, turbulence. The estate measured its own band: width 3 through 10 clean, 109.4M tok/h at 10, 12 sustained, 15-16 untested, burst contact produces 1302 turbulence (AGENTS.md width law). The dispatcher refill "every 20 min, holds one fire after any 3-lane rate-limit death cluster" (BURN_WAVE_STATE.json laws) is the heating element with a thermostat. The burn wave is the estate's convection cell, and the width law is its measured critical Rayleigh number.

Verdict: yes, a dissipative structure, with one honest disanalogy. A Benard cell's order is automatic given the gradient; the estate's order requires demons (seats, gates, patrols) actively sorting, and the demons' memory is itself append-only and unaudited. The estate does not self-organize its self-organizers. Bataille's accursed share (1949) names the surplus that must be burned: the burn waves are exactly that, deliberate expenditure of surplus quota to keep the structure far from equilibrium. Schrodinger's 1944 "negentropy" diet is the percolator's job description. Friston's free-energy principle is the same claim at the organism scale: minimize surprise, which is what the invariant distillation does when it converts 290,507 surprises into 366 expectations.

## 6. The total order the estate can afford (the design)

**The identity.** Affordable order per day = flux (tokens of bought inference + free-lane throughput + local janitor cycles) divided by c_order (marginal cost of one unit of durable order). Both are already instrumented. Flux: the width-law receipts give a measured ceiling of 109.4M tok/h sustained at width 10, the DRIVING LADDER (AGENTS.md) arms $180 of funded Nous workhorse tier and declares everything else sensing-only, and the local janitors are thermal-limited, not dollar-limited. c_order: the estate's own organs quote the price per unit: a hard signal costs up to 5000 tokens on a strong lane versus 600 on groq (mag/fuel_feed.py:127-135); one_sizer rows (memory/learn/sizing.jsonl) record the route actually bought per ask; one law costs roughly 794 sightings plus one quantizer compile; one receipt costs a markdown file and a ledger row.

**The three-ledger balance sheet.** The estate already writes every entry; nothing folds them. Design: one daily EQUILIBRIUM row with three sections. STOCK: today's census numbers, each stamped with instrument and moment (B2-216 is already the law that makes this honest). FLUX: token meters per lane class from the one_sizer and lane profile files, plus cron wall-times (the FTS pump's 0.637s is a metabolic cost; print it). DEBT: the ratchet's 941 claims with their named owners and expiry dates (tools/violation_ratchet.py:70-71 already gives accepted entries an "expires" field that rots into violation, so debt in this estate genuinely decays into visible disorder, the correct thermodynamics), plus the 13 recorded false positives, plus open ALERTs. Difference two consecutive days and you finally have dS, the estate's actual entropy derivative, instead of two incomparable snapshots.

**The unaffordable orders, named and priced.** (a) Unbounded append-only stock: 6.4GB inbox and 3.5M ESTATE_INDEX rows with no compaction organ. The law to add: receipts are the negentropy, raw rows are the heat; after a receipt lands, the substrate it certifies becomes compactable (the estate already demonstrates the right ratio: 581,477 catalog rows certify into 2,880 receipts, about 200:1). (b) Unminted corrections: ladder-02's five-for-zero day is the worst exchange rate in the estate; the mint (an AGENTS.md line plus commit, c1c0136cd proves the mill works on gopher triggers) must run on operator corrections at the same priority as receipts. (c) Zombie surfaces: every polled-404 and orphaned bundle is a standing read-path tax; the f-19 census is the audit, deletion is the pump.

**Why this is affordable.** The marginal cost of the whole design is one daily local script folding files that already exist, plus one compaction pass behind an existing receipt gate, plus a minting habit. It requires zero new funded inference. The estate's cheapest pumps are all sub-token: the ratchet is AST scanning, the patrol is globbing, the FTS pump is 0.637 seconds. The entropy budget's punchline is that the estate's scarcity is not compute, it is folding: the information to know its own state exists in a dozen ledgers, and no single page adds them.
