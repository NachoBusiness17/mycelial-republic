## 1. The gap is a number, and the estate already owns the mathematics

The epistemic gap is the divergence between the estate's internal model of itself and external reality: what `state/CURRENT.md`, the briefs, the verkle chain, AGENTS.md census bullets, and every RIB claim about the world versus what the world would report if asked directly. The triangulation document states the correct frame at line 71: the failure is "a system that measures its own rate of change and calls that measurement orientation, while the truth instrument that would tell it whether the movement is in the right direction sits unused." Line 87 names it: metric-referent decoupling. Line 167 delivers the constitutional claim: "no system can measure that space from inside the system without first building the instrument that lives there."

`tools/drift.py` is that instrument, built. Its core is `drift_check(metric, referent)` (drift.py:50), which computes the Pearson correlation of the two series' deltas, corr(delta-M, delta-R) (drift.py:8-10, 36-47, 60-68), and returns one of six verdicts. Its law line (drift.py:90-91): "a rate metric answers 'is it moving'; only corr(delta-M, delta-R) answers 'is it moving north'." So the gap sensor asked for here is not a new mathematics. It is the generalization of drift.py from six hand-registered teleological pairs to the whole estate, with the metric side being the internal model's claim series and the referent side being reality measured by instruments the model does not write to.

## 2. What the chaos plate contributes

`CHAOS_THEORY_mathemetica.png` is a three-panel plate: butterfly effect (two trajectories from nearly identical initial conditions separating into blue and red), the Lorenz attractor, the Rossler attractor, with the Lorenz system printed underneath: dx/dt = -sigma(x-y), dy/dt = r*x - y - x*z, dz/dt = -beta*z + x*y. Read as a specification for the gap sensor, it makes four contributions.

**First: divergence, not error, is the expected behavior.** The butterfly panel shows two nearly identical trajectories separating. The estate's internal model and the world are exactly such a pair: the model was true when written (the census line, the DNA claim, the brief), and separation afterward is not a bug but the system's normal dynamics under different update rules. The model updates only when someone rewrites it; the world updates continuously. This inverts the naive design. A gap sensor that alarms on "gap > 0" would alarm always. The correct quantity is the divergence rate: how fast does a claim's truth halve? This points at a Lorenz-style exponent for the fleet, developed in section 6.

**Second: two regimes of being wrong.** The Lorenz attractor supports a distinction the estate's incident history already demonstrates. A trajectory can be wrong about the attractor (the manifold: the shape of what is possible) or wrong about the phase (where on the manifold the system currently is). Estate examples of manifold error: s2-13 found DNA.md asserts a `session_end_hook` symbol that "exists nowhere except DNA.md:76 itself" (commit 99281683e), a mechanism in the model that is not in the world at all. Estate examples of phase error: the census drift class, where the test suite exists but its count is wrong (B2-215: the retired "1,341 tests / 264 files" headline was hand-copied, never byte-backed, and rode 12 days stale while 31 files grew; B2-216: census 1,837 at 09:49 EDT versus 1,854 by 10:04 the same morning). The sensor must route these differently: manifold contradiction means delete or repair the claim (the model is asserting fiction); phase drift means re-measure and refresh (the model is stale, not false).

**Third: simple rules, emergent divergence.** The Lorenz equations are three lines and fully deterministic, yet unpredictable. Likewise every gap incident in the estate's ledger has a trivial local rule: a doc not refreshed, a hand-copied number, two lanes writing one file (s2-11: concurrent seat rewrites of `memory/context_pack_latest.json`, last-writer-wins, owning process "not attributable from disk"). The design consequence is that the sensor should not try to be a smarter model. It should be continuous re-observation: the innovation term of a filter, predicted minus observed, taken fresh at every sample. Re-observation is cheap (a command); model improvement is expensive (a rewrite); the chaos plate says the second never rescues you from skipping the first.

**Fourth: Rossler's lesson, no global scalar.** Two different strange attractors on one plate: different systems have different divergence dynamics. The estate's claim classes likewise. A single fleet-wide "gap score" would average a daily-decaying census number with a months-stable constitution claim and destroy both signals. drift.py's per-pair design (one verdict per (metric, referent) pair) is therefore correct and must be preserved in the generalization. Ashby's requisite variety says the same thing: the regulator's variety must match the perturbation spectrum; one scalar cannot carry the variety of a 3,563,077-file estate (f-22's walk count, commit e79f36eab).

Zero INVERTED in 18 stamps is itself data: either the fleet never optimizes a counter-productive metric (unlikely, see Goodhart in section 7), or INVERTED requires the referent to be measured at all, and most referents are not, which lands the pair in VACUOUS-or-never-registered instead. The deepest gap is the unregistered pair.

**The verdict paths.** I executed drift_check live. INVERTED fires: corr -1.0, note "stop trusting the metric" (drift.py:80-82). ALIGNED fires: corr 1.0. DRIFTING fires: 0.3852. VACUOUS fires on a frozen referent. And the exercise exposed a real instrument defect: linear series (1,2,3,4,5,6 against 6,5,4,3,2,1) return DEGENERATE, not INVERTED, because linear motion has constant deltas, zero variance, and an undefined Pearson (drift.py:44-46, 74-76). The instrument is blind to constant-velocity divergence, which is exactly the first, longest phase of a butterfly divergence, and exactly the shape of "the model drifted N counts per day" gaps B2-216 documents. Any generalized sensor must pre-whiten: difference out any fitted constant-velocity term before verdicting, or linear divergence regimes will hide in DEGENERATE forever.

## 4. The design: what the gap sensor compares

Three registers, all writing one append-only ledger, all verdicted by drift_check where a series exists.

**Register A: claim versus byte (existence and shape).** Every durable factual claim the estate makes about itself is a row: {claim_id, claim_text, claim_file:line, instrument_cmd, world_value, ts}. The claim side comes from the model surfaces (AGENTS.md census bullet, DNA.md, RIBs, briefs). The world side must come from an instrument the model cannot write to: `pytest --collect-only -q` for counts, `git ls-files` and `Test-Path` for existence, `rg -c` for symbol counts, `curl -I` for live URLs (AGENTS.md already carries this law for Vercel URLs: "verify with `curl -I` before reporting live"). Filed precedents: the s2-13 fact-check of 35 DNA.md claims (21 verified, 8 partial, 2 contradicted, 2 not found, plus a file truncated mid-word at L147 committed in HEAD) and oral-02's posture rot (configs/mag-secret.yaml absent from the live tree, 60 of 63 cited modules exist). These were one-shot audits. The sensor makes them a standing sampler with a claim registry.

**Register B: rate versus referent.** The existing T1-T6 pair table (drift_pairs_stamp.py:8-40) plus new model-world pairs: AGENTS.md's collected-count series versus actual collect output; brief-claimed service state versus probe results; claimed FTS freshness (B2-465's decay-to-scan-fallback surface) versus `corpus_fts_rebuild.py --check` exit codes. Weekly idempotent stamps on the (ISO week, pair) key (drift_pairs_stamp.py:45-49), referent-age guard intact: ALIGNED on a referent younger than 7 days downgrades to FRAGILE-ALIGNED (drift_pairs_stamp.py:69-75), rendered amber-green, never red (B2-218, drift_dashboard.py:203-209).

**Register C: prediction versus outcome.** The internal model is predictive, not just descriptive: boot timings, envelope claims ("width 10 clean"), URL liveness, task schedules. Each prediction is filed with an expiry timestamp; the sensor grades it at expiry and the graded series feeds drift_check against whatever referent the prediction named. This is Dawid's prequential idea (section 7) applied to the estate's own forecast stream, and it reuses the trust-bytes law the census bullets already embody.

## 5. Cadence: matched to divergence time, not to convenience

B2-216 supplies the key measurement: a count can rot by 17 in 15 minutes during a burn wave, while a constitution claim can hold for months. Uniform cadence is therefore wrong on both ends. The sampler takes a per-class cadence equal to the class's observed divergence time (the time for a fresh claim to cross the phase-error threshold):

- Census-class counts (tests, files, modules): daily, boot-anchored. This exists: MagCensusPatrolDaily at 08:30 with `--collect` deep check, exit 1 on drift of 20 or more (AGENTS.md B2-244/B2-246). The sensor adopts it rather than duplicating it.
- Doc-claims (DNA.md, RIBs, registry): weekly rotating sample, N claims per week, full re-audit on any incident or on release. The s2-13 audit becomes the prototype of one rotation step.
- Rate pairs: weekly idempotent stamps (existing, drift_pairs_stamp.py).
- Live-world probes (URLs, ports, services): at reliance time, immediately before the claim is used, not on a schedule. A liveness claim sampled daily and used at hour 23 is still a gap at the moment of use.
- Predictions: at expiry, no polling.

Cadence tuning is closed-loop: if a class alarms repeatedly between samples, halve its interval; if a class has been ALIGNED for eight stamps, double it. The cadence table is itself a claim under Register A.

## 6. Alarms: what triggers

Six triggers, each with a route:

1. **INVERTED (corr <= -0.5):** hard alarm, the Goodhart tripwire. The metric rose while the referent fell: the estate is optimizing a decoupled number. Route: attention.md plus board red (drift_dashboard.py:209 already colors INVERTED #ff7a7a) plus operator escalation, because this regime actively rewards the wrong work.
2. **VACUOUS:** structural alarm. The referent never moved during the window; the metric measures motion with no measured destination (drift.py:70-73). This is STATE I of the triangulation ("nine seats designed the same experiment; none ran it") as a standing condition. Route: pair re-registration or metric retirement; a VACUOUS pair that persists four stamps is deleted, not monitored.
3. **Register-A contradiction:** a claim's instrument returns a different byte truth. Route: fail-closed where a gate owns the claim (the census patrol's exit-2 model, B2-249: rewording the claim breaks the gate rather than silently rephrasing it), attention line with a repair task where no gate owns it. Manifold errors (referent mechanism absent entirely, the session_end_hook class) additionally mark the source doc "model-fiction suspected" for Register-A rotation priority.
4. **Divergence acceleration:** the chaos-specific trigger. Two consecutive samples with widening gap of the same sign means the model and world are in a separating regime; sample the pair at double frequency and escalate tier. A constant gap is calibration error, boring; an accelerating gap is the butterfly panel, urgent. The gap series' second derivative is the alarm, not its level.
5. **FRAGILE-ALIGNED:** no alarm by design; render amber-green, provisional only (drift_pairs_stamp.py:75-87). Alarm on it would train operators to ignore alarms.
6. **Instrument negative-control failure:** the f-22 lesson (commit e79f36eab): the secrets census instrument's batched parser silently dropped every entry and mislabeled all 133 untracked files as NOT_IGNORED; only a direct single-path cross-check (`git check-ignore -v -- .env` returning .gitignore:19) exposed it. Two-level epistemics: the model of the world is read through a model of the instrument, and each layer can rot independently. Every sampler must be probed periodically with a planted defect (a false claim, a broken command) and must disagree with it; a sampler that agrees with everything, including falsified input, is itself INVERTED.

## 7. Theory: what the estate is rebuilding, and from whom

The gap sensor sits on real literature and should say so. Korzybski's map-territory relation is already a RIB (RIB_MAP_PRECEDES_TERRITORY per oral-02's law mapping, commit f576b0ec9); the sensor is its instrumentation. Ashby's requisite variety (1956) justifies per-pair verdicts over one scalar. Conant and Ashby (1970, "Every Good Regulator of a System Must Be a Model of That System") supplies the inversion the estate lives: a system that regulates itself must model itself, and a model without an error channel is a regulator regulating noise; the gap sensor is that error channel. Goodhart's law, in Strathern's form ("when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure"), is exactly the INVERTED verdict: the fleet's lane-trail counts, receipt counts, and stamp counts are all targets, and drift.py's corr(delta-M, delta-R) is the arithmetic form of watching for their Goodhart decay. Lorenz (1963, "Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow") and the 1972 butterfly lecture ground the divergence-rate framing; Packard, Crutchfield, Farmer, and Shaw (1986, "Geometry from a Time Series") grounds the hardest part: the estate observes itself almost entirely through its own scalars (trail rows, jsonl counts, log tails), which is state-space reconstruction from a single observed channel, and reconstruction can recover the attractor's shape while being systematically wrong about phase, precisely the manifold-versus-phase split of section 2. Dawid (1984, prequential calibration) grounds Register C: a probability-issuing system is evaluated by the series of its own expired predictions. Hayek (1945, "The Use of Knowledge in Society") names the failure of any central plan run on the planner's own ledgers: the estate's briefs are a price system of one, and the sensor is its market signal. And Box's "all models are wrong" is the license for the whole design: the sensor does not demand a correct model, it demands a model with a measured divergence.

## 8. The self-referential hazard, and the fleet's epistemic constant

The triangulation names its own flaw in line 1 and again at line 155: convergence of documents is evidence that one reader noticed a pattern five times, not that the pattern is real; and the drift instrument itself "will be designed, then shelved behind the next org" if unguarded. The gap sensor is an internal model of the gap and inherits the disease. Three mitigations, all already estate-native: its receipts land hashed in `watch/inbox/` under the shared-surface contract; its verdicts are recomputable from pure functions (drift.py:27: "Pure computation. Reads nothing, writes nothing"); and it carries a falsification budget, one hand audit per week (the s2-13 pattern) whose results are compared against the sensor's own verdicts, disagreement logged as a first-class row.

The original number this design proposes the estate compute for itself: **the epistemic half-life**. Run Register A over the full claim registry daily; fit the fraction of claims still byte-true at age t; the time constant of that decay is the fleet-level analog of the inverse Lyapunov exponent, the estate's own butterfly rate, measured not assumed. It prices every design argument about documentation discipline: a claim class with a 3-day half-life needs a gate, not a reminder; a class with a 300-day half-life does not deserve the attention it gets. The chaos plate hangs above the desk for exactly this reason: the Lorenz system's rules are three lines and its trajectory is unguessable, so the estate stopped asking whether its model is right and started measuring how fast it is wrong.
