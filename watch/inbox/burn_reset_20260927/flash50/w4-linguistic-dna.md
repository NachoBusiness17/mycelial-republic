## 3. Original findings

**1. The germline has a reverse transcriptase, and it is a card.** In Weismann's terms the ribs are germline (they survive seat death and are read at boot) and sessions are soma. Most architectures never let soma rewrite germline; this estate built an explicit enzyme for it: the CORRECTION card (RIB_BEHAVIOR_CARDS_20260928.md:28-31) converts a live operator correction into "mint as LAW: rib + AGENTS.md if load-bearing + enforcement in code (lessig) -> commit." The driving ladder is a worked example: spoken correction (09-27 night) -> rib -> AGENTS.md -> code comment, all inside ~24 hours, all in commits `a23928aca` and `709e2928a`. The linguistic DNA is not a fossil record; it is an actively transcribed genome with a somatic-to-germline pipeline whose trigger is operator irritation.

**2. The liturgy is the answer to "load-bearing vs decorative."** The most-replicated sentence in the estate (574 files) is not in AGENTS.md, not in a receipt law, and was not written by the operator as such: it is the four-phrase invariant bundle `republic_loop.py:178` stamps into every dead drop. The operator authored each phrase once (Stratum I); the machine now repeats the bundle 537+ times without him. A phrase's true rank is therefore not how often humans write it but whether the machine's own dispatch path emits it. By that criterion the liturgical four (chain-line, coldest vertex, steal-and-adapt, the nod is mine) outrank "trust bytes, not reports," which is counted 446 times by the percolator but is spoken by seats, not structurally emitted by the loop.

**3. Dominance is the missing mechanism; the stale allele is live.** `RIB_FREE_USAGE_POLICY_20260823.md:25` still reads "DeepSeek (funded), the reliable workhorse for real volume" while the driving ladder supersedes the funded rung (LADDER:33-37 names its parent and declares the update). `_rib_doctrine` (context_pack.py:305-339) loads ALL ribs with no superseded logic, so a pack can carry both the extinct and the current allele with nothing marking which expresses. The estate's amendment style is epistasis by adjacency (the newer rib says "this rib updates its funded rung"), which works only when the reader loads both and notices. The genetic vocabulary is exact: the estate has mutation, transcription, and translation, but no dominance mechanism to silence a superseded allele. Cheapest fix in its own idiom: a one-line SUPERSEDED-BY banner convention the reader parses (the same literal-string trick G7 already uses), rather than editing history, which RIB_MD_RECORD forbids (:20-21, corrections append).

**4. The register compresses as the fleet matures, and the ribs are the only archive of the long-form operator.** Stratum I quotes multi-sentence flowing theses ("it was the whole build your castles in the sky and put the foundations beneath them that's what we are doing here", RIB_CASTLES:3-6). Stratum II compresses to decision rules with measured numbers. Stratum IV is one-line imperatives shouted mid-run ("stop running shit and start remembering"). The operator's spoken grammar condensed roughly an order of magnitude over seven weeks; anywhere else (receipts, commits) only the compressed form survives. Then RIB_BEHAVIOR_CARDS (09-28) abandons lexicon entirely and models him as a shape recognizer: the compilation of the operator has moved from his words (v1, quoted verbatim) through his phrases (v2, indexed and counted) to his input-output mapping (v3, the card deck), which is exactly the sovereign-mirror purpose statement quoted at :3-4.

**5. Phrase velocity is measurable and the youngest law is the fastest.** "warmed ribs" has a complete origin chain: operator pushback 09-19 ("warmed RIBs should negate all this extra shit", two-machine-drive-bridge-20260919.md:15), operator commission 09-20 ("theirs should be pre-warmed RIBs of their models: life works, quotes, novels, correspondence, podcasts", RACTOR_PREWARMED_RIB_SPEC_20260920.md:3), law 09-27 (rib + AGENTS.md:32), code gate 09-27 21:09 (stealth_driver.py:486-491), and 19 tracked carrier files by 09-28. Eight days from idiolect to enforced boot-order. The 09-20 spec also shows the concept predating the law by a week as an export product (pre-warmed context for OTHER people's models) before it curled back inward as self-discipline.

**6. The decorative class, honestly separated.** Teeth-checked and found without: "turtles all the way down" (84 files, zero code references found in my greps: organizing metaphor only), "consensus illusion" (90 files; measured once on 08-10, RIB_MINDSWEAVE_EVAL:22-25, and now functions as a commemorative name for a result rather than a live rule), "spinning plates" (6 files, effectively dead, its referent renamed twice), "steer, don't command" (3 files, displaced by its own hyphenate).
