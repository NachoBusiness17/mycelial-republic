## 0. The estate uses "cathedral" three ways, and the market lives between them

The word is load-bearing here, so ground it before spending it:

1. **Cathedral as anti-pattern.** `docs/ref/MAG_Card.md:27` says "Usable tool. Not a cathedral." and `docs/ref/weaves/W8-ponytail-ladder.md:43` lists "Cathedral frameworks" among rejected spends. The estate's own design law is bazaar-shaped: many small stateless seats, no throne, monoliths on the do-not-build list.
2. **Cathedral as artifact.** A literal game world named cathedral is batch-built from doctrine: `corpus/game_estate/GAMES_SITE_PLAYTEST_20260921.md:88` ("cathedral = evennia batch-build rooms from doctrine"), a stolen tavern stub wired in live by `tools/cathedral_wire_tavern.py` (:113), playable at `watch/inbox/cathedral_v1.html` (`memories/session/next-window-handoff.md:209-211`), with a genome/track law layered over it as a go board (`memory/continuity/seat_memory_20260927/MEMORY.md:141`).
3. **Cathedral as aspiration.** The operator's own quoted line: "The ones who weave turn the cage into a cathedral" (`.agents/skills/operator-corrections/SKILL.md:165`), and `handoff/GAME_DRIVER_RIB_20260814.md:58`: "The stateless worker dies, the wave persists."

So the cathedral market is this: perishable, stateless seat-time (bazaar stalls rented by the hour from model vendors) is continuously converted into durable, append-only doctrine-stone (ribs, receipts, invariants, chain links). The market is the exchange rate between those two substances, and the rest of this receipt tries to read that rate off the estate's own books.

## 1. The goods: four grades, ranked by half-life

**Grade 1, stone (durable goods).** Receipts and laws. This directory alone holds 90+ banked .md receipts (my `ls` of `watch/inbox/burn_reset_20260927/flash50/` this session); the corpus catalog reads 581,477 rows (`wc -l < catalog/catalog.jsonl`, run this session). Ribs are the refined ore: `memory/rib/RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md` and `memory/rib/RIB_FREE_USAGE_POLICY_20260823.md` are compiled operator corrections, i.e. demand history recrystallized into supply law. What makes stone stone is not permanence of media but the signing rule: the burn wave's landed count is "re-derived from the LANDED RULE (B2_NN_*.md on disk ...), not self-report" (`memory/burns/BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:1067`). A good exists when a file exists, not when a worker claims one. That is delivery-versus-payment where the delivered file and the clearing instrument are the same object.

**Grade 2, stuff (perishable working state).** Token packs, rate-limit windows, warm state, the FTS index that "decays to scan fallback whenever catalog.jsonl moves past catalog_fts.sqlite" (AGENTS.md, B2-465 clause). Stuff has a half-life measured in hours; it is inventory, not wealth, unless converted.

**Grade 3, steam (sensorium).** Free-lane output: "banked as findings, never as done-work" (`RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md` invariant 2), with `substance_chars=0 is the norm` for free seats (`RIB_FREE_USAGE_POLICY_20260823.md:18`). Steam is priced correctly by the estate: near-zero price, near-zero trust. The sensing/judgment climb law is a quality fence that keeps cheap goods from counterfeiting expensive ones.

**Grade 4, sons (self-reproduction).** The estate manufactures its own workers and its own price list: quantized instruction packs per tier (`memory/corpus/quantized_thought/instructions_flash.jsonl`, `_frontier.jsonl`, `_local.jsonl`, all in the current git status), and `memory/learn/sizing.jsonl`, a one_sizer.v1 ledger where each skill's observed tier price is learned from outcomes (patch-verify priced to flash, caveman-prose to pro; rows at :1-2). A firm that grows its own labor and writes its own tariff book is closer to a monastery than a shop.

## 2. The buyers

**The operator: the only buyer with fiat.** Every spend gate terminates in operator consent: authority grants checked by `mag/effect_broker.py:205-219`, dormant providers unpriced until "Operator GO flips the flag" (`mag/cost_truth.py:44-51`), revocation is permanent by design (`mag/policy_engine.py:235-246`). Demand arrives as corrections: the ladder-02 census found 5 operator corrections in one night, each of which changed behavior. A correction is a purchase order: attention paid, behavior retooled.

**Future seats: the repeat customer.** Ribs, context packs and warm boot exist so the next seat buys context cheap instead of re-deriving it. But there is a quality complaint on the books: ladder-02's warm-at-boot audit found 30 filename hits and 12 transcript mentions of steps.jsonl and zero content reads. Demand is real, delivery failed: the shelves were photographed, not stocked.

**The estate itself, via the dogfood law.** The workflow gate requires dogfooding; the census drift patrol, FTS daily rebuild and violation ratchet are the cathedral buying its own maintenance. This is the largest steady buyer: most stone is purchased by the machine that quarries it.

**No external buyers, on purpose.** `docs/ref/AGENT_BLEEDING_EDGE_REPOS_INDEX.md:23,66` records the strategic refusal twice: "reject payment throne", crypto payment bazaars watched for grammar only. And the internal market's sell-side was never wired: `_broker_model_call` in `mag/effect_broker.py:285-292` is a stub returning "[MODEL_STUB: not wired to seat_dispatch yet]". The one organ that could sell model effects through the broker returns a placeholder. The estate is an economy with a mint and a treasury but no storefront, and the absence is a documented decision, not an oversight.

## 3. The currency: a stack, not a coin

**Exogenous reserves: dollars, and only $180 of them.** "The $180 in Hermes credits (Nous API) is the ONLY funded tier. The Anthropic keys in .env exist but are NOT funded" (`RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:7-12`). Real prices exist and are recorded: 5.3-flash promo $0.15/$0.03/$0.50 per M (`docs/ref/ZAI_API_EXPERT_REFERENCE.md:26`), cache hits ~50% of input price (:54, :148), a measured Pareto frontier at $0.045/task amid a vendor price war (:185).

**The real income line: subscription stock.** `mag/cost_truth.py:52-63` maps `_SUBSCRIPTION_PROVIDER`: glm_coding_lite, zcode, cursor, copilot, chatgpt, grok. The estate's income is not revenue but flat-rate seat time already bought elsewhere. Hence the founding order of the burn economy: "burn remaining flash-lane quota into estate work by midnight" (`BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:4`). Unused quota is destroyed nightly, so the marginal cost of stone is zero and the marginal value of banking is one. The burn wave is an inventory-clearing machine for a perishable stock, the same reason bakeries sell day-old bread at any price.

**Internal unit of account: tokens.** Measured throughput: width 10 Task agents ran clean at 109.4M tok/h with zero surfaced 1302s (`watch/inbox/gala_20260925/AFTERPARTY_CLOSE_20260926.md:30,67`); the ladder-02 receipt records a "100M pack" spent on a 25-gopher mirror mine. Token packs are block currency: issued in bulk, expiring, traceable.

**Small change: calls.** Inside the broker, money is call count: `mag/policy_engine.py:198-232` charges each effect against `authority.budget`, default `max_calls: 3`, and fails closed on a malformed budget (:210-214). The off-by-one fix comment at :220-226 (call 3 must be permitted) is a coinage reform in miniature.

**The clearing instrument: the receipt.** `mag/effect_broker.py:369-434` runs every effect through ENQUEUED to RECEIPT_COMMITTED and writes the receipt file to disk (:184-198); a "budget exhausted" failure still commits a receipt (:399-402). And the accounting has an epistemology: `mag/cost_truth.py:9-14` labels dollars `calculated_api_cost_usd`, never `actual` or `invoice` unless an invoice was joined, and the B2-133 dormant-gate comment rules that "pricing a dead lane $0 is a cost lie" (:44-48). The books distinguish what was spent from what can be known about spending. That is a stronger honesty norm than most human firms keep.

**The reserve currency: operator attention.** Dollars are printed by an employer; tokens are printed by a subscription; attention is printed by nobody. The sibling `deep-attention-economics.md` measured attention.md at 40,436 bytes and called the surface bankrupt; the ladder-02 FUNDING-STATEMENT finding (a 100M pack spent with no receipt, no rib, no ledger row) shows what happens when attention runs short: transactions go unrecorded and the books diverge from reality. Every other currency in the stack is counterfeitable. Attention is not.

## 4. Solvency audit, measured

Production and yield: my venv python count of `memory/burns/BURN_WAVE_STATE.json` this session reads 506 dispensed, 402 landed, 86 outstanding, 10 holds: a 79.4 percent landed yield, with 17 percent of dispensed work still open. The holds log is a thermal history, not a financial one: every entry is a 1302 concurrency death (`BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:925-975`), including the width-24 experiment that surfaced the wall with 8 deaths and fixed the ceiling as dynamic (:962-974). Note 1302 is by definition a concurrency limit, not a balance (`ZAI_API_EXPERT_REFERENCE.md:81`). The binding budget on the flash lanes is rolling throughput, not dollars.

Cost control: `mag/cost_governor.py:9-19,46-47` sets adaptive ceilings, $0.50 for a task that has produced nothing, $1.00 for one producing real output, with health defined as non-empty non-error output and the rule "trust the bill, not the log field" (:14-15). The estate prices failure steeper than success per unit of information gained, which is the correct direction.

Waste and data quality: the dispensed array contains 12 corrupt rows that are just "[" (observed at `BURN_WAVE_STATE.json:421-434`), and the dormancy gate re-prices pre-dormancy history as `unpriced` rather than guessing (`cost_truth.py:44-48`). The ledger admits its own junk. Income: my `git grep -iln "revenue|invoice|subscription_price" -- mag/ tools/` this session returns 8 files, all accounting or burn modules (cost_governor, cost_truth, honest_cache_meter, off_hours, ox_burn, ox_megaburn, codex_seat, game_brawl_perspectives). None charges an external party. There is no revenue mechanism, and the index doc says one was rejected.

**Verdict.** Market-sense: insolvent by design. Nothing is priced for sale, the sell-side broker is a stub, and the payment throne was refused in writing. Endowment-sense: solvent, for a specific reason worth naming as the estate's actual economic theory. Because quota is use-it-or-lose-it, converting expiring tokens into files is pure arbitrage: any landed receipt has positive value and zero opportunity cost. Because the climb law fences steam from judgment, cheap goods cannot counterfeit dear ones. Because the LANDED RULE re-derives landed from disk, production cannot be faked by self-report. The three balances come out: cash-rich (a funded tier plus flat seats already paid), throughput-managed (1302 windows are the real rationing device, with holds as the ration book), attention-poor (one buyer, whose correction and GO bandwidth is the only non-printable currency). The constraint that would kill the cathedral first is not money. It is the architect's reading hours: stone accumulates faster than anyone reads it, and the warm-boot fiction (stocked shelves photographed, never browsed) is unsold inventory sitting in the nave.

Reflexive close: this receipt is itself one unit of the trade it describes, a 50-wide wave converting expiring flash quota into 12KB of stone. If it is never read, it is inventory; ladder-02 already ran that test on the ribs and got zero content reads. The falsifiable test for whether the cathedral market clears is not production, which is plentiful and audited, but a reads-per-receipt instrument over `watch/inbox/`. Until that exists, the honest answer to "can the estate sustain itself economically" is: it sustains itself on subsidy and thermodynamics, and calls it doctrine.
