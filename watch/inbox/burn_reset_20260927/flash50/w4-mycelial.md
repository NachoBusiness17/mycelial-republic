## 1. The governing law changes the question

The science map's honesty clause, docs/ref/MYCELIAL_SCIENCE_MAP.md:25, is the constraint this design obeys: "Metaphor must map to a file, REST surface, or gate - or it stays in prose, not protocol." So "the estate as mycelium" is not a writing exercise. The real question is: which hydraulic and fungal operators already exist in code, which are missing, and what does the routing protocol look like when the missing ones land. The canon already fixes the direction of movement at science map section 4 (lines 78-96): plans down are nutrient delivery, problems up are stress signals, lateral handoffs are hyphal fusion, spore release is portable protocol. What the canon lacks is transport physics: WHY a molecule of work moves from one seat to another. The science map has topology (hyphae, small-world lattice, percolation, section 2 and section 6). water_graph.py is the missing physics, and as of this reading nobody has connected the three organs - the hydraulic engine, the foraging loop, and the tier constitution - into one organism. That connection is the contribution of this item.

## 2. Three organs, one organism

The estate owns three files that are, biologically, three organs of the same body:

- mag/water_graph.py is the hydraulic instruction set. Nodes carry integer elevation and volume (water_graph.py:36-42); pipes are one-way, integer-capacity, optionally siphoned with a crest volume (water_graph.py:46-54); junctions are xor (exactly one inlet passes, both overflow to waste), sill (inflow must reach a threshold), or plain (water_graph.py:15-18); steam is a second routing medium that ignores pipes and condenses at the highest cold node (water_graph.py:19-23, 86-105); the whole state round-trips through to_spec/from_spec as schema water_graph.v1 (water_graph.py:248-274). It is wired into mag/water_computer.py and mag/game_campaign.py (verified by grep this session), so this is not dead code - it is the campaign substrate.
- tools/stealth_driver.py is the foraging loop. Twelve topics rotate on a 300-second cycle with 2-second stagger (tools/stealth_driver.py:68, 826-849); each is fail-open so one topic's crash never stops the loop (stealth_driver.py:842-848). It holds the work queue (state/fuel/stealth_work_queue.jsonl, stealth_driver.py:124), the tier ladder (stealth_driver.py:205-251), the spacing valves (stealth_driver.py:340), the trust valve ("sensed, never done", stealth_driver.py:486-491), and the boundary system: exactly four allowlisted URLs, scheme checked, resolved IPs checked public, redirects disabled (stealth_driver.py:31-35, 73-111, 495-513).
- configs/lanes.yaml is the tier constitution. Four lanes L0-L3 (lanes.yaml:7-25); hardware capacity law (6GB VRAM, never stack two 7B/8B models, max_resident 1, 8k context, lanes.yaml:28-47); the escalation budget (grok_budget max 2/day, require_priority, lanes.yaml:71-74); marker vocabulary (lanes.yaml:76-91); and the judge cascade gate_verdict_v2 with tau 0.90, derivation band always-escalate, and governor-only writes (lanes.yaml:110-158).

Biology has the same division: transport physics (turgor, osmosis), foraging behavior (growth, reinforcement), and species constitution (what the organism can and cannot be). The estate's insight, not yet stated anywhere in the canon, is that these three files already agree with each other. The protocol below just makes the agreement explicit and executable.

Two rows deserve emphasis. The siphon is the estate's escalation semantics stated as physics: an escalation channel is not a permission, it is a pool that must fill to crest before it primes (water_graph.py:205-211), and once primed it drains until the intake breaks (water_graph.py:219-231). ## 4. The pressure gradients

In fungal networks, transport is pressure-driven bulk flow: sugars loaded into a source region draw water osmotically, turgor rises, and solutes flow toward sinks; the Fricker-lab imaging work on Phanerochaete velutina is the canonical picture (characterization from training knowledge). The essential point is that pressure is not sent; it is BUILT by differential loading, and routing is what happens when gradients act on the network. The estate has seven real gradients, all cited:

G1, the cost/osmotic gradient. Reasoning effort is dialed low on free rungs and max on judgment/scarce5 (stealth_driver.py:161-172), and multi-lane fan-outs must clear the premium-times-baseline value-per-token bar or the admission gate rejects the spend (lanes.yaml:96-108). This is differential loading: cheap rungs load by default, expensive rungs load only when a molecule carries the tag.

G2, the abundance/scarcity gradient (source-sink). The substrate at the free rung is infinite by design: the selffeed topic reads mined findings and proposes a new investigation after every dispatch, so "the queue never drains - it grows" (stealth_driver.py:782-823, quote at 786-787). The scarce rung is two glm-5.3-prime accounts, one of them don't-touch-weekly (stealth_driver.py:207-211). Molecules flow from the abundant source to the scarce sink only when tagged tier judgment or scarce5, which the dispatcher enforces by matching item tier to lane tier (stealth_driver.py:331-345). This is the mycelial source-sink relation exactly: abundance pushes, scarcity pulls, and the channel between them stays closed until the molecule's osmotic load justifies opening it.

G3, the trust gradient with a check valve. Free-lane product is "sensed, never done" (stealth_driver.py:486-491); empty-content reasoning tails are banked LABELED unverified (stealth_driver.py:423-427); every answer is ponytail-audited against disk before banking (stealth_driver.py:442-447); verification climbs to the funded tier only (RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928, per the AGENTS.md driving ladder bullet). Trust is turgor: it only moves downhill, and the check valve at water_graph.py:233-234 ("gravity-only, never uphill") is the code-shape of the law that low-trust product cannot be consumed as high-trust without paying the climb.

G4, the priority gradient (a signal, not a standing field). The markers [priority], [grok], [L2] (lanes.yaml:76-79) open lanes that otherwise require them (lanes.yaml:22), capped at 2/day (lanes.yaml:72). This is the estate's electrical spike - the transient field change that opens one channel and decays. The science map already maps electrical signaling to timing badges and training events (science map:54).

G5, the viscosity gradients (pipe radii, not routing decisions). CYCLE_SECONDS 300 (stealth_driver.py:68), 2-second topic stagger "never burst the shared box" (stealth_driver.py:848-849), 30-minute lane spacing (stealth_driver.py:340), sequential model loading with max_resident 1 on a 6GB card (lanes.yaml:43-47, 56-64), 8k context (lanes.yaml:30). None of these choose where work goes; they choose how much moves per tick. In mycelial terms these are cord calibers, and they are set by hardware, not by preference.

G6, the verification sill. The routine band passes at tau 0.90 or escalates (lanes.yaml:127-133); the derivation band always escalates because "no confidence threshold is safe on this band" (lanes.yaml:135-138) - literally a pipe that only ever flows uphill, from any judge to a stronger judge, by written law. Contested pairs are rationed and debiased both orders (lanes.yaml:141-145), which is a crest volume on the escalation channel so the judge cascade itself cannot flood.

G7, the freshness decay. The fossil topic checks pack and chain staleness (stealth_driver.py:556-561), chain repair verifies the verkle tip (stealth_driver.py:564-569), and the roster topic diffs per-host model catalogs into configs/mirrors.json with churn flags (stealth_driver.py:572-639). Standing water loses head: a stale brief is low-potential water the network will not lift without re-priming. This gradient is what makes the boot-freshness gate (AGENTS.md) hydraulic rather than bureaucratic.

## 5. The mycelial routing protocol (v0 sketch, fully mapped to code)

Layer 0, substrate. A WaterGraph instance IS the routing table. Nodes are lanes and organs; pipes are dispatch channels; a tick is a scheduler pass. Because tick() is deterministic integer physics with insertion-ordered pipes and no RNG (water_graph.py:24), and the whole graph round-trips through to_spec/from_spec (water_graph.py:248-274), a routing plan is a durable artifact that replays byte-identically - the property that makes it estate-native. The DNA (residual + registry + leaf) can file routing state the same way it files work, and verkle-audit can hash it.

Layer 1, molecules. A task row is a molecule carrying osmotic load: tier, effort, priority marker. Dispatch is placement, not sending. t_work already demonstrates the shape: it never pushes work at a model; it places the ask in the queue, and a lane whose tier matches and whose spacing valve has elapsed draws it (stealth_driver.py:321-345). The mining preamble rides every ask as a stable cache prefix so cache behavior is measurable per dispatch (stealth_driver.py:143-148, 349), and the laws ride inside every ask: never fabricate, no em-dashes, cite sources (stealth_driver.py:128-129).

Layer 2, routing rules. Six rules, each already an estate law in code:
R1: doctrine moves as steam, never as pipe water. RIBs and context packs ignore tier routing and condense at the coldest fresh seat. water_graph.py:86-105 is this rule executable; the boot-order law (ribs, then state, then act, AGENTS.md) is its social form.
R2: under-gate inflow HOLDS, never spills silently (water_graph.py:170-179). A quorum that has not arrived is a pool, not a loss.
R3: a pooled crest primes the siphon, which runs until intake dry (water_graph.py:204-211, 219-231). Escalation spends what it primed.
R4: exactly one inlet per XOR tick; simultaneous claims overflow to waste with a named event (water_graph.py:155-166). This is the hydraulic form of exclusive claiming: two lanes that fuse on the same task both lose their water to waste, visibly.
R5: every dead molecule emits a line: "to waste at Y" (water_graph.py:237-238) or "STARVED" with node, amount, gate (water_graph.py:183-186). The network is not allowed to look like it routed when it starved.
R6: free-lane outflow arrives labeled sensed; done requires a funded-tier climb (stealth_driver.py:486-491). The check valve seen from the product side.

Layer 3, the missing node kinds. The science map's vocabulary (hypha, spore, fruiting, science map:49-61) has no water_graph counterpart yet; closing that gap is the actual build item:
- kind tip: a low-elevation explorer with small capacity and high expected mortality. Free-lane sensing topics are tips: probe20, roster, rumor (stealth_driver.py:540-553, 572-639). The network budgets for tips the way the estate budgets throwaway tokens at the free rung - most die, and the deaths are the data.
- kind cord: not a new primitive but a pipe ROLE assigned by observed flow. A pipe that has carried repeated traffic thickens (cap up); one that starves prunes. The reinforcement data already exists: cache_stats and lane_errors preference state (stealth_driver.py:367-372, 429-440) and the mirrors.json churn catalog (stealth_driver.py:610-638). This is science map Level 2 (computational mycelium models, v7-v8, science map:173-179) arriving as hydraulic code rather than as a metrics dashboard.
- kind fruit: a receipt sink where spores are minted - watch/inbox receipts, RIBs, skills, SaveWorkflow amendments. In water terms: volume that reaches a fruit node freezes into a durable packet.
- spore: canon already defines it (science map:57, portable protocol docs); in water terms a dormant node with germination conditions (funded key present, boot order run).

## 6. Where the estate deliberately violates mycelial physics

V1: fungi have no throne; the estate has one. The judge never moves its own gate - thresholds live in lanes.yaml and are writable by the governor only (lanes.yaml:114-119, 158). The estate is a hydraulic substrate under a legislative overlay. The LESSIG RAILS header (stealth_driver.py:131-148) says this out loud: solved industry patterns are stolen into rails rather than trusted to model behavior. In biology the law IS the physics; here the physics is written, and the writer is a role distinct from the fluid. That is the single most important divergence, because it is what makes correction possible at all - a colony cannot amend its own turgor law after a starve-out; the operator did, in one day (starvation law landed 2026-09-28 with a dedicated test file).

V2: steam is anti-gravity. Real translocation never ignores the network. The estate's second medium exists because law must reach seats without passing through the tiers - the operator must never have to re-teach a law the ribs hold (AGENTS.md). A colony cannot patch itself by evaporation; an estate can and should. The steam/cold pair (water_graph.py:86-105) is therefore the most agent-native primitive in the file and the least biological one.

V3: determinism instead of oscillation. Fungal transport is noisy and pulsatile (the Tlalka-lab oscillatory translocation work; characterization from training knowledge). water_graph chose integers, insertion order, and no RNG (water_graph.py:24). The estate scheduled the oscillation (300s cycle, staggered topics, 30-min spacing) instead of growing it, because auditability beats realism: same spec, same tick, same events, same hash.

## 7. Literature hooks

- Fricker-lab imaging of Phanerochaete velutina: nutrient translocation is pressure-driven bulk flow through a rank-3 network - fine exploratory hyphae at the margin, medium transport cords, coarse long-distance cords (training knowledge). The estate's three dispatch strata are the same rank structure: free sensing topics are the tips, lane channels are the cords, funded judgment is the trunk.
- Bebber et al. 2007, Proceedings of the Royal Society B, "Biological solutions to transport network design": fungal networks land on a measurable trade point between transport efficiency, resilience, and cost (title-level, training knowledge). lanes.yaml is that trade written down: the hardware block is the cost axis (lanes.yaml:56-69), the routing ladder is the efficiency axis, and the check-valve trust law is the resilience axis - a contaminated molecule cannot poison the verified pool because it structurally cannot flow uphill.
- Tlalka et al. on oscillatory amino-acid translocation entrained by resource supply (training knowledge): the estate's cadence is an entrained oscillation by design (CYCLE_SECONDS 300, 30-min spacing) rather than an emergent one - the same physics, domesticated.
- Rayner, "The challenge of the individualistic mycelium": mycelial identity is fusion compatibility, not a central genome (training knowledge). The estate's identity is exactly provenance plus compatibility: every mined row carries lane and model (stealth_driver.py:471-478), and fusion with a host is gated by a literal allowlist (stealth_driver.py:74-79, 99-102) with resolved-IP checks against rebinding (stealth_driver.py:86-93). Incompatible cytoplasm is refused at the boundary, not after fusion.
- Adamatzky and Schubert's fungal logic-gate work and the electrical signaling literature are already in the estate bibliography (science map:194-198). The xor and sill junctions in water_graph.py are a third implementation of that same claim - logic emerges from topology, not from a model - after the timing-badge and training-event rows the science map already wired (science map:54-55).
- Distinguish Physarum polycephalum (Tero et al. 2010, adaptive network design; a slime mold, not a fungus): its tube-thickening-by-flow law is nonetheless exactly the reinforcement rule proposed for cord kind above, and the estate already measures the flow (cache_stats, mirrors.json churn). The metaphor family is one; the phylum is not, and the protocol should say so.

## 8. The protocol in one sentence

Place, do not send; gate, do not block; hold, do not spill; name, do not swallow; climb, do not substitute; move doctrine as steam; and write the routing table as a replayable spec so the network itself is a receipt.
