## What was ported (source → module)

All three flock laws live in NEW `roblox/src/shared/Flock.luau` — a pure module
(no Instances, no services), executable against a bare Vector3 stub:

1. **Flee / tire / rest** (swarm.html:951-968 as read at ask start; see the line
   note below): an airborne fish flees the player inside the flee radius at
   `max(minSpeed, baseSpeed − flee·slope)` with the same speed scalar pushed ×6
   on the plane and ×4 on height, accumulates `flee` fatigue, then coasts 0.8s
   (drag only, no gravity) once out of range, then TIRES: fatigue ×`0.4^dt` and
   gravity 140 (scaled) pulls it down; floor contact flips it to `rest` with the
   source's ×0.6 horizontal damp. Drag 0.985 per source frame, frame-normalized
   `0.985^(60·dt)`; arena walls bounce at half restitution (the source's x-walls
   generalized to the port's two horizontal axes). The braver-in-rain radius
   (68/95 ratio kept) rides a `raining` parameter; the port passes false (the
   zone has no weather yet).
2. **School** (swarm.html:970-981, the boids core): a RESTED fish is steered by
   direct position nudges only, never velocity — cohesion toward the centroid of
   neighbors inside the school radius (rate 0.9 plane / 0.55 height, verbatim),
   separation inside the separation radius (0.6 / 0.35, verbatim), plus the
   identity wiggle `sin(t·7 + x·0.05)·amp·dt` along world X, then the floor
   clamp. Composition order is the source's: separation inside the neighbor
   loop, cohesion after it reading the live pos, wiggle reading the
   post-cohesion x — the check replicates that composition exactly.
3. **Pheromone trail** (steering law swarm.html:842-851, drop law :1113-1121):
   the horde steers at the freshest trail point whose manhattan score
   `life·(1 − d/radius)` beats the verbatim floor 1.2, else straight at the
   player. Walking drops life 2.5; the loud action drops life 5 (source: the
   harvest header; port: the Fire pellet, window 0.45s ≈ the source's harvT
   0.45). Drop interval 0.15s, cap 140, oldest evicted — all verbatim.

Crop loop: kills drop fish with the toss law (swarm.html:631 — random
horizontal velocity, upward start), the "the fish stir..." warn fires once per
session at 30 unharvested (swarm.html:1074), and the population caps at 45.

**Scale law** (one mapping, recorded in `roblox/docs/CLONED_MECHANICS.md` §3.7
and the module header): the source runs a 2400-wide canvas with a 200 u/s
skater; the port arena is 90 studs with a 16 studs/s walk. Speeds scale ×0.08
(the player ratio; gravity and the toss inherit it); lengths are tuned inside
that envelope: flee 95→14, catch 15→3.5 (the port's existing touch-collect
radius), school 155→20, separation 15→3, trail radius 300→38, wiggle amp 6→1.5.
Rates, thresholds, lives, the 1.2 score floor, drag and the 0.4^dt fatigue decay
carry over verbatim.

## Wiring (MOD `roblox/src/server/SwarmZone.luau`)

- `gems: {BasePart}` replaced by `school: { { part: BasePart, f: Flock.Fish } }`
  — the visual part is anchored and rides the pure state tick by tick.
- `dropFish(pos, toss?)` is the one spawner: killEnemy drops a tossed fish per
  kill; the ambient spawner (kept, port addition) seeds RESTING fish so the
  school is visible solo; both respect POP_CAP 45.
- Per tick: airborne fish flee their nearest diver (the source's single-player
  law generalized) and can be cornered by the catch law; rested fish school;
  parts sync from state; the trail ages.
- The horde chase (Q-043's Trove loop) now steers via `Flock.trailSteer` — the
  trail pulls when fresher than the floor, else direct pursuit.
- Pet-orb/touch collect radii (3.5 / 12, the established port law) untouched;
  the tired ones are the catchable ones emerges for free (a fleeing fish
  outruns the ring until it rests).
