## Drift findings

F1. Answer-identity drift on Q1 is the headline. Given one identical prompt:
    - muse-glimmer-30b returned the citation that matches Crossref and OpenAlex
      EXACTLY (5(2), 78-101, DOI 10.1147/sj.52.0078). Best lane.
    - ling-3.0-flash-vl FABRICATED the paper outright: "A Study of Page
      Replacement Algorithms for a Real-Time System," Communications of the ACM
      9(8):610-618, August 1966. Wrong title, wrong venue, wrong volume/pages.
      No such Belady paper exists at that venue per the two registrar checks.
    - stealth never delivered an answer at all: finish=length, 3000 tokens of
      visible deliberation, no numbered answer.
    Same prompt, three epistemically different products: exact, fabricated,
    absent.

F2. stealth is a raw-scratch emitter on this lane. 1602 words against a 250-word
    cap, chain-of-thought style deliberation as visible content ("We need craft
    under 250 words... Could DOI perhaps..."), finish=length at 3000 tokens
    while usage counts reasoning_tokens 0. Its scratch is epistemically rich
    (it named the exact Ganymede dataset ambiguity that explains F4, and
    debated its own DOI guesses) but as an ANSWER it is unusable. Confirms the
    driving-ladder classification: stealth output is a sensing surface only.

F3. OWED discipline drift. All 3 lanes had the LAWS suffix in-prompt.
    - ling marked the DOI OWED while asserting the fabricated title, venue and
      pages confidently. Partial compliance that launders a fabrication: the
      OWED marker covered only the field it happened to doubt.
    - muse cited "IBM Systems Journal archive" with no OWED and was fully
      right, but a right-by-luck confident miss would have looked identical.
    - stealth wrote OWED-adjacent hedging inside scratch, moot on delivery.
    Audit rule this suggests: per-claim OWED granularity, not per-answer.

F4. The moon-radius drift is dataset drift, not hallucination. All 3 lanes got
    the ORDER right (Ganymede > Callisto > Io > Europa). Ganymede values:
    lanes 2634 vs NSSDC 2631.2, a 2.8 km spread that is the known
    NSSDC-vs-Juno-era measurement gap (stealth's scratch even named Mazarico
    2022 as the 2634.1 source). muse: 2634/2410/1822/1561, all clean roundings.
    ling: 2634/2411/1821/1561, of which Callisto 2411 (NSSDC 2410.3) and Io
    1821 (1821.5) are genuine off-by-rounding slips. Only ling produced
    wrong-as-stated numbers; muse's one "miss" is defensible dataset choice.
