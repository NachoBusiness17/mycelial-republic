## Honest caveats

- `usage.prompt_tokens_details.video_tokens` stayed **0** in both runs, while
  the burn prompt counted 7,494 tokens (vs 228 text-only). The video's tokens
  are folded into plain prompt tokens, or the Stealth provider does not report
  the split. The `video_tokens` slot exists but is not being metered. Do not
  build cost math on video_tokens until a nonzero sighting exists.
- Training-data prior on this exact famous clip cannot be fully excluded; the
  control removes prompt-prior explanations, and the temporal arc (struggle,
  charge-through, downed at the end) matches the real frames in order.
- The mandatory reasoning trace is present (2,552 chars) even at default
  effort; latency 13.6s for the video call vs 2.4s text-only.
