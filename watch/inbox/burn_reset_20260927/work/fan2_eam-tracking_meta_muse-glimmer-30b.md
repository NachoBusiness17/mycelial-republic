# FAN2 eam-tracking

lane model: meta/muse-glimmer-30b

---

Open source tracking of US military EAM broadcasts is limited to the existence of hobbyist monitoring communities, not to verifiable attribution of message content.

Verifiably public:

* HFUnderground.com exists as a hobbyist forum for HF monitoring. The site hosts discussion threads about military HF activity. Name cited: HFUnderground.
* RadioReference.com exists and hosts forums and wiki pages for radio monitoring hobbyists. Name cited: RadioReference.
* The general concept that EAMs, Emergency Action Messages, are a class of US military HF voice traffic used for nuclear command and control is widely described in open literature on radio history. Name cited: none for a single definitive source, general public knowledge.

What can and cannot be attributed:

* Cannot be attributed: Specific real time EAM transcripts, specific sender or recipient unit identification, specific operational meaning, and specific frequency usage at a given time. Hobbyist logs are anecdotal and not authenticated by any official source. I could not verify any open source that provides authenticated attribution of EAM content to a specific US military unit or operation. OWED: attempted verification of authenticated EAM transcripts and unit attribution from public hobbyist networks, none found verifiable.
* Cannot be attributed: A current, verified public list of EAM frequencies. I could not verify a reliable public frequency list for EAM use that is maintained by a verifiable open source. OWED: attempted verification of a current public EAM frequency list from HFUnderground and RadioReference, no verifiable authoritative list found.
* Cannot be attributed: Claims that a particular EAM broadcast correlates with a specific real world event. Such correlation is hobbyist speculation and not verifiable.

In summary, the existence of hobbyist monitoring networks is verifiably public. The content, attribution, and operational interpretation of specific EAM broadcasts from those networks is not verifiably public and remains OWED.
