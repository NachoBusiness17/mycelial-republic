## Context & Empirical Analysis

Schmidhuber (2008) formalized curiosity and creativity as reinforcement learning driven by intrinsic motivation.
The intrinsic reward is defined not as static compressibility (which rewards boring, highly redundant noise or trivial patterns) nor as raw unpredictability (which traps the agent in incompressible white noise), but as the first derivative of compressibility over time: compression progress.
In our sovereign multi-agent estate, agents continuously ingest unstructured observational logs, execute verification runs, and bank knot files.
Without an active curiosity mechanism, queue generation degenerates into redundant query repetition or intractable, arbitrary rabbit holes.
