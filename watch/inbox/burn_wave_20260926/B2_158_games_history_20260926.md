## The 5 steals (each: what history says, then what we do with it)

1. FAUCET/SINK LEDGER AS A FIRST-CLASS ARTIFACT (from EVE Online; CCP hired a central-bank economist, Eyjolfur Gudmundsson, and ships Monthly Economic Reports; external anchor, PLAUSIBLE-per-canonical-status: gamespress.com announcement, CCP MERs). History says a player economy without measured faucets and sinks drifts blind. Steal: Virtue Marks and Candy Bones get a faucet/sink ledger (every grant and every burn journaled with reason codes) and a weekly census receipt, before any new sink ships; the B2-165-style grading ledger pattern already in this repo is the shape.
2. THE EXCHANGE MUST NEVER OUTSHINE THE DROP (from Diablo 3's Real Money Auction House; Blizzard admitted the AH gutted the loot loop and shut it down in 2014; external anchors, OPENED-via-search-summary: pcgamesn.com 2013 dev admission, gamingnexus.com gold-dupe RMAH collapse). History says when trading becomes the optimal path, the core loop dies and every dupe bug becomes a bank run. Steal: keep the Ossuary's best moments drop-shaped, not purchase-shaped; the Sixth Candle pass sells comfort (torch radius) not victory; never list loop-winning power on the Exchange, and cap transferable currency per account per day to make dupe-class exploits bounded.
3. SINKS MUST SCALE WITH EMISSIONS (from Axie Infinity; SLP minted per win with breeding as the only major sink collapsed when inflow slowed; external anchors, OPENED-via-search-summary: ehandbook.com web3 post-mortem, rugdoc.io soft/hard token taxonomy). History says a reward token whose emission outgrows its sinks hyperinflates no matter how good the game is. Steal: model Candy Bones as the soft token (earnable, spendable, no real-money exit promise) and price prestige sinks elastically (Skeleton Court cosmetic tiers) so sink capacity grows with emission; watch the earn-rate/burn-rate ratio weekly in the steal-1 ledger.
4. PLAYER-RUN INSTITUTIONS SHIP WITH TRUST INFRASTRUCTURE OR NOT AT ALL (from EVE's player banks: EBANK and the 2009 scandals made scams legend, but EVE is a 20-year-old game with escrow, contracts, and killmails as the audit surface; external anchor, PLAUSIBLE: news.ycombinator.com retrospectives, ResearchGate EVE economy studies). History says player institutions (banks, courts, guilds) are the strongest retention engine ever found and also the fastest trust-destroyer when the audit surface is missing. Steal: if player trading or guild vaults land in THE MOUNTAIN, ship the server-mediated escrow + public ledger day one; never let players hold each other's funds through unlogged channels; a small estate cannot afford an EBANK moment.
5. PERSISTENCE IS BEING REMEMBERED, NOT STATE SAVING (from the local MUD canon verified line-by-line plus the tabletop lineage: roguelike ladders, D&D campaigns, Ultima Online player towns; local citation VERIFIED: FROM_MUD_TO_WOW_20260914.md ascension ladder and learning horde). History says players stay for worlds that remember them; the cheapest deep form of this is named traces, not simulated simulation. Steal: THE MOUNTAIN persists player traces in the Ossuary (named bones where players died, lanterns lit that stay lit for the next visitor, a Court ledger of who defeated the Skeleton Court that week); implementation is a per-player row in a store rendered into the world, the exact move the MUD's persistent JSON horde already proved.
