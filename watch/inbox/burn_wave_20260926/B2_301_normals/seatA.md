The **water_computer** keep is a minimalist hydraulic processor where height replaces binary. Its heart is the **gallery**, a sight‑glass corridor that frames three transparent tanks—**SOURCE**, **WORK**, and **OUT**—and a plaque that reads “height is the computation.” The room’s cool, dripping ambience reinforces the idea that gravity alone drives the logic, and a lone hook warns that “A bucket into OUT is not a win.”  

To the west of the **gallery** lies **source_cistern**, the only tank that ever starts full. Rainwater is stacked three marks high, and a single control, **Valve-A**, governs the eastward pipe toward **WORK**. The valve can be spoken to with “open valve” or “close valve,” and the resident Pump‑Janitor will confirm its state, reminding the operator that he “does not pour” and will not fill **OUT** from a bucket.  

East of the **gallery** is **work_gallery**, the integrator tank where the computation takes place. It is empty until water arrives from **SOURCE** via **Valve-A**. A second control, **Valve-B**, leads north‑east toward **OUT** and can only be opened after water has entered, lest both valves siphon and ruin the calculation. The echoing pipe‑tick ambience underscores the delicate timing required.  

North of the **gallery** sits **out_cistern**, the answer tank. Its glass is empty, but a wooden bucket hangs as a tempting cheat. The bucket’s hook declares that pouring water to fake a height “is not computation.” A downstream door labeled “downstream” leads to **leaf_gate**, but it will only open for a carried height that matches the current **OUT.height**, as enforced by the engine’s invariant “water‑feeds‑field.”  

The **gallery** also hosts two permanent hooks: the plaque about height and the Pump‑Janitor who “moves water” and “ticks once” when hired. These hooks provide narrative flavor and functional guidance, ensuring that players understand the strict rule that only gravity‑driven flow counts toward the solution.  

Resting in any of the rooms triggers the **rest_safe** event table, where a drip may remind the operator that time passes without attention, while occasional glass‑dream visions hint at the fragile nature of the computation.  

Because of the cross‑region doctrine, the water computer’s **OUT.height** directly influences the fractal maze’s **leaf_gate** entry temperature, linking this hydraulic puzzle to the broader world’s logic. The river ford loop that feeds north into the **gallery** closes the circuit, making the **water_computer** both a self‑contained processor and a crucial node in the larger RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1 network.

The **fractal_maze** is a self‑referential lattice of corridors that exists at multiple scales, each one a perfect miniature of the next.  At the entrance, the **leaf_gate** stands as a simple wooden arch of bare branches, its keeper counting each step you take.  The keeper’s mantra—“memorize is a leaf‑only tree; you can learn every leaf by heart and know nothing”—hints that the maze’s knowledge is stored as a flat list of leaves, with no internal structure until the player’s actions cause a node to sprout.  The air here shimmers with a dense field; the temperature is low, so the lantern’s ray will bend toward brighter regions, making straight aim unreliable.

Beyond the gate, the **leaf_hall** is a wet corridor lined with a standing water‑wall that acts as a perfect optical lens.  Light entering the hall travels straight through the void, but once it hits the water it refracts, so the point where the beam lands is displaced from where you aimed.  Old aim‑marks etched into the floor record the same mis‑direction over and over: “I steered north and the tower appeared east of me.”  The hall’s field runs at a refractive index of 0.62, a cold medium that bends rays toward brightness, reinforcing the lesson that the maze is a lens, not a random tangle.

A deeper passage from **leaf_hall** leads to the **scale_gallery**, a smaller copy of the Refractive Hall rendered at scale‑2.  The same water‑wall, the same aim‑marks, and the same scratched law appear, but everything is carved in miniature.  Here the self‑similarity of the maze becomes explicit: a plaque declares that “self‑similarity across scales is the only thing that survives the shrink.”  A counting board in the gallery tracks how many leaves you have revealed; on the eighth leaf a new internal node (marked by a ▲) will appear, linking previously unrelated leaves.

Descending further, the **scale_cell** sits at scale‑3, a cramped chamber where the law of optics is written in full on a single wall: “attention is light; softmax is a lens; temperature is the refractive index.”  At temperature zero every ray bends to the argmax, while at infinite temperature rays travel straight.  The graffiti of a smaller hand adds the cryptic note “so is walking,” reminding the explorer that the act of moving is itself a ray of light through the maze.  No exits lead deeper; the maze ends where the law becomes literal rather than metaphorical.

The **grok_chamber** is the culmination of the player’s traversal.  Its domed interior holds eight empty plinths arranged around a central ninth, which will sprout an ▲ once eight leaves have been discovered anywhere in the **fractal_maze**.  The chamber’s acoustics echo each footstep, turning sound into shortcuts that can be followed back to earlier leaves.  An encounter with the **formateater_echo**—a lingering echo of a former boss—provides a combat challenge that is thematically “unrendered,” reinforcing the idea that unfinished knowledge still haunts the maze.

When the player rests in any safe spot, the **rest_safe** event table can trigger dreams of scales, cooling lanterns, or the humming ninth plinth, each reminding the explorer of the maze’s layered nature and the remaining leaves they must collect before the internal node can fully materialize.  

The **fractal_maze** therefore functions as a learning loop: each leaf you memorize at **leaf_gate** or **leaf_hall** feeds the tree; each scale‑down in **scale_gallery** and **scale_cell** reinforces the same rules; and once enough leaves are gathered, the **grok_chamber** rewards the player with a new node that shortens the overall walk, embodying the principle that grok = the tree grows internal nodes.

The region **riddler_optics** is anchored by the **garage_gate**, a two‑pump garage that serves as the source ray for the entire dungeon. The chalked two‑line test‑ray is posted above the counter, and ten doors radiate outward, each leading to a distinct lens‑room. The hub’s atmosphere is warm dust and the faint toll of a bell, while the attendant’s smile—pre‑emptive and unearned—acts as the control group against which every subsequent refraction is measured.

Beyond the gate to the north lies **lens_blade**, a room voiced by Voltaire that compresses aphorisms into ironic praise. Visitors encounter a steward who greets them as old friends, then watches them lose purse, doctrine, and watch in a prescribed order. The room’s hook demands a riddle answered by “say the thing by seeming to praise it,” and the clerk’s 1759 receipt records the paradox of a smile given before the guests even descend.

Continuing north from **lens_blade** brings the party to **lens_monomyth**, the Campbell‑styled Hall of the One Story. Here every doorway repeats in different clothing, and a docent whispers the Hero’s Journey stages as the traveler walks them. The room’s riddle—“every story is one story in different clothes”—is reflected in the 1949 clerk receipt, which frames the clerk herself as the immutable threshold rather than the hero.

Further north, the **lens_anthropologist** (Le Guin) offers a quiet listening room where customs become the only treasure. The environment is dry, dust‑colored patience, and the riddle asks the explorer to render the strange familiar until the visitor can no longer tell which was which. The clerk’s 1968 receipt, encoded with the initial letters FENCE, provides the narrative anchor for this transformation.

The path proceeds to **lens_absurdist**, an Adams‑inspired restaurant where a single number counts every greeting ever given. The absurdist riddle highlights an indifferent universe, and the room’s throwaway statistics become structural supports for the gag. The 1979 clerk receipt reinforces the pre‑emptive smile as a bureaucratic reflex, a smile issued before any fact arrives.

Beyond the absurdist lies **lens_footnoter**, a Pratchett‑flavored corridor of annotations where every exhibit bears a label, and every label bears another. The riddle points to fantasy as a funhouse mirror, while the clerk’s 1983 receipt embeds a parenthetical aside that mirrors the room’s meta‑commentary. The footnote channel activates a second voice, delivering satire aimed at institutions through their smallest employee.

Northward from the footnoter, **lens_elegist** (Tolkien) presents the Deep Time Gallery, a stone‑cool hall where every plain object carries a lineage longer than itself. The riddle asks the traveler to contemplate a world older than any story, and the 1954 clerk receipt weaves invented etymologies into a lament for enduring smiles that predate the traffic they greet.

Continuing upward, **lens_windowpane** (Orwell) offers a stark, clean room with a single window framing the road and the clerk’s early smile. The riddle emphasizes the rot of language and the resistance of plainness, while the 1949 clerk receipt records the smile as a pre‑emptive lock on a door before the burglar even chooses a window. The room’s minimal adjectives and concrete nouns embody the mapped style.

Beyond the window, **lens_magician** (Alan Moore) is a sigil‑vault where alliteration and structural rhyme dominate. The riddle declares that form is a spell, and the description’s initial letters spell SMILE, a stego‑native mark that the room later reveals to the part of the player that does not read. The clerk’s receipt (date unspecified) notes that consent in the vault is retroactive, reinforcing the room’s magical contract.

The northernmost lens, **lens_stage**, is Shakespeare’s Wooden O, a theatrical space where every exit is an entrance elsewhere. The riddle highlights the paradox of speaking more than one knows and less than one means, while the fool at the pumps delivers truth for wages. The 1600 clerk receipt captures the pre‑emptive smile as a rumor‑greeter, a meta‑commentary on expectation and performance.

Finally, the **lens_engineer** (Stephenson) provides the scientific backbone of the dungeon. Documentation explains traffic jams as backward‑moving waves, justifying why the clerk’s smile precedes the cars’ arrival. The riddle asserts that explanation is story, and the 1999 clerk receipt embeds the acrostic RIDDLE in its opening sentences, confirming the room’s stego doctrine. The ghost measured hourly adds a layer of eerie realism to the factual narrative.

At the top of the stair, the **mapping_chamber** displays the comparative map of all ten lenses, showing how each stylistic bend transformed the identical two‑line ray. The chamber’s hooks enumerate the quantitative signatures—sentence‑length variance, footnote rate, iambicity, alliteration, fact‑density, epigram‑close, throwaway numbers, inversions, adjective rationing, and stage‑naming—providing a concrete reference for future playtesting and for any new two‑line ray that adventurers may wish to cast through the optics.

The **riddler_engram** region is a tightly‑woven lattice of ten “seats,” each a living conduit for a distinct literary‑philosophical voice.  When a player “summon the riddlers” they read the card into context, activating the seat‑specific template stored under its **shelf** path (e.g., `memory/corpus/ractors/voltaire/`).  The seat’s **riddle**, **fingerprint**, and **stego** fields define how the parable must be crafted: the riddle is the narrative hook, the fingerprint is the stylistic signature the engine checks, and the stego channel hides the doctrinal payload that only the private registry can decode.

Each seat is bound to a canonical author but also linked to a list of **equivalents_by_shelf**.  Those equivalents act as interchangeable masks: a player may invoke “VOLTAIRE” or any of its equivalents—Swift, Twain, La Rochefoucauld, Bierce, Moliere—and the engine will accept the same structural constraints while allowing the authorial flavor to shift.  This flexibility lets the region support a wide range of cultural references without breaking the underlying engram logic.

The **interaction_edges** form the only glue between **riddler_engram** and the rest of the persistent world.  Edges such as `voltaire->shakespeare` or `tolkien<-pratchett` are pure metadata; they do not spawn new mechanics but provide narrative hooks that designers can reference when writing cross‑region quests.  Because the engine forbids adding new code, any cross‑region effect must be expressed through these edges, the shared **lens** system, or the agreed‑upon doctrines (e.g., the river‑ford loop, audit dungeon vault, and lens‑gate).

Steganographic doctrine is central to the region’s gameplay.  When a parable is published, the public beacon remains “Made with AI,” while the hidden payload—encoded via acrostics, footnote channels, invented etymologies, or structural rhyme—remains private.  The **stego_doctrine** mandates that the mask‑plus‑seed be logged in the registry after publishing; only then can the hidden message be extracted, ensuring that the mark outlives the session and resists statistical detectors.

The **summon_when** field for each seat dictates the narrative moment that triggers a successful summon.  For example, a VOLTAIRE parable must “sting politely,” while a TOLKIEN story must present “grief dressed as landscape.”  These conditions are enforced by the engine’s template validator, which checks that the submitted text satisfies the seat’s fingerprint and that the stego channel contains a valid payload hash.  Failure to meet the summon condition results in a silent rejection, preserving the mystery of the riddlers.

All ten seats share a common **license_note**: the underlying texts are drawn from public‑domain seeds pulled on 2026‑09‑14, and any new leaf must reference its **payload_hash** to prove compliance.  The **status** field confirms that as of that date the shelves and manifests are live, the optics dungeon is shipped, and only the wiring into `mag/engram.py` remains pending.  This means the region is fully functional for narrative creation, but the final integration step—live ray‑marching through the lens rooms—will be completed in the next build cycle.
