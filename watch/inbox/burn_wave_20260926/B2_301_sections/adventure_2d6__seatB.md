The 2d6 Adventure Stub region is a compact, self-contained test ground built on the 2d6-adventure-lite system. Its core resolution is a single 2d6 roll with a pass threshold of 7: any total of 7 or higher succeeds at ordinary tasks, while combat uses opposed 2d6 plus modifiers. The region is anchored by the Wanderer, a traveler with 10 HP, AC 12, attack bonus +1, and 1d6 damage, carrying a walking stick and waterskin. The Wanderer begins at the crossroads, the central hub from which all other rooms branch.

The crossroads is a Dusty Crossroads, marked by a post with carved notches that count journeys, not kills. It has four explicit exits: north to hill_shrine, east to market_ruin, west to river_ford, and a lantern exit to tavern_lantern (a cross-module connection). A riddle is scratched on the post: "what walks without feet when the map is true?" This room also serves as the entry point for the audit dungeon's git vault, which opens onto the crossroads per the agreed doctrine—a hidden or narrative exit that the build must respect.

hill_shrine is a broken stone ruin with a wind-blown bowl for coins long stolen. Its only exit is south back to crossroads. The shrine's hooks note that the notches on its stones match the crossroads post, implying the same counter has been here. This room is a quiet, atmospheric node, likely used for rest or lore, but it holds no encounter or mechanical challenge.

market_ruin is a collapsed market with one stall still shaded. It contains the Hungry Dog encounter: a 5 HP, AC 12 creature with attack bonus +2 and 1d4 damage. The dog guards a child's satchel, which is the primary hook. The room's only exit is west back to crossroads. The encounter is straightforward—combat or negotiation—but the satchel may hold items or clues for the wider world.

river_ford is a shallow crossing with stones placed for small feet. Its exits are east to crossroads and north to gallery, which is the water_computer.gallery per the loop-closes doctrine (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1 AGREE). The ford's hooks mention upstream smoke and downstream singing, suggesting environmental storytelling. The invariant explicitly states that river_ford loops to water_computer.gallery, so the build must ensure that connection is bidirectional or at least functional.

The region's event table, rest_safe, offers two outcomes when resting: "stars" (weight 3) and "dream" (weight 1). The stars event describes marking another notch on a stick; the dream event describes a post that writes itself. These reinforce the counting and journey themes. The region has no other event tables, so rest is the only random downtime mechanic.

Cross-region doctrine beyond the river_ford loop includes: the audit dungeon's git vault opens onto the crossroads (already noted); the audit doctrine library's ten lenses gate the optics garage at 10 of 10 collected (lens-count-gate AGREE)—this implies that the crossroads or another room may need to track lens collection, but the module itself does not define those lenses; and the fractal maze leaf gate entry needs the water computer's OUT height as its temperature (water-feeds-field AGREE). These are external dependencies that the build must honor, but they do not alter the internal room structure.

The region is intentionally minimal, serving as a stub for testing 2d6 mechanics and cross-module hooks. Its rooms are small, its encounter is singular, and its resolution is simple. The build document should treat this module as a baseline for how 2d6-adventure-lite handles exploration, combat, and rest, while ensuring the named exits and invariants are implemented exactly as specified.
