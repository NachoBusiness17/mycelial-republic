The 2d6 Adventure Stub (module_id `adventure_2d6_stub`) is a compact, non-5e generalization test region built on the `2d6-adventure-lite` system. Its start room is `crossroads`, where the player character `Wanderer` begins with 10 HP, AC 12, attack bonus +1, and 1d6 damage, carrying a walking stick and waterskin. All task resolution uses 2d6 with a pass threshold of 7; combat is opposed 2d6 plus modifiers. The region is deliberately sparse, relying on hooks and cross-region exits to weave into the larger persistent world.

`crossroads` is a dusty junction with four ways: north to `hill_shrine`, east to `market_ruin`, west to `river_ford`, and a lantern-lit path to `tavern_lantern` (an external module). A carved post counts journeys, not kills, and bears a riddle: "what walks without feet when the map is true?" This room is the hub for the region and also receives an external exit from the audit dungeon's git vault, which opens directly onto the crossroads, making it a natural meeting point for travelers from other modules.

`hill_shrine` lies north of the crossroads, a broken stone ruin with a wind-scoured bowl for coins long stolen. Its only exit is south back to `crossroads`. The shrine's notches match those on the crossroads post, implying the same unseen counter has marked both locations. This hook suggests a shared history or a hidden observer, but no further mechanics are defined within this module.

`market_ruin` is east of the crossroads, a collapsed market with one stall still shaded. It contains a scripted encounter: the `Hungry Dog` (5 HP, AC 12, attack +2, 1d4 damage) guards a child's satchel. The dog is not hostile by default but will defend the satchel if approached. The only exit is west back to `crossroads`. This room offers a simple combat or roleplay challenge, and the satchel may hold a clue or item for the wider world.

`river_ford` is west of the crossroads, a shallow crossing with stones placed for small feet. Its exits are east back to `crossroads` and north to `gallery` (the water computer's gallery, per the agreed doctrine). The north bank features a glass door leading into a keep of clear pipes, an impossible sight that bridges into the water-computer module. Hooks mention upstream smoke and downstream singing. The invariant `loop-closes (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1: adventure_2d6.river_ford loops to water_computer.gallery - AGREE)` confirms this connection is canonical.

The region's rest mechanic is defined by the `rest_safe` event table: on a safe rest, the Wanderer may roll on a weighted table (3:1) to see either "Stars wheel. You mark another notch on a stick." or "You dream of a post that writes itself." These events reinforce the counting/notch motif and provide a light narrative texture without altering mechanics.

Beyond the internal rooms, the region participates in several cross-region gates. The audit dungeon's git vault opens onto `crossroads`, making it a direct entry point from that module. The audit doctrine library's ten lenses gate the optics garage at 10 of 10 collected, a condition that may be triggered from this region if the Wanderer carries lenses. The fractal maze leaf gate entry requires the water computer's OUT height as its temperature, a value that can be obtained from the `gallery` reachable via `river_ford`. These connections ensure the 2d6 stub is not isolated but a functional node in the combined world, with its simple rooms and hooks serving as a low-stakes entry for players.
