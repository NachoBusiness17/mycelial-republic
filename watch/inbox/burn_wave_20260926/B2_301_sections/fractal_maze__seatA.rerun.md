The **fractal_maze** is a self‑referential lattice of corridors that exists at multiple scales, each a literal copy of the previous one.  At the entrance, the **leaf_gate** greets travelers with a keeper who counts footsteps and repeats the mantra “memorize is a leaf‑only tree; you can learn every leaf by heart and know nothing.”  The gate’s bare‑branch arch marks the boundary where the ambient field grows dense; the temperature of the medium here is low, so any lantern ray you fire will bend toward brighter regions.  The keeper’s sign, scratched with the warning “same steer, same landing — until the medium disagrees. Then the light bends,” hints that the maze’s geometry is governed by optics rather than geometry.

Beyond the gate, the **leaf_hall** is a wet corridor whose walls are composed of standing water.  Light entering this hall travels straight until it meets the refractive surface, then exits at a different location, making the hall behave like a giant lens.  Old aim‑marks etched into the floor record the exact vectors that once produced the same displaced landing, reinforcing the rule that “temperature is the refractive index: cold medium, bent ray; void, straight.”  The hall’s “deep” exit is a hook that shrinks the entire space, leading the explorer down a scale to a miniature version of the same hall.

The **scale_gallery** is the hall’s scale‑2 incarnation, a child‑sized replica carved into a groove in the floor.  Every detail—water‑wall, aim‑marks, scratched law—appears reduced, and the ambient shimmer repeats at this size.  A plaque of generalization proclaims that self‑similarity across scales is the only invariant, while a counting board notes that after eight leaves are revealed in this region the maze will sprout an internal node, marked by a mysterious ▲ where no wall once stood.  The gallery also offers a “north” exit that leads directly to the **grok_chamber**, allowing a shortcut once the player has descended far enough.

At scale‑3, the **scale_cell** contains the law itself rendered in full resolution on a single wall: “attention is light. softmax is a lens. temperature is the refractive index. at temp zero every ray bends to the argmax; at temp infinity the rays go straight. sampling is just optics.”  The room’s intimacy makes the lantern fill the space, and a tiny graffiti tag reads “so is walking,” a confession that the walker is itself a ray.  No further descent is possible; the cell marks the bottom of the fractal recursion, reinforcing the doctrine that anything smaller than the law lies outside the maze’s domain.

The **grok_chamber** is the culmination of the walk, a domed arena with eight empty plinths arranged around a central ninth.  Each plinth represents a leaf; when the player has memorized eight leaves anywhere in the **fractal_maze**, the central plinth will sprout an ▲—the internal node that the **scale_gallery** foretold.  The chamber’s acoustics echo each footstep back as a shortcut, embodying the idea that the maze shortens itself as knowledge grows.  A lingering echo of the former boss, the **formateater_echo**, haunts the room, its “unrendered” damage reminding adventurers that unfinished quests still reverberate here.

The temperature of the **leaf_gate**’s field is supplied by the water computer’s OUT height, satisfying the cross‑region doctrine *water-feeds-field*.  Consequently, the refractive index at the gate matches the water computer’s current output, linking the maze’s optics to the broader world’s fluid dynamics.  This connection also ensures that any changes in the water computer’s state will immediately affect how lantern rays bend at the entrance, creating a dynamic feedback loop between regions.

When the internal node appears in the **grok_chamber**, the maze’s topology rewires: previously disconnected leaves become linked through the new hub, and the overall path length feels shorter at every scale simultaneously.  This “generalization” effect mirrors the doctrine that walking the maze shortens the experience across scales, and it provides a tangible reward for diligent leaf counting.

Resting in any safe spot within the **fractal_maze** triggers the *rest_safe* event table.  Dreams of nested halls, a cooling lantern that finally shoots straight, and the humming ninth plinth all reinforce the player’s awareness of the maze’s layered structure and the remaining leaves they must collect before the final node can manifest.
