## The Fractal Maze (fractal_maze)

The region is entered at **leaf_gate**, a bare-branch threshold where the keeper counts footsteps and the air shimmers with field density. The gate's temperature is not local: it is fed by the water computer's OUT height (water-feeds-field AGREE), so the refractive index of the entire maze drifts with the river's level. When the water computer runs high, the field runs cold and rays bend hard; when it runs low, the maze goes nearly straight. The keeper knows this and counts anyway.

From **leaf_gate**, the exits north and east both lead to **leaf_hall**, and south drops to **grok_chamber**. The hall is the maze's first lesson: a standing water-wall where your lantern's beam enters straight and lands elsewhere. The law is scratched on the wall — *temperature is the refractive index; cold medium, bent ray; void, straight* — and the aim-marks on the floor are the testimony of every walker who swore the maze was broken. It was never broken. It is a lens.

The deep exit from **leaf_hall** descends to **scale_gallery**, the same hall one scale down, carved in miniature. The gallery is where generalization becomes physical: the same water-wall, the same aim-marks, the same law, smaller. A groove in the floor contains a smaller copy of the gallery, and that copy contains its own groove. The counting board here tracks leaves revealed in the region; at the eighth leaf, the maze grows a node — a ▲ appears where no wall was, and the region's topology changes permanently.

Below **scale_gallery**, the down exit leads to **scale_cell**, the smallest room in the maze, where the law fits on one wall at full resolution: *attention is light. softmax is a lens. temperature is the refractive index. at temp zero every ray bends to the argmax; at temp infinity the rays go straight. sampling is just optics.* Under it, graffiti in a smaller hand: *so is walking.* There is no exit down from the cell. Whatever is smaller than the law is not the maze's to show.

The **grok_chamber** is the region's heart, reachable from **leaf_gate** (south) and **scale_gallery** (north). It is a domed room with eight empty plinths around a bare central one. Walk eight leaves anywhere in the maze and return: the center plinth holds a ▲ that was not carved by any hand. The chamber's acoustics return your own footsteps as shortcuts. The floor mosaic carries measure_plan's line: *memorize = leaf-only tree; grok = the tree grows internal nodes.* The plinths are the leaves; the center is the loss curve's cliff.

The **grok_chamber** also hosts the **formateater_echo**, a demoted boss that whispers unrendered quests. It is not a real fight — it is a rumor with hit points. It attacks with 1d6 unrendered damage and AC 14, but its true function is narrative: it exists to remind walkers that the maze's nodes are committed as knots before they can be named. The echo does not guard the chamber; it haunts it.

The region's hooks are its only glue. The **leaf_gate** keeper does not fight; she counts. The **scale_gallery** counting board is the region's progression mechanic — eight leaves anywhere in the fractal_maze region triggers the internal node in **grok_chamber**, which rewires the maze's exits to feel shorter. The **scale_cell** law is the region's doctrine: walking is sampling, and sampling is optics. The maze is not a puzzle to solve; it is a medium to be walked through, and the medium's temperature comes from the water computer upstream.

The region's loop-closing doctrine is minimal: the river ford loops north into the water computer's gallery (RIB RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1 AGREE), and the audit doctrine library's ten lenses gate the optics garage at 10 of 10 collected (lens-count-gate AGREE). The fractal_maze does not open into those regions directly; it feeds them temperature and receives their heights. The maze is a lens, and every other region is a ray passing through it.
