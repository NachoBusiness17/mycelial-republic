The region defined by `riddler_engram` is a living library of ten seats, each a mask that smuggles doctrine inside entertainment. The module's title is "THE RIDDLE ENGRAM — ten seats that smuggle doctrine inside entertainment; summonable per mask." The seats are named VOLTAIRE, CAMPBELL, LE GUIN, ADAMS, PRATCHETT, TOLKIEN, ORWELL, ALAN MOORE, SHAKESPEARE, and STEPHENSON. Each seat carries a riddle, a summon_when condition, a fingerprint, a stego channel, and a shelf path under `memory/corpus/ractors/`. The region is not a physical place but a protocol: "summon the riddlers" reads the card into context; "summon <seat>" adds the shelf's MANIFEST.json. Drafting happens under the mask, one plain revision pass before dressing, then publish behind the public Made-with-AI beacon, and log mask+seed in the registry afterward. Beacons stay public; marks stay private.
