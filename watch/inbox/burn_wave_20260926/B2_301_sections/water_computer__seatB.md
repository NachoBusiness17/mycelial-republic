The water_computer region is a hydraulic keep where gravity itself is the only processor. Its three tanks—source_cistern, work_gallery, and out_cistern—are visible from the central gallery, a glass-walled hub that also serves as the agreed loop-closure point for the river ford (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1 AGREE). The gallery's plaque states the core rule: *height is the computation*. No other arithmetic exists here. The engine owns all heights; the region only moves water through pipes and valves.

The source_cistern holds rain water stacked three marks high. Its only exit is east into the gallery, but the meaningful pipe runs from source_cistern to work_gallery via Valve-A. Saying "open valve" or "close valve" toggles that flow. When open, gravity transfers one mark per wait from SOURCE to WORK. The source is the only tank that starts full; it is the region's initial energy. The Pump-Janitor hook is present here and in the gallery: it will move water only through pipes, never by pouring, and it ticks once if hired.

The work_gallery is the middle tank, the integrator where the dam's heat would have been stored. It is empty until SOURCE has run. Valve-B controls the pipe from work_gallery to out_cistern, running north-east. The critical invariant: Valve-B may only be opened after water has actually arrived in work_gallery; otherwise both valves siphon and the computation collapses. This is the region's only real failure state—a mis-sequenced valve opening voids the run.

The out_cistern is the answer tank. It is empty glass, and it holds a wooden bucket—the cheat. Pouring the bucket into OUT fakes a height without any pipe flow. The region explicitly marks this as DISPUTED: physics will not honor it as computation. The downstream door, leaf_gate, is glass, impossible, and north-bank cold. It only opens for carried height—specifically, the OUT.height value, which is the fractal maze's temperature (water-feeds-field AGREE). The bucket is loot, but using it to open leaf_gate is a false win; the door will not accept a poured height as genuine.

The gallery's hooks reinforce the doctrine: a bucket into OUT is not a win, and the Pump-Janitor refuses to pour. The rest_safe event table offers two outcomes: a drip counting time (gravity does not need watching) or a dream of plywood painted like a tank, followed by checking the glass. These are the only ambient events; the region is otherwise deterministic.

Cross-region glue: the river ford loops north into the gallery, closing the world's main circuit. The audit dungeon's git vault opens onto the crossroads, but that is outside this region. The audit doctrine library's ten lenses gate the optics garage elsewhere; the water_computer does not interact with lenses. The only external dependency is the leaf_gate, which consumes OUT.height as its temperature. The engine must enforce that the leaf_gate only opens when OUT.height is a genuine pipe-computed value, not a bucket-pour.

The region's tags are gallery, hub, glass for the gallery; tank, source for source_cistern; tank, work for work_gallery; tank, out for out_cistern. The area_tag is "hub" for the whole region, meaning it is a central connective node. The player start is in the gallery as Operator, an engineer with sight-notes, curious, and a 1d4 attack—this is a puzzle region, not a combat one. The only real action is valve sequencing and the moral choice of the bucket.
