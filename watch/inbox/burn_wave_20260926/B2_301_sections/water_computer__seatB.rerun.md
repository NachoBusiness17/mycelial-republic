The water_computer region is a hydraulic keep where gravity itself performs computation. Its three tanks—source_cistern, work_gallery, and out_cistern—are visible from the central gallery, which is the hub and the only room with exits to all three. The gallery's plaque states the core rule: *height is the computation*. The region's entire puzzle loop is about moving water from source to work to out, and then using that height to open the leaf_gate downstream. The engine owns all heights; no manual override is permitted except through the explicit cheat of the bucket.

The source_cistern begins full to three marks. Its only exit is east into gallery, but the pipe from source_cistern to work_gallery is controlled by Valve-A. Opening Valve-A lets one mark of water flow per wait, draining source_cistern and filling work_gallery. The work_gallery is empty until that happens. Its pipe to out_cistern is controlled by Valve-B, but opening Valve-B before water has arrived in work_gallery causes a siphon that drains both tanks without producing output. The correct sequence is: open Valve-A, wait for water to arrive, then open Valve-B. The out_cistern then fills, and its height becomes the temperature for the leaf_gate entry.

The out_cistern contains a wooden bucket as loot. Pouring the bucket into out_cistern fakes a height without any pipe flow. This is explicitly not computation—the engine will mark the resulting height as DISPUTED, and the leaf_gate will not open for a disputed height. The bucket is a trap for the impatient. The downstream exit from out_cistern leads to leaf_gate, which is gated by the water-feeds-field invariant (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1 AGREE): the OUT.height value is the fractal leaf_gate entry temperature. Only a genuine, pipe-computed height satisfies the gate.

The region's only NPC is the Pump-Janitor, who appears in gallery and source_cistern hooks. He moves water but refuses to pour. Hiring him (likely via a command) causes him to tick once—meaning he will open or close a valve on your behalf, but he will not fill OUT from a bucket. He is a convenience for players who understand the sequence but want to avoid typing valve commands repeatedly. He does not alter the physics.

The rest_safe event table provides two rest outcomes: a drip counting time (neutral) and a dream of plywood tanks (a hint that the bucket is a lie). These reinforce the theme of honest glass and gravity as the only computer. The region is cool, dripping, and honest—no hidden levers, no magical pumps. The only cheat is the bucket, and it is a dead end.
