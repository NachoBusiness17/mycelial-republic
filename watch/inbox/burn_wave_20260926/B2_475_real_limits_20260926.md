## What the 50-wide question is, answered from source

There is no client-side throttle on model requests to stay under: the only client concurrency knob is tool-call concurrency (10). The 50-wide cap lives server-side and reaches the client as business codes: 1302 (rate limited, RETRYABLE - absorb with the 2s/4s/8s.../60s jittered ladder, 11 attempts, which is why the estate's waves show 429 x N fully absorbed) versus 3008/3009/3010 (concurrency cap for the tier, NOT retried by design - the client is expected to reduce parallelism or upgrade, never to hammer). Estate translation: our fan-out width law (max_concurrency <= 3 on this plan) is stricter than the client requires and correct for quota hygiene; the absorbed-1302 storms cost nothing but wall time, while a 3008-class response would be a hard stop the harness will not retry away.
