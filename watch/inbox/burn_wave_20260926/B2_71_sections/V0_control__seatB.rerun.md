The Dusty Crossroads is the absolute hub, a four-way junction with a post carved with notches that count journeys, not kills. Exits lead north to the Hill Shrine, east to the Market Ruin, west to the River Ford, and along a lantern-lit path to the Tavern Lantern. The riddle scratched on the post is the region's central hook, and the notches themselves are the only clue—they are a record of passage, not of violence.

The Hill Shrine is a broken circle of stones, wind-blasted, with a bowl for coins long stolen. The notches on its stones match the crossroads post exactly, confirming the same counter has been here. It is a place of quiet and absence, where the only event is the wind and the memory of offerings, and it loops back south to the crossroads.

The Market Ruin is a collapsed bazaar where one stall still stands, shaded, and is guarded by a Hungry Dog (HP 5, AC 12, attack +2, 1d4). The dog guards a child's satchel, a hook that implies a story of loss or protection. The dog is not hostile by default, but its loyalty suggests the satchel holds something precious or personal, and the encounter is a test of approach rather than pure combat.
