The game resolves all checks with **2d6**; a total of **7 or higher** is a success on ordinary tasks, while combat uses opposed rolls of **2d6 + modifier**.

**Dusty Crossroads** – the starting hub. Four ways lead out: north to **Hill Shrine**, east to **Market Ruin**, west to **River Ford**, and the lantern‑lit path to the **Tavern Lantern** (a non‑module exit). A carved post bears a riddle and a series of notches that count journeys.

**Hill Shrine** – a wind‑blown stone altar with a shallow bowl for coins. The only exit returns south to the Crossroads, and the notches on its own counter match those on the crossroads post, hinting at a shared tally.

**Market Ruin** – the remnants of a once‑bustling bazaar, with one shaded stall still standing. The westward exit leads back to the Crossroads. A hungry stray dog guards a child’s satchel; defeating or befriending it may yield a small reward.

**River Ford** – shallow water crossed by stepping stones, with a mysterious glass door on the north bank that seems to open into a keep of clear pipes. Exits lead east back to the Crossroads and north into the **Gallery** of the water‑computer region.

**Cross‑Region Loop Invariant** – the north exit from River Ford closes the world loop by connecting to the water‑computer’s **Gallery** (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1: adventure_2d6.river_ford loops to water_computer.gallery – AGREE).

A faint wind carries the scent of distant incense, as if the notches themselves whisper of journeys yet taken.
