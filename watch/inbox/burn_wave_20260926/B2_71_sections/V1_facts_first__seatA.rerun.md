**Resolution** – All checks use **2d6**; a total of **7 or higher** succeeds on ordinary tasks. Combat is resolved as opposed **2d6 + modifier** rolls.

**Crossroads** – The Dusty Crossroads is a four‑way junction. Exits lead **north** to *Hill Shrine*, **east** to *Market Ruin*, **west** to *River Ford*, and a hidden **lantern** path to *Tavern Lantern*.

**Hill Shrine** – At the hill’s broken altar the wind whistles through scattered stones. The only exit is **south**, returning to the *Crossroads*; the notches on the shrine’s bowl echo those on the crossroads post.

**Market Ruin** – Among the collapsed stalls a single shaded stall remains. A **Hungry Dog** (5 HP, +2 attack, 1d4 damage) guards a child’s satchel. The sole exit is **west**, back to the *Crossroads*.

**River Ford** – Shallow water crosses here, with stepping stones and a mysterious glass door on the north bank that leads into a keep of clear pipes. Exits are **east** to the *Crossroads* and **north** into the *Gallery*.

**Cross‑region Loop Invariant** – The north exit from River Ford **loops** into the Water Computer’s *Gallery* (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1: adventure_2d6.river_ford ↔ water_computer.gallery – AGREE), and the Audit Dungeon’s Git Vault opens onto the *Crossroads*.
