All checks in this region use 2d6. A total of 7 or higher succeeds at ordinary tasks; combat is opposed 2d6 + modifiers, with the higher total winning the exchange. The Wanderer starts with 10 HP, AC 12, attack bonus +1, and 1d6 damage.

The region opens at the Dusty Crossroads, a four-way post with carved notches. Exits: north to Hill Shrine, east to Market Ruin, west to River Ford, and a lantern-lit path to Tavern Lantern (external). A riddle is scratched on the post.

Hill Shrine: broken stones and a stolen coin bowl. Exits: south back to Crossroads. The notches on its stones match the crossroads post, implying a single counter.

Market Ruin: collapsed stalls, one still shaded. Exits: west to Crossroads. A Hungry Dog (HP 5, AC 12, attack +2, 1d4 damage) guards a child's satchel here.

River Ford: shallow water with stones placed for small feet. Exits: east to Crossroads, north to Gallery (external water computer). Upstream smoke, downstream singing.

The River Ford's north exit loops into the water computer's gallery, and the audit dungeon's git vault opens onto the Crossroads, closing the world's geography.

The post counts journeys, not kills.
