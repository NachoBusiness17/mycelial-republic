Resolution uses 2d6. A total of 7 or higher succeeds at ordinary tasks; combat is opposed 2d6 + modifiers, with the higher total winning and damage applied as listed.

**Dusty Crossroads** — the start. Exits: north to Hill Shrine, east to Market Ruin, west to River Ford, and a lantern-lit path (lantern) to the Tavern Lantern (external module). A post with carved notches bears a riddle: what walks without feet when the map is true?

**Hill Shrine** — broken stones, wind, a bowl for coins long stolen. Exits: south back to Crossroads. The notches on the shrine match the crossroads post, implying a single counter.

**Market Ruin** — collapsed stalls, one still shaded. Exits: west to Crossroads. A hungry dog (AC 12, HP 5, attack +2, 1d4 damage) guards a child's satchel.

**River Ford** — shallow water, stones placed for small feet. Exits: east to Crossroads, north to the Gallery (water computer). Upstream smoke, downstream singing.

Cross-region loop invariant: The river ford's north exit loops into the water computer's gallery (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1: adventure_2d6.river_ford loops to water_computer.gallery - AGREE). Additionally, the audit dungeon's git vault opens onto the crossroads, closing a larger loop.

The post counts journeys, not kills.
