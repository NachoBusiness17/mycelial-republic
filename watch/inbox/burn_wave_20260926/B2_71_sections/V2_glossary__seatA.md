The **crossroads** is the hub of this slice of the world, a dusty intersection marked by a solitary post whose carved notches count journeys rather than kills. Four ways branch out from here, each leading to a distinct locale: north to **hill_shrine**, east to **market_ruin**, west to **river_ford**, and a lantern‑lit path to the **tavern_lantern** (the latter defined elsewhere). The post bears a riddle: “what walks without feet when the map is true?” – a hook that invites the **Wanderer** to contemplate movement beyond physical steps.

To the north lies **hill_shrine**, a ruin of broken stones buffeted by wind. A shallow bowl sits empty, its coin‑collecting purpose long abandoned. The only exit returns south to the **crossroads**, reinforcing the shrine’s role as a contemplative waypoint rather than a destination. The notches on the shrine’s remnants echo those on the crossroads post, hinting that the same counter tracks both sites and encouraging the **Wanderer** to compare the two markings.

Eastward the **market_ruin** presents a collapsed bazaar where one shaded stall still clings to ruin. Within this stall the **Hungry Dog** encounter awaits: a gaunt canine with 5 hp, 12 ac, a +2 attack bonus, and 1d4 damage. The dog guards a child’s satchel, offering a potential reward for a successful 2d6 combat resolution (opposed 2d6 + mod) or a clever non‑violent solution. The satchel’s contents are left to the **Wanderer**’s imagination, but the hook “The dog guards a child's satchel” suggests a narrative thread to follow.

West of the hub, the **river_ford** offers shallow water and stepping stones for small feet. Its description mentions a glass door on the north bank that leads into a keep of clear pipes – an impossible portal that foreshadows the loop‑closure doctrine. Exits from the ford lead east back to the **crossroads** and north into **gallery**, the water computer’s domain, as affirmed by the invariant “loop‑closes (RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1: adventure_2d6.river_ford loops to water_computer.gallery - AGREE)”. Upstream smoke and downstream singing provide atmospheric hooks for exploration.

The **gallery** itself is not defined in this module but is linked via the loop‑closure, meaning that travel north from **river_ford** drops the **Wanderer** into the water computer’s realm. This cross‑region connection must be respected by any engine handling exits: the north exit of **river_ford** should resolve to the external room identifier “gallery” without creating duplicate rooms. The loop ensures continuity between the adventure‑lite system and the broader water‑computer world.

Resting safely in any of these locations can trigger the **rest_safe** event table. Rolling 2d6 with a pass‑on threshold of 7 yields outcomes such as “Stars wheel. You mark another notch on a stick.” (weight 3) or the rarer “You dream of a post that writes itself.” (weight 1). These events reinforce the motif of notches and posts, tying the **Wanderer**’s personal journey to the physical counters scattered across the **crossroads**, **hill_shrine**, and beyond.
