A weather‑worn post stands in the centre, its notches worn smooth by countless travelers who have counted journeys rather than kills. The post bears a riddle: “what walks without feet when the map is true?” – a hook that invites the **Wanderer** to contemplate the nature of paths and perhaps discover a hidden mechanic tied to the notches. Exits lead north to **hill_shrine**, east to **market_ruin**, west to **river_ford**, and a lantern‑lit doorway to the **tavern_lantern** (the latter defined elsewhere).

The ruin’s only exit returns west to **crossroads**, making it a concise combat vignette.
