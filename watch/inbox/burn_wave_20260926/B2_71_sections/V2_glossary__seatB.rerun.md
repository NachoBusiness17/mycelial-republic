The 2d6 Adventure Stub region is anchored by the Dusty Crossroads, a four-way junction where a notched post records journeys rather than kills. This module, built for the 2d6-adventure-lite system, uses a resolution mechanic where any 2d6 total of 7 or higher succeeds at ordinary tasks, while combat is opposed 2d6 plus modifiers. The Wanderer begins here, a curious traveler carrying a walking stick and a waterskin, with 10 hit points, AC 12, and a +1 attack bonus dealing 1d6 damage.

The crossroads itself is a sparse, wind-scoured place. Its exits lead north to hill_shrine, east to market_ruin, west to river_ford, and a lantern-lit path to tavern_lantern (a connection beyond this module's immediate rooms). A riddle is scratched on the post: "what walks without feet when the map is true?" This hook ties to the region's theme of counting paths and the unseen logic of the world.

North, the hill_shrine is a broken stone circle with a bowl for coins long stolen. Its only exit returns south to the crossroads. The notches on its stones match those on the crossroads post, suggesting the same hand—or the same counter—has marked both places. The shrine offers no encounter, but its silence and symmetry reinforce the sense of a deliberate, hidden pattern.

East, the market_ruin is a collapsed market square where one stall still stands in shade. Here the Hungry Dog guards a child's satchel. The dog has 5 hit points, AC 12, a +2 attack bonus, and deals 1d4 damage. It is not hostile by default but will defend the satchel if approached. The satchel's contents are unspecified, but the hook implies a story of loss and survival.

West, the river_ford is a shallow crossing with stones placed for small feet. Its east exit returns to the crossroads, but its north exit leads to a glass door—impossible, yet present—opening into a keep of clear pipes. This is the agreed loop-closes invariant: river_ford loops north into water_computer.gallery, as per RIB_CATHEDRAL_WORLD_V1. Upstream, smoke rises; downstream, singing can be heard. The ford is a threshold between the mundane and the strange.

The region's rest_safe event table offers two outcomes when the Wanderer rests: the common "stars" (weight 3) where stars wheel and the traveler marks another notch on a stick, and the rare "dream" (weight 1) of a post that writes itself. These events reinforce the motif of counting and self-inscription, hinting that the world itself is keeping score.

Cross-region doctrine confirms that the audit dungeon's git vault opens onto the crossroads, providing an additional entrance from another module. This means the crossroads is not only a hub for this region but a nexus for the larger persistent world. The river_ford's glass door is the only other known inter-module link, connecting to the water computer's gallery.

The entire region is a study in quiet mystery: a post that counts journeys, a shrine that mirrors it, a dog guarding a child's past, and a river that leads to impossible architecture. The 2d6 system keeps resolution simple, but the hooks invite deeper investigation. The Wanderer's starting inventory—a walking stick and waterskin—suggests a traveler prepared for the road, not for combat, but the Hungry Dog and the unknown beyond the glass door may test that readiness.
