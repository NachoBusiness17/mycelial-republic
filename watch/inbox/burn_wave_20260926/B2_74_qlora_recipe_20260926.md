## Evidence

The recipe, keyed to the quoted sources.

1. Method: QLoRA/LoRA adapters, not full SFT. arXiv 2511.00130 (Bohnet et al., Google): "LoRA provides the most effective balance, successfully instilling new skills with minimal impact on the base model's general knowledge" while "SFT excels at skill acquisition, [but] it is highly susceptible to catastrophic forgetting". Unsloth Hyperparameters Guide: "LoRA uses 16-bit precision, while QLoRA is a 4-bit fine-tuning method", QLoRA "reducing VRAM usage by over 75%" (16-bit LoRA "slightly more accurate and faster" if VRAM allows; on 12GB the 4-bit path is the default).

2. Learning rate. Unsloth Hyperparameters Guide: LoRA/QLoRA start "2e-4 (0.0002) to 5e-6 (0.000005)"; start 2e-4, "Lower for slower but more precise fine-tuning. Try values like 1e-4, 5e-5, or 2e-5" (Fine-tuning Guide). Disclosed regime difference: 2511.00130 ran LoRA at "a learning rate of 0.005" with rank 4 on Gemma 4B for tiny corpora (8 to 8192 samples), an order of magnitude hotter; treat 2e-4 as the vendor default, sweep down before up.

3. Rank and alpha. Unsloth Hyperparameters Guide: rank values "8, 16, 32, 64, 128", "Choose 16 or 32"; "Using a rank that is too large can cause overfitting and harm your model's quality"; "it usually is between 4 and 64"; "It's best to set lora_alpha = 2 * lora_rank or lora_alpha = lora_rank". arXiv 2511.00130: "diminishing returns for higher ranks (e.g., r > 8 for 128 samples)" and its sweep starts at "the standard settings (e.g. learning rate 0.001, LoRA rank 4)". For 10K parts: start r=16, alpha=32; if validation stalls, the papers' direction says sweep DOWN capacity before up.

4. Epochs and overfit signals. Unsloth: "num_train_epochs = 1 (1-3 epochs recommended to avoid overfitting)"; "If your training loss drops below 0.2, your model is likely overfitting" and "If the loss goes to 0, that could mean overfitting, so it's important to check validation too"; a final loss "around 0.5 to 1.0 is a good sign".

5. Batch and memory. Unsloth: "per_device_train_batch_size = 2", "gradient_accumulation_steps = 8" with Effective Batch Size "Recommended: 16"; on OOM "always prefer to set a smaller batch_size and increase gradient_accumulation_steps"; use_gradient_checkpointing "unsloth" "saves ~30% memory"; "max_seq_length = 2048 ... we recommend 2048 for testing" (raise only if the reasoning parts need it; Llama-3 supports 8192).

6. Packing. ACL 2025 Findings 256 (Wang et al., "Packing Analysis: Packing Is More Appropriate for Large Models or Datasets in Supervised Fine-tuning"): packing is "an optimization technique designed to maximize hardware resource efficiency by combining different training sequences", and the study's scope is "SFT datasets ranging from 69K to 1.2M and models from 8B to 70B". INFERENCE LABELED AS INFERENCE, not a quote: a 10K-part corpus sits an order of magnitude below the smallest studied dataset, so the conservative 2026 posture for this corpus is packing OFF (or masked packing) until a run proves otherwise; the abstract itself does not test 10K.

7. Forgetting mitigation. arXiv 2410.21228 (Shuttleworth, Andreas, Torralba, Sharma): LoRA weight matrices grow "new, high-ranking singular vectors, which we call 'intruder dimensions'"; forgetting is "vastly localized to the intruder dimension"; scaling those singular values down improves pre-training distribution modeling "with a minimal drop in downstream task performance". Practical read: keep rank low and monitor the base model, because the damage concentrates in identifiable directions.

8. Eval-before/after design. Unsloth: "take 20% of your training data and use that for testing", "evaluation_steps = 100", early stop on validation loss; plus a held-out GENERAL capability probe per epoch so skill gain cannot silently pay for base-model loss (motivated by 2410.21228 and 2511.00130's "minimal impact on the base model's general knowledge" metric, not by any benchmark list those pages name).

9. Scheduler and modules. Unsloth Hyperparameters Guide: scheduler "linear or cosine"; warmup "5-10% of total steps"; weight decay "0.01 (recommended) - 0.1"; target "all major linear layers: q_proj, k_proj, v_proj, o_proj, gate_proj, up_proj, down_proj", QLoRA-All "performs best overall".

Rocinante fit (Oct 27 deadline): 10K reasoning parts lands at the top of the regime 2511.00130 actually measured (validation peaked at "8192 training samples and 6k training steps"), so the paper's data-scarce conclusions are in-range, not extrapolated, for this corpus.
