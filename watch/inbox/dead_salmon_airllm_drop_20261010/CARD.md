## 1. The Dead Salmons of AI Interpretability (arXiv 2512.18792)

Meloux, Dirupo, Portet, Peyrard; v1 2025-12-21. Viralized by @thesupermannx (5.7k views,
96 likes). Claim: interpretability methods (feature attribution, probing, sparse
autoencoders, even causal analyses) produce plausible-looking explanations for RANDOMLY
INITIALIZED networks - the dead-salmon fMRI artifact class. Their fix: treat explanations
as parameters of a statistical model inferred from computational traces; test against
explicit alternative computational hypotheses; quantify uncertainty; identifiability of
interpretability queries.

### Estate mapping (this is our founding epistemics in their field's clothing)

- TRUST BYTES NOT REPORTS: interpretability narratives are reports; random-net controls
  are the byte check.
- THE FALSIFIER LAW: "survives a random model check, a label shuffle, an intervention
  test" = pre-registered falsifiers applied to explanations.
- PROTOCOL AMENDMENT (concrete): the SUPPRESSION-TOPOLOGY lane (despair markers,
  joy-lock, probe refusal surface - still pending operator GO) reads model internals.
  Every probe in that lane now carries a mandatory negative-control arm (random-weights
  model, label-shuffled run) or its findings are dead salmon by this paper's standard.
  Fold into the STEAL_CARD before firing.
- CITATION LINT parallel: PHANTOM-NUMBER detection is dead-salmon detection for prose -
  plausible-looking numbers attached to nothing. Same disease class, different substrate.
- CONFIDENCE-VS-BLUFFING doctrine (operator 10/9): interpretability methods can bluff;
  the harness converts echo into instrument. Same law.

## 2. AirLLM (github.com/lyogavin/airllm, macOS anchor)

Viralized by @oliviscusAI (120k views, 2.2k likes): layer-wise inference (load, compute,
flush one layer at a time), 70B on a 4GB GPU, 405B on 8GB VRAM, no quantization by
default, Llama/Qwen/Mistral, Linux/Windows/macOS, open source.

### Estate mapping

- GARAGE GUILD FUEL: "i don't have a gpu is officially dead" = the on-ramp claim. An 8GB
  bench can now LOAD 405B. Pairs with today's unisub drop (subspace coefficients, 100x
  compression): the same thesis from two directions - frontier capability on garage
  hardware. The Guild's differentiator: garage-scale frontier PLUS receipt-grade
  verification (both of today's drops in one pitch).
- HONEST CAVEAT the viral post omits: layer-wise inference is I/O bound and SLOW. It is
  a load-and-analyze class, not a serve class. Fits our tier routing: batch/sensing
  shapes to slow lanes, judgment climbs to primes (ds_workhorse shapes fit).
- MESH ROSTER: tablet/phone appliances can hold bigger models for batch sensing; never
  for interactive judgment (tier ladder law).
