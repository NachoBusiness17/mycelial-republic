4.2 Optimizing Prompt Structure for Cache Efficiency
Cache efficiency in game prompting can be significantly improved through careful attention to how game elements are ordered and structured within prompts:

Most-Variable Components First: Place elements that change most frequently between generations (like specific rules or themes) at the beginning of prompts, while stable elements (like basic game mechanics) should come later

Parameter Separation: Clearly separate the game's core mechanics from its thematic elements, cultural adaptations, and implementation details
