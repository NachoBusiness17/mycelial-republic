== Overview ==
The '''AFK World''' is an alternate place, similar to the Pro Servers, where you can go '''A.F.K. (Away From Keyboard)''' and still earn '''rewards'''.

It takes '''15 minutes''' to get a '''Grand Prize''' and '''5 minutes''' to get a '''Cash Prize''' which is '''{{Yen|Amount=250}}'''.

Every '''10 seconds''', your Roblox Avatar kicks the ball and misses. When you get a '''Grand Prize''', your Roblox Avatar kicks the ball into the goal and scores, but If the reward is a '''Cash Prize,''' you will still miss.
