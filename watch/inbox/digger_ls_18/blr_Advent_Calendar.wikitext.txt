The '''Advent Calendar''' was introduced during the '''[[Updates|''SnowLock [Christmas] Week 1'' update]]''', specifically on the 29th of November. Visually, it is a calendar with 25 places for 25 rewards, with day 25 being the biggest on the calendar. It will be around until the end Of the ''SnowLock'' update. 

== Rewards 🎁 ==
Rewards were given out daily (except on the day it was released) to players who signed up to the calendar. On this table, there are most of the rewards that were given out. Currently, there are rewards up to Day 25.
