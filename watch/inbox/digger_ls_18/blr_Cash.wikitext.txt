== <span style="color:#39FF14; text-shadow:0 0 5px #39FF14;">Overview</span> ==

'''Cash''' is the main currency in '''Blue Lock: Rivals'''. The icon of Cash is the Japanese Yen <span style="color:#39FF14; font-weight:bold;">(¥)</span> symbol.

Cash can be used to purchase:

<ul style="list-style-type:square; color:#00ffff; text-shadow: 0 0 5px #00ffff;">
  <li>Style Spins</li>
  <li>Flow Spins</li>
  <li>Emote Packs</li>
  <li>Player Card Packs</li>
  <li>Goal Effect Packs</li>
</ul>

== <span style="color:#39FF14; text-shadow:0 0 5px #39FF14;">'''Best Methods for Getting CASH'''</span> ==
There are a lot of good methods for getting Cash, the easiest being from either [[AFK World]] or the Daily [[Quests]]. This is because AFK World gives a {{Money|500}} bonus every 5 minutes, and the quests give about {{Money|1000}} - {{Money|4000}}. Another easy way is to regularly play the game, as you can get money by just playing the game.

Being in possession of the '''VIP Status''' will grant you '''2x''' more cash from actions and quests.
