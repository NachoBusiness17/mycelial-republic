== Codes ==
In order to use '''Codes''', the player must be at least '''Level 10''', '''Liked''' the game (although this is not required, most likely the code is either bugged or not properly implemented), and joined the Official '''Blue Lock: Rivals''' group. This means one cannot simply start creating new alternative accounts to obtain '''[[styles|Style]]''' and '''[[Flow]]''' spins through using the codes.

'''Codes''' usually grant the player [[Styles|'''Style''']] {{Spins|StyleLucky}} and '''[[Flow]]''' {{Spins|FlowLucky}}. But other times, they can also award Style {{Spins|Style}}, Flow {{Spins|Flow}}, '''[[Cash]]''', or  even temporary '''Cash Boosts/Multipliers'''.

* '''Note: Codes can expire relatively fast. Furthermore, some codes expire within 1 day .'''
