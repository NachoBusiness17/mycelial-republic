==Description==

'''Flow''' is a core feature of the game. They allow the player to benefit from various different buffs, depending on the current equipped Flow. This feature was released alongside with '''[[Styles]]''' when the game first launched.

There are six different unique tiers to '''Flows''', identical to '''Styles'''. These tiers are {{Rarity|Rare}}, {{Rarity|Epic}}, {{Rarity|Legendary}}, {{Rarity|Mythic}}, {{Rarity|World Class}}, and {{Rarity|Master}}!

To activate your equipped '''Flow''', you must fill the orange bar above your move-set up by either waiting or performing actions. Once your '''Flow Bar''' is at least at the 30% capacity, you are allowed to activate it and gain all the desired buffs until it runs out. Some '''Flows''', such as '''[[Genius]]''' will grant your team more '''Flow Percentage'''.

Along with '''Styles,''' you are given a slot which houses the '''Flow''' you currently have equipped. When rolling a '''Style''' or '''Flow''', it will roll the '''Flow''' in the slot you currently have equipped. You can switch between slots to change the current '''Style/Flow''' you have without needing to reroll it, allowing you to have multiple '''Styles''' or '''Flows''' in storage.

In total, there are '''Six Flow''' slots, and you can see them by opening the '''Flow''' menu and scrolling through the slots on the left side of the screen. There is also another button above the slots which shows a green button titled <span style="color:green;>"'''LOCK'''"</span>. By pressing <span style="color:green;>'''"LOCK"'''</span>, it will make it so when you equip that slot, you are unable to roll the '''Style/Flow''' that is currently equipped in it until you <span style="color:red;>"'''UNLOCK'''"</span> it. This acts as a safety measure, so you don't have to worry above losing a valuable '''Flow'''.

The first '''Flow''' slot is unlocked by default, which means you will always have at least one flow active at a time. However, you can purchase a total of '''5 Flow slots''' using <span style="font-weight:bold; color:white; text-shadow: 0 0 5px white, 0 0 10px gold;">[[File:Robux.png|20px]]Robux.</span>
