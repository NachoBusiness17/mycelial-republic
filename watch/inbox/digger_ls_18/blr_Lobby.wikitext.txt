== The Lobby ==
The '''Lobby''' in ''Blue Lock: Rivals'' serves as the central hub where players begin their journey to becoming the ultimate striker. It's the first area players enter upon launching the game and it acts as a gateway to access all major features and game modes.

== Overview ==
The Lobby is designed to reflect the high-stakes, competitive atmosphere of ''Blue Lock.'' It consists of sleek, modern architecture with an immersive training facility vibe. It gets players into the vibe of elite soccer from the moment that they join!

== Key Features ==

* '''Game Terminals''': Gives access to the 5v5 team-based games and Limited-Time '''[[Events]]'''.
* '''Leaderboard Displays''': View the top players globally, updated real-time.
* '''Daily Missions Board''': Complete daily challenges for rewards like coins, [[Styles|'''Style''']] spins, and '''[[Flow]]''' spins. With milestone rewards too like {{LuckySpinsStyle}}, extra cash, and even Flow {{Lucky Spins Flow}}.
* '''Gacha Area''': Spend in-game currency or Robux to spin and unpack for new Styles, Emotes, Flows, Goal Effects, and even Cosmetics.
* '''[[Codes]]''': Redeem codes for free and easy Style{{LuckySpinsStyle}} and Flow{{Lucky Spins Flow}}.
* '''Inventory''': Open up your inventory to customize things from Equipped Emotes to even Awakening Outfits!
* '''Spectate''': Spectate players with ease from the Lobby!
* '''Stats''': Check out your stats and show your friends.

== Tips ==

* Check out the Daily Missions Board every 24 hours for free and easy rewards.
