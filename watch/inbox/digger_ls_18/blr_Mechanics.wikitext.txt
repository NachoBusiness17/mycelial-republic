These mechanics are ''vital'' to gameplay, and it may be difficult to play without them.

==Dribbling==

'''Dribbling''' is a core gameplay mechanic that allows you to resist any incoming steals ''that are dribble-able.'' Without '''Dribbling,''' it is pretty easy to take the ball from somebody. 

'''Dribbling''' has a cooldown of ''Placeholder'' and lasts for around ''Placeholder'' seconds.

To activate a '''Dribble,''' the user must press the:
* "Q" Key on '''PC'''
* "Dribble" Button on '''Mobile''''
* "LT" on '''XBox'''
* "R1" on '''Playstation'''

If somebody slide tackles you, or uses certain stealing abilities, they will be ''ankle-broken''. When you are ankle-broken, you are rag-dolled on the ground and cannot move, and when this period ends you are put into a get-up animation. After this effect ends, you are able to move around again. Learning how to effectively '''Dribble''' opponents can help you more effectively shoot, pass, and play make as you don't have to worry about losing the ball.

==Shooting==

'''Shooting''' is another crucial gameplay mechanic, allowing you to kick the ball forward based on a power meter and where you are aiming at. To start shooting, you must hold down M1 on '''PC''' or the ''Shoot'' button on '''Mobile.''' To get more power in a kick, hold down the shooting button until you reach your desired power level. After a few seconds or when you release the shoot button, the ball will be sent outwards at the direction you were facing.

'''Shooting Strength''' can be increased by flows and character passives, such as {{EmperorText}} or '''King's Awakened Passive.''' '''Shooting''' can also be affected by the position of your character.

Without '''Shooting,''' it is very difficult to score goals as you would need to rely on other forms of scoring or by using abilities which isn't favorable.

===Volleying===

'''Volleying''' is a variant to '''Shooting,''' if the ball is loose and you hold the shoot button, then touch the ball, you will '''Volley''' it.

'''Volleying''' has a few differences from regularly '''Shooting:'''

* More Acceleration,
* Slightly less range & power,
* Faster speed-drop off.

'''Volleying''' is useful in certain situations where you don't have time to set up a regular shot. You can also use a volley after faking a shot or by doing a feint, which if you are close to the goal, has a higher chance of scoring then doing a regular kick.

===Shooting Abilities===

Certain abilities mimic a shot, kicking the ball forward and is usually stronger than a regular one. Using these abilities may make it easier to score and allow you to score from farther distances and more precise angles.

==Header==

'''Headering''' happens when the ball falls on top of you are jumping, which then the ball will be bumped in the air and towards the direction you are looking. 

You can perform a '''Header''' in multiple ways, ranging from doing a self-pass to using a header to control a pass by looking down.

While there isn't much to a normal '''Header,''' the community has discovered a way to utilize it in the form of '''Header Dribbling.''' '''Header Dribbling''' involves starting a '''Header,''' before looking downwards so it doesn't fly off and continuing to move forward. You are able to do four '''Headers''' in a row before the ball is forced back to the ground where it can be contested again.

You can use '''Header Dribbling''' in case your regular '''Dribble''' or '''Dribbling Abilities''' are on cooldown, allowing you to soar the ball over defender's heads or use your last '''Header''' to pass to a teammate.

==Rainbow Flick==

'''Rainbow Flick''' is an action that is automatically performed when you jump while having the ball, causing you to flick the ball over your head and out in front of you.

'''Rainbow Flick''' on its own isn't too useful, but you can use it to get the ball over someone you might have started '''Slide Tackling''' towards you or use it as an extremely short-ranged pass. '''Rainbow Flick''' can be paired with dashing moves to be used more effectively.

Outside of a few uses, '''Rainbow Flick''' isn't too great for normal gameplay and it's recommended to use a '''Header Dribble''' instead.

==Passing==

'''Passing''', in a similar way to shooting, is a crucial mechanic if you want to play effectively. By pressing either M2 on '''PC''' or the ''Pass'' button on '''Mobile,''' the closest teammate you are facing towards will be locked on to, and you will perform a quick kick towards them. This has very little wind-up, with the tradeoff being it has only a decent amount of range.

To make your '''Passes''' more effective, you can jump before activating one which can give you more range and let you reach further away team members. '''Passing''' can also be boosted by certain flows such as '''Ice''' or '''Master of All Trades'''.

'''Passing''' is important for playmaking and creating strong attacks, as without it you could become vulnerable and be pressured into making a bad move, which can cost your team possession of the ball. Instead, you can pass the ball around to slowly make your way up the field with little risk involved.

==Slide Tackle==

'''Slide Tackling''' or '''Sliding''' is the main form of defense in ''Blue Lock: Rivals''. You can slide on '''PC''' by pressing the "E" key or by pressing the ''Slide'' button on '''Mobile.''' '''Sliding''' is a basic form of defense and is recommended for most situations. '''Slide Tackling''' can be tackled by using a '''Dribble''' or other '''Abilities,''' but it can still catch people off guard at moments.

If you jump and then use a '''Slide Tackle,''' if you touch the ball in midair, you will actually '''Header''' it. This can be used to clear and intercept any airborne balls.

==Ability Rack==

The '''Ability Rack''' houses your abilities, for most characters, you will have a given two slots. If you are using certain characters or any that are above the {{Rarity|World Class}} rarity however, you will have three ability slots.

No matter what character you use, they also have a special awakening that will play a cutscene before either adding a third move slot with a unique move, replacing your current moves, or doing both.

By default, each ability slot is binded to these buttons/keys:

* ''First Ability:''
** '''PC: C'''
** '''XBox: X'''
** '''Playstation: ◼'''
* ''Second ABility:'' 
** '''PC: V'''
** '''XBox: Y'''
** '''Playstation: 🔼'''
* ''Third Ability:'' 
** '''PC: B'''
** '''XBox: B'''
** '''Playstation: ⚫'''

==Flow Bar==

The '''Flow Bar''' is a small orange bar located above the ability rack. When starting a match, it will be completely empty. Over time or by doing certain actions such as dribbling, it will fill up slowly. Once you reach the '''Flow Bar''' reaches at least 30% capacity, you are able to activate the equipped '''Flow''' you have. 

You are also able to increase the '''Flow Bar''' of your teammates with '''Genius''' for +30% '''Flow Bar''', or {{AGeniusText}} for +20% '''Flow Bar'''.

While less important than abilities and styles, the '''Flow Bar''' is still very useful in case you need to apply yourself or even other teammates with crucial buffs.

==Invincibility Frames (I-Frames)==
Certain Abilities or Actions will give you a status effect known as '''"Invincibility Frames"''' or '''"I-frames"''' for short.

In basic terms, '''I-frames''' will make it so you are unable to be slide tackled or stolen from by most abilities and actions. Certain things may go through '''I-frames,''' such as a '''Goalkeeper's Dive,''' or stealing moves like {{RinText|'s Destroyer Domain}}. This effect is different from '''Armor,''' as '''Armor''' ankle-breaks them, but '''I-frames''' make it so you will completely go through them.

There is a secondary form of '''I-frames,''' being '''Ball Frames. Ball Frames''' are applied after the ball is released from possession, whether it be from a pass, shot, or rainbow flick. '''Ball Frames''' determine how long it takes for the ball to be able to be controlled or headered by other players on the field. '''Goalkeepers''' when in box are automatically allowed to bypass '''Ball Frames.'''

Some '''Ball Frames''' are very short, such as '''Hiori's Laser''' or a simple '''Kick.''' Some moves have high '''Ball Frames,''' where it takes a while for the ball to be able to be controlled, such as {{SaeText|'s Perfect Pass.}} There are also some actions that give the ball infinite '''Ball Frames,''' such as {{RinText|'s Ahnillation.}} 

However, no matter what type of move it is, as soon as the ball bounces off the ground, '''Ball Frames''' will be moved and the ball is able to be controlled again.

==Ball Physics==

Aside from '''Ball Frames,''' there are other '''Ball Physics''' that can affect gameplay.

* '''Pass Control'''
** During certain passing moves, you may notice the ball attempt to readjust its path towards its target. This effect is more noticeable on high control moves such as {{SaeText|'s Perfect Pass}} or '''Hiori's Curved Pass.''' Regularly passing or moves such as '''Monk Pass''' have low amounts of '''Pass Control,''' which may lead to them seeming inaccurate or indirect, while moves with high amounts of '''Pass Control,''' may seem more accurate.

* '''Anti-Ball'''
** Certain actions or abilities grant the user the ability to hold on the ball for long periods of time. Some examples of this would be '''[[Nagi|Nagi's]] Trap,''' which allows the user to stay in the air with the ball for long periods of time. However, to prevent unfair gameplay, things that can grant I-frames and have complete freedom last only 8 seconds. This includes Trapping and goalkeepers holding the ball.

==Awakening System==
At certain points during a match, a countdown will be displayed at the top, indicating the time until the '''Awakening''' distribution. After the countdown ends, a player receives '''Awakening''', which can be activated with the "G" key on '''PC''' or by '''tapping''' the awakening bar for '''Mobile'''. In most cases, the player must have the ball to activate their awakening.

The first '''Awakening''' is given to the player who is the '''''MVP''''' or has the '''highest points.''' After that, it alternates between teams and players with the highest points. A player who has already received '''Awakening''' in the match cannot get it again. There cannot be two Awakenings in a match at the same time — If someone already has an '''Awakening''', if a new '''Awakening Distribution''' starts, it will be removed and ended prematurely if already activated.

The '''Awakening''' cutscene is shown to everyone, including any '''Goalkeepers. Awakening''' grants at least one new ability - up to three new abilities depending on the style - and can replace current ability slots with new moves. During the '''cutscene,''' actions in the game do not stop except for walking and abilities. For example, stamina can be restored during the cutscene.

Certain styles do not require the ball to activate '''Awakening,''' these being '''Aiku, Karasu, NEL Reo and Gagamaru.''' '''NEL Reo''' is also the only style to have more than one awakening cutscene, two reespective modes.

==Request Chemical==
"Request Chemical" is a feature allows you to use a Chemical Reaction, if you use a style which has it. If the player has the required style and is in same team with you, when they the ball, you will get an option to Request Chemical by using "F" on '''PC''' or the ''Chemical Request'' button on '''Mobile'''. After you use Chemical Request, the player will get an additional move at the bottom of their screen which will start the '''Chemical Reaction''' if they press it. After they use that move, they will shortly pass to you, doing the Chemical Reaction. Chemical Reactions can only be done if two players of required styles are in same team, the sender has the ball, and the receiver asks for Chemical Reaction by using F in PC or CR button on mobile. Chemical reactions have cooldowns which depends on the chemical reaction.
