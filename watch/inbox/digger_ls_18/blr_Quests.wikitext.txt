'''Quests''' are simple tasks you can complete to earn rewards like:
<br>• '''Style Spins'''
<br>• '''Flow Spins'''
<br>• '''Cash'''

Complete '''8 quests''' in about 3 days (if you grind enough) and you’ll receive a bonus:
<br>→ '''2 Lucky Flow Spins''' + '''2 Lucky Style Spins'''

== 🔹 Trivia & Info ==

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">What Quests Do</span>'''  
<br>• Give rewards like '''Cash''', '''Spins''', and sometimes '''Packs'''

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">When They Reset</span>'''  
<br>• Quests usually refresh daily at '''7:00 PM EST'''  
<br>• Note: This may vary. Some users get them at slightly different times

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">Extra Rewards</span>'''  
<br>• Completing quests fills a reward bar that gives additional prizes

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">Types of Quests</span>'''  
<br>• Score goals  
<br>• Win matches  
<br>• Complete other in-game tasks

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">How Much You Get</span>'''  
<br>• Most quests reward around '''$2,500 – $4,000'''

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">Special Quests</span>'''  
<br>• Appear during events  
<br>• Offer boosted or exclusive rewards

'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">Why Do Quests? 🤔 </span>'''  
<br>• Fast way to earn spins, cash, and XP  
<br>• Ideal for '''F2P players''' to get lucky flow/style spins


'''<span style="color:#1e90ff;">Easy Tip</span>'''  
<br>• Hop into a small server and use a friend or alt to help finish quests faster!
