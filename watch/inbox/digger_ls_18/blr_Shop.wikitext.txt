== Shop ==
[[File:Whatever.png|thumb|347x347px|Cosmetics shop]]

'''The Shop''' is an area in '''[[Lobby]]''' where players can obtain cosmetics to allow customization of their game. The shop also includes permanent perks that the player can buy using '''Robux''' to enhance their gameplay experience. They can be bought at the shop.

== Emote Pack ==
Each [[Emotes|'''Emote''']] pack contains '''1 emote''' of any rarity excluding '''Mythic''', and they cost {{Yen|Amount=1200}} or alternatively {{Robux|Amount= 99}}, They have different chances of obtainment.

== Goal Effects Pack ==
Each [[Goal Effects|'''Goal''' '''Effect''']] pack contains '''1 Goal Effect''' of any rarity excluding '''Mythic''', and they cost {{Yen|Amount=2000}} or alternatively {{Robux|Amount= 199}}, They have different chances of obtainment.

== Player Cards Pack ==
Each [[Player Cards|'''Player Card''']] pack contains '''1 Player Card''' of any rarity excluding '''Mythic''', and they cost {{Yen|Amount=1800}} or alternatively {{Robux|Amount= 149}}, They have different chances of obtainment.

== Chances ==
Every pack has the same chance of dropping each rarity. Below are the chances for each of the rarities
* {{Rarity|Legendary}} 1% / 35% Robux odds
* {{Rarity|Epic}} 15% / 65% Robux odds 
* {{Rarity|Rare}} 84% / 0% Robux odds

There are '''[[Limited]]''' time items as well, including '''Starter Packs'''.

== Trivia ==

* '''Location and Access''': The Shop is accessible from the Lobby or via the player's hot bar.
* '''Currencies Accepted''': Items can be purchased using Robux (Roblox's premium currency) or in-game currency (¥). Notably, only Packs can be bought with ¥.
* '''Available Items''':
** '''Goal Effects''': Visual effects that play when a player scores a goal.
** '''Emotes''': Animations allowing players to express themselves during matches.
** '''Player Cards''': Customizable cards showcasing player information.
** '''Cash''': In-game currency available for purchase.
* '''Packs''': The Shop offers various Packs, each containing one collectible item. There are three types:
** '''Emote Pack''': Contains emotes with the following rarities:
*** Rare: 84% chance
*** Epic: 15% chance
*** Legendary: 1% chance
** '''Goal Effects Pack''': Offers goal celebration effects with similar rarity distribution.
** '''Player Cards Pack''': Provides player card designs, also following the same rarity percentages.
* '''Cosmetics''': The game features cosmetics categorized into two unnamed rarities, presumably Common and Legendary. Most cosmetics are either pay-to-win or obtained from special events. Currently, there is one common cosmetic and 14 legendary cosmetics available.
* '''Limited-Time Items''': The Shop periodically features limited-time items, including Starter Packs, which offer exclusive content for a restricted period.
