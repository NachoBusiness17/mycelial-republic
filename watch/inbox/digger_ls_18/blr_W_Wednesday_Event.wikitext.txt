== Overview ==
'''W Wednesday''' is a weekly community event in ''Blue Lock: Rivals'', hosted by Miro, the owner of the game. Every Wednesday, Miro rewards the community with generous giveaways including '''Lucky Style Spins''', '''Lucky Flow Spins''', Event Spins (during active events), rarity boosts, and various other rewards. He also shares exclusive hints and teasers about upcoming styles and features, though these previews are not always fully confirmed and may change.

Launched on November 5, 2025, W Wednesday has quickly become a fan-favorite tradition that builds hype every week while helping make the game far more free-to-play friendly by giving everyone better access to meta styles and pulling it out of the heavy pay-to-win bubble.

'''<u>The event  is usually held on Wednesday at 06:00 PM / 7:00 PM EST. Delays are announced in the Blue Lock: Rivals official Discord server and extra rewards are given to compensate for the delay.</u>'''

From large batches of spins and boosts to other valuable items, these giveaways have been a major part of the game's community support.
