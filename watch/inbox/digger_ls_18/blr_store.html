Birth of the brand

In 2021, BlueLock Store came to life with the first jersey designs inspired by the Bluelock universe. Driven by two enthusiasts: one for manga and the other for clothing, it was born from our shared vision of merging fashion and manga.

Investment in premises

Building on our first year, we chose to invest in premises to structure our production and prepare for BlueLock's anime adaptation. This space allows us to improve organization and quality of our products.

Participation at Japan Expo

This year, we had the opportunity to participate in Japan Expo, an iconic event where we met fans, shared our passion and presented our latest creations. This experience was very enriching and strengthened our commitment to our community.

Growth and Continuous Improvement

In 2024, we are in full expansion on social media, strengthening our presence and community. We are committed to constantly improving our products to offer our customers a superior quality experience.

Towards new horizons

In 2025, we continue our growth with exciting new projects and an ever more engaged community. Our goal remains the same: to offer the best Blue Lock products to our fans.
