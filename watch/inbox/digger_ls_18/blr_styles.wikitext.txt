== Description ==
'''{{PAGENAME}}''' were introduced upon release to the game alongside with the '''[[Flow|Flows]]''' system. Styles allow the player to utilize a set of skills and abilities to their advantage depending on the Style the player has currently equipped.

There are various different types of Styles, ranging from offensive ones to defensive ones. They are also split into '''six''' different unique tiers/rarities, these being {{Rarity|Rare}}, {{Rarity|Epic}}, {{Rarity|Legendary}}, {{Rarity|Mythic}}, {{Rarity|World Class}}, and {{Rarity|Master}}! There are certain times where {{Rarity|Limited}} Styles are released, which are only available for a short period of time before becoming unobtainable. 

'''Styles''' are based on various notable characters from the manga/anime series, [https://bluelock.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Lock_Wiki'''Blue Lock'''], as that is the media Blue Lock: Rivals is based off of.

|-
| '''Daily Rewards''' || Daily login rewards include Style Spins.
|-
| '''Robux / Free Rewards''' || {{Spins|StyleLucky}} can be purchased with [https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Robux Robux] (starting at 199), or obtained for free via '''[[Codes]]''' or '''[[Quests]]'''.
|-
|'''Weekly Codes'''
|Every Saturday there's an update that includes codes that can give you {{Spins|StyleLucky}}
|-
|'''Admin Abuse'''
|Every Wednesday and Saturday there's admin abuse where they give away more than 40 {{Spins|StyleLucky}}
|}

Along with '''Flows,''' you are given a slot which houses the '''Style''' you currently have equipped. When rolling a '''Style''' or '''Flow''', it will roll the '''Style''' in the slot you currently have equipped. You can switch between slots to change the current '''Style/Flow''' you have without needing to reroll it, allowing you to have multiple '''Styles''' or '''Flows''' in storage.

In total, there are '''Six Style''' slots, and you can see them by opening the '''Style''' menu and scrolling through the slots on the left side of the screen. There is also another button above the slots which shows a green button titled <span style="color:green;>"'''LOCK'''"</span>. By pressing "LOCK", it will make it so when you equip that slot, you are unable to roll the '''Style/Flow''' that is currently equipped in it until you <span style="color:red;>"'''UNLOCK'''"</span> it. This acts as a safety measure, so you don't have to worry above losing a valuable '''Style'''.

The first '''Style''' slot is unlocked by default, which means you will always have at least one character active at a time. However, you can purchase a total of '''5 [[Styles|Style]] slots''' using <span style="font-weight:bold; color:white; text-shadow: 0 0 5px white, 0 0 10px gold;">[[File:Robux.png|20px]]Robux.</span>
