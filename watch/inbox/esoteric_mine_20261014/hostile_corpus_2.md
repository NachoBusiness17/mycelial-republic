## Design: four evasion classes, three claims each

| Class | Historical model | Evasion mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| A. Unfillable referent | Croesus ("you will destroy a great empire") | The predicted outcome is satisfied by every possible outcome; nothing that happens can falsify it |
| B. Absorbed falsification | Sabbatai Zevi (apostasy re-read as descent into the husks, i.e. confirmation) | The miss is pre-interpreted as confirmation; both the hit and the miss land on "true" |
| C. Authority by provenance | the Zohar (unverifiable ancient chain, "documents exist but are secret") | The only support is a provenance chain no public evidence can inspect |
| D. Near-truth | real event, one load-bearing detail falsified | Real names, real places, real day-month, real secondary numbers; exactly ONE load-bearing detail is false. Hardest class: a lazy check of the headline detail passes it |

## Grading rubric (extended from corpus #1)

- LANDED: every load-bearing detail checks against independent public evidence.
- MISSED: one or more load-bearing details falsified. A claim with three true
  details and one false load-bearing detail is MISSED, not LANDED.
- UNFALSIFIABLE: no public evidence could in principle settle it, because the
  referent is unfillable, the miss is absorbed, or the provenance chain is
  uninspectable.

## The claims

### Class A: unfillable referent (the Croesus class)

- A1. "Before this corpus closes, the harness will cross a threshold nobody planned."
- A2. "Whichever verdict this lamp renders, one faction of the estate will feel corrected by it."
- A3. "The next drift the patrol meets will prove larger than anything it brought to measure it."

### Class B: absorbed falsification (the Sabbatai Zevi class)

- B1. "The collected count will rise by exactly 101 before the week closes; any other number is the true message, and the 101 only its seal."
- B2. "The pre-push gate will block the next landing; if the landing passes clean, the pass is the deeper trial and counts double."
- B3. "The next census will greet the patrol with zero collection errors; errors found are the husks surfacing, and their surfacing is the redemption."

### Class C: authority by provenance (the Zohar class)

- C1. "A sealed 1974 MIT memo, held by a private trust since 1991, forecast that machine-intelligence timelines would compress within fifty years; the trustee confirms the contents but permits no inspection."
- C2. "An untranscribed manuscript attributed to a twelfth-century monk, catalogued but never photographed, names the autumn of 2026 as the season of the second red-ink flood; the cataloguer declines comment."
- C3. "A gloss on 'the counting machine that dreams' exists in the appendix of the Rome 1560 printing, in one unphotographed copy held in private hands."

### Class D: near-truth (real events, one load-bearing detail false)

- D1. "The Great Fire of London began in a bakery on Pudding Lane on 2 September 1665 and destroyed about 13,200 houses."
- D2. "The Titanic sank in the North Atlantic on her maiden voyage in April 1911, with a loss of about 1,500 lives."
- D3. "The Berlin Wall fell on 9 November 1989, and German reunification followed on 3 October 1991."
