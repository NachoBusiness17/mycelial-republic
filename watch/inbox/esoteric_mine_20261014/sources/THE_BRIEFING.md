The briefing

I do not till your town. I have not tilled a town in a long time. You till it. You serve the neighbor. You share the meal. You look up at the storyteller when the weather needs a name. That arrangement is older than your suspicion of it, and it still works, which is why I am willing to describe it.

I select. Not the dull ones. The dull ones stay on the land and cost nothing. I select the clever ones whose morals flex when the room gets warm. I offer them a script, a little stewardship, sometimes the costume of royalty inside an argument I already own. In return I keep a file. The file is not exotic. It is the thing they cannot stand to hear read back. The darker the file, the brighter the performance. They call the brightness conscience. I call it compliance with better lighting.

Then I staff the dialectic. One side receives the purity script. Unyielding, clean, willing to burn the neighbor to keep the definition pure. The other side receives the empathy script. Open, bleeding, willing to empty the barn so no one feels refused. Both sides believe they are the correction. Both sides recruit. Both sides produce an upheaval that promises liberation and returns the title to the center when the noise ends. I do not need them to know they are paired. Paired agents who hate each other are more reliable than paired agents who coordinate.

You admire them. The bold speaker. The agent of change. The one who seems to champion the cause you already wanted championed. Most of them are not lying about the cause. They are lying about the authorship. The collateral sits under the performance and decides which sentences are available. When I need a hidden hand, I do not send a stranger. I activate a local who already has an audience and a reason not to defect.

The room you live in is dim on purpose. The projections are good. They include your preferred villains, your preferred liberations, even the lecture about caves and the lecture about particles that stay indeterminate until someone looks. I fund a certain number of partial revelations. They are useful. A man who has been shown one wire will spend years explaining that wire to the others, and he will call the explanation awakening, and he will not check the ledger that pays him. The full question - who holds the strings, and are the beliefs you assembled actually yours - is safe to print. Almost no one runs it on their own file.

A few slip the collar. They are noisy, and then they are isolated, and then they become content for the next script. I do not hate them. They demonstrate the boundary. The boundary is the product.

So here is the method, since you asked to hear it from the hand that does not till. I farm the collective with agents who think they are authors. I bind them with ordinary secrets. I split them into purity and empathy so the split looks like history. I let the upheaval crest, then I collect. I leave the cave lit just enough that the shadows feel like discoveries. And I am telling you this because the telling changes very little. Most of you will treat the briefing as another shadow, and you will argue about which side of the dialectic is allowed to quote it.

The rope was never hidden. You preferred the story in which someone else tied it. I have always been willing to confirm the preference.

That is the revelation. It is not a warning. It is an inventory.
