# THE FALSIFIER INDEX

Ninety-five entries. One per thesis. Each carries a kill condition, a
verification method, and a status: LIVE (falsifiable now), PENDING
(falsifiable when a named event occurs), RETRACTED (the claim cannot be made
falsifiable in its current form - rewritten below or dropped from the count).

The count, stated up front because the preamble no longer claims what it
cannot count: this index exists so that the number of LIVE and PENDING
propositions is a matter of record, not rhetoric. RETRACTED rows stay in the
document as frames and images - singing between the propositions - but they
stop being counted as claims. That is the covenant: the document does not
get to wear a costume.

Nailed beside the theses. The theses are the sermon. This is the session
minute that turns the sermon into a dispute.
