# falsifier-index

**The link above the door is a claim.** For one week this repository did not
exist, and its absence was the falsifier of the document it serves: the
outerweb theses claimed falsifiability, and a claim of falsifiability with
no index is a homily with a GitHub link. This repo is the difference between
a document and a covenant.

## What is here

- **[THESES.md](THESES.md)** - the disputation on the permission economy.
  Ninety-five theses and one thesis Luther could not write.
- **[INDEX.md](INDEX.md)** - the falsifier index. One entry per thesis: a
  kill condition, a verification method, and a status.

## The statuses

- **LIVE** - falsifiable now. The kill condition is observable; produce the
  observation and the thesis dies.
- **PENDING** - falsifiable when a named event occurs. The event is named.
  Until it occurs, the thesis is not yet testable; after it, it is.
- **RETRACTED** - the claim cannot be made falsifiable in its current form.
  These stay in the document as frames and images, and they are out of the
  count. **The count at indexing: 55 LIVE, 8 PENDING, 32 RETRACTED.**

## The fuses

Two entries carry dates because a falsifier without a deadline is a
prophecy, and thesis 53 says prophecies without falsifiers are sales decks:

- **Thesis 73**: the eight grams settle when the fence finally asks the
  chain. Fuse: ninety-five days from the door. If the fence has not asked
  the chain by **2027-02-03**, the thesis is recorded FAILED here, publicly,
  by the man who nailed it.
- **Thesis 75**: if the fence returns the grams and the dispute does not
  dissolve, the document convicted itself. Self-executing. No date needed.

## The covenant

1. Every thesis has an index entry. No exceptions, including the ones that
   get retracted.
2. Retractions are published, not quietly deleted. A retraction is the
   proof the mechanism works; a silent edit is the proof it never did.
3. Owner-owed evidence is named in the index (the council logs, the four
   bounce hashes, the audit protocol). Each converts a LIVE row's evidence
   from asserted to linked. Owed-and-unlinked past the door is scored
   against the document, not the reader.
4. Anyone may kill a thesis by meeting its kill condition. Open an issue
   with the observation. A thesis dies by its own kill condition or it does
   not die at all; it never dies by argument alone.

## The founding link

The name this repository was announced under was
`github.com/outerweb/falsifier-index`. That did not exist at announcement -
that was the point, and the 404 was the falsifier. This repository is the
claim made true. If the outerweb name is claimed, the founding link

a namespace.
