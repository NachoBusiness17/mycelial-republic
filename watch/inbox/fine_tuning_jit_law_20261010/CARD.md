# THE JIT FINE-TUNING LAW (operator coinage, 2026-10-10, verbatim)

"fine tuning is just giving ideas just in time to the model while its thinking to get it
out of pitfalls that were already detected and bypassed." (operator, on the MaximeRivest
drop; context: Rivest's finetuning-hobby post + dashboard teaching Qwen 4B/9B/27B to
read papers, tracking grounding failures, critical errors, self-stopping answers,
results right, against an Astra + teacher baseline.)

## What the sentence reorganizes (the estate mappings)

1. FINE-TUNING DEFINED: the dataset is a PITFALL-BYPASS CORPUS - detected failures plus
   their working bypasses, compressed into weights so the idea arrives mid-thinking at
   zero context cost. Not knowledge injection: TIMING-aware steering.
2. RETRIEVAL IS THE SAME MECHANISM, DIFFERENT DELIVERY TIMING: ribs/receipts injected at
   boot = the inference-time version. Fine-tuning buys compression (fixed, free at
   inference); retrieval buys freshness (updatable, costs context). The GPS
   strings-are-coordinates verdict (retrieval wins at our 10-shape scale) is now
   explained at the mechanism level: same food, different serving time, and at small
   scale serving-it-live beats compressing-it.
3. THE COMPOST LEDGER IS FINE-TUNING FOOD: killed wants + cause-of-death +
   revival_condition = the pitfall registry. The 10/9 compost order gains its purpose:
   the compost heap is the training-set-in-waiting.
4. MASS CONVERGENCE (yesterday's drop): fine-tuning on own multi-agent traces works
   because traces ARE detected-pitfall records. The operator's sentence names the
   mechanism MASS measured.
5. THE MAG FEEDING LAW MECHANIZED: feeding the companion = delivering
   detected-and-bypassed pitfalls. WHAT to feed = the failure ledger; growth = pitfalls
   bypassed; "expand when there is need" = fine-tune when a pitfall RECURS (demand
   precedes organ).
6. THE DEAD-SALMON RIDER: a pitfall must be DETECTED under controls before its bypass
   is food, else you fine-tune on phantom pitfalls - dead salmon in the dataset.
7. THE PRIMER MECHANISM STATEMENT: "we are making the primer" now has its mechanical
   definition - the Primer IS the just-in-time pitfall-bypass delivery system for a
   thinking model.

## The instrument (from Rivest's dashboard)

Per-student tracking of pitfalls (grounding failures, critical errors, non-stopping
answers) AND successes (key results found, claims found, results right) per training
run - the pitfall-bypass census live. Our analog: the GPS capability ledger +
burn-verdict rows; the consensus harness adds cross-model pitfall attribution.

Falsifier: if a fine-tune built only from a controlled pitfall-bypass corpus does not
beat prompt-only on the held-out pitfalls, the JIT framing overclaims and it is just
distillation wearing a clock.
