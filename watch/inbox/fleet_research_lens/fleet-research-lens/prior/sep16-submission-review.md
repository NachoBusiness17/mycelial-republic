**2.4 — Four twins violate the frozen information-availability-only rule. Disputed conformity; confidence high.** S1 removes corruptions and changes the key. **2.5 — Finding and filing key defects is Supported; “both repaired” is too broad and therefore Disputed; confidence high.** SEED's amendment says the original scan-day bytes are unrecoverable; a surviving different chain/store pair yields 18 missing items instead of 12. That is a replacement reconstruction, not reproduction of the original observation. **2.6 — The seal's resampling procedure is not operationally complete. Disputed as written; confidence high.** It declares six task-family blocks but requests an interval for each family. Resampling those six blocks conventionally omits any particular family with probability `(5/6)^6 = 33.49%`; at least one is absent in `1 − 6!/6^6 = 98.46%` of replicates. The affected per-family statistics are undefined unless an extra rule is supplied, and duplicating the sole family block supplies no within-family replication. Ten thousand resamples do not create ten thousand independent observations. Proposed repair: hold task families as fixed strata, resample the genuinely independent paired units within them, preserve shared clusters, and freeze an executable confidence procedure. [Official bootstrap documentation](https://www.stat.math.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/boot/html/boot.html) describes within-stratum resampling. Conservative Bonferroni marginal bounds can provide simultaneous coverage without an independence assumption, provided the marginal bounds themselves are valid; this does not cure inadequate data. [NIST's inequality](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc473.htm). **Change evidence:** executable analysis and simulation checks under the actual sampling structure, adequate independent replication and a predeclared precision/power target.

**2.7 — The two signed comparison rules need an explicit population map. Unproven coherence; confidence medium.** SHAM requires `single − best fleet ≥ 5`; SEAL requires `bundled − single > 5`. They can coexist if solo superiority is required on shams while fleet superiority is required on different complementary-information tasks. They conflict if imposed on the same means. The current language mixes task and model families without a sufficient executable map. The twins bundle additionally proposes combining calibration with scored episodes after observed revisions, which risks selection bias. **Change evidence:** unique population and arm IDs for each signed estimand, fixed comparator selection, and a fresh confirmatory set isolated from adaptive development.

**2.8 — Byte closure and seal completion remain Unproven; one detached digest is demonstrably stale. Confidence high.** Ten of eleven targeted artifacts match their last listed detached hash. **3.3 — “The structure teaches in-context” is Unproven as a causal explanation of S3; confidence high.** A single correct refusal is compatible with the packet, prior model training, generic caution, direct instruction or their interaction. **Change evidence:** equal-budget randomized packet and control conditions on held-out tasks.
