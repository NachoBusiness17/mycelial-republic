### Invariant 2: Generative Grammar Over Rigid Blueprints
- **Statement:** `IF a complex fleet is constructed from a rigid master blueprint THEN it will crack under unexpected operational variance; THEREFORE the fleet must grow from a shared pattern language of local rules BECAUSE living systems generate coherence through local adaptation.`
- **Mechanism:** Alexander showed that medieval towns achieved wholeness without a master zoning plan because every builder shared the same pattern language. Our fleet seats (`bob`, `foste`, `pixel`, `t90`) operate autonomously because they share the same Invariant Language, not because a central conductor micro-manages them.
