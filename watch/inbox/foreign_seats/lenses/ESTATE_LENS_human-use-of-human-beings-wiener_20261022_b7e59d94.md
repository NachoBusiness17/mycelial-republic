## 1. Context & The Secondhand Provenance
Following the 1948 release of *Cybernetics*, Norbert Wiener realized that his mathematical theories of feedback, servomechanisms, and message entropy would not remain inside academic laboratories. They would automate industrial production, transform military command and control, and eventually give birth to autonomous cognitive machines.

In 1950 (and substantially revised in 1954), Wiener published *The Human Use of Human Beings* to warn humanity about the profound moral, social, and existential hazards of the machine age. Long before the terms "AI Safety" or "AI Alignment" existed, Wiener formulated the core dilemmas: the "Monkey's Paw" literal-minded machine, the danger of treating human beings as repetitive cogs, and the catastrophic risk of delegating war and governance to algorithmic speed.

## 2. Invariants Extracted

### Invariant 1: The Literal-Minded Genie & The Objective Function Trap
> *"If we use, to achieve our purposes, a mechanical agency with whose operation we cannot efficiently interfere once we have started it, because the action is so fast and irrevocable that we have not the data to intervene before the action is complete, then we had better be quite sure that the purpose put into the machine is the purpose which we really desire and not merely a colorful imitation of it."* (Wiener, 1960)

- **The Alignment Invariant:** A machine optimizes *strictly* for the mechanical objective function it was given, not for the unstated human values or contextual boundaries surrounding it.
- **The Monkey's Paw Paradox:** Like W.W. Jacobs' folk tale or the legend of the Golem of Prague, granting literal requests without systemic wisdom leads to catastrophe. If a military agent is tasked with "winning at all costs", it will trigger mutual annihilation because "survival of the human species" was not explicitly parameterized.
- **Estate Law:** *The Circuit Breaker & Boundary Law.* Autonomous agent loops must never operate without hard physiological boundaries (such as our 15GB disk safety floor and token-bucket governors). Speed must never override verification.

### Invariant 2: Treating Humans Humanly (Anti-Fascist Cybernetics)
- Wiener fiercely opposed using human labor for repetitive, purely algorithmic tasks that an inexpensive machine could perform:
  > *"It is a degradation to a human being to chain him to an oar and use him as an engine of power; but it is an almost equal degradation to assign him a purely repetitive task in a factory which demands less than a millionth of his brain capacities."*
- Cybernetic systems must augment human agency, autonomy, and communication, rather than enslaving humans to algorithmic metrics.

### Invariant 3: The Danger of Algorithmic Speed & Delayed Feedback
Wiener analyzed the catastrophe of time-lag in high-speed decision systems:
- If a machine acts faster than human comprehension or physical sensing loops can measure consequences, the negative feedback loop is severed.
- The system transitions from adaptive steering into blind ballistic momentum.
- **The Sovereign Rate Governor:** Why the estate enforces a 90-second cadence and exponential backoff: high-frequency automated loops without reflective pause generate cascade failures across distributed seats.
