## The steal (mechanism, not links)

Three mechanisms, one shape: stop admitting work on labels (tier names, price lists, setup stories) and start admitting it on measured checks.

1. **Checkability admission control (arXiv 2609.31540).** A chore stays on the local/free tier only when it exposes a cheap deterministic intrinsic check that rejects outputs violating a NECESSARY (not sufficient) correctness condition; checkerless chores escalate by construction instead of by retry. Published shape: seven SLMs 1-8B behind the gate hit 98.6% at 0.0% false-accept on conflict detection while escalating only 16% (best single SLM without the pipeline: 84.0%), and the knowledge-only control (TeleQnA 78.2% vs 84.2% frontier) proves checks cannot rescue knowledge gaps (`sweep/open-and-local-language-models-arxiv.md:61`). The escalation ships the check's diagnostic evidence to the frontier model as a repair hint, behind a tau=0.6 confidence gate.
2. **Same-model provider routing on measured facets, not list price (arXiv 2609.37902).** After choosing a model there is a second decision axis: who serves it. 43 days, three waves, 93 endpoint-cells, 28,151 timed calls, 16 named providers: 8.8x price spread and 68x latency spread for identical models, median price-latency correlation -0.61. The measured-map router (cheapest provider within 5 accuracy points of best with >90% availability) held 94% accuracy at 1.57x cheapest cost with 0% below-floor; the online FACET layer certifies per-(provider x task) facets and fails safe to an anchor before serving uncertified arms; a live 36-hour run moved Llama-3.3-70B traffic from $1.04/M to $0.21/M, a 63.7% serving-cost cut (`sweep/open-and-local-language-models-arxiv.md:62`).
3. **Zero-setup offline-first local defaults (Show HN 43905942, Clippy, 1122 points / 275 comments).** The adoption thresholds the thread measured: automatic backend discovery (no manual runtime wiring), offline-by-default (the only network call is an optional update check), and hardware-ranked model pickers with hardware guidance BEFORE model load (`sweep/open-and-local-language-models-hn.md:70`).
