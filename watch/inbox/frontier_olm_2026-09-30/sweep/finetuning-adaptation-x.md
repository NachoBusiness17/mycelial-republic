## Q1 - QLoRA/LoRA recipes for janitor-class models on session-tape corpora

**Strongest read: "Internalizing Tool Knowledge in Small Language Models via QLoRA Fine-Tuning"** (Shemla, Yakobe, Agarwal, Patel, El Maghraoui; arXiv 2605.17774, v1 2026-05-18, v2 2026-05-26; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17774). Abstract page read end to end this session. Recipe: two ~4B models (Gemma 4 E4B and Qwen3-4B; the estate's short worker is literally gemma4), 8-bit QLoRA, ~1,700 tool-use examples (schemas, question-to-plan mappings, execution-style traces), evaluated description-free (prompt omits the tool catalog entirely). Results: fine-tuned beats the informed unfine-tuned baseline; input length cut 82.6%; best Gemma run AT-F1 0.65 vs 0.47 baseline, judge 3.88 vs 2.88; Qwen3-4B judge 3.78 with 62% less memory and 2.5x faster than Gemma, but more catastrophic forgetting on general multiple-choice benchmarks. Rank ablation: r=32 maximizes planning quality while smaller ranks preserve more general knowledge, i.e. a measured quality-retention dial. Estate translation: ~1,700 curated examples (not the 11,870 raw seat_pool rows, corpus.md:12) sufficed, and retention was measured on held-out planning scores, not loss curves.
