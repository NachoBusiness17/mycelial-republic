## Pass 2: deep reads (fetched and read in this session)

1. Attuner (2609.36722, 2026-09-29, cs.CL; Tencent Youtu + HK PolyU + EIT), FULL TEXT. Studies position-independent caching (PIC) for agent artifacts (skills, documents, memory entries). Isolates the quality loss: positional mismatch has minor effect; single-artifact reading stays faithful; failure is selecting among multiple artifacts. Replacing PIC attention scores with full-prefill scores while keeping cached KV unchanged recovers task quality (MuSiQue EM 34.84 to 47.91 vs Full 47.79; BigCodeBench pass@1 38.98 to 44.53 vs Full 43.63). Fix: low-rank adapters on query projections only, trained by distilling full-prefill response distributions, <0.05% of parameters, cached artifact KV never rewritten. Qwen3-4B/8B, seven benchmarks: in-domain avg gap to Full cut 6.74 to 1.61 (4B) and 8.27 to 0.51 (8B) at 1.79x/2.45x TTFT speedup; 1,024 distillation examples already lift MuSiQue F1 48.70 to 56.40.
2. ARC-KV (2609.36835, 2026-09-29, cs.AI; UMD + Meta), FULL TEXT. Reconstruction-based compaction for long reusable prefixes. Decomposes Attention Matching into per-context reconstruction (kept context-specific) and anchor selection (amortized: a frozen value-aware indexer trained once per base model, one scoring pass per context), plus convex-hull-constrained key merging and closed-form attention-mass/compact-value fitting. Hard-subset retention at 5% has relative l2 error 0.8778; key merging + value fitting 0.3200. QuALITY at 10% retention, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct: accuracy 0.6409 to 0.6474 over OMP-based AM while compaction time drops 959.8s to 37.3s (25.73x).
3. CacheRepair (2609.35139, 2026-09-28, cs.LG; CUHK), FULL TEXT. A lightweight external network predicts the residual between stale (independently precomputed per-chunk) KV and joint KV for every document token; block-causal attention (bidirectional within chunk, earlier-to-later across chunks); per-frozen-LLM repairer trained on a generic retrieval corpus and reused across downstream datasets. Error structure: boundary-local hotspots AND layer-persistent bands into chunk interiors. Qwen2.5-3B / Llama-3.1-8B / Qwen2.5-14B x MuSiQue/HotpotQA/MultiHop-RAG/TriviaQA: Pareto frontier in 11 of 12 model-dataset pairs; 1.69-4.61x median-TTFT speedup over full prefill with +2.1-26.1 F1 over direct reuse.
4. RelaxKV (2609.33503, 2026-09-27, cs.AI; SUNY Buffalo + TeleAI), FULL TEXT. Selective cache repair as a JOINT allocation over repair targets and recomputation context: layer-wise query attention picks anchors per layer; a target attends only the causally valid subset of the anchor union, not its full prefix. Four decoder models (Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-14B, Phi-4-14B, Llama-3.2-3B): at 15% anchor ratio it beats ProphetKV on aggregate LongBench on all four; best selective results on RULER-MV and LV-Eval at 16K/32K; matched-target control RelaxKV_G isolates context selection as a real factor.
5. Tessera (2609.32999, 2026-09-26, cs.DC), FULL TEXT. Disaggregated Retrieval/KV/Generation pools where the retrieval tier is the control plane for KV retention and cache-aware routing. The measurement that matters to us: on OpenClaw+OpenViking traces (LoCoMo 642 requests, tau2-bench 219 memory-augmented requests, GPT-5-mini driving), 73.9% (LoCoMo) and 76.3% (tau2) of injected memory-record tokens recur BEYOND the matching prompt prefix; prefix-covered repeats are 24.8% and 2.8%; non-prefix repeats are 31.5-53.8% of complete prompt tokens. Prototype extends SGLang; up to 3.6x lower mean TTFT than SGLang and LMCache with EPIC at matched request rates; releases the first public request-level traces of agents with retrieval-based memory. Takeaway (verbatim): non-prefix recurrence makes "the context unit, not the prompt prefix, the natural unit of reuse."
6. CADOC (2609.37012, 2026-09-29, cs.AI), FULL TEXT. Cache-Aware Dynamic Object Context: reversible externalization of structured objects (file contents, tool results, logs) into retrieval Cards (object:// refs with immutable versions; exact byte recovery via a tool call), a protected raw hot tail, and an LLM-free EOQ-style online rule for WHEN to commit replacements in batches: commit when waiting loss + cached-token shortening benefit crosses the shared cache-reconstruction premium (commit iff W_t + w_r*G_t >= Q_t, w_r = cache-read cost weight 0.1). The pricing number: on Nous Hermes Compression Evaluation histories, immediate replacement removes 55.93% of cumulative input tokens yet RAISES input cost 3.67% vs Full Context, because the edit rekeys the cached suffix. CADOC: ~40% input-cost reduction at comparable task quality; lowest cost of all commit policies at every hot-tail setting h in {2,4,8,16} without per-setting tuning.
7. How Fast Do Agents Rot? (2609.01660, 2026-08-31, physics.soc-ph + cs.AI), ABS ONLY (no HTML). Controlled degradation study: nine models (six open, 1.2B to 671B parameters, plus three proprietary), four task families, five horizons, three context regimes, n=10,664 analyzed trajectories. Task success follows a geometric law with a single per-step reliability parameter that saturates well below 1 even for the strongest models. On the agentic task every model falls from near-perfect to near zero within sixteen steps. Degradation is driven by step count rather than context length: bounding the context window STEEPENS decay (logit slope -0.69 vs -0.44, p=3e-6), contradicting a pure lost-in-the-middle explanation.

## Answers to the five research questions

RQ1 (what landed upstream of lmcache; what applies to the frozen-prefix discipline at 6.5% to 98%). The estate's frozen byte-identical prefix (mag/context_pack.py:470-522 per the 09-28 sweep's overlap baseline; hit-ratio proof in memory/rib/RIB_SELF_MY_EFFICIENCY.md:6-9, cited via laws lens :28) is prefix-keyed reuse. What moved upstream this window is all reuse-beyond-the-prefix: (a) Tessera measures that prefix keying leaves 74-76% of injected memory tokens unreusable in exactly our workload shape (memory records injected after system prompt / after history), and ships the shared-pool + cache-aware-routing serving layer explicitly benchmarked against LMCache; (b) the PIC lineage (EPIC/CacheBlend) matured three ways: read-side (Attuner: adapt the query, never the cache), repair-side (CacheRepair: learned all-token residual; RelaxKV: joint target+context repair), and build-side (ARC-KV: amortized reconstruction of a long reusable prefix's compact cache, built once, reused for every downstream query - the "pay the context tax once" discipline, now with a 25.73x cheaper tax). Which apply to the frozen-prefix discipline: ARC-KV for the estate's own giant shared prefix (compress it once per context, keep serving all siblings), Attuner for per-seat artifact loading (RIBs/skills as position-independent artifacts), CacheRepair/RelaxKV when the tail delta changes but the prefix holds, Tessera's numbers as the argument for unit-keyed (RIB-id-keyed) rather than position-keyed cache entries.

RQ2 (janitor-class context-length collapse; the real budget). HONEST: the 30-day window contains no paper measuring context-length collapse for 2B-class janitor models specifically; the small-LM feed's nearest items are out-of-window (SLM survey 2409.15790, Infini-attention small-scale pretraining 2512.23862). The in-window evidence that bears on budget-setting is horizon-shaped, not window-shaped: How Fast Do Agents Rot? (1.2B models included) shows per-step reliability saturating below 1 and bounding the context window making decay STEEPER, not gentler. So the unmeasured assumption stays unmeasured by literature; what the window adds is a warning that cutting the window is not a free quality lever, and the collapse variable to instrument is steps-and-decisions, not tokens alone. The estate's own 2B context-budget experiment (proposed in the 09-28 sweep RQ2 as the 3-arm gemma battery) remains OWED and is still the only way to set the context-pack budget honestly.

RQ3 (do context_checkpoint / context_compression invariants re-derive, or are they stale virtual edges). Both, separately. As graph edges they are stale BY CONSTRUCTION: graph.md:11-19 (this run's lens) shows 823 of 825 knowledge-graph edges target truncated virtual ids (invariant ids truncated to 40 chars at mag/knowledge_graph.py:165), and graph.md:24 lists context_checkpoint and context_compression among the modules at the 4-invariant-link lenient ceiling - so their 4 links each sit in the dangling class and resolve to nothing until the three id fixes (graph.md:35-37) land. As CONTENT, the invariants re-derive from this window's literature: checkpoint/commit timing now has a measured cache-aware answer (CADOC: immediate commits can cost MORE than doing nothing, 55.93% token cut vs +3.67% cost; batch on the crossing rule), and compression now has unit-reuse and repair mechanisms (Tessera, CacheRepair, Attuner). Treatment: apply the three graph fixes first, then re-hang the invariants with these citations; before that, the edges carry no meaning to any id-keyed consumer.

RQ4 (swarm child payload shape: full pack vs delta vs rib pointer). No paper runs that three-arm experiment; the deliberate-ask shape (frozen prefix + tight ask + output contract) remains the estate's only measured shape. But each arm now has published economics: full pack is priced by Tessera (74-76% of injected memory tokens are repeats that a unit-keyed cache could serve; sending them raw every time is the expensive arm); the pointer arm is Cards (CADOC: exact-retrieval object refs), with the critical warning that an untimed pointer swap RAISES cost (3.67%) by rekeying the cached suffix, so pointers need commit scheduling, and CADOC's ~40% cut is the measured upside when the timing rule is right; the delta arm is CacheRepair/RelaxKV (reuse per-chunk caches, repair the delta: 1.69-4.61x TTFT with F1 gains). The missing instrument - arms x (cache hit rate, answer fidelity, tokens-to-correct) on the estate's own swarm battery - stays OWED; CADOC's w_r=0.1 cache-read weight and its replay protocol on frozen histories is the closest published template for running it.
