## Answers to the four research questions

### Q1. What 2026 quantization results show measured quality retention at janitor-class scale on held-out evals rather than perplexity alone?

The month's strongest convergence: **perplexity is now openly called insufficient by three independent groups, and the replacement metrics are behavioral.**

- **2609.23125 (2026-09-19):** 12 models, 4 families, 780 within-model comparisons, induction + retrieval accuracy instead of perplexity. Perplexity picks the better arm in all but 2.1 percent (induction) / 4.0 percent (retrieval) of cases, BUT small perplexity rises (1.2-1.5x) hide breakdowns: "induction still keeps 0.959 of its intact accuracy while retrieval has already fallen to 0.554". A rotation-based quantization restores induction from 0.001 to 0.980 at ~3 average bits/token. Direct lesson for the janitor fleet: a good-looking perplexity delta does not tell you which computations survived; run task-shaped probes.
- **2609.32042 (2026-09-25, GGUF, the serving-format study):** three 7B-11B Polish models, six GGUF levels Q8_0 to Q2_K, held-out agentic suite (PolAgentBench, 67 tasks + 46-task arithmetic ladder). "Only the collapse threshold replicates": all three fall off a cliff between Q3_K_M and Q2_K (11B 0.716 to 0.045; 7B 0.463 to 0.149; PLLuM 0.224 to 0.015; McNemar p < 0.001 each), while failure MODES do not generalize (long-winding vs immediate confabulation vs content failure). Practitioner rule from the full text: never run these models below 3 bits for agentic work; the 11B safe band is 6-8 bit; weak models are capability-bound, not precision-bound.
- **2608.28809 (2026-08-28, 3 days outside window, marked):** the closest to janitor scale: Qwen3.5-0.8B (752M) ternary-converted with 72.4M QAT tokens, evaluated on 29 benchmarks. Degradation is capability-stratified: specialist knowledge collapses (linear probe on MMLU representations 43.76 percent teacher vs 26.19 percent converted, near chance) while general tasks keep on average 77.1 percent of teacher performance; verdict "unsuitable as a drop-in general replacement yet remains valuable as a compact substrate for task-specific models". Also catches an eval trap we should steal: the converted model answered "A" on 98.6 percent of MMLU under letter scoring, so they switched to continuation scoring.
- **2609.32902 (2026-09-26):** 2.5M-50M GPT-2-style models trained from scratch, synthetic biographies as a known-information-content yardstick (factual bits per parameter, not perplexity): ternary retains "as little as 6% of an identically trained fp16 model's capacity" while perplexity rises only 1.4-1.6x, and post-training quantization recovers no measurable capacity below 4 bits. Schedule matters more than method: a warmup-stable-decay schedule with 10 percent cooldown lifts ternary capacity 2.7x (25M) / 4.3x (50M).
- **1.58-bit successors:** **2609.16338 (2026-09-14)** measures zero density in 29 ternary LLMs (zeros "up to 51.5% of all weights") and exploits it: BITCOS, "a simple distribution-adaptive layout comprised of a dense presence bitmap plus a compacted sign vector", costs 2 - z bits per weight, beating five-trit packing (1.625 bpw) in 26 of 29 models, best case 1.485 bpw, with measured throughput gains (1.18x CPU, 1.27x GPU decode). This is a storage/throughput result, not a quality result; the quality side of sub-1.58-bit stays carried by 2608.28809 (negative at 0.8B general scale).

Janitor-class gap, stated as OWED: no in-window paper I read evaluates a 2B-class model on held-out evals across GGUF levels. Nearest evidence: 7-11B GGUF (2609.32042), 0.8B ternary conversion (2608.28809), and one feed-gist-only hit at exactly our scale, Gemma-3-1B layer-wise INT8 (2608.28003, 13 W8A8 variants, latency vs top-1 agreement and perplexity degradation; not read, unbanked). A gemma:2b/Gemma-3-1B held-out sweep is the funded-tier experiment this gap names.

### Q2. Do the 4 auto_quantize invariants survive the five-axis emergence test?

The 4 invariants, extracted this session from `memory/knowledge_graph.json` node `module:auto_quantize.py` and confirmed in code at `mag/auto_quantize.py:22-24` (laws), `:113-118` (variance-to-level ladder with anomalies forced to L0), `:77` (per-bucket Verkle chain anchor). Five axes per `watch/inbox/frontier_olm_2026-09-30/lens/laws.md:34`: invariant alignment first, then cost, quality on held-out data, variance/reproducibility, generalization.

- **Inv 1 "SCHEMA: auto_quantize.v1"**: a schema tag, not an empirical claim; nothing to test against literature. Stands.
- **Inv 2 "Anomalies are NEVER quantized. Keep raw forever."**: ALIGNED and the literature has converged on the same shape from the weight side. 2609.23125 shows quantization damage concentrates in specific computations (induction/retrieval) invisible to aggregate loss, and that protecting them (rotation) restores 0.980 induction; 2609.24799 shows the content that quietly dies under W4A4KV4 is exactly the exceptional material (answer-supporting evidence in rationales) while modal accuracy holds. "Keep the outliers raw" is the invariant both papers re-derive. Alignment axis passes.
- **Inv 3 "Variance determines resolution. Low variance = high compression."**: HALF-STALE as written. The 2026 mixed-precision literature allocates bits by quantization SENSITIVITY (curvature/probe-based: 2609.33923 random-probe sensitivity, feed gist; 2609.01587 causal mixed-precision interventions, feed gist; 2609.28270 priced covariance selection, feed gist), not by variance of the observed data. 2609.23125 supplies the reason a single scalar is unsafe: an average (perplexity, or a variance) can be nearly unchanged while the protected computation is destroyed (induction at 0.554 retrieval). For the analytics domain the invariant still works because the metric variance is the quantity of interest; for anything model-adjacent it must be restated as sensitivity-based allocation verified by held-out eval. Quality axis: fails as stated, fixable by restatement.
- **Inv 4 "The chain root is always recoverable from any quantization level."**: TRUE in its own domain (the code keeps L0 raw events plus a Verkle `chain_anchor` per bucket, `mag/auto_quantize.py:77,113-118`) and the reading that survives literature contact is: the root lives outside the quantized representation; you never invert a lossy level. That is exactly what 2609.05881 demonstrates negatively: 5 of 305 official GGUF artifacts (1.6 percent) silently defective, scoring 0/164 across both backends, two producing "normal-looking surface statistics", i.e., the field's quantized derivatives are NOT reliably re-derivable without an execution gate against the full-precision root. Generalization axis: survives only in the restated form "raw root retained + every derivative execution-gated".
- **Variance axis (the emergence-test axis, not the invariant):** 2609.07901 is the direct measurement and it flips the naive expectation: quantization reduces output variance at small scale (see Q3), so any variance-driven mechanism must measure OUTPUT distribution, not just input metric variance.
- **Verdict:** inv 1 stands, inv 2 aligns and is literature-corroborated, inv 3 needs restatement (data variance to sensitivity + held-out verification) before any promotion, inv 4 needs restatement (invertibility to root-retention + derivative gating). None of the restatements are promotable on sensing alone: per the driving ladder (`watch/inbox/frontier_olm_2026-09-30/lens/laws.md:23-24`) they are banked findings; funded-tier verification is owed before promotion.

Stale-cite finding, reported straight: the estate map names "its promote gate (mag/percolator.py:264-272)". In the current tree those lines are the Z15 heal-merge MAX-count code (`mag/percolator.py:265-266`), and `grep -n "promote"` over mag/percolator.py and mag/auto_quantize.py returns nothing. No promote gate exists at the cited location. The prior sweep reached the same shape conclusion from a different angle (`watch/inbox/frontier_olm_20260928/sweep/evals-self-improvement-x.md:68`).

### Q3. Does agreement survive aggressive quantization (consensus-illusion extension)?

Agreement does not merely survive: **aggressive quantization MANUFACTURES agreement**, which makes the estate's consensus-illusion finding (laws.md:33) stronger, not weaker.

- **2609.07901 (2026-09-07, full text read):** ~71,000 paired completions, Qwen3-8B/14B/32B at W4A16 AWQ, W8A16 FP8, bf16, same 20 seeds per tier. At 8B, int4 raises cross-sample concentration: "the probability that two samples for the same scenario recommend the same brand increases by 5.1 percentage points" (Holm p = .023, replicated at +4.4pp); one prompt flips from bf16 Tesla 13/20 to int4 Tesla 20/20. Mechanism dissociation: token-level entropy RISES (+0.091 bits) while semantic distributions concentrate (+2.6pp first-token collision), i.e., "a shift from semantic alternatives to surface variation". At 14B/32B the effect vanishes into stylistic drift (em-dash rate +0.46/1k and +0.61/1k words). Their audit rule is the estate's rule: audits should measure concentration (collision probability across k repeats), because agreement-looking concentration can pass a bias audit while the model narrows the answer set.
- **2609.24799:** consensus on the ANSWER survives W4A4KV4 while the evidence under it weakens "substantially", so answer-level agreement at quantized scale masks lost justification.
- **2608.28809:** degenerate convergence in the extreme: the ternary-converted 0.8B picks "A" on 98.6 percent of MMLU items, the purest possible agreement with itself and the least information.
- Marked out-of-window context (feed gists + abs summaries, not banked): 2607.20129 runs five-sample self-consistency on INT-quantized reasoners as a MONITORED signal (70.0 percent MATH-500 at ~4.84x token cost) rather than a correctness oracle, and 2606.02628 finds self-consistency detectors underperform linear probes on 4-bit NF4 models.

Net: at janitor-class scale, multi-sample agreement under quantization is an artifact of modal collapse, not evidence of correctness. The flock's high-consensus-wrong failure mode has a quantization amplifier; any consensus decode must run full-precision or treat concentration itself as the alarm.

### Q4. What quantize target belongs in a deliberate ask so outputs stay byte-verifiable and re-derivable (map law, laws.md:35)?

Synthesis of what I read, stated as the contract the evidence supports (design, not an executed mechanism):

1. **Target:** Q4_K_M floor, Q3_K_M working edge, Q2_K never (2609.32042: threshold cliff replicates at 3-to-2 bit on all three models; failures at 2-bit are well-formed confabulations, the worst kind for a harness that parses shape). For sub-4-bit, expect capability-stratified loss first in specialist knowledge (2608.28809), so quantize task-specific janitors, never the generalist-of-all-trades, matching 28809's own verdict.
2. **Pin bytes, not labels:** the ask names the exact artifact hash, quant level, runtime and backend (2609.05881: same file healthy on Metal, crippled on CUDA; 1.6 percent of a national registry silently defective). Byte-verifiability of OUTPUTS starts with byte-identity of the MODEL; a quant label is not a spec.
3. **Acceptance gate in the ask:** a 15-task smoke screen scored against the same model family's high-precision baseline before the artifact is trusted, escalating suspects to a full eval plus an independent-conversion referee (2609.05881's chain, median 30s per artifact). "Would have caught every defect we found."
4. **Contract on content, not shape:** shape survives quantization (2609.32042's 2-bit confabulations are well-formed; 2609.07901's int4 outputs stay well-formed), so the output contract must demand re-derivable content (verbatim source lines, byte-referenced paths) rather than valid JSON, and verification must be execution/held-out based, never agreement-based (Q3).
5. **Per-model, never cross-model:** thresholds generalize, failure modes do not (2609.32042), so every quantize target in a deliberate ask carries its own receipt.
