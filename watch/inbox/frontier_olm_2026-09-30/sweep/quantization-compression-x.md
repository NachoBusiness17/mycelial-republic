## Answers to the four research questions

### RQ1. 2026 quantization results with measured quality retention at janitor-class scale on held-out evals, not perplexity alone

The strongest janitor-class-scale datapoint this window is NEGATIVE for ternary-at-small-scale: "Post-Training Ternarization of Qwen3-4B" (2609.01962, Sep 2 2026) reports a 4B model ternarized to 1.641 effective bits across 81.62 percent of parameters falls from 64.5 to 54.7 percent accuracy across ten scored capability comparisons, with retention wildly uneven by capability (BoolQ keeps 84.6 percent of chance-corrected teacher performance, ARC-Challenge keeps 43.8 percent) while perplexity moves look tame by comparison (WikiText-2 13.639 to 18.748). The pack step (8.29 GiB to 3.96 GiB) left perplexity unchanged but was never benchmarked end to end, and their Triton GEMV microbench was 4.6x SLOWER than FP16 cuBLAS on the tested shape: compression claimed, speed not, honesty stated.

The mechanism behind "PPL lies" got its own paper: "Linger and Lose: Knowledge Collapse in Low-Bit Language Models" (2609.32902, Sep 26 2026) trains GPT-2-scale models (2.5M-50M params) at five precisions against synthetic biographies with known information content and finds ternary models "first acquire capacity and then lose most of it" (as little as 6 percent of an fp16 twin's knowledge capacity) while perplexity rises only 1.4-1.6x. Post-training quantization below 4 bits recovered no measurable capacity. Cause: a learning-rate dwell instability localized in the output head. Implication the estate should steal: any auto_quantize-style variance gate that watches loss-space variance is watching the wrong axis at low bits.

Held-out-eval hygiene on the GGUF side: Unsloth's Qwen3.5 GGUF benchmarks page (read this session) ran "over 150 KL Divergence benchmarks, totally 9TB of GGUFs" and still warns "Perplexity and KLD can be misleading as they're highly influenced by calibration", plus a tensor-sensitivity law ("Quantizing any attn_* is especially sensitive"). Even the most industrial GGUF measurement pipeline does not trust its own calibration-conditioned metrics; held-out task evals remain the decider.

On the 1.58-bit line and successors: "Breaking the 1.58-bit Barrier for Ternary LLMs" (2609.16338, Sep 14 2026, Intel) measures symbol distributions across 29 ternary models, finds "zeros account for up to 51.5% of all weights", and ships BITCOS, a distribution-adaptive layout (presence bitmap + compacted sign vector) costing 2-z bits per weight, hitting 1.485 bpw on the sparsest model with 1.18-1.27x end-to-end decode gains. NOTE: its claims are storage and speed only; the abstract makes NO accuracy claim, so it extends the 1.58-bit story on the compression axis, not the quality axis.

AWQ at small scale got a privacy-shaped answer (2609.25014, flagged id/date mismatch below): across five open models 0.5-7B, calibration-based AWQ/GPTQ reproduced NONE of planted PII records at 4 bits while calibration-free GGUF Q4_K_M reproduced 5.3 percent; "AWQ leaks least at every size and in both families". Quantizer choice is a measured, per-deployment decision at exactly janitor scale.

### RQ2. Do the 4 auto_quantize invariants survive the five-axis emergence test, or are they stale?

Invariant alignment gate first, per laws.md:34:

1. `Anomalies are NEVER quantized. Keep raw forever.` SURVIVES, and this window's literature strengthens it. 2609.05881 (held from 09-28, re-verified today on its abs page): two of five silently defective official GGUF artifacts "produce output whose surface statistics sit inside the healthy range, invisible to any low-noise heuristic short of execution". 2609.32042 (held, deep-read 09-28): 2-bit failures confabulate well-formed answers, i.e. the anomaly class LOOKS healthy on aggregates. 2608.06564: even strong full-precision decision preferences can flip under quantization, so per-instance raw evidence is the only safe artifact. Anomaly-keeps-raw is the aligned answer to all three.
2. `Variance determines resolution. Low variance = high compression.` SURVIVES ONLY WITH A RE-SPECIFIED VARIANCE AXIS. 2609.32902 is the direct hit: loss-space variance stays near-flat (PPL 1.4-1.6x) while knowledge capacity collapses 16x. A gate computing variance over behavioral loss/stats, as auto_quantize.py's L-ladder does over behavioral stats, would see LOW variance and compress a model that is silently collapsing. Verdict: not stale, but its variance input must be capability-space (held-out probes), not loss-space. This is a funded-tier verification item before any promotion: re-run the estate's variance gate against a held-out capability probe set on gemma:2b/gemma4 quant variants.
3. `The chain root is always recoverable from any quantization level.` SURVIVES as design, with a verification rider. 2609.16338 (BITCOS) proves distribution-adaptive sub-nominal-bit layouts with full recoverability are real when the symbol distribution is measured (2-z bits per weight). But 2609.01962 is the cautionary twin: a packed artifact with "essentially unchanged perplexity" was NOT benchmarked end to end. Recoverability claims need an execution receipt (the 2609.05881 quantcheck pattern), not a packing argument. The estate's cryptographic anchors at every resolution level (auto_quantize.py:14) are the right shape; the delta is the smoke-suite gate.
4. `SCHEMA: auto_quantize.v1` INTERNAL SCHEMA, not literature-testable. No external claim to verify; it carries no falsifiable content under the five-axis test and needs none. Stated as out of scope, not as passing.

Net: no invariant is refuted; invariant 3 (variance) is the one needing funded-tier verification before promotion, because the failure mode (wrong-axis variance) is now published.

### RQ3. Quantization-induced divergence in multi-sample / consensus decoding

Two 2026 papers sharpen the consensus-illusion finding (laws.md:33) from "agreement is not correctness" to "quantization makes agreement structurally misleading":

- 2608.06564 "Which Decisions Low-Bit Quantization Breaks, and How to Predict Them" (v4 Sep 28 2026; 16 models, 8 families, 4/3/2 bits): quantization acts as PROPORTIONAL MARGIN SHRINKAGE, "m'" ~ c*m + b" (their notation), not random noise. Two consequences the estate needs: (a) errors are CORRELATED across samples, which is the worst case for majority voting, since consensus amplifies the shared shrinkage direction instead of averaging it away; (b) the flip rate is PREDICTABLE with median error 1.0 percentage point on held-out combinations, and calibrated quantizers flip fewer decisions ("Under GPTQ and GGUF far fewer whether-to-call decisions flip than under plain rounding"). Extends the consensus-illusion: agreement does not merely fail to survive aggressive quantization, it survives WRONG in a predictable shared direction.
- 2609.32042 (held; the generalization axis): "Only the collapse threshold replicates" across three models; the failure MODES do not (7B dies on long trajectories, 11B confabulates instantly, PLLuM fails content at every precision). Multi-sample agreement between DIFFERENTLY quantized replicas is therefore measuring the shared threshold, not truth.
- Estate translation: the flock's consensus decode rides surrogate vocabularies (laws.md:33 root cause); quantization adds shared margin bias on top. Any fleet consensus experiment must sample across DIFFERENT quant levels and report flip-direction correlation, not just agreement rate.
