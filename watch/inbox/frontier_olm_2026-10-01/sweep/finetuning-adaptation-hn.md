## RQ4: catastrophic forgetting under continual adaptation of small models

- **Continual Learning Mechanisms Compose for Long-Horizon Memorization** (arXiv 2609.06986, Zhang, Zhang, Khashabi, Shu, submitted 2026-09-07). 100-task continual SFT without task IDs: naive sequential fine-tuning retains 1.2%; the best composed method (data + function + weight anchors with merged LoRA; anchors and low-rank allocation as the two composing dimensions) retains 34.9%, a 28-fold improvement, top-3 on all three 100-task datasets, data-anchor x merged-LoRA interaction super-additive. Quote: "Together, these results show that composing complementary mechanisms substantially improves long-horizon memorization beyond what any individual mechanism achieves."
- **Mini-AGI, dynamic continual learning on 8 GB VRAM** (github.com/volotat/mini-AGI, HN 49783133, 2026-09-21, 277 pts / 81 comments). Byte-level model, ~559M params paged from disk (32 experts of ~175 resident), reading and training are the same code path. Forgetting mechanism: trunk (98% of gradient norm) runs at 0.1x the experts' LR; PG19 probe "learned per nat forgotten" 3.25 vs 0.23-0.25 for unmitigated arms (97.3% retention); interleaving a new domain as a ninth lane retained 100.13%. Quote: "It stores its weights as ordinary files on disk and pages them onto the card as it needs them, so the parameter count is bounded by free disk space."
