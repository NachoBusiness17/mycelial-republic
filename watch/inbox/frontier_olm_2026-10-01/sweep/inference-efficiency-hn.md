### 4. Prefill Concurrency in SGLang: Consistent TTFT Under Multi-Tenant Load - sference.com (HN 49856359, 2026-09-26; article dated 2026-09-22)
https://sference.com/blog/prefill-head-of-line-blocking
The provider-side concurrency envelope, with math: effective prefill concurrency = `chunked_prefill_size // long_prefill_token_threshold` (Kimi-K3: 8192//4096 = 2; DeepSeek-V4-Flash: 16384//4096 = 4 concurrent partial prefills).
