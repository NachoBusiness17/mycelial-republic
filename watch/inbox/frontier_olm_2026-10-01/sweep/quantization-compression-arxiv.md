## Answers to the four research questions

### Q1. Which quantization formats give the best measured quality-per-byte for 2B-8B janitor-class models on llama.cpp/Ollama?

Nothing this window overturns the standing verdict the prior sweeps already banked (Q4_K_M safe floor, Q3_K_M working edge, Q2_K cliff per PolAgentBench). The delta this window is on the production side:

- **QATFactory (2609.39223, read this session)** closes the train-to-serve gap: quantization-aware training AND distillation that simulate deployment formats (NVFP4, MXFP4, and llama.cpp's own Q4_K) during BF16 training, then "exports checkpoints directly to vLLM and llama.cpp without an additional lossy conversion step or added inference overhead" (abs page, verbatim). On Qwen3.5-9B their QAD arm reaches 68.9 percent average benchmark accuracy under NVFP4 and 66.0 under MXFP4 vs best-PTQ 65.4 and 56.4. For the estate this is the first in-window recipe that targets GGUF Q4_K natively at training time rather than post-hoc.
- **Tetra (2609.35465, HTML full text read this session)** marks the sub-3-bit frontier and its cost: at 2.70-2.73 bits/parameter whole-model, Qwen3-4B/8B/14B score 4.76/4.21/2.46 MMLU points below 4-bit AWQ, and the 4B file beats llama.cpp IQ2_XXS by 23.6 points at 2.73 vs 2.48 bits/parameter. Conditions: single NVIDIA L40S, author's custom CUDA engine for Tetra vs llama.cpp build b10689 for the baseline, preregistered main experiments; no GGUF path, and the authors state three calibration draws spread MMLU by more than the row-scale training gains at 4B. Takeaway for the janitor fleet: below ~3 bits the llama.cpp-native formats give up enormous quality (IQ2_XXS at 2.48 bpw is 23.6 MMLU points behind a same-lattice research format), which reinforces treating Q3 as the floor rather than chasing 2-bit GGUFs.
- **Broken on Arrival (2609.05881, full text read this session, banked 09-30)** remains the intake tripwire: 5 of 305 official Ollama GGUF artifacts silently defective (all solving 0 of 164 tasks; two producing fluent, healthy-looking prose), and two TheBloke community conversions (deepseek-coder-6.7B q3_K_M and q4_K_M) that score 5/15 and 6/15 on CUDA yet 13/15 and 15/15 on Metal at the same pinned Ollama runtime. The estate's Windows/CUDA janitor seats are on the failing side of that split; their quantcheck-style 15-task smoke gate (median 30 seconds) is the receipt-shaped defense.

### Q2. Measured Q4 vs Q8 quality delta with hardware and task conditions (a verdict without conditions is not a verdict)

- **Calibrated Instrument (2609.18005, abs read this session; held in catalog since 09-17, not re-banked)**: with a formally calibrated LLM-judge (null condition must measure zero; resolution plus/minus 0.3 points), "A 4-bit model was indistinguishable from its 16-bit original down to our design's +/-0.3-point resolution, in English prose and Chinese alike." At 3-bit the same prompts lost 0.5 points on English prose, 0.9 on Chinese, 1.1 on multi-step math, and the same quantizer cost Meta's model 1.8 points where it cost Alibaba's 0.7. Conditions: judge-calibrated design, domain-stratified prompts, two model families. This is the domain-dependence law in measured form: Q4-vs-Q8 has no single delta; it has a per-domain delta, and any estate quant verdict must name its domain and judge resolution.
- **PolAgentBench (2609.32042, HTML re-read this session)**: agentic tool use on one RTX 4090, llama-cpp-python 0.3.19, temperature 0, 8192 context: Bielik-11B goes 0.806 (Q8_0) to 0.537 (Q4_K_M) to 0.716 (Q3_K_M) to 0.045 (Q2_K); Bielik-7B 0.448 to 0.507 to 0.463 to 0.149; PLLuM-8B 0.194 to 0.224 to 0.224 to 0.015. The non-monotonic dips (11B Q5_K_M 0.746, Q4_K_M 0.537) did not survive multiplicity correction, and repair-on raises 7B Q8_0 to 0.567: for a mid-capability model the repair layer beat every precision choice.
- **Green AI code completion (2609.33918, abs read this session)**: 25 open-weight LLMs on RepoBench and McEval; "Smaller and heavily quantized models frequently achieve Pareto-optimal trade-offs" (abs, verbatim), with energy drivers split by task: input context dominates repo-level completion, output generation and active parameter count dominate fill-in-the-middle. The abstract does not name the quantization levels or hardware; those conditions stay OWED to a full-text read.

### Q3. 2026 KV cache / context compression that fits a local-first harness, with reported quality cost

- **KV-COBRA (2609.24298, abs read this session)**: "We argue that it is not the choice of compression scheme, but how its budget is allocated across attention heads" (abs, verbatim); co-optimizing per-head rank truncation and bit-width dominates uniform allocation with the largest gains at 0.5-4 bits per dimension, no per-token overhead. Allocation over scheme is a cheap lesson: the estate's llama.cpp KV-quant knobs should be tuned per-layer/per-head before any scheme swap is considered.
- **WUSH-KV (2609.38121, abs read this session)**: calibration-built second-order key/value transforms enable strong 2-bit KV caches; "At 2-bit, WUSH-KV performs comparably to or outperforms the OSCAR transform across all evaluated models and downstream tasks" (abs, verbatim), integrated into SGLang. Not llama.cpp, but it is the current 2-bit-KV reference point and its transform idea (whitening K/V before quantization) is exactly the kind of thing llama.cpp could adopt upstream.
- **D-Quant (2609.19880, abs read this session)**: entropy-coded KV is variable-length, which fights parallel kernels; "a drift mechanism to convert entropy-coded representations of each token into fixed-size bitstreams" (abs, verbatim, LaTeX markup as rendered). Storage-side enabler if the estate ever offloads long-context KV.
- **The KV Cache Is the New Memory Wall (2609.30854, abs read this session, held, not re-banked)** supplies the governing condition for all of the above: "the binding resource shifts from model weights to the Key-Value (KV) cache as sequence length grows" and "below a hardware-specific crossover, weight traffic dominates and KV compression yields negligible speedup" (abs, verbatim). For janitor-class short-context runs the KV tricks are noise; they pay only when context grows past the machine's crossover. The 09-28 kv-cache lane already established the honest negative for our scale: no in-window paper treats llama.cpp/Ollama KV quantization specifically.

### Q4. Frontier-published distillation of reasoning models into small deployable form factors, reproducible without datacenter hardware

- **QATFactory (2609.39223)** is the strongest reproducible recipe: open-source, QAT + distillation + RL, dense and MoE, full-parameter and LoRA, format-faithful export to llama.cpp; 32K-token sequences gain 1.9 points over 4K at fixed token budget (abs). Training cost is not stated on the abs page (models up to 230B are claimed as supported); at janitor scale a LoRA pass is the plausible estate shape. OWED: a local feasibility estimate before any dispatch.
- **Bangladesh legal 2B (2609.24177, abs read this session)**: the end-to-end proof at our exact form factor: Gemma-2-9B teacher distilled to a 2B student, exported as a 1.6 GB GGUF Q4_K_M, "On a 50-query English benchmark, the distilled model reaches ROUGE-L 0.4715 and BERTScore F1 0.5679" (abs, verbatim; baselines 0.2323/0.2340), 4-8 tok/s fully offline on a Pixel 6, human pilot 4.16/5 from a practising lawyer. A domain RAG janitor in a phone footprint, no datacenter anywhere in the serving path.
- **RLC (2609.39346, abs read this session)**: the no-training alternative: "RLC stores these experiences in an external bank and retrieves them according to the SLM's current reasoning state, enabling the SLM to reuse LLM-derived corrections during inference without any online LLM calls" (abs, verbatim). Fixed SLM parameters, LLM accessed only offline. This is the descent sherpa leg in academic form: bank premium corrections as retrievable experiences and let the cheap seat apply them; the estate's equivalent substrate already exists (ribs + residual + registry), so the steal is the retrieval key (reasoning-state-conditioned) rather than the storage.
- **RATIO (2609.39801, abs read this session)**: quantized reasoning models inherit overthinking: token-level penalties tuned per model improve accuracy up to 9.8 points while shortening chain-of-thought up to 51.3 percent vs quantized baselines. The abs page does not name the models or PTQ schemes (OWED to full text). Directly relevant to the reasoning-tax law: a quantized janitor that reasons burns the same token tax, and the fix is token-level steering, not a bigger model.
