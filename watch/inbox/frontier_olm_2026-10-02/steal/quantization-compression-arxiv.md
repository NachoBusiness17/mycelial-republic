## The steal (the mechanism, formula corrected)

A quantization ladder is specifiable as four laws plus one scaffold rule:

1. **Capacity floor, not perplexity floor.** PTQ recovers no measurable knowledge capacity below 4 bits; ternary training retains as little as 6% of fp16 knowledge capacity while perplexity rises only 1.4-1.6x (find 1, ask quote). The acceptance gate for any quantized janitor scores held-out recall against the fp16 root; a 1.5x perplexity delta is compatible with a 94% knowledge loss and gates nothing.
2. **Rotation cost collapse.** ThinQuant completes W4A4KV4 rotation calibration for Llama-3-70B in under 12 minutes (DartQuant: 111) at WikiText-2 ppl 5.63, scaling to 405B on one H200 at W4A4 ppl 2.97; KV4 rides a fixed random Hadamard; no runtime bytes-per-token figure (find 2, ask quote and why; sweep :24 concurs). Calibration cost feeds the gate-3 purchase decision; the recipe pins under gate 4.
3. **KV is the memory wall, with a corrected closed-form budget.** Per-token KV bytes = 2*L*n_kv*d_h*w_kv (no s); for Llama-3-70B BF16: 327,680 B = 0.33 MB/token; an s-token sequence costs per-token x s (42.9 GB at 128k, matching the stub's 42 GB; the stub also gives 140 GB weights vs 80 GB HBM and 0.07 MB/token for DeepSeek-V2 MLA per the ask's why). 8-bit KV free, 4-bit near-free with per-channel keys, naive 2-bit collapses more than 1 ppl without a residual window, and weight+KV degradation composes sub-multiplicatively (2.05x measured vs 2.63x multiplied) (find 3, ask why; sweep :24).
4. **Scaffold law for the sub-3-bit regime.** A trained NVFP4 prefiller improves 1-bit decode accuracy by +32.5 MMLU-Pro / +35.3 MMMU-Pro without modifying the decode checkpoint, verified verbatim in the stub (`state/arxiv/2026-09-23/2609.26333v1.txt`, read this session); each engine needs its own path (vLLM disaggregated serving, custom llama.cpp fork for SSD-offloaded prefill; the stub adds ODP at 1.78x time-to-first-token over weight-only at 8K prompt in llama.cpp). Sub-3-bit serving survives only scaffolded; the 3-bit agentic floor stands for unscaffolded decode.
