## The five questions, answered from what was read

**Q1 judge designs vs judge-weakness / imagined-grader hacking (banked: Ising aggregation 9-14 pct, DeepSWE naming GLM 5.3, CRJudgeBench GLM-5.3 weakness 76.60 minus 6.13).** Three instruments survive the measured weakness classes. (a) Test-theory honesty: 2609.29709 shows the reliability number a judge bank produces is mostly a property of the BANK, not the judge: at fixed 4.72 pct judge error, sweeping bank true-score spread moves KR-20 from 0.007 to 0.679, and judge error moves the coefficient by 0.456 vs 0.722 for bank spread; cells show identical coefficients at DOUBLE the judge error. So judge2 must report bank characteristics next to every reliability number and compute the same coefficient on gold verdicts (their four-line reporting rule). (b) Latent-solver gating: 2608.18719 bounds reference-free judges by their own solving competence (AUC > 1/2 iff c > 1/k, sec 3.2) and measured false-accepts of 0.33 (factual) and 0.50 (math) for judges failing the screen vs 0.00 for the passing judge (Table 5); its non-intervening probe is exactly the cheap pre-deployment screen judge2 needs before GLM-5.3 duel dollars route. (c) Aggregation design: 2609.38847 states "We show that this additive aggregation is the weak point" - additive rubric aggregation lets criteria compensate for each other; grouped protocol-level dimensions (ProRubric) recover task appropriateness. This is the direct complement to the banked Ising-aggregation 9-14 pct row: aggregation structure is an attack surface, not a fixed choice. Also: 2609.34198 shows judge false-acceptance on imperfect patches RISES with agent capability and transported calibration raises comparison error 3.8 -> 19.5 points, so historical duel rows re-judged by a today-judge (laws.md:58 mass re-judging) must re-judge in PAIRS on current outputs, not reuse transported scores.
