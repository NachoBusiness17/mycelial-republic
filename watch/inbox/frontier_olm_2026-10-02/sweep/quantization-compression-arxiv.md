## Research-question verdicts

**RQ1 (agentic/task metrics vs perplexity; do the laws survive?):** CONFIRMED and sharpened, not overturned. The held PolAgentBench (2609.32042, three Polish models, Q8_0..Q2_K, 67-task deterministic benchmark) shows the 3-bit-to-2-bit cliff replicates across a fourfold capability spread (11B 0.716 to 0.045; 7B 0.463 to 0.149; PLLuM 0.224 to 0.015, McNemar p<0.001) while failure modes do not replicate (7B fails long at 2-bit, median 9.1k tokens; 11B confabulates first-step answers, 37 of 64 failures; PLLuM fails content with protocol fluency intact). That is the 3-bit floor and Q2_K-never laws confirmed at benchmark scale, and it supports "perplexity alone never gates" because no perplexity curve predicts the cliff. NEW this sweep, Linger and Lose (2609.32902) sharpens the gate law into a quantified falsifier: on synthetic biographies with known information content, ternary-trained GPT-2-style models retain as little as 6% of fp16 knowledge capacity while perplexity rises only 1.4-1.6x, and every PTQ method tested (RTN, GPTQ, AWQ, NF4) recovers no measurable capacity below 4 bits. Perplexity conceals collapse; a capacity metric gates. NEW RATIO (2609.39801) adds the drift-shaped metric the question asks about: PTQ on reasoning models inflates overthinking, and token-specific penalties recover up to 9.8 points accuracy while cutting CoT length up to 51.3% - behavior, not perplexity. One boundary condition, not an overturn: Disaggregated Quantization (2609.26333) makes 1-2-bit GGUF decoders usable by pairing them with a separately trained NVFP4 prefiller (+32.5 MMLU-Pro at IQ1_S) - sub-3-bit serving survives only with scaffolded prefill; the floor for unscaffolded agentic decode stands. No refusal-rate metric appeared in any in-window item; PolAgentBench saw refusal only as a 2-bit-adjacent artifact (JSON "cannot comply" objects at Q4_K_M on one rerun arm).

**RQ2 (joint weight+KV; bytes-per-token math):** The SoK (2609.30854) delivers the math directly: B_kv = 2*L*n_kv*d_h*s*w_kv; Llama-3-70B costs 0.33 MB/token BF16 KV (128k sequence = 42 GB; 1M tokens = 328 GB), Llama-3.1-405B 0.52 MB/token, DeepSeek-V2 MLA 0.07 MB/token (~5x under GQA). Design rules: 8-bit KV storage is free, 4-bit near-free once keys are per-channel and outliers isolated, 2-bit only with structure-aware layout plus a full-precision residual window (naive 2-bit loses >1 perplexity point). Composition law: speedups are sub-multiplicative (2-bit + r=0.5 + sigma=2.91 predicts 2.63x, measures 2.05x) - "evaluate at the joint byte factor, never by multiplying per-method speedups." ThinQuant (2609.36120) makes W4A4KV4 practical to produce (70B rotation calibration under 12 minutes, WikiText-2 5.63 vs DartQuant 7.55 at 111 minutes; scales to 405B on one H200 at W4A4 ppl 2.97), but honestly: it reports no runtime bytes-per-token or KV-memory savings figure; KV4 rides fixed random Hadamard rotations. WUSH-KV (2609.38121) shows 2-bit KV with data-adaptive transforms (value transform folded into the weights, key transform after RoPE) matching or beating OSCAR end to end - but demonstrated in SGLang, not llama.cpp/Ollama; the estate's stack currently lives in the SoK's 4/8-bit near-free zone, and going lower means porting transform ideas.

**RQ3 (one artifact across llama.cpp/Ollama/vLLM):** QATFactory (2609.39223) is the strongest one-artifact statement in window: one deployment-aligned QAT checkpoint supports NVFP4, MXFP4, and llama.cpp Q4_K, and "exports checkpoints directly to vLLM and llama.cpp without an additional lossy conversion step or added inference overhead" (Qwen3.5-9B: QAD 68.9% NVFP4 vs 65.4% best PTQ). The counter-evidence on "one artifact = one behavior": the held Broken on Arrival (2609.05881) audited 327 GGUF artifacts (305 official Ollama library, 22 HuggingFace community), found 5 silently defective official artifacts (1.6%; four Qwen2.5-Coder-3B and one phi3.5-mini conversions scoring 0/164), two backend-dependent community conversions (deepseek-coder-6.7B: 5-6/15 on CUDA vs 13-15/15 on Metal), and released `quantcheck` (github.com/aditi-p31/quantcheck), a 15-task smoke suite whose screen runs 12 s to 7 min (median ~30 s); "execution is the only reliable discriminator." Disaggregated Quantization shows fragmentation persists by design: vLLM for disaggregated serving, a custom llama.cpp fork for SSD-offloaded prefill, per-format GGUF decoders; prefillers transfer partially across decoders (IQ1_M-trained prefiller on IQ1_S decoder: 65.02 vs 61.54) but training-matched pairing wins at the lowest bits. Residual-steal: quantcheck is a dogfood-shaped acceptance gate the model garden's ingest could run per artifact.

**RQ4 (agent-memory estate compression as first-class):** Yes - it is now a named subfield, and the newest item (2026-10-01) takes the estate's own side. MemFit (2610.00872) stores every turn verbatim in an append-only store with LLM-free insertion, indexes with segment summaries instead of replacing content, and hits SOTA on LoCoMo, MemGallery, LongMemEval-S while cutting memory-construction cost and latency several-fold vs LLM-summarizer memory. That is external validation of the estate's biography-mass position (rows verbatim; rollups as index, never replacement). The compression side is also live: MemForest (2609.08273, stub-held) merges EventTree nodes to 50% compression at 97.1% retention (1.89x retrieval speedup; 99.7%/2.24x multimodal); EnSIMem (2609.27279, stub-held) keeps [entity][type][property: value] entries with source turns and answers from preserved evidence, not lossy summaries. The tension to steal: index-more/compress-never (MemFit, EnSIMem) beats compress-the-store (MemForest) on accuracy; the rate-distortion framing (2607.08032, outside the 30-day window) names the trade. Nothing in window compresses hundreds of thousands of behavior rows at estate scale; LoCoMo-class benchmarks are orders of magnitude smaller. That gap is the estate's opening, not its defeat.
