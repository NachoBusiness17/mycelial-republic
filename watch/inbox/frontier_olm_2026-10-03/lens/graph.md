## Reading

What the estate knows most, in graph terms: its own modules' self-declared laws and schemas (96 percent of all edges are module-to-own-invariant), with rib_bus, swarm_surface, and game_world as the most reused organs. What it knows least: everything the graph ingests but cannot join - knots, case law, doctrines, steals (1,057 unlinked nodes), the 300 content-free case_law rows, and the frontier families wired to ids that do not resolve. The thinnest edges are not weak links between known things; they are the missing links between four knowledge stores. The cheapest next fix: give case_law rows real ids and join knot/steal/doctrine ids at ingest, and the orphan half of the graph becomes navigable.
