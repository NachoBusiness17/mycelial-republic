## The steal (mechanism, not links)

One law, three legs: quantization damage surfaces as BEHAVIOR (longer reasoning trajectories) and as STATE (the recurrent/KV pool under concurrency) before it surfaces as a weights-side score drop, and both surfaces carry cheap decode-side or metadata-side mitigations the estate can run this week.

- Leg 1, behavior (RATIO): PTQ on a reasoning model worsens the overthinking channel; at W3 on a 1.5B model CoT length inflates 2.94x (GPTQ) to 3.77x (AWQ) while accuracy falls 60.13 to 33.82 (GPTQ) or 30.88 (AWQ), and training-free token-specific penalties recover up to 9.8 accuracy points while cutting CoT length up to 51.3 percent. The mitigation lives in the sampler, not the weights. Estate consequence: the janitor tier runs Q4-class arms on a 6 GB card (configs/lanes.yaml:28-37), a reasoning-capable arm (deepseek-r1:1.5b) is on box TODAY, and the owed Q4_K_M-vs-q8_0 A/B (burns.md:122, fold probe 5) gains the thinking-token channel and the rung-below-4 warning.
- Leg 2, provenance (Minima 2609.04098, delta bank): whole-model W4A4 works only with the recurrent gates INSIDE the quantized set (the community recipes that left GDN at 8/16-bit were protecting the safest tensors) and with the fused-GEMM global scales carried end to end; the failure is silent scale mismatch after kernel fusion, and the fix is metadata surgery ("rewrite each fused group to the shared global scale"). GGUF records none of it. Estate consequence: the weights-side toolchain just became real (tools/llamacpp/llama-quantize.exe, landed 09-23, check 7) and the parser that can see the gap already exists (mag/gguf_cartography.py).
- Leg 3, state (STEPQuant): under concurrent serving the recurrent state pool, not weights, is the memory wall; 6-bit state matches FP32-state accuracy with over 5x state compression and up to 68.7 percent total serving-memory cut in SGLang, and the FP32 state pool of Qwen exceeds its BF16 weights at 70-way concurrency (sibling digest :50 plus the ask's full-text quote). Estate consequence: the same Qwen3.8-27B already sits in the estate's GGUF ladders (09-28 sweep), the KV-slot pilot is the on-box state-precision experiment (its q8_0 KV line is the knob), and the water graph is the house engine for the wall (a pool running full bore fractures, water_graph.py:26-34).
