Held prior art already banked by the siblings covers the deterministic-repair side (GVD 2609.17696: LLM-free dedup F1 0.97/0.94) and the merge-rule warning (Bisimulation 2609.38778: similarity clustering cannot separate nodes with no distinguishing surface - the 300 law: rows are exactly that). Net: normalize ids at ingest for anything new; heal the backlog by re-ingest from source records, not by merging over the broken ids.

### RQ2 - does the re-derivability bank validate residual+registry+leaf, and what unit makes it rib-citable

Fetched arxiv.org/abs/2609.32802 this session: "Re-derivability Decides What a Staged Agent Pipeline Recovers After an Upstream Fault" (cs.AI, submitted Sat 26 Sep 2026; Bu, Peng, Tu, Xiao). The OWED outcome-metric unit closes: task accuracy as proportion correct on gsm-hard math problems, 120 items per arm, temperature zero, four open-weight backbones with thinking disabled. Rib-citable form: "re-grounding downstream stages in the original problem beats transcript forwarding after an upstream first-stage fault, +0.608 [95% CI +0.517, +0.700] proportion correct on gsm-hard (120 items/arm, temp 0; 4 open-weight backbones; arXiv:2609.32802)". Three scope guards the rib must carry: the effect is a staged-pipeline fault-recovery result, not a session-continuity result; on Llama-3.1-8B the fault carried no detectable cost; without a fault the staged pipeline loses to one direct call by -0.267 to -0.317 on most backbones. Verdict on validation: directional yes - re-deriving from the source (files + chain, docs/DNA.md:4) beats forwarding the transcript is exactly the DNA position, and held item 2609.05339 independently shows repair succeeds 34/48 when raw source history is retained vs 0/48 from compressed notes - but no paper read measures tokens-to-competence for a cold seat, so residual+registry+leaf stays externally unbenchmarked; that remains the estate's own instrument to build (RQ5).
