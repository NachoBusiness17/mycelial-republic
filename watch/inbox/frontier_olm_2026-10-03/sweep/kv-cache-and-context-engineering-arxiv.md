## Answers to the five research questions

**Q1 - prefix-reuse gains vs the measured 98%-hit ~$3.60 result (laws.md:23).** Nothing in the window beats 98%; the window independently CONFIRMS the magnitude from the serving side and bounds where it holds. PrefixBench-H100 (read): both vLLM 0.21 and TensorRT-LLM 1.2.1 cache 92% of an 8,192-token shared prefix, worth 5-6.5x TTFT reduction and 24-31% energy per request; the governing variable is the working-set-to-KV-capacity RATIO, not model size (capped-7B and natural-32B pools hit the same eviction wall at the same ratio: 68.2% -> 3.6% hit). The dollar channel is validated by the pass-0 bank (Beyond Token Savings: rewriting policies re-price saved tokens at the fresh rate) and extended by Who Pays 2609.24991 (unalloc: joins OpenCost + LiteLLM + provider bills into one exact ledger; the attribution seams are where "the cache saved money" claims usually cannot be audited). Estate consequence: the 44x lever is real and now triple-measured (billing, TTFT, energy); the risk side is attribution and prefix invalidation, not cache mechanics.

**Q2 - does the 4,800-char budget transfer to paid tiers; what rule does prefix_cache_sim predict per tier.** The sim does not model dollars; it models chars/hit-rate, and this session's run gives the rule shape: hit rate saturates at ~64 blocks (16 KB) for the estate's frozen_append shape and collapses below 32 blocks; the transferable object is NOT the 4,800-char number but (a) byte-identical frozen prefix + append-only tail (0.9387 vs 0.3771 window vs 0.0005 volatile), and (b) capacity sized to the shared-prefix working set. The literature's transfer answer is Risk-Controlled Eviction (read): do not carry a budget across deployments at all - fix a reliability contract (tolerance + allowed frequency), re-derive the operating point per deployment from calibration data with a finite-sample guarantee, and fall back to full context when nothing certifies. Its measured warning: the SAME contract certifies SnapKV at 75% retention on one benchmark and certifies nothing on another; empirical-threshold selection picks operating points 5-10 points more aggressive than certified ones. Per-tier dollar rule the estate can actually run: uncached-delta billing only (cached input at the provider's cached rate) - i.e., pay for the append, never re-pay the prefix; audit it with an unalloc-style joined ledger, not provider dashboards.

**Q3 - context shapes minimizing tokens-per-correct-verdict; post-10-02 structure-vs-prose measurement.** No post-10-02 paper in this window measures structure-vs-prose directly; the strongest measured result remains ARSM 2609.32852 (09-26, banked by the pass-0 draft: structured state 0.4899 vs plain summary 0.3305 vs structured summary 0.1321). This window adds two things. How Much Prompt Is Enough 2609.36289 (abstract-verified): blackbox few-shot minimization cuts exemplars 65.3% (+/-15.8%) in characters while "fully preserving propositional output fidelity" - the tokens-per-correct axis measured from the minimization side. CDCE 2609.27354 (read, doctrine only, zero measurements): constraints are first-class context with three axes (explicit/implicit, predefined/dynamic, hard/soft) and three roles (guide, enforce, verify) - the same role split as the estate's invariants-vs-gates-vs-verification layers; its "making a constraint available as context is not always sufficient to ensure it is satisfied" is the memweave feed-structures-not-prose doctrine stated as software-architecture law.

**Q4 - one tool enforcing byte-identical ask construction across the seven fire surfaces (laws.md:83).** No paper ships such an enforcement tool; the window supplies the evidence and the instrument shape. AgentReplay (read): greedy decoding does NOT pin the ask stream - 50 identical greedy runs on SymPy varied CV 28.66% (turns), 142.21% (cumulative input tokens), 103.49% (output tokens), 31/50 runs trapped in unproductive loops; forcing recorded token IDs cut cumulative-time CV to 1.30%, and "if a response differs from the recorded history in the next prompt, its KV cache may no longer be reusable" is exactly the cache-contract mechanism the estate's byte-identical law protects. The estate shape this implies for closing the seven-surface bypass + EMPTY_REASONING gap: one recorder/enforcer that owns the ask bytes (token-id-level, like AgentReplay's trace) and is the ONLY writer of dispatch params, with `mag/inference_budget.py` as the gate it calls - the seven surfaces (stealth_driver, duel_harness, tesuji_fire, lane_runner, lite_scale, wave_fire, ds_workhorse) become clients. That is estate work, not a vendor steal; the paper justifies it with failure numbers.

**Q5 - eviction policy for the repeated-boot pattern (every seat reloads the same rib chain tip).** Three laws compose. (1) Timing: Dge (read) shows the eviction TIMING axis dominates the selection axis - deferring the cut by one decode step lifts SnapKV from 35.34 to 44.17 LongBench average (FullKV 46.24) across 15 backbone-budget cells, and the timing-only control equals the full method; "before optimizing what to keep, fix when to evict." Estate analogue: never compact the rib chain tip at load time; let the seat take its first action, then evict. (2) Certification: Risk-Controlled Eviction - evict only what a calibrated contract certifies, else fall back to full reload; for a shared frozen prefix that is always certified-by-construction (byte-identical, hits by definition), so repeated-boot prefixes should sit OUTSIDE the eviction candidates entirely. (3) The null: LRU is the policy to beat (estate G1 gap law + HN 108-point consensus post + When Fancy Eviction Fails, prior bank). LMCache lineage (corpus.md:20, 2,331 rows): PrefixBench's related-work maps LMCache/CacheGen/CacheBlend as the multi-tier mechanism-design line; the HN practitioner post "Keeping vLLM's Prefix Cache Warm Between Agent Turns" is the operational pattern for the estate's warm-boot rib. HeteroFold (read) is the cross-family extension: if seats on DIFFERENT model families must share the loaded context, sender KV can be mapped to the receiver (10.7x faster than receiver prefill at 32K, both models frozen) - but it trades away the byte-match guarantee (reconstruction error does not predict receiver behavior; accuracy lands at 55-90% of native prefill on long-context QA), so it fits mesh hand-offs, not the cache-contract path.
