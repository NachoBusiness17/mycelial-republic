## Answers to the five research questions

**RQ1 - Does Q4_K_M-matches-BF16-on-agentic-work hold on the estate's own qwen2.5:7b Q4_K_M-vs-q8_0 A/B (burns.md:122)?** NOT RUN HERE - the A/B is one of the fold's five $0 probes (burns.md:122) and needs a multi-GB Ollama pull plus an eval harness; this sweep runs no models. It stays OWED. External evidence this sweep adds: at 27B the 4-bit-class result replicates on a THIRD independent stack - 2609.04098's NVFP4 W4A4-all-layers build matches BF16 within seed noise on six suites (MMLU-Pro, GSM8K, AIME'25, GPQA-Diamond, LiveCodeBench, RULER) while 17.5 GiB, joining the estate-held quesma GGUF bench (Q4_K_M matches BF16 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, candidates.jsonl row 16) and 2609.32042 (Q4 safe, Q3 model-dependent, Q2 collapse). RATIO's W3 rows warn the rung BELOW 4 is where agentic work dies: W3 on 1.5B/7B drops avg accuracy 60.13->33.82 (GPTQ) / 30.88 (AWQ) on the 1.5B and multiplies CoT length 1.4-3.8x. Design note for the owed A/B: judge on agentic/task metrics with an output-length channel logged, not perplexity alone (BCC and PreDE both show bit-width alone predicts neither behavior nor task loss).

**RQ2 - Post-10-02 quantization papers with agentic-task (not perplexity) retention; do any contradict the banked 2-bit +25 pct output-token inflation (burns.md:103-106)?** Boundary check run (API, abs:quant OR abs:quantization OR abs:"low-bit", submittedDate desc): the newest submissions in the index are dated 2026-10-01 (2610.01537, 2610.00983, 2610.01058); ZERO quantization papers dated 10-02 or 10-03 exist yet. The closest post-bench cohort is 09-29/09-30, and far from contradicting the +25 pct inflation finding, the strongest of them amplifies it: RATIO's measured CoT-length inflation at W3 is 2.94x (GPTQ, 1.5B: 10.09k -> 29.66k tokens) and 3.77x (AWQ: 38.04k), citing Lian et al. 2026 that "quantization can also increase the number of reasoning tokens, even when answer accuracy is preserved". Agentic-task retention (vs perplexity) appears in: 2609.04098 (task suites incl LiveCodeBench, seed-noise statistics), 2609.32042 (held; McNemar on pass rates), and BCC's behavior-charging certificates. No contradiction found anywhere in the window; the 25 pct number is if anything the OPTIMISTIC end (measured on a 27B at 2-bit; RATIO shows worse at 3-bit on small models).

**RQ3 - Can GGUF metadata alone fill a routing-grid serving column (predicted throughput per host class), and where does the prediction break?** Speed yes (with calibration), quality no. The estate's own holding (2609.14864, banked today by the prior seat; abstract re-fetched by me) already carries the break points: host-specific held-out MAPE 13.1/14.4/36.1 pct for the active-parameter decode model (the 36.1 pct host had only 2 held-out configs), leave-one-host-out refits needed, prefill prediction collapsing to 108.2 pct MAPE on one host, and the abstract's own verdict "fitted efficiencies are not universal" with low-bit ladders changing ordering across runtime stacks. This sweep adds the QUALITY half of the boundary from PreDE (2609.19441): "quantization produces configuration-dependent task losses that cannot be explained by bit width alone or a shared deviation threshold" - task-loss prediction requires policy-calibrated thresholds from a small closed-loop dev set, and 3 of 28 held-out configs had to be deferred, one carrying a 33-point loss. And Minima (2609.04098) adds a serving-stack landmine invisible in any metadata: per-module-calibrated checkpoints break when kernels FUSE adjacent modules into one GEMM (global-scale mismatch; error 0.35/0.57 -> 0.002 after repair), which no GGUF field records. Routing-grid design: metadata predicts tok/s per host class after per-host fitting; the quality column must come from a measured spot-check ladder per model+role, not from metadata.

**RQ4 - Where does NVFP4/W4A4 (GatedDeltaNet 2609.04098) sit relative to GGUF K-quants for linear-attention architectures?** The paper itself does NOT benchmark GGUF - its comparisons are Unsloth Dynamic v3 and RadixArk ModelOpt, both of which PROTECT the GDN block at FP8 W8A8 (gates in BF16) and quantize only MLPs to NVFP4. Minima quantizes all 496 linear layers including GDN gates and matches BF16 within seed noise while being smallest (17.5 GiB) and fastest-prefill (+14-19 pct; TTFT 6.90s -> 4.03s at 32K); decode lands within 4 pct of the protective recipes because decode is weight-bandwidth-bound. So at the 4-bit class, NVFP4-full ~= GGUF Q4_K_M on quality (both match BF16 on their respective suites at ~17.5 GB) - the difference is serving stack, not accuracy: NVFP4 needs accelerator-native kernels (Blackwell-class, vLLM/SGLang lineage per the paper), GGUF K-quants run on the estate's llama.cpp/Ollama tier today. Two NVFP4-specific steals: calibrated FP8 KV-cache scales are performance-free and recover 83 pct of the long-context KV penalty; and the recurrent half is the EASY half (gate nonlinearities squash ~11 pct GEMM error to ~2 pct; delta rule erases state error in hundreds of steps) - the community's 8/16-bit GDN protection was guarding the safest tensors. The state-side frontier moved the same week: STEPQuant (6-bit state ~= FP32-state accuracy, >5x state compression, up to 68.7 pct total serving-memory cut in SGLang) and LeapQuant (windowed 8-bit state, near-lossless, 1.47x end-to-end on RTX 5090/PRO 6000/B200). The estate's linear-attention serving question is now state-precision, not weight-precision.

**RQ5 - Does quantization level interact with EMPTY_REASONING? Do lower-bit models produce hidden-reasoning-empty failures at a higher measured rate?** No paper in the window measures empty-content failure RATE by quantization level on agent asks; reported as a gap, not faked. The estate's guard context: mag/inference_budget.py:1-20 (EMPTY_REASONING is terminal for the (ask, family); the B1/B5 signature is finish=length + full reasoning budget + empty content; seven fire surfaces still bypass the guard, watch/inbox/frontier_olm_2026-10-03/lens/laws.md:83). What the sweep does establish, adjacent: quantization on reasoning models INFLATES reasoning trajectories (RATIO: 1.4-3.8x CoT length at W3; its central claim is that PTQ "exacerbates overthinking, leading to longer reasoning trajectories"). Inference, labeled as inference: longer trajectories at a fixed reasoning-token cap raise exactly the B1/B5 precondition (budget exhausted by hidden reasoning, content channel empty), so a Q4-vs-Q8 EMPTY_REASONING rate difference is plausible and MEASURABLE for near-zero dollars by logging the estate's own empty-content classifier per quant level on the same asks - fold it into the owed Q4_K_M-vs-q8_0 A/B as a third logged channel. Related capability-loss evidence: the Efficiency-Safety paper shows quantized reasoning models fail by "attempted but failed" degradation, not clean refusals - meaning quantized janitors' failures arrive as degraded output, exactly the class the empty-content classifier watches.
