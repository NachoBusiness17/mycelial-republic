## Steal 1: REGEXROUTE, learned regex routing with no encoder (GO for the router instrument)

Verified live: fetched the abs page (round 1); the title matches and the abstract carries verbatim "Across four benchmarks, one fixed set of 128 features achieves 76.43% average routing accuracy, comparable to 76.41% for the strongest neural text encoder baseline, with much smaller latency and strong robustness."
