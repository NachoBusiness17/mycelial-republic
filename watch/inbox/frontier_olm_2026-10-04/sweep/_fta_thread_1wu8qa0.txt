  [AutomataManifold | 2026-09-30 16:16 | score 17] It's a tradeoff.

Early on, research showed that bigger quantized Llama always outperformed smaller, less quantized models. There's an inflection point around 4.65 bits IIRC (where GGUF Q4 approximately sits) where things got sharply worse but sometimes people found Q2 or Q3 depending on what they were using it for.

Nowadays there's a wide enough variety of models that it's more complicated and bigger isn't always strictly better. But in general, bigger but quantized beats smaller. Though it might be worse at your particular purpose, and there's mostly still an inflection point around Q4. And now we can do very selective quantization, leave the KV unquantized, etc.

  [hermansinho | 2026-10-01 17:05 | score 1] One practical rule is to compare at equal memory and latency, not just parameter count: a well-trained distilled model can retain more capability per bit than an aggressively quantized parent. For tool calling, test a fixed set of multi-step prompts at the exact quant and context length you plan to use—very low quants often fail on JSON/schema adherence before ordinary chat quality collapses. If the tiny quant is still stable at Q4_K_M or Q5_K_M, it’s usually the safer baseline; below that, a good distill often wins.

  [EvolvingDior | 2026-09-30 18:34 | score 1] Everything is a trade-off. I have enough GPU/RAM to run q38fn at 3.5 bits (GSQ RCO IQ3\_S). It works great in one sense -- it's output is on par with bf16. It's too slow and overthinks like the bf16 version. The GSQ RCO Q2\_0 is worse -- it does not explore the problem space in the same way. But it is \*way\* faster and does not overthink. It gets the job done better and faster than anything that consumes the same amount of system resources. To me, it \*feels\* better because I can have a reasonably interactive session with it.

The best is actually neither of the options you mention. The best is a model that consumes the least amount of VRAM for each token of context. That's the measurement I look for. It's another reason I choose q38fn over q38-27b; it uses far less VRAM per token for it's KV cache.

The model has to fit. It has to perform well. And it has to have enough context to perform meaningful work.
