### 1. When Does Selection Replace Extraction? (2609.34227, 2026-09-28) - READ FULL TEXT
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34227 and https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34227v1.
Pre-registered (Zenodo DOIs) comparison of raw-turn selection (one typed-decision-model call, "Jev") versus LLM fact extraction at matched token budgets. The knee is budget-relative, not absolute: at k=3 (139 to 251 tokens rendered) selection plus rerank is non-inferior to extraction ("one-sided 95% bound -3.0 points against a -5-point margin") and raw turns cost "3,061 times less to write"; at k=20 extraction wins (engram v2 82.4 percent on LoCoMo at 1,238 tokens vs Jev 77.6 at 496). Decisive for Q5: on LoCoMo, 139 tokens of selected raw turns land within 1 point of 23,631-token full context; on LongMemEval full context scores 63.0 percent at 111,770 tokens, BELOW selection at 66.8 to 73.8 percent while costing 29x. Stuffing loses on both axes past the knee. Reranking also suppresses correct abstention (cosine 63.6 to Jev 54.1 to LLM 48.8 percent at k=3).
Estate mapping: warm_state.json brief-plus-tips (per AGENTS.md) is a fixed small warm block, i.e. below the knee; the measured answer is keep it small and select hard at read time, never grow the warm block toward full-context. The abstention result is a second-order warning: any relevance-filtered context makes the model less willing to say "not mentioned," which is exactly the failure shape of a phantom-heavy MEMORY.md index (the 93 percent phantom recount in commit 0838e150e).

### 2. Memory Consolidation Flattens the Temporal Shape of User Facts (2609.36457, 2026-09-29) - READ FULL TEXT
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36457 and https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36457v1.
Names "aspectual flattening": consolidation rewrites "I am driving a Peugeot" to "the user drives a Peugeot," destroying the cue that the fact can expire. LAPSE benchmark: of 381 matched pairs, writers destroyed the progressive member 244 times and the simple-present member never; mem0 2.0.19 flattened 49/128, Graphiti 0.30.1 15/32, Letta 0.16.8 26/29 stored notes. Reader models act without asking more often on flattened notes (GLM-5.2 187/291 vs 116/291, corrected p=2.8e-12). The fix they land on is schema, not prompt: "Store source wording, normalized fact, temporal form, and observation time in separate fields"; explicit end dates survived every writer; a "preserve tense" instruction helped one writer (61.7 to 3.1 percent destruction) and catastrophically hurt another (59.4 to 100).
Estate mapping (Q3): this is the drift mechanism behind census-carrier drift in miniature: a rewrite pass that is individually plausible and systematically lossy. zknot's logon rewrite (tools/zknot.py rewriting memory/zknot/LATEST.md) and the graph.md backfill idea (backfilling knot theme/summary from verkle rows, watch/inbox/frontier_olm_2026-10-04/lens/graph.md:27) should copy the schema law: never let the rewrite destroy the source row; keep observation time and temporal form as separate fields next to any normalized claim. The LAPSE result that blanket expiry rules are too rigid but end-date fields are safe is directly portable to the 400 content-less knots: regenerate summaries from the underlying verkle rows (which still carry ts), do not hand-normalize.
Overlap: catalog "Flattens the Temporal" 0; a bare-numeric grep for "36457" returned 17 rows but those are unverified numeric coincidences, asserted as nothing; memory/ hits only trajectory/steps.jsonl. NEW.

### 3. Stashbird: Efficient Speaker-Indexed Memory (2609.34242, 2026-09-28) - READ FULL TEXT
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34242 and https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34242v1.
The Q2 schema answer. Views: Episodes (append/delete), Entities (merge/invalidate), Relations (replace/version), Communities (re-summarized). Every entity or relation x carries provenance P(x), the set of episode ids supporting it; the dedup law is one line: "duplicates extending provenance and contradictions replacing the earlier relation." Deletion is episode-anchored: strip the episode from every P(x), drop entities whose support empties unless they serve as connective anchors ("Entity Meta-forgetting with Neighbor Adoption"), mark communities dirty for deferred re-summarization. Numbers: 76.4x fewer ingestion prompt tokens than Graphiti on LoCoMo (484K vs 37M on GPT-4.1-mini LoCoMo), 8.1x fewer retrieval tokens than Hindsight, accuracy 80.4 to 87.5 on LoCoMo (behind Hindsight), 75.6 to 81.0 on LongMemEval-S (ahead), GroupMemBench 51.1 vs 24.9.
Estate mapping: the 300 case_law rows collapsing onto the shared empty id `law:` (graph.md:28, builder `law:{str(r.get('id') or '')[:12]}` at mag/knowledge_graph.py:123) are exactly the disease Stashbird's P(x) cures: identity is derived from supporting source rows, never from a truncated prose field, and a duplicate is not a new node but another provenance entry on the same node. Applying P(x) to the estate graph means each of the 1,057 unlinked knowledge nodes (graph.md:26) carries the row paths that produced it, which makes both dedup and episode-level deletion mechanical.

### 4. MemFit: Efficient Long-Term Agentic Memory (2610.00872, 2026-10-01) - READ ABSTRACT (HTML full text 404)
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00872.

### 5. The Epistemics of Agent Memory (2609.33013, 2026-09-26) - READ ABSTRACT
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33013.
Four-phase study of the consolidation decision (keep, compress, abstract, forget). Phase 2 hit +22.7 percent task success at 7x compression; Phase 3's ConsolidationBench found production retrieval systems that "retain information" yet "score zero on cross-level transfer"; the headline negative: "a two-benchmark study with 2,532 real answer cells found pooled Spearman rho = -0.24 (n = 12, CI spanning zero)" between consolidation quality score and real transfer accuracy. Governance (poison-resistance, reversibility, auditability) is statistically separable from quality (partial r = 0.27).
Estate mapping (Q3, Q4): a direct license to distrust internal quality scores of any consolidation pass (the census carrier's own self-report is the local example); pair any zknot rewrite with reversibility and audit hooks rather than a quality grade. Also a warning for the two-module framing in burns.md (compress module A, select module B): compressing harder does not predict retrieval value; measure transfer, not compression ratio.

### 6. RoutePrism: Tracing Construction Order Effects in Agent Memory (2609.34160, 2026-09-28) - READ ABSTRACT
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34160.
Builds the same memory twice from an identical pool in two processing orders with content, timestamps, policy, and answer model fixed, so "any observed difference is localized to the memory construction step." Result: "Restoring a single displaced record recovers 'over 60 percentage points of lost accuracy'"; substituting an equal-length non-supporting record does not. Benchmarks: PersonaMem-32K (63 primary queries) and 470 LongMemEval-S questions, replicated across five answer models.
Estate mapping (Q1, Q3): regeneration from source rows is not automatically deterministic; processing order silently selects survivors, and one displaced row is worth 60 points. Concretely: any backfill of the 400 knots or 300 law rows from verkle/skill_ledger source rows must fix a canonical row order (and record it), or two rebuilds of knowledge_graph.json will diverge in content while both looking healthy. This is the published mechanism behind census carriers drifting within hours.

### 7. UpliftMem: Learning Set-Level Uplift for Agent Memory Retrieval (2609.36805, 2026-09-29) - READ ABSTRACT
Fetched: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36805.

Best success rates among baselines on ALFWorld, WebShop, BigCodeBench (abstract gives no per-benchmark numbers).

Probe-on-non-preferred-sets is the cure for a selection module that only ever sees its own favorite rows.

## Direct answers to the five research questions (as far as the reads support them)

1. Linking vs pruning vs regeneration (1,057 unlinked nodes, 400 knots): the in-window evidence favors regeneration from source rows with provenance anchoring (MemFit, SourceLearning, Stashbird's P(x)) over linking passes; pruning is not what any of the eight recommend, and Epistemics shows compression quality scores do not predict transfer. GraphRAG-line work in-window (EvLink 2609.29695, NexusRAG 2609.37661) still buys points from linking when the source corpus is external, but for a graph whose source rows (verkle_chain.jsonl, skill_ledger.jsonl) are already local and live, backfill from rows dominates.
2. Identity/dedup schema for the `law:` collapse: Stashbird's scheme is the concrete transplant: node identity from canonical entities, duplicates extend provenance, contradictions replace with version history, deletion is support-driven. The 300-rows-one-id bug dies the moment id derivation stops truncating a prose field (mag/knowledge_graph.py:123) and starts keying on the ledger row identity (schema/skill_id/job/ts).
3. Consolidation schedules and drift: LAPSE proves logon-window rewrites are structurally lossy unless the rewrite keeps source wording, temporal form, and observation time as separate fields; RoutePrism proves even order alone silently changes the product. zknot should rewrite pointers, never prose, or should store the diff-with-source; a consolidation without reversibility and audit (Epistemics phase 4) is unmeasurable.
4. Selection reward designs measured end-to-end: UpliftMem (execution uplift plus EVSI probing) and the Selection-vs-Extraction budget law are the two in-window measured designs; both put the reward on downstream task effect, not on compression or internal quality scores (Epistemics negative result is the cautionary control).
5. Warm-start size vs retrieval: below a few hundred rendered tokens, selection quality decides; around 1,200+ tokens, extraction/summarization wins; full-context stuffing loses accuracy AND cost on both benchmarks read (23,631 tokens within 1 point of 139 on LoCoMo; 111,770 tokens at 63.0 percent vs 73.8 for selection on LongMemEval-S). warm_state.json's brief-plus-tips shape is on the right side of the knee; the failure mode to police is relevance-filtered context suppressing correct abstention.
