## PART 1 - THE STANDING LAWS THAT CONSTRAIN RESEARCH WORK

### 1.1 Grounding and anti-fabrication (the first chair)

- **Trust bytes, not reports. Never claim first unless read from data.** "Honest over
  over-claiming - the refusal to fake a root IS the mechanism" (memory/rib/RIB_SELF_MY_LAW.md:6-12).
  The quantized frontier laws carry the same imperative 18+ times: "Verify with git diff / actual
  file bytes before declaring done. Trust bytes, not" (memory/corpus/quantized_thought/instructions_frontier.jsonl:1-6,12-24).
- **The empty-claim law:** a claim is a chair; empty means "we do not know yet". You sit only on a
  primary record, never a blog, rumor, or vibe. Score supported / unproven / disputed; the human
  says when it is supported; software does not auto-truth (memory/rib/RIB_GAME_CLAIMS_DESK.md:6-10).
- **Honest state, not claim:** report grounded truth (armed vs running) backed by trail/queue
  evidence, never overclaim (memory/rib/RIB_PROBLEM_AUTONOMOUS_LOOP_20260811.md:27-30).
- **No artifact claims frontier provenance unless real output was captured**; the grok:True flag
  lie (request flag mistaken for output) is a named correction
  (memory/rib/recreation-coding-history.json:52-55,76-79).
- **Refusal is data:** when you cannot produce a real reply, send nothing; "a gloss is a lie";
  patch the cause, not the symptom (memory/rib/RIB_SELF_MY_CORRECTION_LOOP.md:6-12).
- **The lenses each sit against a named lie** (accuracy vs "trust the report"; operations vs "the
  theater is the work"; dogfood vs "a finding filed is a finding wired"; lineage vs "new is
  better"; security vs "the gate that tests itself"). Boot law: a lens that cannot name its stone
  is cargo (memory/rib/RIB_LENS_STONES_PARABLE.md:15-35).

### 1.2 Verdicts carry their conditions (the one-bug law)

- "A VERDICT WITHOUT ITS CONDITIONS IS NOT A VERDICT." Name the instrument and its exit code (never
  piped away or masked), the moment (nothing compared across moments without saying so), and the
  context assumptions (cwd, basetemp, order, budget). New instrument runs that cannot name their
  three conditions are treated as unfired (memory/rib/RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:53-76).
- **Instruments only:** a routing cell fills from estate instruments (duel logs, census receipts,
  usage.jsonl), never vendor claims or benchmarks; hand-copying a vendor number into an empty cell
  is fabrication. UNMEASURED is a legal default state (memory/rib/RIB_MODEL_GARDEN_ROUTING.md:47-53).
- **Rows expire:** a measurement older than 30 days, or spanning a family event (fingerprint
  change, carrier swap, deprecation, price change), is STALE and must be re-measured before it
  routes dollars. Amend, never rewrite: supersede by appending a new-moment row, keep the old row
  as lineage (memory/rib/RIB_MODEL_GARDEN_ROUTING.md:54-62).
- **Question-class routing law:** the question (readable vs simulation) explains 36.3% of score
  variance, family 3.6%; evidence-readable asks route cheap-forever, runtime-simulation asks route
  premium; transport is not quality (no cell routes dollars without a blind-grade pass);
  cost-per-blind-point is the champion metric (memory/rib/RIB_ROUTING_CLASSES_20261002.md:11-26;
  memory/rib/RIB_MODEL_GARDEN_ROUTING.md:234-242).
- **Every paid dispatch banks usage (tokens in/out) in its receipt** (memory/rib/RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md:49;
  memory/rib/RIB_THE_DESCENT_PROTOCOL_20261001.md:53-56).
