## The steal (REV 2, rescoped)

In a prefix-cached agent loop, cost is a function of WHERE the context mutates, not how big it is. Every token is paid at least once as a write (full price or premium; the premium CONSTANTS are pre-banked estate knowledge); reads are the discount. An edit at position i re-prices everything after i, so deleting context to save tokens can cost more than it saves: the tokens removed were cheap reads, and the rewrite is a premium write over the whole tail. Theo adds the practitioner weight: over 60% of his Claude Code and Codex spend is cache writes. Corollaries: the arithmetic CONTRADICTS the banked Iusztin continuous-microcompaction policy (per-tool-call deletion is net-negative on cost); a per-tool-call trimmer is doubly self-defeating (it decides without the tool result - new - and it hides the tried-things that prevent stupid loops - the sibling card's row 7); on Anthropic-shape wires, full-history preservation is also the price of reasoning payloads (substrate pre-banked); and hand-rolled compaction fights models already trained on their own compaction flows.
