## The steal (mechanism, not a link)

Serving cost tracks the active side; capability tracks the total side. Apache 2.0 full weights plus a containerized deterministic serving path (vLLM + named parsers kolibri1) makes that split OWNABLE: judgment capability as pinned, hashable, offline bytes - the sovereignty claim is a deployment property, not marketing. That is the estate's own auto_quantize law - variance determines resolution, anomalies never quantized - demonstrated in weight space: the scarce resource (bits, or pack tokens) is allocated by sensitivity, never uniformly.
