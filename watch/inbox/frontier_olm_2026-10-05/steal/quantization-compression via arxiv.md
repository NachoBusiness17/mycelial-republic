## The steal (mechanism, not a link)

Two papers, one missing organ part. The estate's quantized_thought compiler is a context-compression pipeline with shape gates and a staleness gate but NO decision gate and NO pricing model. Find 1 supplies the missing gate, find 2 supplies the missing price, and the estate's own bytes already show the failure mode find 1 exists to catch.

FIND 1, THE GATE (PAIR, 2609.36526). The method, verified live on the abstract this sitting: trajectory-level comparisons of full-context vs compressed runs "cannot isolate individual compressions and are confounded by agent stochasticity", so PAIR runs "matched counterfactual continuations that compare execution from the same agent state" - one continuation with the compression applied, one without - isolating each compression event's causal effect. Per the sweep's full-text read: the measured quantities are outcome hazard (terminal reward change) and interaction burden (extra steps); thresholds flag harmful compressions; an optimizer LLM "revises the relevant sections of a fixed compression template"; candidates are validated end to end. The headline law: "compression degrades reliability before solvability" - cross-run consistency breaks before task success does, and damage "concentrates at isolated compression events" (x-sweep :70-72). The full-text numbers (sweep :92-109): on AppWorld test-normal (168 tasks, GPT-5.6 Luna) PAIR lands 84.7 accuracy / 79.8 Pass3 vs the uncompressed baseline's 84.1 / 78.6 - the COMPRESSED agent exceeds the uncompressed one on reliability - and prefix-conditioned PAIR reaches 85.9 / 80.4; OfficeBench holds 81.1 to 81.4 vs an 82.1 baseline at roughly half the peak context (4K vs 7K tokens); the best ACON variant is beaten by 2.1 to 10.0 Pass3 points. The paper's revision lessons are literally quantized_thought compile gates: preserve exact literals and goal constraints, reconcile summaries against the newest evidence, never promote unsupported answers. Why this maps 1:1: thought_quantizer's classify() (:106-139) judges LAW SHAPE (is this row imperative? telemetry?), never LAW EFFECT (does the compiled render change what a consumer does with it?). to_flash and to_local (:170-178) are the estate's fixed compression template, truncation-shaped: first sentence to 220 chars, first 10 words. No compile stage exists where a consumer answers from the compiled row and from the full row and the two answers are compared - that is exactly PAIR's paired continuation, and it is cheap on a local janitor. The 13/338 literal-loss datum measured this sitting is the static prediction of what the dynamic gate would catch: rows whose compiled render dropped the bytes a correct answer needed.

FIND 2, THE PRICE (2609.22114, abstract verbatim this sitting). The token bill of a real multi-turn agent decomposes into levers that save at fundamentally different rates: "Tool-schema filtering removes a fixed block every turn, roughly 21K-57K tokens on a typical turn; it is linear in the turn count N and the only unambiguously and reproducibly positive lever." Content compression "saves only about 2% of the cache-priced prefix per turn, but compressed reads accumulate in history and are re-sent on every later turn, so its cumulative saving grows quadratically, about 3350*N^2 tokens (measured), overtaking the fixed tool-filter saving within roughly 6 turns until the context window caps it." Recall on a non-destructive gateway: "each recall re-sends exactly the one segment just compressed away, so its cost is fixed and bounded rather than a multiplicative blowup, and heavy recall spends the accumulated saving back one segment at a time." And the citation ban in its sharpest form - the authors apply it to THEIR OWN model: "a strong single-shot compression benchmark - 86.5% of SWE-bench quality retained at a 25.7% compression rate, achieved by the model this gateway deploys (Paritok-4B, reported separately) - is orthogonal to multi-turn agent cost and must not be cited as a cost-saving argument." Why this maps 1:1: the estate prices quantized_thought by linker statistics (kept == packed, 374) and budget arithmetic (the 1800-char cap at context_pack.py:377) - exactly the single-shot-shaped accounting the paper bans. The pack pays only if its rows are RECALL-FREE by construction: a seat that answers from the flash row but re-opens the invariant stream to verify has re-bought the bytes, one segment at a time. And the lever hierarchy gives the pack a trim order that is currently implicit: when the boot pack is over budget, dropping whole fixed blocks (schema-shaped, linear, reproducible) beats squeezing law text (content-shaped: quadratic upside that only accrues when compressed reads persist across turns, which a per-boot pack does not guarantee, plus recall downside). The estate's own "keep the original as fallback" law (mag/context_compression.py:17) is the non-destructive gateway in miniature - this paper is the first measured pricing of what that fallback costs when actually exercised.

SYNTHESIS: compile, then verify the compile the PAIR way (paired renders of the same law, reliability scored across reruns, never one-shot), and price the compile the gateway way (recall-free by construction, or the savings are theater). Both instruments are janitor-grade: deterministic exact-match scoring, no LLM judge, $0 of paid budget.
