### 1. @omarsar0 amplifying the Salesforce harness-co-evolution study (2026-09-10, status 2097958286146605446)
THE Q2 answer and the direct Gero-4B battery design. Salesforce evolved an agent harness with a weak model across seven enterprise agent tasks, then SFT'd that same weak model on a stronger expert's full trajectories under the same harness. Verbatim: "Performance dropped on all seven tasks, by 4 to 30 points" across Qwen3-Coder and Gemma 4, while "The same fine-tuning helps under the unevolved harness." Cause, verbatim: the weak model "adopts the expert's planning strategy without the competence to execute it." Fix: a meta-agent locates the failing turn and the expert rewrites only that turn, preserving the student's planning style. Steal for the calibrator: a finetune that passes its eval under one harness can regress double-digit points under the harness it will actually run in; the battery must include a production-harness arm, and expert-trajectory SFT needs turn-level repair, not wholesale imitation.
