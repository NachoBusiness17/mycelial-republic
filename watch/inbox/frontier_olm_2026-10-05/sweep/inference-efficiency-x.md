## Answers to the four research questions

RQ1 (2026 serving wins for 2B-4B janitors on consumer hardware): the wins split by target size. For 12B-class short workers, MTP draft heads are the real 2026 unlock: 1.4-2.2x lossless on Gemma 4 with GGUFs shipping the drafter in-package (items 1, 2), enabled in llama.cpp by `--spec-type draft-mtp` at ~2 GB extra headroom. For 2B-class scut janitors the same mechanism INVERTS: on a 0.8B target MTP dropped generation 342 -> 128 tok/s despite 96.6% acceptance (item 5), and the generic failure conditions are acceptance <60-70%, VRAM pressure pushing the target into CPU offload, and concurrent/batched serving (item 6) - the janitor fleet's exact regime. The stack-level win that DOES transfer to small serial backends is admission reordering, not drafting: Clairvoyant's zero-modification SJF sidecar cuts short-request P50 70-76% on the same Ollama/llama.cpp serial path our fleet runs (item 3). Estate mirror: gemma:2b scut gains nothing from a drafter; the cheap tier's speed lever is scheduling (sidecar SJF or simply splitting short/long janitor queues), while gemma4-class workers are the MTP beneficiaries if/when llama-server MTP lands for Gemma 4 (llama.cpp tracker issue #22747, search-level only, not fetched).

RQ2 (speculative decoding for structured tool-call outputs): the frontier moved from "draft faster prose" to "draft the structure". XGrammar-2 makes dynamic per-request structure switching (tool-call dialects, response protocols) near-zero-overhead with 6x faster grammar compilation (item 4). The estate already owns the two sharpest agentic drafts: AgSpec (state/arxiv/2026-10-02/2610.01108v1.txt:6 - retrieval over session/workspace/global corpora, 4.37x at batch 1, beats EAGLE-3) and the constrained-decoding-on-0.6B-4B result (flagged a known holding in sibling sweep inference-efficiency-arxiv.md:37: XGrammar lifts schema validity to 100% at 4-8 ms compile and only 1.6-3.7% throughput cost). Synthesis: for janitor tool calls, constrained decoding is effectively free and should be default-on; speculative drafting only pays when the drafter is fed from the agent's own repeated text (session/file corpora), not from a generic small model; and on small targets even perfect acceptance cannot beat the draft-overhead floor (item 5).

RQ3 (reproducible per-tier inference-budget accounting): the reproducible public standard is OTel GenAI semconv (item 7): per-call `gen_ai.usage.input_tokens`/`output_tokens` spans plus `gen_ai.client.token.usage` histograms filterable by `gen_ai.token.type` and `gen_ai.request.model`, cost derived by joining token counts to a per-model price table - which is exactly a per-tier ledger if tier is carried as a resource attribute. The estate already has both halves: gen_ai semconv tooling in-repo (catalog: gen_ai_semconv.py, LiteLLM genai_otel Grafana dashboard readme at mine/raw/agent_platform/BerriAI/litellm/cookbook/litellm_proxy_server/grafana_dashboard/dashboard_genai_otel/readme.md) and a witnessed-tokens carrier convention (descent protocol leg 8, usage.jsonl; envelope receipt watch/inbox/burn_wave_20260926/B2_25_envelope_remeasure_20260926.md:25 shows the arithmetic: 37,386,533 / 0.34165 h = 109.4M tok/h). Operationalizing the ribs' inference-budget law = emit OTel genai metrics per tier (local janitor / free-lane / funded) into one store and enforce budgets at the gateway, the pattern third-party guides converge on (search-level: Braintrust 2026-06-21 roundup, Solo.io agentgateway budget enforcement - snippets only, not deep-read).

RQ4 (is 109.4M tok/h width-10 account-specific?): yes in absolute value, and no public literature measures anything like an account-level rolling-throughput envelope, so it can only be internally re-measured. My independent reads agree with and extend the sibling arxiv door's verdict (watch/inbox/frontier_olm_2026-10-05/sweep/inference-efficiency-arxiv.md:47: "treat 109.4M tok/h as an account-and-epoch-specific measurement... nothing published contradicts it, nothing published confirms it"): the edge-serving literature measures per-request latency under fixed hardware (Clairvoyant: P50/TTFT on named GPUs, item 3), the fairness literature presupposes a provider-side rate limiter as an exogenous input (FairInference, sibling-banked today as 2609.18112: a token taking d in isolation completes within d+delta multi-tenant), and engine comparisons measure raw engine tok/s, never agent-harness witnessed tokens across a retry/backoff dispatch path. The number is a property of the zai-individual-coding-plan token bucket plus Task-agent retry shape (AGENTS.md fan-out law: maxAttempts 11 on Task agents, workflow children maxAttempts 0); it transfers as a METHOD (witnessed tokens / wall-clock window, N-wave confirmation), not as a constant. Re-measure after any provider-side change; treat first 429/1302 contact as a HOLD signal, per the standing envelope law.
