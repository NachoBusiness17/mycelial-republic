## The mechanism (port the audit protocol, not the model)

The paper's method is a portable instrument independent of its subject: (1) re-price the vendor's headline cost claim at the competing commercial model's documented batch prices; (2) benchmark accuracy against published human coders; (3) measure single-shot choice-probability calibration against BOTH a mid-tier commercial model AND an open-weight alternative; (4) render the verdict in the paper's shape: cost advantage gone at documented prices, calibration edge not consistent against the open-weight arm, surviving advantage only parseability of the fixed answer set (`state/arxiv/2026-10-06/2610.06625v1.txt:6`). Its design finding matters more than its verdict: the decision-model readout is not a magic calibration source, because an open-weight LLM asked once over the same fixed answer set matches it. So the honest competitor to a vendor decision model is a local readout over a fixed answer set, not the vendor's product.
