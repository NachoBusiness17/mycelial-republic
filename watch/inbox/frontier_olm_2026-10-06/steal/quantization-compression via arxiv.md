## The mechanism (one structure law, two faces)

**Codebook quality is measurable, and balance is its sufficient condition: equal fitting mass per codeword (entropic OT with uniform marginals, finite floor-guarded Sinkhorn) is what keeps every codeword updated; and the fitting stage is separable from the runtime stage, so balance work is an offline move that changes what gets built, never what gets looked up.**

- **Face 1, the meter (abstract-verified).** "Equal positive fitting mass for every codeword" is the condition; max codeword share and assignment entropy are its cheap meters; the codeword-mass floor is the retention condition that makes finite Sinkhorn iterations safe. Quality without balance leaves codewords "insufficiently updated" - dead centroids that silently shrink the effective codebook while the nominal K stays on the label. Any pipeline that clusters anything (law rows, error classes, docs) inherits this meter for free.
- **Face 2, the seam (stage-specific, RQ3 ruling plus the abstract's own discard step).** Soft assignments are discarded at runtime; only the refined codebook survives into hard nearest-codeword encoding. A balance refinement is therefore a build-time artifact: it never adds runtime cost, never changes the encode path, and never touches lanes whose quantization is scalar K-quant shaped - the AQLM rule's boundary, now confirmed from the method's own abstract.

## The falsifiable probe: CODEBOOK-CENSUS on the 625 pack rows ($0, CPU-only, an afternoon, no model invoked)

Hypothesis (RQ5 in falsifiable form): quantized_thought pack rows have codebook structure, and balanced fitting (BARQ's condition) improves the balance-vs-quality frontier on law text.

Steps:
(1) Load the 625 kept rows' `frontier` full texts from the three pack files (verified live this ask: the field exists on every row of all three tiers).
(2) TF-IDF vectorize, L2-normalize (sklearn 1.9.1, verified in `.venv` this ask).
(3) For K in {4, 16, 64, 256}: arm A plain k-means; arm B identical init, then balanced assignment via Sinkhorn with uniform marginals (small epsilon, codeword-mass floor per the abstract's retention condition), barycentric centroid update, 2-3 refinement rounds - BARQ's fitting loop in miniature.
(4) Meters per arm per K: max codeword mass share and normalized assignment entropy (balance); mean cosine distance of a 20% held-out split to its nearest centroid (conditioning quality).
(5) Verdict against falsifiers:
- COLLAPSE (the x-sweep's metaphor clause, `quantization-compression-x.md:261-263`): assignments concentrate (max share above 0.5 at every K) under BOTH arms - law rows do not have codebook structure; the HyperThink bridge stays metaphor on estate evidence; bank the census in compile_report.json and spend nothing further.
- NO-TRADEOFF: balanced fitting degrades held-out reconstruction by more than 10% relative at every K while buying balance - the sufficient condition does not transfer to law text; the bridge fails its cheap half and the same verdict stands.
