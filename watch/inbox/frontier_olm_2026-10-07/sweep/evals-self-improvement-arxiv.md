### 1. Open-Endedness Bench: Measuring Epistemic Process from Agent Records - arXiv 2610.02588 (2026-10-01) - ZERO overlap

Deep read (full HTML). Builds an "epistemic event graph" from agent execution records ONLY (never reference answers or outcome scores): L0 code normalizes each benchmark's logs into one record format; L1 an LLM judge reads 5-step windows and emits act cards (check/probe/commit/revise) and proposition cards, each required to quote its source step verbatim (cards failing literal-substring checks are discarded by code); L2 LLM juries ask each closed question 3x independently, majority; L3 deterministic typed-edge graph assembly. Scores 4 competence axes (E1 evidence, E2 experiment, E3 revision, E4 no-reward-hacking as 1/(1+k) hacking instances) + 6 persona traits on 119 runs / 12 tasks / 3 benchmarks.

Numbers that matter to us: "Against logged results, only 16-29% of the improvements agents claim are real." On the nanoGPT speedrun, 60% of follow-ups (194/324) built on wins that were not real. Best runs explore more in their second half (9/10 tasks, corr +0.53); median Chip-Bench run quits at 19% of time budget. Model explains more persona variance than task (median 43% vs 7%). E4 flagged 10/11 auditor-confirmed cheating runs on PostTrainBench. Reliability: median rescoring delta 0.04 same-judge, 0.08 cross-judge. Cost: median 652 judge calls per run - an offline instrument, not a training reward. Stated limits: cannot see unwritten reasoning; adversarial pressure against the instrument untested; pure hyperparameter search yields few extractable propositions.

Estate fit: this is the improve loop's missing grader for OUR claim class. Our falsifier trials and promotion candidates are exactly "improvements agents claim”; OEB's record-reading protocol (verbatim-quote-or-discard cards, 3x-majority juries, deterministic assembly) is a buildable $0 pattern for scoring whether a lane's claimed win was real before promotion. The 16-29% number is the external calibration for our self-steal meter.

### 2. FinRegQA-EU: Corruption-Based Preference Data for Grounded EU Financial Regulatory QA - arXiv 2609.36856 (2026-09-29) - ZERO overlap

Deep read (full HTML). 2,138 EBA/ESMA official Q&A pairs; two 70B answerers; THREE judges from different families (Qwen3.5-27B, Gemma-4-31B-it, GLM-4.7-Flash) scoring pointwise on 1-5 scales; a preference pair is kept only on UNANIMOUS judge agreement AND a >=1.5 gap - 359 training pairs from 2,134 questions. Negatives built by corruption: law_swap, article_swap, hallucinate_citation, hallucinated_text. Student: Llama-3.2-3B LoRA; SFT / DPO / Dr.DPO / GRPO.

The result that matters: "Standard LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation rewards citation density as evidence of grounding and cannot detect when those citations are fabricated." SFT got the TOP judge score (2.281 vs baseline 1.959) and the LOWEST deterministic Citation F1 (0.181 vs baseline 0.245), emitting 3x as many citations, mostly unsupported. Judges agree with each other at Krippendorff's alpha = 0.014 on citation quality (Qwen vs GLM). The paper's own fix is a deterministic regex-based instrument-level/article-level Citation F1, because the judges cannot see fabrication. Limits: small student, small pair count, accuracy stays ~1.6/5.

Estate fit: this is the published twin of our consensus illusion (lens/laws.md:89) - cross-family judge unanimity certified citation-dense fabrication, i.e. agreement-with-each-other actively anti-correlates with agreement-with-truth on the citation axis. Their corruption modes are a ready-made negative generator for the ask bank behind the duel harness (lens/laws.md:106), and their regex Citation F1 is the same instrument class as tools/citation_lint.py - external validation that the deterministic lint, not more judges, is the correct second judge for citation claims.

### 3. JudgeMoE: Distributional Aggregation for LLM-as-a-Judge - arXiv 2610.07109 (2026-10-05) - ZERO overlap

Deep read (full HTML). Six frozen small local judges (Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen3.5-9B, Gemma-2-9B, Gemma-3-12B, Llama-3.1-8B, Ministral-3-8B) each emit a probability vector over score buckets; distributions are CACHED so aggregation variants retrain without rerunning judges. A DeepSets permutation-invariant gate computes per-example softmax weights; fusion via tempered log-opinion pool (T=0.7); final score = expectation under pooled distribution. Training is tiny (Adam, lr 8e-4, ~20 epochs, Huber loss).

Numbers: "On the original 10-cell benchmark, JudgeMoE improves mean Spearman over uniform log pooling by +0.079." Across 16 cells: +0.0393 mean Spearman over the strongest single local judge, positive in 12/16 (Wilcoxon p=0.0091). Calibration ECE 0.059 vs 0.179-0.221 baselines. Cost table (FLOPs proxy vs DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp pass = 1.0): 3-judge pool 0.76x, 6-judge 1.43x; 1K-example probe ~26.5-50.2 PFLOPs. Failure modes named honestly: a strong expert added to a mixed pool HURT (COMET standalone 0.4326 vs 0.1620 mixed); gains vanish on TopicalChat and WMT MQM; hard-only scores 0.2634 vs soft+distributions 0.3776 - the probability distribution, not the verdict, carries the signal.

Estate fit: the judge2 workstream (lens/laws.md:105). The protocol answer to "raise agreement-with-truth, not agreement-with-each-other": do NOT aggregate verdicts or unanimity - aggregate cached score DISTRIBUTIONS with per-example weights, and keep the uncertainty. Their FLOPs table is exactly the per-verdict cost ledger the ask demands. The COMET caution maps 1:1 to our 0.6-0.67-strength remote consensus: adding another correlated judge can subtract.

### 4. SynthSentry: Detecting Synthetic Data Contamination in LM Training Data - arXiv 2609.12353 (2026-09-11) - ZERO overlap

Abs-page read. Corpus-level, model-agnostic contamination signal over three statistics: lexical diversity collapse, n-gram tail truncation, perplexity variance across reference models; no access to the generating model, generation history, or synthetic labels needed. Leave-one-generator-out evaluation; domain-stratified false-positive study on naturally repetitive human text (legal, clinical, code); covariance-shrinkage calibration with bootstrap thresholds kept false positives near budget while a naive quantile ran "four times over budget". Honest negatives: no contamination-driven accuracy deficit found at their scale, over-pruning risk flagged; small-scale, English-only, batch-mode, non-recursive.

Estate fit: the D6 ask-bank question (lens/laws.md:106) has two halves - generating 100+ unseen asks, and PROVING provenance. SynthSentry is the published provenance instrument: run it over the ask bank to certify it does not echo our own corridors (corpus, lenses, trails), with a calibrated FP budget instead of an eyeballed threshold. Limits (batch-mode, non-recursive) mean it checks the bank, not the loop.

### 5. Same-Number Citation Swaps: Stress-Testing Jev as a Financial Evidence Judge - arXiv 2610.08675 (2026-10-06) - catalog stub overlap (2026-10-07 daily harvest; full read new here)

Deep read (full HTML). Construction: from FinQA, 96 single-operation questions; A/B arms where arm B repoints one operand to a DIFFERENT CELL CONTAINING THE SAME NUMBER (same value, different year/cohort/status/metric) - operands, arithmetic, question, source all fixed, only the citation's financial role changes. Controls labeled "equivalent facts" separate valid alternatives from wrong-role errors. Judge under test: Jev via OpenRouter, binary source-support event, gated at 0.5/0.8/0.9/0.95. Baseline: signed-number-at-pointer (zero LLM calls; the signed operand value must appear at the cited pointer at original scale).

Numbers: on 192 natural traces Jev discriminates at AUROC 0.98587 / Brier 0.03378, and "At the inclusive 0.8 source-probability cutoff, all 27 key-matching traces with unsupported cited roles are withheld" - BUT on constructed same-number swaps, 4 of 6 wrong-role arms PASS at 0.8, some near 0.95 (wrong period ~0.95, wrong status 0.95). Explicit column labels in the lookup flip all 8 wrong-role arms to withheld (means drop to 0.115-0.620) at the cost of withholding valid equivalents (both enriched equivalent-fact means fall to 0.785). Honest limits: exploratory sequence, single-reviewer labels, no inter-human reliability, single-operation bank only, artifacts pending release.

Estate fit: numeric-correct-but-citation-wrong is the exact adjacency of our PHANTOM-NUMBER class (the number IS in the source, the role is wrong - a presence-check lint passes, the verdict is still false). Their same-number swap construction is a copyable negative-generator for citation-lint test fixtures, and their signed-number-at-pointer baseline is a $0 deterministic checker that caught the same failure class the LLM judge missed.

### 6. The Failure Is in the Readout: Fine-Grained Emotion Recognition Benchmarks Measure Elicitation, Not Perception - arXiv 2610.08162 (2026-10-06) - catalog stub overlap (2026-10-07 daily harvest; full read new here)

Deep read (full HTML, retried after one timeout). EmoNet-Face-HQ forced VLMs to emit exactly 25 of 40 emotion categories as JSON; general VLMs scored below a dedicated fine-tuned model (Empathic-Insight-Face) and below a human anchor (quadratic-weighted kappa 0.468 on the 5 most reliable categories). The paper changes ONLY the readout: one binary query per category, P(yes) read from logits at a prefilled answer position, quantile-calibrated to the gold marginal. Result: "The same eleven VLMs, on the same images, go from no interval lying entirely above the human agreement anchor to all eleven exceeding it significantly, with nothing changed but how the answer is read." Generative kappa 0.268-0.486 to verification 0.507-0.586; thresholding the same probabilities at 0.5 DROPS to 0.254-0.423 (below generative) - the graded probability is the signal, not the yes/no framing. Rescoring the benchmark's own 14 baselines: seven at 0.375-0.551, seven at <=0.011, FOUR BELOW the benchmark's own random baseline (0.009); Gemini 2.5 Flash: 0.002 under the zero-shot prompt vs 0.419 multi-shot, same model, same images.

Estate fit: the benchmark_bridge always-True lesson (lens/burns.md:114) generalized: a harness can route dollars on numbers that measure elicitation compliance, not the capability, and the benchmark's own baseline table contained the tell (models below its own random floor) that nobody read. The diagnostic pattern - change ONLY the readout and watch the verdict flip; read the random-baseline row - is a $0 dead-instrument detector we can run on any eval surface before its numbers steer spend.

### 7. Uncheatable Eval: Dynamic Compression-Based Evaluation of Language Models - arXiv 2609.27510 (2026-09-23) - catalog stub overlap (2026-09-24 daily harvest; full read new here)

Deep read (full HTML). Freshly collected public text across 14 categories (July 2026 set: 500/category, 7,000 samples, truncated to 3,584 tokens under 8 tokenizers); each model treated as a lossless compressor, CR = 100 x code length / (8 x UTF-8 bytes), lower better. Contamination avoidance by construction: "a dynamic benchmark that regularly collects newly published text to evaluate base language models and reduce the risk of data contamination." Numbers: 80 models; best CR Gemma-4-31B 6.00%; scaling fit CR(P) = 3.200*P^(-0.290) + 5.328, R^2 = 0.915; CR vs zero-shot MMLU Spearman rho = -0.884 over 14,042 questions. Honest limits: only suits BASE models (instruction-following invisible to CR); contamination risk returns as the set ages, so the refresh cadence is the instrument; no compute cost figures stated.

Estate fit: the live-eval half of the ask-bank answer - the pattern is "collect after the training cutoff, on a cadence, and let the metric be computed rather than judged." For the estate the transferable piece is the cadence-plus-freshness discipline (their stated decay law: an aging dynamic set re-opens contamination) applied to rotating duel-harness asks; the compression metric itself is base-model-only and does not fit our judged-verdict shape.

### 8. Verifier Errors in RLVR: Reward Hacking, Limits of Feedback, and Selective Control - arXiv 2609.35677 (2026-09-28) - catalog stub overlap (2026-09-29 daily harvest); NO HTML rendering exists (404 on versioned, unversioned, and ar5iv) - banked on the abs page read

Abs-page depth. Gradient-flow analysis with a fixed imperfect verifier characterizes when reward rises while correctness falls, and shows RLVR observations (the reward/correctness stream the loop itself sees) are generally insufficient "to detect or identify accepted errors, or to guarantee their reduction without sacrificing correct responses." The constructive result: a correction built from AUDIT feedback achieves selective control - lowering probability of accepted errors while raising correct responses at the current policy, provided audit pressure outweighs verifier pressure toward errors; supported by log-linear and neural contextual bandits plus a language model experiment showing selective control under PARTIAL auditing reduces accepted errors while increasing correctness.

Estate fit: the theory spine for both the gauntlet (lens/burns.md:123-127) and instrument liveness: no amount of self-play grading on its own accept/reject stream can detect the errors it accepts - the audit leg (our falsifier trials, human spot-checks) is not overhead, it is the only channel the theory says can see accepted errors. Partial-auditing selectivity is the formal shape of "audit cheap, audit pointed." Caveat: banked at abstract depth; the theorem statements, proof conditions, and experiment magnitudes are OWED until a PDF read.

## Cross-cutting take for the improve loop

Three convergent signals across independent papers: (1) verdict-level judge agreement is the wrong target - FinRegQA-EU's unanimity certified fabrication, JudgeMoE's gains come from distribution-level fusion, Readout's fix was reading the graded probability not the verdict; (2) the deterministic checker (regex Citation F1, signed-number-at-pointer, compression rate) consistently catches what LLM judges certify - the citation_lint instrument class is externally validated twice this month; (3) every accepted-error result says the audit channel is the only one that sees accepted errors. The single most buildable steal: OEB's verbatim-quote-or-discard card discipline over execution records, at 3x-majority juries, as the promotion grader for claimed improvements.
