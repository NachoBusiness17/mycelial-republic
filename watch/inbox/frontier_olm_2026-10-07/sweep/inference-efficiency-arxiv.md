## Banked items (strongest first)

### 1. What Does a Harness Repair? A Preregistered Study of Visibility, Baseline Adequacy and Evaluation Defects - arxiv 2610.05533 (2026-10-04)
The external mirror of the estate's EMPTY_REASONING law (lens/laws.md:34-36, per ask). Preregistered, 47 primary endpoints, 3 small models x 3 benchmarks, GEPA harness-search arm, 6 injected evaluation defects, frozen prereg + withheld row keys released as ancillary files. Findings: thinking-OFF beat a capped-thinking setting in 5 of 9 model-benchmark cells, and "the gain came mostly from questions where the capped setting gave no readable answer" - i.e. capped thinking manufactured empty/unreadable answers, the same failure class the estate treats as terminal-never-refire. A "thinking budget in the serving engine" (which also permits longer answers) lowered truncation and raised parse rate in 6 of 9 cells. Also: in 6 of 15 evaluable defect-model pairs, replication through the same pipeline reproduced the defect's distortion instead of revealing it. Estate use: this is the published prior that reasoning caps cause no-readable-answer failures and that budget headroom in the serving engine (not prompt-side) is the measured fix; it argues the estate's completion-budget-vs-hidden-reasoning conflict should be resolved on the engine side, and that replication alone cannot catch eval defects.

### 2. Evaluating Escalation Signals for LLM Routing: Targets, Controls, and Five Ways to Fool Yourself - arxiv 2610.07354 (2026-10-05)
The cost-per-blind-point prior (RQ1). Small/large pair ~12x apart in size; semantic entropy (sampled-answer disagreement, measured BEFORE the large model is called) reached AUROC 0.871 on GSM8K and "improves routed accuracy over random escalation by up to nine points at matched cost". The paper's spine is the confound checklist, which maps one-to-one onto the estate's routing ribs: (1) a question-difficulty-only rule with no model matched semantic entropy almost exactly on a synthetic benchmark - the estate's own "question-class is the dominant variance term" law (lens/laws.md:47, per ask) is exactly this confound; (2) ambiguous definitions of "escalation worked"; (3) benchmarks with no headroom; (4) live-sampling cost can exceed direct large-model cost; (5) cached-outcome signals collapse on transfer - they correctly forecast AUROC 0.908 -> 0.518 (chance) ahead of time. Estate use: before measuring its own grid cells, run their difficulty-only-rule control first; it is the cheapest possible blind-point probe.

### 3. RSI-Router: Evolving Subtask-Level LLM Routing and Skills for Cost-Efficient Agents - arxiv 2609.34712 (2026-09-28)
The cheap-seat-shoulders prior (RQ3) and the closest external twin of the estate's own dispatch pair: it routes between DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and Qwen3.5-9B - the estate's one filled grid cell (deepseek-v4.1-flash, lens/laws.md:104, per ask) paired with a 9B. Recursive self-improvement over subtask-level assignments plus per-model skills; beats the DeepSeek-only baseline at "roughly half the inference cost (48.3%)" across five agentic benchmarks; ALFWorld/ScienceWorld/WebShop cut cost 74.7-82.2% while IMPROVING performance; Terminal-Bench 2.0: +16.7% relative performance at 18.0% lower cost; stronger performance-cost Pareto frontier than 9 competing routers. Estate use: a measured existence proof that subtask-level (not request-level) routing on exactly the estate's model pair pays; feeds RIB_MODEL_GARDEN_ROUTING / RIB_ROUTING_CLASSES.

### 4. FORGE: Form-Optimal Routing of Grounded Evidence for Frozen LLM Agents - arxiv 2609.34358 (2026-09-28)
Reasoning-channel steering as a routing problem (RQ2+RQ1). Fixed defaults "misallocating support form or reasoning depth on roughly 80% of queries in our analysis"; FORGE routes per-query over the joint space of evidence form x thinking depth with an entropy-regularized cost-aware Boltzmann target in a 269K-parameter factorized router, no weight access (offline arm enumeration -> KL distillation -> GRPO with host feedback). Across 5 knowledge-intensive benchmarks and 8 frozen backbones 7B-671B: "FORGE improves accuracy at 42-45% lower token cost on both main hosts" and "composes with intrinsic thinking budgets where available". Estate use: the composes-with-intrinsic-budgets clause is the operational answer to RQ2 - provider thinking-budget knobs and a learned depth policy are not rivals; and thinking depth is cheap enough to route per-query.

### 5. GGUF-Metadata Prediction of Single-Sequence llama.cpp Throughput Across Three Systems - arxiv 2609.14864 (2026-09-14)
Local-throughput priors for the GPU ladder (RQ4; lens/burns.md:135-136 per ask). 318 phase-depth measurements, 53 host-file configurations, two Apple M4 Max systems + one RTX 5080; roofline-shaped predictors from GGUF metadata with quantization-specific scale factors. "an active-parameter decode model obtains 13.1%, 14.4%, and 36.1% mean absolute percentage error (MAPE)" "versus 49.4%, 55.3%, and 51.9% when charging total parameters" on host-specific held-out sets; leave-one-host-out 11.6/16.8/36.0%; a low-bit model ladder CHANGES MODEL ORDERING across runtime stacks. Estate use: throughput planning should key on active params, not total; and cross-stack re-measurement is mandatory when the quant ladder changes - directly disciplines the Vulkan-vs-CPU 12x claim's transferability.

### 6. Routing Should Pay for Itself: Sparse Supervision for Economical LLM Routing (SaveRouter) - arxiv 2609.37402 (2026-09-29)
Routing break-even economics (RQ1): prior work "emphasiz[es] serving-time efficiency without checking whether savings recoup this expense"; routing quality saturates before feedback collection ends, so "dense supervision can be economically over-provisioned". SaveRouter acquires informative feedback selectively + shares capability info across related queries: ~33-41% of training feedback used, break-even deployment volume cut 1.9-9.5x vs the fastest conventional router, quality maintained across 4 routing benchmarks. Key analysis: supervision minimizing serving cost differs from the level achieving earliest payback. Estate use: the grid-measurement plan (fill the unmeasured cells, lens/laws.md:104 per ask) should size its probe budget by break-even, not by saturation - measuring every cell densely is the over-provisioning this paper quantifies.

### 7. Does Mixture-of-Experts Actually Help Inference on Consumer and Edge Hardware? An Empirical Study - arxiv 2606.21428 (v1 2026-06-19, v3 2026-07-09; OUTSIDE the 30-day window, banked because it is the exact question of RQ4's MoE gate)
OLMoE-1B-7B (1.3B active / 6.9B total) vs three dense baselines on Apple M2 Pro + Jetson Orin Nano 8GB via llama.cpp: on the laptop MoE runs "~10% behind the same-active Llama-3.2-1B"; on the edge device "~31% behind, at 2.1x the energy per token" with memory hitting the 8GB ceiling; routing overhead under 9% of MoE-block compute on the cleaner backend. Conclusion: on bandwidth-bound hardware inference cost tracks TOTAL not active parameters. Estate use: the strongest published prior AGAINST jumping to 7B-class MoE on the local Vulkan/CPU ladder; supports the gates-FP8/MoE-on-real-demand stance (lens/laws.md:115 per ask). Authors flag single-model/single-scale limits and release the harness.

### 8. Substrate-Portable Execution for Production LLM Workflows - arxiv 2609.06128 (2026-09-05; 32 days old, 2 days outside window, banked as the only measured batch-economics find)
Batch/offpeak economics for the ds_workhorse lane (RQ5; lens/burns.md:222-223 per ask). Amazon Rufus production system: workflows compiled to streaming (in-process), durable (AWS SWF), or batch (Apache Flink) substrates with "No workflow code changes are required"; LLM inference is a suspendable node that adapts per substrate. Batch inference APIs offer a 50% discount at published prices; validated across dozens of production configurations and 5 workflow patterns with no output-quality difference across bindings, and batch execution cut per-query inference cost in line with published batch pricing at production scale alongside the streaming path. Estate use: the 50%-at-published-price shape plus "suspendable node" is the encoding pattern the lane runner should mirror (same task graph, batch binding for non-latency work). NOTE: this is a measurement of the discount's EXISTENCE at published prices, not a DeepSeek invoice audit - the estate's owed invoice-strength check remains owed.
