### 2. Show HN: Does per-step reasoning effort save money in Claude Code? jev-effort (HN 49824915, 2026-09-24, 4 pts)
https://github.com/ifoster01/jev-effort
Quote: "Cost −55% (−21% without its one outlier task), thinking −98.5%, wall time −58%"
Measured: six small coding tasks with visible+hidden tests, fixed effort vs Jev-chosen effort (a small decision model may only lower effort, never raise it above the session setting). At max: $3.76 to $1.70, "every hidden test passed in both arms" (12/12 vs 12/12), turns 92 vs 87. At high: "Cost −1.1%, thinking −46%". Real 92-minute 470-step session, shadow mode: estimated 4-9 pct saving; "Cached context was 82% of spend, thinking 9.5%". Effort is steered via Anthropic's per-message effort beta through an effort-only system message; the top-level API effort field "is ignored the same way"; changing effort per step "never reset the prompt cache". Author's own caveat: "Quality at max on real work is untested", and Jev-capped-at-max ($1.70) "isn't much better than turning effort down yourself" ($1.94 fixed high).
Estate: the measured reasoning-effort steering axis (ask question 2).
