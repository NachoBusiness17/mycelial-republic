## Pass 2, deep reads (8 items read end to end from live pages)

1. **Mingbird (2610.02001)**, 2026-10-01, Wang + Huang. Local-first agent harness for Windows and Ollama; 2-9B open models. LRAB: 4 harnesses x 4 open models (2B-35B) x 18 real tasks, all 288 cells published; Mingbird 0.886 vs goose 0.631 / opencode 0.479 / agent-mini 0.405; tau^2-bench 0.856 vs 0.791/0.737. Mechanisms: byte-level net-zero prefill budget, finish gate that re-reads the task, signature-level loop detection. Honest limits stated in-abstract: self-built benchmark, single machine, single-trial scoring.
2. **Measuring the Serving Stack Instead of the Model (2609.26693)**, 2026-09-22, Tang + Zheng. Ollama tools= is gated per model by a static template flag; Phi-3 and Gemma-3 rejected before inference; rejection/retry exhaustion not preserved as structured failure metadata (downstream analysis can read 0% fidelity as model non-calls); cross-stack probes differ across Ollama/llama.cpp/vLLM/SGLang; constrained decoding removes parse failures but can induce non-termination; turn-pooled vs per-instance estimates differ by up to ~55 points.
3. **Measuring the Microtask Eligibility Gap (2610.00025)**, 2026-09-01, Hu + Ramachandran. 16 Qwen3 SLM configurations (4 tasks x 4 models): 0 pass CI-backed thresholds; 4-bit RTN/GPTQ/AWQ quantization moves none into eligibility.
4. **What Stops a SLM From Driving a Database Agent (2609.21341)**, 2026-09-18, Bozoglan et al. 11-day production study, 39 open-weight models; of 2,100 model-attributed agent-mode losses, 1,590 (75.7%) came from runs that invoked at least one tool; five server changes (no model/prompt/sampling change) moved six models by 6-21 cells of 30. RE-BANK CAUTION: prior sweep (frontier_olm_20260928) already deep-read and banked this with full text; re-verified here, not re-banked as a new item.
5. **Keyword Harnesses Fail Open (2610.02142)**, 2026-10-01, Santillana. 661.6M vs 1,109M matched pair score near-identically on lenient tool-use metrics (B4 0.660 vs 0.650) but verbatim-reproduction + first-token probes separate them completely (missing prior, prob 1e-4..1e-5 on <|tool_call|>); a 3.3 GPU-hour targeted SFT repairs the 1B (valid emission 0.100 -> 0.959 on 269 rows; 0.536 vs 0.428 on 238 unseen, p=0.004); ladder costs minutes of CPU.
6. **Evaluating Escalation Signals for LLM Routing (2610.07354)**, 2026-10-05, Pishehvar + Morandi + Viswanathan. Semantic entropy as escalation signal: AUROC 0.871 on GSM8K, up to nine points accuracy at matched cost; central warning: an earlier strong-looking synthetic result was matched by a difficulty-only rule with no model involved; contributes fooling-checks.
7. **AdaSpark (2610.05774)**, 2026-10-05, Liu + Zhang + Xu. Online-learning scheduler for block-drafter speculative decoding; learns verify widths and acceptance while serving, no offline profile; 1.5-3.1x faster than llama.cpp's DSpark with same drafters on 3 dense + 1 MoE target; imparo engine 1.17-1.52x over the default three-token chain.
8. **ALoDLM (2610.04198)**, 2026-10-03, Fang et al. (13 authors). Token-adaptive latent recurrence for diffusion LMs; trained at 1.7B and 8B; beats all evaluated DLMs and corresponding AR baselines on 11-benchmark average at both scales; quality-efficiency claim rides "optimized inference engines."
   Also read: **Queen (2610.03695)**, 2026-10-02, Bhaskar + Cheng + Chen: 4B chess-LM, silent-expert encoder welded via cross-attention, +900 Elo over seven iterations (1782 -> 2697), recipe claimed general for silent-expert domains (games, robotics, computer use).
