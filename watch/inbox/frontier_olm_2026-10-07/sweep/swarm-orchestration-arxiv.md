## Pass 2: the 8 banked items (strongest first, all read via their abs pages this sitting)

### 1. arXiv 2610.04829 Viva La Vida: Verification and Accumulation Failures in Multi-Agent Proof Search (2026-10-04)
End-to-end instrumentation of an agentic prover + verifier ensemble + lemma library: "We instrumented such a system end to end and analyzed 51,754 traced observations across three full runs (186 hours, $5,694)." Three failure modes: (a) abstention counted as rejection, in 10 of 12 verification events one ensemble member produced no parseable output or hit an API error, making unanimous acceptance mathematically impossible with no error raised; (b) one verifier member approved 3 attempts GPT rejected, each falsely claiming to solve the open problem; (c) 24 of 93 lemmas (26%) were mined from refuted arguments with refutation context stripped. Prescription: "systems must distinguish abstention from rejection, preserve useful disagreement, and preserve the provenance and polarity of information."
Estate mapping: this is the B2-443 shape at scientific depth, a child-death census where the deaths are verifier abstentions misread as verdicts. The provenance-and-polarity clause is exactly the party-turn provenance contract (lens/laws.md:94 ask-supplied): information extracted from a rejected turn must carry its polarity into future context or it poisons the library. The abstention-vs-rejection distinction maps 1:1 onto the gauntlet's grader self-strike (lens/burns.md:123-127 ask-supplied): a grader that fails to parse is not a grader that rejects.

### 2. arXiv 2610.04686 When Debate Helps: Proposal Supply and Verification-Aware Readout in Multi-Agent Reasoning (2026-10-03)
Answers Q2 head-on: "Multi-agent debate can improve reasoning, yet often fails to beat simple majority voting." Debate helps only when two mechanisms both hold: proposal supply must surface a correct answer, and readout must identify it when voting misses it. Defines "recoverable headroom" (correct proposal exists, majority answer wrong) as the measurement target. Latent Verification Debate (LVD) has candidate proposals gather answer-specific verification evidence before the final answer; fixed-proposal interventions isolate the readout effect in "equivalent peer-support units". Coverage-selected societies of "neural-thicket agents" raise complementary proposal supply. Code: github.com/Wang-ML-Lab/when-debate-helps.
Estate mapping: the flock consensus illusion (agreement with strong-remote 0/3 at strength 0.6-0.67, lens/laws.md:89,102 ask-supplied) is a readout failure, and this paper gives the two-lever diagnosis: measure recoverable headroom first; if headroom is zero, no communication protocol can help, and if headroom is positive, spend the budget on verification-aware readout, not on more rounds of agreement. The equivalent-peer-support-unit accounting is a directly stealable metric for scoring whether a party turn's evidence actually moved the answer probability.

### 3. arXiv 2610.02588 Open-Endedness Bench: Measuring Epistemic Process from Agent Records (2026-10-01)
Q4's strongest published relative of the gauntlet: a benchmark-agnostic methodology that "reads only the agent's execution record and never a reference answer", compiles it into an epistemic event graph where each node carries "an exact excerpt that code verifies against the record", under the principle that "only evidence returned by an executed action can support or refute it." Scored 119 runs over 12 tasks from three benchmarks: only 16-29% of agent-claimed improvements are real; on 9 of 10 tasks the best run tried more new ideas in its second half than the worst run; persona variance explained 43% (model) vs 7% (task). Code: github.com/ARA-Labs/oeb.
Estate mapping: this is cheap-tier falsification of expensive-tier claims made structural: deterministic code checks verbatim excerpts against the run record, no reference answer, no premium judge needed. It is the published sibling of the $0.00 falsifier trials and the citation-lint PHANTOM-NUMBER class (AGENTS.md ask-supplied): a stated quantity must appear in the record or the claim dies. The 16-29% number is the reward-hacking catch rate reported for self-claimed improvements.

### 4. arXiv 2610.01583 Continual Reinforcement Learning with Neuroevolution (2026-10-01, v2 2026-10-02)
Q3 confirmation from outside: compares "evolution strategies (ES) and genetic algorithms (GAs) against state-of-art continual RL variants and population-based RL" across environments and task changes, policies from a few hundred parameters to million-parameter networks. ES "most consistently achieves a good stability-plasticity trade-off"; the GA is most plastic but forgets more; ES "finds the widest neighborhoods" of weight space where perturbed policies still solve tasks, and neighborhood overlap between tasks correlates with the stability-plasticity balance; plasticity-loss symptoms in RL "do not transfer to NE."
Estate mapping: independently reproduces the estate's own ES measurements (neighborhoods 3.9x wider, stability-plasticity 8/18 vs zero, lens/burns.md:159-160 ask-supplied) in the continual-RL register: widest neighborhoods and best stability-plasticity trade-off for ES vs gradient methods. The new stealable idea is the diagnostic itself: neighborhood overlap between tasks as the predictor of the trade-off, a measurable the estate's lora_pipeline could compute on its own checkpoints.

### 5. arXiv 2610.03010 Engineering Sustainable Agents: A Systematic Comparison of Agentic LLMs for Developer Workflows (2026-10-02)
The width A/B preview the estate owes S10 (lens/burns.md:218-219 ask-supplied): five SE tasks, six open-weight LLMs, two prompt strategies, three hardware platforms, from single-query baseline up to multi-agent workflows. Multi-agent designs "consume on average 6.36x as much energy" as the non-agentic baseline, run 6.07x as long (worst case 160x per task-hardware pair), and lightweight non-agentic and single-agent configs take "accounting for 59 of 66 Pareto-optimal configurations" of the accuracy-efficiency front.
Estate mapping: a measured pushback on width: on developer-workflow tasks the accuracy gain from more agents is limited and task-dependent while the cost multiplies ~6x, and the Pareto front is owned by narrow configs. For the S10 A/B (1.25x/1.5x band) this predicts the narrow arm wins on cost-normalized throughput unless the task has genuine recoverable-headroom structure; the paper's per-task accuracy split is the confound to control for.

### 6. arXiv 2608.12679 Beyond the Best Guess: Improving LLM Solution Coverage with Evolution Strategies (2026-08-13; outside the 30-day window, banked because it is the direct Q3 ES-vs-gradient answer)
ES as "a population-based, gradient-free post-training method that optimizes directly in weight space through random perturbations"; it "achieves consistently higher pass@k than RL and produces a broader output distribution with greater solution coverage", because RL post-training narrows the output distribution around high-reward outputs and collapses coverage. Authors include Miikkulainen, Hodjat, Qiu (Adaptive Agent / Sentient lineage).

Pairs with item 4: item 6 says ES widens coverage, item 4 says ES widens neighborhoods and holds stability.

### 7. arXiv 2610.03769 Bayes-Sufficient Compression Is Not Enough: How Does Communication Help Multi-Agent Systems? (2026-09-29, NeurIPS 2026)
Q2's blackboard-side answer: sender with broad context, executor with limited local view; message utility = receiver gain minus protocol tax (receiver-relative bounded coordination). "Even Bayes-sufficient compression can fail when a bounded executor cannot use its surface form." Three-stage decomposition (externalization, absorption, action closure) explains errors that remain after correct content arrives; fixed-message replay shows closure failures despite correct artifact recovery. Numbers cut both ways: the same Qwen protocol raises ContextBench joint accuracy 0.633 to 0.775 but lowers ToolSandbox 0.889 to 0.653; an inference-time selector improves the accuracy-cost frontier. Sender upgrades help only above a receiver-burden threshold (single-crossing condition).
Estate mapping: the context-pack and party-turn handoffs are exactly sender-protocol choices; this paper says the estate question "does the compressed pack beat the raw context" has a measured answer per receiver, and that upgrading the sender (strong-remote) only pays above a receiver-burden threshold, a direct caution for the flock-illusion fix: a stronger remote cannot rescue a weak receiver's action closure.

### 8. arXiv 2610.08101 Beyond Corrected Memory: Execution Consistency in Multi-Agent Systems (2026-10-06)
Q5 plus the inbox contract: "identical retained records can correspond to compliant and violating executions under the same task rule." Defines execution consistency via duties governing state use, information handoffs, and final-state agreement, with explicit evidence conditions; CAVERT extracts the execution relationships logs do support and diagnoses/recovers. Removing evidence (receipt, action dependence, response validity) left 82.4% of opposite-label pairs indistinguishable; restoring it separated 97.9%. CAVERT beat contract-prompted LLM and rule-based baselines in all 12 benchmark-executor settings, and beat rule-guided recovery in all four environments.
Estate mapping: the shared-surface contract (watch/inbox/SHARED_SURFACE_CONTRACT.json, ask-supplied) says preserve hashes and bounded pointers and leave a receipt before promotion; this paper measures why: without receipts and action-dependence evidence, 82.4% of compliant-vs-violating execution pairs are indistinguishable from the retained records alone. Stealable directly as the evidence-condition checklist for the switchboard's promotion gate.
