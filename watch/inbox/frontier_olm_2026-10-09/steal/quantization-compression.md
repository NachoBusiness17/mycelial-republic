## The steal (mechanism, not links)

On-policy self-visited-state recovery for quantized models: recovery training data generated BY the low-bit model, scored by a frozen full-precision teacher on exactly the prefixes the low-bit model visits. Buys accuracy below 4 bits that fixed-completion recovery leaves on the table (57.28 W3A16, 32.52 W2A16 on Qwen3-1.7B; state/arxiv/2026-10-08/2610.09346v1.txt:6). Not portable to T0/T1 as a training loop (needs the FP teacher and a QAT pass, funded-tier Lab compute); the on-box act is consuming released QAT checkpoints.

Why the estate cares: its own instrument says the current quant question is closed and flat. The 2026-10-08 quant_ab run (watch/inbox/frontier_olm_2026-10-08/sweep/quant_ab/run_ab.py, 10 tool-failure-recovery tasks x 5 phrasings = 50 runs per arm, temperature 0, paired seeds, $0 stdlib) measured Q4_K_M vs q8_0 as TIE AT CEILING: 50/50 pooled both arms, strict 49/50 both, all 50 output pairs byte-identical, per-task spread 0.0 pp, only decode speed moved (SUMMARY.json:3-31; RECEIPT.md:38-43,54-63).
