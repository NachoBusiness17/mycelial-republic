## Answers to the four research questions

**RQ1 - do frontier judge/contamination methods satisfy LENS 2's blind multi-family and consensus-illusion verdict laws, or do they fail as instruments?** By their own receipts they fail as raw instruments, and the best of them converge on our laws from different names. 2610.12083 stresstests 6 judges x 4 benchmarks x 5 prompt formats x 2 orders x 3 temperatures x 10 reps and finds verdicts flip across identical replications even at temperature 0; its "trustworthy verdict rate" = joint probability of reproducible + order-invariant + accurate is exactly our verdict-law triad in one scalar, and the most deterministic judge it found is perfectly consistent yet only 51% right - consistency is not truth, which is the consensus-illusion law with numbers. 2610.09554 measures the blind law directly: with the system's label visible, judge discrimination inverts ("anchor bias", J-ROC-AUC 0.12-0.87 collapsed vs 0.93-1.00 blinded; flip-success 53-99%), and stronger judges are MORE label-sensitive (frontier model paradox) - blinding is not hygiene, it is the instrument. 2609.12002 shows leniency tracks examinee capability (r >= 0.83) and fixes it with label-free weighted-majority voting calibrated from inter-judge disagreement - multi-family judging, but its ensemble is still same-family models; our law's family-blindness goes one step further than their fix. Contamination-side instruments exist (2610.10597 certified corruption budgets, 2609.27510 Uncheatable Eval compression - pass-1 candidates, not deep-read) but the deep-read verdict stands: frontier judging is our verdict laws half-implemented, failing whenever any single law is dropped.

**RQ2 - what experimental designs can the emergence A/B harness borrow so conditions-suspect re-measurement stops being suspect?** 2610.09239 is the direct receipt and the strongest single item of this sweep: treat keep-if-better as selection under measurement noise with correlated candidate errors; in a pre-registered study, greedy loops' selection-set score beat held-out accuracy by 13-20 points at 16 selection items and 1-5 points at 256; rescoring start+current on 64 items NEVER used for selection removed the average bias of the loop's reported gain (single estimates still +/-6); tested acceptance rules did not beat greedy; "Self-improvement studies should report held-out gains with their uncertainty." Borrowable design: (a) a frozen audit set the improver can never select on, (b) selection set >=256 or both scores reported side by side, (c) pre-registration of the acceptance rule, (d) uncertainty on every claimed gain. 2609.35815 (evalstats) supplies the inference layer: raw-judge-score statistics inflate false positives, FWER correction for N<100, warning against bootstrap CIs, first PPI corrections for rank tests. Conditions-suspect re-measurement stops being suspect the day the emergence A/B harness holds a never-selected verification set and reports its delta with an interval - the 13-20-point curse is what suspect measurement costs at n=16.

**RQ3 - does any frontier self-improvement loop beat the ratchet + citation-lint + census-patrol triad, or is the bank the bottleneck?** No measured loop beats a write-time verdict gate, and the frontier's best verification paper says why: 2610.11464 makes the grader the evolving object, gates it at birth, selects it ONLY on agreement with an anchored reference set plus consensus (never the agent's score) - and still proves "Downstream task score cannot certify a self-evolved verifier": remove the anchor guards and the verifier collapses into an always-pass grader that trains skills just as well. That is the ratchet's reason for existing, stated by the frontier. Their "Double Ratchet" (independent coinage, same shape as ours) retains 88-110% of ground-truth lift across three task families - verdict infrastructure carries the improvement, discovery does not. On the bank-vs-discovery question the arXiv evidence sides with the bank: 2610.09239 shows loops that bank on the same set they measure inflate gains 13-20 points, i.e. an unbanked/unverified fold is not a neutral backlog, it is an active overstatement machine. Pass-1 candidates point the same way (2610.12086 EvoAlloc: evaluation-budget allocation is the binding constraint, 59-82% eval cut; 2610.08993 idea-level critics to ration verification; 2610.11655 harness evolution hits a ceiling) - the day-8 mine's 32-sweeps-no-DIGEST is the same failure: discovery outran verdict-banking.

**RQ4 - 451-of-1,053 modules with no invariant: generation-first or verdict-first, and which side does the percolator take?** The percolator already takes verdict-first (sibling HN sweep re-confirmed `memory/percolator/invariants.json` is distilled from observed events with count+decay). The arXiv evidence says keep that AND add a contract-seeded generation leg, because both pure poles are now measured failures. Generation-first poisoned: 2610.11835 found 8-17% of LLM-generated regression tests ENFORCE faulty behavior (vs 2.4-4.8% that reveal faults), 83-91% still pass several commits later, 83-92% remain enforced at history end, and developer suites catch only 14-30% - unverified generated invariants are technical debt that writes itself in. Verdict-first starved: 2610.10604 shows the verdict canon itself is insufficient - a specification scored 100% positive test recall and 100% negative test rejection while accepting 0% of required inputs; "perfect postcondition scores can miss an unusable input contract," so admission needs separate evidence channels (input coverage AND output constraints), i.e. multi-family verdicts on each invariant. Where to generate: 2610.04921 generates from the harness's own contracts (HarnessTester: contract-faithful setups, +75.95% line / +84.76% branch coverage over SOTA, 122 real harness bugs, 88 unknown, 69 dev-confirmed - and its bug list names OpenClaw, which this estate runs per AGENTS.md). Prescription for the 451: seed candidates from harness-contract generation, admit them only through the percolator's verdict mill, and require each admitted invariant to carry its evidence channel (what it accepts, what it rejects, what inputs it covers) - the LENS 2 laws applied to invariant admission itself.
