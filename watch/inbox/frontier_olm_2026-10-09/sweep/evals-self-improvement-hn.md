## Answers to the four research questions

**RQ1 — do frontier judge/contamination methods satisfy LENS 2's blind multi-family and consensus-illusion laws?** No — in the wild they fail, and the frontier's own receipts say so. Every mechanism that hardens a judge converges on our law from a different name: position-bias swap checks (softwaredoug: forced 75.08% precision → 87.99% with both-ways check → 90.76% adding abstention, at recall cost), order-swap replication with a same-order control run BEFORE reversals are observed (Viridian), and judge-validity audits as a first-class module (Causeval: bias probes, calibration, PPI; its live benchmark found a judge picking the second-presented answer ~79% of the time). None of the read mechanisms are family-blind (Viridian explicitly blind-to-identity only). Consensus-illusion is alive on HN itself: the 166-point Jev thread rode a title the commenters flagged as editorialized ("In fact Jev does reasonably well in both tests"), and the article admits no calibration metrics were reported while a commenter's die-roll test showed ~80% confidence on a known die roll. LiveBench's contamination answer (monthly fresh questions grounded in recent sources, objective ground truth, no judge at all) is the strongest contamination-side instrument seen.

**RQ2 — what experimental designs can the emergence A/B harness borrow?** The evalship noise paper is the direct receipt: measure the noise floor on the unchanged default branch first (2.5% of 2,690 passing evals flipped on next run; median 73.5 flips/night with zero code change on a 2,268-eval suite), then size detection to it (20-eval suite needs 3 failures to beat chance at 95%; 2,268-suite needs 88; a real 16-eval break in the big suite is caught only half the time), compare each eval to its own history, separate errors from answers, repeat 3-10x with multiple-comparison correction (HumanEval significant-difference floor: 12 points → 2-4). Causeval wraps that in a harness shape: `plan_power` sizing, paired bootstrap/Wilcoxon/McNemar + Holm, ICC variance decomposition, seeds and provenance (dataset hash, git SHA), and the three-valued gate where "inconclusive" is first-class. (auto)²-research adds matched seed panels (3-seed exploration, 10-seed final). Conditions-suspect re-measurement stops being suspect exactly when the noise floor is measured, the gate is three-valued, and seeds are paired — all three borrowable today.

**RQ3 — does any frontier self-improvement loop beat the ratchet + citation-lint + census-patrol triad, or is the bank the bottleneck?** (auto)²-research is the strongest frontier loop found, and it validates bank-first while proving the bank is the bottleneck: weights reset every run, everything rides a 15,099-node knowledge graph (Trackinizer), 6,164 experiments in 3 days — and every named failure mode is bank-side: plateau from "repository of failed experiments", 35 completed panels unqualified by a review gap, completed work waiting ~50 minutes for qualification. That is the day-8 mine's 32-sweeps-no-DIGEST pattern at frontier scale: discovery outran verdict-banking and the loop slowed. No loop found beats a write-time verdict gate (our ratchet/citation-lint class); their coordinator-qualification analog batches verdicts and becomes the constraint. The triad's shape (verdict at write time) is the advantage; the fold-in discipline (DIGEST) is the debt.

**RQ4 — 451-of-1,053 modules with no invariant: generation-first or verdict-first, and which side does the percolator take?** The percolator is verdict-first: `memory/percolator/invariants.json` entries are distilled from observed events (text, count, first_seen/last_seen, decay, cycle_hits keyed to `behavioral_event` — read this session, 6,231 lines). The frontier's own taxonomy (dair.ai collection, 31 papers, sections "Improving the improver" and "Where the loop breaks": self-correction limits, model collapse, misevolution) says verdict-only starves; (auto)²'s generation leg ran 6,164 experiments wide. Prescription for the 451: generate candidates from patrol/trace sidecars (generation-first for coverage, seeded externally), but let invariant status pass only through the percolator's count-and-decay verdict mill (verdict-first for truth). The percolator should not generate; the sweeps feed it.
