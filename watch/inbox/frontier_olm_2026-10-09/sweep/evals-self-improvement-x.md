## Answers to the four research questions

**RQ1 - do frontier judge/contamination methods satisfy LENS 2's blind multi-family and consensus-illusion verdict laws, or do they fail as instruments?** By their own measured receipts they fail as RAW instruments and validate the estate's laws as the fix. The sermakarevich article (Oct 3, 148k views) is the practitioner canon in one read: a judge with 60% raw human agreement scores Cohen's kappa 0.15 ("a judge that agrees with humans only 60% of the time will tell you your product is fine when it is not" is its Level-4 headline); same-family judges inflated their own provider's agents by ~0.75 on a 7-point scale in the GAUGE audit (its stated mitigation: use a judge from a different family than the system under test - exactly LENS 2's blind multi-family law); consensus is an illusion twice over - Acharya/RoPoLL proves one broken judge drags a panel AVERAGE arbitrarily far (fix: geometric median) and "three judges capture most of the benefit, because judges' errors are correlated" (agreement is correlation, not confirmation), while Parikh 2026 shows JSON structured output collapses answer diversity (modal share 41% -> 64%), so identically-prompted judges agreeing is partly format, not truth. harshbhatt's article (Sep 20) lands the same law in plain words: a judge "can subtly prefer answers that sound like something it would have written itself," and HealthBench's meta-eval puts the model grader "roughly in the range of a typical physician's, not a gold standard above it." Contamination-side instruments are real and measured (InSilico: Morgan-fingerprint audit found a re-split carries 12.8% flagged cross-set pairs vs the published split's 2.0%, with stereoisomers reading as "exact" Tanimoto-1.0 matches; harshbhatt: OpenAI's SWE-Bench Pro audit found ~30% of tasks with meaningful issues, "passing the verifier != solving the real task"; MMLU-Redux ~6.49% wrong answer keys) - they audit the corpus, not the judge, and so cover the other half of LENS 2.

**RQ2 - what experimental designs can the emergence A/B harness borrow so conditions-suspect re-measurement stops being suspect?** The X lane hands over four borrowables. (1) The serm article's Level 5 is the complete kit: bootstrap intervals on every rate, PAIRED comparisons on the same cases (interval narrows because ticket difficulty cancels), McNemar for the per-case win/loss trade, a stated detection floor from a power calculation ("with 60 cases, the smallest difference we can reliably detect is about 12 percentage points. A 5-point regression is invisible at this size"), multiple-comparison correction, and "no measurable difference" treated as a real result. (2) Pin the conditions tuple: Performance = f(Model, Harness, Tools, Reasoning Budget, Environment) (harshbhatt) - conditions-suspect re-measurement is suspect exactly because harness and reasoning budget ride inside the number; record the tuple or the re-measure is not the same instrument. (3) Error bars as leaderboard furniture, with the negative result taught by example: Arena's top six sit within 8 points with overlapping error bars (TeksEdge, Sep 27) and Real-SWE gets publicly praised for including error bars at all (daveholtz, Sep 10) - the default state of close races is within-noise, so the A/B harness should publish its interval next to every delta. (4) Same-settings independent runs (ValsAI's "one benchmark, the same settings for everyone, run independently") is the protocol shape for re-measurement: freeze settings, third-party execution, one number per setting.

**RQ3 - does any frontier self-improvement loop beat the estate's ratchet + citation-lint + census-patrol triad, or is the day-8 mine's unbanked fold proof the bank is the bottleneck?** No loop found beats write-time verdict gates; the frontier's own best practice converges on the triad's shape and then adds two things the triad lacks. serm Level 8: the merge gate fails the build on a >3-point pass-rate drop against a recorded baseline - a ratchet by another name - and its two upgrades are (a) STATE THE GATE'S DETECTION FLOOR ("a gate is a floor, not a ceiling"; the 3-point tripwire walks 5-point regressions through) and (b) calibrate-then-trust: audit the cheap gate once against verifiable ground truth, trust it in CI only inside that region, re-audit when model/judge/domain changes. The verification-horizon section adds the held-out-grader law: any grader that influences selection gets gamed, so keep one the improver never sees (their behavior-monitor cut hacked solutions 28.6% -> 0.6% while raising genuine ones 40% -> 61%). suraj's stage 11 ("data flywheels turning failures into tests") is the loop's fuel feeding the bank, not a replacement for it. On the day-8 mine: this lane adds no counter-evidence to the bank-bottleneck reading, and inherits the siblings' caveat that "32 unbanked" did not verify as a literal count (inference-efficiency-x.md line 76) - the fold discipline (DIGEST) remains the named debt either way.

**RQ4 - 451-of-1,053 modules with no invariant: generation-first or verdict-first, and which side does the percolator take?** The X evidence says the percolator's observed-verdict-first stance is correct and generation must be failure-taxonomy-seeded, not imagined. serm Level 2: "the failure list comes from reading outputs, not from imagining them," "the failure list is the plan" (one grader per observed failure type, most frequent first), "a grader built for failures that do not occur gives a reassuring number and misses the ones that do," and criteria drift means the rubric sharpens over 2-3 grading rounds - i.e., invariants are distilled from observed verdicts and then iterate, which is precisely count+decay admission. harshbhatt supplies the SHAPE of an admitted invariant: rubric line items ("flags this as a medical emergency") are independently checkable verdicts you can disagree with one at a time - "the number is a verdict, not an argument" is the anti-holistic law, and HealthBench's 48,562 physician-written criteria show the scale at which itemized verdicts still beat one holistic score. Prescription for the 451: mine the patrols' and sweeps' failure taxonomies for candidates (open coding -> axial coding), let each candidate through the percolator's verdict mill before it gates anything, and store it as a line-item verdict (one checkable claim with its evidence channel), never as a holistic score.
