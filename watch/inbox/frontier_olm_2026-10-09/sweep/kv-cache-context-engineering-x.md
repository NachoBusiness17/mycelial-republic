## Answers to the four research questions

RQ1 (prefix-cache economics at 50-children fan-out vs the byte-identical-ask rule). The frontier's measured answer is the SemiAnalysis AgentX 1.0 benchmark (Apache 2.0, 1M-context agentic traces built from Claude Code, "$3M" budget claim) linked from the vLLM post: ask-level identity alone does NOT hold the cache. Prefix hit rate spans 5.6% (bad retention policy) to 96.45% (ATOM sliding-window retention); MiniMax M3 at concurrency 32 hits 28.8% against 96.0% theoretical; B300 with 3TB DRAM offload holds 91% HBM hit at concurrency 384. What buys the hit: consistent-hash routing by session/subagent ID, session stickiness to the rank holding the cache, idle-expiry so stickiness does not unbalance the cluster, and KV offload tiering. Our playbook law (corpus/steal/ZCODE_OPERATING_PLAYBOOK_20260925.md:10-12: amend-cache keyed on byte-identical ask text, FNV-1a) stays necessary but is not the dominant variable at width 50; the frontier adds route-level affinity on top of ask identity. OpenAI is meanwhile pushing hit rate to provider-default ("up to 90%" cached discounts on GPT-6), which shrinks the marginal value of our own ask-shaping on hosted lanes and concentrates it on our self-hosted/local lanes.

RQ2 (paged attention / sliding windows / recompression vs the L0d context-pack shedding fix and the Hermes prefetch-spilling port). Three bearings. (a) The Hermes upstream receipt: the Sep 6 PR wave fixed exactly our diagnosed failure class, "oversized external memory prefetch no longer floods every turn", plus a guard so a consolidation batch cannot silently empty USER.md/MEMORY.md; commit f676ee787 (2026-10-09 hermes digest, in-repo) already ported the ACTION, so this item banks as the upstream receipt, not new mechanism. (b) KV-streams (repo github.com/Emilianopp/KV-streams, paper already held at state/arxiv/2026-09-29/2609.35750v1.txt) is compaction WITHOUT re-prefill: it removes discarded turns directly from the live KV cache, drops complete vLLM blocks, keeps RoPE rotations, training-generation KL 0.001, and reached peak agentic-RL performance in ~32h vs ~60h for re-prefill. That is the direct alternative to our shed-and-repack context-pack: re-pack re-runs prefill over everything retained; KV-streams pays only for the excision. (c) DeepSeek V4.1-Flash (held) shows the tiering pattern: global KV persistent 72h+ on SSD, sliding-window state lives minutes in a shared pool, bounded replay reconstructs it. Our L0d fix should name which class each shed page belongs to before choosing rebuild-vs-persist.

RQ3 (does LLMLingua-class compression clear the instruments-only and blind multi-family judging laws, LENS 2). No compression vendor post found practices blind multi-family judging. The only direct LLMLingua head-to-head surfaced is SOMA's own marketing run (LongBench v2 at 40% compression: 36.5 vs 33.5/33.1), vendor-reported, no code link in the post, so not byte-reproducible from the post; LongBench v2 is public so only the LLMLingua side is independently re-runnable. Two supporting frontier facts we already hold or newly confirmed: arXiv 2609.31415 (in catalog since 2026-09-28) shows KV-reuse evaluations systematically inflate effectiveness and ships Boxoffice to generate honest reuse benchmarks; and the istdrc/Raft post is the nearest live example of the LENS 2 verdict shape done right (63 blind judge verdicts across three model families). Verdict: compression results remain aggregate-benchmark claims, not verdicts; our instruments-only law stands.

RQ4 (frontier analogue pricing the one-time context tax against per-ask re-packing). 
 Yes, and it is concrete: Raft's "Constructed Initial Context" (Oct 5) prices it with receipts. Median cold wake was a 643K-token cache write; their rule (idle over an hour AND session over 128K tokens) starts fresh from a ~10K seed (median 9.7K: MEMORY.md head capped ~4K plus newest 25 messages after dedup), covering 89.8% of cold wakes, cutting cold-wake cost 60%, per-request cost 29%, compaction stall 73%. The equivalence claim was judged blind: 60 of 63 verdicts across Claude, GPT, and Kimi found the fresh-seed session indistinguishable from full-context behavior; shipped in Raft Computer 1.0.40 (Sep 29). That is deliberate-ask's pay-the-context-tax-once doctrine in provider-TTL terms: pay once while the session is warm; after TTL death, do not resurrect the slab, re-seed small. Uber Engineering supplies the write-side price menu: 5-minute entries cost 1.25x to write, 1-hour entries 2x, reads 0.1x, which is the curve our T-30 pre-stage and context-pack cadence ride.
