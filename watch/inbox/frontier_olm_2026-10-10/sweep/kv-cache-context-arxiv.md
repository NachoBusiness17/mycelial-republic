**RQ2 (do current results address the exact long-session stall class: stalled compaction marks, overflow_reason, f06008308).** Yes, and the estate already holds the two best answers. ReFold (held, 2610.07863) avoids "up to 92% of forced compactions" by reversible rendering-layer folding, which structurally removes the stall class: nothing destructive is mid-flight when the window caps, so marks cannot stall mid-compact, and removals are reversible. HEAR (held, 2610.06597) is the overflow_reason port's blueprint: engines returning runtime state (occupancy, capability, outcome classes) to the harness is exactly the control direction; overflow_reason is a one-field HEAR instance and the port should be specced against its vocabulary. PCR (held, 2610.02766) prices how long a stalled session's paid-for prefix stays worth retaining (ordered timeouts hit the unrestricted optimum in 118/120 cases on Mooncake traces). New this sweep: Coda's admission layer covers the queue-level stall sibling (turns wait for compatible warm state instead of thrashing).

**RQ3 (does the frozen-prefix law, $161 to $3.60, extend to multi-turn agent sessions; what breaks it).** It extends, with measured boundaries, and it breaks at exactly the points the estate has already named on disk. Extends: the production trace (held, 2609.34432: 11.7M requests, 10k+ GPUs) shows "context reuse across task boundaries", so the reuse structure survives past single dispatches and past task boundaries. Breaks: (1) mid-session re-route or re-pack rebuilds the cache (Harness Tokenomics, held: routing is legal only where no running conversation rebuilds its cache; 13-21% spend recovery is the priced upside of respecting it); (2) compaction is prefix-cache death (verified on disk this session at memory/runs/gapwave/context_economics_1m.md:46: "compaction invalidates the whole prefix", cached share 55 to 95 percent and TTFT 26-28 s to 7.3 s when serialization and layout are stabilized); CADOC (held) is the repair kit: batch object edits to preserve reuse; (3) tool schemas and state blocks mid-prefix: the deterministic tool-schema serialization fixes are already merged upstream (bifrost#7170 + mcp-go#984, per the 10-02 steal), so the estate's forward gate is key-order discipline in any tool-carrying dispatch. Coda (new) adds the fourth boundary: admission timing at turn boundaries.

**RQ4 (measured prefix-cache-rate vs answer-quality relationship for sticky routing).** The window's evidence says the cache itself is quality-neutral and the risk lives in state and order. Held: persistent-memory null (2610.07782: no detectable accuracy change, structural null); KL-scored eviction preserves behavior at matched budgets (2610.06479); compression degrades reliability before solvability (PAIR 2609.36526); and the decisive one, 2610.05833 (the ask's own bank): an unchanged prompt answers differently depending on prior requests, 69.0% variation under token top-k selective reuse vs 26.1% document-aligned. New: ReadKV bounds the byte-precision axis (0.66% perplexity at half read precision) and Attic-KV shows quality can RISE under aggressive keep ratios versus full rereading. Adoption verdict for sticky routing: adopt, but with the quality gate being order-stability (document-aligned reuse classes, fixed request order per session) rather than cache-hit telemetry; the ask's 95-to-58-to-22 perturbation-class figures (carried in the ask's note; I verified :46 of the gapwave file but not those three numbers on disk) mark the sensitivity to perturbation classes, which is the same conclusion from the other side.
