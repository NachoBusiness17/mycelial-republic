## Answers to the four research questions

**Q1 - janitor-scale families that beat the incumbents (gemma:2b scut / gemma4 short worker), instruments-only law.** Yes, with two named mechanisms. (a) *Per-call-site, not per-model*: 2610.09021 measured 2,520 production calls across five call sites and found the best local model statistically indistinguishable from hosted models at 4 of 5 sites; only code generation separated. The falsifier gem inside it: aggregate accuracy hid that Gemma4 E2B actuates on devices the site does not own 87.2% of the time - the incumbent family's failure mode is safety-shaped, invisible to accuracy instruments. It also caught a 4B underperforming its 2B sibling at grounded actuation. (b) *Family evidence*: 2610.10827 fine-tuned Qwen3.5 0.8B/4B and beat prompted GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.6 Sol on precision/recall at nested strictness, 16x serving-cost cut - external measured validation for the estate's standing move-to-Qwen3.5 plan (`corpus/library/kev/PLAN_Qwen35.md` is already in the catalog). Displacement verdict: at 4/5 call-site classes a local model displaces the funded-remote tier today; code generation stays remote; and the incumbent Gemma rung needs a device-scope safety probe before further trust.

**Q2 - local-serving stacks mature enough to pull work down from the funded tier.** The serving frontier is now system-level, not kernel-level: SEIS (2610.04646) let an agent redesign a whole mini-SGLang engine to 3.27x, beating vLLM/TensorRT-LLM/SGLang at single-request on H100 serving Qwen3-0.6B. Methodologically this is the estate's own self-improvement doctrine arriving in serving systems. 2610.09778 supplies the instrument: a sequential-isolation protocol that cuts benchmark CV from 15.2% to 2.2% on vLLM 0.9.1 - exactly the measured-not-vendor rigor the instruments-only law demands, adoptable for the estate's own local-model gating. Together: single-request local latency is improving fast enough that batch-1 janitor work has headroom; the missing piece for the estate is adopting the isolation protocol so local rungs are compared under 2-3% CV, not vendor numbers.

**Q3 - does routing dominance (36.3 vs 3.6 percent variance) replicate, or does its falsifier fire?** Both, in the same paper. 2610.07354 found semantic entropy "reliably distinguishes the small model's mistakes (AUROC 0.871)" - the signal class is real. But its own controls fired the estate-style falsifier: a question-difficulty-only rule "matched semantic entropy almost exactly" on an earlier benchmark, two success definitions diverged on identical data, some benchmarks leave no headroom over always-large, and live-sampling cost can exceed just calling the big model. Direct action for RIB_MODEL_GARDEN_ROUTING: run the difficulty-only control on the 36.3-vs-3.6 claim before promoting it from law to routing-cell input; and price the probe's own token cost into the routing decision.

**Q4 - free-tier provider map, lanes that die.** Two current data points. (a) Death mode: 2609.31288 - "82% (95% CI 79-84) of migrations away from retired models were committed after the shutdown date"; notice length is what predicts it (89% post-shutdown under Anthropic's 60-114-day notices vs 13% under OpenAI's one-year); 94% of apps hard-code model IDs; only 8% switch provider. Seat-hunt cadence implication: treat every short-notice lane as dying unmigrated; the estate's provider abstraction is the minority pattern that survives. (b) Cheap-lane quality: 2610.09112 measured DeepSeek V3.2 at 57.9% vs Claude Sonnet 4's 61.7% on a 103-task tool-using benchmark - 93% of capability at 11.3x lower list price, and the cost-accuracy Pareto frontier is mostly open-weight models. The retired ling-3.0-flash-fin rung's replacement class is measured, not vibes.

Quote: models "keep generating fluent text while no longer knowing what it means." Quote: semantic entropy "reliably distinguishes the small model's mistakes (AUROC 0.871)" on GSM8K, up to +9 points routed accuracy at matched cost; and the falsifier - a difficulty-only control matched the signal on an earlier benchmark; predicted a cached-outcome method's collapse from AUROC 0.908 to 0.518 in advance.

7. **2610.09112 - GeoNatureAgent (GNA)** (Diaz-Ireland et al., 2026-10-06, ACM SIGSPATIAL GeoIndustry '26). Quote: DeepSeek V3.2 at "93% of Claude's capability at 11.3x lower list-price cost" (57.9% vs 61.7% over 103 MCP-tool tasks, 3 seeds); Pareto frontier mostly open-weight.
