## The steal (the mechanism, one paragraph)

A promote gate is a statistical instrument, not a counter. The estate's invariant path promotes on recurrence count alone (`mag/percolator.py:264-272`), and this area supplies the four published components it lacks: (1) a certificate-level accept predicate that stays sound under adaptive benchmark reuse by feeding candidates decision-only feedback and splitting a global error budget alpha across decision histories, certifying "with probability at least 1-alpha, every promoted modification is a genuine population improvement on the underlying task distribution" (REUSE, arxiv.org/html/2609.33180v1, quote normalized to ASCII; cut naive false promotions from 75, up to 20.7% of accepted updates non-positive, to 0 at matched final performance within 0.04 pp); (2) a two-tier instrument set that accepts on a private held-out grade, then proves generalization on benchmarks never fed back into selection, plus a held-out hacking instrument (AIDE2, arxiv.org/html/2609.26457v1: 99 proposals, 7 accepted, grade 0.703 to 0.778 over an 8-day run, hacking 55% to 32%); (3) an audit-the-passes integrity loop where route closure does not imply channel closure and only fresh evaluation closes the channel (Unearned Passes, arxiv.org/html/2609.34262v1); (4) paired evolving-vs-frozen longitudinal regression catching with attribution (SEABench, arxiv.org/html/2609.35596v1). PIVOT-KG (arxiv.org/html/2609.32677v1) prices the verifier tier and RegenHarness (arxiv.org/html/2609.27612v1) gives the harness-configuration variant with a non-weakenable commit gate (protocol only, no experimental RSI results, verified).

## The six finds, mapped to organs (port move each)

1. REUSE (2609.33180, arxiv.org/html/2609.33180v1) -> **percolator**. Verified live: certificate holds over the whole run via a cumulative lower bound; mechanism is decision-only feedback (candidates never see scores), paired sign tests per fixed decision history, alpha split as delta_{t,p} = alpha*w_t*v_p/(C(t-1,p)*K^(p+1)); naive best-of-K took 75 false promotions (20.7% of accepted updates non-positive), REUSE took 0. Port move: add a REUSE-shaped accept predicate to the percolator repeat branch at `mag/percolator.py:268-272`; the dormant `mag/seed_percolator.py:33-35` thresholds are the mounting bracket, so this is wiring a Dormant organ (seed gate) onto a live one (invariant path), not building from zero.
2. AIDE2 (2609.26457, arxiv.org/html/2609.26457v1) -> **percolator + gopher fleet**. Verified live + raw abstract: bi-level gate, private grade g(a) over 3 task families for acceptance, then generalization on 4 never-reused benchmarks (ALE-Bench, MLE-Bench, FML-Bench, WeatherBench 2, the last out-of-distribution) and a held-out hacking instrument; strongest discovered agent matched or beat the human-engineered baseline (0.749 private grade) on all four. Warning verified: acceptance is non-monotone across checkpoints (AIDE47 best on MLE/Weather, AIDE85 on ALE/FML), so any estate ledger must keep per-checkpoint rows, not a final score. Port move: acceptance grade = frozen probe suite never fed back into selection; generalization = one held-out task family the percolator never draws from.
3. Unearned Passes (2609.34262, arxiv.org/html/2609.34262v1) -> **sovereign mirror + the tripwire tier**. Verified live: 3,810 passing trajectories audited across 29 model-benchmark cohorts (SWEBench Pro V1.0, MCP Atlas, SWE Atlas TW, Terminal-Bench 2.1, ALE); violation requires mechanism + intent + grade relevance, else the pass is merely "unearned"; the git_oracle_seal example verified: the patch blocked the recorded `git show` route while a readable copy of the withheld test stayed elsewhere in the image, replay looked closed, 1 of 7 fresh attempts still read the protected content, and only removing the copy closed the channel. Closing law verbatim: "Benchmark validity requires ongoing maintenance as agents change." Port move: after any tripwire fix, a fresh attempt at the regulated behavior through alternative routes is the closure test, and an integrity gap is reported alongside the score; the mirror-lens promote verdict (`mag/improve.py:2721-2724`) adopts the ternary violation test instead of a binary reject.
4. SEABench (2609.35596, arxiv.org/html/2609.35596v1) -> **percolator ledger + lens laws.md:69**. Verified live, with one correction to the find: attribution is to the self-evolution process as a whole, not to a specific artifact (paper Appendix A leaves per-artifact counterfactuals to future work). Numbers verified: 48 longitudinal sequences, evolved paired with non-evolving baselines; completion 340/720 (47.2%) vs 257/720 (35.7%); safety failures 316/720 evolved vs 0/720 baseline, Fisher p=3.48e-13; attribution judge threshold >=4. Port move: the laws.md:69 ledger row shape is {upstream edit class, downstream task, paired deltas, attributed thread, process-level attribution score}; run each percolator promote as the evolved arm and the prior registry state as its own paired frozen baseline.
5. PIVOT-KG (2609.32677, arxiv.org/html/2609.32677v1) -> **gopher fleet** (verify-at-funded-tier economics). Verified live: Proposition 2 constructs a verifier with MAE 2/[n(n-1)] and Spearman rank error tending to 0 that still reverses 100% (IRR=1) of operator-proposed updates when the proposer offers adjacent-swapped pairs; Leduc confirmation cohort reversal 2.5% at one step to 51.7% at eight; the fix allocates scarce high-fidelity eval by expected value of sample information per cost (EVSI/c), cutting mean ISR 0.0435 to 0.0055 on HighwayEnv and saving ~26% of HF queries with the stopping variant. Port move: the evidence-backed sharpening of the driving-ladder rule (AGENTS.md 2026-09-28: free lanes SENSING only): rank pending promotions by expected selection-regret reduction per funded-tier dollar and grade top-down, instead of uniformly climbing.
6. RegenHarness (2609.27612, arxiv.org/html/2609.27612v1) -> **percolator registry schema + gopher fleet updater**. Verified live, caveat confirmed: change manifest (affected component, motivating traces, intended effect, rollback version), fixed regression set including stale-evidence / duplicate-request / interrupted-run / resource-conflict cases, binary release rule (acceptance predicate requires preserved safety and evidence contracts, no regressions, improvement on a predeclared target metric, release authorization), rollout at mission boundaries with rollback, and the updater "has no permission to weaken its own acceptance predicate, commit gate, or lower-level safety interlocks". Verified: no experimental RSI results; their Sec 6.8 only defines how RSI would be evaluated. Banked as a design pattern, not evidence. Port move: invariant registry rows gain manifest/regression/rollback fields before any gate port, and the non-weakening rule becomes a ratchet-style pre-push check on the promote gate itself (the estate already machine-enforces exactly this shape via `tools/violation_ratchet.py check --changed`, per AGENTS.md Build discipline).

## Composite port (the whole steal in one move)

Wire the dormant seed gate to the live invariant path and grade it: `_compile_invariant`'s repeat branch (`mag/percolator.py:268-272`) routes repeats into a paired blind win-rate gate (`mag/seed_percolator.py:7-12` thresholds), acceptance decided on a frozen probe suite the proposer never sees scores from (REUSE decision-only + AIDE2 tier 1), one task family held fully out for never-reused generalization proof (AIDE2 tier 2), every promotion carries a manifest and rollback version with the updater barred from editing its own gate (RegenHarness), post-fix closure requires a fresh alternative-route attempt (Unearned Passes), and funded-tier grades are spent by EVSI-per-cost rank (PIVOT-KG). SEABench supplies the ledger rows and PIVOT-KG's Leduc numbers warn why the current count-only gate is unsound in principle, not merely unmeasured.

## Probe (one falsifiable probe, cheap this week)

Held-out reproduction of the top-count invariants. Draw the 20 highest-count rows from `memory/percolator/invariants.json` (top row count 232,214). For each, write 5 fresh probe prompts: new phrasings with zero token overlap with the sentences that percolated the invariant, half constructed to trigger it, half to not. Blind-grade whether the invariant's prescribed behavior actually fires (judge never sees counts; janitor tier with a 20-prompt funded-tier spot check). Prediction from the verified numbers (REUSE: up to 20.7% of naive accepted updates non-positive; SEABench: 316/720 vs 0/720 failures, evolved arm only): 1 to 5 of the 20 fail held-out. Falsifies both ways: 0 failures means the recurrence gate is empirically adequate and the port is unjustified for now; 6+ failures means the estate gate is worse than the published naive baseline and the REUSE-shaped predicate jumps the queue. Cost: about 200 judgments.
