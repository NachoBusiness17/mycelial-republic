## Find 1: Inference-Time Consensus for Mitigating Hidden Behaviors (arXiv 2607.23394v1)

**The steal as a mechanism.** Fine-tune one reference model per data source, then aggregate the references' next-token distributions at decode time. Four decoders, all verified on the paper page this session: token-wise minimum (cap each token at the lowest probability any reference assigns); base-relative minimum (revert to base-model probability when references move a token in opposing directions); quorum aggregation (support threshold: q-th largest probability); semantic smoothing (embedding-matched short continuations so shared intent expressed with different tokens supports one another). Cost is one forward pass per reference per generated token; semantic smoothing adds rollout and embedding calls. Verbatim, confirmed on the page: **"Consensus measures support, not correctness."**

**Why it lands here, with the baseline stated honestly.** The estate already holds the FIRST half of the task-output-space decode: after the consensus-illusion measurement (flock 0/3 correct at strength 0.60-0.67 while single-local went 3/3, memory/rib/RIB_MINDSWEAVE_EVAL_CONSENSUS_ILLUSION_20260810.md:14-25), a grounded-task raw-majority channel landed in commit 052732ebf dated 2026-08-14 (`git log -1 --format=... 052732ebf` this session), four days after the rib: mag/surrogate.py defines `raw_answer_consensus` "majority raw final answer (grounded-task consensus)" (:56) computed as majority of raw votes (:314-319, in-file comment "the fix for consensus illusion"), surfaced in mag/flock_steal.py:149-151 ("consensus-illusion fix"), and the current evaluator scores raw-answer agreement, not the surrogate verb (mag/mindweave_eval.py:152-157, "The surrogate-action verb is orthogonal to correctness - score raw"). What the paper adds is the SECOND half: probability-space aggregation (quorum over per-token distributions plus smoothing) as an upgrade over plain raw-majority. That comparison - does aggregation beat majority on grounded tasks - is open in the ribs: lens Q1 records the redesign as demanded and undesigned (watch/inbox/frontier_olm_20260928/lens/laws.md:68), and the ribs hold no majority-vs-aggregation measurement. What stays open per the find: the paper's benefits are synthetic-evaluated, so the correctness yield here is unproven until run.

## Find 2: LK Losses: Direct Acceptance Rate Optimization for Speculative Decoding (arXiv 2602.23881, ICML 2026)

**The steal as a mechanism.** Standard draft-model training minimizes KL divergence as a proxy; KL and acceptance rate share a global optimum, but capacity-limited small drafts settle off it. LK losses train the acceptance rate directly, add no inference overhead, and integrate into any speculator training framework; gains up to 8-10 percent in average acceptance length across 4 draft architectures and 6 target models spanning 8B to 685B (title, ICML 2026, mechanism and gain re-verified on the arxiv abs page this session; the find's quote "we propose LK losses, special training objectives that directly target acceptance rate" carried by the ask).
