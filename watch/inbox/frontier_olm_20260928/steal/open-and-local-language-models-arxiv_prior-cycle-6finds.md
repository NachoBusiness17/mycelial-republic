## The mechanism

Local-model failure is mostly harness failure, not capability failure. The field numbers say: fix the server side, cap and record the serving context, constrain the decode, keep the general rung at or above 3-bit quantization and 3 to 4B parameters, reserve sub-2B capacity for narrow fine-tuned specialists on fixed schemas, gate every pulled GGUF with an acceptance battery before trusting it, and run grading on a calibrated open-weight mid-size verifier under funded-tier supervision. Capability-threshold thinking about small models is the wrong frame; the harness around the model moves the score more than the parameter count does.
