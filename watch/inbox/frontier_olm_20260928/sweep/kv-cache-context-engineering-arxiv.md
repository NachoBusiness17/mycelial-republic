## Pass 2: deep reads (fetched and read; 7 full text end to end, 1 abs-only)

1. RR-Evict (2609.32278, 2026-09-26, cs.DC), full text. Names "recency synchronization": one agent turn refreshes all its cached prefix nodes together, so LRU concentrates eviction on a few idle agents' whole histories, and the returning agent recomputes nearly everything (TTFT outlier). Fix: round-robin tail-chunk (1,024-token) eviction across idle trajectories. SGLang, H100, Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B + Qwen3-8B, tau2-bank (222 trajectories) + SWE-bench traces. Numbers: P99 TTFT down up to 75.4%, P99 uncached prompt tokens down up to 65.7% vs LRU; up to 60.5%/56.2% vs LRU+completion signals on coding agents; PD-disaggregated 60.7%/60.8%. Analytic regime: useful when na < m < n (n trajectories, idle fraction s, capacity m in prefix-equivalents). Limitations: short idle intervals, GPU-HBM only, some burst misses remain.
2. EfficientAgent (2609.33762, 2026-09-27, cs.DC+LG), full text. KV offload pays only if the host tier holds the pool's "reuse working set" (stack-distance model, (A-1)*N*beta). SWE-bench Verified replay of 4,427 live calls (147.1M prompt tokens) via vLLM 0.13.0 + LMCache 0.3.12 on H20/RTX 3090/H800, Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B + Qwen2.5-Coder-32B. Working-set-sized tier: recomputed prompt tokens 76.7M to 5.3M (93.1% cut), makespan 209 to 128 min (38.7%); tier below working set: 79.2% of written chunks evicted unused; always-filtering writes at 40 GiB: 4.3x MORE recompute (+30.6% makespan). Offload NEVER pays on H800 (1.08-1.87x slower; compute-per-host-byte too high). 98.1% of prompt tokens were already processed in a prior call. CRITICAL for us: Appendix G measures context folding: history-rewriting condensers drop prefix hit rate from 96.5% to 50.8-60.85% because early edits rekey all later chunks (vLLM hashes the entire preceding context).
3. Contiguity, Not Importance (2609.17983, 2026-09-16, cs.AI), full text. Stale-cache repair after document edits: a local edit corrupts downstream KV under causal attention (stale-cache failure rate >= 98.8% on answer-relevant edits). Budgeted recomputation K=32: a CONTIGUOUS edit-local window recovers >= 0.94 of the post-edit answer margin; importance-based (attention/structural/CacheBlend-style) rivals recover 0.015-0.533; repair is 13-21x faster than full re-prefill (818.1 ms vs 39.6 ms at 8K context, Llama-3.1-8B). Adjacency is load-bearing: moving the answer-carrying text 250 tokens downstream collapses recovery to 0.01-0.09. Models: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3.
4. ContextEvo (2609.34649, 2026-09-28, cs.AI), full text. Learns a CONTEXT POLICY (input assembly, history maintenance, orchestration) from long-horizon trajectories by counterfactual replay: hold the execution prefix, edit only the model-visible context at one decision point, resample. DeepSeek-V4-Flash +3.1 to +4.4 pp across LHTB/DeepSWE/BrowseComp-Plus, beating frozen Codex/OpenCode/OpenClaw harnesses; cross-harness transfer +26.44 pp on OpenClaw. The memweave-relevant numbers: skill-only (prose injection) reward FELL 0.417 to 0.370 while tokens rose 8.57M to 9.61M, while the structured context policy hit 0.448 on FEWER tokens (7.52M); removing paginated trajectory review (structure) costs 12.15 pp. Policy transfers across models in-environment, NOT across environments.
5. Adaptive Consistency Graph (2609.32754, 2026-09-26, cs.AI+CL), full text. Structure-not-prose, measured: execution evidence + provenance in a persistent graph; per-decision a temporary requirement-centered view (TopK=8, 5,000-token cap) is rendered read-only. GPT-5.6-luna average success 44.5% (ReAct) to 50.2%; BrowseComp-Plus 62.4 to 73.5; failure-mode deltas: repetition 8.2% vs TDP* 96.4%, missing deliverables 0.9% vs ReAct 26.2%. Effect is model-dependent (DeepSeek-v4-flash 45.9 vs 43.8, small).
6. When Fancy Eviction Fails (2609.28870, 2026-09-24, cs.DC+LG), full text. Production traces (FreeInference 327.5K requests/10.5B tokens/7d; Chutes 515.8K/9.7B/130.9d; AgentX = 393 Claude Code sessions). 14 eviction algorithms: sophisticated policies (ARC, LHD, LeCaR, 3LCache, LRB, Workload-aware, AsymCache) roughly equal or worse than LRU; LFU/W-TinyLFU collapse; Belady still has headroom. The mechanism of prefix reuse: median intra-session interval 8.2 s; 99.7% of reuse gaps under 22 minutes; block lifetimes under 30 min; top 10% of sessions hold 76.2% of KV bytes. Recommendation: keep recency as foundation, add partial-node compute-aware eviction only where recompute costs vary; that cuts mean TTFT 19.9% and lifts prefill throughput 18.8% at 48 GiB.
7. Harness Tokenomics (2609.28919, 2026-09-24 v1 / 09-26 v2, cs.AI+CR), full text. Cache-safe routing doctrine: the prompt cache is scoped to one model, so mid-task model switches force a cold write plus a rewrite on return; route only at session start, in side lanes (seeded by a ~20k-token project ledger, not the transcript), and at subagent launch (fresh conversations are cache-free); one empty request refreshes the home cache after a side lane. 13-21% of model spend recoverable in a 10k-seat emulation ($3.3M-$5.1M/yr); crossover: above ~71k context at 18 tool steps/turn the priciest model is cheapest; top 1% of sessions carry 31-53% of spend; savings collapse to ~9% on pause-heavy sessions unless a 1-hour cache TTL exists (15.5%).
8. KVSET (2609.27746, 2026-09-23, cs.DC), ABS ONLY (both HTML doors 404). Defines the "KV cache working set": the minimum cache capacity required to achieve a target hit rate; estimates it online via Mattson stack distances over prefix pages (one pass serves all candidate capacities), validated against production traces. This is the measurement instrument our memory/working.md:217 P1 task ("measure local KV-cache hit-rate") lacks.

Also fetched, abs only: Faithfulness Is Not Free (2608.30996, 2026-08-31): Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, INT8 KV near-lossless; INT4 KV: over 90% of faithfulness changes are NEGATIVE while accuracy metrics stay blind to it; harm grows with noisy retrieval and more chunks. "Faithfulness must be audited before compressed caches are deployed."

## Answers to the five research questions

RQ1 (prompt-cache patterns beyond the playbook, measured hit rates). The playbook (corpus/steal/ZCODE_OPERATING_PLAYBOOK_20260925.md:10-37) holds byte-identical ask keying, first-write-closes-cache, compact cliff; mag/context_pack.py:470-556 already embodies frozen-prefix + evolving tail. What is NEW in the literature beyond that: (a) eviction policy for agent loops (RR-Evict: recency synchronization causes whole-history evictions; round-robin tail chunks cut P99 TTFT 75.4%); (b) the recency-foundation law from production traces (sophisticated eviction adds nothing; reuse gaps are minutes-scale); (c) working-set capacity planning (KVSET's Mattson-stack minimum capacity for a target hit rate, and EfficientAgent's stack-distance host-tier sizing: right-sized tier = 93% fewer recomputed tokens, undersized tier = 79.2% of offloaded chunks wasted); (d) cache-safe routing (Harness Tokenomics: route only at cache-free boundaries, side lanes seeded by a ledger not the transcript, empty-request home-cache refresh, 13-21% spend). Measured hit-rate numbers to calibrate against: 98.1% of SWE-agent prompt tokens already processed in a prior call (EfficientAgent); emulated 95.2-98.0% cache-read share (Harness Tokenomics); top 10% of sessions hold 76.2% of KV bytes (Fancy Eviction).

RQ2 (structure-not-prose beats prose injection?). Two in-window measured results support the memweave doctrine, neither from a paper that names it: ContextEvo measured prose-skill injection REDUCING reward (0.417 to 0.370) while RAISING tokens (8.57M to 9.61M), and structure (paginated review + policy views) winning on fewer tokens; ACG measured a persistent provenance graph with bounded per-decision views lifting GPT-5.6-luna 44.5% to 50.2% and cutting repetition failures from 96.4% to 8.2% vs TDP*. Neither paper isolates "structure vs prose" as a single variable with cache behavior as the dependent measure. Estate-side replication experiment: on gemma:2b + gemma4 via llama.cpp, run the same 20-prompt scut battery (the abliterate-p1 battery is the precedent) with three arms holding content constant: (1) prose paragraphs, (2) memlang structures (tables/blocks), (3) structures + frozen prefix (context_pack.py freeze). Measure: llama.cpp cache_tokens reuse per follow-up (byte-identical prefix arm should hit ~100%, re-rendered arm ~0), answer fidelity vs the named battery, and tokens-to-correct-answer. Prediction from ACG/ContextEvo: arm 2-3 win on fidelity per token; the cache deltas are the new measurement the literature has not published for small local models.

RQ3 (KV quant/offload state for llama.cpp/Ollama-class 2B-8B serving; janitor choice/context budget). HONEST FINDING: the last-30-day arXiv window contains NO paper specifically on llama.cpp or Ollama KV quantization/offload; the quant/offload literature this month is GPU-serving-tier (vLLM/SGLang, H100/H20/RTX 3090, MoE 30B-class). What transfers: (a) Faithfulness Is Not Free: INT8 KV near-lossless, INT4 KV silently breaks answer grounding (over 90% of faithfulness changes negative, accuracy blind) on Qwen2.5-7B RAG, so the janitor configs should treat q8_0 KV cache as the aggressive floor and audit grounding before any q4 cache-type; (b) EfficientAgent's working-set law governs ANY offload tier (the estate has no host-tier offload for janitors today; the law says size it to the concurrent-agent reuse working set or skip it); (c) the digest-duped SoK (2609.30854) gives the three-regime bandwidth structure for placement choices. Janitor model choice: unchanged by this window's evidence; context budget: no published result licenses stretching 2B-class KV beyond its tested envelope; the INT4 audit argues the opposite of stretching via 4-bit KV.

RQ4 (mechanism for KV/ram-cache drift; closing the rib). The rib (memory/rib/RIB_RAM_CACHE_STALE_SNAPSHOT_20260825.md) observed: a module-level cache serves a frozen snapshot; new disk writes are invisible until restart; mtime-fingerprint auto-invalidation fixed it. Published causes now on record, citeable: (1) Contiguity, Not Importance: under causal self-attention ANY local edit corrupts all downstream KV entries (stale-cache failure >= 98.8% on answer-relevant edits), and the repair operator must be contiguous-from-the-edit-point (0.94 margin at K=32, 13-21x cheaper than re-prefill, but collapses if the dependent text moved 250+ tokens). The rib's mtime fingerprint is exactly the edit detector this paper assumes; the paper adds the missing half: the invalidation should reload a CONTIGUOUS window from the change point, not just flip a stale flag to full-reload. (2) EfficientAgent Appendix G: history-rewriting compaction drops prefix hit rate 96.5% to 50.8-60.85% because a rewrite rekeys every later chunk; this is the same drift class one level up (KV cache vs our ram snapshot), and it prices the estate's compaction-amend loop: amend-in-place preserves the cache; rewrite-amend silently reprices the whole tail. (3) RR-Evict's recency synchronization explains the cross-session shape of the drift (whole histories evicted together under LRU). Proposed rib closure text: ram-cache drift = stale-snapshot under causal dependency + rekey-on-rewrite; cite 2609.17983 and 2609.33762 App G.

RQ5 (context-window engineering at 100k-plus scale; compaction-amend transfer). The in-window cohort (ContextEvo, ICLR-forget, StateComp, DRSR, PaMER, evidence-preserving compression) converges on: treat the context policy as a first-class learned/edited object, decide compression per-decision (not per-window), and keep provenance so evidence can be re-surfaced. ContextEvo's counterfactual replay (hold execution prefix, edit ONLY the model-visible context at one decision point, resample up to 5) is a directly stealable evaluation for the estate's fold gate: it converts "did the fold help" from a vibe into a measured next-action delta. Its transfer caveat is useful law-shaping: learned context policies transferred across models within an environment but NOT across environments, so an estate-folded context policy should be gated per environment class (repo vs browse vs ops). The 100k+ workloads measured: DeepSWE median ~124k trace tokens, 103.5 turns, 120.5 tool calls (ContextEvo); contexts capped 262,144 with 32,768 output. What transfers to the compaction-amend loop: (a) byte-preserving amend over rewrite (EfficientAgent App G cache math), (b) contiguous edit-local repair instead of full re-prefill after any amended section (Contiguity), (c) decision-time bounded views (5,000-token cap) rendered from a persistent graph instead of dragging full history (ACG), (d) per-decision compress gating learned from replay (ContextEvo), (e) recency-foundation retention with partial-node eviction for the serving layer underneath (Fancy Eviction, RR-Evict).
