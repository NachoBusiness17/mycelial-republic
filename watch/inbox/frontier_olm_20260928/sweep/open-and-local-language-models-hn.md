## Findings by research question

### RQ1: open-weight beaters of gemma-class 2B janitors on agentic tool-calling, equal or smaller VRAM

- Cactus Needle 3 (cactuscompute.com/needle, HN 2026-09-18, 236 pts / 93 c): 29-121M param ladder model, 2-bit CQ2, ships as 8-29MB binaries, decode 400-4k tok/s and prefill 1-10k tok/s on a Raspberry Pi 5. Tool calls + structured JSON extraction + embeddings; empty list instead of a guess when no tool fits; calibrated confidence head with a 0.1 floor and suppressed_calls. Vendor benchmark (BFCL v4, Mobile Actions, DroidCall): Mobile Actions 86.0 through the shipped 2-bit binary vs LFM2.5 1.2B 82.4, Qwen3.5 0.8B 76.0, Apple on-device 57.6 at f16.
- BUT the thread is the signal: the "matches DeepSeek V4 Flash" claim holds only on narrow exact-call suites after fine-tuning (founder: "stress on narrow task"; DroidCall fine-tune lifts subnetworks 18-36 points). IanCal and others got nonsense on open-ended phrasing. Scaevolus's real 230-case fine-tune head-to-head: FunctionGemma fine-tune BF16 209/230 (90.9%) correct tool shape / 196/230 (85.2%) exact arguments, vs Needle 3 fine-tuned W4A8 74/230 (32.2%) / 47/230 (20.4%). So a Gemma-family function-calling fine-tune beat the new tiny architecture 3x on a real workload. "FunctionGemma" has zero hits anywhere in catalog or memory: new to the estate.
- Independent tool-calling bench in the same thread (viccis, 36 scenarios, 16GB Mac Mini / 3080): "Qwen3.8 on my local machine does pretty poorly even relative to Gemma4 E4B (~9.6gb)" - a counter-intuitive local datapoint that our gemma4 short worker may already be the right janitor class; Needle failed number conversion, never declined with a full toolset, and broke on follow-ups.
- Quesma Qwen3.8 27B quant ladder (quesma.com, HN 2026-09-09, 286 pts / 139 c): BF16 55GB; Q4_K_M 17GB matches full model on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (89 agentic tasks, 3h timeout, 98k ctx) on a 24GB card; UD-Q2_K_XL 10.7GB falls to "the level of Opus 4.7 or Gemini 3.1 Pro" on the same bench while taking the same turns and ~25% more tokens; 1-bit UD-IQ1_S 6.2GB collapses to random chance on GPQA and longer reasoning makes it worse. Method burned ~$3k on Modal GPUs, llama.cpp build from 2026-08-16 required. Thread adds: KV-cache quant to q4 costs almost nothing on this model (arxiv 2609.04098 cited); NVFP4 weights+cache on one RTX 5090 gives 264k ctx at 200 tok/s single stream.
- Estate read: the VRAM floor for frontier-ish agentic quality is now ~10.7GB (27B Q2), not 2B-class; the true sub-2B janitor beaters are narrow function-callers (Needle family, FunctionGemma), not general chat models. MiMo-V2.6 Pro (Raschka 2026-09-22) is the new open-weight benchmark No.1 (GQA + 128-token SWA; agent-task-heavy post-training; DeepSWE held-out harness pass@1 50->66%) but no size/VRAM was stated in the note, and the estate already holds an intake card (watch/inbox/MIMO_FREE_WINDOW_20260922.md in catalog).

### RQ3: any free/open rung as a verification surface under the driving ladder?

- arxiv 2609.20824 "Do small language models know what they don't know?" (submitted 2026-07-21, HN 2026-09-22): in 91% of dataset-model combinations, sub-3B models' mean token entropy is near zero regardless of correctness, so token-level confidence is unusable at janitor scale; semantic entropy (multi-sample + cluster by meaning) recovers a usable signal; routing uncertain queries to a bigger expert yields up to +50pp accuracy; cross-family routing averages +22.0% vs +6.8% same-family. This is published support for the estate's sensing-only rule: small free lanes cannot self-certify, but a cheap local multi-sample confidence check plus escalation is the sanctioned shape.
- agent-engineering.ch routing math (2026-08-15, HN 2026-09-26; caveat: byline says AI-generated, human-reviewed): LangChain + NVIDIA Switchyard on 145 multi-step tasks: only 7% of turns required Claude Opus 4.8, a 30B model carried 93%, 74% cost cut for ~6 points accuracy; the 6 points are router mis-calls, cluster them before paying the frontier tier. Also: judge must be a different family than the judged model. This matches the driving ladder's shape (cheap executor tier + scarce judgment tier + cross-family verification) with public numbers.
- No in-window publication claims a free or open rung as a reliable verification surface; the closest artifacts are confidence-gated narrow callers (Needle) and semantic-entropy sampling. Driving-ladder sensing-only rule stands; published consensus does not contradict it.
