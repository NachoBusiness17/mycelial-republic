--- [/u/Several_Guide_2274] /u/Several_Guide_2274 on Ctx size in Ollama Vs llama.cpp
Ollama probably defaults to a different cache quantization or offload strategy than whatever llama.cpp build you're using. 260k on the same hardware means it's spilling to CPU or using q4 cache by default, while your llama.cpp setup might be trying to keep everything in VRAM with higher precision. Check your n_gpu_layers and cache type flags.

--- [/u/mineshop] /u/mineshop on Ctx size in Ollama Vs llama.cpp
That 10GB is the KV cache for the 260k context — it scales with context length, not model size. If you rarely need the full window, lowering num_ctx is the direct fix; OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0 halves the cache bytes at some quality cost.
