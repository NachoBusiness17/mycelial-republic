### 2. Do small language models know what they don't know?
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20824 (HN 49801865, 2026-09-22; paper submitted 2026-07-21)
- Quote: "token-level entropy is effectively blind in SLMs: in 91% of dataset-model combinations, mean token entropy is near zero regardless of answer correctness, rendering token-based confidence signals unusable at this scale."
- Facts: seven approaches tested on 7 model pairs under 3B parameters, 5 NLU benchmarks, consumer hardware; semantic entropy (multi-sample, cluster answers by meaning) recovers a usable confidence signal; semantic-entropy routing of uncertain queries to a larger expert yields up to +50pp accuracy; cross-family routing (SmolLM 360M -> Phi-3.5-mini) averages +22.0% vs +6.8% same-family: expert quality dominates family match.
