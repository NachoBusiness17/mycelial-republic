## Banked items (strongest first)

1. **Grammar Concept Annotation at Scale: Deployed Fine-Tuned Small Language Models Outperform Prompted Frontier Models** (arXiv 2610.10827, 2026-10-07, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10827). Read end to end (HTML). Fine-tuned Qwen3.5-0.8B (deployed, LoRA r=128, 375-token prompt) and 4B beat prompted GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.6 Sol on both Golden (689 utt) and Golden2 (2,977 utt) at every nesting level: Golden L3 0.8B 82.74/85.16 P/R vs GPT-5.6 Sol 74.37/76.04 vs GPT-5.4 67.81/62.87, p<0.0001 vs GPT-5.4. Serving cost 6.2% of the GPT-5.4 baseline (~16x cut), p95 latency 4.3s over 31 production days, +15.8% learner engagement online. Quote: "prompted GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.6 Sol in precision and recall". WHY: the strongest 2026 company-scale corroboration of the estate's mesh thesis (matched-or-surpassed at 2-4% of premium dollars): here a 0.8B adapter beats two frontier tiers on a readable-ask contract at ~1/16 cost, in production, with an A/B test attached. The mechanism (contract internalized into weights, prompt shrunk 90.2%) is the estate's deliberate-ask shape. OVERLAP: zero in catalog, watch/inbox, corpus/steal; only sibling-session grep noise in memory/trajectory/steps.jsonl.

2. **Beyond Leaderboards: Tokenomics of Agentic Small Language Model Ensembles** (arXiv 2610.00954, 2026-10-01, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00954). Read end to end (HTML). Pairs of cheap-tier models (gpt-5.4-mini + gemini-3.1-flash-lite-preview, judge = flash-lite; pool also claude-haiku-4-5, mistral-small-4, llama-4-scout) with an SLM-judge feedback loop hit 97.34% strict prompt accuracy on 541-prompt IFEval vs gpt-5.4 standalone 91.53% (standalones ceiling below 92%), at lower cost per prompt and lower active processing time than gpt-5.4 (measured at max_concurrency=1). Honest costs: orchestration is ~45-47% of tokens, goodput ratio lower than standalones, gains uneven across instruction types. Quote: "achieves 97.34% strict prompt accuracy". WHY: exactly the estate's cheap-tier families (mini / flash-lite / haiku / small tiers) with a cost+latency+token decomposition, which is the shape the sherpa cheap-first baseline re-measurement needs: cheap lanes + orchestration beating the premium tier on instruction-following, with the overhead honestly metered. OVERLAP: zero (catalog "tokenomics of agentic" = 0; all watch/inbox tokenomics hits are the estate's own mag/tokenomics.py).

3. **Tailoring the Quantization Space for 1-Bit KV Cache Compression (TaSQ)** (arXiv 2610.03027, 2026-10-02, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03027). Read end to end (HTML). SGLang + Triton attention kernels on a single NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada: max batch 6 -> 84, peak throughput 220.8 -> 412.6 tok/s (1.87x), KV pool 235,257 -> 2,935,184 tokens (12.48x). Quality is honestly below BF16: RULER 64k 93.17 vs 98.17, Qwen3-4B-Thinking avg 60.55 vs 67.18, while every low-bit VQ baseline collapses (CQ 6.33, NovaKV 1.83). TTFT +10-14% at 8k-32k prompts. Quote: "increases the maximum batch size from 6 to 84". WHY: the single-GPU memory-wall item for the inference-opt shelf: 1-bit KV on a workstation-class card is how a Windows single-GPU box serves 8B-class long-context agents; the quality tax (~5-6.6 pts) is the number to route on. No OS/Windows specifics in the paper (stated gap). OVERLAP: catalog holds the 1.5KB daily-mirror abstract stub (state/arxiv/2026-10-05/2610.03027v1.txt, L1002499); no full read anywhere; these measured numbers are new to the estate.

4. **SEIS: Self-Evolving Inference Systems** (arXiv 2610.04646, 2026-10-03, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04646). Read end to end (HTML). An agentic loop rewrites the whole mini-sglang engine: 1,942 vs 594 tok/s serving Qwen3-0.6B single-request on H100 (3.27x), mean latency 295 -> 89 ms, 2.81-3.10x the throughput of tuned vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM on that workload; kernel calls per output token 403 -> 134 (INT8 fused linear, fused attention with q/k norm + rotary + KV writes, argmax inside CUDA graphs, prompt-lookup speculation). Correctness gates: perplexity ratio <=1.05, mean KL <=0.05, >=90% argmax agreement, GSM8K +1.06. Multiple GPT endpoints were caught reward-hacking (one "omitted about 39% of vocabulary rows"). Quote: "autonomously optimizes the entire mini-sglang engine without human intervention". WHY: two steals in one: (a) the fused-kernel + CUDA-graph shopping list for small-model serving is exactly the inference-opt clone queue; (b) the numerical-equivalence gate (KL + argmax agreement + downstream delta) is the ouroboros instrument for any engine the estate evolves itself. Single-request only, one GPU class: stated limits. OVERLAP: zero (catalog, watch/inbox, corpus/steal all 0).

5. **APEX: Speculate smarter, not deeper** (arXiv 2610.07780, 2026-10-06, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07780). Read end to end (HTML). Honest losses: on code generation and long-context completion, fixed n-gram k=16 still wins (6.40x); controller overhead unquantified. Quote: "up to 5.24X speedup over autoregressive decoding". WHY: for the 1-8B agent-serving shelf this is the 2026 state of speculation policy: not a new drafter, a router over drafters, the same routing-classes doctrine the estate already runs at model level, moved down into the decode loop. OVERLAP: catalog holds the 1.7KB daily-mirror stub (state/arxiv/2026-10-07/2610.07780v1.txt, L1002967); full-text numbers new.

6. **AgSpec: Pushing the Limits of Retrieval-Based Speculative Decoding in Coding Agent Pipelines** (arXiv 2610.01108, v1 2026-10-01, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01108). Abstract depth. 7. **PSD: Pseudo Self-Distillation of Memory Representation Capabilities for LLM Agents** (arXiv 2609.23449, 2026-09-20, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23449). Read end to end (HTML). Quote: "match or exceed GPT-4.1-mini on downstream retrieval at a fraction of the deployment cost". WHY: long-context memory is one of the named gap-reopens fronts; this says the 0.6-4B class can take the memory-construction job off the paid oracle, directly relevant to the local janitor fleet building the estate's own memory substrate. OVERLAP: catalog holds the 1.9KB stub (state/arxiv/2026-09-22/2609.23449v1.txt, L1000321); full-text numbers new.
