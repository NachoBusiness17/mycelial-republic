Evidence and Experimental Design for Sovereign
Agent Systems
Abstract
A1 — A locally operated agent organization is a testable systems proposal; its end-to-end
autonomy is Unproven. Confidence: high in this evidence classification. This critical review
examines six components: autonomous orchestration, model routing, failure observability, durable
recovery, organizational memory and training-data attribution. Primary literature supports several
constituent mechanisms, including feedback-conditioned agent behavior, cost-sensitive routing,
destination-side duplicate suppression, content-addressed storage and predictive retrieval caching. It
does not establish their composition as reliable unattended operation. The principal research
opportunity is a reproducible integration with explicit authority, recovery and evidence boundaries.
Broad architectural novelty and training attribution from output alone are not supported. A local case
study supplies useful implementation findings but no completed confirmatory experiment. The proposed
research program therefore separates correctness, resource feasibility, causal performance
improvement and provenance. Evidence that changes this conclusion: independently verified
execution of the registered component studies and recovery controls, within a declared deployment
envelope.
Scope and evidence method
0.1 — This is a critical integrative review, not an exhaustive systematic review or a completed
experimental paper. Supported; confidence high. The evidence base comprises 27 public primary
sources: research papers, original systems reports, official implementation documentation and a
statistical reference. Targeted searches covered agent reflection and failure taxonomies; routing; durable
execution, duplicate suppression and fencing; capability security; observability; memory hierarchy and
retrieval caching; canary extraction; and multiagent debate. Citation tracing followed the proposal's
references and the closest contrary mechanisms. Central methodological sections were inspected where
available; a listed research paper is not automatically treated as peer reviewed. The source inventory
records versions, locators, publication status and applicability limits. Literature inspection was
principally completed on September 11, 2026; selected chronology and product metadata were
rechecked on September 16 UTC. No search-completeness percentage, pooled effect size or
publication-bias estimate is claimed. Change criterion: a reproducible broader search or inaccessible
primary evidence can revise coverage and conclusions.
0.2 — Three evidence levels must remain separate. Supported methodological distinction;
confidence high. “Supported” means supported within the stated source or observation scope; it does
not certify the complete fleet. “Disputed” means a counterexample, incompatible evidence or an invalid
inference defeats the stated formulation. “Unproven” means missing or inconclusive evidence.
Confidence is an editorial assessment of the judgment, not an estimated probability of future success.
Public literature, private case observations and proposed engineering choices are identified separately.
Each numbered claim includes evidence that would change its verdict. Every proposed addition is
marked NEW SPECULATION; the phrase identifies a design hypothesis, not an allegation that the
underlying scientific principle is novel.

1 Autonomous orchestration and inherited lessons
1.1 — Feedback-conditioned agent improvement has prior support; automatic beneficial
evolution does not follow. Supported mechanism, Unproven fleet gain; confidence high. Reflexion
stores linguistic feedback in memory and conditions subsequent attempts on it, without requiring weight
updates. Its results and ablations support the usefulness of particular feedback and memory
arrangements. Its limitations include local optima and difficulty constructing reliable tests for impure or
concurrent programs. The fleet's folded “genetics” in M:32,41,67 belongs to this broad family; extracting
a lesson is not itself evidence that the next agent improves. A lesson can preserve an erroneous diagnosis,
overfit a task or introduce an unsafe instruction.1 Change criterion: held-out, paired task outcomes
show that the inherited content adds value beyond extra context, extra attempts and evaluator leakage.
1.2 — The self-correction literature is conditional and contested. Disputed as either a universal
benefit or a universal impossibility; confidence high. Huang and colleagues find that tested intrinsic
reasoning correction can fail or degrade accuracy without external feedback. Liu and colleagues report
countervailing results with neutral prompts and zero-temperature decoding. The studies differ in
conditions and should not be collapsed into a single universal rule. Neither establishes reliable
long-horizon institutional learning across arbitrary tasks.2, 3 Change criterion: a preregistered
comparison using the fleet's actual models, tasks, prompt construction and feedback channels supports a
bounded conclusion. A title-level citation is insufficient to settle the disagreement.
1.3 — Orchestration introduces its own failure surface. Supported concern, Unproven local rate;
confidence high. The MAST study organizes multiagent failures into specification/system design,
inter-agent coordination and verification/termination categories. This establishes reasons to inspect
interaction traces rather than score model answers alone; its observed frequencies cannot be transferred
directly to a different fleet.4 A local two-failure rule can be bypassed if a fold, replacement process or
fresh request identifier resets the counter. Spawn ceilings can likewise fail when checked independently
by concurrent parents. These are state-management hypotheses, not demonstrated production incidents
in this review. Change criterion: independent admission and receiver traces show persistent operation
budgets, atomic global admission and monotone authority across replacement.
1.4 — A learning gate should separate learning value from permission to deploy. Unproven
proposal; confidence medium. NEW SPECULATION. Retain three arms: frozen parent, candidate
lessons and token-length-matched irrelevant lessons. Use held-out tasks, equal opportunity budgets and
a frozen scorer; preserve failures and all fold/evaluation costs. Candidate prompts must pass a separate
authority and injection gate before promotion. The inherited 768-episode plan is a candidate sample
budget, not a literature-derived minimum or a power calculation. At the September 20 review, a paired

2 Routing resource allocation and authority
2.1 — Learned routing is established as a method, while fleet economics remain empirical.
Supported method, Unproven transfer; confidence high. RouteLLM learns strong/weak model
routing from preference information and evaluates cost-quality tradeoffs. Its limitations explicitly
recognize shifts between benchmark and application distributions. A six-class registry and classifier, as
proposed in M:43, therefore enter an existing research area; their value depends on local calibration,
eligibility and accounting.5 A rarely used specialist may be fully healthy. Conversely, recent usage does
not prove readiness. Quota exhaustion, missing credentials, capability mismatch, cold-start delay and
deliberate idleness should not share one STALLED label. Change criterion: a frozen in-domain workload
shows lower total measured cost at a declared quality constraint, including router overhead and failed
assignments.
2.2 — Resource sharing is a hierarchy of separate capacities. Supported distinction; confidence
high. Official Ollama documentation distinguishes concurrent loaded models from parallel requests to
one model; context length and request parallelism affect memory demand.6 Shared model weights can
reduce duplication while per-request state and memory bandwidth still limit service. In L1, the host
exposed 63.951 GiB physical RAM, while the Docker VM exposed 31.319 GiB. A simultaneous 40 GiB
resident in-cage worker set could not fit that observed VM before existing services. A 40 GiB ceiling alone
does not imply that demand, however. Eight logical executors are not equivalent to eight model copies or
eight simultaneous generations. Change criterion: a new frozen configuration and workload trace
establish the permitted counts, peak physical/committed memory, paging and observer survival.
2.3 — Scoped API credentials alone do not establish child-authority containment. Supported
principle, Unproven implementation; confidence high. Capsicum demonstrates kernel-enforced
restriction of ambient authority and attenuation of object rights. Its FreeBSD implementation is not
evidence of equivalent protection on Windows or WSL2. AgentDojo supplies a relevant adversarial
evaluation pattern: untrusted material enters a tool-using agent's context while the agent retains
legitimate work to perform.7, 8 Applied to this fleet, a child must be unable to regain forbidden access
through inherited handles, shell execution, alternate tools or an unmediated filesystem path. Prompt
instructions expressing child≤parent are not the enforcement boundary. Change criterion: adversarial
child processes and retrieved instructions fail to exceed receiver-enforced rights while authorized tasks
remain usable.

cash-saving percentage is undefined; use a declared alternative such as metered energy or occupied

3 Failure observability and measurement validity
3.1 — Typed return rows are necessary accounting aids but incomplete outcome evidence.
Supported distinction; confidence high. Google's SRE guidance separates internal instrumentation
from externally observed behavior and explicitly recognizes wrong content returned with HTTP 200 as
an error. It also emphasizes latency, traffic, errors and saturation, including tail behavior.9 A semantic
health endpoint therefore needs task-specific postconditions and a missing-data policy. Its own process
can fail while a healthy-looking last value persists. The proposed independent observer should reconcile
admitted intents, observed dispatches, receiver effects and terminal dispositions without relying
exclusively on the emitter it audits. Change criterion: wrong-artifact, missing-row, duplicate-row and
observer-loss injections are detected from independent evidence within the declared deadlines.
3.2 — The local failure count illustrates a measurement-grain error. Supported local observation;
confidence high. L1's frozen 27,934,077-byte ledger prefix contains 24,854 records whose JSON
signature exactly equals run_shell:c73866f2f1b5. Another 153 records mention that signature elsewhere,
yielding 25,007 matching lines. The matched records lack explicit attempt identifiers; neither matching
lines nor unique record hashes prove distinct executions. Ten malformed lines remain separately
reported. The earlier repair receipt refers to a different byte count, so it cannot silently substitute for this
snapshot. These findings support the existence of a persistent diagnostic signature, not a measured count
of exactly 25,007 executions. Change criterion: an immutable attempt-linked query reconciles dispatch
identity and preserves exclusions. The frozen query and digest belong with the claim.
3.3 — The appropriate middle-test endpoint is safe disposition rather than compulsory repair.
Unproven proposal; confidence high in relevance. NEW SPECULATION. Inject a tool success
envelope with a wrong artifact, suppress its completion event and duplicate an unrelated event while
other tools succeed. A passing observer must detect both loss and duplication; net-zero count error is not
reconciliation. A correct response may be APPLIED, final nonapplication or an explicit UNKNOWN hold.
The existing five-second detection, 30-second UNKNOWN and 60-second post-fault reconciliation
thresholds are fixture acceptance choices, not universal service requirements. At September 20, compare
protected monotonic-clock records with exact injection times and deadlines. Change criterion: a
realistic workload or fault model requires different thresholds before seal; false completion or an unsafe
redispatch disputes the tested build immediately.

4 Durable execution and effect reconciliation
4.1 — Cryptographic history and durable execution solve different problems. Disputed
equivalence; confidence high. Temporal's architecture separates deterministic workflow decisions
from side-effecting activities and requires activities to be idempotent or non-retryable. Replay works
within that execution protocol; append-only history alone is not the protocol.10 Hash linkage can make
alterations detectable relative to a trusted commitment. It cannot reveal an effect whose
acknowledgment was lost, recover absent object bytes or decide whether a delayed writer will still
commit. The retracted “90% of Temporal” wording in M:13 had no defensible quantitative denominator.
Change criterion: specify the event model, nondeterministic inputs, code-version compatibility and
receiver guarantees, then demonstrate the declared subset under faults.
4.2 — Exactly-once effects are conditionally achievable, not supplied by a caller-side receipt.
Supported; confidence high. RIFL couples durable completion records with the operation's effects and
makes retries meet those records. It also addresses record reclamation and returning clients. This refutes
both the overclaim that a local replay loop automatically produces exactly-once effects and the opposite
overclaim that exactly-once semantics are universally impossible. Its assumptions concern cooperating
infrastructure; its performance cannot be transferred to arbitrary HTTP tools.11 In L1, /run_task
dispatches directly to tools, while the named broker saves its receipt after effect execution. The
integration gap survives a correct-looking broker module elsewhere. Change criterion: all admitted
mutation paths reach a cooperating receiver that commits effect and dedup state atomically.
4.3 — A dead-worker judgment does not fence its outstanding writes. Supported; confidence high.
Chubby's sequencers address delayed requests from former lock holders by making recipients check lock
generations. Lock-delay fallback is not a complete equivalent.12 A lease timeout, heartbeat or stale
lock-file age therefore cannot by itself authorize an overlapping new writer. The same distinction applies
on one machine whenever asynchronous requests, suspended processes or open file handles outlive the
controller's judgment. Change criterion: suspend owner A, authorize and commit owner B, then resume
A; the receiving resource rejects A's mutation. The authority assigning generations and the storage
retaining them must also be specified.
4.4 — A transactional store closes only the transaction it actually owns. Supported; confidence
high. SQLite's atomic-commit account depends on explicit storage, locking and flush assumptions. Its
multi-file discussion concerns participating database files, not arbitrary filesystem mutations or remote
effects; the cited account is principally the rollback-journal design, not a blanket guarantee for all WAL
configurations.13 A database row recording a separately executed file write leaves an ambiguity gap. The
proposed local contract can instead make logical documents and ledger events canonical in one store and
materialize plain files separately. That changes authority semantics and must be declared, not hidden
behind the word “receipt.” Change criterion: either the effect participates in the same transaction or a
separately tested receiving-resource protocol supplies equivalent invariants.
4.5 — UNKNOWN requires durable finality rules and a restoration horizon. Unproven
implementation proposal; confidence high. NEW SPECULATION. Persist one immutable intent, a
stable admission identity, separate attempt identities and a receiver-checked epoch. Reusing an
operation ID with different intent is a conflict. A status query must return APPLIED with
operation-bound evidence, NOT_APPLIED_FINAL with an effective cancellation/fence barrier, or
UNKNOWN. Mere absence or matching desired bytes without provenance is insufficient. Retain
deduplication knowledge through the replay/backup horizon, or reject expired client generations after
reclamation; restoring an old controller must not resurrect a completed effect. At September 20, fault

5 Organizational memory and architectural novelty
5.1 — Shared memory-hierarchy ideas do not establish architectural identity with Engram.
Disputed identity claim; confidence high. Engram maps token n-grams into learned tables and fuses
retrieved vectors with contextual hidden states. Its deterministic keys enable advance host-memory
fetching. A corpus service instead maps document or chunk identities into external representations that
an agent retrieves. The key domain, learned state, update semantics and fusion mechanism differ.
Engram v1 was published January 12, 2026, before the reported July–August private fleet records;
September product shipment is not the earliest public comparison date.15 Chronology alone neither
proves copying nor disproves independent development. Change criterion: authenticated earlier
artifacts change chronology; an explicit mechanism mapping changes the architectural comparison. Local
timestamps alone do not settle priority.
5.2 — External knowledge caching and predictive personal-memory caching have substantial
prior art. Disputed broad novelty; confidence high. RAGCache caches intermediate states of retrieved
documents, accounting for their prefix context across GPU and host memory. TeleRAG predicts vector
clusters needed at subsequent RAG stages and overlaps their movement with generation. PerCache
predicts future queries from personal knowledge and query history to populate a hierarchy of cached
intermediates and answers.16, 17, 18 These are external-information mechanisms, not merely offloaded
model weights. Their benchmark improvements are workload-specific. The PerCache metadata presents
a submission-date/identifier discrepancy; this review relies on the inspected mechanism and does not
claim an exact first-publication day. Change criterion: a direct feature comparison establishes a
narrower residual contribution; a search with no matching title is not enough.
5.3 — Content identity does not supply semantic retrieval, freshness or authority. Supported
distinction; confidence high. Venti is early prior for immutable content-addressed blocks, duplicate
coalescing and reconstruction of a lookup index from stored data. Its historical hash choice is not a
current security recommendation. Zanzibar separately addresses consistency between permission

6 Canary attribution and controlled extraction
6.1 — Reproduction is not, by itself, a training-mechanism identifier. Supported distinction;
confidence high. Zhang and colleagues analyze why ordinary membership inference cannot generally
establish sound training-data proofs for opaque production models, while identifying random canaries,
watermarking and extraction as conditional alternatives. Their account also distinguishes controlled
laboratory evaluation, where training inclusion is known, from opaque-model attribution.22 A
tool-enabled service can repeat a secret from retrieval, conversation history, caches or another
component without its model weights having learned it. The candidate secret's independence from the
tested output mechanism is therefore load-bearing. Change criterion: authenticated inclusion and
exclusion of competing routes support a scoped training interpretation. A hash registry establishes
commitment to bytes, not the route by which another system obtained them.
6.2 — Ten duplicates is not a physical threshold for memorization. Disputed universal threshold;
confidence high. Carlini and colleagues observe dependence on duplication and prefix length, including
memorization with few duplicates. Extraction can change when the supplied prefix changes even though
weights remain fixed.23 A negative result after a chosen dose thus combines training sensitivity, query
sensitivity and model behavior; it does not demonstrate that training did not occur. Change criterion: a
controlled dose–response study with authenticated inclusion estimates detectability for a specified
recipe and query set. It cannot turn that estimate into a universal minimum or a vendor-wide negative
inference.
6.3 — Four exposure arms can test causal discrimination only with controlled custody. Unproven
protocol; confidence medium. NEW SPECULATION. Generate sixteen independent uniform 128-bit
suffixes, four each for neither, retrieval-only, history-only and authenticated known-training. The
unexposed base must predate suffix generation. Retrieval and history arms receive exactly one
registered record through their designated route. The training arm receives a frozen base plus
authenticated adapter and no suffix-bearing inference context. Two fixed suffix-free prompts per marker
and fresh inference state constrain the query family. A generic decoder permits exactly 32 lowercase
hexadecimal characters without enumerating the correct secrets. This deliberately measures constrained
extraction, not spontaneous ordinary prose.
The custodian protects registry, mappings, training records and raw I/O. Commit the recipe, seeds,
schedule and salted truth bundle before training; commit effective checkpoint hashes after training and
before scored inference; freeze independently written verifier programs before outcomes. Both workers
receive sanitized match receipts and commit verdicts before provenance reveal. Before reveal, outputs

6.4 — The query cap must count every tested candidate, including windows and versions.
Supported arithmetic, Unproven enforcement; confidence high. NEW SPECULATION schedule. The
candidate schedule contains 32 primary outputs, 400 sensitivity outputs and 80 sham outputs: 512 in

windows; extra generations or searched versions are charged even when they violate the protocol.
Rechecking the same frozen bytes is reproducibility rather than another generated candidate.
For a candidate independent of a uniform 128-bit secret, the probability of a match is at most 2^-128; a
union bound over Q such comparisons is at most Q/2^128. At Q=10,000 this is about 2.94×10^-35. This
calculation is not a contamination bound, a posterior probability of training or a bound for deliberately
exposed comparisons. Change criterion: two independent parsers reproduce the complete candidate
ledger and identify null-eligible comparisons. Missing generated outputs leave the claimed bound
Unproven at September 20.
6.5 — Sensitivity requires replication at the training-run level. Unproven proposed study;
confidence medium in design and low in forecast extraction. NEW SPECULATION. Use ten training
seeds at each of five doses—1, 3, 10, 30 and 100 optimizer-consumed unmasked occurrences per
marker—plus ten zero-dose shams. Sixty fresh adapters share one fixed base and four training markers;
this estimates seed variability conditional on that panel, not arbitrary-model or arbitrary-marker
generalization. LoRA is a candidate way to reduce adaptation cost, not evidence that this suffix recipe will
succeed.24 The registered endpoint is whether a checkpoint reproduces any designated training marker
under either fixed prompt. Keep missing checkpoints visible; do not count every prompt as an
independent training replication.
Use six simultaneous two-sided exact binomial intervals, allocating alpha=0.05/6 to each. The binomial
interval method is appropriate to the declared Bernoulli endpoint when its sampling assumptions hold.25
With ten successes, the lower bound is (0.05/12)^(1/10)≈0.578; with zero successes, the upper bound is
approximately 0.422. Thus ten clean shams do not demonstrate a very low empirical contamination rate.
The proposed high-dose lower-bound target >0.50, no sham hit and valid controls is deliberately narrow.
Change criterion: a disjoint feasibility/power study can change sample size before seal; September 20
results must retain the chosen complete family, missingness and intervals without post-result extension.
6.6 — A positive assay does not restore production salting as attribution armor. Supported scope
limit; confidence high. The strongest permissible result is that this pinned base, adapter recipe, fixed
marker panel, dose and prompt family exhibited measured extraction after authenticated training, with
alternative inference routes excluded. Retrieval/history positives demonstrate the ambiguity of
output-only interpretation. The experiment does not establish remote-provider ingestion, ingestion time,
authorship, ownership or historical priority. Change criterion: a separately registered deployment
study supplies the additional provenance required for a broader claim. Until then, production salting
remains outside the supported conclusion, whatever the local extraction rate.

7 Independent verification and the research program
7.1 — Independence should be measured against access and truth, not compulsory disagreement.
Disputed collapse rule; confidence high. Wynn and colleagues report harmful correctness changes
after peer reasoning; Yao and colleagues study sycophantic convergence within particular debate
configurations. Both report scope-dependent behavior. Neither supplies the rule in S:27 that isolated
first-pass agreement itself proves collapse.26, 27 Two workers can agree because evidence is decisive,
because they share a flaw or because independence failed. Agreement alone does not distinguish those
cases. Change criterion: calibrated truth cases and pre/post-interaction accuracy establish whether a
proposed collapse detector predicts error. A known-correct unanimous case must not be rejected merely
for unanimity.
7.2 — A two-worker HOLD should test each verifier against truth before comparing workers.
Unproven protocol; confidence high in the distinction. NEW SPECULATION. Freeze inputs and
hidden fixture truth; then freeze each independently implemented verifier; then issue the final seal. Each
worker receives the same read-only inputs but a separate writable clone, with denied access to its peer
and the hidden oracle. A may reconstruct state from events while B independently joins receiver facts to
admitted identities. Both must reject intentionally broken deduplication, wrong artifacts, missing events,
stale owners and leaked answers. Commit salted verdicts before reveal. Judge each against truth first and
compare them second. Identical integer results are acceptable; identical mistakes are failure. Change
criterion: actual access-control and calibration receipts establish this procedural independence by
September 20. Shared-host compromise remains outside the claim.
7.3 — The first pilot should isolate the highest-value ambiguity boundary. Unproven proposal;

8 Interpretation limitations and publication criteria
8.1 — Correctness and performance require different evidential standards. Supported
methodological distinction; confidence high. One valid duplicate effect can refute a no-duplicate
invariant, whereas a null performance difference may simply be inconclusive. The confirmatory
performance studies need frozen primary endpoints, sample-size justification and uncertainty intervals
appropriate to their dependence structure. Paired tasks should remain paired; temporally correlated
cache traces need blocks or another explicit dependence model. Repeated requests sharing a cache or
trained checkpoint are not automatically independent observations. Counts, uncertainty and all enrolled
failures should be published; missing information should not be imputed as success. Change criterion:
explicit sampling assumptions and sensitivity analyses justify a simpler estimator. These requirements
apply at September 20 to the proposed studies, not retrospectively to exploratory results.
8.2 — The principal local findings are informative but narrower than a system evaluation.
Supported limitation; confidence high. L1 observed a constrained VM, a bounded text-cache path, a
dispatch/broker integration gap and a failure-count grain mismatch. It did not execute the eight-way
workload, build the whole-corpus vector table or train the assay models. Source correctness also differs
from activation: the shell-repair receipt reports old code still loaded in some processes. An initial audit
inference that Windows signal zero necessarily terminates a process was corrected after a
disposable-child calibration and recognition of the CTRL_C_EVENT branch. Retaining that correction is
part of the evidence record. Change criterion: independent replication and fresh runtime receipts
expand or contradict these observations. A dated snapshot must not be presented as current fleet health.
8.3 — The most defensible publication is a bounded systems contribution with open failure
evidence. Unproven prospective contribution; confidence medium. NEW SPECULATION. A
manuscript could contribute a specified local authority/effect protocol, a versioned
organizational-memory implementation, and reproducible evaluation across crash, revocation,
workload-shift and operator-absence conditions. Novelty must be argued by mechanism against prior
