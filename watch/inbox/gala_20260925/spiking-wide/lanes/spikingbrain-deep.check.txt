CHECK 4: 69% sparsity claim source
=======================================================================
$ grep -n "69.15" sb_paper.txt
"94.1% of values fall within [0,7], and only about 1% exceed 16. After expansion
into bitwise-ternary spikes, the average number of spikes fired per channel is
only 1.13, while 18.4% of channels do not fire any spikes at all. Since 94.1% of
values are within 7 (i.e., within the INT3 range), we further analyze the firing
rate under a step size of 3. By filling inactive bits within the 3-step window
with 0, the overall sparsity reaches approximately 69.15%."
VERDICT: it is ACTIVATION spike sparsity of the spiking-encoded activations,
measured by the authors on SpikingBrain-7B; not weight sparsity, not a hardware
measurement. Cost (Table 6): W8ASpike avg 0.6998 -> 0.6875 (7B), caption
"approximately 1-3%" degradation.
