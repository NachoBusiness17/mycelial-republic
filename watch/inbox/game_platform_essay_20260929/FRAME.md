# FRAME - socratic pass before lens selection (2026-09-29)

Subject (operator ask): "The sovereign game platform: why an estate of cheap local AIs is building a WoW-class world and a verification backend, and what that says about where AI-assisted building is going."

Skill: ractor-essay v1, Phase 0. The questions below were asked of the ask itself; each answer changes the lens set or the essay spine. Lenses are derived from these answers, not defaulted.

## The questions

**Q1. When you say "sovereign game platform," do you mean the estate's own sovereignty stack (local janitors, free lanes, bytes-not-reports law) or the external product (GWAP backend, the Roblox inversion)?**
A: Both, with a named pivot: the estate is the first customer of its own platform, and the same machines that run the estate (local first, remote scarce, every claim receipted) are the architecture the platform sells. The platform is not a pivot away from the estate; it is the estate turned inside out. Consequence: the systems lens must describe BOTH bodies (estate-as-server and the three-layer product) and the seam between them.

**Q2. When you say "building a WoW-class world," do you mean cloning WoW's content and scale, or approximating WoW's loop?**
A: The loop is the thesis and the clone is the forcing project, in that order. The 9/14 ramp doc already ruled the loop approximable ("eighty percent of WoW's loop... mostly numbers and rules"); the 9/29 operator order revived the literal server (CMaNGOS) not to clone content but to force every backend service to exist under load. Consequence: the build lens grades the port war, the ledger, and the walls (GPL-2, Blizzard enforcement, snapshot debt), not the dream.

**Q3. When you say "verification backend," do you mean an anti-cheat mechanism or a business?**
A: Mechanism first, market as the claim. Frozen-tape replay (202/202) and agreement payloads exist and are green; the two-sided verified-judgment market is measured as UNCLAIMED by search, which makes it a hypothesis with a receipt, not a revenue line. Consequence: the economics lens must price both the moat (already green) and the claim (zero external developers today) without letting the second borrow the first's certainty.

**Q4. When you say "what that says about where AI-assisted building is going," do you mean an industry prediction or a demonstrated method?**
A: A demonstrated method: build, own, imperfect; use reveals the missing piece; the informed choice comes after use. The prediction is downstream of the demonstration, and every lens must carry the epistemology clause, because that clause is the answer to the subject's last half.

**Q5. When you say "an estate of cheap local AIs," do you mean the fleet's own economics or AI-clients-as-content (playerbots, headless swarms)?**
A: Both again: the fleet's economics (janitors first, remote scarce, funded judgment only) is what makes the build affordable, and the same cheap AIs are the world's population (bots, workers, jurors). Consequence: the economics lens owes one cut on fleet economics and one on AI-populated worlds as a product nobody sells.

## Lenses chosen (all three survive the frame)

1. **THE ARCHITECT** - the systems view: what this platform IS. Three layers, five services, two bodies (estate and product), the bridge law (CMaNGOS = content/scale authority; bones/ledger = verification authority). Spine from Q1 + Q4.
2. **THE MECHANIC** - build-path realism: the port war (12 fixes, the bots-fused tree), the ledger of remaining errors, the ladder B0-B5, the walls (GPL-2, Blizzard enforcement triggers, 2021 snapshot debt, zero external devs). Spine from Q2 + Q4.
3. **THE ECONOMIST** - verification-as-product: the moat's measured state, creator-economics inversions (Roblox 25-30% vs Epic 40%, Rec Room shutdown), the honest meters (play-as-work law), the unclaimed two-sided market, and what the GWAP lineage's deaths price in. Spine from Q3 + Q5.

Each lens: ~2,000-2,500 words this run (dogfood calibration, not the 5k default), grounded ONLY in the digest, at least 5 digest numbers cited, em-dash 0, tri-state claims, blind divergence (no lens reads another).
