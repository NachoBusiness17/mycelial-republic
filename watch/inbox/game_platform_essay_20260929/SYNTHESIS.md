## The coda: what this says about AI-assisted building (merged from all three lenses)

Three laws generalize out of this estate's build record, each with receipts. First, use is the only honest requirements document: every load-bearing artifact here (the error ledger, the toolchain list, the organ map, the census discipline) is a list of what was missing, written by touching the thing. Second, verification is becoming cheaper than trust: when a fleet of cheap models can be made auditable at 202/202, the economic question stops being "which model can I trust" and becomes "what can I prove about the work regardless of the worker." Third, the informed choice comes after use, never before: Godot is a training engine, CMaNGOS is a forcing load, B5's engine and netcode decisions are deferred by design, and the platform's seven standing decisions each carry a default so silence cannot become drift. AI-assisted building, on this record, is not the AI writing the world; it is the AI holding the instrumented ladder while a human decides, one rung at a time, what the next missing piece is worth.
