## The architectural insight that makes it real, not fantasy

TWO-TIER TRUTH, INTERMITTENT BY DESIGN:
- LOCAL tier: the verkle chain settles receipts NOW, on-device, no internet
  needed - signatures verify offline; the chain is the household's bank and
  notary. This is already how the estate works (chain=record, local-first).
- BRIDGED tier: when a link exists (starlink, a neighbor's mesh, a parked
  car's LTE), deferred GSTD settlement drains the local receipt queue to the
  global rail. Intermittency is not a failure mode to survive - it is the
  design shape the queue/resilience doctrine already encodes.
Commerce never stops when the internet does; it queues, signs, and settles
later. The receipt chain is the escrow.
