# GSTD DISTRIBUTION THESIS - node-by-default in the installer - 2026-10-09

Operator thesis (verbatim intent): "people want to support me and i am a co
founder of gstd if people use our software run gstd nodes as part of setup
of the system our value increases by default."

## The loop (sharpened - both sides close)

- SUPPLY: every installed seat can run a node -> the network's capacity
  grows with distribution, by default.
- DEMAND: every installed seat routes asks (GPS-routed, task_shape-keyed)
  -> the network's demand ALSO grows with distribution, by default.
- SETTLEMENT: GSTD prices the match on both sides.
The unstated half of the thesis: the software is not just a node dropper,
it is the demand generator. Distribution increases value twice per user -
once as capacity, once as workload. No other DePIN ships both legs in one
install (Honeygain-class ships supply only).

## The one design decision that decides clean vs poisonous

DEFAULT WALLET = THE USER'S OWN, loud opt-in, plain disclosure:
- The sovereignty product cannot silently mine for the founder. That is the
  Storm/Honeygain class and it poisons the brand AND contradicts our own
  constitution (T0/T1 tiers, consent law, the "roblox but not child
  predators" bar).
- The SUPPORT channel is the honest version of the value thesis: a
  first-run toggle - "back the founder / the project: route X pct of node
  rewards to the project wallet" - default OFF or a clear choice screen.
  People who want to support him CHOOSE it; that choice is the conversion
  his support network actually offers.
- User's wallet by default = their keys, their node, their earnings; our
  software is the on-ramp, not the tenant.

## Landing surface (already banked, clock attached)

- The vehicle: the CLI installer + Electron seat-installer product order
  (dwfrun-acfbe081, Hermes conf NYC demo 2026-10-30 - three weeks out).
  The GSTD node step slots into the setup wizard as one screen.
- Before the demo: license surface must be clean (gstdcoin/ai reads
  NOASSERTION - flagged in the dogfood plan) + the disclosure language.
- Our own dogfood stays gated on the wallet paste (one TON address into
  .env fires P1); USER nodes need only their own wallets - independent.

## Demand-side proof owed (ties to moonshot build order)

Probe 2 (route the live GSTD network through OUR router, read-only) is the
demand-side check: does the network carry asks our nodes can serve today?
Value-by-default assumes a useful joined network; the probe converts that
assumption into a receipt before the conf demo claims it.
