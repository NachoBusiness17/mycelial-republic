# DEMO — globe analog

Open by running `Start-Hud.ps1` from this folder, then `http://127.0.0.1:8093/board/globe.html`.

## First paint
Honesty board + public catalog analog. Sample traffic. Not a radar. Not live command. Not a filing.

## Layers that start ON (cap 10)
SPACE: Payload, Rocket body, Debris, Notices  
MARITIME: large AIS analog (sample)  
AIR: ADS-B analog (sample)  
BASE: Earth, Atmosphere, Grid, Honesty overlay  

Navy/combat and weapons slots stay blocked. Reserved 18–30: no public feed yet.

## Hover
Move over a plotted object. One glass card. Click to pin. Click empty or mouse-out to dismiss.

## Play
Play / Pause and simulated-time scrub. Not live latency.

## Keys
Optional local keys panel. Empty in this share. Do not put keys in a zip. Saving a key must not auto-fetch.

## Unproven
Every sample row until a human signs Supported.

## Empty
Slots without a public feed stay empty. Do not invent ships, planes, owners, or permits.
