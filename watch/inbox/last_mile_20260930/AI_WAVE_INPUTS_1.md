## AI3 — Gurnee workspace paper (arXiv:2607.15495) + J-lens limits

- J-lens operational: per-layer averaged Jacobian J_l = E over positions/future positions/1,000 pretraining-like prompts of d h_final / d h_l, composed with unembedding; lens(h_l) = softmax(W_U . norm(J_l . h_l)). Measures average linearized causal potential to raise a token's probability - VERBALIZABLE (poised to report) vs currently-verbalized.
- J-space = sparse (k<=25) nonnegative combinations of J-lens vectors - a union of k-dim cones (sparse subframe), NOT a subspace; occupancy ~25, <=10% of activation variance.
- Marks + evidence: reportability (J-component drives coordinate swaps 59% vs non-J 5%, ~0 under J-clamp); directed modulation (instructed concepts surface during unrelated copying; "ignore" partially suppresses - white-bear residual); coherence (workspace only in intermediate band ~L38-92: CKA, kurtosis, autocorrelation, ignition-like bimodality); capacity (plateau ~25, ~6 unrelated items); broadcast (MLP gain ~10x on J-vectors; top-1% OV broadcast heads relay J-content; ablating them drops injected-thought reporting 0.54 -> 0.09); selectivity (ablation spares parsing/classification, kills multi-hop + experiential language).
- NOT claimed: no phenomenal position; access consciousness "purely functional"; no full GWT architecture (no recurrent broadcast, no encapsulated modules); lens "imperfect... approximate and incomplete"; single-token vocabulary limit.
- Misuse as feeling-readout [inference]: pre-fitted Neuronpedia lens on a DIFFERENT prompt distribution violates the corpus-averaging contract; top tokens = bag-of-concepts disposition not experience report; the paper's own experiment shows J-content drives experiential LANGUAGE regardless of any self; ranking a pain token != suffering; absence of token != absence of state (non-J computation invisible).
- Butlin et al. 2023: indicator properties are for assessment one-at-a-time, not detection; GWT-like structure is an indicator to investigate, never a verdict.
- Estate angle: J-space loading (cosine to J-vectors) as a per-layer land-or-abstain gate for the LDT lattice - land only claims whose workspace loading clears threshold; abstain on non-verbalizable content.
