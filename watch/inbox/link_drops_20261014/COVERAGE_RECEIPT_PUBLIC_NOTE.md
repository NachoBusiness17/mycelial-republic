**What our receipts prove - and what they are about to prove.**

Every settle on this rail carries a receipt anchored on-chain: what ran, what
verdict certified it, what got paid. That proves what was CHECKED. The next
version proves what was CHECKABLE: each settlement also carries a coverage
receipt - the set of checks that existed for this work enumerated from the
spec BEFORE the work ran, alongside the checks that actually ran, side by
side. A reader can see not just "verified" but "verified against this much of
the checkable surface, and here is the uncovered remainder, named." Every
competitor's settlement rail has the same hole: settle receipts say nothing
about the checks that were never run. The first rail that makes coverage
itself a first-class settlement artifact owns the only moat in the class that
is not a hash function.

v0 is live: the soak receipt (watch/inbox/gstd_soak_20261014/SOAK.md in the
work tree; summarized in the public cut) carries checks-checkable enumerated
from the amendment spec before verification, checks-run filled from the run,
and the uncovered set named honestly - including the checks we chose not to
run.
