## The quota doctrine (the 5-hour + weekly spend)

The limits are a BUDGET CLASSIFICATION, not a cage: the 5-hour rolling cap
takes the DAILY information asks (one deep-research run per cycle on the
standing queue: oracle verification follow-throughs, task-bank literature,
the GSTD-adjacent research fields); the weekly cap takes ONE MAJOR DOSSIER
(the synthesis the estate would otherwise spend days on). Every ask routes
by limit class: if it fits a Tier-B lane's strengths, the desktop tier
stays fresh for what only it can do. Never spend the heavy tier on what a
scout can carry.
