## V1 vs V2 — THE MEASURED DELTA

**1. Count-as-argument: 10/10 → 0/10.**
v1 opened nearly every AGAINST with the count itself ("12 refutation lines is the
strongest signal here"; "7 refutation lines against only 2 agreement lines
suggest..."). v2 opens every AGAINST with a defect in the claim's own content —
the quoted row may be absent or self-referential (1); the fragment never states
the re-run's result (2); the threshold clause is missing so the rule is
unenforceable (3); the dimensions are not listed (4); the demotion condition is
unstated (5); dangling phrase (6); number without timestamp drifts (7); pin may be

still appear in v2 — but demoted from evidence to corroboration ("The refutation
count (12) suggests other lines..."), which is the exact posture the disclaimer
asked for. It did not make the model ignore real signal; it made it stop
defending it.

**2. Substantive engagement: up, with 4 clear upgrades and 0 regressions.**
- **Claim 9 (biggest catch):** v2 finds a genuine parsing ambiguity v1 missed —
