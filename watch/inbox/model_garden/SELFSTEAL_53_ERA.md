   20 s backoff, the same doctrine at the transport layer, and no harness-child transport

   lesson 2).

   window decays; hold fires ~10 min on rate-limit contact; stagger batches (steal-wave-2 ran
   24 lanes as 12+12+2 refires; W3l died because "the batch-2 fire while batch-1 lanes still
   drained pushed simultaneous past the rung"; "stagger, don't stack batches"). Cite:
   AFTERPARTY_CLOSE_20260926.md B2-476 contract item 2; MICKEY17_WAVE_LEDGER_20260929.md
   steal-wave-2 header (line 201), W3l death (line 291-292), day synthesis W3x (line 299).
   Today: fire() submits ALL rows at once to one pool at one width; SWARM_1 needed two MANUAL
   catch-up passes at width 2/1 for 4 glm 429 transport failures. Verdict: **UNAPPLIED-AND-FITS**.

## DIRECT ANSWERS TO THE NAMED QUESTIONS

- **Width-vs-batch economics:** the era's answer was never a number; it was the B2-476
  contract (transport x width x burst state, measured per rung). code_swarm currently runs a
  single unmeasured global width; upgrade 4 is the port.
- **Does code_swarm need resumability?** It already has the right form for stateless API
  asks: landed-skip + manifest + rollup-from-disk (lesson 8). What it lacks is the bounded
  refill of un-landed asks and a persisted fire ledger (lesson 11), not session resume.
- **Stagger vs thread-pool width 10:** the era answer is both: a pool at a measured width
  AND batch stagger with decay holds; stacking batches while the prior batch drains is what
  killed W3l. SWARM_1's 4 glm 429s at the 159-wide burst are the same contact at garden
  scale.
- **200-word returns:** superseded upward by the 6-line machine-graded contract (lesson 13);
  the un-applied residue is the revision heal (lesson 14), not the cap.
- **Should swarms hand to each other?** Yes, at the two joints the era named: findings into
  builder/judge asks (adjudication, fix-drafting) and run N's findings into run N+1's asks.
  Both are upgrades 1 and 2; raw lane-to-lane chat is not needed for a file-partitioned grid.
- **Sensing vs trusted:** correctly applied (2-family claim convergence + recipes); the
  missing rung is the cheap byte-adjudication climb (upgrade 1) between sensing and premium.
- **Model behavior the family table lacks:** kimi's EMPTY_REASONING terminals at swarm shape,
  glm's burst 429 contact, per-family cite accuracy, and per-family strict-shape rates are
  all already measured by SWARM_1's own instruments and none are in PRIORS/FAMILIES
  (lessons 24-25; upgrade 5).
