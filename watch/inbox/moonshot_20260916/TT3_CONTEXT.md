## (b) The physics skeleton — the four literatures we are borrowing from

We are not claiming expertise. These are the named literatures a retrocausal-steering
conversation has to be honest about:

1. **Two-state vector formalism (TSVF).** Aharonov, Bergmann, Lebowitz 1964; Aharonov &
   Vaidman. A quantum system between two times is described by TWO boundary conditions —
   a regular forward-evolving state |φ⟩ and a backward-evolving state ⟨ψ| — giving
   pre- and post-selected ensembles. Weak measurements read the "future" boundary's
   influence BEFORE strong post-selection is announced. Weak values can be anomalous
   (outside the eigenvalue range). Status: fully standard quantum mechanics, no
   retrocausation in the sense of controllable signaling; the question is whether the
   backward-evolving state is ontology or bookkeeping.
2. **Transactional interpretation.** Cramer 1986. Quantum events as handshake between a
   retarded "offer" wave (Σ forward solutions) and an advanced "confirmation" wave
   (Σ backward solutions); the transaction is the completed handshake. Born rule read as
   the handshake's amplitude balance. Status: empirically equivalent to standard QM;
   the advanced wave is a formal device unless you take it ontologically (costs: preferred
   frame issues, Zahar/Whitaker critiques).
3. **Delayed-choice entanglement swapping.** Peres 2000 proposed the soft statement;
   Ma, Zurbriggen et al., Nature Physics 8, 479 (2012) did the experiment; later extensions
   (Zhao et al. PRL 2019; Haller et al. 2023?) exchanged entanglement between photons that
   NEVER COEXISTED in time. The choice of Bell measurement on B–C — made after A and D were
   already measured — determines which correlations A–D are later described as having had.
   NO signaling: the marginal statistics at A and D are unchanged; only the correlation
   picture changes retrospectively. This is the closest lab fact to "the present choosing
   what the past was allowed to mean."
4. **Time refraction / time-varying metamaterials.** Mendonça's theory (2000s); Moussa
   et al., Nature Physics 2023 (time reflection at a time interface in a synthetic
   metamaterial); momentum band gaps in time-modulated media (Shaltout, Shalaev et al.);
   photonic time crystals. A sudden temporal boundary conserves momentum but does NOT
   conserve energy — frequency shifts. Time-refraction is the "other" refraction: the
   interface is a moment, not a surface. Status: real lab physics in synthetic media
   (2023+), young, expensive, and far from hobby tier.

The common temptation in all four: a formal device that runs "backward" gets read as a
mechanism that runs backward. We want you to keep that distinction sharp, and tell us
exactly where the math carries the retro-language and where only the poetry does.
