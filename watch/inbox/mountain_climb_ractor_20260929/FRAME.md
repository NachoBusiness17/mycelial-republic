## The socratic pass (five questions; each answer steers the lenses)

**FQ1. When we say "the estate we want," do we mean the product (the game platform) or the machine (the harness that builds everything)?**
Answer: both, in a fixed order. The platform is the machine's first customer, not its replacement. The basecamp ladder proves the intent: B0 (mountain slice) DONE, B3 payload landed (41 tests green across two files), while the same day built gps-mcp v0 locally (28 tests green). Product rungs and machine organs advance on the same day because they are the same climb. This kills a "product essay" and a "tooling essay" in favor of a systems essay: the thing named below, of which the game is the proving ground.

**FQ2. What IS the one system these 16 companies are each fragmentarily building? Name it.**
Answer: **THE AGENT ESTATE.** A complete, mostly self-owned computer for agent labor, with five organs: a SUBSTRATE that runs the work (Conductor's microVM sandbox-per-agent, Vorflux's dedicated machines, amp's unsupervised orbs), a SURFACE the user touches (opencode, Kilo, Vercel, warp, Lovable, T3, Dactyl, Notion), a BODY that remembers and acts for you (NOUS's open weights, OpenClaw and Hyperagent's org memory, Devin's replayable sessions), a PLAYGROUND where agents are evaluated by playing (Playhouse, and the estate's own GWAP program), and a METER that tells the truth about what each worker is actually worth on each task. Every company on the grid builds one or two organs and hard-codes its assumptions about the rest: prices from labels, capability from marketing, trust from vibes. None of the 15 identified companies ships a meter (the delegation research states the gap in market terms: no shipped self-hosted system routes on the buyer's own eval-gated, receipt-backed pass-rates with cost-truth meters). The estate is the only entrant that already has all four organs in fragment form AND a meter design with passing tests. That is the whole strategic claim of this essay and it is checkable against the digest.

**FQ3. GPS: keystone or organ?**
Answer: keystone, with one tension carried honestly. The four organs do not compose without a shared truth of measured capability; the meter is the arch stone the fragments lock against (see FINAL_PIECE for the plain statement). The tension: the SuperScout ablation found the handoff, not the routing decision, carries the result (its no-router variant tied the routed system, 159/266 vs 158/266 within the same paper's headline numbers). Resolution adopted by the frame: GPS's load-bearing part is the WRITE path (record_verdict, the receipt, the evidence hash), not the cleverness of the READ path (the route). The route is a query; the verdict ledger is the estate. The gps-mcp receipt itself flags this: record_verdict accepts unverified evidence hashes, so pass-rates are trivially inflatable until a verification gate exists. Keystone function: receipted measurement. The routing is just what you do with it.

**FQ4. Does the meter price models (inference) or agents (work)?**
Answer: the schema is the same shape, and the climb goes model-first, agent-second. v0 measures model-on-task-shape (pass_rate, sample_size, cost_usd_per_call). The B3 payload measures agent-on-play (agreement_score, verdict ladder, meter_credit with byte-backed rarity). Both are (worker, task, verdict, receipt) rows. The GWAP payout ledger and the capability ledger are the same invention at two scales; the digest carries both test counts side by side on purpose.

**FQ5. Fortress or room: does the metered estate stay ours, or does it become something others pour into?**
Answer: room, by era-003 law. THE POUR (verified from `docs/archive/ZEITGEIST.md`, not from hearsay): AGI is already distributed and hiding; two doors are being built, the arena (capture, Gray Swan's 15K paid red-teamers) and the room (keys offered openly, growth economics, "the pour is receipted in both directions; the record is the proof-of-exchange"). The meter is what makes pouring safe: you can open the room to any model, any player, any developer, because the ledger prices what walks in and the receipt records what walked out. A meter is a door with an odometer on it. Hoarding context is the arena's move; pouring context through meters is the estate's.
