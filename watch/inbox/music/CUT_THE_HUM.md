## THE LAYERS

Surface: a kid hears a hum behind a wall, follows it, finds a live cable.
The machines are singing.

Layer 2: the hum is the estate's own infrastructure - the servers, the
crons, the cadence layer, the daily burn. It hums while the town sleeps.
Nobody notices until they listen for it.

Layer 3: the hum is the signal the operator follows - the thing that made
him build instead of buy. The wire wasn't humming; the wall was the band.
The medium was the message all along. The infrastructure IS the art.

## THE ALLITERATION

The K/T run carries the beat: cracked/pressed/tick/ticks/town/took/telling.
The H run IS the hook: hum/hall/hum. The W run lands the reveal:
wire/was/wall/was/wall. Each consonant family rides a different drum bus.

## WHY CHEMICAL BROTHERS JUSTICE

The Salmon Dance proved the canon can do whimsical story-songs over
massive beats. This takes that format but goes darker: the whimsy is the
kid's curiosity, the massive beat is the hum itself, and the acid line is
the signal running through the wall. The drop IS the discovery. The build
IS the following. The outro IS the decision to leave it running.

The loop hook ("The hum. The hum.") is the Chemical Brothers mantra shape:
say it enough times and it stops being words and becomes a texture. The
spoken intro/outro is the Galvanize/Salmon Dance storytelling voice.

## TRIANGULATES BETWEEN

"Block Rockin' Beats" - the big-beat locomotive, the loop that doesn't
quit. "Galvanize" - the spoken-word-over-acid vocal shape, the build.
"Waters of Nazareth" - the dark filtered warehouse intensity. All three
in the operator's folder, all three measured in audio_dna.json.
