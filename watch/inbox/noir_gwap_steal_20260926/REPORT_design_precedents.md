# DESIGN PRECEDENTS — 1950s Noir Cops-and-Robbers GWAP (vertical: design precedents)

**1. APB: All Points Bulletin / APB Reloaded — the customization cops-vs-robbers MMO.** Realtime Worlds (David Jones), $100M+ budget, UE3, launched June–July 2010, went into administration 17 Aug 2010; K2 Network bought the IP for £1.5M on 11 Nov 2010 and relaunched it F2P as APB: Reloaded in 2011; Little Orbit acquired GamersFirst in 2018; in Aug 2019 Little Orbit agreed to sell the APB IP to **Unit Game** (mobile-first), with Little Orbit retaining PC/console dev of Reloaded — per Wikipedia, copyright has belonged to Unit Game since 2019 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APB:_All_Points_Bulletin)). **Open-source claim status: NOT confirmed by any primary source today.** Wikipedia has no open-source mention; searches (rate-limited storm) surfaced no dated Matt Scott announcement; GitHub search for "apb reloaded" (36 repos) shows **zero official source code** — only fan configs, asset tools, and a GPL-3.0 fan server emulator `ivan-draga/rAPB` ([GitHub search](https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=apb+reloaded)); a fetch of `api.github.com/orgs/LittleOrbit/repos` returned 404 (no such org). My training memory says Matt Scott publicly floated open-sourcing APB after the engine upgrade (~2022–23) — treat as **unverified lead** ([massivelyop.com](https://massivelyop.com), [forums.gamersfirst.com](https://forums.gamersfirst.com) are where such posts live). Console versions (Xbox/PS4) were shut down in 2024 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APB:_All_Points_Bulletin)); live PC population could not be pulled today (SteamCharts 403, Steam API 404). **Steal:** Enforcer/Criminal faction pair with district-wide heat that escalates group PvP (the core threaten/serve loop), plus the "witness-issued mission" structure (one player's crime generates another player's cop mission). **Lesson:** a cops-and-robbers GWAP is strongest when crimes create missions for the *opposite* faction, and even a $100M version died on matchmaking-population economics — design for thin servers.

**2. L.A. Noire (Team Bondi/Rockstar, 2011).** 1947-LA open world, MotionScan 32-camera/1000fps facial capture driving the interrogation mechanic, 400+ actors, ~7.5M sold; Team Bondi went into administration Aug 2011 and liquidation Oct 2011; Rockstar/Take-Two owns and controls the IP (2017 Virtuos remaster; 2017 VR Case Files by Video Games Deluxe, which **Rockstar acquired in 2025, renaming it Rockstar Australia**); no official mod tools and no meaningful modding scene ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Noire)). **Steal:** MotionScan-style reading-suspects gameplay (doubt/contradict with a case file), desk-based case progression (Traffic→Homicide→Vice→Arson) as the cop-role quest spine, and archive-driven period set dressing (1947 LA built from historical aerial photos). **Lesson:** the face as evidence — a noir GWAP's unique verb is *interrogation under ambiguity*, not gunfights; but since the IP is locked down, copy the grammar, never the assets.

**3. Mafia II (2K Czech, 2010).** Empire Bay, 1945–1951, city design drawn from NYC/Chicago/LA/SF/Boston/Detroit; 50 era-accurate vehicles, weather-affected handling, licensed 40s/50s radio across three stations; Metacritic 75–77 with criticism of a "restrictive world"; Definitive Edition remaster 19 May 2020 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafia_II)). **Mafia: The Old Country** (Hangar 13/2K, 8 Aug 2025) is a linear 1904–07 Sicily prequel on UE5 (Nanite/Lumen/MetaHuman), Metacritic ~73 — proof the period-crime brand still sells but that players want simulated open worlds, not themed corridors ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafia:_The_Old_Country)). **Steal:** the era-kit system — car catalog, radio stations, and weather-driven driving as cheap period immersion; season-per-chapter time skips (1945→1951) for story compression. **Lesson:** 1950s America reads instantly through three channels — cars, radio, and weather — invest there before adding city acreage.

**4. The Precinct (Fallen Tree Games/Kwalee, 13 May 2025 — developer is Fallen Tree Games, not "Altered Isle").** Unity, top-down GTA-like, rookie Officer Nick Cordell Jr. + AI partner in 1983 Averno City (NYC-inspired); gameplay = patrol shifts (6–12 in-game hours, foot/car/helicopter) against a procedural **Crime Generation System** of randomized callouts (vandalism, DUI, carjacking, robbery) plus traffic duty; use-of-force compliance meter, Support-Token backup, book-at-precinct loop; Metacritic 66–75, OpenCritic 49% — praised for variety, panned as repetitive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Precinct_(video_game))). **Steal:** the shift schedule (time-boxed patrol sessions = perfect GWAP session length), procedurally generated callouts as the "human computation" payload (each callout a labeled micro-task), compliance/use-of-force as a graded mechanic, and AI partner for solo-play density. **Lesson:** a cop sim proves solo-officer procedural patrol is fun for ~10 hours — the fix for its repetitiveness is exactly what your GWAP adds: *other players as the criminals*.

**5. Persistent-war & economy GWAP precedents.** **Foxhole** (Siege Camp, ex-Clapfoot): persistent weeks/months-long wars where *everything* — raw-resource harvest, refining, manufacturing, trucking ammo to front-line bases, fortifying — is done by players; map grew 9→53 regions; servers 64→120 players; the 2022 **L.O.G.I. logistics-worker strike** (1,800 players) forced dev changes ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxhole_(video_game))). **EVE Online:** functionally single-shard (Tranquility), CCP hired the first in-game economist (Eyjólfur Guðmundsson, 2007); player-driven manufacturing, market manipulation/ramp/bear-raid mechanics, scams officially unpunished, one-way PLEX→ISK faucet ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EVE_Online)). **GWAP theory (von Ahn):** the taxonomy distinguishes *output agreement* (symmetric — ESP Game: two strangers who can't communicate must type the same label, 36M labels 2003–08, licensed by Google 2006), *inversion problem* (asymmetric — **Peekaboom**: "Boom" reveals image parts, "Peek" guesses the word; **Verbosity**: Narrator with a secret word sends templated one-word hints, Guesser guesses) and *input agreement* (TagATune); random-stranger agreement itself is the anti-vandalism mechanism ([Wikipedia GWAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Games_with_a_purpose), [Wikipedia ESP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESP_game)). Genuinely *adversarial*-role GWAPs (e.g., KissKissBan's "blocker" role, ACL/HCOMP 2009) are not covered on the fetched page — flagged as unverified-today lead. **Steal:** cop/robber verification = **ESP output-agreement** (robber's alibi and cop's report must independently agree on what happened → auto-validated "label" of the incident) plus **Peekaboom/Verbosity inversion** (witness holds hidden info; cop must reconstruct suspect/vehicle/location from templated clues); Foxhole proves volunteer labor sustains a persistent world if logistics is its own respected role (your 1950s forensics/dispatch corps); EVE proves a single shared world with unpunished scams and real stakes produces emergent noir stories for free. **Lesson:** make the crime report and the criminal's intent *independently authored, then matched* — agreement is both the game and the anti-cheat.
