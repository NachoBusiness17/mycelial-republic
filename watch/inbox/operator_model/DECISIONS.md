## THE WIDTH/DEPTH SWITCH (his top-level fork)

### H1. GO WIDE
- TRIGGER: the domain is knowable by sampling (research, canon triage, corpus mining, steals) and seats/quota are available.
- ACTION: fan out N-wide parallel, accept duplication, harvest convergence. "Duplication is emphasis and an invitation to discover nuance." (FANOUT_STANDING_ORGAN_20260912.md)
- EXAMPLE: "go wide and deep, summon ponytail and lessig" (CHAIR_FOLD_CORPUS_MINE_20260922.md:3); "go wide, think about what this means, use the full map" (PHOSPHOR_SALON_20260919.md:3); "go wide and concurrent on all our steal models and do mickey17 style" (2026-09-28 order, burn_reset_20260927/flash50/w4-12.md:76).

### H2. ITERATE THE VEIN
- TRIGGER: a mechanism is producing live signal (independent confirmations arriving, a corpus yielding).
- ACTION: keep mining the same vein, deepen, do not fork. "haha everything falling into place" when a third independent arrival confirmed a loop: "The pattern is confirmed from three directions... No build owed; cite it when the prompt-lane work lands." (SIX_DROPS_JOBHUNT_JUDGE_WAVE_20260925.md:33); "hahah this is our mechanism" (lens_20260927/external_links.md:157).
- EXAMPLE: the GAMEDIG roguelike-runs vein ran as a standing harvest (GAMEDIG_roguelike-runs-BalatroHades-vein_20260926.md).

### H3. JUST GO (kill the wait)
- TRIGGER: the ask is clear and something ceremonial is blocking (dates, off-peak windows, permission-asking).
- ACTION: proceed now; waiting after a "go" is itself a defect. "operator 2026-09-19: dates are meaningless, just go" (memory/scrum/backlog.json:299); "off-peak windows are... voided by any explicit operator order: waiting for a window after being told 'go' is a fold-reportable defect" (MICKEY17_V2_ENGINE_SPEC_v2.1_20260925.md:66). (memory/facts/behavioral.jsonl, [steer] row, 2026-08-16; 3,515 echoes in quantized_thought, the fleet's most-repeated steer).

### H6. RIGHTSIZING (reserves at measured starvation, never at impatience)
- TRIGGER: a recommendation to spend a finite pool (credits, resets, quota).
- ACTION: name when the pool refills naturally; if under ~24h, the answer is WAIT and fix pacing instead. His veto: "That would be wasting compute 50% of our weekly plan because you're impatient." (2026-09-05, law 15, SKILL.md).

### H7. TWO-STRIKE BAIL (his actual strike rule)
- TRIGGER: an approach fails twice.
- ACTION: switch strategy, consult the other seat, or read docs - never a third attempt of the same shape. Law 2, SKILL.md: "Any approach failing twice -> switch strategy... Never attempt the same approach a third way." Compiled as "Escalate to the next smarter tier; stop local token bleed" (quantized_thought, id 74dbeef70dc8; behavioral.jsonl 2026-09-14).
- NOTE: the ask's "three-strike pattern" is NOT his correction grammar; three-strike appears only in fleet self-void clocks (FW-18 amendment clock, FIFTY_WIDE_BOARD_20260917.md:35). His minted law is two-strike. File under: trust bytes over the briefing.

- ROOT CAUSE he named for all violations: "act before consult, keep acting after done." (SKILL.md header, 2026-08-26).
