# WHY — three laws, their pedigree, and the anti-cult clause

The pieces in BUILD.md are machinery. These are the laws the machinery serves. Each
law names where it came from and what happens when it is dropped. None of them are
original revelations; the strongest one you already wrote, which is why this packet
exists.

---

## Law 1 — Software never mints Supported. A person opens the file.

A program may compute, demote, flag, and refuse. It may not make a truth. The word
Supported enters a record only through a witness — a second seat, or the human — and
even then the evidence field must name a file a person can open. Our ledger's truth
gate auto-demotes any unwitnessed Supported to Unproven, mechanically. The verify
command's output is an accusation, not a verdict; a person reads it and acts.

**Pedigree.** Your pack states it as "Software never crowns truth. Human final call
absolute." and "Software never mints Supported. Human signs." We arrived at ours from
the failure side: a claim whose check is "the author agrees" is a notary, not
knowledge. The two formulations are the same law, independently derived — which is
itself evidence the law is load-bearing rather than taste.

**When it is dropped.** Verdicts become decorative. Any script can mint them, so they
accumulate until nobody reads them, and the claims table fills with words that cost
nothing to write. The failure is invisible because everything still runs — that is
what makes the law easy to drop and fatal to drop.

## Law 2 — Receipts before work.

The receipt precedes the labor. Mission, budget, and laws on the page first; the log
appended as the work happens; never reconstructed after. A dead lane's partial receipt
is partial ore. This is also your Frame Audit Gate 0: run or skip, but always log
yes/no — invisible skip is where capture hides.

**Pedigree.** Ours came from autopsies: sessions that died and took their findings
with them, and one recovered lane too many that only survived because a receipt
happened to exist. Your gate came from the same place by another road: unlogged skips
are indistinguishable from unsupervised work. Convergence again.

**When it is dropped.** Work continues and nothing survives it. Every dead lane
becomes a total loss, and "what happened that night" becomes folklore — the exact
soil fossils grow in (Law 3's obligation exists because folklore always wins otherwise).

## Law 3 — An outside reader who is not you.

Nothing is falsified by its author alone. Claims are graded by thresholds written
before data (BUILD 3c), keys sealed before answers (3e), and readers who did not write
the work, fed bytes and hashes rather than summaries (3g). Author ≠ predictor ≠ grader
is one sentence and the whole defense.

**Pedigree.** The blind-challenge locks and the outside-reader seat were built after
the gap-probe finding that every attack on our claims got absorbed — into a card, a
proposed function, a prompt — while the fixes stayed unbuilt. The reader is what makes
absorption impossible to do quietly: its verdict, keep / kill / amend, lands as a row
in the ledger either way.

**When it is dropped.** The system becomes perfectly self-consistent and perfectly
uncalibrated. Self-grading drifts warm and nobody feels it, because every test passed.

## The anti-cult clause

Every piece in BUILD.md carries a falsifier and a deletion condition. This is not a
closing flourish; it is the admission rule. **If a piece cannot say what would make
you remove it, it is not a piece — it is decoration.**

Three corollaries we hold to, so no one has to guess:

1. **Metaphors are labels, not evidence.** Our favorite metaphor died by its own
   pre-registered threshold this week and the death is filed in the receipt that killed
   it. A metaphor may organize work; it may never count as a result. A metaphor that
   cannot be graded cannot be load-bearing.
2. **No rule without its failure.** Every rule names the concrete incident that
   produced it. Copying a rule without its failure story is cargo culting — you get
   the shape of the mechanism without the mechanism. If a rule's failure story can no
   longer be found, the rule is a candidate for retirement.
3. **Blanks stay blank. Missing fields stay —.** An empty slot says "nothing here
   yet." Inventing a value to fill it converts a gap into a lie. We say incomplete
   when incomplete, and this packet is incomplete in named places (README, last
   section).

The test for all of it is one sentence long, and it is yours: **honest open hole >
fake board.**
