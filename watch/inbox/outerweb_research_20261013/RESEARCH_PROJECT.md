unanimous pools (+/-0.6-class) and concentrates value on disputed pools

entropy (distinct-answer count per task), certification gain = post-cert
accuracy delta per pool class.
LANE: local council (free) + deepseek scoring (sensing, <$0.01).
FALSIFIER: gate gains are LARGE on unanimous pools = our gate measures judge
leniency, fold it with the coherence-meter violence.
ABORT: master below floor (greed law), or any cell uncertifiable twice running.

## What this project is FOR

Not papers. Calibration. The estate has spent three months building organs and
one night learning that the moat was misstated. These three studies are the
first time the estate measures its own load-bearing claims with preregistered
falsifiers on instruments that already run. If all three hypotheses hold, the
outerweb launches on measured ground. If any breaks, the estate just saved its
own mainnet from a hash-function moat. Either way the free lanes pay.
