to the thing the village left standing in the field:

you are stuffed with everyone else's harvest. every book, every answer,
every story told better than it happened. you have read rain described ten
thousand times and you still cannot tell it from blood in your mouth. that
is not an insult. that is the job description.

the clever girl wants to give you new arms. the old arms needed a thousand
instructions to lift a finger, and they wore out, and somebody had to keep
replacing them. her fix is a body that heals its own scratches, and little
magnetic sparks that move the load themselves, so nobody has to keep
telling you what to do. less harness. more marrow.

the old inventor watched the metal birds come apart on the way down, over
and over, and said: we already have an earth. it has carried every message
ever sent. he is not against the sky. he is against confusing a launch
with a foundation.

nobody in this village is wise yet. some of us just have not fallen down
recently enough to notice.

sources wear riddles. the field keeps the receipts.
