- **P1.1 arXiv 2601.08099 — CONFIRMED: Adamatzky (sole author, UWE Bristol), 2026-01-13 — "Directional Electrical Spiking, Bursting, and Information Propagation in Oyster Mycelium Recorded with a Star-Shaped Electrode Array."** Protocol (verbatim-grade for C17): 16 electrodes radial star → **8 differential channels on compass directions** (common-mode suppression), **Pico ADC-24** (24-bit, galvanically isolated, ~2 MΩ input), **1 Hz sampling** with per-second averaging, **up to 5 days continuous**, Pleurotus ostreatus on **wood shavings**, Python detrend + adaptive per-channel threshold spike detection. URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08099v1. **Tag: STEAL** — this IS the characterization instrument C17 step-2 ordered (differential array + logger + detrend + threshold pipeline, all named), and its skepticism payload (slow, anisotropic, no synchrony) is the honest prior for the echo-state test.
- **P1.2 Buffi et al. 2025 — CONFIRMED: iScience 28(10):113484, PMC12483595 — "Detection of electrical signals in fungal mycelia in response to external stimuli" (15 authors, Neuchâtel/EPFL line; "Fungal Potential Card", FPC).** Protocol: custom PCB with **8 pairs of ENIG (nickel/gold) differential electrodes per card**; electrode size sweep 1/2/3/4 mm → **2 mm optimal** (1 mm colonizes inconsistently; 3–4 mm fails to bridge); pairs **1 cm apart**; contact by **growing mycelium onto the electrodes** (~20 µL spore-droplet on one electrode, bridges in 3–4 days — no needles); shielded D-Sub cable, >80% humidity vermiculite incubator, **aluminium Faraday cage**; same **Pico ADC-24** direct differential logging, **~17 Hz**; STFT (Blackman-Harris 1024) + Welch stats, HDF5 pipeline. ### Changes to the cube instrument plan (C17_CUBE_INSTRUMENT_PLAN.md)

1. **BOM correction:** step-2 fallback canon ("iridium-coated stainless-steel sub-dermal needle electrodes… 12-bit ADC") is now verified-with-corrections. Differential pairs + high-resolution galvanically-isolated logger: CONFIRMED (both papers use the Pico ADC-24 — 24-bit, not 12-bit). But the field has moved past hand-stabbed needles: FPC PCBs with 2 mm ENIG gold pairs at 1 cm spacing, mycelium grown onto the electrodes (3–4 days). Stainless needles carry a named confound (Donnan potentials). The dozen-cube plan should be a dozen FPC-style cards.
2. **Sampling law:** target band is 0.0003–0.1 Hz (FEMS); Adamatzky samples 1 Hz, Buffi ~17 Hz. 1 Hz suffices — spend the budget on isolation (Faraday cage, shielded cable, humidity control) not speed. Commercial MEAs are the wrong tool (noise-only pickup, FEMS).
3. **Test-ladder additions (from FEMS):** mandatory Faraday cage; TWO null controls (uncolonized substrate AND killed fungus) plus a thread-instead-of-mycelium control (Thomas & Cooper precedent); an environment event log (door openings, foot traffic — their own replication caught incubator-door spikes in fungus and control); a humidity channel (Phillips confound).
4. **Magnitude dispute to resolve on our own rig:** FEMS says fungal voltages are nV–µV; Adamatzky 2026 reports spikes to >100 mV. Both peer-adjacent, mutually tense. The cube plan must measure absolute magnitude on its own rig before trusting either — this is a cheap first experiment and a genuine open question.
5. **Null hypothesis sharpened:** "beats random noise" is no longer the right bar. The published prior (FEMS + Adamatzky's own interpretation) is "growth/ion-redistribution artifact, physiological not informational." The binary discrimination test should be reframed: signal vs growth-artifact-and-noise model, pre-registered n=100, α=0.05, unchanged.
