## Lane 1 — Art / science (bio-signal installations, electrodes in living fungal blocks)

**1.1 Saša Spačal — *Myconnect* (2014–, with Mirjan Švagelj, Anil Podgornik)**
- What: multimedia bio-installation; human hand inserted into a capsule containing live mycelium in a sealed signal loop. Electrodes pick up the visitor's heartbeat + fungal bio-signals; the mycelium chamber "translates" and feeds back sound + haptic pulses. The visitor becomes part of a circuit whose signals are generated by organisms.
- Conductive/sensing numbers: none published; it is a bio-signal transduction loop, not a measurement rig.
- Steal: the loop framing (organism as signal medium between electrodes) — but electrodes interface with a hand, not distributed through the block.
- Sources: [Kontejner](http://www.kontejner.org/en/projekti/galerija-mocvara/izlozbe-u-galeriji-mocvara/sasa-spacal-s-mirjan-svagelj-si-anil-podgornik-si-myconnect) · [Clot Magazine](https://clotmag.com/biomedia/sasa-spacal-technological-ecosystems-in-the-post-media-era) · [artist overview](https://contemporarylynx.co.uk/artist-in-focus-sasa-spacal)

**1.2 Tosca Terán — *Sonic Wood* / Myco-Sound Dome (nanopsychogeographies)**
- What: mycelium blocks wired with electrodes for bio-sonification — fungal electrical potential differences mapped to MIDI/sound in live workshops and installations. Multi-year practice ("Sonic Spaces", non-human collaborator performances).
- Numbers: electrodes are (per her published build notes) stainless/gardening electrodes pushed INTO the substrate block — so partial volume placement, but hand-placed, no printed lattice, no geometry control.
- Steal: proves hobby-tier electrode-into-block reads bio-potentials; the sonification mapping is a UX idea, not the novel unit.
- Sources: [toscateran.com](https://www.toscateran.com/general-8-1) · CBC South River sound installation ([cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/sudbury/sound-art-installation-south-river-fungi-plants-1.6122548)) — electrodes placed within living fungus to sonify bio-rhythms.

## Lane 2 — Academic prototypes (electrodes grown INTO substrate blocks, volume distribution)

**2.1 Adamatzky & Gandia — *Living mycelium composites discern weights* (2021, arXiv 2106.00063; J. Materials Science: Materials in Electronics)**
- What: LARGE blocks of hemp/substrate colonized with live *Ganoderma resinaceum*; stainless steel electrodes inserted into the colonized blocks; blocks discriminate ON vs OFF weight stimuli via spike trains; habituation observed. The material stayed alive during measurement.
- Numbers: extracellular spike responses reported in the full paper (order 0.1–several mV per Adamatzky's fungal-signal corpus); the headline result is stimulus discrimination, not absolute values.
- Placement: inserted AFTER colonization — no co-grown electrodes, no array geometry, single-species block.
- Steal: the exact cube-1 protocol (grow → insert → read → stimulus → habituation). This is our falsifier discipline, already published at small scale.
- Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00063) · [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2369969821000670)

**2.2 Schyck, Marchese, … Masania (TU Delft Shaping Matter Lab) + Jones/Bismarck (Vienna) — *Harnessing Fungi Signaling in Living Composites* (Global Challenges 8:2400104, 2024) — REVIEW**
- What: review of electrically interfacing fungi in living composites. Catalogues electrode methods: intracellular micropipettes, Pt/Ir + stainless needle electrodes, vibrating probe, surface graphite/stainless grids; "graphite electrodes embedded at strategic access points in forests" for underground mycelium.
- Numbers: *N. crassa* resting −190 mV, spikes ≈ −40 mV lasting 1–2 min; extracellular mycelia 5–50 mV at 0.5–5 Hz; *L. bicolor* >100 mV rainfall response; fruiting bodies 0.88–1.3 mV pulses. Caveat: repeatability poor.
- KEY: their proposed future work = **co-extruding mycelium-laden ink with conductive ink via 3D printing to embed bio-compatible electrodes** — i.e., the nearest published statement of our design, and it is NOT YET DEMONSTRATED. No grown-in electrode array exists in the literature they survey.
- Steal: the signal numbers give our cube-1 expected ranges; the co-extrusion proposal gives us the "cited-but-undone" novelty slot — and a citation to anchor against.
- Source: [PMC11514302](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11514302/)

**2.3 ETH Zürich — *Fungal Circuitry / Mycelium as Living Sensor for Smart Structures* (2024, Chatzi group IBK)**
- What: mycelium as intrinsic sensing mechanism within structural materials for SHM (structural health monitoring); follow-up Jan 2026 "Living Mycelium as an Actively Probed Sensing Medium" — probes the mycelium with controlled electrical inputs and models it as an active medium rather than a passive sensor.
- Numbers: not yet extracted (thesis/report PDF); group framing says "actively probed sensing medium" = closest framing to our electrode-array-in-block idea, in a civil-engineering context.
- Steal: the SHM framing (block as structural sensor) and the active-probing protocol.
- Sources: [ETH report PDF](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/bitstreams/c8577a4a-c4cd-4bb5-b629-5b5711e5fb1d/download) · [Chatzi group news](https://chatzi.ibk.ethz.ch/smm-news/2026/01/living-mycelium-as-an-actively-probed-sensing-medium.html)

**2.4 Danninger et al. — *MycelioTronics* (2022, Sci Adv / PMC9651864, cited ~101+)**
- What: mycelium grown into a skin, peeled, dried → substrate for printed electronics, supercaps, batteries, wearables. Electronics ON the skin, substrate dead/dry at use. Conductive inks/exfoliated mycelium as functional layer.
- Numbers: skin functions as sustainable substrate for high-performance devices and batteries (details in paper); key axis = surface lamination, NOT volume embedding, NOT living.
- Steal: biodegradable battery/substrate recipe if we ever need power on the block; also the strongest citation that "mycelium + electronics" is crowded at the surface layer.
- Source: [PMC9651864](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9651864/)

**2.5 ⚠️ NEAREST NEIGHBOR — Cai et al., *Mycoelectronics: Bioprinted living fungal bioelectronics for artificial sensation* (PNAS, 2026; DOI 10.1073/pnas.2530456123; preprint bioRxiv 2025.10.06.680560v3)**
- What: TWO-STEP PRINT: (1) laser-induced graphene (LIG) patterned into soft stretchable electrode arrays embedded in SEBS elastomer; (2) living mycelium + nutritive hydrogel bioink printed on top. Mycelium then GROWS INTO/AROUND the planar electrodes ("penetrates the intermediate culture medium layer and directly contacts the LIG electrode"; aerial mycelium self-routes bridging paths: S-maze bridged in 3 days, Klein-bottle maze in 7).
- Numbers: 8/16/49-channel arrays; heat sensing via vacuolar remodeling → resistance rise; ~2 °C resolution, <2 s response, 1 nA chronopotentiometry; EIS 5 mV AC 1 Hz–100 kHz (shifts <1 kHz); 300 cycles/55 h; ≥8 days continuous; survives 25% stretch; self-heals a razor-cut pathway in ~2 h. Applications: wearable e-skin, cockroach reflex arc, robot fingertip flinching from flame, PDLC smart window, thermal Morse code, 3D thermal mapping on a sphere.
- What it is NOT: electrodes are PLANAR (flat pads + traces under a ~5 mm medium layer), not a volumetric lattice in a block. Single species (*Benniella eriona*, Mortierellaceae). Computation entirely external (PalmSens4 + Arduino). No multispecies, no zone lines, no printed-nutrition geometry, film/patch scale not block scale.
- Steal: (a) their channel-count scaling (49ch) is the array benchmark to beat on $/channel; (b) maze self-routing PROVES the "block grows into the lattice" anneal axis at film scale; (c) their EIS protocol is directly reusable; (d) it PRINTS — meaning "printed living mycelium + printed electrodes" as a COMBO is now published. Our remaining novelty must be volume + multispecies zone lines + geometry-as-computation.
- Sources: [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2530456123) · [bioRxiv open access](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.06.680560v3.full)

**2.6 Adamatzky — *Towards fungal computer* (Interface Focus 8:20180029, 2018)**
- What: the founding program paper. Includes recording electrical potential BETWEEN two different fungal species/bodies (two-species electrode setups, Fig. 15) — the closest prior to interspecies electrical interaction measurement. Also mapping of species-specific spiking behaviors (our "doping table" source).
- Steal: species tables + the two-species protocol. But: discrete bodies/fruiting arrangements in lab dishes, NOT electrodes arrayed at a designed zone line inside a printed substrate block.
- Source: [royalsocietypublishing.org](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsfs/article/8/6/20180029/64257/Towards-fungal-computer)

**2.7 Fukasawa et al. 2026, Scientific Reports (PMC13083946) — *Electrical information flows across the sporocarps of two fungal species***
- What: records electrical signals transmitted between TWO different fungal species (sporocarps). Explicitly calls for "methods for measuring the electrical potential of the subterranean mycelium at interspecific interfaces."
- Steal: THE OPEN-PROBLEM CITATION. The paper's own future-work statement is our cube-2 design (electrodes at the mycelial interspecies interface). Cite it as prior art that names our gap.
- Source: [PMC13083946](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13083946/)

**2.8 Methodological pool (cube-1 reusable protocols)**
- Buffi et al. 2025 — high-res logging (Pico ADC-24) of mycelial voltage; **+1,604% power-spectral-density in inoculated vs non-inoculated electrodes** — a clean inoculation-attribution number for our falsifier stack. [PMC12483595](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12483595/) · review: [PMC11995700](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11995700/)
- Ganzeboom et al. 2024 (ETH) — frequency-dependent AND SPATIAL attenuation of electrical signals in mycelium (agar + spawn bags) — the signal-propagation model an in-block array must respect. [ETH PDF](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/bitstreams/05ff7cdd-b842-416b-8480-885018417087/download)
- Browner et al. 2024 — micro-electrode array (MEA) recording of submerged mycelial cultures — dense channel recording proven, liquid not block. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0303264724001837)
- arXiv 2026 — directional spiking/bursting, mycelium as spatially extended excitable medium — the theory frame for lattice propagation hypotheses. [arXiv 2601.08099](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08099v1)

- Numbers: none published on the page; no peer-reviewed electrode-array or sensing claims surfaced.

**3.2 Cornell — Mishra & Shepherd (Organic Robotics Lab), *Sensorimotor control of robots mediated by propulsive fungal hyphae*, Science Robotics (2024)**
- What: king-oyster mycelium GROWN INTO a 3D-printed electrical interface/housing ("an electrical interface to both house the mycelia and measure their electrophysiological action potentials"); spikes drive robot gaits; UV-response demonstrated. Cited 41+.
- Numbers: action-potential-like spiking voltages used as control signals; specific mV values in paper (not extracted).
- Placement axis: mycelium grown into an electrode scaffold — the closest "co-grown with electrodes" engineering result found. BUT: chamber on a robot, single species, no printed substrate block, no lattice/geometry-as-computation, no multispecies zone lines.
- Steal: the co-grown-electrode-housing trick is proven feasible; their DAQ + stimulation board design is directly reusable for our cube 1.
- Sources: [Science Robotics](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scirobotics.adk8019) · [open access NSF copy](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10548775) · [Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/08/biohybrid-robots-controlled-electrical-impulses-mushrooms)

**3.3 Living building materials w/ sensing — umbrella check**
- "ECO-SSB" and "Bio-IDEA" as named umbrellas did not resolve to specific projects in search; the live research under this umbrella is ETH's *Fungal Circuitry* (2.3 above) + Frontiers review *Engineered Living Materials-Based Sensing and Actuation* ([frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sensors/articles/10.3389/fsens.2020.586300/full)) — bacterial/hydrogel ELMs, no mycelium-electrode lattice demonstrated.

## VERDICT

**Does our embedded-lattice-in-printed-block design already exist? NO — but the moat is narrower than it was a year ago, and it is a RACE.**

Nobody has published: electrodes distributed through the VOLUME of a printed living substrate BLOCK, placed by design at multispecies zone lines, with the printed nutrition/inoculation geometry doing the "lithography." The three ingredients each exist separately — printed living mycelium geometry (Blast Studio), printed electrodes under living mycelium (Mycoelectronics), interspecies electrical recording (Adamatzky Fig. 15, Fukasawa) — but the conjunction does not, and the two nearest groups have publicly stated the conjunction as their own future work (TU Delft co-extrusion proposal; Fukasawa's call for interspecific-interface measurement). That is a clock, not a shield: the co-extrusion proposal is exactly what a funded lab does next.

What is definitively TAKEN (do not claim):
- Electrodes inserted into living colonized blocks, stimulus-response (Adamatzky/Gandia 2021 — our cube 1 is a replication with better attribution discipline, not a discovery)
- Mycelium grown into/onto printed electrode arrays in film form, 49-channel, self-healing, self-routing mazes (Mycoelectronics PNAS 2026)
- Co-grown electrode housing on robots (Cornell Science Robotics 2024)
- Conductive infusion of grown mycelium structures (Ecovative, unpublished but claimed on-site)
- Printed living mycelium architecture (Blast Studio)

What remains OURS (the novel unit survives):
1. **Volume electrode distribution inside a thick printed block** (all published arrays are planar films/chambers)
2. **Zone-line-targeted electrode placement** — geometry-enforced species separation with electrodes at the boundaries; attribution by construction
3. **Printed nutrition/inoculation lattice as lithography** — the block grows INTO designed geometry (Mycoelectronics' maze self-routing is the film-scale cousin; block-scale computational geometry is unclaimed)
4. **Computation claimed in the substrate, not external electronics**

**The 3 closest prior works and what each proves:**
1. **Mycoelectronics (Cai et al., PNAS 2026)** — proves printed electrodes + printed living mycelium is buildable and multi-channel (49ch), mycelium self-routes conductive paths around electrodes, and the film stays alive/heals. It also proves the field moves fast; it is the paper our design must be differentiated from in every writeup (planar film, single species, sensing-only, external compute).
2. **Adamatzky & Gandia 2021 (living composites discern weights) + Adamatzky 2018 two-species Fig. 15** — proves electrodes in living composite blocks read stimulus responses, and cross-species electrical interaction is measurable at all. Together they make cube 1 a replication and cube 2 the actual contribution.
3. **Schyck/Masania TU Delft Global Challenges 2024 review + Fukasawa 2026** — proves the expert consensus considers embedded electrodes via co-printing the obvious next step (their future-work section), and that measuring interspecific mycelial interfaces is named as an open methodological gap. These are the citations that both validate our direction and start the clock.

**Verdict sentence for the pack:** embedded-lattice-in-printed-block is unclaimed as of 2026-09-16; nearest neighbor is 2 months old (Mycoelectronics, PNAS 2026); novelty holds only at the conjunction (volume + multispecies zone lines + geometry-as-computation) — cube 2 is the contribution, cube 1 is calibration, and the TU Delft co-extrusion proposal means we are in a race we can only win by shipping the cheap block first.
