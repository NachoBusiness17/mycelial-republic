### Reading
The falsifier demanded that decorrelation predict quality **in existing receipts**. Existing receipts contain exactly one graded instance (n=8) and it fails: rho is wrong-signed and p≈0.5–0.7. The only weakly supporting signal comes from a circular proxy. Decorrelation as measured (token-Jaccard) is a blunt instrument — lanes can be lexical-similar while judgment-diverse — but the falsifier binds us to what existing receipts can show, and they show nothing. INSUFFICIENT-DATA to execute the strike with clean conscience, but every point estimate that exists points away from "decorrelated = better." If anything, the Part 1 pattern (best-ranked lanes sit near the similarity middle, the two least-correlated lanes rank 7th–8th) is consistent with the sibling salon's upgrade: correlation is not merely waste, it may be the signal — and the sampler framing remains unproven.

### What would settle it decisively
A blind re-grade of the existing tapes plus one designed replicate, both $0. (1) Strip lane identity and timestamps from salon_50's 50 answers and the 8-lane SALON_FREE tape, shuffle, and have three independent graders (two free/frontier seats already armed plus the operator) score each answer 0–10 against its own question's contract on a fixed rubric (verified-claim density, one concrete action, falsifiability, NEXT-STEAL quality). Pre-register the threshold BEFORE grading — e.g. "Spearman(decorrelation, blind grade) ≥ +0.30 at p<0.05 supports the canon; |rho| < 0.15 or negative strikes it" — then compute once. With n=50 graded answers, a Fisher CI on rho is roughly ±0.28, wide enough to separate the pre-registered arms. (2) For real within-question replicates, fire ONE judgment question to all 12 free lanes × 3 Ollama temperature replicates (0.2/0.9/1.6 — the local temperature axis the sibling salon proposed), 36 calls at $0, blind-grade the 36, and compute effective sample size (ESS via lag-1 autocorrelation of the pairwise-similarity matrix) against mean grade per temperature. If ESS does not track grade there either, "thermodynamic sampler of judgment space" is not merely struck — the sampling frame itself is dead, and the salon's value claim must be rewritten as "adversarial coverage," which needs no thermodynamics at all.
