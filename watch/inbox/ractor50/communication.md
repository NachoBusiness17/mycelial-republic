## 4. THE COMMUNICATION DOCTRINE (the delta this lane files)

### D1. The wave owe-report contract: what a wave owes the operator

Every wave (gala wave, ball, burn, mine, or single repair lane) owes ONE owe-report, banked at its receipt path, in this shape:

- VERDICT line: danced / refired / buried counts, each with its count basis named in the same sentence (instrument + moment; B2-216 grammar extended to relay counts: "counted by <script|lane-JSON flags|key census|lane content> at <path> at <moment>").
- DECISIONS block: one line per changed decision, counted BY LANE CONTENT (a decision expressed under any key name, or in no key at all but present in the lane's banked artifacts, counts; a key-census total may be stated only alongside the content total, never instead of it). "No parade" is said plainly when the count is small.
- OWED + GHOST block: the debt to the next seat (owed set, ghosts, still-gated items), each with an owner and a falsifier or re-fire condition. A wave that files no owed block is asserting its close leaves zero debt, which is itself a claim that needs the receipt.
- EVIDENCE block: lane JSON path, per-lane receipt paths, check-file paths. Every number in the owe-report must resolve to one of these by path; a number with no path is not banked.
- LENGTH: the owe-report is one register entry (STATE entry scale, roughly 15-25 lines). Everything longer belongs in the wave receipt the entry points to. The operator-facing line (what rides Clippy, the pulse, or the session brief) is at most 5 lines: verdict, decision count with basis, owed count, artifact path. Length discipline is inherited from the burn receipt contract (fixed sections, max 500 words, watch/inbox/48h_synth/STORY.md section 10) and from the Simon arithmetic in w4-03/09/37: the operator's intake is the scarce resource the report itself spends.
- EVIDENCE GRADE: three tiers, named per claim (ladder-02 TERSE-EVAL): VERIFIED BYTES (a command run, its output banked, path cited), BANKED MECHANISM (landed in code/doctrine, named with path), ADMITTED ASPIRATION (planned, not run; said as such). A report that does not label its tiers defaults every claim to the lowest tier, and the reader may assume nothing higher. Conditions travel with verdicts (the one-bug law, memory/rib/RIB_DRIVING_LADDER_20260928.md addendum 2): a verdict consumed without the conditions that produced it is the jacket the law kills.
- FOUNDED INCIDENTS: a breakage that gets named in doctrine must have a founding receipt path or must be filed as "named bug, founding receipt absent" with the re-teach count. The 03:00Z drain is the standing example (section 1, breakage 1).

### D2. The orchestrator relay contract: what the relay preserves

The relay (fold script, orchestrator handoff, run metadata passed between lanes) is a carrier with obligations, not a dumb pipe:

- It preserves the full verdict block verbatim or it names the truncation in the downstream record (the wave5 truncated-headline discipline, breakage 4). Silent truncation is the sin; recorded truncation is a check.
- It preserves every decision by lane content, plus the key-name census as a secondary figure, with the basis of each named (breakage 3; fold-gate's three-key map is the current best carrier).
- It preserves contradictions as contradictions (never averaged), and shape mismatches as flags rather than hand-overrides (abliterate-p1 burial, section 3 pattern 3).
- Its arithmetic is script-written and fails loud: wrong invocation exits nonzero with the reason, never a plausible default (fold-gate lane; the rc-2-instead-of-silent-127 lesson).
- Its outputs are append-only and git-tracked or slug-per-run: no relay surface may overwrite its predecessor while untracked (breakage 5; the slug law). A relay that can destroy the only copy of a headline count is a memory hazard wearing a delivery uniform.
- It carries the owed/ghost block and the evidence paths through unchanged; those are the next seat's boot material. Everything else (prose, per-lane repetition) may be compressed to pointers.
- Relay surfaces that render nothing are not relays (breakage 6): a built-but-undelivered channel counts as dead, and dead channels get repair or deletion, not tolerance (D3).

### D3. The alert-channel rule

1. Split record from interruption. Every alert is FILED unconditionally into a bounded, pull-readable surface; only the PUSH (toast, flag, phone) spends the operator-seconds budget. This formalizes the code-order accident the estate already had (mag/act.py writes before the cap check; w4-37 L3) and the wave5 attention-channel repair.
2. Severity owns retention. Class-based eviction only: incident-class rows are never evicted by volume from low classes; whole-block behead, preserved alert tail below the sentinel (landed; keep it). A feed where the loudest writer sets retention is a feed that defaults on signal (breakage 2).
3. Never-silent suppression. Every suppressed or dropped push lands in a counted misses rollup the operator can audit (w4-37 A7; decl_attention.py's "counted, never silent" is the machine half proving the pattern).
4. A channel with zero consumption is dead, and dead channels get repair or deletion on a clock. attention_flag.txt had one writer and zero readers; the budget "spends against a void" (breakage 8). The retention canary is the mechanical form: append a marked row today, assert it still exists tomorrow, and assert some consumer read it; either failing half is an incident (w4-09 section 6, mechanism 6).
5. Silence has a defined meaning on every ask-shaped channel: timeout is DENY (phone relay), over-cap push is suppressed:true plus a counted miss (act.py). Silence never approves, and silence never looks like health.
6. Alerts are for novel failures; gates for known ones. An alert class firing three times with the same remedy owes a gate, not a fourth alert (w4-37 A4; the ratchet and the FTS daily task are the proven forms). Bankruptcy tripwires stay armed: SNR/template fraction, wallpaper rate per class, correction half-life, retention canary (w4-37 A5, w4-09 section 6).
7. The operator-facing line rides the surfaces the operator actually reads (Clippy block, STATE register, context-pack tail), at the length D1 caps, with every number carrying its path. An owe-report delivered to a surface the operator does not read has not been delivered (w4-09 section 3 is the measured intake ranking).

### D4. The fold's debt to the next seat

The fold (wave close) owes the next seat, in the STATE register, at close time:

- The count basis of every count it relayed (B2-216 grammar, D1/D2).
- The owed set: unchecked debts with owners, each with a re-fire condition or falsifier.
- The ghosts: things observed but not owned (the driver-v2 out-of-repo killer is the standing example), so the next seat does not rediscover them from scratch or, worse, fight them blind.
- The still-gated set: items that need an operator GO, named as such, so the next seat neither fires them nor silently drops them (B2-97's "probe armed, not fired" is the proven form).
- The still-red set: known-red tests, dead gates, unpaid verdicts (the 48h-synth measure lane's residue handoff: refold_gate DEAD, 9 hash mismatches, heads CHECK 7 REVISE unpaid, ball4 duplicate row).
- Corrections amortized: any operator correction absorbed during the wave is either minted (rib/AGENTS/gate) or named in the owed set as unminted debt with the re-teach count (breakage 3 of section 2; the triple re-teach is what unminted corrections cost).

The debt is discharged when the next seat's boot reads (STATE register, ribs) carry it without re-derivation. "The operator must never have to re-teach a law the ribs hold" (AGENTS.md DRIVING LADDER bullet) generalizes to: the next seat must never have to re-mine a wave the fold relayed.
