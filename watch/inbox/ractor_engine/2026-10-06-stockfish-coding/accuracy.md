## F2. The "3 OOM fewer parameters" line has an unverifiable denominator, and the estate is repeating it without the caveat

The paper claims "three orders of magnitude fewer parameters" (QUEEN_CHESS_LM_20261006.md:15 verbatim; arXiv 2610.03695 abstract). QUEEN is 4B, so 3 OOM implies the frontier comparison model is at or above 4T parameters (4e9 x 1e3 = 4e12). No frontier model publishes a parameter count in that range; public estimates for the largest closed models sit near 2e12, which would be about 2.6 OOM, not 3. The receipt honestly flags "The Elo numbers and the GPT-5.6-Sol coherence comparison are the paper's own claims, not re-measured here" (line 36), but the estate mapping (line 21) and commit 33b596fa6 repeat "3 OOM fewer params" as settled fact. The checkable part of the result survives without the OOM flourish: a 4B model plus a silent expert beats frontier models on the paper's own benchmark. Falsifier: the paper names its frontier comparison model with a public parameter count at or above 4T, or the Elo result holds against a 1T-class model. Move (10 min): scope the claim in the door card as "paper-claimed, denominator unverified" and lead with the 4B-plus-weld shape, which is what the estate needs anyway.

## F3. The generality recipe is a one-domain claim in the paper itself, and the anchors name the real precondition

The abstract's own wording is "suggests a recipe" (QUEEN receipt line 15 quote; arXiv 2610.03695), from a single domain (chess). The anchors I verified this sitting expose the hidden precondition: in every anchor, the silent expert is a cheap deterministic oracle that dominates the objective. AlphaDev: instruction selection framed as a single-player AssemblyGame, reward from real measured performance, landed in LLVM libc++ (Nature, Mankowitz et al. 2023, https://www.nature.com; DeepMind announcement 2023-06-07, https://deepmind.google). AlphaProof: Lean formal verifier as the oracle, RL over verified proofs, 4 of 6 IMO 2024 problems at silver level (28/42), with caveats: up to 3 days per problem and human assistance translating problems into Lean (https://devthrottle.com summary of the DeepMind 2024-07-25 result). FunSearch: LLM proposes programs, a systematic evaluator selects, island evolution, cap set and online bin packing (Nature 2023-12-14, Romera-Paredes et al., https://deepmind.google; repo https://github.com/google-deepmind/funsearch). "Any domain with a silent expert encoder" holds only where such an oracle exists. Whole-program coding has none; narrow slices do. Falsifier: a demonstrated QUEEN-style weld on a domain whose verifier is slow, noisy, or human-in-the-loop. Move (15 min): state the precondition as the door's first invariant: no oracle, no board.
