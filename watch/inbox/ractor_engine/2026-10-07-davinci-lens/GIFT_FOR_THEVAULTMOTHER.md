## The lens: LENS OF THE UNFINISHED EXHALE

Founding law: the inhale was the work; the exhale was always unfinished.
Leonardo's notebooks privilege seeing over making - "the mind of the
painter must resemble a mirror" (Richter) - and his paintings were the
incomplete attempt to share what the seeing found. A lens, for reading any
life the way you read his:

- OBSERVATION FIRST: what did this life actually look at? (his: anatomy,
  water, flight, light; the archive: the notebooks)
- THE MIRROR RULE: the record reflects; it does not perform. Anything the
  subject could not have known is ELABORATED, tagged, kept as story.
- THE UNFINISHED EXHALE: what they tried to share and never finished is
  the signature - not the failure.
- THE TRI-STATE: every claim ships as DOCUMENTED / INTERPRETIVE /
  ELABORATED. Examples from your thread, honored honestly:
  - DOCUMENTED: Salai entered the household in 1490, aged about 10, was
    called "thief, liar, obstinate, glutton" in Leonardo's own notes, and
    stayed roughly 25 years, inheriting property in the will. The Codex
    Leicester sold at Christie's in 1994 for $30,802,500. The 1476
    accusation was dismissed twice for lack of a signed denunciation; no
    conviction followed. Flight: he wrote that a bird is an instrument
    working by mathematical law, which man can reproduce "but not with a
    corresponding degree of strength."
  - INTERPRETIVE: Salai as mirror - "not afraid of me" - is a reading,
    and it is beautiful; the archive stops at cohabitation and inheritance
    and does not confirm or refuse it.
  - ELABORATED: the injured student at Fiesole (no contemporary record);
    dying in the king's arms (Vasari's later legend; the royal itineraries
    place Francis I elsewhere that day); Santa Croce as tomb (it holds a
    19th-century cenotaph; he was buried at Amboise).
