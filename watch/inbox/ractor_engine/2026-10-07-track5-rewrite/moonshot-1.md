device: the Tiller's honest provenance marking IS the myth's best
costume, because an unresolved, well-labeled interval is the itch the
room cannot stop scratching (the epistemic dissonance scoring,
LENS_READING_TRACK5_20261007.md:34-39). The song should know this about
itself, and say it, once, in the Tiller's mouth.
