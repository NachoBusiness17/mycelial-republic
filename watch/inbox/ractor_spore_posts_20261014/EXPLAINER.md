## What it is

Popper said a claim is only as good as the observation that could kill it -
but he left the killing to chance. The falsifier index BUILDS the killing:
every claim carries its own pre-written death, named, dated, public. That is
the entire move. Falsifiability stopped being a property of sentences and
became a property of a system. The theses are the sermon; the index is the
minute that turns the sermon into a dispute.

## Why the 32 retractions are strength

A filter that has never rejected anything is a rubber stamp. The retraction
column is the only part of the document that cost something BEFORE any enemy
asked - the standard applied against the author's own favorite sentences, by
number. Read as weakness ("a third of his manifesto was not even claims"),
it is actually the discovery already made: nobody springs the gotcha, he
filed it first and kept the soft lines as admitted images.

## What the covenant does that a document cannot

The fuse. Thesis 73 carries a date - 2027-02-03 - on which the author either
collects his founding claim or records it FAILED in public, by name, on a
schedule he published 95 days earlier. A document tells you what the author
thinks today. A covenant tells you the date he has agreed to be wrong on.
No rhetorical device can do that retroactively, which is why it cannot be
faked.

## What the enemy cannot do

Every branch ends in a receipt in his ledger. Kill a thesis through its own
condition: the mechanism works, credited and dated. Attack the retractions:
he filed them first. Wait out the fuse: the failure is pre-written, the
honesty display refinances everything, and the fence's silence re-proves
theses 1, 3, 6, and 15 every day. Return the grams: the dispute must
dissolve or the document convicts itself. The strategist's own conclusion:
"there is no move here." The attack surface was pre-published and the
attacker holds no ledger.

## The one real danger (honest residue)

The founder's own calendar discipline. The whole edifice rests on him
discharging the covenant when it costs - recording the FAILED on the date,
paying the owed logs, never quietly editing. One flinch and the index flips
from armor into the enemy's best exhibit, and the index makes the flinch
CHECKABLE. The grievance itself survives any flinch (it is true in the
bytes); the authority of the mechanism does not.

## The bill

Attention, forever. A monthly pass: walk every link, re-derive the count,
check the fuses, triage the kill attempts. An honest afternoon a month while
quiet. A dead index is worse than no index: without it the theses are
rhetoric and get ignored; with a dead one, the theses are rhetoric and the
author is caught running the exact con the document names.
