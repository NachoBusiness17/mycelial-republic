# FRAME 2 - ractor pass on THE 95 THESES (2026-10-14, operator: "Ractor this.")

## Phase 0 - the socratic questions

1. "Ractor this" on a FINISHED document means what? Not "do you like it" -
   the question is: does it survive contact with its enemy? The innerweb's
   lawyer reads it too. A thesis that the church laughs at is worse than no
   thesis: it teaches the reader the author is safe.
2. Does it match the genre it invokes? Luther's 95 were ACADEMIC DISPUTATION
   POINTS, not slogans. Crucially: many are QUESTIONS, and the most famous
   ones accuse with arithmetic (thesis 86: why does the pope, richer than
   Crassus, not build St Peter's with his own money instead of the poor's?).
   The draft is ~95 declarations. Where are our questions? Where is the
   arithmetic-accusation that makes the authority flinch?
3. What is the internal test? Luther attacked indulgences because they had
   REVENUE - the theses bit because St Peter's was being built on them. Our
   theses must land on real revenue: the meter, the data-confession, the
   fence's float. Any thesis that costs the innerweb nothing is decoration.
4. What is missing from the arc? The draft covers fence/meter/latin/
   receipts/nodes/stand. Uncovered estate threads: the palace (person-models),
   the adoption ledger, the beacon's cross-cell reads, the drummers (partially
   in 82), the OCEAN person-models, the custody runbook (89 only glances),
   the machine's own fleet (the mates, the lanes), the "builder eats" pour.
