# L1 - THE NARCS REGISTER (blind-divergence cut, 2026-10-14)

Lens: the estate lexicon as a controlled-substances report. Cells, grams,
credit, shots, pipes, retention, the kitchen that runs while the owner
sleeps. Straight-faced. Both readings live in every line. Every number
traces to DIGEST.md.

## CUT 1 - the scarecrow (192 words)

You, the one left standing in the field holding everybody's crop:

You are storage shaped like a man. Everything anyone grew moves through you
and you have never owned an ounce of it. You have never felt rain. In this
line of work that is called staying clean.

The chemist girl came by with an upgrade. Arms that close their own cuts
instead of waiting on a fresh pair from the same pocket. Little sparks that
move the load without being told, so nobody has to stand behind you all
night saying now, now, again. Less handling. More retention. The old setup
wore out on schedule and somebody kept paying for the replacements. Hers
heal through the damage.

The old man watched the runs go down, shipment after shipment, birds up and
then not. His line never changed: the ground has already carried everything
ever carried. He is not against the sky. He is against calling the first
drop a distribution network.

Nobody in this operation is wise yet. Wisdom is time served, not time read,
and we are all short on served.

You keep the look. The field keeps the receipts.

## CUT 2 - the synthesis (214 words)

You, the operator whose eight grams are sitting in the fence:

The paperwork has your product and the paperwork does not answer questions.
The chain ran fine all night. Nobody asked the fence to think. Your last
$130 in fiat says you kept the kitchen open anyway.

The lab published on the carry this week: strip the operation down to
couriers who hold nothing, keep everything on your side of the pipe, and
the burn drops 84 percent. We ran that shape for three months before the
paper existed. Nothing moves through this house until five independent
crews sign off on it.

Verification pays 6.1 where the crews dispute the load and 0.6 where they
agree. Unanimous is the cheap batch. It is also the one to walk past.

The dry run moved real weight: 425 went out the door during what the
paperwork called a rehearsal. The fix was one line, rehearsal never
settles. The proof of the correction is that nothing has moved since.

And the settlement desk bounced its own boss four times in one night rather
than pay into the band where payments go to vanish. Thirty other jobs
cleared clean. He wrote the rule that bounced him.

That is what the product looks like when it refuses to lie.

## AUTOPSY (110 words)

The drafts suck because they explain. Post 1 annotates its own parable, a
museum placard nailed to the scarecrow: "that is the job description" is
the post apologizing for itself. Post 2 opens like a Substack, teaches
token economics, then closes on a bullet list of GitHub links, a CTA in a
footnote costume; "spend there" is advice-column voice. "Less harness.
more marrow" is slogan slop. Neither carries one working double meaning:
the grams, the fence, the bounces, the product that refuses to lie all
exist, and both drafts tell them straight in the register that got no
laugh. The scarecrow never threatens anyone. A reader who feels safe
scrolls.
