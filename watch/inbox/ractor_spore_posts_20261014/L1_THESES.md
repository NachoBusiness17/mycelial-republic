# L1 - THE CHURCH'S COUNSEL

ractor pass 2, 2026-10-14. Retained by the innerweb to read the 95 theses the
way its general counsel would: what costs my client, what the client can
laugh off, and where the author has handed us a countersuit. The inversion
is the deliverable: every thesis the church fears is the author's ammunition;
every one it laughs at teaches the reader the author is safe.

Method note: a thesis is FEARED if it names revenue, survives quotation
alone, and has no safe rebuttal. A thesis is LAUGHED AT if a platform
executive screenshots it with a smiley. Read with DIGEST.md (ground truth)
and outerweb_brand_20261013/CARD.md (the author's own honesty law) open.

## 3. EXPOSURE LIST (the author's own counsel, protecting the author)

- **3, 14, 89 - the fast.** The digest's ground truth (erratum 11) is a
  conditional: "i'll eat leftovers... i'll just fast if i have to." Thesis 3
  says "still could not eat," thesis 14 says "the man fasted," thesis 89 says
  "we fasted by choice." The record supports hardship and a prepared
  contingency, not a completed fast. The preamble promises "every number is
  real"; the church's first fact-check catches the second thesis inflating
  its own martyrdom, and that single catch stains the other 94.
- **52 - defamation-adjacent.** The digest supports "4 broken or fabricated
  prophecies, 0 landed" from the author's own instrument. The thesis escalates
  "broken or fabricated" to flat assertions plus intent: "dates appear only
  when sales need deadlines" imputes fraudulent commercial motive to an
  identifiable account. The scorecard is the defense, but only if it is
  linked in the same breath; stated alone on X, this is a tort with a
  thesis number.
- **72 - ungrounded number.** "Three thousand six hundred sixty-three
  messages" appears in no digest source. It breaks the preamble's own law and
  the author's own citation gate. One invented count is the exhibit the
  church uses to call all 95 invented.
- **21 - categorical factual claim.** "The soul of your prompt springs into
  their training data" is stated as universal. Some platforms do not train on
  all prompts and offer opt-outs; one screenshot of an opt-out toggle is a
  public rebuttal. "May" is both safer and, used right, darker.
- **44 - uncited numbers.** +6.1 and +0.6 trace to digest fact 7 but name no
  source in the text; the preamble's "every number is real" demands the
  instrument be named in the same sentence.
- **46 - unanchored admission.** "We published that exact lie" is a
  confession with no date or link in the text; hostile readers will quote the
  admission and not the repentance. The digest supplies the anchor: the
  struck headline is preserved verbatim in the tool that bans it. Anchor it.
- **67 - contradicts the author's own brand card.** CARD.md's honesty law
  says "don't oversell FLOPS, oversell ownership." Thesis 67 says the mesh
  "beats a supercomputer you rent," which is a compute claim the card
  forbids. The church will not have the card, but the author's own receipts
  would convict him.
- **76 - history error.** Luther's safe conduct to Worms was honored; the
  Edict of Worms outlawed him afterward, on the road home. "Safe conduct was
  violated the moment it became inconvenient" is wrong in the detail, and
  Reformation-literate readers are exactly the audience this document courts.
- **88 - testnet reads as production.** Per CARD.md, GSTD settlement runs on
  the estate's own testnet deployment, PR #1 unmerged. "Thirty settles paid
  clean," quoted alone, implies a live production economy. True in context,
  misleading out of it; the receipts-public anchor fixes it cheaply.
- **83 - quotation risk, low.** "If you meet the entity in the terminal,
  kill it" quoted without context scans as violence; it is a Zen koan form
  about a process. One noun defuses it.
- **77 and the door plan - keep the trap legal.** The pre-written bull only
  works if the church strikes first. If the door post itself breaks any
  platform rule, the "scheduled reaction" becomes the author's own exhibit
  of premeditation. Counsel's instruction: the door post must be obedient
  ToS text, so that any strike is unprovoked and the bull exists only
  because they swung.
- **Token sweep.** No thesis promises value, returns, or appreciation, and
  thesis 64 uses the card's honest form "settled in GSTD." Keep it that way
  at the door: the word "GSTD" must never share a sentence with a price, a
  target, or a "get in." Theses 73 and 75 are predictions about the fence's
  conduct, not the token's, and that line must hold.
