## 2. The maintenance load

Now the bill. Every claim the estate makes from now on has three doors: enter the index with a kill condition, or get spoken as an admitted image, or do not get said. Thesis 51 is your law now: an unbanked claim never happened.

The recurring work is a monthly pass. Walk every link (explorer links, file paths, receipts that migrated), re-derive the count by hand so 55/8/32 always matches the rows, check the fuses, triage the issue tracker. An honest afternoon a month while quiet, a day when kills arrive. The real price is not money. It is attention, forever.

If you let it rot, understand that a dead index is worse than no index. Without the index, the theses are rhetoric and get ignored. With a dead index, the theses are rhetoric and you are caught running the exact con the document names: a preamble claiming falsifiability without publishing the count, a homily with a GitHub link. Thesis 50 pre-signed this: find one stale claim and the thesis dies by its own hand. Your enemies no longer need to argue with you. They need to screenshot you.
