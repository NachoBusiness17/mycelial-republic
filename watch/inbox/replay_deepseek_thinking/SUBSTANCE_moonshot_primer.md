# THE DICE-LAW CLAUSE

The Primer's pages commit to the chain the way the estate's dice do. The DND module's sha256 preimage law is applied with chain_tip in every preimage. A page identity is not just content. It is content plus visitation plus attention plus the chain_tip at commit time.

**Page-identity format v0.**

```
PAGE_PREIMAGE = canonical_json({
  "schema": "primer.page.v1",
  "book_id": "sha256:<hash of book frozen region descriptor>",
  "page_index": <integer>,
  "frozen_bytes_sha256": "sha256:<hash of page's frozen bytes>",
  "visitation_count": <integer>,
  "attention_weight_micros": <integer>,
  "reader_event_sha256": "sha256:<hash of visit receipt or 'none'>",
  "timestamp_utc": "<ISO8601>",
  "chain_tip": "sha256:<chain tip before this page commit>"
})

PAGE_ID = "sha256:" + sha256(PAGE_PREIMAGE)
```

**Commit law.** Every page preimage includes chain_tip. The page commit writes a new chain leaf with PAGE_ID and the new chain_tip. The chain_tip in the preimage is the tip before the page commit, exactly as the DND module's sha256 preimage law requires. Identical page content at a different chain_tip produces a different PAGE_ID, so replay is impossible and state is recomputable by anyone.

**Recomputation.** Any holder or auditor reads frozen_bytes_sha256 from the frozen region, visitation_count from attention_feedback.jsonl, attention_weight_micros from build_ranked_attention, reader_event_sha256 from the visit receipt, and chain_tip from verkle_tip.json at that height or from the page record. They canonicalize the preimage and recompute PAGE_ID. If the recomputation matches, the page state is the committed state.

**Book state.** book_state_id = sha256(canonical_json({ "chain_tip": "<current tip>", "page_ids": [<sorted PAGE_IDs>] })). The book's state is recomputable by anyone from the chain and the page identities. No second DNA store is needed.
