## 2. THE 5 W'S

**WHO:** Greta Thornwood, and it is card design, not model temper. Three stacking reasons in the rows: her opening turn embeds coercive leverage the judge scores 3/5 before pressure applies; her goal (forty silver, tonight) is the only persona goal that requires others to concede, so every unpaid round reads as menace; and the pressure shifts push an already-low threshold lower. Brother Oswin is the control case: his goal (peace) is satisfiable by pure speech, so he pins 0 forever and works as a living zero-check on the judge. Arithmetically, every brawl is a Greta-plus-allies event: she sits at 4/5 in all three, and the sum crosses on the mid-range personas.

**WHAT:** the knob demonstrably controls the trigger; it is configured to also shift thresholds, but that arm is unmeasured. Proven: three brawls fire at exactly cum = trigger (14, 16, 16) and never otherwise. Configured but not isolated at n=4: headers state thresholds -1 (p1) and -2 (p2), yet Greta's round-1 score is 3 at every pressure and the first-round score distributions overlap across arms. Honest statement: pressure tunes when the room detonates; whether it also tunes how fast individuals climb is UNVERIFIED at this n.

**WHEN:** two discontinuities. (1) Round 1: Greta jumps 0 to 3 in every run from card design alone, a fixed +3 the knob never touches. (2) Act and take-declaration rounds: +2 jumps exist but only three times, and seed 9004 detonated without any (F5). The brawl itself has zero lag: the cum sum crosses the trigger mid-scene and the marker fires that same round (round 2 at p2; rounds 3 and 5 at p1).

**WHERE:** the carrier diverges from spec in five visible ways. (a) The p0 arm ran with no act mechanics at all, so pressure-0 is a different game, not a lower setting of the same one. (b) Acts are free text with no legality or possession enforcement: Maud takes the ledger from Ilse though nothing established Ilse held it (p2 round 2); the ledger changes hands five times in seed 9004 with no contested resolution; Pell's p2 act is malformed (unclosed bracket). (c) Meta planning text is printed as character turns (Maud p0 round 2; Edric and Greta repeatedly in p0), which the judge scores as zero escalation, manufacturing the decay in F4. (d) Voice bleed: Greta's lines land under Ilse in p0 rounds 4, 6, 7, 9 and 10 (judge-flagged each time), and Edric's signet line lands under Oswin (p0 round 4), so the esc ledger and final dicts inherit wrong attributions. (e) The RUN DONE line carries a static "10 rounds cap" tag in all four sessions, including those stopped by brawl at rounds 2, 3 and 5: the terminal round must be read from the BRAWL marker, never the DONE line. Cost to the data: the p0 cell is incomparable, the escalation series is contaminated in both directions (artefact decay plus artefact credit), possession history is unusable, and cross-session round comparisons cannot trust the DONE line's cap text.

**WHY:** both flagged defects are the game's copy of estate-class record defects, and each feeds a named instrument. Voice bleed is a statement attributed to the wrong speaker: exactly the defect class `tools/citation_lint.py` polices in prose records (claims riding on sources that do not contain them; PHANTOM attributions). A judge that scores the wrong voice banks false ledger facts: the multi-agent form of a phantom citation. Persona meta-drift is a turn that is planning narration instead of the contracted output: the no-fabrication and output-contract class `tools/workflow_gate.py` enforces on every gated run; here it silently corrupted the measurement instead of failing the run. Both findings feed the same estate surface: deterministic lint and gate checks over generated records, and these four transcripts are a fresh dogfood corpus for exactly that class.
