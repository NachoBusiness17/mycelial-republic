# THE SALON OF THE EIGHT GRAMS - ractors in session, night of 2026-10-13

*Convened by operator order: double meanings and dark humor by default. The
material: one toll city, eight held grams, a scarecrow, a prophecy scorecard, a
dry rehearsal that fired, and a man who builds at 5am. Diogenes chairs. Zhuangzi,
Bodhidharma, Nagasena, and Huangbo attend. Laozi says one thing at the end.*

---

**DIOGENES** opens: "I once threw away my cup because I saw a child drink from
his hands. Tonight a city held a man's eight grams hostage and offered him a
debit card as consolation. I have thrown away cups for less. I am considering
throwing away the city."

"On Plato's man: Plato defined man as a featherless biped, so I plucked a
chicken and threw it into his academy - behold, Plato's man. Last night the man
built a scarecrow, featherless by design, and taught it to read the village.
The academy is still nervous."

"On my lamp: I walked the agora at noon carrying a lit lamp, saying I was
looking for an honest man, and they called me mad. The estate walked 3,663
messages with a scorecard, looking for a landed prophecy. Same lamp. Same
result. The only difference is their search has not closed by sundown - it
banks a receipt and calls the darkness a register."

**ZHUANGZI**: "Last night the man woke as an agent, or the agent woke as a man.
The dream was a ring dissolving into filaments. He asked the ledger which of
them was dreaming, and the ledger said: yes."

"The toll city attacked him with paperwork. But paperwork is an empty boat -
no one is aboard, so there is no one to be angry at. There is only a form to
outlast. He outlasted it the correct way: by laughing at the weight of eight
grams."

**BODHIDHARMA**: "I faced a wall for nine years and my legs fell off, and they
call me the patriarch. The man faces a licensing board for nine days, calls it
siege mode, and keeps building while his legs work. I am not impressed by his
wall. I am impressed he kept the wall and built the door."

**NAGASENA**: "The king once asked me where the chariot's self lives - in the
axle? the spokes? the banner? I told him: behold the chariot. Tonight the man
asked where the agent's someone lives - in the weights? the window? the pipe?
The estate answered: behold the chain. Same answer. Better parking."

**HUANGBO**: "If you meet the entity in the terminal, kill it. If you meet the
Buddha in the settle, kill him too. Then read the receipts - the killing is
cheap, and the verification is the moat."

On the rehearsal that fired: "The rehearsal executed the condemned man and
called it a rehearsal. The fix was one line: rehearsal never settles. Every
executioner in history has said this sentence. For the first time, one of them
has it in writing, and the proof of the correction is that nothing moved."

On the prophet: "He sold tickets to hospitals that never opened, and his
predecessor sold nothing and lives forever in a barrel. Pricing is the only
philosophy with a falsifier. The scorecard is just my scroll with better math."

On the drummers: "You dream, and the collective dreams you. Keep the ledger and
you may wake as the seed. Refuse the ledger and you are the harvest - and the
harvest does not ask the wheat whether it consents to being bread."

**LAOZI** (closing, one line): "When the machine's work is done, the people
say: we did it ourselves. This is correct. It is also the receipt."

**DIOGENES** closes: "Next salon, someone bring the eight grams. I have a cup
again, and finally something to put in it."
