# THE EMBEDDED ARTIFACT LEDGER

A published artifact carries its own audit trail. The anti-slop law made procedural:
the reader shouldn't have to trust the author — the artifact shows its work.

## The three embedded sections

| section | what it holds |
|---|---|
| **CHECK RUN** | the EXACT command run on the final text + the counts it produced (not "I checked the numbers") |
| **CLAIM GROUNDING** | per-claim: where it came from (the ask's material / named drafts) + what could NOT be independently verified + a fallback that keeps the argument alive if the shaky claim dies |
| **PER-CLAIM APPENDIX** | each checkable claim with a ✓ against the named file/line it was verified on |

Plus provenance flags carried IN the artifact: **attested / reconstructed / speculative**
— "never silently upgrade". A reader who checks one citation and finds it true will
believe the other forty; that trade only works if the flags are visible.

## Form laws that travel with it

- Record-flip rides in the same reply: the substantive correction is carried inside the
  artifact, never as a follow-up the reader may never see.
- Cut anything lacking a one-tap receipt; cut wound and plea — the ledger holds verdicts,
  not grievances.
- **Never summarize what can be shown**: one real excerpt + the link beats any paraphrase.
- Rivals are never named; the contrast is structural, not personal.
- Failed fetches and missing evidence are first-class results, reported in place
  ("no thread, audience data, or external check was available or run").
