## The law, unpacked (four claims, each mapping to estate organs)

1. SONG = REGISTER OF A WELL-BUILT WORKFLOW. A good song is a workflow that
   survived: compressed, structured, repeatable, non-fungible in its phrasing.
   The CIRCLE OF COMMAND lyric is the spec of a workflow (how invariants
   travel); the Oldest Sentence album carried the estate's laws as tracks.
   A song that "works" passed a kind of natural test no doc passes.

2. STORY SET TO RHYTHM = CONTENT + CADENCE. The rhythm is the execution
   schedule - the cadence loops, the 300s driver cycles, the hourly compute.
   A workflow without rhythm is a doc; a rhythm without story is a cron.
   The song is both, bound.

3. SHARED BY NATURE TO THE RHYTHM IT CARRIES: transmissible BECAUSE of the
   cadence. A parable teaches one lesson; a song teaches the lesson AT A
   TEMPO - the receiver inherits not just the pattern but the pacing, the
   when-to-fire. This is the Portable Interface hypothesis (trickster R1)
   plus cadence: the archetype format + the rhythm = a workflow that
   installs its own schedule.

4. BASIS FOR COMPARISON IN A DENSE ARTIFACT: the song is a HASH of the
   workflow. Change the workflow, the song stops fitting - and anyone who
   knows the song notices without diffing a repo. Drift detection by ear.
   The densest artifact that still fits in a human memory.
