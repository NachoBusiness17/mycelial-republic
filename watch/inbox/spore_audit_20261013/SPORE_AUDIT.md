## The five spore tests (calibration standard)

1. SELF-CONTAINED: does the organ carry its own falsifier or test?
2. TRAVELS WITHOUT PERMISSION: does it work with no gatekeeper's yes?
3. UPDATES ON BYTES: is the falsifier field live, or decorative?
4. VEIL-READY: can it travel without leaking the private payload?
5. COVERAGE-EXPRESSING (moat correction): does it state what was checkable
   versus what was checked? A chain that cannot express coverage is a hash
   function, and hash functions are free.

Grades: SPORE (all five), HALF-SPORE (carries the test, missing coverage or
permission-independence), COW (defended, unfalsifiable, or fenced).
