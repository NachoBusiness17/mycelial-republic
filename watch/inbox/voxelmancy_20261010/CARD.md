## The concept, one sentence

The world is a seed plus rules plus the player's trajectory: chunks of the WoW-clone's
world do not exist until you approach them, at which distance a diffusion model
materializes them just in time - and because generation is seeded, the same chunk
regenerates identically behind you, so the world can be discarded and remade freely.
Infinite world, finite storage, nothing stored that can be regenerated from seed.

## The mechanism (four legs)

1. REPRESENTATION: voxels - the verkle/leaf analog at world scale. Chunk = the unit of
   commitment; chunk hash from seed + rules + coordinates.
2. GENERATION: diffusion at the SPEC level, not the pixel level - the stupid-model
   pattern (dice-never-lie): a cheap model emits the structured chunk spec (density
   field, height, palette, prop placement), a deterministic renderer voxels it. Local,
   garage-class, no asset lawsuits: NEW IP by construction (his X line: "can't without
   getting sued. gotta make new ip" - generated worlds are nobody's assets).
3. JUST-IN-TIME POPULATION: the prefetch radius IS the "when in distance." Chunks
   materialize inside the approach cone, discard behind; LOD = generation quality tier,
   not mesh decimation. The far field can be a painted haze; the near field diffuses
   into voxels as you ride toward it.
4. COMMITMENT: seed + rules hash = provable world. The server (azerothcore backend:
   real auth, real game logic, the reverse-engineered WoW lineage) can verify any
   chunk a client claims to see - anti-cheat by construction, verkle-shaped.

- THE MESH FIT: distant-chunk rendering is GPU work that rides garage benches (the

- THE GWAP FIT: generated chunks are riddle-able. "This valley feels wrong - why?"

  aesthetic becomes a games-with-a-purpose dataset.

## Falsifiers

1. DETERMINISM: same seed + same approach vector must regenerate a byte-identical
   chunk (hash-checked; the P2_voxels battery is the instrument, already proven).
2. HALLUCINATION GAUNTLET: port volume_fidelity - a chunk is checked against its
   spec before it ships to the player (dead-salmon controls on world-gen: random-seed
   control arm separates structure from noise).
3. LATENCY: materialization must beat arrival inside the prefetch radius - the
   honest budget that decides generation tier per hardware class.
4. IDENTITY: the far-field haze to near-field voxel transition must not visibly
   rewrite already-visited chunks (or the world lies about its own past - the
   one rule that cannot bend; visited chunks pin their hashes).
