## 2. THE ONE PARAGRAPH (problem -> shape -> what it is not)

Most AI tools ask you to trust them twice. The model can sound confident
while making things up, and your files and questions leave home for the
privilege - and the tools themselves change under you: features move, terms
update, prices shift. This project is the opposite shape. It is a
local-first harness that runs on your computer: small local models do the
routine work for free, private material is labeled so it cannot leave by
design, and every claim the agents make must cite its evidence and leave a
receipt - a file with the real numbers that you can open. Bigger models
swap in when a problem genuinely justifies the cost, and swap back out.
It is not a chatbot, not a cloud service, and not a cult or a cause. It is
a workshop you own. (shape: AGENTS.md; "cannot leave by design": constitution §7)
