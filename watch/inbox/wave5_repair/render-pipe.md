Stage-4 hard clip is unchanged as the declared last resort (:1370-1372); the offload stage contract stays 1-4 (tests/test_dirac_loop.py:125-131 pins it).

Budget root cause (the w4-27 L2 class: erasure by budget). The old budgets predated the mandated core and could never carry it: measured post-fix floors after every sheddable block is gone are 6,925 chars (full) and 7,192 (build, including a 2,500-char standing scope card). Rationale: a budget smaller than the core it must deliver is not a budget, it is a silent eraser; sizing the budgets to the core is the root fix, capping the core to slivers to spare the old budgets would have delivered one-law fragments and repeated the disease one level down.
