# Recurring verbs & laws (re-confirmed / extended)

**His verbs, cross-corpus:** find · mine · steal · structure · **iterate deeply** (game2.txt, twice) · update · perfect (each tier) · fold · descend · cache · verify · patch (the corpus, not the request) · light (the lantern, twice) · claim (not move) · recount. Note the ratio: every verb is either acquisition (find/mine/steal), durabilization (structure/cache/patch/verify), or continuation (iterate/descend/fold/light/recount). Not one verb is "win."

**Laws re-confirmed:**
1. **Trust bytes, not reports** — every corpus file carries hashes, scores, receipts; the Gauntlet Loop's 3.6–5.05/10 is filed, not spun. Reconfirmed everywhere.
2. **Perfect each tier before climbing** — the ramp law; FROM_MUD_TO_WOW Part III convicts the drift ("the ramp exists; the complexity hasn't ramped") and re-issues the law.
3. **Cache the contract; compute only the delta** — reconfirmed and *extended*: now spans prompts (genome), context (TokenPilot/RACS), hardware (Ising TSU, mycelium chips), and the estate (verkle chain, lore-prefix DM). The house law and the library law are the same law met twice.
4. **The genome tells how, not what** — "It does not claim to contain the game" (game2/game3/basecamp) = the estate's RIB law ("the key is the rule executed, not the artifact venerated").
5. **Tri-state honesty** — VERIFIED/PLAUSIBLE/UNVERIFIED (estate) = attested/reconstructed/speculative (corpus). Same protocol, independently invented — 5,200 years of Senet scholarship and one Windows machine.

**Laws extended (new this corpus):**
