# CLASSIFICATIONS — the estate through Carse's lens

Rubric lines (James P. Carse, *Finite and Infinite Games*, 1986): **(a)** "A finite game is played for the purpose of winning, an infinite game for the purpose of continuing the play." **(b)** "Finite players play within boundaries; infinite players play with boundaries." **(c)** "The rules of an infinite game must change in the course of play." **(d)** "There is no such thing as playing an infinite game alone." Titles are the finite winner's prize — an infinite game, having no conclusion, confers none.
