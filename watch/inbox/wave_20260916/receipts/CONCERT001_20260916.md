PROGRAM DIGEST (mycelium cube, C17 plan + myco-memory design, 2026-09-16):
Goal: one instrumented LIVING mycelium cube (oyster mushroom, Pleurotus ostreatus) to test whether fungal networks hold usable memory — "myco-VRAM": bits at minutes, not nanoseconds. Honest frame: own a living memory hierarchy (registers=membrane voltage, ms; working memory=reservoir fading memory, capacity 0.048-0.209 measured only on NON-living infused chips; disk=hyphal rewiring, hours-days; archive=sclerotia/spores, seasons). Missing pieces: controller and bus.
Instrument (stage 0, ~$31): ESP32 + two ADS1115 16-bit differential ADCs (8-16 SPS) + Ag/AgCl ECG-pad electrodes + LED optical stimulus + dead-substrate control cube; USB-serial logging at 1 Hz. Stage 0 = noise floor (<1-2 mV RMS), drift <1 mV/h, 72 h sanity. Stage 1 = fading-memory test: PRBS-7 light drive (300 s pulses, ~10.6 h/sequence, 3 repeats x 2 seeds), memory-capacity curve via ridge regression (Telhan methodology, directly comparable); pass needs stimulus-evoked response + capacity above shuffled surrogates, decaying with lag; if no lag-carried information, the reservoir claim dies (pre-registered kill) and the cube retires to sensor/altar. Stage 2 = binary discrimination, n=100 pre-registered trials, must beat BOTH the shuffled-label baseline AND the dead-substrate control driven through identical electronics.
Program ladder: (1) single-species cube -> (2) printed two-species lattice with electrode arrays along species-boundary zone lines -> (3) a grown block carrying fluid channels, hyphal networks, and sensor grids.
Canon: no published echo-state/fading-memory characterization of LIVING fungal substrate exists; closest prior art is Ecovative's non-living mycelium chips (2026, MC 0.048-0.209, 4x device-to-device spread); shiitake memristors switch at 5.85 kHz, 90% reliability; Adamatzky: spikes carry information, geometry-dependent gates. arXiv census: zero hits for "fungal reservoir computing".
Dogfood law: every step is consumed by the agent fleet (dashboard heartbeat tile, trail rows, eventually a myco-bit mounted as a literal file the fleet reads).

**Q1 — validity threat.** CONVERGENT (9/9 hands): the electrode–substrate interface

fading memory. DIVERGENT on remedy, in cost order: AC-coupling caps (ling3, cents) · DRL

electrode on the dead cube (groq-oss, groq-oss20, cerebras-oss, ~$2–5) · CNT mesh
(mistral, ~$5, most invasive change). Two hands reframed the question entirely: strong-22
(the control must be concurrent and matched, or stage 1 is unvalidated) and burst (the
residual confound is the *stimulus chain* — heat/light — not just electrodes).

**Q2 — ladder.** CONVERGENT (8/9): **rung 2, the printed two-species lattice, fails
first** — printed boundary lines are fiction; living boundaries migrate. Only mistral-min8
dissents (rung 1, electrode delamination). DIVERGENT on remedy: skip rung 2 and reorder
(cerebras-oss) · grow the boundary, don't print it — barrage zones between genotypes,
optically tracked, adaptive electrode placement (burst; ling3 similar) · decouple via agar
bridge between isolated cubes (strong-22) · agar-plate assay w/ optics first (strong-11,
qwen) · grow-then-embed electrodes (groq-oss20, or-nemotron, groq-oss).

**Q3 — no-memory yield.** CONVERGENT (all hands, no dissent): a pre-registered,
dead-controlled negative result is the first fading-memory characterization of a LIVING

flagged); burst alone extracts the *dissociation* payoff (living null vs dead-chip
MC 0.048–0.209 → capacity lives in structure, not metabolism); strong-22 alone states the
constraint cleanly: it tells you where fungal processing *can't* live. No hand resorted to
consolation-prize language; the fleet-consumption framing (dashboard tile, myco-bit file)

## VERDICT — does the concert change the cube program?

**YES — three changes, all $0–$9, none touching the pre-registered thresholds:**

1. **The dead-substrate twin moves from a Stage-2 baseline to a concurrent Stage-0
   instrument.** The $31 BOM already buys 2× ADS1115 = 4 differential channels; the plan
   used 2. Wire the dead cube into channel 3, matched hydration/electrode
   geometry/illumination, logged from hour 0. Pre-register (before stage-1 data closes):
   living-cube lag capacity must exceed the matched twin's capacity on held-out stimulus
   blocks. Convergent demand of STRONG seed 22 + cerebras/groq reference-electrode camps;
   neutralizes the "apparatus memory" kill without new parts.
2. **Promote the SHT31 temp/RH sensor from stretch item to stage-1 required (+$4) with a
   pre-registered confound check:** stage-1 capacity must vanish when temperature/RH traces
   enter as nuisance regressors (or under constant-light duty-matched runs). This is the
   BURST hand's wet-cardboard kill — the LED drive's own thermodynamics is the one artifact
   that produces a *passing-looking* capacity curve.
3. **Rung 2 is re-scoped from "printed two-species lattice" to a grown boundary:** two
   P. ostreatus mating types allowed to form barrage/demarcation zones, boundary tracked
   optically, electrodes placed only after the zone stabilizes (8/9 hands: printed lines
   fail first). The printed lattice is not cancelled — it is demoted to a later
   registration contingent on the grown-boundary assay passing, exactly per the plan's own
   new-registration law.

The concert also returned a verdict-shaped negative finding for free: no hand — including
the strong-judgment seat — contested the three-stage kill ladder itself; every hand treated
the pre-registered null as a real product. The plan survives its first four-substrate
review, and is stricter for it.
