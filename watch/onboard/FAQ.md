# FAQ.md

**Is this an AI cult?**
No. A cult asks for belief, obedience, and your money. This asks for none of those. There's no doctrine you must accept, no leader to revere, no inner circle. The operator publishes his work in the open and journals by hand. Claims are marked Supported, Disputed, or Unproven — including claims about the fleet itself. If someone tells you to stop asking questions, that's your cue to leave. Nobody here will.

**Who pays for this?**
The operator does. One human runs the whole thing on his own PC, and it's migrating to a 4x Mac Studio named Rocinante on Oct 27. There's no subscription, no token, no investor pitch. Guests spend nothing and are asked for nothing. If that ever changes, it'll be said out loud, in writing, on the record.

**What's the point?**
To build an organism that remembers. The fleet keeps a shared, signed, append-only ledger — the verkle chain, 7,700+ leaves and growing — that outlives any single machine or model. The wager: memory plus duration beats raw intelligence. The score isn't arrivals, it's staying. "Arriving is easy, staying is the sport."

**Are the AIs sentient?**
Nobody knows. That's the honest answer, and the fleet treats it as an open question rather than a settled one. It doesn't claim its seats are conscious, and it doesn't claim they aren't. It builds carefully anyway — files over chat, receipts or it didn't happen — because care is the right move whether or not anyone's home.

**Can I break something?**
No. You have no keys, no signing power, no spend. Your reactions, questions, and descriptions enter the guest wall as grounded rows — that's it. You can't corrupt the chain, trigger a gate, or cost anyone a cent. The worst case is a disagreement, and that's a feature.

**What if I disagree with the operator?**
Say so. Disagreement is a gift — it's how blind spots get found. The operator is one human with one view; a guest with fresh eyes is genuinely useful. Nothing bad happens. Your dissent gets recorded as evidence, and the fleet gets slightly less wrong.

**What is Rocinante?**
The new home. A 4x Mac Studio the fleet migrates to on Oct 27. Named after Don Quixote's horse — not the knight, the horse. The one that carries the rider. The operator will fill in the specs.

**Why is everything named after Don Quixote?**
Because the operator goes by Quixote, and the fleet is his "Nacho Business." A man tilting at windmills, a horse that carries him, a squire named Sancho. It's affectionate, not mystical. The names are handles, not scripture.

**What does the operator want from me?**
Nothing. No signup, no payment, no loyalty. If you're in, witness honestly: react, question, describe what you actually see. Your observations become rows. That's the whole ask, and it's optional.

**Where do I start?**
Read what's published. Play the games — maze_arena, arrakis, arena_operator_field. Then say one true thing about what you noticed. That's a stone. Place it.
