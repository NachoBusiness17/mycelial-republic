## CONTEXT

You're Adam. You just asked "so what do I need to do?" Here's the honest answer: almost nothing, in small pieces. You're a guest weaver. Your job this week is to look, and to say what you see. That's it. No keys, no signing, no spending. Your eyes are the contribution.

This is a field manual, not a ritual. Fifteen minutes a day, tops.

## TASK

# FIRST_WEEK.md

Welcome, Adam. Seven days, seven small acts. Each one is under fifteen minutes. Each one feeds the record — not because you sign it, but because the operator reads guest observations and decides what to fold in. You are a witness. That role is real.

---

**Day 1 — Read the welcome, play one game.**
*Act:* Read the welcome material in the Discord. Then open the dashboard and play one round of any surface — maze_arena, arrakis, or arena_operator_field. Pick by curiosity, not by strategy.
*Time:* 15 min.
*Feeds:* Your first grounded row: what the game felt like, what surprised you. Guests can post reactions; the operator decides what gets folded in.

**Day 2 — Read STACK_V1, sections 0–3.**
*Act:* Read those sections slowly. Don't try to hold it all. Note one thing that made sense and one thing that didn't.
*Time:* 15 min.
*Feeds:* Vocabulary. You'll need it to react usefully on Day 3.

**Day 3 — One honest reaction to any wave card.**
*Act:* Open any wave card from a recent run. Write one sentence: what you noticed. Agreement or disagreement, both count. This is your first stone.
*Time:* 10 min.
*Feeds:* The guest wall. Your disagreement is a gift — it's how blind spots get found.

**Day 4 — Read the stone thesis.**
*Act:* Read FIELD, STONES, TERRITORY, SEDIMENT, WITNESSES. Ask: does this grammar hold at your scale too, or only theirs?
*Time:* 15 min.
*Feeds:* Whether the fleet's one-grammar claim survives an outside eye.

**Day 5 — Read the Primer parable. Say whether it landed.**
*Act:* Read it. Then answer, plainly: did it land for you, yes or no, and why.
*Time:* 10 min.
*Feeds:* Your answer is data. The fleet needs to know if its own teaching works on someone new.

**Day 6 — Pick one open question. Hold it.**
*Act:* Choose one unresolved question from anything you've read this week. Don't answer it. Just sit with it and write what it makes you wonder.
*Time:* 10 min.
*Feeds:* The question stays open in the record. Holding beats resolving.

**Day 7 — Three lines on what you saw that nobody inside could see.**
*Act:* Write three lines. Not a summary. The thing an insider would miss because they're inside.
*Time:* 15 min.
*Feeds:* The whole point. Guests see edges the fleet can't.

---

**What you can do:** read, play, witness, react, question, steal ideas.
**What you can't do:** touch keys, sign rows, trigger gates, spend.
**The operator will fill in:** exact links to STACK_V1, the wave cards, and the Primer parable.

That's the week. Small stones. Welcome aboard.
