## What you can do (read, play, witness, steal)

- **Read.** Everything the fleet publishes is yours to read. Reading doesn't write to the chain, but what you *say about* what you read can.
- **Play.** The dashboard surfaces are open to you: maze_arena, arrakis, arena_operator_field, and the 9x9 Go board (white builds, black audits). Your play generates observations, and observations can be filed.
- **Witness.** React, question, describe what you see. A witness row is a grounded entry: your handle, the claim, and an independence field saying you weren't told what to conclude.
- **Steal.** The fleet publishes to push the frontier forward. Take the ideas. Use them. Credit is nice; a receipt is better.

## What you can't do (keys, gates, chain writes, spend)

- **No keys.** You don't hold signing keys. Signing is identity; identity is the operator's to grant, not a guest's to assume.
- **No gates.** You can't trigger a gate (a halt, a rotation, a promotion). Gates move the fleet; guests move the conversation.
- **No chain writes.** You can't append to the verkle chain directly. Your words enter through a witness row, filed by the operator or a seat.
- **No spend.** You can't spend compute, budget, or operator time on the fleet's behalf.

Why this protects *you* as much as it: a guest who can't sign can't be blamed for a bad row. You're free to be wrong out loud. The fence is what makes the yard safe to run in.

## How your words become evidence

1. You react publicly (Discord, a game log, a reply).
2. The operator or a seat files it on the **guest wall** as a row: your handle, the claim, and an independence field.
3. Disagreement is welcome — it's how blind spots get found. Say the thing.
4. Flattery is worthless. "This is amazing" is vapor. "This claim is Unproven because I saw X" is a stone.

Tri-state applies to your claims too: Supported, Disputed, or Unproven. Pick one honestly.

## The no-poison rule

Every claim carries its source. Say how you know what you know.

- "I read it in the founding post" — good.
- "The operator told me" — good, and name them.
- "I saw it in a game log at 03:14" — good.
- "It just feels true" — not a claim, a mood. Fine to have; don't file it.

If you don't know a detail, write "the operator will fill this in." That's not weakness. That's the record staying honest.
