## How to read a wave card

- A wave card is a claim plus its receipts, not a vibe.
- Verdicts are tri-state: Supported / Disputed / Unproven. "Unproven" is a real answer, not a failure.
- Every claim should name its falsifier — the thing that would prove it wrong. If none is named, treat it as Unproven.
- Your reaction to a card is evidence. Disagreement is a gift; it is how blind spots get found.
