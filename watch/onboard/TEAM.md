# THE TEAM — play, write, build

This is a small open shop: one person and their AI making browser games and short
fiction, everything shipped as a single HTML file you can play anywhere. No launcher,
no install, no waiting. Made-with-AI stays public — it's on every tag.

Play first. Everything else is optional.

## PLAY (one click)

**/games** — the shelf.

- **MUD** — the flagship. A torch-lit dungeon crawler: real darkness with wall shadows,
  enemies that *learn your tactics* (kite them and spitters start mining your escape
  routes; survive a swarm and packs start hunting as one), a First Dragon that shatters
  into whelps that shatter into sparks, a SUPERHOT powerup (time moves only when you
  move), keys and pressure plates and a vault that grinds open, and level-up boon
  drafts — pick one of three god gifts and build a run around it. Every loop, the whole
  horde gets meaner. Forever.
- **maze_arena** — a light-bending arena.
- **swarm** — the disco science skate.
- **arrakis** — the desert wing.

## THE DOORS (yours, not ours)

**If you write** — this is the easy one: send story beats, NPC lines, item names, or a
whole world. Books count. A "Skyrim-ish but in my book's world" idea is a playable
browser dungeon in about a week — you write the world and the words; the code gets
built around your text, never the other way around.

**If you build** — every game here is a single self-contained HTML file: no install, no
framework, no build step. Yours can ship the same way. Working on games mapped to AI?
So is the house stack — the dungeon's enemies already run on it. Bring the file, it
goes on the shelf with your name on it.

**If you play** — play. Break things. Tell us what you saw. "The fish thing shot me
through a wall" is a bug report we can actually use. Your descriptions get written
into the world.
