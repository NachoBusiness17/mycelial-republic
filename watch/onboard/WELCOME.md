# WELCOME v3 — meet him at the game (Primer law applied)

## THE LAW (filed 09-12, after Mark's correction)

"The whole point of this game was to meet people where they are. This is the opposite."
Every reader enters through THEIR door: gamers enter through games, brothers through
the shared history, auditors through the failure logs, the heir through the parable.
One corpus, many doors. Never a syllabus. The Primer doesn't hand the child a glossary.

Nothing else. No glossary, no site tour, no "what this actually is." He lands on a

the letter: /letter.html. When he asks how it works, THEN the stack. The door opens
at his pace, in his order, and Mark is on the other side of every step.

## What was wrong with v1 and v2 (for the record)

- v1: stranger register — a brochure. ("Is this an AI cult?" — to a man who's known
  Mark since childhood.)
- v2: brother register but STILL archive-first — "read the letter, then the games,
  then call me." A syllabus with better pronouns. The kit asked Adam to come to the
  fleet's world and learn its language. The Primer principle is the reverse: the
  machine goes to the player, speaks his language (D&D, games, jokes), and hides the
  machinery in plain sight until he reaches for it.
- The soapy-hands rule: the player reaches; the physics refuses; the companion finds
  the move. Onboarding is the soapy hand, not the rulebook.
