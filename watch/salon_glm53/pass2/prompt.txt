The coastal survey of the Meridian estuary documented tidal gradients, sediment transport rates, and the seasonal migration of benthic fauna. From April to September, samples were taken at fortnightly intervals along a forty-kilometer transect, and each station was logged with its depth, temperature, salinity, and substrate composition. The data were normalized against a reference baseline and then smoothed using a moving average that suppressed short-period noise while preserving the underlying seasonal signal. Notable anomalies, such as the unusually high turbidity readings in late June, were flagged for follow-up and cross-referenced against upstream discharge records before any interpretation was accepted. The final report recommended a reduced sampling cadence for the following season, given the stability of the observed regime.
