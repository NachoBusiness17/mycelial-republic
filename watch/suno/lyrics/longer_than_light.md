I was thinking of light when the daylight went thin
something moved in the trees, then folded within
A face like mine, or a face I once knew
one eye in shadow, one turning blue
We carried a fire, we carried a name
somebody vanished, somebody changed
I don't remember who lifted the stone
only the eyes, and the light on the bone
The sea keeps a line where the blue disappears
the dead keep their silence, the living their years
A face for a moment, a room left somewhere
some things disappear, some things stay near
Out past the shoreline, out of our sight
sometimes the image lives longer than light
A king in a portrait, gold at his side
a man underneath it with nowhere to hide
The painter caught velvet, the title, the name
something went missing outside of the frame
For one little second the distance was gone
then only the canvas, the water, the dawn
Sky into water, water into sky
a thousand small colors escaping the eye
Phosphene burning where nothing had been
a little false light at the edge of the seen
The tide took a shadow, the shore kept the glow
the same disappearing, the same afterglow
Whole years went missing, one window stayed bright
one face came back in a different light
I couldn't remember the room or the year
only the shape of what still felt near
Something moved once at the edge of the trees
something looked back from a century's sleep
Then the water went dark, and the picture went white
nothing was there but the remainder of light
