Blings are the currency used in Wildfrost. Blings are used to purchase goods from shops and vendors, and they are collected and spent exclusively during a run. Blings do not persist between runs, so hoarding them in hopes of keeping them for a later run is futile.

Earning Blings

Players can earn Blings in several ways. The most common way is by defeating enemies in battle, although enemies that run away after battle will also drop their Blings. However, enemies in waves that have not entered battle will not drop their Blings. There are also two effects that grant extra Blings when killing enemies: the Bling Bank is a Clunker that awards extra Blings whenever an enemy is defeated, and the Bling Charm awards extra Blings whenever the attached card kills another card.

Defeating multiple enemies within the same turn creates a kill combo, which can rack up a lot of extra Blings. Each kill after the first in the turn will drop 5 bonus Blings. For this purpose, a turn starts when the player gains control, and ends when the player next gains control. This means that indirect damage sources like Shroom and Teeth still count towards the same combo if enemies were killed directly that turn (such as by an attack or Item).

The Gobling is a unique enemy that appears in most fights, and it drops 4 Blings when hit by anything. However, it also escapes from the battle when it triggers, giving only a limited window of opportunity to earn Blings from it.

The final way to earn Blings is by visiting Blingsnail Caves on the map. Each cave randomly awards 40 to 80 Blings.

Using Blings

Bling can be spent at The Woolly Snail and the Charm Merchant during a run. Depending on which shop the player visits, they can purchase Items, Charms, or Crowns.

Bling can also be hoarded for cards with Greed, granted by the Greed Charm. This allows the affected card to deal 1 extra damage per 50 Blings held, and can be devastating when carrying hundreds of Blings or combined with Frenzy.

Enemy Drops

Every enemy besides Goblings and the Naked Gnome drops a base amount of Blings and an additional 0-2 Blings when killed. All minibosses have a base value of 13, as do Bam and Boozle.
