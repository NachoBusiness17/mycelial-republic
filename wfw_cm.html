The Charm Merchant

The Charm Merchant is a Map event. It is a shop for spending Blings to purchase Charms, and an upgraded card.

Stock

  • 3 Charms, costing on average 65 Blings.
  • 1 Item or Clunker with 1-2 charms, costing its Shop value or Clunker price + 10-20 Blings for each charm, then reduced by 5
  • There is a 1% chance the item has 2 charms.

See below for accurate calculations of prices.
