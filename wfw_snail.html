The Woolly Snail

The Woolly Snail is a Map event. It is a shop for spending Blings to purchase Charms, Items, and Crowns.

Contents

  • 1 Stock
  • 2 Storm Bells
  • 3 Item Prices
  • 4 Trivia
  • 5 Other Languages

Stock

  • 3 non- Consumable Items, costing about 30-50 Blings.
  • 1 Item with Consume, costing about 45-65 Blings.
  • 1
    Crown
    , costing 75 Blings.
  • Up to 3 uses of the Charm Dispenser, costing 45 / 65 / 85 Blings. Each use grants a random Charm that has not been obtained in the current run from the Woolly Snail, Charm Dispenser Map event, and Charm Merchant. The price of the Charm Dispenser resets to 45 each time the Woolly Snail is visited again.

Additionally, exactly 1 of the 4 offered Items will be discounted by 50%, indicated by striking through the original price and displaying the sale price next to it.

If the player is exactly 1 Bling short of being able to purchase something, repeatedly attempting to purchase it will eventually cause the shopkeeper to relent and sell it for 1 Bling cheaper.

See below for accurate calculations of prices.

Storm Bells

  • All prices are 5 Blings higher when the Blingsnail Bell is active. Additionally, each Crown costs 10 more Blings than the last (this increase does persist between visits). So, Charms cost 50/70/90 Blings and crowns cost 80/90/100/110 Blings.
  • If the Gloom Bell is active, exactly 1 of the 4 offered Items gains a Cursed Charm.
  • If the Tyrant Bell is active, exactly 1 shop between the 2nd and 6th fights will contain a Cursed Crown instead of a normal Crown. This shop is not guaranteed to be on the player's path, however.
  • The Broken Vase and Lumin Goop may be offered at the Woolly Snail if the player has at least 10 points' worth of Storm Bells active, in which case one of the non- Consumable cards will be replaced.
  • These cards will not necessarily appear at a Woolly Snail event, and may appear at the Gnome Traveller or Treasure Chest instead.

Trivia

Shop interior

The interior of The Woolly Snail contains several references to other games and cartoons[1]:

  • The Knight's mask from Hollow Knight
  • A Spiny's shell from the Super Mario franchise
  • Lurie's Spark Pendant, from Gaziter's personal project Sparknight
  • An Idol from Spelunky
  • Stygius, Zagreus's sword from Hades
  • A Strawberry from Celeste
  • Pum's Pendant, from Gaziter's personal project The Pumpkin Hunters
  • The Rainbringer, Gaziter's OC Tyra's weapon
  • Shovel Knight's helmet
  • The Finn Sword from Adventure Time
  • A crewmate from Among Us
