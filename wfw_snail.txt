Up to 3 uses of the Charm Dispenser, costing
45 / 65 / 85
Blings. Each use grants a random Charm that has not been obtained in the current run from the Woolly Snail,
Charm Dispenser
Map event, and
Charm Merchant

Additionally, exactly 1 of the 4 offered Items will be discounted by
50%
, indicated by striking through the original price and displaying the sale price next to it.
If the player is exactly 1 Bling short of being able to purchase something, repeatedly attempting to purchase it will eventually cause the shopkeeper to relent and sell it for 1 Bling cheaper.
See below for accurate calculations of prices.
Storm Bells
All prices are
5
Blings higher when the
Blingsnail Bell
is active. Additionally, each Crown costs
10
more Blings than the last (this increase
does
persist between visits). So, Charms cost
50/70/90
Blings and crowns cost
80/90/100/110
Blings.
If the
Gloom Bell
is active, exactly
1
of the 4 offered Items gains a
Cursed Charm
.
If the
Tyrant Bell
is active, exactly
1
shop between the 2nd and 6th fights will contain a
Cursed Crown
instead of a normal Crown. This shop is not guaranteed to be on the player's path, however.
The
Broken Vase
and
Lumin Goop
may be offered at the Woolly Snail if the player has at least
10
points' worth of
Storm Bells
active, in which case one of the non-
Consumable
cards will be replaced.
These cards will not necessarily appear at a Woolly Snail event, and may appear at the
Gnome Traveller
or
Treasure Chest
instead.
