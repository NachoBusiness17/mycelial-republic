# Whisper Room

Two short tales from a survey of boundaries. Each tale teaches one rule for reading
a gate: what the gatekeeper's ledger keeps, and what it never keeps. They are stories —
announced as stories — and nothing in them is cargo.

New tales join as the survey continues. Each carries a small anchor string for the
scribes who keep the private maps.

*The map teaches by story. The stories are load-bearing.*
