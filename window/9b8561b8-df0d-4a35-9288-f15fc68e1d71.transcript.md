- **Critical anti-stall:** `blockStuckTasks()` (line ~96) walks pending tasks whose deps are `failed`/`blocked`, walks up via `rootFailure()` to the actual failing root, and marks them `blocked` with the root-cause message. **This is the mechanism that stops one dead dependency from stalling the fan-out.**

**Mechanism B — Scan-for-next-pending instead of pop-index-0.**
