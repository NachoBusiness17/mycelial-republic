Explain this page

  1. Anchor. “This fund pays about {{DEBT_SERVICE_Y1}} a year for {{PROJECT}}. Who collects that interest?”
  2. Structure, not villain. “Not a conspiracy. Book-entry. Munis live at DTC under Cede & Co. The county cannot see beneficial owners because the system was built for settlement speed.”
  3. Priority. “The first question is what we can see at the speed of the vote — the roll call, the contract, the yearly hit, what else that fund pays.”
  4. Influence. “Local capture shows up in campaign checks, vendor contracts, advisor fees, ethics filings. The threshold rule catches the rare case where a local actor holds the paper.”
  5. Privacy. “No public list of every private holder. European courts already blocked open natural-person ownership registers. Fund-level is allowed. Private names are not, unless they are covered persons who must self-disclose.”
  6. Close. “Borrowed in public, paid in public, lenders behind a 1980s–90s market. Demand the conflict file and the budget line first, a targeted disclosure rule second.”

2Conflict check — threshold self-disclosure

The rule that has not been run as a standing local practice: covered persons swear to whether they hold the paper they voted on. Yes/No + amount band. Nothing else is collected.

The threshold, in plain words

Disclose if you received more than $5,000 of interest in a fiscal year from this issuer's bonds, or if you (or your spouse or dependent children) hold 5% or more of an entity that holds them. Disclosure is a short sworn statement to the clerk: Yes / No + amount band. Non-response is published as “no response received,” with the date.

Covered persons

  • Elected officials who vote on the borrowing
  • County manager and finance director
  • Project vendors and their principals
  • Spouses and dependent children of the above

What this page refuses to do

  • It does not request or display DTC participant beneficial-owner files.
  • It does not build a live public list of natural-person bondholders.
  • “Fund-level” means “Fund Family X reports a muni sleeve.” It never means “Resident Y holds $50,000.”

European courts have already blocked open natural-person ownership registers as a disproportionate privacy interference (CJEU, joined cases C-37/20 and C-601/20, 22 November 2022). Legitimate-interest access — press, research, oversight with a stated purpose — remains the lawful road.

4Method statement

The county borrowed in public and pays from the General Fund in public. Current lenders sit behind a book-entry market built in the 1980s and 1990s. Sunlight that matches the speed of county government is the vote, the yearly payment, the advisor file, and a targeted conflict rule — not a claim that nothing is known until every bond fund is named.

Boundaries this method stands inside

  • Privacy boundary. CJEU, joined cases C-37/20 and C-601/20 (22 Nov 2022): no general-public natural-person holder list. we-have-a-record
  • Federal-filing boundary. Tower Amendment (Exchange Act §15B(d)) bars SEC/MSRB from forcing issuer filings. It does not bar a county from an ethics rule for its own officials and vendors. we-have-a-record
  • Enforcement line. If a covered person fails to disclose and is later shown to hold the bonds, that is an ethics violation, not a missing owner registry. we-have-a-record
