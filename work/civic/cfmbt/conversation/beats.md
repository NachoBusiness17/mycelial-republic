# Conversation beats — six spoken beats, public-safe

Use these in the app's "Explain this page" drawer and in spoken briefings.
No shop codes, no desk names, no helper names on this surface.

1. **Anchor.** "This fund pays about {{DEBT_SERVICE_Y1}} a year for {{PROJECT}}.
   Who collects that interest?"

2. **Structure, not villain.** "Not a conspiracy. Book-entry. Munis live at DTC
   under Cede & Co. The county cannot see beneficial owners because the system
   was built for settlement speed."

3. **Priority.** "The first question is what we can see at the speed of the
   vote — the roll call, the contract, the yearly hit, what else that fund pays."

4. **Influence.** "Local capture shows up in campaign checks, vendor contracts,
   advisor fees, ethics filings. The threshold rule catches the rare case where
   a local actor holds the paper."

5. **Privacy.** "No public list of every private holder. European courts already
   blocked open natural-person ownership registers. Fund-level is allowed.
   Private names are not, unless they are covered persons who must self-disclose."

6. **Close.** "Borrowed in public, paid in public, lenders behind a 1980s–90s
   market. Demand the conflict file and the budget line first, a targeted
   disclosure rule second."
